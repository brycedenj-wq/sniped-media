## 10. What Could Go Wrong

**Teaching collapses positioning.** If the book is marketed as a how-to for anyone to replicate SNIPED's results, it dilutes the Sacrifice Doctrine ("what you choose NOT to do defines what you are," OS_MASTER_DOCTRINE §3). The frame must be: "here is the method, here is why it works, here is what it costs you to use it without the lineage depth behind it." The book teaches; it does not commoditize.

**Launch without the email list is structurally fragile.** Platform dependency (LinkedIn, IG) makes the launch subject to algorithm behavior. The OS names this risk explicitly (OS_MASTER_DOCTRINE §8). If the launch happens without a prior-built email list, the Perennial Seller model still works (long-horizon, word-of-mouth), but the 30-day revenue target becomes unreliable.

**Time pressure from Direction Days competes with Reset capacity.** One Direction Day per month at $2,500 is $30K/year. But it consumes a production day. The OS's Phase B trigger requires $3K MRR sustained before expansion. If Direction Days are pulled in before Phase B trigger, they are a bottleneck pressure, not a relief valve. Gate: no Direction Days sold until Phase B is achieved or until each Direction Day is demonstrably margin-positive relative to its Reset opportunity cost.

**Category name failure.** Launching without a coined, owned category name means the book competes in an existing mental shelf (posing guides, photography business books) rather than creating the shelf. Category kings get the economic premium (OS_MASTER_DOCTRINE §3, Play Bigger). Without the category name, the book gets shelved alongside competitors it is structurally superior to but cannot signal that superiority to buyers rapidly.

**IP succession gap creates licensing risk.** Without a formal IP ownership chain documented and succession-planned, any studio licensing deal signed carries legal ambiguity. The IP ownership audit skill must run before any licensing deal enters signature stage.

## 11. The Smallest Proof Loop

**Total cost: $0 cash, 3 hours of BJ's time. Goal: confirm demand exists before any production investment.**

Step 1 (90 min): Write one LinkedIn POV post. Subject: the one Direction Stack protocol that most photographers ignore (Protocol 06 cascade, or the Continuous Direction Current). No announcement. No "coming soon." Just doctrine with authority. Measure: saves, DMs, follower questions within 48 hours.

Step 2 (15 min): Add a single-sentence CTA at the end of the post: "I'm drafting the Direction Stack as a field manual. If you want early access, reply with 'field manual' and I'll add you to the list." This is the email-list seed test. No landing page. No tool. Just DMs.

Step 3 (gate): If 10+ people reply within 72 hours, the demand signal is real. If 0-2 reply, the post reached the wrong audience or the language was wrong. Kill-or-iterate on the framing. No production cost lost.

Step 4 (if 10+ replies): Send a 3-question DM to each responder (Mom Test: "What is the hardest part of directing subjects for you?" / "Have you ever lost a client because the images felt stiff or performative?" / "If you had a protocol for that, would you pay for it or figure it out yourself?"). This is the `sniped-mom-test-intake` skill applied to a product validation, not a service sale.

That loop takes one week and costs zero dollars. The data it produces directly informs whether the book is a Green/Yellow/Red signal before any production investment is made.

If 3 posts generate 0 DMs, the audience does not exist on LinkedIn for this product yet. Route to IG instead. ## 13. Optionality: Does It Preserve or Collapse It

**It preserves optionality.** The Direction Stack book is one expression of Bryce's method, not a permanent identity lock. It does not require abandoning photographer identity, SNIPED Media identity, or BASEPLATE identity. The book can be authored under any brand umbrella. The cohort and Direction Day can run under SNIPED. The studio licensing can run under Baseplate, LLC.

The Formlessness Check (OS_CAPABILITY_MAP §2, `sniped-formlessness-check`): is the commitment reversible? Yes, a self-published PDF can be taken off sale. Does it hand competitors a fixed target? Partially: once published, competitors know the protocols. But the Lineage Doctrine creates the protection: the protocols are reproducible; the cultural authority behind them is not. Does it close optionality? No: Bryce can stop teaching cohorts or licensing the method at any time.

The one optionality risk: if the book is positioned as "Bryce the photography teacher," it competes with other education brands and risks pulling him into that identity against his preference. The Sacrifice Doctrine applies: refuse "photography teacher" as identity. The book is authored by "Bryce, the practitioner who documented the method." The distinction is non-trivial. Keep the positioning language on proof, not pedagogy.
