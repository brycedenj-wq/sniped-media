# Clip Selection Rubric - Alma Love reel

Use this to pick from the ~380 raw clips. Watch clips, do not sample thumbnails. Target: ~12 to 16 selects, then ~8 to 12 in the final cut. Fewer great clips beats more okay clips.

## Buckets to fill (you only need a handful in each)
- **HOOK (1-2):** one striking opener that grabs the eye in under 1.5s.
- **HERO (1-2):** the model looking confident and expensive in the best-graded shot. This is the held beat. The most important pick.
- **MOTION (2-3):** a walk, a turn, a movement with intention.
- **TEXTURE / INSERT (2-3):** close shots - fabric, water, jewelry/detail, hands. These give it richness so it is not just full-body clips.
- **BUTTON (1):** a clean closing frame.

## KEEP a clip if (all of these)
- Model reads confident / in-register (not awkward, not caught mid-blink).
- Sharp and well-exposed (no soft focus, no blown highlights, no muddy shadows).
- On-brand Alma world (warm retro Americana energy, not generic).
- Motion is intentional (a real move, not a dead or shaky hold).
- Grade/skin matches the rest (same person, same look across clips).

## KILL a clip if (any of these)
- Soft, blurry, shaky without intent, or badly exposed.
- Awkward pose, mid-blink, unflattering frame.
- Off-brand or off-world.
- Identity drift (does not match the model in the other selects).
- Nothing happens (dead frame with only a tiny push).
- It only "works" with a template effect slapped on it.

## Priority order if time is tight
1. Lock the HERO. 2. Lock the HOOK. 3. Fill MOTION. 4. Fill TEXTURE. 5. Pick the BUTTON.
The hero and hook are non-negotiable. Everything else supports them.
