## 02 · The problem
Your recruiters are good. Your placements hold. But to a candidate weighing three offers, your firm looks like one more logo in the feed. In a market where commissioning agents, critical-facilities techs, and MEP operators pick between multiple offers, the firm that looks the most credible and the most worth joining wins the signature. Right now that call is being made on a thin careers page and a LinkedIn feed that does not show what you actually are. And the timing cuts against you: the build-out is pulling every strong tech and every serious bid into a bidding war, so looking generic has never cost more.

## 03 · The offer
The Capability Dossier is a premium proof asset built insider-to-insider. It captures who your firm is, the caliber of your people, and why a top operator should sign with you, in language a critical-facilities professional actually respects. You own it. You reuse it across your careers page, recruiter outreach, candidate follow-up, and client trust. It is built to move one thing: the yes.

## 04 · Who it is for
- Critical-facilities and data-center staffing and recruiting firms who need top techs to choose them.
- Specialty subcontractors in the data-center supply chain (electrical, mechanical, commissioning, cooling, networking, field service) using it for bid-support and recruiting.
- Owner-led firms that win on trust and need to look as good as they actually are.

Not for firms looking for cheap content, headshots, or social media management.

## 05 · What is included
- A tight, credible capability narrative: who you are, what you deliver, the standard you hold.
- Approved imagery of your people and your work, captured in non-secure settings you control.
- A reusable, ownable image set for your careers page, proposals, and outreach.
- Candidate-attraction copy blocks: why work here, what we deliver.
- A one-screen capability profile, ready to drop into a careers page or a proposal.
- Private, branded delivery.

Larger engagements add fuller crew coverage, a combined recruiting and bid copy set, and a one-page capability or careers landing asset.
