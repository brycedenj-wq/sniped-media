# OS BOOK OPERATIONALIZATION AUDIT
### Chunked != done. Operationalized = the OS behaves differently because of it.
2026-06-05. From knowledge-to-capability-sprint-001 book auditor.

## Summary
The book layer is ~60 chunk families covering roughly 200+ titles, and the audit finding is blunt: chunked is not operationalized, and the priority-1 money/sales/offers cluster is the biggest gap. FULLY_OPERATIONALIZED is limited to the ~22 books already turned into certified intel_* atoms and sniped-* skills (BATCH_003 Tier 2 in full, the photo-theory core of BATCH_005, Hit Makers/distribution) plus SNIPED's own self-authored OS corpus (BATCH_001/004/006/007), which is the live operating system rather than extractable books. The highest-value un-operationalized work, all priority 1, is the offer/copy/persuasion engine sitting raw in chunks: BATCH_009 (Hormozi, Schwartz, Cialdini, Hopkins, Dunford, Godin, Heath), ADVERTISING_RECOVERY (Ogilvy, Sugarman, Halbert), BATCH_009_EXPANSION (Play Bigger category design, Christensen JTBD, Voss negotiation), CONSULTING_SERVICE (Weiss value-based fees, Lencioni Getting Naked), FASHION_LUXURY_STRATEGY (Kapferer luxury anti-laws), and The Mom Test from POSITIONING_DISRUPTION. These should be distilled into two or three new atoms (intel_offers, intel_copywriting, intel_category_jtbd) that plug directly into sniped-vib-outreach, sniped-pricing-decision, sniped-discovery-to-close, the BATCH_007 email templates, and the hook/caption libraries, each behind a hard pre-send gate (value-equation + awareness-stage + Mom-Test). Priority-2 taste/creativity (EXPERTISE_CREATIVITY with Rubin/Ericsson/Berger, STORYTELLING_NARRATIVE, BRAND_CANON naming) and a few pre-distilled operator kits (DECISION_SYSTEMS_SUPP, TOOLCHAIN_DISTRIBUTION_SUPP, DECISION_JUDGMENT_COGNITION) are low-cost, high-leverage promotions that are already doctrine-shaped. Everything else, the founder memoirs, classical strategy/history, finance/investing canon, media oral histories, hip-hop and literary canons, is correctly LOW_PRIORITY_REFERENCE_ONLY: real value as voice/lineage/case-study texture (notably BATCH_010 and the Black literary canon for the Lineage Doctrine) but not rule sources, and several (CULTURE_AND_STATUS, scaling books, classical strategy) are largely redundant with or doctrinally counter to existing atoms. Net: extraction effort should concentrate almost entirely on the priority-1 money/offer/copy families next, since that is where chunked-but-dormant knowledge maps most directly to revenue.
