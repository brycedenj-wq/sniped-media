## BOOK 1: The Intelligent Investor (Benjamin Graham, 2003 Collins Business)

**Coverage line:** 7/7 segments read; full text including Zweig commentary, chapters 1-20, appendix, and index.

**1-line thesis:** Sound investment is the disciplined practice of buying value below its intrinsic price with a margin of safety, sustained by temperament over intelligence, across long cycles that routinely punish speculation and reward patience.

### 4-8 Most Useful Frameworks (Dense)

**1. Margin of Safety as a universal operating principle.**
Never pay full value for anything. Leave buffer between what you pay and what the thing is worth. The buffer absorbs error, bad luck, and timing. This is not conservatism; it is precision. Without it, there is no room for mistakes and every operator is at risk of a zero.

**2. Investor vs. Speculator distinction.**
Three tests: (a) thorough analysis, (b) safety of principal, (c) adequate return. If an action fails any one of these, it is speculation, not investment. The boundary matters because speculators fund the intermediaries, not themselves.

**3. Industry Selection Fallacy.**
Obvious growth does not produce obvious profits. Airlines grew for decades; investors lost money. IBM was the right pick but funds held only 3%. The lesson: a growing market rewards its customers and its operators, rarely its investors. Own the moat, not the trend.

**4. Temperament over IQ.**
Ordinary people with emotional discipline outperform credentialed experts without it. The investor's worst enemy is himself. Self-control, patience, and the ability to sustain a position through irrationality are the rarest competencies.

**5. Pendulum and Rule of Opposites.**
Markets swing between unsustainable optimism and unjustified pessimism. When enthusiasm is highest, return expectation should be lowest. Peak consensus signals impending correction. Maximum bearishness at the bottom, maximum bullishness at the top. The intelligent response is to fade the crowd.

**6. Valuation Tripod (P/E ratio, dividend yield, bond yield).**
When stock earnings yield falls below bond yield, equities become unattractive. Shiller's CAPE method extends this: 10-year cyclically-adjusted P/E above 20 is a warning zone. Valuation discipline prevents buying good businesses at disastrous prices.

**7. Survivorship Bias in all historical narratives.**
Pre-1871 stock returns overstate reality by 1-2 percentage points annually because failed companies are excluded. The 100 "best practices" from successful operators are the filtered residue of 10,000 failures. Always ask what the sample excludes.

**8. Realistic return decomposition.**
Total long-run stock return = real earnings growth (1.5-2%) + inflation (2-3%) + dividend yield (1.9%) + speculative sentiment. Baseline: ~6% nominal, ~4% real. Anything pitched above this for sustained periods requires extraordinary justification.

### SNIPED Application

- **Pricing floor as margin of safety.** The $1,500 floor is not stubbornness; it is a margin of safety against scope creep, client mismatch, and undervaluation. Never negotiate the floor; scope flexes, price does not.
- **Counter-cyclical positioning.** When the AI photography market is at peak enthusiasm, SNIPED's analog-grounded, lineage-documented practice holds intrinsic value. Build during the hype; the correction will come and SNIPED will still be standing.
- **Photography portfolio as tangible asset.** The archive, the methodology, the relationships, and the composite environments are the "book value." Brand story without these is growth-stock speculation: you are paying for the narrative, not the asset.
- **Temperament as competitive advantage.** SNIPED's operating tightness (casting doctrine, edit register, environmental rotation) is the discipline layer that compounds. Founders without this discipline chase each trend. The system beats individual brilliance.
- **Industry Selection Fallacy applied to AI photography.** The AI photography market will grow. That does not mean AI photographers will prosper. The moat is craft authority and human trust, not category momentum.
- **Reject speculative behavior in business decisions.** Content output is not a speculative bet on virality. It is a steady dividend-paying operation. Reps compound; single-viral-moments do not.

### Updates/Contradicts Existing Doctrine

**UPDATES:** Strengthens "Payment Follows Proof" by providing the theoretical backbone: do not overprice proof. Seed valuation precedent locks in forever. First terms cascade. Start clean, compound favorably.

**CONTRADICTS:** None. Graham's framework is a meta-discipline layer that sits beneath all existing SNIPED tactical doctrine. No conflict found.

## BOOK 2: The Creative Act: A Way of Being (Rick Rubin, 2023, Penguin)

**Coverage line:** 8/8 segments read; full text covering Source/Vessel/Filter cosmology through editing, why artists make, harmony, and the paradox of self-expression.

**1-line thesis:** Creativity is not a talent but a practice of perception: the artist who disciplines attention, separates phases of work, operates from internal standards rather than external validation, and ruthlessly edits to essence will generate work that reverberates.

### 4-8 Most Useful Frameworks (Dense)

**1. Three Creative Phases: Seeds, Experimentation, Crafting.**
Each phase has different logic and must not be mixed. Seeds: collect without judgment, receive transmission, do not compare, do not prune. Experimentation: follow excitement not intellect, test everything including opposites, allow accidental discovery. Crafting: directed labor, removal as much as addition, deadlines set only when vision is clear. Mixing phases kills potential. Premature pruning is the most common creative failure.

**2. Source, Vessel, Filter.**
Source is external and boundless. The vessel collects lived experience. The filter is the artist's sensory apparatus, memory, and beliefs, which reduce raw data. Training expands the filter's bandwidth. The goal is not to generate ideas but to receive them more clearly by lowering noise.

**3. Temporary Rules as Palette (Constraint Breeds Originality).**
Perec wrote a novel without the letter "e." Klein painted only in monochromatic blue. Dogme 95 imposed a 10-rule manifesto. Constraints force novelty within structure. "A rule is a way of structuring awareness." The most obvious solutions become unavailable; what remains is character. Limits are not restrictions; they are the creative engine.

**4. Ruthless Edit Protocol (Essence Extraction).**
Saint-Exupery: "Perfection is finally obtained not when there is no longer anything to add, but when there's no longer anything to take away." Cut to irreducible core. Ask: if I remove this, is the work still the work? Strip to least decorative form. Taste is demonstrated through what is excluded, not what is included.

**5. The Ecstatic as Compass.**
Not cerebral but visceral: a sudden shift, a small tweak, a revelation that takes your breath. Three types: relaxed excitement, astonishment, gentle dream-state transport. When the needle jumps, that seed or direction is worthy. When work is silent for long while after initial excitement, it may be a false start. The body knows before the mind articulates.

**6. Apocrypha and the Myth of Process.**
Artist origin stories are fiction. Methods are exaggerated. The same ritual will not replicate the same results. "We are unreliable narrators of our own experience." Do not cargo-cult a system. Documents operational decisions; proof tests them. Process is evidence, not law.

**7. Cooperation and Choice C.**
Multiple creative visions generate a third option neither party imagined alone. Not compromise (splitting A and B) but synthesis (discovering C). Continue until all parties genuinely prefer the outcome. Specific feedback on the work, not on the person. Recipient solves; advisor diagnoses.

**8. Success as Private Satisfaction Pre-Release.**
Not popularity, critics, or revenue. Success: the private moment when you release work after maximizing its potential, before any opinion enters. Variables beyond that: algorithm, timing, luck, market sentiment. Not controllable. Only controllables: make the best work, share it, start next, do not look back.

### SNIPED Application

- **Composite Environment Rotation is Temporary Rules in action.** Seven locked environments per chapter is a Rubin-style palette constraint. The environments force originality by removing the most obvious options. This is not limitation; it is generative structure.
- **Repetition over Novelty validated.** Rubin's Crafting phase = locked architecture, executing reps. Cathedral built, now run the office. Experimentation within the locked frame (vary subject, vary brief, vary direction cue) is still valid. Experimentation of the architecture is not.
- **Demo-itis is the retoucher trap.** Living too long with unfinished composites embeds them as "final." Step away. Return with clean eyes. Discard the note list. Listen as first-time viewer. Failed cleanup artifacts are worse than honest source.
- **B&W Card / HERO Color split is the Context principle.** Same image, different container, different meaning. This is Rubin's core method. The register is not a messaging choice; it is a filter that emerges through the constraint of medium.
- **Ecstatic as quality gate.** If a regen, edit, or composite does not activate the charge, it does not ship. Not metrics, not automation, not client pressure. Body signal governs.
- **Prism Self protects the Possibility Engine.** Rubin: accept prismlike nature; each refraction is true. SNIPED stays operator, not a single identity. Each lane (photographer, director, educator, institutional builder) is a valid color. None is the throne.
- **Gatekeeper role is non-negotiable.** Human eye in the final curatorial pass is irreplaceable. AI tools, presets, and vaults are pre-gatekeeper. The gatekeeper amplifies vital, removes excess, decides context.
- **Sincerity as by-product.** Do not shoot for "authentic founder story" directly. It emerges through non-head-on approaches: cultural doc, lineage work, institutional depth. Audience accesses truth through photographic ambiguity.

### Updates/Contradicts Existing Doctrine

**UPDATES:** Adds precision to "Strongest Photograph != Most Processed" by providing theoretical grounding (demo-itis, ecstatic compass, essence extraction). Also clarifies that Repetition over Novelty applies to architecture, not experimentation within the architecture. Experimental flexibility inside locked environments is healthy; architectural revision is not.

**CONTRADICTS:** Minor tension with "Maximum-by-Default" rule. Rubin warns against treating each piece as the life's defining work, which leads to endless revision. Maximum depth on shipping does not mean maximum time spent. Resolve: maximum creative depth applies to what ships, not how long it takes to complete. Speed in crafting, full in delivery.

## BOOK 3: Venture Deals (Brad Feld and Jason Mendelson, 2011, Wiley)

**Coverage line:** 11/11 segments read; full text covering VC fundraising mechanics, term sheet anatomy, negotiation tactics, M&A deal structure, tax/securities compliance, and sample LOI.

**1-line thesis:** Venture financing is a two-axis negotiation (Economics and Control) played as a long-game where relationship quality compounds across rounds and where founders who understand the full playbook in advance hold the only real leverage.

### 4-8 Most Useful Frameworks (Dense)

**1. The Two-Axis Filter: Economics and Control.**
Every term sheet provision maps to either economic return (liquidation preference, antidilution, option pool, price) or decision control (board composition, protective provisions, drag-along, voting rights). Everything else is theater. If a VC fights hard over something that touches neither axis, that is information about their negotiating quality, not the substance of the deal.

**2. Liquidation Preference Architecture.**
1x non-participating preferred in early rounds prevents cascading unfavorable structure. Participating preferred with a 2x or 3x multiple compresses founder upside in any outcome below the threshold. Stacked preferences across Series A, B, C create governance gridlock and founder demotivation. Rule: keep it lightweight early. Heavy structure compounds adversely.

**3. Multiplay vs. Single-Play Game Theory.**
VC financing is multiplay: reputation, fear of retaliation, and relationship quality are real constraints. Transparency and cooperation win in multiplay. "Just win, baby" wins in single-play (stranger acquisitions). Misreading which game you are in is the most common negotiation error. VCs who treat financing as single-play are flagging their own dysfunction.

**4. BATNA and Walk-Away Credibility.**
Know your walk-away point before you start. Make it deliberate, not emotional. If you threaten to walk and do not, your leverage is gone permanently. Never bluff. The walk-away must be credible, executed sincerely, and without anger. Post-walk-away re-engagement often produces better terms.

**5. Competitive Leverage via Simultaneous Term Sheets.**
Multiple interested VCs is the only reliable price-discovery mechanism. Drive each to term sheet in the same timeframe. Be transparent about optionality (you have other interest) without disclosing names or showing actual term sheets. Anchoring: state a few reasonable positions clearly, stick to them, concede on low-priority items.

**6. Valuation Precedent Lock.**
The first terms cascade forever. Seed valuation establishes the floor that Series A must exceed. If seed is overpriced relative to performance, Series A creates cap table friction. Price to "back with performance," not imagination. The more conservative the seed terms, the more flexibility exists in later rounds.

**7. Time-Gated Tax Traps (83(b) and 409A).**
83(b) election: 30-day window from stock grant, two-minute form, capital gains protection. Missing it triples the tax owed on vesting gains. 409A: post-2000 IRS requirement for professional valuation of common stock options; failure triggers excise taxes. Both are "if you do not do it right at the beginning, you cannot fix it later" problems. The professional valuation industry (409A) exists because of regulatory capture; the cost is not the fee but the 20% compression of employee equity returns.

**8. The LOI's Binding vs. Non-Binding Split.**
Part One (price, structure, employment) is non-binding. Part Two (exclusivity, access rights, confidentiality, breakup fee, termination, costs) is binding. The non-binding section signals intent; the binding section locks the negotiation timeline. Breakup fees trigger only if the seller walks and closes an alternative deal within six months. The no-shop window (45-60 days) should be bilateral and finite; open-ended no-shops are unacceptable.

### SNIPED Application

- **Pricing discipline as term sheet discipline.** The $1,500 floor holds the same logic as a walk-away BATNA. State it, mean it, execute it once, and it gains permanent credibility. Negotiate scope, not price.
- **The Two-Axis filter applies to every partnership.** When evaluating any deal (talent agreement, brand partnership, referral arrangement, platform deal), filter first through: does this affect economics? Does this affect control? Everything else is noise.
- **Avoid overpriced early proof.** If SNIPED builds brand equity at an implied valuation built on positioning mythology rather than demonstrated proof, any institutional conversation will find the gap. Price to performance, not aspiration. Proof first, valuation follows.
- **Multiplay behavior in all founder relationships.** LinkedIn founder network, VIB outreach, Pearl referral pipeline: all multiplay games. Transparency, consistency, and relationship compounding are the right strategy. Aggressive single-play behavior permanently damages the field.
- **IC agreements for retouchers and crew.** Fixed-fee, project-scoped, defined-deliverable IC agreements. Transfer IP explicitly. No hourly tracking. No non-solicitation clauses (they tilt toward employment misclassification in California). Separate NDA for sensitive methodology.
- **No-shop timing in client engagements.** Any exclusive engagement with a brand or institutional client should be time-bounded. SNIPED retains optionality to pursue competing opportunities after the window closes.
- **83(b) and 409A are immediate action items** once equity is issued to any team member. No delay. 30-day window is absolute.
- **Protective provisions apply to SNIPED governance.** Even without VC, codify who decides what. BJ unilateral authority on creative direction and pricing. Advisory input on strategic pivots. Board-level (even informal) consultation on entity structure and capital deployment. Prevents mid-crisis ambiguity.

### Updates/Contradicts Existing Doctrine

**UPDATES:** The "Payment Follows Proof" doctrine gains a formal framework. Seed valuation precedent lock makes it imperative not to overclaim valuation before proof exists. Also adds IC classification discipline (fixed-fee, project-scoped, no non-solicit) as operational infrastructure for crew hiring.

**CONTRADICTS:** None. Feld/Mendelson validates SNIPED's lean-by-design and capital-light approach. The book's VC machinery is future reference, not immediate constraint.

## BOOK 4: series_1_intake.md (32/32 segments)

**Coverage line:** 32/32 segments read; full text spanning invoice payment terms, model releases, photography contracts, copyright/licensing doctrine, independent contractor classification, bookkeeping fundamentals, tax planning, business insurance, and AI copyright law.

**1-line thesis:** The operational infrastructure of a professional creative business (contracts, payment terms, licensing, releases, bookkeeping, tax, insurance) is not administrative overhead but a compounding trust asset that separates premium operators from amateurs and directly determines whether proof captures value or surrenders it.

### 4-8 Most Useful Frameworks (Dense)

**1. Four-Gate Model Release Decision Tree.**
(a) Location: public space vs. private property. (b) Recognizability: visible face, unique distinguishing marks (tattoos, piercings). (c) Sale intent: who is selling and for what purpose. (d) Usage type: editorial (narrative connection to story, no release required) vs. commercial (selling product/service/idea, release required). Silhouettes and non-identifiable figures are always clear. Gray zone: get the release. Verbal consent is unenforceable.

**2. Copyright Ownership Default + Licensing Architecture.**
Photographer retains copyright by default unless explicitly transferred in writing. Client licenses use, not ownership. Licensing is not double-charging; it separates labor (creative fee) from reproduction rights (license fee). License variables: use-case, term, territory, exclusivity. The pricing is multiplicative: brand size x use-scope x temporal reach x geographic reach x exclusivity. Secondary licensing on a single shoot can exceed the original commission.

**3. Contract as Expectation Architecture (Who/What/When/Where).**
Four interrogatives map to all contract risk. Scope of work (shot count vs. time-based, included revisions, delivery format, delivery timeline). Payment (deposit structure, balance timing, late fees, cancellation windows). IP/Copyright (ownership retention, usage boundaries, portfolio rights, sublicensing restrictions). Creative authority (artistic release clause, editing discretion, no minimum count guarantee). Artistic discretion clause: photographer maintains creative authority; dissatisfaction with aesthetic is NOT grounds for refund or cancellation.

**4. Language Precision Governs Obligation.**
"Shall" = covenant (obligation). "May" = discretionary authority (no obligation). "Will" = futurity or declaration (not obligation unless specific context). Conditions (triggered by "if," "provided that," "unless") are never breached, only satisfied or failed. Failure creates no damages unless a separate covenant covers it. Vague language is the source of all disputes; specificity eliminates them before they arise.

**5. Payment Term Risk Ladder.**
CIA/CWO/CBS (upfront) > COD/CND > Net 7-15 > Net 30 > Net 60-90. Professional creative services standard: 50% deposit at signature, balance at delivery or shoot day. Early-pay discount (2/10 Net 30 model) = 36.7% annualized ROI; offer it when cash velocity is a priority. Never extend Net 60+ unless project revenue justifies the float. Quote validity: 2-week maximum to prevent stale pricing claims.

**6. IC Classification Defense.**
Fixed-fee, defined-deliverable, project-scoped agreements signal independent contractor status. Hourly pay, ongoing availability requirements, non-solicitation clauses, and restrictions on outside work all tilt toward employment classification. California and New York are the most hostile jurisdictions. Remove Section 11 (other business activities) and Section 12 (non-solicitation) from IC agreements to offset the risk created by confidentiality clauses. IP transfer must be explicit in the agreement.

**7. Bookkeeping as Strategic Visibility Tool.**
Two key metrics: Runway (cash on hand divided by monthly burn = months until insolvency) and Net Profit Margin (profit per revenue dollar; benchmark against industry). P&L reveals which revenue lanes are profitable and which are subsidized. Cash basis accounting is incorrect for retainer models; accrual accounting is required to match revenue to the period earned rather than the period received. Bank reconciliation monthly catches duplicate invoicing, failed processing, and vendor billing errors.

**8. AI Copyright Doctrine (Current State, 2025-2026).**
Thaler v. Perlmutter (D.C. Circuit March 2025): human authorship requirement is constitutional and statutory; pure prompt-based AI outputs cannot be copyrighted. Bartz v. Anthropic (June 2025): training on pirated books qualifies as fair use under transformative-purpose doctrine. Substantial similarity test applies to AI outputs as to traditional works. "In the style of" prompts do not necessarily infringe (style is not copyrightable). Voice simulations may violate state right-of-publicity laws. Composite works (human subject + AI environment): copyrightability depends on demonstrated human creative control over "expressive elements." This is unsettled but favorable to SNIPED's hybrid model.

### SNIPED Application

- **Casting Call Doctrine requires model release integration.** Pre-shoot: brief talent on release requirement. Post-shoot: sign physical carbon-copy release (not PDF; easier to forge). Fireproof file storage. California compliance mandatory. Guardian on-set for any minor talent (KOTS school context especially). Easy Release app for field-fast execution.
- **Licensing as the revenue layer the OS currently lacks.** SNIPED images license to: competing brands in non-conflicting categories, editorial publications, architecture and interior design lookbooks, tourism boards, wellness brands. The 7 composite environments map to specific licensing markets (Monochromatic Void to wellness/meditation; Brutalist Monument to architecture and luxury goods; Cinematic Urban to fashion and lifestyle). Secondary licensing can generate tens of thousands beyond the original shoot commission.
- **Artistic release clause is non-negotiable.** Every SNIPED client contract must include: photographer controls editing style, selection, and creative approach. Dissatisfaction with aesthetic is not a grounds for refund. This immunizes the Direction Stack visual authority from "make it pop" requests.
- **Invoice as trust artifact.** The invoice is a positioning touchpoint. Branded, precise, line-itemized (session duration, image count, revision rounds included, usage rights purchased). Explicit calendar due date, not "Net 30." Late fee policy stated upfront. This signals professionalism before the work is delivered.
- **Accrual accounting is required.** SNIPED sells retainers (upfront payment for deferred delivery). Cash-basis bookkeeping creates false P&L: shows $30k revenue in month 1, $0 in months 2-3. Accrual matches revenue to period earned. Without this, runway calculations are unreliable and margin analysis is corrupted.
- **AI copyright positioning is validated.** Hybrid model (human subject + AI environment) is the strongest available position. Human photographer controls expressive elements; AI handles secondary world-construction. This aligns with Thaler, and with Copyright Office guidance, and with anti-identity-AI doctrine already locked in the OS. Document the creative decision-making process for each composite to establish authorship record.
- **Tax planning is leverage, not compliance.** The segment's ROI example (tax planning saves $10-50k/year; every dollar saved at 40-50% marginal rate is worth 40-50 cents immediately) makes this a high-priority investment once revenue is proven. Year-end Section 179 purchases (camera bodies, lenses, production equipment) reduce liability while building the tool inventory. S-Corp election threshold: $60k net profit.

### Updates/Contradicts Existing Doctrine

**UPDATES:** Adds the licensing revenue layer explicitly. The OS has IP, composite environments, and visual assets but no systematic plan to monetize them beyond the original commission. This shelf establishes the framework. Also adds IC agreement doctrine as operational infrastructure for any crew hiring.

**CONTRADICTS:** Minor conflict with current informal operations. The "fastest legit link/invoice now" doctrine (Payment Follows Proof) is correct directionally but the invoice artifact itself needs upgrading. A generic QuickBooks output at the current posture contradicts the "hardest to say no to" doctrine. The invoice must become a trust artifact.

## SHELF SYNTHESIS: RE-READ SHELF 3

### What This Shelf Is

Four sources that together constitute a single operational doctrine: the discipline required to capture the value that creative proof generates. Graham provides the valuation and behavioral framework. Rubin provides the creative phase architecture and quality gate logic. Feld/Mendelson provides the institutional negotiation playbook. The series_1_intake.md provides the ground-level operational infrastructure (contracts, releases, licensing, bookkeeping, tax, insurance, IP law).

Alone, any one of these is useful. Together, they describe the full stack from creative quality through institutional credibility through contractual capture through financial visibility.

### Five Convergent Principles Across All Four Books

**1. The margin of safety principle runs through everything.**
Graham: price paid must be below intrinsic value. Rubin: essence is what remains after removal; anything extra is dilution. Feld: keep early-round terms lightweight or they cascade adversely. series_1_intake: deposit upfront protects against flakiness; artistic release clause protects creative authority. Every domain applies the same principle: leave room, do not overcommit, build in the buffer.

**2. Temperament and discipline compound; intelligence and credentials do not.**
Graham's most important finding: ordinary people with self-control outperform credentialed experts without it. Rubin: the album that is finished and released beats the masterpiece that is never done. Feld: the founder who prepared their BATNA in advance outperforms the one who improvises under pressure. series_1_intake: consistent enforcement of payment terms beats selective enforcement that destroys credibility. Discipline is the compounding engine across all four books.

**3. Proof before narrative; evidence before extrapolation.**
Graham: never extrapolate past returns into perpetuity; survivorship bias corrupts all historical records. Rubin: "old stories are false walls"; the work that exists beats the work that is planned. Feld: price to performance not aspiration; overpriced seed creates downstream friction. series_1_intake: the invoice as trust artifact only works if the work behind it is real. The OS rule "old work informs, current proof decides" has full four-source validation here.

**4. Clarity of terms is the foundation of trust, not a constraint on relationship.**
Graham: specificity over vague reasonableness in protective provisions. Rubin: specific feedback on the work not on the person enables collaboration. Feld: define who can speak for the syndicate; state employment terms before signing the term sheet. series_1_intake: contract as expectation architecture, invoice as clarity artifact, payment terms stated upfront prevent disputes. Every source validates that ambiguity is the enemy of trust, not a sign of flexibility.

**5. Internal standards beat external validation.**
Graham: the investor's enemy is himself; discipline against the crowd, not with it. Rubin: audience of one; devotional standard; success is private satisfaction pre-release. Feld: you never make money on terms; core terms only; do not chase prestige VCs whose incentives misalign. series_1_intake: artistic release clause reserves creative authority; copyright is retained by default. The operator who holds internal standards above market pressure compounds. The operator who chases external validation surrenders the franchise.

### Gaps and Tensions This Shelf Surfaces

**Gap 1: Licensing revenue is underdeveloped in the OS.**
The composite environments and portfolio work have multi-use licensing potential that is not currently structured or priced. This is a recoverable gap but it requires deliberate contract language and a pricing architecture before the first major institutional shoot.

**Gap 2: Invoice as trust artifact is currently misaligned.**
"Fastest legit link" is directionally correct but the artifact itself needs to be as premium as the work it invoices for. Line-itemized, branded, explicit dates, late-fee policy stated, early-pay incentive included. The invoice signals operating quality before the work arrives.

**Gap 3: Accrual vs. cash accounting.**
If SNIPED accepts retainers (upfront payment for deferred delivery), cash-basis accounting produces unreliable margin and runway figures. This corrupts the strategic visibility that bookkeeping is supposed to provide. Flag for resolution once retainer structure is in place.

**Gap 4: Model release integration into the Casting Call Doctrine.**
The doctrine specifies confirmation timing, standby tiers, wardrobe requirements, and the two-strike rule but does not specify release protocol (physical vs. digital, timing, storage, California compliance). This is a legal infrastructure gap that should be resolved before the next shoot.

No lane crowned. Six lanes remain open until proof decides. This shelf strengthens the operational infrastructure layer across all of them. The doctrine is denser; the gaps are named; the synthesis is available for routing.
