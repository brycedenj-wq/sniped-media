### BOOK 1: Alan Weiss - Value-Based Fees (2008, Pfeiffer)

**Coverage:** 13/13 segs ok

**Thesis:** Fees are a function of perceived value, not time or deliverables. The consultant's job is to manage value UP, not fees down. Brand, shared outcomes, and conceptual agreement are the architecture of premium pricing.

**4-8 Densest Frameworks / Lessons:**

1. **Three-Column Value Model.** Column 1 (credentials/past), Column 2 (interventions/methods), Column 3 (client outcomes). Most consultants market Columns 1-2 and ignore Column 3. Fees must be anchored in Column 3: what changed for the buyer.

2. **Conceptual Agreement as Linchpin.** Three inputs required before any proposal: (a) business objectives as outcomes, (b) measures of progress, (c) buyer's stipulated organizational value. Without conceptual agreement, proposal acceptance rate is under 5%. With it, 80%+. Proposals confirm, never explore.

3. **"Choice of Yeses" (Options Architecture).** Shift the buyer from binary yes/no to "how do I use you?" Present 3-5 tiers of distinct value (not price bands, distinct outcomes). Buyer self-selects up. Buyers confronted with three escalating value options choose Option 2 or 3 approximately 60% of the time. Fees migrate upward without negotiation.

4. **Mercedes-Benz Syndrome.** Higher price signals higher quality, higher ego satisfaction, higher perceived result. Premium buyers don't boast about cheap consultants. The fear that high fees will repel buyers is almost always wrong among qualified ICP. Lower fees signal doubt in your own value.

5. **Retainer = Access, Not Labor.** Retainers compensate for access to the consultant's thinking, not for time or deliverables. If the client barely calls, no guilt and no obligation to extend or reduce fee. Define boundaries (who has access, what channels, what response SLA, finite time frame). Separate retainer (access) from project (outcome) in every proposal.

6. **Four Resistance Points (Ladder of Objections).** Trust, Need, Urgency, Money. Money is the loudest stated objection and almost always the shallowest root. When trust, need, and urgency are present, money is reallocated. Diagnose actual resistance; don't respond to the stated objection.

7. **Fee Progression Table (5-Phase Arc).** Entry Level > Going Concern > Word of Mouth (Deadly Trap) > Brand > Ultimate Consultant. Most operators plateau at Word of Mouth, achieve "fat and happy" complacency, and stop building. Advancement requires: IP-branded methodology, selective intake, maximized margins, passive/non-consulting revenue, reduction of labor intensity.

8. **"Drop 15% Every 2 Years" Discipline.** Cast as serving the client: "I can't give you the priority you deserve at your current fee level; here's someone better." Frees capacity for higher-fee work. Companion to abandonment audit: 8 questions (learning? unique value? paid/profit as well as new clients? growing? innovating? referral springboard? experimenting? having fun?). Two or more "no" = plan exit.

**SNIPED Application:**

- Column 3 is SNIPED's moat. Photography = Column 2 (intervention). Column 3 = founder's repositioning, inbound authority lift, VIB network access, personal brand vault. Every SNIPED engagement must articulate the Column 3 outcome explicitly before discussing scope or price.
- "Choice of Yeses" restructures SNIPED pricing from binary ("do you want the shoot?") to ladder (Portrait + 50 images | + Cultural Doc participation | + Brand System + quarterly refresh). Fees migrate without standoff.
- Conceptual Agreement Protocol: no proposal until the economic buyer (founder, not CMO) has stipulated objectives, measures, and organizational value. VIB outreach routes to founder directly.
- Retainer model: SNIPED advisory retainer = access to BJ's visual strategy and positioning thinking, not unlimited photography delivery. Retainer scope defined up front: who has access, what channels, 90-day minimum, quarterly renewal.
- Word-of-Mouth Trap Warning: SNIPED is at or near this phase. The advancement moves (IP brand around methodology, margin maximization, non-consulting passive income, labor intensity reduction) map directly to Direction Stack book, Cultural Doc cadence, composite environments as product, and remote coaching tiers.
- Passive income target: Weiss's model (75% passive at peak) shows the runway for SNIPED's Direction Stack / Brand System / Card system as recurring revenue without proportional labor growth.

### BOOK 2: Eric Berne - Games People Play (1996, Ballantine Books)

**Coverage:** 8/8 segs ok

**Thesis:** Human social interaction is structured by recurring, unconscious transactional patterns ("games") that operate on two levels simultaneously: a social (Adult) level and a psychological (Child/Parent) level. The hidden level determines the actual payoff. Game-free living requires awareness, spontaneity, and intimacy.

**4-8 Densest Frameworks / Lessons:**

1. **Ego State Triad (Parent / Adult / Child).** Parent = internalized authority, automatic responses. Adult = data-processing, objective reality appraisal. Child = archaic, emotional, creative, intuitive. All three have survival value. Imbalance creates dysfunction. Premium positioning requires Adult-to-Adult transactions. Games exploit misaligned ego states.

2. **Transaction Types.** Complementary (vectors aligned, communication flows). Crossed (vectors cross, communication breaks down, topic suspended until realigned). Ulterior (two ego states active simultaneously: social level masks psychological level). Games are sequences of ulterior transactions progressing to a predictable payoff.

3. **Stroke Economy.** Social contact = exchange of "strokes" (units of recognition). Absence of expected strokes = social offense. Excess = suspicion. Stroke calculation is intuitive and constant. Every community-building touchpoint is a stroke transaction. Deficit creates attrition; correct calibration builds loyalty.

4. **Game Anatomy (9-Part Structure).** Title. Thesis (stated position). Aim (hidden payoff). Roles. Dynamics (underlying drive). Social Paradigm (stated transaction). Psychological Paradigm (actual transaction). Moves. Advantages (internal psychological / external psychological / internal social / external social / biological / existential).

5. **Antithesis Principle.** Games break when the complementary player withdraws from their expected role. The game-breaking move forces the player's true motivation into the open, often triggering frustration or rage. This is diagnostic: a strong negative reaction to the antithesis confirms the game was real and serious.

6. **"Why Don't You, Yes But" (YDYB).** The advisor-helpless pair. White presents problem. Others offer solutions. White rejects each. Silence = White wins. Payoff is NOT solutions but proof that others are inadequate. This game runs in every advisory relationship. SNIPED antithesis: when prospect rejects every offer, stop offering. Ask: "That's tough. What will you do about it?" Shift responsibility back.

7. **Autonomy as Game-Free Living.** Three capacities: (a) Awareness (eidetic perception, seeing without socialized framing, living now not future/past). (b) Spontaneity (freedom to choose from full Parent-Adult-Child repertoire, not just programmed responses). (c) Intimacy (game-free candor with an aware person, evokes reciprocal affection, distinct from seduction). These three are the architecture of relationships that create genuine trust.

8. **Game Transmission Across Generations.** Games pass down 5+ generations through child-rearing. Intervention breaks the chain with exponentially growing effect. Cultural lineages carry game repertoires. Documenting a lineage from inside (Lineage Doctrine) IS documenting its strategy, mythology, and game structures.

**SNIPED Application:**

- VIB outreach diagnostic: when a DM gets read but not replied, apply the crossed-transaction framework. Did the message trigger a threatened position (Child reacts to Parent authority)? Did it mistake Adult request for Parental judgment? Frame VIB outreach at Adult-Adult level: peer curiosity, no rescue.
- Stroke economy operationalizes LinkedIn Comment Doctrine precisely: 5-10 comments per day on Tier 0 founders is a calibrated stroke investment. Gap of 3+ weeks requires a higher-signal reconnect (not a perfunctory "Hi").
- YDYB is the #1 game in premium sales. When founder rejects every proposal angle, STOP proposing. "What are you going to do?" is the antithesis. Identifies game players before engagement begins.
- "Harried" client detection (overcommit, collapse, blame contractor) and "Look How Hard I've Tried" (client uses your service as martyrdom proof, not investment) are early-exit signals. Build detection into intake interviews.
- Casting call doctrine is anti-Schlemiel infrastructure already: 24-hr confirm, tier-2 standby, wardrobe photos. Explicit rules prevent the "spill then demand forgiveness" loop.
- Autonomy as brand defensibility: SNIPED's anti-AI moat is NOT "better algorithm" but "we document the autonomous human." Person who perceives directly, unmediated by script. Eidetic awareness is the photographic quality that cannot be algorithmically generated.
- "Cavalier" Good Game (artisan offering virtuosity for appreciation, not domination) is SNIPED's ethical frame. Authority through mastery, not coercion. Self-orientation removed. Stops before exploitation.
- "They'll Be Glad They Knew Me" (constructive variant of "I'll Show Them," building allies' respect through honorable means) = SNIPED's long game in LA Black founder scene. Not revenge, not performance. Evidence accumulated over time.

- REINFORCES intel_status_psychology.md: hidden-motive signaling in premium buyers = Child-level payoff (status, belonging, exclusivity) wrapped in Adult-level rationale (portfolio, ROI, narrative).
- REINFORCES feedback_casting_call_doctrine.md: pre-staging = antithesis to production games.

### BOOK 3: The Offer Stack (docx)

**Coverage:** 2/2 segs ok

**Thesis:** Offers are architecture, not creative expression. Validate before building, ladder buyers from free to premium, use brand psychology (exclusivity, identity bridge, storytelling) to justify 3-10x pricing, and multiply proven winners rather than inventing new ones.

**4-8 Densest Frameworks / Lessons:**

1. **Validation Before Production (3-5 Days).** Four methods: (a) Amazon 1-star review mining for complaint language. (b) Medium traction test (3 articles, 200+ claps signal viability). (c) Reddit/community thread counting (weak top answers = room to win). (d) Competitor testimonial + gap mining. Strong signal = build. Mixed = refine angle. Weak = kill and return to three questions.

2. **Offer Ladder (6 Rungs).** Free lead magnet > Low-ticket ($7-27) > Mid-ticket ($27-97) > Video course ($97-497) > Coaching ($500-5k+) > Done-for-you ($1k-20k+). Each rung qualifies buyer for the next. LTV of full-ladder customer = 100-500x the lowest rung. Start one rung above highest proven offer, never three.

3. **Third Layer Niche Discipline.** Not "Fitness" but "knee pain for marathoners over 50." Not "Photography" but "personal brand photography for Black LA tech founders at Series A." Third-layer specificity: high conversion, low competition, buyer feels spoken to. Specificity is the moat, not breadth.

4. **THCPP Launch Framework.** Tease (3-4 wks, BTS, no reveal) > Hype (2-3 wks, world-building, lifestyle, influencers) > Collect (ongoing, landing page, 500+ signups = minimum viable launch, 2k+ = strong) > Push (launch day + 48 hrs, email+SMS simultaneous) > Pull (day 3-30, retarget non-buyers with best organic creative as paid). Never run paid during Tease/Hype. Organic validates before paid amplifies.

5. **Brand Vector (Non-Negotiable Anchor).** Single marketing decision that filters all future moves. "Never discount." "Never use stock photography." "Never mass retail." "Handwritten thank-you on every order." Prevents strategic drift and discount dilution. Compounds brand equity because it is unrepeatable by trend-followers.

6. **Identity Bridge.** Current identity (specific frustrations, buyer's language) -> aspirational identity (concrete details) -> gap -> bridge mechanism (specific and credible, not magic) -> proof (testimonials from buyers who crossed). Price is secondary when identity transformation is clear. Premium psychology requires: exclusivity (scarcity manufactures desire), identity (uniform of aspirational self), storytelling (world the buyer gets, not product features).

7. **Operator Tier Discipline.** Job changes at each revenue level. 0-10k/mo: PMF and manual acquisition only. 10-50k/mo: paid on proven organic, first key hire. 50-100k/mo: systems/SOPs, 2-5 team hires. 100k+/mo: leadership/delegation, brand equity moves. Doing higher-tier work too early locks expense. Staying in lower-tier work too long wastes leverage. Most operators fail by doing the wrong job for their current tier.

8. **Compound Improvement Loop.** Publish version. Review (improved/regressed/neutral). Identify ONE 10% improvement. Implement. Repeat. Most operators quit at rep 7. Winners run to rep 50-100. 100 reps of 10% improvement beats 1 rep of perfect design. Applies to offers, content, visual systems, client sequences.

**SNIPED Application:**

- SNIPED offer ladder: Free (IG content + LinkedIn POV) > Low-ticket (Card system / Direction Stack chapters, $27-97) > Mid-ticket (Brand System entry, $500-2k) > High-ticket (Composite Portrait Series + Cultural Doc, $5-25k) > Done-for-you retainer ($2-5k/mo). Each rung qualifies upward.
- Third-layer niche: not "founder photography" but "personal brand photography for Black LA tech founders at pivotal narrative moments (Series A, capital raise, board formation)." This is the sentence.
- THCPP applies directly to Direction Stack book launch and any Card system drop. 4-week Tease/Hype cycle before any public launch. Email/SMS list is the owned asset; IG followers are borrowed.
- Brand Vector for SNIPED: "Never shoot without Direction Stack alignment." "Never deliver without retouching standard." "Never replicate AI-generated identities." These are the non-negotiables that create the brand gap competitors cannot close.
- Identity Bridge in SNIPED copy: current (founder has a LinkedIn photo from 2021 and no visual narrative) -> aspirational (founder's visual presence signals insider scene membership, cultural authority, Series B gravitas). Bridge = SNIPED engagement. Proof = case studies from existing shoots.
- Validation discipline: before any new Direction Stack format, composite environment, or product tier, run validation (LinkedIn post traction test as Medium proxy, Twitter/Reddit community language mining). Not every idea ships to production.
- Compound Improvement Loop is the operational form of "Repetition > Novelty" (already locked). SNIPED's reps are: Card iterations, composite environment variations, Cultural Doc episodes, LinkedIn POV sequences. Track improvement per rep.
- Physical product (apparel, apparatus prints): 3% Rule applies. Take proven luxury tee silhouette, refine for SNIPED tribe (mark/colorway/paper weight/edge finish). Validation-first (waitlist 500+ before production). Tech pack required before any factory contact.
