## 1. 30-day objective

Get at least one critical-facilities staffing firm or specialty subcontractor to pay for (or sign a paid LOI for) a Pilot Capability Dossier and open one real room, proving the wedge moves a real outcome.

## 2. Success / fail thresholds (Red / Yellow / Green)

Activity targets (the inputs): approximately 45 targeted contacts (about 25 staffing, about 20 subs), about 15 real conversations, 6-10 booked discovery calls.

- **GREEN (continue to 60-day repeatability):** at least 1 paid Pilot or paid LOI **AND** at least 1 room opened (a non-secure shoot granted, an intro, or a named bid/role use commitment).
- **YELLOW (iterate one variable, re-test):** 0 paid, but at least 1 of: strong verbal pull, valued-but-unpaid, access granted without payment, or a warm "next quarter" tied to a named role/bid. Change exactly one thing (segment, offer framing, or price) and run again.
- **RED (kill infra-first, fall back to C4):** 0 paid **AND** 0 rooms after about 15 real conversations. Switch to the C4 founder/operator-presence fallback as the cash+proof engine and reconsider whether the infra beachhead responds to BJ's edge.

## 3. Week 1 (Days 1-7) · Build the sales surface

- **Day 1:** Confirm Google Workspace from-address (bj@). Decide payments = Pixieset Studio Manager (one tool). Build the Carrd one-pager using WEDGE_OFFER_SPEC section 8 (hero, problem, offer, who-it's-for, included, security-safe, proof placeholder, process, CTA, FAQ). Output: Carrd draft.
- **Day 2:** Create the Calendly "15-min capability call" event; embed the link in the Carrd CTA; publish the Carrd at a clean URL. Output: live page + booking link.
- **Day 3:** Pixieset Studio Manager: build the lead pipeline, the Pilot contract template, and the deposit/invoice template ($2,500-4,000). Output: contract + invoice ready.
- **Day 4:** Google Workspace: prep 3 emails (booking confirmation, post-call Pilot proposal, delivery). Set up the proof log block (section 12) in PROOF_LOOPS_30_60_90. Output: email drafts + log ready.
- **Day 5:** Build the spec/sample dossier (section 11): a clearly-labeled illustrative sample, security-safe, no real client. This is the asset the call shows. Output: 1 sample dossier.
- **Day 6:** Build the LinkedIn/target list (section 8): about 45 targets with name, firm, title, segment, and urgency signal noted. Output: target list.
- **Day 7:** Dry-run: read the Carrd aloud, click the Calendly flow, rehearse the call script once against the sample dossier; pre-load the first batch of DMs/emails. Buffer for anything unfinished. Output: ready-to-send queue.

## 4. Week 2 (Days 8-14) · First outreach sprint

Split staffing-first (about 60/40). Log every send.

- **Day 8:** 8 staffing DMs + 4 staffing emails. Log all.
- **Day 9:** 8 subcontractor DMs + 4 sub emails. Log.
- **Day 10:** 8 staffing DMs; reply to any inbound within 2 hrs; book calls. Log.
- **Day 11:** 8 sub DMs; follow-up #1 to Day-8 non-responders (48-hr rule). Log.
- **Day 12:** 5 staffing + 5 sub contacts; book/confirm calls; reply to all. Log.
- **Day 13:** Follow-ups to Day-9/10 non-responders; reply to replies; book calls. Log.
- **Day 14:** Review week-2 metrics (sent / replies / calls booked). Adjust the *target list*, not the offer. Log a week-2 summary entry.
- **Target by Day 14:** about 45 contacts attempted, about 6-10 replies, about 3-6 calls booked.

## 5. Week 3 (Days 15-21) · Calls + refinement

- **Days 15-19:** Run booked discovery calls (2-3/day as they land) with the section 10 script. Pitch the Pilot on every qualified call. Send the Pilot proposal within 24 hrs of each qualified call. Keep light outreach flowing (4-6 contacts/day) to refill the pipeline. After every call, log a proof entry + the objection heard.
- **Day 20:** Review objection patterns. Revise *one* Carrd line or message **only if at least 3 calls show the same failure point**. Otherwise change nothing.
- **Day 21:** Follow-ups on all outstanding proposals; book stragglers; log. Output target: at least 1 Pilot proposal live with a real buyer.

## 6. Week 4 (Days 22-30) · Close / kill / iterate

- **Days 22-26:** Push to close at least 1 paid Pilot or paid LOI. Send/resend proposals, handle objections (section 13), set start dates and collect the deposit via Studio Manager.
- **Day 27:** If no paid interest, isolate the failure point: no replies points to targeting/message; replies but no calls points to offer clarity; calls but no close points to price/proof/trust.
- **Day 28:** Tally the run: real conversations, calls, paid, rooms opened.
- **Day 29:** Apply the R/Y/G rule. Write the decision into PROOF_LOOPS_30_60_90; update H1/H2 status (UNTESTED to TESTING to KEEP/KILL/ITERATE).
- **Day 30:** Green points to scope the 60-day repeatability plan. Yellow points to define the single iteration and restart. Red points to switch to the C4 fallback. Log the call.

## 10. Discovery call script (runnable, 20 min)

**Opening (verbatim):** "Thanks for the time, I keep these short. I come from the operator side of critical facilities, and I build capability proof assets that move a hire or a bid. I want to understand your situation for ten minutes, then I'll tell you honestly if I can help. Sound good?"

**10 core questions:**
1. "What does your firm do, and where does most of your data-center / mission-critical work sit right now?"
2. "Is your bigger squeeze right now staffing the work, or winning the work?"
3. "When you lose a hire or a bid you should've won, what usually decided it?"
4. "How do clients or candidates form their first impression of you today?"
5. "What do you use now, careers page, capability statement, referrals, and who runs it?"
6. *(staffing)* "What's a hard role open right now, and what does that vacancy cost you per week?" *(subs)* "What's a bid coming up you really want, and what's it worth?"
7. "What can and can't be shown given your clients' security and NDAs?"
8. "Who else weighs in on a decision like this?"
9. "If one asset helped you fill that role or win that bid, what's that worth to you?"
10. "What would make this an easy yes, and what would make it a no?"

**Pricing transition:** "Based on what you said, the Pilot fits, that's a focused capability dossier built around [the specific role/bid], $2,500-4,000, 1-2 weeks, and you approve every image and every line before anything ships. Want to run one on [named role/bid]?"

**Close language:** "I can start this week. I'll send a one-page proposal and the deposit invoice today; once that's in, we book the shoot. Does [day] work to begin?"

**Handle "send info":** "Happy to, but info rarely lands without context. Give me five minutes to show one sample on the call and you'll know immediately if it's worth a follow-up. Can I share my screen?" (If still no: send the 60-second sample + a single proposal, set a follow-up date, log as Yellow.)

**Handle "we already have marketing":** "Good, this isn't marketing. Your marketing person makes you look fine. This is a capability proof asset built by someone who's stood on the floor and knows what real capability looks like, tied to a hire or a bid. They can use everything I deliver."

**Handle security concerns:** "That's the default assumption, not an afterthought. Nothing sensitive gets shown, no secure floors, no equipment layouts, no client names. We shoot your people and your work in non-secure settings you control, and you approve every asset before it exists anywhere. I design around your NDAs."

## 11. Spec / sample dossier plan

- **What it shows:** an illustrative capability dossier for a composite "example firm" (clearly fictional/representative), demonstrating the format, the proof narrative voice, the people/capability visual treatment, and the copy blocks.
- **What it must NOT claim:** no real client; no real testimonials; no "verified operator" or platform language; no facility-specific or security-sensitive imagery; no implication BJ has done this engagement before.
- **How to label:** watermark/header on every page: "SAMPLE · ILLUSTRATIVE FORMAT · NOT A REAL CLIENT."
- **Sections it should contain:** cover (firm name placeholder + "Capability Dossier"); one-paragraph proof narrative; capability bullets ("what we deliver"); crew/people visual treatment (BJ's own approved imagery or clearly staged/representative); a "why work here" candidate block; a "why award us" bid block; a one-screen capability profile layout.
- **Security-safe:** use BJ's own approved/staged imagery or neutral representative visuals, never a real facility; keep all content generic and non-identifying.

## 13. Objection bank

- **"We already have marketing":** "This isn't marketing, it's a capability proof asset tied to a hire or a bid. Your marketing person can use everything I deliver."
- **"This sounds like photography":** "It's not. Photography is one instrument. The deliverable is a capability dossier, the document a client or candidate actually believes."
- **"Security won't allow this":** "Assumed by default. Nothing sensitive, no secure floors, no client names. We work in non-secure settings you control and you approve every asset. I design around your NDAs."
- **"We win on relationships":** "Exactly, and this makes that relationship transferable to the procurement team or the candidate who hasn't met you yet."
- **"Send info":** "Happy to, but five minutes on a call with one sample tells you more than any PDF. Can I show you?" (Then sample + proposal + follow-up date.)
- **"No budget":** "Understood. Priced against one hire ($20-45K) or one bid (six figures), the Pilot is a rounding error, but if there's genuinely no discretionary spend this quarter, I'll follow up when a hard bid or req lands."
- **"Not right now":** "Fair. When's the next bid or the next hard role? That's the moment this earns its keep, I'll check back then." (Log named trigger, set follow-up.)

## 14. What NOT to do

- Do not build the registry or any platform.
- Do not build n8n / Twilio / AI-agent automations.
- Do not build a full brand site (the Carrd one-pager is enough).
- Do not rewrite the offer daily (change one thing only on at least 3 matching call signals).
- Do not create fake case studies or testimonials (the sample is clearly labeled illustrative).
- Do not use or show any confidential or facility-sensitive information.
- Do not use employer resources, time, or contacts that conflict with BJ's NDA/non-compete.
- Do not avoid outreach by polishing the page, the sample, or the copy. Sending beats perfecting.
