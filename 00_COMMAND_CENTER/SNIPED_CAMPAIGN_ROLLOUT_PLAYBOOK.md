## 0. Framing (read first)
SNIPED is visual authority engineering / status image-making, not a photography business; photography is the instrument. This campaign is not "content." It is **proof, distribution, the launch vehicle for the Reset, and the first cultural-documentation artifact** of the practice. Finish it and weaponize it; do not start a new one.

## 1. Purpose and scope
Finish the campaign already shot and turn it into marketing (inquiries + proof), not random posting. Scope: the rollout of the existing 6-model campaign and converting each drop into Reset inquiries. No new campaign is shot during this window.

## 2. Current campaign state
- A recent collab campaign with about 6 models (free/collab register, not paid Reset).
- Roughly 36 finals (about 6 per model), in the v3 LUXURY editorial register.
- Rollout partially done; one model's second drop is underway and was paused.
- Content already shot; the picks were chosen against rough comps (see section 5).

## 3. Campaign goal
Convert the existing inventory into: (a) visible, real proof of SNIPED's standard and method; (b) reach via the models' audiences; (c) Reset inquiries; and (d) the first artifact of the cultural-documentation body of work (the long ceiling). The campaign is the funnel and the proof; the Reset is the offer.

## 4. Content buckets
- **HERO posts:** color, the moment, the strongest single frames.
- **B&W Chapter Cards:** the document register (per the dual-register rule).
- **BTS / process:** light, only if it adds to the story; never the rough-comp artifacts.

## 5. Pick review rule
Before posting, review each pick against the RAW capture; swap any frame the rough comp carried but the underlying capture does not support. **Do not post the rough-comp artifacts as final proof.** Post only frames that stand on their own in the locked register.

## 6. Rollout order
1. Finish the in-progress model's second drop first (do not leave it half-done).
2. Then sequence the remaining models strongest-first (exposure mechanic: lead with the best to compound attention).
3. Steady cadence over the 14 days; do not dump everything at once.

## 7. Model tagging / collab strategy
Every post tags the model. Treat each model as a distribution node ("audience of my audience"): their repost puts the work in front of their circle, which is where warm inquiries come from. The collab goodwill is the engine; honor it.

## 8. Model DM / repost ask
DM each model: thank them, send their finals, ask them to post and tag SNIPED, and ask for 1-2 referrals. (Exact message in SNIPED_OUTREACH_MESSAGES.) The models are collab/free; the ask is reposts + referrals, not payment.

## 9. Caption strategy
Register = Direction Stack / lineage / quiet-luxury editorial, not hype. **Pull from the campaign captions/copy that already exist in prior sessions, with attribution; do not fabricate new "final" captions as canon** (the carousel-attribution rule). A caption may reference one Direction Stack protocol as a taste (never the full method). Captions carry the work, they do not sell hard on the grid.

## 10. Story strategy
Stories carry BTS, the human layer, and the soft offer CTA (the grid stays pure mythology per the platform-split doctrine). The Reset offer lives in stories, the bio link, and DMs, never as a grid sales post.

## 11. CTA strategy
One soft CTA, in stories and bio only: "a few Reset / presence sessions open this drop, DM 'reset'." No hard-sell captions on the HERO/Card posts. The work earns the DM; the DM carries the offer. The CTA supports the Reset without making the grid cheap.

## 12. How each drop converts into inquiries
Post (HERO/Card) to tag model to model reposts to their audience sees to story CTA / bio link to DM to Reset booking (see SNIPED_CASH_NOW_PLAYBOOK, $750 deposit on the $1,500 Reset). Each drop is a funnel-top event, not an end in itself.

## 13. What not to post
Rough-comp artifacts as work, off-register frames, anything that crosses into BASEPLATE's infra surface, anything sensitive about a person without consent, hard-sell sales graphics on the grid, the full Direction Stack method, or a flood that breaks the steady cadence.

## 14. What not to claim
No fake testimonials, no "booked out" or fake-scarcity claims, no invented results, no AI-generated images passed as real campaign work, no implied endorsements the models did not give. It is real work; show it straight and let the quality speak.

## 15. Campaign proof log
Log per drop: which model, posts published, model reposted y/n, engagement/saves/DMs, inquiries generated, bookings traced to it. Kept in the SNIPED proof log, separate from BASEPLATE's PROOF_LOOPS.

## 16. Stop / continue rules
Finish the existing campaign rollout within the window; do not start a new campaign before this one is weaponized. If the rollout drives inquiries, keep the cadence; if it is pure vanity with zero inquiries after the warm-network push, tighten the CTA and the DM ask rather than posting more. Review at day 14 with the cash playbook.

## 17. Guardrails
SNIPED is visual authority engineering, not photography; photography is the instrument; this campaign is proof/distribution/launch/cultural-documentation artifact, not just content. SNIPED separate from BASEPLATE; no reduction of BASEPLATE to photography; no cross-domain redirects; no fake proof, scarcity, or testimonials; no AI-generated evidence; the Direction Stack stays the hidden method (a taste at most, never the full method); no new campaign before this one is weaponized; no merge with BASEPLATE; no overbuild; carousel/social copy attributed to its prior-session source. Grid stays mythology; offer stays in stories/bio/DMs. Old material is historical evidence, not law. Bible held. Anchor-class: not chunked, not in master files, total_chunks unchanged at 1,837. This playbook posts nothing.
