## 1. Poor Charlie's Almanack (Charles T. Munger, 2005)

**Coverage line:** 26 of 29 segments OK; segments 23 and 27 missing due to corrupted/absent source text files; segment 17 heavily corrupted OCR.

**1-line thesis:** A life built on multidisciplinary mental models, radical honesty, and concentrated conviction in a narrow circle of competence compounds into durable wealth and wisdom that outlasts any single bet or business cycle.

### 4-8 Most Useful Frameworks (Dense)

**1. Multidisciplinary Mental Models Latticework**
Hold 80-100 big ideas from the most fundamental disciplines (math, physics, biology, psychology, economics, history). Never explain in a less-fundamental way when a more-fundamental way exists. Attribute explicitly. The single-discipline thinker who only has a hammer sees every problem as a nail. Fatal unconnectedness (Whitehead) is academia's core failure and most operators' core blind spot. This is the organizing engine of everything else Munger does.

**2. Lollapalooza Tendency**
When multiple psychological or competitive forces align in the same direction simultaneously, the result is not additive but exponential. Coca-Cola works not because of one advantage (flavor, distribution, branding, Pavlovian association) but because all six factors compound. Most failures are also lollapalooza events: Milgram's obedience experiment required six converging psychological tendencies, not one. Diagnosis requires the full checklist, not the nearest explanation.

**3. The 25 Psychological Tendencies as a Systematic Taxonomy**
Munger catalogs human misjudgment as a working system: Reward/Punishment Superresponse, Liking/Loving, Doubt-Avoidance, Inconsistency-Avoidance, Reciprocation, Pavlovian Association, Simple Pain-Avoiding Denial, Excessive Self-Regard, Overoptimism, Deprival-Superreaction, Social Proof, Contrast-Misreaction, Stress-Influence, Availability-Misweighing, Use-It-or-Lose-It, Senescence-Misinfluence, Authority-Misinfluence, Twaddle, Reason-Respecting, and the master tendency Lollapalooza. These are not personality flaws; they are evolutionary adaptations that misfire in modern contexts. The antidote to each is a designed structural response (checklists, habits, rules), not individual willpower.

**4. Circle of Competence + Selective Conviction**
Know exactly where your edge ends. Inside the circle, bet heavily and rarely (Berkshire's billions came from ten major insights, not thousands of trades). Ted Williams's 77-cell strike zone: only swing at pitches in the sweet spot. Patience is the prerequisite; decisiveness is the execution. The worst outcomes come from swinging at pitches outside your circle with full conviction.

**5. Inversion (Jacobi: "Invert, Always Invert")**
To solve hard forward problems, study the reverse: how do you guarantee misery? What produces failure? Munger's Harvard commencement speech built entirely on this. Darwin's method: prioritize disconfirming evidence over confirming evidence because human brains naturally do the opposite. Einstein's method: invert Newton to satisfy Maxwell. Backward reasoning reveals what forward reasoning obscures.

**6. Institutional Moral Hazard via Incentive Drift**
Systems corrupt via incentive misalignment, not villain behavior. Accounting firms, investment banks, and corporations drifted into moral sewers one small step at a time because each step presented low contrast from the prior position (the frog-in-heating-water failure mode). The Quant Tech parable shows how one accounting convention decision poisons the entire downstream over 20 years. Fix the system; don't just fire people.

**7. Reliability as the Master Virtue**
Unreliability single-handedly counterbalances all other virtues. The dyslexic roommate who excelled through extreme reliability reached CEO-level success while more talented but inconsistent peers stagnated. This is not moral posturing; it is a causal observation. Systems, partnerships, and careers built on word-honor outperform those built on contracts alone.

**8. Omission Cost Accounting**
Track the invisible losses. Berkshire "rubbed its own nose" in mistakes of omission (Walmart stock: $10B loss; See's near-miss). Most operators ignore omission costs because they don't appear on financial statements. Tracking them is a discipline that forces confrontation with the most psychologically comfortable form of failure: not doing anything wrong, just not doing the right thing.

### SNIPED Application

- The multidisciplinary OS corpus is not intellectual accumulation for its own sake; it is the lollapalooza engine. Each discipline (photography theory, trust mechanics, status psychology, distribution physics, pricing logic) is a separate force. When they converge on the same moment (a founder shoot, a Direction Stack chapter, a VIB outreach), the compound effect is exponential. This is why full-OS synthesis beats single-book recall.
- Circle of competence maps directly to LA Black founder photography scene. Do not exit this circle for volume or novelty. The 7-composite-environment rotation, the locked casting doctrine, the 5 lineages are all circle-defining constraints that permit concentrated conviction.
- Inversion is the standing strategic tool. Instead of "how do I build authority," ask "what destroys authority in photography?" (broken consistency, AI identity replacement, scope creep, missed production days). Every locked doctrine is an inversion answer.
- The 25 tendencies are the operating psychology of the VIB method, pricing conversations, and casting discipline. Deprival-Superreaction explains why last-minute casting changes trigger cascading bad decisions. Social Proof explains why scene-density building works faster than individual DM campaigns. Reason-Respecting explains why positioning copy that explains the "why" converts better than aesthetic claims alone.
- Reliability doctrine = casting call discipline exactly. The 24-hr confirmation, two-strike rule, and wardrobe photo requirement are structural responses to the Reliability Tendency, not optional courtesies.

### UPDATES / CONTRADICTIONS to Existing Doctrine

**Updates:** The Omission Cost Accounting framework adds a new lens not explicitly codified. SNIPED tracks what it does; it should also track what it declined, missed, or failed to pursue. This belongs in the quarterly Constraint Audit.

**Contradictions:** Munger's "willingness to destroy best-loved ideas" is in mild tension with the "Repetition > Novelty / no new frameworks for 90 days" lock. Resolution: the lock applies to framework generation (no new SOPs), not framework auditing (quarterly audit of locked doctrines is Munger-correct). Running the office and auditing the system are different operations.

## 2. No Filter: The Inside Story of Instagram (Sarah Frier, 2020)

**Coverage line:** 18 of 18 segments OK; full book recovered.

**1-line thesis:** Instagram's rise and institutional capture proves that aesthetic restraint and community curation are defensible moats right up until the moment a parent company's growth math overrides founder autonomy, at which point values are optimized away regardless of revenue or user love.

### 4-8 Most Useful Frameworks (Dense)

**1. Aesthetic Constraint as Competitive Moat**
Square format, no re-share, filters as art equalizers, 15-second video limit. Each constraint was a deliberate editorial choice that shaped user behavior more than any feature addition could. Constraint produced aspiration; aspiration produced adoption. The moment constraints were relaxed under growth pressure (rectangular photos, algorithm, Stories), the aesthetic moat began eroding. The no-reshare rule specifically prevented the Vine death spiral (viral resharing killed creator motivation; top Viners left). Product architecture is culture architecture.

**2. Curation Before Scale (and the Failure Point)**
Instagram's early community team (Toffey, Zollman, Porch) operated as human taste-makers: personal relationships with creators, handpicked featured accounts, InstaMeets. This built trust faster than any algorithm. But it did not scale. Once user count hit hundreds of millions, the curation model collapsed into algorithmic proxies (engagement rate, verified-comment ordering) that were gameable from the first day of deployment. The lesson is not "don't curate." It is "build the proof of concept on curation, then architect how curation becomes structural before it becomes a bottleneck."

**3. Authenticity Theater and the Manufactured Genuine**
Kardashian image releases designed to look casual. Mark Seliger portrait studio at the Vanity Fair party designed so celebrities would share "without corporate fingerprints." Influencer posts with undisclosed sponsorships that "felt like friend recommendations." At scale, Instagram did not produce authenticity; it produced the most compelling simulation of authenticity ever built. The gap between "feels honest" and "is honest" is the entire influencer economy. 93% of top-50 influencer branded posts violated FTC disclosure rules post-2016. The platform's stated values ("meaningful and genuine") were marketing copy while the economic structure demanded the opposite.

**4. Network Effects as Regulatory Blind Spot**
The FTC and UK Office of Fair Trading evaluated Instagram's 2012 acquisition against current market share (Path: 3M users, Hipstamatic: 4M). They missed that Instagram had already won via network effects. Once a network reaches critical mass, switching costs become insurmountable; no competitor can rebuild the social graph from scratch. Regulators measure what is, not what will inevitably be. This is the predictive blindness that allowed Facebook to swallow its most significant rival with a one-hour fairness hearing.

**5. Acquisition as Autonomy Ceiling**
Systrom's independence lasted exactly as long as Zuckerberg believed it served Facebook's interests. The mechanics: hiring cap (68 people for 1B users), attribution erasure (removed "Posted from Instagram" links), growth sabotage (killed free Facebook-to-Instagram promotions), forced adoption of Facebook's notification-spam playbook after Instagram was deliberately starved. The founders lasted 6 years. The WhatsApp founders lasted fewer. The pattern is structurally determined: parent company's crisis priorities always override subsidiary's product autonomy. Acquisition is not a funding mechanism; it is an autonomy clock with an unknown but finite countdown.

**6. Algorithm as Behavior Architect (Not Reflection)**
Instagram did not reflect human behavior; it shaped it. Metric optimization (follow count, likes, comment velocity) changed how restaurants designed menus, how architects designed spaces, how teenagers managed their identities ("finsta" as the escape valve from the pressure the main feed created). Fyre Festival was the extreme case: $250K for a single Kendall Jenner post, zero delivered value. The platform's stated culture ("art, beauty, creativity") was permanently overwritten by what it actually measured (follower count, engagement rate). What you measure is what you are.

**7. Founder Personality Equals Product Personality (and its Limit)**
Systrom's liberal-arts-plus-craft sensibility produced Instagram's aesthetic. His rejection of hacker culture, his obsession with visual craft, his editorial instincts about beauty all became the platform's culture. But this is only true while the founder controls the resource allocation. Once Mosseri was installed as product head and Zuckerberg's quarterly math governed headcount, Systrom's personality became irrelevant to product outcomes. Founder-product alignment is real but fragile.

**8. The Finsta Insight (Structural Product Failure as Market Signal)**
15-20% of users maintained private secondary Instagram accounts specifically to escape the pressure the main feed created. Snapchat built an entire product on the same insight. When users invent workarounds at scale, it is not a user error; it is a product architecture failure. The finsta is the market telling you exactly what your product is missing. SNIPED's scene-density model is the answer to the problem finsta represents: create depth and authentic context inside a bounded, trusted community rather than performing for algorithmic approval.

### SNIPED Application

- Instagram's constraint-as-moat logic directly validates the locked visual direction (Meisel/Roversi lane, 7 composite environments, B&W card dual-register). These are not arbitrary limits; they are the architecture that produces aspiration. Releasing them for novelty is the exact mistake Instagram made post-2016.
- Frier's curation model validates scene-density over audience breadth. Instagram won by making tastemakers visible in specific communities (fashion, photography, music). SNIPED builds the same cluster depth in LA Black founder culture. The lesson: depth in one scene creates more durable authority than reach across many scenes.
- The authenticity theater finding is the most operationally important. SNIPED's anti-AI-identity stance + lineage doctrine + transparent hybrid methodology (real subject + AI environment + disclosed composite process) are not just ethical positions. They are structural moat builders. In a market where 93% of sponsored content violates disclosure rules, radical transparency is a competitive differentiator.
- The acquisition-as-autonomy-ceiling pattern argues for staying bootstrapped, capital-lean, and referral-funded as long as proof allows. Every external dependency is a potential Zuckerberg clock.
- Algorithm as behavior architect applies to LinkedIn. SNIPED's comment doctrine (5-10 per day, Tier 0 CRM only, genuine opener) deliberately refuses algorithmic gaming because gaming the system produces the finsta problem: followers who don't trust you.

### UPDATES / CONTRADICTIONS to Existing Doctrine

**Updates:** The "finsta insight" adds a new diagnostic tool: when your audience builds workarounds, read the workaround as product feedback, not user failure. This applies to any SNIPED content or client-service surface where people are finding alternate paths to what they actually need.

**No contradictions.** The book's entire arc confirms existing doctrine at every major node (editorial restraint, scene density, anti-scale, founder autonomy, transparency over theater).

## 3. FIGMA 2.0 last.docx (Design + AI Workflow Curriculum)

**Coverage line:** 10 of 10 segments OK; full curriculum recovered.

**1-line thesis:** The designer's role in 2026 is workflow architect across a multi-tool AI stack (Claude Code, Codex/Codeex, Figma, Windsurf/Cursor, Stitch), where token economics, training discipline, and spatial reasoning replace pixel-pushing as the core competency.

### 4-8 Most Useful Frameworks (Dense)

**1. Multi-Tool Competency Stack (Not Single-Tool Mastery)**
No single AI tool does everything. The working stack: Google Stitch (fast, free, mid-fi concept exploration at zero token cost), Claude Design (premium hi-fi output, expensive, poor iteration loop), Claude Code (best code quality + Figma attribute precision: auto-layout, fill/hug), Codeex (3-4x token efficiency for iterative refinement), Figma (review surface, design system versioning, component master). The job is knowing which tool to deploy at which stage, not mastering one tool deeply. Stitch first (stakeholder alignment), Claude Design only after layout locked (token conservation), Codex for tactical micro-refinement.

**2. Three-Phase AI Design System Training**
Never "import and generate." Phase 1: Train on variables/tokens with light/dark values and explicit use-case descriptions. Phase 2: Train on components grouped by category (form/navigation/data-display). Phase 3: Train on type scales including responsive values and variable bindings. Each phase becomes a reusable Claude Skill (markdown decision tree). Only after three-phase training can you ask AI for complex widgets/modals with reliable output. Skip this and you pay the reverse-engineering tax: AI produces 80% incomplete output that costs more in polish time than building from scratch.

**3. Component Complexity Sorting**
Simple components (buttons, fields, checkboxes): do not use AI generation. Cost-benefit ratio is inverted: 6 minutes + 5,400 tokens for a button, plus iteration cycles. Buy pre-built system ($50-150), customize via component copying. Reserve AI for complex layouts: modals, dashboards, multi-state tables, full page compositions. This is the same principle as Munger's circle of competence applied to tool use: know where the tool adds value and ignore it everywhere else.

**4. Figma Auto-Layout as Flex Parity**
Auto-layout is Figma's implementation of CSS flexbox. Master it as flex (row/column, gap, padding, hug/fill width, alignment) and responsive design becomes structural rather than manual. Auto-layout is the mechanism that makes Figma-to-Wix/Vercel imports automatically responsive. If your Figma layers respect auto-layout, responsive breakpoints generate themselves on export. Build with auto-layout from the start; retrofitting is expensive.

**5. Visual Reference Over Prose Description**
AI generates faster and more accurately from visual examples than from prose descriptions. The "kitchen metaphor": showing a dark cabinet style produces better output than saying "I want something dark." Mobbin as design reference library: pull real app flows and screens before prompting AI. This anchors AI expectations visually and reduces token burn on miscommunicated style. The same principle extends to Claude Code: provide screenshots plus spatial description ("top part has text + logo, bottom has subtext + button") rather than text-only prompts.

**6. Emotion-First Reverse Engineering (OAR Studio / Pro Design Thinking)**
Pick one core emotion the design must produce. Reverse-engineer every decision (color, type, motion, spacing, imagery) to serve that single emotion vector. This is the Headspace case study. Design for divergent emotions produces incoherence. Anticipatory scalability extends this: design at launch for three moves forward (animation, merchandise, mobile, formats, 6-month future). Burger King's 2020 rebrand was engineered for TikTok, 3D, merchandise, and billboards at launch, not retrofitted. Lock the feeling first; everything else follows.

**7. File Architecture as Trust Signal (Stranger Test)**
Clean naming conventions, logical folder grouping, README docs (fonts/colors/export specs) are not polish; they are infrastructure. The "stranger test": if a cold collaborator cannot onboard to your file in 5 minutes without asking, your system is not scalable. File discipline appears in no portfolio but compounds trust across every handoff. This applies to any deliverable (Brand System, Cultural Doc, Direction Stack, client brief).

**8. Windsurf/Cursor Planner Mode as Hallucination Guard**
For iOS/web app builds in AI code editors: write a markdown plan file before building. When AI repeats mistakes, check the plan. "Ask me questions before we start" (warming the model) prevents the million-fork problem where AI makes consequential architectural decisions without surfacing them. Build one screen at a time; do not batch multiple pages into a single prompt. Sonnet 4 or equivalent is required; lower models fail structurally on complex builds.

### SNIPED Application

- The three-phase design system training maps to SNIPED's Brand System build. Train Claude on color tokens (luxury editorial palette, no teal/orange, Adobe Neutral foundation), then on components (card system, apparatus layer, hero post format), then on type scales (chapter card body, caption, title hierarchy). Only then generate at scale.
- Component complexity sorting applies to Chapter Card system: buy or source base card component structure, customize via copying, reserve Claude for complex composite layout generation.
- Emotion-first reverse engineering is the Direction Stack's design method stated in Figma terms. The locked emotion for SNIPED visual direction: quiet authority. Every design decision (B&W card register, restrained palette, negative space, Meisel/Roversi visual DNA) serves that single emotion.
- The stranger test applies to every SNIPED deliverable: Cultural Doc, Brand System, Direction Stack book. Can a client, collaborator, or future operator onboard without asking? If not, the system is not done.
- Figma-to-Claude-Code-to-Vercel is the live production pipeline for the Direction Stack digital presence, Chapter Card system, and Brand System client deliverables. The pipeline is validated and documented in this curriculum.

**Updates:** The planner-mode + markdown-guard pattern for AI code editors is new operational doctrine for SNIPED's web/app builds. Add to TOOLCHAIN_ACTIVATION.md: Windsurf over Cursor for builds exceeding 3-4 pages (Cursor degrades past that threshold per segment 6). ## SHELF SYNTHESIS

### What These Three Books Share

All three books are arguments about the relationship between constraint and compounding.

Munger builds wealth and wisdom through circle-of-competence discipline, concentrated conviction, and 60 years of refusing to swing at pitches outside his strike zone. Instagram's Systrom builds cultural authority through editorial restraint (square format, no reshare, curation over virality) until the moment an external force (Zuckerberg's growth math) removes the constraints. The Figma 2.0 curriculum shows that the same discipline applies to AI tool selection: define the constraint (Stitch for exploration, Claude for hi-fi, Codeex for refinement), respect the workflow, do not collapse everything into one tool.

The shelf, read whole, produces one compound lesson: the operator who defines their constraints first and holds them longest wins. Constraint is not a limitation on ambition; it is the mechanism of compounding.

### The Lollapalooza Convergence

Reading all three together reveals a single lollapalooza configuration relevant to SNIPED right now:

1. Munger: concentrated conviction in a narrow circle + checklist-driven execution + omission cost tracking = the operating system for a decade-long moat.
2. Frier: editorial restraint + community depth over audience breadth + radical transparency = the content and positioning architecture.
3. Figma 2.0: emotion-first design + phased AI training + multi-tool workflow discipline = the production infrastructure.

These three forces are not separate. They describe the same system from three different disciplines. When they all align in SNIPED's operations simultaneously, the effect is exponential, not additive. This is the lollapalooza event that the 90-day cathedral build is designed to produce.

### Key Cross-Book Tensions (Report, Not Resolution)

**Tension 1: Curation vs. Scale**
Munger argues for concentrated conviction (few holdings, big bets). Instagram demonstrates that curation works only up to a scale threshold before it must become structural (algorithm, rules, architecture) or it collapses. SNIPED currently operates at curation scale. The question of when and how to make curation structural (encoded in systems, not dependent on founder attention) is unresolved and worth monitoring. The 5-lineage constraint and 7-environment rotation are early structural attempts.

**Tension 2: Restraint as Moat vs. Restraint as Ceiling**
Instagram's quality gate ("Instagram is not for half-eaten sandwiches") built the premium aesthetic moat, but also created posting paralysis that ceded the intermediate-content market to Snapchat. Munger's circle of competence has a similar risk: the circle can become a cage if not periodically audited. SNIPED's quarterly Constraint Audit (Munger-derived) is the mechanism that prevents the moat from becoming a ceiling.

**Tension 3: Autonomy vs. Toolchain Dependency**
Figma 2.0 argues for multi-tool orchestration (Claude, Codeex, Stitch, Figma, Windsurf). Instagram argues that platform dependency is an autonomy risk. SNIPED's "Connected toolchain default" must account for the difference between tools that extend capability (owned relationships, portable outputs, open formats) and platforms that capture dependency (proprietary distribution, locked social graph, parent-company override). Use the tools; do not become the tools.
