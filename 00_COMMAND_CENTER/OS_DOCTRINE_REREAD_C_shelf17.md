### BOOK 1: ArtOfWar.pdf

**Coverage line:** 8/8 segments confirmed, full Giles translation including 13 chapters, 11 commentators, historical authentication, and all tactical doctrine through spy networks.

**1-line thesis:** Victory is the product of systematic pre-calculation, information asymmetry, and disciplined positioning, not raw force or inspired genius.

**Densest Frameworks / Lessons:**

1. **The Five Factors + Seven Comparisons Diagnostic** (Ch. 1-2): Pre-battle audit across Moral Law, Heaven, Earth, Commander, Method yields a systematic comparison against the opponent on all seven derived metrics. This is a decision framework, not inspiration.

2. **Deception as Operating Layer** (Ch. 2-6): "All warfare is based on deception." Appear weak when strong, feign disorder from perfect order. Strength underneath is mandatory. Concealment only works if real capability exists beneath it.

3. **Economic Fatigue Doctrine** (Ch. 3): Protracted campaigns destroy the attacker's state faster than the enemy's. Speed-to-proof, lean operations, and constraint-driven focus are structural advantages, not just preferences.

4. **Terrain + Scene Intelligence** (Ch. 9-11): Six terrain types, 28+ sign-reading rules, environmental warning zones. The general reads micro-signals (dust patterns, bird behavior, troop posture) to decode macro-state. Intelligence precedes movement.

5. **SHUAI-JAN Serpent Principle** (Ch. 12): Army responds to threat at any point (head, tail, middle) as a unified organism. Structural unity across all functions beats heroic individual action. Signal systems (drums, flags) coordinate mass as one.

6. **Nine Situations + Constraint as Commitment** (Ch. 12): Hemmed-in ground forces soldiers to fight to the death. Removing escape routes triggers commitment. Forced constraint sharpens execution. Han Hsin's river-backed formation wins because men have no retreat option.

7. **Five-Class Spy Architecture** (Ch. 14): Local, Inward, Converted, Doomed, Surviving spies form an interlocked intelligence system. The Converted spy is the hub: unlocks all others. Without foreknowledge, the army is blind. Intelligence is not optional; it is the lever that separates ordinary from extraordinary results.

8. **Enterprise Mandate**: "Unhappy is the fate of one who tries to win battles without cultivating the spirit of enterprise." Sitting on advantage without action is strategic death. Favorable moments require deployment.

**SNIPED Application:**

- The Five Factors framework becomes a pre-shoot, pre-campaign, and pre-launch audit tool. Before each chapter, map: Moral Law (is the narrative coherent, does the team align?), Heaven (timing/seasonal/cultural moment), Earth (LA scene density, resource constraints), Commander (right people with balanced courage/wisdom/discipline?), Method (is the casting doctrine, outreach stack, and production SOP wired?).
- The Converted spy maps directly to VIB network mechanics. First founder photographed becomes the hub: teaches scene rules, introduces to other nodes, accelerates trust pathways. Each repeat client is an upgraded spy with deeper access.
- The "deception requires real strength" rule is the armature under SNIPED's hybrid-operator stance. The AI-for-world-construction position only holds if the editorial identity output is genuinely excellent. Without that foundation, the positioning is hollow.
- Five fatal general defects (recklessness, cowardice, hasty temper, honor sensitivity, over-care) map to five founder failure modes BJ must audit against. Character discipline IS revenue discipline.
- Enterprise mandate directly calls out the "repetition > novelty" doctrine. Architecture is locked. Reps are the only move now. The favorable moment is active.

**UPDATES / CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine:**

- STRENGTHENS: "Payment follows proof," "scene-density thinking," "repetition > novelty," "connected toolchain default," "casting call doctrine."
- ADDS: Pre-campaign Five Factors audit (not previously formalized as a structured diagnostic).

### BOOK 2: The Mom Test (Fitzpatrick, Rob, 2016)

**Coverage line:** 5/5 segments confirmed, covering core methodology, bad data taxonomy, commitment/advancement doctrine, conversation design, segmentation, and operational checklists.

**1-line thesis:** Customer conversations are systematically broken by default; the only reliable signal is past specific behavior paired with a commitment that costs the respondent something real.

**Densest Frameworks / Lessons:**

1. **The Mom Test (3-Part Rule):** Talk about their life, not your idea. Ask about specifics in the past, not hypotheticals about the future. Talk less, listen more. Strips social courtesy bias from any conversation.

2. **Three Bad Data Categories:** Compliments (worthless), Fluff (generic claims, future promises, hypotheticals), Ideas (feature requests that mask non-customers). All must be deflected, anchored, or dug beneath before any signal is trusted.

3. **Premature Zoom as False-Positive Engine:** Asking about sub-problems (gym pain) before confirming the top-level problem exists (do they care about fitness?) generates elaborate false signals from non-customers.

4. **Currencies of Conversation:** Time commitment, reputation risk, financial commitment are real signals. Compliments are zero-cost and therefore zero-data. The more someone gives up, the more their words can be trusted.

5. **Commitment + Advancement Doctrine:** Every meeting either succeeds (ends with concrete next step + something of value given up) or fails (ends in compliment or stall). No middle ground. Zombie leads die by this standard.

6. **Earlyvangelist Identification:** "THAT IS THE WORST PART OF MY LIFE AND I WILL PAY YOU RIGHT NOW" is the signal. Rational positive responses are noise. The crazy first customer has: a known problem, budget, active search, and a makeshift solution already running.

7. **The Three-Part Process Loop:** Before (segment, 3 big goals, desk research, best guesses) > During (frame, Mom Test questions, deflect/anchor/dig, notes, commitment push) > After (team review, permanent storage, belief update, next 3 questions). Velocity rules: 1 hour of prep max, 1-2 weeks of research max, then build.

8. **Good/Bad Meeting Taxonomy (8 cases):** Diagnosed and prescribed: "I love it" is fatal. "Let me know when it launches" is fatal. "When can you meet the rest of the team?" is good. "Can I buy the prototype?" is great. "I would definitely buy that" is dangerous. The taxonomy is a decision gate, not a feeling.

**SNIPED Application:**

- VIB DM warm-up must never ask "would you be interested?" Replace with: "What was the last photo project you did that actually worked? What made it different?" Past behavior, specific, about their life.
- Client intake before any shoot must run the Mom Test. Deflect "love your style" compliments immediately. Anchor to: last photographer they worked with, what failed, what the deliverable actually needed to do. Design the shoot from their real constraint, not their dream project.
- Casting calls require behavior anchoring, not interest confirmation. Wardrobe photo requirement, 24-hour confirmation, two-strike rule are all behavioral signals. Fitzpatrick validates the doctrine already in place.
- KOTS founder conversations with Eric need the Commitment + Advancement standard applied. If Eric hasn't given up something of value (time, reputation, a specific next step), the conversation did not advance. "Let me think about it" is a zombie lead.
- The earlyvangelist test applies to Direction Stack book subjects. The subject must have an emotional stake in being documented, not just appreciation for the project. Filter for emotional urgency, not intellectual enthusiasm.
- Before any VIB outreach batch, run the prep step: define the 3 big questions this batch is designed to answer. After the batch, review signals as a team, update beliefs, set next 3 questions.

**UPDATES / CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine:**

- STRENGTHENS: "Hardest to say no to" rule. Fitzpatrick makes the mechanics concrete: the hardest to say no to is the offer that forces real commitment rather than social courtesy.
- STRENGTHENS: "Hospitality vs service" (Guidara). Hospitality IS the Mom Test in client delivery: ask about their real situation, not the polished brief.
- ADDS: Formal Commitment + Advancement gate as a check on every sales conversation, DM exchange, and KOTS family conversation. No existing doctrine captures this at this level of precision.
- ADDS: Earlyvangelist filter as a talent/subject recruitment standard. Not previously explicit in casting doctrine.
- ADDS: The Currencies of Conversation framework as a complement to the Trust Equation. T = (C+R+I)/S is the long-run relationship model; Currencies is the per-conversation signal quality check.
- MILD TENSION with "full engagement before direction": Fitzpatrick says go talk after 1 hour of prep. OS engagement protocol is deeper. Resolution: the prep IS the OS read; once architecture is locked (which it is), fast conversation deployment is exactly what's called for.

**1-line thesis:** Evoto is a batch-first, identity-preserving AI retouching suite built for high-volume studio professionals; its value to SNIPED is as apparatus for composite world-building and retouching infrastructure, not as a positioning model.

**Densest Frameworks / Lessons:**

1. **Identity-Preserving Slider Architecture:** Every Evoto tool (skin retouching, face contouring, hair color, body adjustment) adjusts one narrow aesthetic dimension while preserving core identity. The slider model prevents over-transformation. This is the anti-plastic-surgery principle made software.

2. **Batch + Preset as Staffing-Free Scale:** Batch processing with saved presets ships consistent brand tone across 100+ images without additional labor. This is leverage-first production: set the rule once, apply everywhere.

3. **Frequency Separation as Texture Preservation Doctrine:** Smooth skin without flattening texture. "No plastic look." Fine detail preserved. This is the technical floor for SNIPED's edit register bifurcation: identity untouched, styling adjustable, world variable.

4. **Composite Apparatus (Background Removal + Transparent PNG Export):** Evoto's batch transparent PNG export + background replacement + edge preservation is the direct technical operationalization of Composite Environment Rotation v1. Real subject stripped to transparent layer, AI world dropped in, Photoshop assembly complete.

5. **Social Proof by Use-Case Cluster:** Each feature maps to specific professional lanes (wedding, fashion, e-commerce, headshot) with quantified business outcomes (25% sales lift, 40% conversion uplift, referral growth). This is "trust through specificity," not vague testimonials.

6. **Edge Quality as Trust Signal:** "Messy edges create visible halos." Hair/fur/complex contour preservation is the hard technical moat vs. simple tools. Professional impression lives or dies at the cutout boundary.

7. **One-Click Framing vs. SNIPED's Craft Framing:** Evoto's dominant copy pattern is "effortless, one-click, no skill required." This is the vending-machine opposite of SNIPED's positioning. Confirms the polarity: SNIPED = restraint and gatekeeping; Evoto = convenience and volume. If Evoto enters the SNIPED workflow, it handles non-creative infrastructure only.

8. **Retoucher Onboarding Stack:** Tool chain sequence (Dodge and Burn > Frequency Separation > HSL > Contour Highlight) becomes a modular onboarding protocol for retouchers hired into the system. Teaches which tools touch which layers, in what order, for natural results. Not invented here but validated by Evoto's docs.

**SNIPED Application:**

- Evoto should never touch the core hero composite or final editorial output. Those stay in Bryce's hands with Photoshop + the Direction Stack guiding every decision.
- The 25% sales lift / 40% conversion uplift social proof pattern is a copy architecture lesson independent of the tool. When building SNIPED case studies, quantify business outcomes per client lane (not just aesthetic wins). "Client booked 3 referrals after portfolio update" beats "client loved the work."

- The retoucher onboarding stack (Dodge and Burn > Frequency Separation sequence) should go into the SNIPED Retoucher Onboarding doctrine as the default tool chain. Currently that doc exists in the OS; Evoto's architecture validates and extends it.

- STRENGTHENS: Edit register bifurcation (identity untouched, world variable). Evoto is the apparatus that makes this operationally real.
- STRENGTHENS: "Strongest photograph is not most processed." Frequency Separation and slider restraint align with the reject gate.

### EVALUATION GATES

**1. Commitment Gate (Fitzpatrick):** Before any client or talent is considered "in the pipeline," have they given up something of real value? Time (scheduled next step), reputation (intro or public testimonial), or money (deposit or pre-order)? If no, they are not in the pipeline.

**2. Past-Behavior Gate (Mom Test):** Before acting on any signal from a founder, client, or talent, confirm it came from a specific past event, not a hypothetical or compliment. "I would definitely work with you" fails. "Here's the deposit" passes.

**3. Composite Identity Gate (Evoto + Direction Stack):** Before any composite or retouched image ships: does the identity layer (face, body, skin texture) remain untouched? Does the world layer (background, environment, styling) match the locked chapter environment? Does edge quality pass the cutout boundary standard?

**4. Five Factors Audit Gate (Sun Tzu):** Before each shoot chapter launch: do all five factors (Moral Law, Heaven, Earth, Commander, Method) score above threshold? Any gap is a production risk to address before day-of.

**5. Earlyvangelist Gate:** Before investing significant shoot time in any Direction Stack book subject or VIB composite subject: do they score on all 4 earlyvangelist criteria (known problem, self-aware, budget/time already allocated, makeshift solution running)? Below 3 of 4 = wrong person for this lane.

**6. Meeting Anti-Pattern Gate (8-case taxonomy):** After every conversation, classify outcome against 8 cases. Any result in the "compliment" or "stall" category triggers a re-engagement protocol with a forced commitment ask, not a follow-up coffee.
