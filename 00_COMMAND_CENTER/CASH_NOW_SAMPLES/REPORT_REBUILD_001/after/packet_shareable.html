The Kingdom of the Sun is the original national high school holiday basketball tournament, going on 53 years. It has produced NBA players including Joel Embiid, Dwight Howard, and Kwame Brown, and championship coaches Joe Mazzulla and Dan Hurley.

This year, every game streams live on NFHS Network, putting your brand in front of a national audience, plus four days of packed-gym attendance in Ocala and year-round presence on the tournament website, thekingdomofthesun.com.
