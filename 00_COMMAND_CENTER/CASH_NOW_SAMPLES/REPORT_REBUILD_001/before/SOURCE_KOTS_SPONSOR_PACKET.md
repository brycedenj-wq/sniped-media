## Why sponsor the Kingdom of the Sun

The Kingdom of the Sun is the original national high school holiday basketball tournament, going on **53 years**. It has produced NBA players including Joel Embiid, Dwight Howard, and Kwame Brown, and championship coaches Joe Mazzulla and Dan Hurley.

This year, **every game streams live on NFHS Network**, putting your brand in front of a national audience, plus four days of packed-gym attendance in Ocala and year-round presence on the tournament website, **thekingdomofthesun.com**.

You're not buying a banner. You're putting your name on 53 years of history, on a national stage.

## Sponsorship tiers

### CROWN · Title Sponsor — $5,000 (one available)
"The Kingdom of the Sun presented by [Your Brand]." Your name on the tournament title, center court, the live stream, the website header, and every contest. Total association. One partner only.

### COURT · Gold — $2,500
Premium court-side signage, your logo on the website, a named contest (dunk or 3-point), a program ad, and live PA recognition all four days.

### SIDELINE · Silver — $1,000
Gym signage, website logo, program ad, and in-game PA reads.

### COMMUNITY · Local Business — $300
For Ocala and Marion County businesses: your logo on the tournament website, a listing in the program, and a PA mention. The hometown wall of support.

### IN-KIND
Provide team meals, hotel rooms, gift bags, or equipment in exchange for tier-level recognition.
