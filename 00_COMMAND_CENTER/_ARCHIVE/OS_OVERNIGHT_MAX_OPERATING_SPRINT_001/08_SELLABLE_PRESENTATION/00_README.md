## The pitch in one line
DEED OFFICE: the last clerk of a closing county Hall of Records files the final deeds of a vanishing town, and every certificate becomes a hand-numbered, wax-sealed collectible. One drawable glyph (a stamp: circle with a bar). One color (vermilion = officially recorded). A built-in ending (the last deed ever filed).
