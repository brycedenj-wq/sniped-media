# REMAINS DAY , runner-up (verdict: PARK)
The championship morning-after shot as a crime scene: the wet locker, the tagged cleat, glory as evidence. Mark: a yellow numbered evidence placard (00). Color: evidence-marker yellow.
## Promising
- Genuine emotional charge (grief, aftermath, reverence). The yellow-placard mark is strong and drawable. Faceless via taping/cord.
## Generic / risk
- The forensic-evidence device is a one-idea aesthetic that can wear thin across a long run.
- Weaker, less obvious money path than DEED (no natural subscription; print + licensing only).
- Harder to keep an athlete faceless across many frames without repeating the same crop.
## Verdict
PARK. Strong enough to keep on the shelf, not differentiated enough to develop now. Revisit only if a sports-culture licensing or drop opportunity appears.
