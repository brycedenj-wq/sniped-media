## SECTION 5 · CLOTHES AND STYLING DIRECTION

### 5.0 Frame: styling is the spine, on whatever face appears

Per `AESTHETIC_STATEMENT_EXTRACTION.md` principle 5 ("styling does the heavy lifting and the retouch finishes the job"), the wardrobe carries the image. Because the campaign is face-independent, the styling logic below applies to ANY subject who appears: a real model, a fictional/animated character, a generated-world figure, or BJ himself (body/wardrobe, not face-led). The SUBJECT_READ is authored once and frozen as a continuity constant.

### 5.1 Author the SUBJECT_READ once, freeze it

- FIRES: `STYLIST_PLAYBOOK_EXTRACTION.md` SUBJECT_READ family (body proportion, coloring, comfort energy, natural baseline, must-not-wears, hand task, lens-aware wardrobe, continuity anchor) authored ONCE and inherited by every later shot.
- Build decision: write the frozen read for the campaign's recurring subject. Placeholders that must be filled before any styled frame:
  - `proportion`: torso/leg ratio, shoulder vs hip, frame size, neck length.
  - `coloring`: skin undertone (warm/cool/neutral), hair tone + contrast, eyes : drives every near-face color pick.
  - `comfort band`: posture + energy, stretch <=20% from baseline (real person, not costume).
  - `natural baseline`: what the subject would actually wear in [DEST]'s climate/context.
  - `must-not-wear`: explicit exclusion list (a negative token; any listed item in frame = reject).
- W1. Reject (SUBJECT_READ gates): block missing/partial/re-rolled, proportion drift across a set, coloring unstated, costume-drift beyond one step, any must-not-wear item in frame.

### 5.2 Every outfit is a sentence (intent), one lane per look

- FIRES: `STYLE_MANIFESTO_EXTRACTION.md` T1 OUTFIT_INTENT_SENTENCE (five words or fewer) + T2 LANE_LOCK (elevated_minimal / streetwear_luxe / tailored_modern) + the why-protocol T13. PLUS `FASHION_KILLA.md` #23 ONE_THING_DOES_THE_TALKING (one focal point per look).
- Build decision: each trip look gets a five-word intent and one declared lane. Switching lanes across the trip is an authored move (a tonal shift between trip chapters), never a drift. Placeholder per look: `intent: "[5 words]"; lane: [one of three]; focal: [one piece]`.
- The destination sets the lane palette via climate and register: a hot/coastal trip leans elevated-minimal lightweight; a cold/urban trip can carry tailored-modern layering. UNKNOWN until [DEST] is known.
- W2/W3. Reject (manifesto gates): no nameable intent (G2), incoherent lane mixing (G10), two-plus competing focal points.

### 5.3 The six logics per garment + the mannequin bar

- FIRES: `STYLE_MANIFESTO_EXTRACTION.md` T3-T12 (shoulder fit, fit geometry, silhouette contrast, material weight, texture mix, color concept, footwear cast, accessory anchor, camera-read, care state) and G11 MANNEQUIN_GATE ("fresher than a flagship-window mannequin"). PLUS `FASHION_KILLA.md` #1 FIT_SOLVES_MOST, #30 FABRIC_QUALITY_DRIVES_PERCEPTION, #44 PANTS_AND_SHOES_ARE_HIGH_LEVERAGE, #61 GRAPHICS_LOGOS_DIAL_BACK (solids read more refined; quiet status).
- Build decision: every garment carries fit (shoulder at the bone), silhouette (volume contrast + one waistline), named heavy natural material, two-color-plus-neutral with one temperature, a cast lane-correct shoe (named, never "shoes"), one accessory anchor max two, camera-read (no logo near face), and care state (pressed, lint-free, conditioned shoes).
- High-leverage spend: footwear and the carrying layer (per #44, #30). For a trip, the climate-appropriate carrying piece (jacket/overshirt/knit) is where premium fabric is non-negotiable.
- W3/W5. Reject (manifesto + FASHION_KILLA gates): wrong shoulder (G1), plastic/thin fabric (G4), three-plus colors no concept (G5), unnamed/scuffed shoe (G6), loud logo near face (G9), wrinkled/linty/dirty-shoe care fail (G8, FASHION_KILLA #92).

### 5.4 Packing as a capsule, not a suitcase

- FIRES: `FASHION_KILLA.md` #53 PERSONAL_UNIFORM / CAPSULE, #54 QUALITY_ESSENTIALS_CAPSULE, #42 PARETO_KEYSTONE_PIECES (20% of pieces do 80% of work), #64 PIECE_FUNCTION_CANON (each piece has a job). ### 5.5 Real product vs generated garment (honest tool note)

- W5 (only if garments are generated). Reject: a generated hero garment described freehand with no real-product reference lock.

## SECTION 6 · VISUAL LANGUAGE AND SHOT LIST

### 6.0 The author-eye is the visual spine

Per `AESTHETIC_STATEMENT_EXTRACTION.md`, the corrective axis is graphic-over-atmospheric: the frame's structure is color-block and shape, NOT a dark/moody/hazy room. This is BJ's measured 8+ pattern and it is the load-bearing taste of the campaign (the face-independent spine).

### 6.1 The seven author-eye tokens become the visual grammar

- FIRES: `AESTHETIC_STATEMENT_EXTRACTION.md` Tokens 1-7: MONO_PALETTE_LOCK, GRAPHIC_OVER_ATMOSPHERIC, POSE_ARCHITECTURE, CLINICAL_RETOUCH_PORE_SAFE, RIM_LIGHT_DEPTH, IN_BETWEEN_DIRECTION, JOINT_CROP_DISCIPLINE.
- Build decision (every campaign frame must show):
  - One tonal family across backdrop + wardrobe + props (ties to 4.3).
  - Shape/color-block composition as the structural read (not mood/haze).
  - Pose architecture: chin forward-and-down for jawline, hands given a task.
  - Clinical retouch: unified complexion, pore texture preserved, no plastic skin.
  - Rim/hair separation so the subject is not flattened (no dark-on-dark melt).
  - In-between direction: caught mid-action / transition, never smile-and-hold.
  - Clean between-joint crops, centered A-frame base.
- W5. Reject (HARD, per AESTHETIC gates): a dark-moody-room frame where atmosphere is load-bearing; flattened-no-rim; multi-family color; slack jaw / limp hands; plastic skin; rehearsed presented pose; crop landing on a joint.

### 6.2 Lighting cards mapped to trip contexts

- FIRES: `LIGHTING_TECHNIQUE_CARDS.md` (25 rigs) + `AESTHETIC_STATEMENT_EXTRACTION.md` Token 5 RIM_LIGHT_DEPTH (rim placed BEFORE the key; the known weak spot).
- Build decision: pre-assign a named rig to each shot TYPE so lighting is decided, not improvised:
  - Hero portrait / editorial face frame: `One Light 45 Degree Beauty Dish` (sculpted, minimal gear, travel-friendly) or `5-Light Studio Setup` if a full kit is on hand (rim + background control).
  - Dramatic character / dark-ground hero: `Hollywood 3-Light` or `3-Light Commercial` (dual rim separation on dark) : directly answers the rim-before-key weak spot.
  - Clean product / object-mark frame (the [OBJECT] in 4.2): `White Background (2-Light Umbrella)` or `4-Light Beauty on White` for a luminous product card.
  - Natural / location daylight beats: `Creative Window Light` as the controllable stand-in for real window/door light at [DEST].
  - Two-subject / companion frames: `Standard Couples`; group: `Standard Group Shot`.
- Travel-honest note: a real trip likely cannot carry a 5-light rig. The single-light beauty-dish card and the window-light card are the realistic travel rigs; mark `[KIT ON HAND]` UNKNOWN. The rim requirement still holds : a single reflector or a window-edge can supply separation if no rim head travels.
- W5. Reject: a face frame shot flat with no rim/separation (AESTHETIC HARD gate G `AUTHOR_EYE_RIM_DEPTH`).

### 6.3 Lens-aware shot discipline

- FIRES: `STYLIST_PLAYBOOK_EXTRACTION.md` lens_token + lens_footwear_gate + near_face_color (wardrobe detail is only valid against a declared focal length).
- Build decision: every shot card declares a lens band and what it must control:

  - 50-85mm (mid/portrait): texture/fabric quality reads; accessories show; collar straight.
  - 85-135mm (tight): near-face color is everything; hair/skin/neckline/jewelry carry it.
- W5. Reject: wardrobe detail authored with no declared lens; a full-body frame that fudges footwear; a tight frame with a clashing near-face color.

### 6.4 The numbered Entry as the publishing unit

- FIRES: `VISUAL_WORLD_RULES.md` grammar rules (one repeatable format: a numbered Entry = image + number + one short field-note line, same lockup daily, true order from 001; object-state verb; deposit rule). PLUS `branding_x_clothes_gold.md` #35 VERTICAL_FIRST_CONSTRAINT (shoot/edit 9:16, gear as forcing constraint) and #34 MOOD_BOARD_COHESION_GATE (every asset matches a master board or it gets cut even if it would sell).

## SECTION 7 · STORY SPINE FOR THE TRIP

### 7.0 The trip needs a spine, or it is a slideshow

Per `STORY_DOCTRINE_MCKEE.md` §9, the Spine (Super-Objective) is the unconscious through-line that survives changing conscious desires. A trip without a spine is "a non-event" (§4: if the value charge is identical from open to close, nothing happened, cut it). The spine is BJ's authorship/taste/decision, not his face : face-independent by construction.

### 7.1 One-sentence Governing Idea (Value + Cause)

- FIRES: `STORY_DOCTRINE_MCKEE.md` §12 (Governing Idea = how and why life changes from the opening condition to the closing condition; Value + Cause) and §11 (Premise is the open "what if," Governing Idea is the proven meaning). PLUS `branding_x_clothes_gold.md` #32 NARRATIVE_CONFLICT_RULE (every video needs a problem + a resolution arc).
- Build decision: write the trip's Governing Idea as one sentence. Placeholder: `[VALUE] shifts from [negative open] to [positive/ironic close] BECAUSE [cause = the action taken on the trip]`. The trip's real purpose ([GOAL]) supplies the value at stake.
- W2/W3. Reject (McKee): a "story" whose charge is identical at open and close (nothing happened); a feed with no conflict/resolution arc.

### 7.2 Inciting Incident + Major Dramatic Question

- FIRES: `STORY_DOCTRINE_MCKEE.md` §11 Inciting Incident (must radically upset equilibrium, spark desire, raise the Major Dramatic Question, project the Obligatory Scene; the central plot's must occur on screen).
- Build decision: the campaign opens on the moment that upset equilibrium and sent BJ on the trip (the decision to go, the stakes), not on the airport or the packing. The Major Dramatic Question is what the audience wonders for the whole campaign. Placeholder: `MDQ = will [BJ/subject] [achieve GOAL] in [DEST]?`
- This corrects the MEMORY law (the-meantime-001-verdict): "open with the becoming, never let the setting own the first sentence." The trip campaign must open on the becoming (the decision/stakes), not on [DEST] as scenery.
- W2. Reject: a launch that opens on the destination-as-scenery instead of the inciting decision/stakes.

### 7.3 Progressive complications across the trip (the gap engine)

- FIRES: `STORY_DOCTRINE_MCKEE.md` §10 The Gap (story = the gap between expectation and result; each gap demands a more demanding action) and §11 Progressive Complications (never retreat to lesser actions; complexity = conflict at internal + personal + extra-personal levels at once).
- Build decision: structure the trip's Entries as escalating, not as a flat highlight reel. Each chapter/stop should raise the stakes over the last (a real trip supplies real obstacles : weather, logistics, the actual hard thing [GOAL] requires). Map at least one gap per chapter.
- W3. Reject: a sequence of equal-weight "nice moment" posts with no escalation (a non-progression).

### 7.4 Image System (the symbolic layer carried by the object-mark)

- FIRES: `STORY_DOCTRINE_MCKEE.md` §21 Image Systems (a strategy of motifs repeating and varying from start to end, built invisibly; the writer originates it). PLUS `VISUAL_WORLD_RULES.md` object-state verb (the [OBJECT] is constant; its state is the only variable that moves).
- Build decision: the [OBJECT] + [THRESHOLD] from 4.2 ARE the image system. The object recurs and varies across every Entry, accruing meaning silently. Per McKee, handle with virtual invisibility : the moment the audience consciously reads "symbol," it dies. The object should never be captioned as meaningful; it just recurs.
- W3/W5. Reject: a declared/explained symbol (McKee: declared symbolism corrupts the art); an [OBJECT] that does not recur and vary across the set.

### 7.5 Penultimate vs final charge, and the Key Image

- FIRES: `STORY_DOCTRINE_MCKEE.md` §11 ("the Penultimate Act Climax must contradict the emotional charge of the Final Climax") and §11 Resolution + §11 Key Image ("one image that, when remembered, makes the whole thing come together"). PLUS §12 three charges (idealist / pessimist / ironic; ironic lasts longest).
- Build decision: plan the campaign's penultimate beat to carry the OPPOSITE charge of the finale (a low/doubt beat before the resolution), so the finale is not halved. Identify ONE Key Image for the trip in advance (the frame the whole campaign collapses into : likely the [OBJECT] at the decisive [THRESHOLD]).
- W3. Reject: a finale whose penultimate beat repeats the same charge (halves the climax); a campaign with no identified Key Image.

### 7.6 Audience relationship + withholding

- FIRES: `STORY_DOCTRINE_MCKEE.md` §19 (keep interest by WITHHOLDING information, not giving it; exposition as ammunition; guard the best for last; three modes : Mystery / Suspense / Dramatic Irony). PLUS `VISUAL_WORLD_RULES.md` captions rule (field-note voice only: short, plain, withholding; no grade/gesture/key) and `branding_x_clothes_gold.md` #23 THCPP_LAUNCH_CYCLE (Tease, Hype, Collect, Push) + #27 LIST_OWNERSHIP.
- Build decision: the campaign withholds the payoff of [GOAL] until late; early Entries tease and build the MDQ. Captions stay field-note plain. The trip resolution (did [GOAL] happen) is the guarded final-act reveal. Move audience to an owned list (the actual sell/offer lives off-platform).
- W2/W4. Reject: front-loading the payoff; captions that over-explain the craft or the grade; a launch dependent only on algorithmic reach with no captured list.

### 7.7 Anti-cliche on the story itself

- FIRES: `STORY_DOCTRINE_MCKEE.md` §3 (cure for cliche: draft 5-15 versions of any cliche moment, pick the truest never-seen-this-way one; never trust the first inspiration) and §2 (archetypes travel, stereotypes stay home).
- Build decision: for each pivotal beat (the open, the midpoint, the resolution), draft multiple versions before committing. The "travel" test: is the trip story an archetype (a real human change anyone recognizes) or a stereotype (generic travel content)?
- W3. Reject: a pivotal beat shipped on first-instinct; a generic-travel-content read.
