# TRIP CAMPAIGN OS 001 · STRATEGY + THESIS + AUDIENCE + TARGETS

Preflight planning packet. Zero spend, zero generation, zero posting, zero naming, no crown. This file holds sections 1, 2, 3, and 11. Every recommendation traces to a cited OS source path. Trip-specific blanks are marked UNKNOWN and reconciled in FACTS I NEED at the end.

Constraint honored throughout: BJ-FACE-INDEPENDENT, not faceless. The campaign must not make the operator's real face the load-bearing reason it works. Faces are allowed (real models, fictional/animated characters, faces in generated worlds), and the operator's voice, writing, taste, strategy, and story are fully in play. His face can become optional later only on his explicit approval.

Gate legend (from the mission brief): W1 source-activation, W2 copy gate, W3 rubric, W4 business gate, W5 pixel activation (only where pixels or generated visuals matter).

## Section 1 · Trip objective and success definition

### 1.1 The objective frame the OS forces
The trip is not the deliverable. The trip is a wedge that opens rooms and earns the right to build something larger. This is the exact logic the OS already runs for the practice: "I am using a sharp, paid service to get inside [the] world and earn the right to build something much bigger there ... The [wedge] is the wedge that opens the rooms; the larger play ... is earned through it, not announced."
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §4 (BJ-facing statement) and §5 (wedge-to-thesis).
- Applied to a trip: the objective is to convert physical presence in a place into opened rooms, real relationships, and proof, on a lane the operator already owns, not to "go on a trip and post about it."

### 1.2 Success must be defined as a staged proof path, not a vibe
The OS never lets "success" be a feeling. It defines a 5-rung proof ladder where each rung proves a specific thing, with a R/Y/G readout at a fixed date.
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §8 (the proof path) and §12 (kill criteria).
- Determinable success ladder for ANY trip (purpose-agnostic scaffold):
  1. First real conversation booked with an in-lane target before the trip starts. Proves the offer/reason-to-meet is real, not just liked.
  2. First room opened on the ground (a meeting taken, a venue walked, an intro made, a shoot or capture granted). Proves access, the real gate.
  3. Repeatable signal (2 to 3 conversations, one referred forward). Proves it is not a fluke.
  4. A pull beyond the artifact (a target asks for more than the thing offered). Proves wedge-to-bigger.
  5. A durable asset accrues as a byproduct (relationships, a content library, a proof record). The earned option.
- Each trip-specific rung target is UNKNOWN until the trip facts are known (see FACTS I NEED).

### 1.3 Success is also a money definition, gated
The OS requires payment to follow proof and refuses to call a campaign successful on attention alone. "Payment FOLLOWS proof ... Keep/kill/scale on real signal (Mom Test)."
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/OS_MONEY_OFFER_DOCTRINE.md` lines on proof-before-price and proof-to-cash; `00_COMMAND_CENTER/OS_OFFER_STACK.md` (which tiers are sellable-now vs AMBER vs HELD).
- Determinable money-success scaffold: a trip succeeds at the business layer when at least one in-lane conversation converts toward a paid tier from the active stack (spec/diagnostic floor $1,500, or private demo floor $2,500), or produces a committed next step that the proof-to-cash router would accept. The dollar target itself is UNKNOWN until the offer being carried on the trip is known.
- Gate: W4 (business gate) owns the money definition of success. W1 (source-activation) confirms the objective actually traces to BASEPLATE/offer doctrine and is not invented.

### 1.4 What would make the trip a FAIL (kill criteria, ported)
The OS already keeps an explicit kill list so a project cannot quietly become a different, worse thing. Ported to a trip:
- The targets praise the reason-to-meet but will not take a real meeting (most likely kill).
- It reads as "travel content" to the people who matter, not as proof or a real offer.
- Access is structurally blocked on the ground (no room actually opens).
- It only ever produces one-off content and never converts past it (then it was a vacation with a camera).
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §12.
- Gate: W4 (business gate) checks fail-conditions before the trip is called a go.

## Section 2 · Core campaign thesis

### 2.1 The one-line thesis shape (determinable, fill-in)
The OS thesis is always a wedge sentence: a high-trust, in-lane, premium reason to be present, that opens a path the operator already has an unfair claim to.
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §1 to §5 (the statement ladder, one-sentence through investor-level).
- Determinable template (placeholders, no naming): "On this trip, [the operator] shows up in [PLACE/SCENE = UNKNOWN] as the operator who can [the in-lane capability he already owns], so [TARGET = UNKNOWN] gets [the dream outcome = UNKNOWN], and that presence earns the right to [the larger play = UNKNOWN]."
- The capability he already owns is determinable from the OS even before trip facts: operator-layer taste, world/visual direction, story/character construction, copy and outreach, and a campaign-house production stack. Moat = the operator layer, not the camera or the tools.
  - Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §4 ("The moat is ... my operator layer").

### 2.2 The thesis must be a grand-slam reason-to-care, scored
A campaign thesis only passes if it raises dream outcome and perceived likelihood while cutting time and effort for the target. "Grand-slam offer = (dream outcome x perceived likelihood) / (time delay x effort)."
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/OS_MONEY_OFFER_DOCTRINE.md` (grand-slam offer line, os_offer_builder).
- Determinable mechanic: whatever the trip is selling/offering, the thesis copy must lead with the target's outcome, not the trip itself or the operator's bio. This is the same law cold outreach uses: "Sell outcomes, not deliverables ... lead with an outcome not your bio."
  - Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §3 (offer-beats-copy, outcome-based pitch formula) and §7 (diagnostics: lead with an outcome).
- Gate: W3 (rubric) scores the thesis on the grand-slam ratio. W2 (copy gate) checks the public-facing thesis line for outcome-first phrasing and no em-dashes.

### 2.3 The thesis must trade scope, never price, and stay in-lane
The active money lane anchors high and trades scope, not price; off-lane or price-shopper framing is a pass.
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/OS_MONEY_OFFER_DOCTRINE.md` (ACTIVE lane = campaign-house/world/IP; anchor high; trade SCOPE not price) and (screen before pitch: in-lane, decision-maker, cares about outcome = FIT).
- Determinable mechanic: the trip thesis is built around the premium lane (campaign-house / world / IP), with the OLD photo-shoot framing held as fallback only. If the trip facts pull toward a cheaper, off-lane "shoot," that is a flag to re-screen, not to discount.
  - Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/OS_OFFER_STACK.md` (active tiers; old SNIPED photo tiers are FALLBACK ONLY, never default).
- Gate: W4 (business gate) confirms lane fit before the thesis is locked.

### 2.4 The thesis keeps the empire private
The OS forbids claiming the platform/registry/billion-dollar layer publicly; the public thesis sells the wedge and the outcome, the larger ambition stays an earned option.
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §7 (parked claims) and Guardrails.
- Determinable rule for the trip: the public banner/identity sells the present, concrete reason-to-meet; it does not announce the bigger build. No fabricated proof, testimonials, or client claims on any trip surface.
- Gate: W2 (copy gate) blocks parked claims and fake proof in any public trip copy.

### 2.5 Face-independence is a thesis property, not a restriction
The thesis must rest on the operator layer (taste, story, world, outreach, production) so that the campaign works whether or not his real face is the subject. Faces (models, characters, generated-world faces) are tools the thesis can use; his face is never the load-bearing reason.
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §4 (moat = operator layer, not the camera) interpreted under the mission's face-independence constraint.
- Gate: W3 (rubric) checks "does this still work if his face is swapped out for a model/character/world." If the answer is no, the thesis is over-indexed on his face and fails.

## Section 3 · Audience and who he is trying to reach

### 3.1 Audience is defined as an ICP before any outreach, not after
The hardest law in the outreach corpus: targeting beats messaging, and no outbound happens before a written ICP exists. "Lead quality beats messaging quality ... Improving targeting by 10% often outperforms improving copy by 50%." "Minimum ICP definition required before any outbound: firmographics, roles and titles, pain points, and buying triggers."
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §1 (targeting and lead quality).
- Determinable mechanic: before the trip, write a one-page ICP with five fields, fit (who they are), problem presence (the pain the offer relieves), decision capacity (can they say yes), location/timing (are they reachable on the ground during the trip window), and trigger signal (why now). The specific values per field are UNKNOWN until trip facts land.

### 3.2 Three audience layers, each with a different job
A trip campaign reaches more than one audience at once. The OS separates them so copy and targeting do not blur.
1. The on-the-ground in-lane targets (the people he is physically trying to open a room with). Highest priority, smallest list. This is the buyer-class audience.
   - Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §11 (this buyer: discretion, real budget, acute urgency, reachable) and `OS_MONEY_OFFER_DOCTRINE.md` (screen before pitch).
2. The pre-warm audience (people who should recognize the operator before the cold touch lands). Built by social warming before the trip.
   - Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §4 (social warming: comment for 1 to 2 weeks before emailing the audience) and §9 (familiarity effect, optimize the profile as a landing page).
3. The broadcast/witness audience (the operator's current following who watch the trip as a story and become proof of momentum). This is where world, story, and visuals carry weight, and where W5 applies.
   - Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §13 (surfaces: site, LinkedIn, outreach) as the channel set; the broadcast story layer is where the metabolized world/character/story doctrines feed (cited as available sources in the brief; specific files to be named by the world/story section owners).
- His current audience size and platform mix is UNKNOWN.

### 3.3 Smaller, angle-matched lists beat one big list
The OS bans the mass blast. "More smaller lists beat one big list ... each with a unique angle." And the "Only Them" test: "if a sentence applies to 1,000 other companies, cut or rewrite it."
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §1 (smaller lists, Only Them test) and §3 (single CTA, length caps, outcome-based pitch).
- Determinable mechanic for the trip: segment the on-the-ground targets into 2 to 3 angle cohorts (for example by target type or by the specific pain each cohort has), and write a per-cohort reason-to-meet. Each message must carry at least one fact that could only be true for that cohort on this trip in this place.
- Gate: W2 (copy gate) enforces the Only Them test and the single-CTA, under-80-word, no-link, plain-text first-touch rules on every outreach atom (those rules live in `COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §3 and §6).

### 3.4 Screen the audience for fit before spending a single touch on them
The money doctrine screens buyers before pitching: low self-orientation, in-lane, decision-maker, cares about outcome = FIT; price-shopper/off-lane = PASS.
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/OS_MONEY_OFFER_DOCTRINE.md` (screen before pitch line) and `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §1 (disqualification has value) and §5 (pre-call qualification filter).
- Determinable mechanic: a target only enters the trip list if it passes the fit screen. Off-lane and price-shopper targets are removed before the trip, not during.
- Gate: W4 (business gate) owns the fit screen.

### 3.5 Timing signals decide WHO gets reached, not just who exists
The OS prioritizes targets by trigger signal (funding, hiring, leadership change, launches, recent public activity), and treats individual behavioral signals as the most reliable timing cue.
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §1 (trigger signals, signal-to-pain mapping, individual behavioral signals beat company-level intent).
- Determinable mechanic for the trip: rank the audience by which targets have a live reason to care during the trip window (an event, a launch, a season, a public post). Write copy about the operational problem the signal creates, not the signal itself.
- The specific signals available depend on who the targets are: UNKNOWN.

## Section 11 · People / brands / venues to target

### 11.1 The target list is built in layers, like an ICP search
The OS builds target searches in a fixed order so the list stays high-quality: firmographics first, technographics second, signal/trigger third, role/seniority last.
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §1 (build ICP searches in layers) and §2 (list building and enrichment).
- Determinable mechanic for the trip: build the target roster in layers, (1) who fits the lane and is physically reachable in the trip location/window, (2) what tools/scene/context they sit in, (3) what live signal makes now the moment, (4) the specific person inside the org/venue who can say yes. One person per org at first contact ("one per company" toggle logic).

### 11.2 Three determinable target buckets (purpose-agnostic scaffold)
Even without the trip facts, the OS forces three target buckets for a presence-on-the-ground campaign:
1. People (individual decision-makers/operators who can open a room or refer one). The OS prioritizes owners with discretion, real budget, acute urgency, reachable on LinkedIn/email.
   - Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §11 (this buyer profile).
2. Brands/firms (organizations in-lane that the offer can move, or that the operator's world/visual work can serve). The OS anchors these to the active campaign-house lane, not the fallback photo lane.
   - Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/OS_OFFER_STACK.md` (active tiers) and `OS_MONEY_OFFER_DOCTRINE.md` (anchor high, trade scope).
3. Venues/places/scenes (physical rooms, events, or locations that are themselves the access point on the ground). These are trip-specific and largely UNKNOWN until the destination and purpose are set.
- The actual names/handles in each bucket are UNKNOWN and must not be invented here (no naming rule).

### 11.3 Warmest-first ordering
The OS reaches the warmest targets first: own-brand engagers are the highest-priority leads, then pre-warmed targets, then cold.
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §1 (own-brand engagers are the warmest leads) and §4 (social warming, speed to lead within 30 minutes on a positive reply).
- Determinable mechanic: order the trip target list as (a) anyone already engaging the operator's content who is in the trip location, (b) targets warmed by 1 to 2 weeks of pre-trip commenting, (c) cold-but-fit targets reached with an Only-Them reason-to-meet. Positive replies during the trip get a response within 30 minutes (mobile reality of being on the ground).

### 11.4 Multichannel, value-first, never a generic ask
The OS ties channels together and leads with value before any ask. "Lead with value before asking for a call ... Asking for a call with no prior value is like proposing on a first date." And the banger micro-offer: a high-perceived-value, low-cost component offered free as the CTA.
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §3 (banger micro-offer formula, offer formula (ROI x Trust)/Friction) and §9 (allbound, value-first beats direct-ask).
- Determinable mechanic for the trip: each target gets a value-first reason-to-meet (a teardown, a frame, a short take relevant to them) rather than a cold "can we meet." The micro-offer is the CTA; the meeting is the pivot after they engage.
- Gate: W2 (copy gate) checks every target-facing message for outcome-first, single-CTA, no spam triggers, plain text. W4 (business gate) confirms each target passed the fit screen before any touch.

### 11.5 List hygiene and the do-not-fabricate rule
The OS enforces verification, deduplication, and a hard no-fake-proof rule. Email verification is non-negotiable before sending; no client names/testimonials may be claimed without real proof.
- Fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §2 (verification non-negotiable, dedup, archive dead ends) and `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §7 + Guardrails (no fabricated proof/testimonials/client claims).
- Determinable rule: target contact data is verified before any touch; no target is named in any public trip copy as a client/partner unless real and approved. Placeholders only in this packet.

### 11.6 Tool readiness for targeting (honest vs the ledger)
The OS keeps a tool-underuse ledger and a standing rule to route to the best premium tool and prove any skip. Honest current posture:
- Targeting and outreach tooling (lead-finder, enrichment, sending infrastructure, reply agent) named in `COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §6 and §8 is DOCTRINE-READY (the playbook exists) but NOT confirmed provisioned for this campaign (no live sending domains, warm-up, or verifier confirmed here). The atoms note tactics are point-in-time: "verify tool names and deliverability rules before use" (`COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` header).
- The OS_TOOL_UNDERUSE_LEDGER (`00_COMMAND_CENTER/OS_TOOL_UNDERUSE_LEDGER.csv`) records prior "premium stack partial" verdicts and flags underused connectors (live CRM/Airtable/Notion bases); a real CRM for the trip target roster is NOT confirmed live.
- Honest readiness call: this is a PLANNING packet. No outreach infrastructure is asserted as ready. Provisioning and deliverability warm-up (weeks) must precede any send (`COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md` §6).
- Gate: W1 (source-activation) confirms the targeting plan traces to real sources (done above). W5 does NOT apply to this section (no pixels).

## FACTS I NEED

The exact smallest facts that turn the UNKNOWNs in sections 1, 2, 3, and 11 concrete:

1. Destination (city/region/country) of the trip.
2. Trip dates and total duration (the on-the-ground window).
3. Trip purpose/goal in one line (why he is going: open rooms, sell, shoot, attend an event, scout, relationship-build, or other).
4. What is being offered or sold on this trip (which active tier from `OS_OFFER_STACK.md`, or a new offer), and at what anchor price.
5. Who he is trying to reach: the in-lane target TYPE (people, brands/firms, or venues) and the one-line buyer profile, even if no names yet.
6. His public banner/identity for the trip (is it under the practice wrapper, a personal identity, or a new placeholder), so the thesis and copy can be locked.
7. His current audience: platform(s), rough size, and how warm they are (so the broadcast/witness layer in 3.2 is sized).
8. Budget posture for the trip (spend ceiling, and whether any outreach tooling/CRM is already provisioned or must be built).
9. Any live trigger signals at the destination during the window (an event, a launch, a season) that make "now" the moment for specific targets.
10. Whether his real face is on the table for this campaign or held optional from the start (so face-independence in §2.5 is calibrated against his explicit choice).
