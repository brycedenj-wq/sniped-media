## 12 · DAILY RUNBOOK

The campaign runs as a HARNESS by default, not a chat turn (CLAUDE.md orchestration laws). The runbook below is the determinable cadence scaffold. Trip-specific timing (how many days out the trip is, the trip's own dates, the on-the-ground days) is UNKNOWN and gates the calendar; the mechanics do not need it.

### 12.1 Three campaign phases (determinable, dates UNKNOWN)
The campaign is three harness boundaries with operator approval between each (CLAUDE.md law 4: approval lives at the boundary, never mid-run):
1. PRE-TRIP build phase: world/identity scaffold, offer, copy bank, outreach list, content bank drafted. No send, no post.
2. TRIP live phase: daily capture + same-day publish + outreach replies handled.
3. POST-TRIP harvest phase: deliverables shipped, follow-ups sent, proof logged, decision on continue/kill.
- Source fires: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/BASEPLATE_OUTBOUND_ACTIVATION_PLAYBOOK.md` (activation sequencing), CLAUDE.md orchestration laws (boundary approval). Gate: W4.
- UNKNOWN: phase start dates, phase lengths, trip dates.

### 12.2 The daily loop (each trip day, reusable)
A fixed five-block day that does not change regardless of destination:
- BLOCK A · Re-read the lock. Re-read the pinned NEXT_ACTION + the "do not do X" constraints (face-independence, no spend, employer-safe, no naming) so goal drift across the day is caught (CLAUDE.md law 2, goal drift defense). Source: CLAUDE.md orchestration laws. Gate: W4.
- BLOCK B · Capture. Shoot/collect the day's raw material against the locked direction (the shoot is the taste core, never automated). Source: `OS_V2_UNIVERSAL_ENGINE/AI_NATIVE_BRAND_LAB/FULL_STACK_PUBLIC_ALPHA_001/PRODUCTION_TOOL_PIPELINE.md` stage 0; photo-craft atoms `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/PHOTO_CRAFT_ATOMS_WAVE001.md` and the lighting cards under `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/PHOTO_CRAFT_*`. Gate: W5 (pixels).
- BLOCK C · Build the day's public asset. Caption + visual in the locked voice and world. Source: `OS_V2_UNIVERSAL_ENGINE/AI_NATIVE_BRAND_LAB/CORPUS_COMPLETENESS_001/METABOLIZED/Bryceden_Voice_Style_Guide.md` (voice), `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COPY_DOCTRINE_CAPLES_TESTED_ADVERTISING.md` (copy). Gate: W2, and W5 if generated visuals.
- BLOCK D · Verify before publish. Vision-reject gate on any frame; second-context judge on any copy claim; no self-crown. Source: `PRODUCTION_TOOL_PIPELINE.md` stage 9 (os-vision-reject-gate + adversarial-verify). Gate: W3, W5.
- BLOCK E · Outreach + reply triage. Send the day's approved outreach, handle inbound, log every reply. Source: `00_COMMAND_CENTER/_reference/COLD_OUTREACH_ATOMS.md`, `00_COMMAND_CENTER/OS_SALES_SCRIPT_LIBRARY.md`. Gate: W2 (copy clean), W4 (payment follows proof, no send before approval).
- UNKNOWN per day: how many posts per platform per day, how many outreach sends per day, which platform leads. These need audience + budget facts (see Section 15).

### 12.3 Approval pins inside the day
No mid-run pause inside a fan-out (CLAUDE.md law 4). Every external action (send, post, deploy, any spend) is queued to a single end-of-block approval gate, not interrupted mid-task. The operator authorizes the batch at the boundary.
- Source fires: CLAUDE.md orchestration laws 4 and 6; `00_COMMAND_CENTER/OS_MONEY_PLAY_GATE.md` hard law "No spend / send / deploy / post / generation / payment rails before a real yes AND operator approval."
