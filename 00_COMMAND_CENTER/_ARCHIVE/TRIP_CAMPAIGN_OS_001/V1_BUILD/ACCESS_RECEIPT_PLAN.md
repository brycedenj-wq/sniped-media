# ACCESS RECEIPT PLAN · open a real room and prove it

This is the concrete, runnable plan for the one thing the trip exists to do: open a real room and produce a receipt that proves it. It upgrades `v0_parts/strategy.md` §2 and carries the cold-access contingency forward from `v0_parts/strategy.md` §7. The receipt is the load-bearing proof of the whole Campaign Universe Buildout offer, the single thing a creative director, an agency, and an AI operator cannot show (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof section; `INTAKE_FILLED_AND_LEDGER.md` field 4).

Constraints in force: zero spend, zero generation, zero posting, no naming (placeholders only), no crown, no em-dashes, no hype. Face-independent: the built work opens the room, not the operator's face. Nothing sends without the operator (`V1_BUILD/APPROVAL_LIST.md`).

The only blanks are real trip facts: [DESTINATION], [DATES], [CURRENT AUDIENCE], and named targets [TARGET NAME] (downstream of destination).

---

## 1 · What counts as a room

A room is opened when a real person, in the real world, on this trip, takes a real next step traceable to the campaign. Any one of these counts (`v0_parts/strategy.md` §1.2 rung 2; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof set):

- MEETING TAKEN: a real conversation with a decision-maker or a person who can refer one.
- INTRO MADE: a warm node connects the operator to a real decision-maker. A referral to a real decision-maker IS an opened room and logs with its trace.
- CAPTURE GRANTED: a person, venue, or scene grants consented access to capture or build something real.
- CONVERSATION TOWARD A PAID STEP: an in-lane talk that moves toward a real yes or a committed next step the proof-to-cash router would accept.

What does NOT count: a like, a follow, a generic "let's keep in touch," a hallway hello with no next step, or any contact that does not trace to the campaign. Pretty content is not a room.

## 2 · Warmest-first sequence (run in this order)

Work the warmest rooms first; spend the cold-access ladder only when the warm ones do not move (`v0_parts/strategy.md` §2.3, §11.3).

1. ALREADY-WARM IN-LOCATION. Anyone already engaging the operator's content who is in [DESTINATION] during [DATES]. These are the cheapest rooms; touch them first.
2. PRE-WARMED. Targets warmed by one to two weeks of pre-trip engagement (real comments, real reads on their work) before any cold touch. The warming window is set by [DATES] minus the runway.
3. COLD-BUT-FIT. Fit-screened targets reached with an Only-Them reason-to-meet (a fact true only for them, on this trip, in this place).

Cadence rule: a positive reply during the trip gets a response inside 30 minutes (`v0_parts/strategy.md` §11.3). Being on the ground is the lever a remote operator does not have; speed is how it pays off.

Fit screen, run before any target enters the roster (`v0_parts/strategy.md` §3.4; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` client-fit screen): in-lane (the machine actually serves them), can say yes (decision capacity), cares about the outcome with low self-orientation, reachable on the ground during [DATES]. A price-shopper or off-lane target is a PASS, removed before the trip, never discounted during it.

The roster fills under three buckets once [DESTINATION] lands: PEOPLE (decision-makers who can open or refer a room), BRANDS / FIRMS (in-lane orgs with a moment coming: a launch, a drop, a tour), VENUES / PLACES / SCENES (physical rooms or events that are themselves the access point). Names stay [TARGET NAME = UNKNOWN] until destination sets them.

## 3 · The value-first micro-offer (what earns the meeting)

Never lead with a generic "can we meet." Each target gets a value-first reason-to-meet built for them, and that micro-offer is the CTA. The meeting is the pivot after they engage (`v0_parts/strategy.md` §11.4; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` why-this-beats-an-agency, Only-Them test).

The micro-offer, in shape: a short, specific, free piece built for THEIR thing (a teardown of where their rollout is leaving reach on the table, a frame for their moment, a single biggest-gap map), delivered before any ask for a call. If it lands, the conversation moves to the meeting. If it does not, they keep the map and the operator walks. No method-leak: the micro-offer shows the result, never the kitchen (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` no-selling-the-kitchen).

The ten concrete first-touch angles that carry this micro-offer live in the content half (`V1_BUILD/OUTREACH_10.md`, upgrading `v0_parts/strategy.md` §4). The first-touch copy gate every angle obeys: single CTA, under 80 words, no link, plain text, outcome-first, no hype, no em-dashes (`v0_parts/strategy.md` §3.3, §11.4).

## 4 · Cold-access contingency (the 5-rung ladder, carried from v0)

Carried forward from `v0_parts/strategy.md` §7. This is the play for the hardest case: a cold-but-fit target who never engages the pre-warm, where there is no warm node to make an intro. Warmest-first covers the easy rooms; this covers the hard one so the load-bearing proof leg has a real fallback instead of hope.

TRIGGER (binds to the existing daily honesty gate, no new metric): the end-of-day gate reports no room moved for two days running, OR the mid-trip mark is reached with zero rooms opened. Either fires the ladder. Do not wait for the last day. The mid-trip mark is set by [DATES].

The ladder, run in order. Every rung is value-first, on the ground, face-independent, never spam, never fabrication.

1. GO WHERE THE SCENE GATHERS. Stop working the inbox. Be physically in the room the target is already in (the venue, the event, the spot, the scene). Presence on the ground is the one lever a remote operator does not have. No pitch on contact; a real reason to be there.
2. THE SHOW-DONT-ASK WALK-IN. Carry a tangible built piece (a printed one-pager, a phone-ready short cut, the frame of THEIR thing already started). The ask becomes "I already started building this for you, want to see it," not "can I have your time." The artifact does the talking, so it works with no face on camera.
3. CAPTURE THE MICRO-CONTACT, SAME-DAY FOLLOW-UP. From any real-world contact, take the smallest next step (a handle, a "send it to me," a name). Send the value within hours while it is warm: single CTA, no link, copy-gate clean. Same-day cadence is the difference between a hallway hello and an opened room.
4. DOWNGRADE THE ASK, NEVER THE PRICE. If the big room will not open, take the smaller real yes: a 10-minute coffee, a DM thread that keeps moving, or a referral to the person who can open it. A referral to a real decision-maker IS an opened room and logs with its trace. This is scope-trade applied to access (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` trade-scope-not-price), never a discount.
5. THE HONEST STOP. If the full ladder runs and no room moves by the trip's end, that is a real result, not a content problem. Log it as "access leg did not fire." Do not fabricate a room. Do not post a fake receipt. A failed access leg is the truth: the offer is not yet proven, and the next attempt needs a warmer pre-trip runway or a different target set. The honesty is part of the moat; a faked receipt would destroy the one thing competitors cannot show (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof section).

Guardrails on the whole ladder: value-first only, no mass-DM, no fabrication, consent required for any capture, face-independent, nothing posts or sends without the operator. The contingency protects the access leg; it never lowers the offer floor.

## 5 · The receipt spec (what gets logged per opened room)

Every opened room gets one row logged the same day, in the proof dashboard CSV (`V1_BUILD/PROOF_DASHBOARD.md`). The receipt is the proof; if it is not logged with its trace, it does not count. Fields per room:

- ROOM_ID: a sequential id (R001, R002, ...).
- DATE: the date the room opened (within [DATES]).
- WHAT_IT_WAS: which kind of room (meeting taken / intro made / capture granted / conversation toward a paid step).
- WHO: the person, brand, or venue, and their role / decision capacity (placeholder until real: [TARGET NAME]). Real identifying detail is logged locally only, never posted.
- COHORT: which cohort they fell in (moment-holder / under-built / venue-scene gatekeeper).
- CAMPAIGN_TRACE: the exact outreach angle, post, or built artifact that produced the room. This is the line that proves it traces to the campaign, not to luck. No trace, no valid receipt.
- WARMTH_PATH: which rung opened it (already-warm / pre-warmed / cold-but-fit / cold-access ladder rung 1 to 4).
- NEXT_PAID_STEP: the committed next step toward a paid yes (proposal to send, follow-up call booked, scope to scope), or "none yet."
- CONSENT: yes / not-applicable. Required for any capture.
- STATUS: open / advancing / stalled / closed-won / closed-lost.

A receipt is only valid when WHAT_IT_WAS, WHO, DATE, and CAMPAIGN_TRACE are all real and filled. A row missing the trace is not a receipt; it is a contact.

---

## 6 · How to run it on the ground (the daily loop)

- Morning: confirm today's warmest target and the value-first micro-offer built for them. Send only on the operator's go.
- Midday: respond to any positive reply inside 30 minutes. Move a warm reply toward a meeting; move a meeting toward a next paid step.
- On contact: capture the micro-contact, take the smallest real next step, follow up same-day with the value.
- End of day: run the honesty gate. Did a room open? Log the receipt row with its trace, or log "no room moved." Two no-move days, or the mid-trip mark at zero, fires the cold-access ladder.
- Throughout: no fabrication, consent for any capture, nothing posts or sends without the operator.

The room is the point. The content is the byproduct. The receipt is the proof.
