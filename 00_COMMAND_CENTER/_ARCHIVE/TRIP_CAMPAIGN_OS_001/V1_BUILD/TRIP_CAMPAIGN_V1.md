## Campaign thesis (concrete now)

The trip is a wedge, not the product. It converts physical presence in one place into opened rooms, real relationships, and a proof artifact, on a lane the operator already owns (`v0_parts/strategy.md` §1.1; `BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md` §4, §5).

The offer the trip runs is Campaign Universe Buildout: take any real input a person brings (a trip, a concert, an artist, a brand, an idea, a script, a character, a world, a launch) and build the whole machine around it, concept through proof dashboard, handed over built and owned (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` commercial thesis). The buyer is an artist or concert with a moment coming, a brand with a launch, or a founder with a world and no machine, screened in-lane, can-say-yes, cares-about-the-outcome, low-self-orientation; a price-shopper is a pass (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` who-this-serves; `INTAKE_FILLED_AND_LEDGER.md` field 5). The purpose of this specific trip is narrow: it is a proof cell. Its job is to open one real room and produce one built campaign artifact that proves the offer (`INTAKE_FILLED_AND_LEDGER.md` field 3).

Thesis sentence (only the place and named target remain open):

> On this trip, the operator shows up in [DESTINATION] as the one who can build the whole campaign machine around a real input, so [TARGET NAME = an artist / brand / founder with a real moment coming] gets their moment turned into a built, owned campaign machine, and that opened room becomes the access receipt that proves the offer.

Three properties the thesis must hold (each gates the copy):

- Outcome-first. Leads with the target's result (their moment turned into a built, owned campaign), never the trip or a bio (`v0_parts/strategy.md` §2.2; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` concept-and-positioning).
- In-lane and scope-traded. Built on the premium lane (campaign-house, world, IP), anchored high, scope traded never price. The 10k is the proven floor, the 25k is the number the market entertained, 50k+ is the owned-world anchor (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` tier sections; `INTAKE_FILLED_AND_LEDGER.md` field 4).
- Face-independent. Works if the operator's face is swapped for a model, character, or world. The access receipt, not a face, is the load-bearing proof (`v0_parts/strategy.md` §2.5).

The public thesis sells the present, concrete reason-to-meet. It never announces a bigger build, never claims a platform, registry, network, or AI company, never carries fabricated proof, never sells the kitchen (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` what-must-not-be-promised).

## The locked offer, in one frame

From `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` and `INTAKE_FILLED_AND_LEDGER.md` field 4. This is the spine; the full detail and the deliverable list live in `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md`.

- OUTCOME SOLD: a real moment turned into a built, owned campaign machine. Not deliverables, the outcome.
- 10k (proven floor): the full campaign brain plus one scoped hero asset set, single-operator deliverable.
- 25k (the number the market entertained): fuller assets, a world bible that keeps producing after handover, a multi-cohort outreach system, a live dashboard, a partner-grade deck, and hands-on operator time.
- 50k+ (the high anchor): an owned-world / IP-class build plus a retainer, set per job.
- The rule that protects all three: anchor high, trade scope, never trade price (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` trade-scope-not-price).

## The BASEPLATE fork (flagged, not active)

A second, older offer lane exists: BASEPLATE, a B2B dossier built for staffing firms at $2,500 to $4,000 (`INTAKE_FILLED_AND_LEDGER.md` ledger A1; `BASEPLATE_CANONICAL_STATEMENT.md`). V1 is built on Campaign Universe Buildout, not BASEPLATE.

If the operator says "this trip is a BASEPLATE B2B dossier trip," the redirect swaps three layers only and leaves the engine intact:

- OFFER layer: Campaign Universe Buildout becomes the BASEPLATE dossier, price re-anchors to $2,500 to $4,000.
- TARGET layer: artist / brand / founder becomes staffing-firm decision-makers.
- COPY layer: the outreach angles re-point to the staffing-firm problem.

What does NOT change on a redirect: the access-receipt logic, the warmest-first sequence, the cold-access ladder, the proof dashboard structure, and the approval boundary. The room-opening machine is the same machine. Only the offer / target / price layer moves. Do not treat BASEPLATE as the active offer; it is the flagged alternative.

## The one rule, in voice

Every day of this trip, the question is not what to post. It is what room got opened, and whether it traces to this campaign. The posts are the byproduct. The room is the point. The cold-access ladder is the plan for the day no room moved, so the proof leg has a real path, not a hope.
