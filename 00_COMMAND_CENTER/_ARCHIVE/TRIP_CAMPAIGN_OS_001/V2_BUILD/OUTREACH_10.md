# TRIP CAMPAIGN OS 001 · V2 · FIRST 10 DM / COLD-EMAIL MESSAGES

V2 of the outreach set. Same offer, same cohorts, same value-first law as V1. The only change is the copy now runs on a canonical variable system so the moment the operator drops in destination, dates, audience, and a screened target, every message goes live with no rewrite.

The offer is locked and written concrete. Campaign Universe Buildout, 10k floor. You bring the real input with real taste behind it, and the OS builds the whole campaign around it. The outcome sold is a real moment turned into a built, owned campaign machine.

Every message is value-first. The first touch leads with a free, specific value drop, a teardown or a map of the single biggest gap in how their thing is being rolled out, not a cold "can we meet." The micro-offer is the CTA. The meeting is the pivot after they engage. Every message drives toward an opened room.

Three cohorts. COHORT A is the moment-holder, an artist or concert or brand with a dated moment coming. COHORT B is the under-built, real taste and real input, no machine, currently doing every job themselves. COHORT C is the venue or scene gatekeeper, whose room is the access point.

---

## VARIABLE KEY

Use these EXACT tokens. Everything not in this list is written concrete because it is known.

- `{DEST}` : the destination. Where the trip and the proof cell run.
- `{DATES}` : the trip window, start and end.
- `{AUDIENCE}` : the current audience. Platforms, rough size, warmth.
- `{TARGET}` : the single screened person, brand, or venue this message goes to.
- `{THEIR_THING}` : the target's real input. Their idea, world, concert, product, or launch.
- `{THEIR_MOMENT}` : the target's dated moment. The launch, show, or drop with a date on it.
- `{DAY_N}` : the relative day index inside the trip window.

Written concrete (known, never a token): the offer, the 10k floor, the buyer framing, the value-first micro-offer (the free teardown or gap-map), the proof-cell purpose, the cohort definitions.

Copy gate held on all ten: under 80 words each, single CTA, no link, plain text. Zero em-dashes and zero look-alikes, zero hype words, no bible or spec voice tells. Every message ends on terminal punctuation. Operator voice, raw, plain, declarative, calm authority. None send until the operator approves.

## STANDING NOTES
- Every message is value-first. The micro-offer (a free teardown or a map of the single biggest gap in how their thing is being rolled out) is the CTA. The meeting is the pivot after they engage, never the cold ask.
- Mix held: DMs (1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9) run short. Cold emails (5, 6, 10) carry a subject line and one more sentence of context. Each stays under 80 words.
- Single CTA each, no link, plain text. The cohort and channel are labeled on every message.
- Angle 10 writes about the operational problem the moment creates (keeping it producing after the date), not the trigger itself.
- The friend-deal anecdote and any market reference are kept out of outreach entirely. The value drop and the offer carry it.
- Face-independent. The built work and the value drop open the room, never the operator's face.
- Variables used: `{DEST}`, `{DATES}`, `{TARGET}`, `{THEIR_THING}`, `{THEIR_MOMENT}`. `{AUDIENCE}` and `{DAY_N}` are defined in the key for the operator's reference but are not needed in these ten first-touch messages, which open cold or post-warm rather than speak to the existing following. Everything about the offer, micro-offer, cohorts, and proof cell is written concrete.
- No spend, no generation, no posting, no naming, no crown.
