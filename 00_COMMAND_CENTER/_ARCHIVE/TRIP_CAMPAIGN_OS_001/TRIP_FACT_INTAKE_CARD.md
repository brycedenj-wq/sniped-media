# TRIP FACT INTAKE CARD

Fill the blank after each prompt. Nine facts. Five minutes. These are the only facts the campaign v0 is waiting on. Everything else is already built around them. Source: the FACTS I NEED blocks in `sections/strategy.md` and `sections/ops.md`.

Rules while filling: real facts only, write UNKNOWN if you genuinely do not know yet, no placeholder names invented for you.

---

1. DESTINATION
   Where are you going (city / region / country)?
   >

2. DATES
   Start date, end date, and how many days on the ground?
   >

3. PURPOSE
   One line: why you are going and what end-state counts as a win.
   >

4. OFFER / OUTCOME
   What are you offering or selling on this trip, and the anchor price? (or which tier from the offer prototype)
   >

5. TARGET PEOPLE / BRANDS / VENUES
   Who are you trying to open a room with? Type (people, brands, venues) and a one-line buyer profile. Names if you have them.
   >

6. CURRENT AUDIENCE
   Which platforms, rough follower count, how warm?
   >

7. PUBLIC BANNER
   Under what name / identity does this run? (practice wrapper, personal, or a placeholder for now)
   >

8. BUDGET CEILING
   Zero-spend, or a dollar / credit ceiling you will authorize? Any outreach tooling or CRM already live?
   >

9. HARD NO'S
   Anything off the table (your face, specific surfaces, named people not to contact, spend lines)?
   >

---

Once these nine land, the strategy half (`v0_parts/strategy.md`) and the offer prototype lock to concrete, and the calendar, target list, and outreach copy stop being parameterized.
