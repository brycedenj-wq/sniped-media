# TRIP CAMPAIGN OS 001 · MASTER PREFLIGHT PACKET

Preflight planning packet. Zero spend, zero generation, zero posting, zero naming (placeholders only), no crown. This is the master index over the four section files and the consolidated fact block that turns the whole campaign concrete.

## MISSION

Run the full refinery for the operator's upcoming TRIP as a real operating campaign across every lane: copy, cold email, DMs, social media, clothes, branding, world/universe, storytelling, scripts, visual direction, outreach, content planning, and execution. This is not keeper-lab R&D. It is a live campaign scaffold built from cited OS sources, ready to go concrete the moment the trip facts land.

The OS frame that governs the whole thing: the trip is not the deliverable. The trip is a wedge that opens rooms and earns the right to build something larger on a lane the operator already owns. Success is a staged proof ladder with a money definition, never a vibe. The deliverable is an OWNED audience-facing world/output built FROM the corpus, never the corpus repackaged.

### The commercial spine (read OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md)

The trip is the PROOF CELL for the paid play, Campaign Universe Buildout: bring any real input with real taste (a concert, trip, artist, brand, idea, script, world, launch) and the OS builds the whole campaign machine around it. The market is real and already transacting (a friend paying 10k for a concert package after a 25k-to-10k walk). The offer, the 10k / 25k / 50k-plus tiers, the edge over a creative director and an agency and a random AI operator, the proof the trip must produce before it sells, and what must not be promised yet all live in `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md`. That offer was run through the wired W4 business gate and W2 copy gate and passes (sells the meal not the kitchen, value-based three-option anchor-high pricing, explicit client-fit fields, clean copy). The trip lanes below are how the proof gets produced.

### The BJ-FACE-INDEPENDENT constraint (stated correctly)

The campaign must NOT make the operator's real face the load-bearing reason it works. This is NOT a faceless rule. Faces are allowed: real models, fictional or animated characters, faces in generated worlds, plus wardrobe, objects, places, POV, hands, and body. The operator's own voice, writing, taste, strategy, and story are fully in play and are the actual spine. His real face can become optional later only on his explicit approval. "Faceless" must never be used as a blocker, and faces must never be blocked entirely. The campaign is engineered so recognition rides a compound stack (object + place + number + one-color law + author-eye + voice), so any face is free to appear without being load-bearing.

## GATE LEGEND

- W1 source-activation: a real OS source must fire and become a cited token, feature, build decision, or rejection, not just get read.
- W2 copy gate: copy is clean, no em-dashes, no hype, follows Caples/voice doctrine, rule-of-one, one awareness stage per asset.
- W3 rubric: the asset scores against its rubric before it is allowed forward.
- W4 business gate: money-play laws hold (owned output not ingredient resale, doctrine + tool basis shown, readiness honest, payment follows proof, employer-safe, no crown).
- W5 pixel-activation: applies ONLY where pixels or generated visuals matter (vision-reject + no-self-crown verify before any frame ships).

W1 to W4 apply across the WHOLE campaign. W5 applies only to the lanes that produce pixels or generated visuals.

## BIGGEST RISK

The single largest risk is the same one the OS kill list names: the campaign reads as "travel content" to the people who matter instead of as proof or a real offer, and never converts past one-off content (a vacation with a camera). The structural defenses are in place (wedge framing, the money definition of success, the in-lane fit screen, the offer-first outreach law, the open-loop story spine, and the W4 kill criteria), but they only bite once Facts 3, 4, and 5 (purpose/goal, what is offered, who he is reaching) are concrete. Until those three land, the campaign cannot be screened for the failure it is most likely to fall into, so those three facts are the highest-leverage blanks in the entire packet.
