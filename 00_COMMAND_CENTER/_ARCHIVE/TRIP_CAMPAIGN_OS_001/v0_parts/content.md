## PART 1 · CONTENT PILLARS (the few repeatable angles)

Five pillars. Every post is one pillar. If a post is not one of these five, it does not ship. This is brandvisual §4.2 recognition-by-repetition (#77 SIGNATURE_REPETITION_RECALL) plus content §8.2 slot logic, made concrete.

- **Pillar 1 · THE BUILD (proof + authority).** The actual work being done on the ground, taught as a process. The load-bearing pillar. Maps to content §8.2 Slot 2 work-beat. This is the pillar that proves the offer (OFFER_PROTOTYPE "the built campaign itself becomes the portfolio piece"). Format default: carousel or short video. Recognition: the [OBJECT] appears in-frame in a new state. Access: the work shown is the reason a real person says yes to a room.
- **Pillar 2 · THE ROOM (access + the receipt).** The opened room itself, handled honestly. A meeting taken, an intro made, a capture granted, a conversation that moved. This is the load-bearing proof of the whole campaign (OFFER_PROTOTYPE: the access leg is the single thing a CD/agency/AI operator cannot show). Format: short video or single-frame proof + field-note caption. NEVER fabricate; if no room opened that day, this pillar is silent. Recognition rides the [THRESHOLD] (the place-edge where the room opened). Access: this pillar IS the access receipt, captured as it happens.
- **Pillar 3 · THE READ (taste + judgment).** One reality-check or systems-reframe line applied to something observed that day (content §9.1 voice signatures; §8.2 Slot 3). Cheap, high-credibility, face-free, voice-led. This is the operator-layer moat showing up as content (OFFER_PROTOTYPE "the moat is the operator layer, the taste, the judgment"). Format: text-only post or story. Recognition rides voice + one-color law. Access: positions the operator as the person worth opening a room for.
- **Pillar 4 · THE OBJECT (the running motif).** A frame built around the [OBJECT] at a [THRESHOLD], its state advanced one notch (brandvisual §6.4 object-state verb, §7.4 Image System). Silent, never captioned as meaningful. This is pure recognition fuel. Format: single-frame proof. Recognition: the entire point. Access: low-direct, high-compounding (it is what makes the campaign one recognizable thing instead of scattered posts).
- **Pillar 5 · THE HAND-RAISE (the close).** The soft ask. One low-friction CTA that moves a watcher toward a reply, a DM, a call, an off-platform list (content §8.2 Slot 4; §10.5 DM lane). The only pillar allowed to ask for something. Format: story with single CTA, or text post. Recognition rides voice. Access: this pillar is the funnel mouth, every other pillar feeds it.

Pillar ratio inside a 7-day week (from content §8.3 Caples small-and-frequent law): roughly 40% Pillar 3+4 (small/frequent, cheap presence), 40% Pillar 1+2 (proof, where the value lives), 20% Pillar 5 (the close). Tune the exact split once [WHO] and audience size land.

THE OPEN LOOP that threads all five (content §8.4): plant ONE question on Day 1, pay it off only at the end. The question is built from [GOAL]: "Will [SUBJECT] [GOAL] in [DEST]?" Every Pillar-1 and Pillar-2 post advances it one notch without resolving it. Resolution is the final-day Pillar-2 post.

## PART 2 · DAILY RUNBOOK (the on-trip loop that drives toward the opened room)

The day is built around opening a room, not around making posts. Posts are the byproduct of a day spent earning access. This is content §8.2 four-slot system reordered so access leads and content follows.

Run this loop every Phase-B (on-trip) day. Number of days = UNKNOWN ([DATES/DAYS]); the loop is identical regardless of count.

**Morning (before the work):**
1. Re-read the pinned campaign card (the one-sentence construct + the open-loop question + today's access target). Re-reading the goal each day is the anti-drift rule. 5 minutes.
2. Post the morning signal (Pillar 3, content §8.2 Slot 1): one sentence of intent + one visual. Keeps the open loop alive. This is the only scheduled-time post.
3. Name today's ACCESS TARGET in one line: who is the one room you are trying to open today, and the one ask that opens it. If there is no access target, the day is a content day, not a campaign day, flag it (OFFER_PROTOTYPE: the access leg is primary, not a nice-to-have).

**Midday (the work + the room):**
4. Do the actual work ([GOAL]-dependent). Capture it as you go for Pillar 1. The capture is a byproduct; the work is the point.
5. Make the access move. Take the meeting, make the intro, ask for the capture, have the conversation. THIS IS THE LOAD-BEARING ACTION OF THE DAY.
6. The moment a room opens, log the receipt immediately in the proof dashboard (who, what was said, what moved toward a paid step, traceable to the campaign). The receipt is the asset, not the post about it.

**Evening (turn the day into recognition + funnel):**
7. Build the work beat (Pillar 1) from midday capture: carousel or short video, taught as process. Run it through the shot card (Part 3) and the Entry card (brandvisual §6.5) before it ships.
8. If a room opened, build the Room beat (Pillar 2) honestly from the receipt, with consent. If none opened, skip, do not fabricate.
9. Drop one Read (Pillar 3) on something observed today.
10. Drop one Hand-raise (Pillar 5): single low-friction ask routed to the off-platform list (content §10.5, §7.6 list ownership). One CTA only.
11. Advance the open loop one notch. Do not resolve it.

**End-of-day gate (60 seconds, the honesty check):**
- Did a real room move today, yes or no? (If no for 2 days running, the daily loop is failing its primary job, escalate the access plan, do not just post more.)
- Did every shipped post carry the compound stack (object/place/number/one-color/voice)? If a post carried none, it was vanity, cut it next time.
- Is the receipt logged with a traceable line to the campaign?

UNKNOWN per day: platform routing of each slot (which post goes to IG vs TikTok vs LinkedIn vs email), set once [WHO] + audience split land. The loop is platform-agnostic.

## PART 3 · SHOT LIST (named shot types + the lighting / author-eye rule each)

Six named shot types. Each maps to a pillar, declares a lens band, a lighting rig, and the author-eye rule it must pass (brandvisual §6.1 seven tokens, §6.2 lighting cards, §6.3 lens discipline). FACE-INDEPENDENT: the subject slot accepts a real model, a character, a world-figure, or BJ body-led, the shot works on any of them because recognition rides object + place + light + frame structure, not a face.

Every shot, regardless of type, must clear the author-eye HARD gate (brandvisual §6.1): one tonal family, graphic-over-atmospheric (shape and color-block, not a dark moody haze), rim/separation present (no flat dark-on-dark), in-between not posed-and-held, crop between joints not on them, pore-safe skin if a face appears.

- **SHOT A · THE BUILD FRAME (Pillar 1).** The work mid-action. Lens: 50-85mm mid. Rig: `Creative Window Light` (the realistic travel rig, controllable daylight at [DEST]) with a reflector or window edge supplying the mandatory rim. Author-eye rule: in-between direction (caught working, never smile-and-hold); hands given a real task (the work itself). Recognition: the [OBJECT] is in frame in its working state.
- **SHOT B · THE ROOM FRAME (Pillar 2).** The opened room / the person / the threshold of access. Lens: 35mm wide (environmental, shows the real place) or 50mm if it is a two-person frame. Rig: available daylight, `Standard Couples` framing logic if two subjects. Author-eye rule: the [THRESHOLD] is the structural element; graphic composition over mood. Consent required if a real person appears. Recognition: rides the [THRESHOLD].
- **SHOT C · THE OBJECT CARD (Pillar 4).** The [OBJECT] alone, state advanced. Lens: 85-135mm tight, or 50mm for a clean product read. Rig: `White Background (2-Light Umbrella)` if a clean card is possible on the trip, otherwise a single window + sweep. Author-eye rule: luminous, one-color law strict, the object is the single subject. Recognition: this is the purest recognition frame in the system.
- **SHOT D · THE FACE / EDITORIAL FRAME (Pillar 1 or 3, optional subject).** Only if a subject ([SUBJECT]) is on camera. Lens: 85-135mm tight, near-face color is everything. Rig: `One Light 45 Degree Beauty Dish` (sculpted, travel-friendly, single light) with rim placed BEFORE key (brandvisual §6.2, the known weak spot). Author-eye rule: chin forward-and-down for jawline, pore-safe clinical retouch, rim separation mandatory. NOT BJ-face-load-bearing: this frame supports recognition, the object/place/voice still carry it if no face is present.
- **SHOT E · THE LOCATION DAYLIGHT WIDE (Pillar 1 or 4).** The corner of [DEST] (one corner, per the limitation principle, brandvisual §4.5), not the postcard. Lens: 35mm wide. Rig: real location daylight, golden-hour or open-shade for skin truth. Author-eye rule: must NOT be the destination cliche (the forbidden-cliche list for [DEST] is written before shooting, brandvisual §4.5); footwear/silhouette correct if a subject is present. Recognition: the [THRESHOLD] or [OBJECT] anchors it so it reads as the campaign, not a travel snap.
- **SHOT F · THE TEXT / NO-IMAGE BEAT (Pillar 3 or 5).** The cheapest, fully face-free format (content §9.4: all-type can out-pull illustrated when the hook is strong). No rig, no lens. Author-eye rule: voice carries it; the one-color law applies to any background card. Recognition: rides voice + the numbered-Entry lockup.

THE ENTRY CARD (brandvisual §6.5) is the per-shot readiness gate. A shot is not ready until every line fills:
```
ENTRY [NNN] | day/stop [X] of [DEST]
  pillar: [1 BUILD | 2 ROOM | 3 READ | 4 OBJECT | 5 HAND-RAISE]
  shot type: [A | B | C | D | E | F]
  lane: [elevated_minimal | streetwear_luxe | tailored_modern]
  lens: [35 wide | 50-85 mid | 85-135 tight | n/a text]
  rig: [named lighting card]
  subject: [model | character | world-figure | BJ body-led | none]  (face NOT load-bearing)
  object-state: [OBJECT] is [carried | set down | passed through THRESHOLD]
  palette: [BASE] + [INK] + [ACCENT once]
  deposit: [the one new real thing this Entry shows]
  access-link: [how this post drives toward an opened room]
  field-note: [<= one short withholding line, no grade/gesture/key]
```

UNKNOWN: [DEST] (sets cliche list + location daylight), [OBJECT]/[THRESHOLD] (the recognition anchors), [SUBJECT] (whether D/face frames apply), [KIT ON HAND] (whether the 2-light object card or 5-light is even possible, default to the single beauty-dish + window-light travel rigs).

## PART 4 · STYLING DIRECTION (outfit-intent + capsule logic, on whatever subject appears)

Styling is the spine on ANY subject (brandvisual §5.0). If no subject is on camera (object-led days), styling logic still governs the [OBJECT]'s presentation and the color story. This section is concrete now; only the climate-driven specifics wait on [DEST].

**The frozen SUBJECT_READ (brandvisual §5.1).** Author once, freeze, inherit on every styled frame. Fields to fill the moment [SUBJECT] is known: proportion (torso/leg, shoulder/hip, frame size, neck length), coloring (skin undertone, hair tone, eyes, drives every near-face color pick), comfort band (posture + energy, within 20% of the subject's real baseline so it reads real not costume), natural baseline (what this subject would actually wear in [DEST]'s climate), must-not-wear (an explicit exclusion list, any listed item in frame = reject). Reject on proportion drift across the set, coloring unstated, costume-drift beyond one step.

**Every outfit is a five-word sentence + one lane (brandvisual §5.2).** Per look: `intent: "[5 words or fewer]"; lane: [elevated_minimal | streetwear_luxe | tailored_modern]; focal: [one piece]`. One focal point per look (#23 ONE_THING_DOES_THE_TALKING). Switching lanes across the trip is an authored chapter-shift, never a drift. The lane palette is set by [DEST] climate: hot/coastal leans elevated-minimal lightweight; cold/urban carries tailored-modern layering.

**The capsule, not a suitcase (brandvisual §5.4).** Author the trip wardrobe as `[N looks] from [M pieces]`, every piece assigned a job, keystone pieces (the 20% doing 80%) over-indexed, the whole capsule locked to ONE tonal family (the one-color law, §4.3) so the color story flows across every look with no palette-bounce. This is also a budget protection (W4): a capsule is asset-light vs over-packing. Build order per look: bottoms, top, shoe, outerwear, accent last. High-leverage spend goes to footwear and the climate-appropriate carrying layer (jacket/overshirt/knit), where premium fabric is non-negotiable (#44, #30).

**The one-color law for the whole campaign (brandvisual §4.3).** Lock before any asset: `[BASE]` warm-neutral (cream to sand) + `[INK]` single readable ink + `[ACCENT]` at most one restrained accent, appearing once per frame. [DEST]'s real light informs the base (desert reads sand, coast reads cool-cream) but the law holds regardless. Reject: more than one unrelated color family, a yellowed/neon base, a second hero color.

**Mannequin bar (brandvisual §5.3).** Every garment: shoulder seam at the bone, one waistline, named heavy natural material (no plastic/thin), max three colors with a concept, a cast lane-correct shoe (named, never "shoes"), one accessory anchor (two max), no loud logo near the face, pressed and lint-free with conditioned shoes. Must read fresher than a flagship-window mannequin.

UNKNOWN: [SUBJECT] (whose read to freeze), [DEST] climate (sets lane + material weight + natural baseline), [WARDROBE ON HAND], [BASE]/[INK]/[ACCENT] values. Note: if a garment is ever generated, lock it from a real product photo via 2-image edit, never freehand (brandvisual §5.5, garment-lock law); a real subject on the real trip sidesteps this entirely.

## PART 5 · FIRST 10 POSTS / SCRIPTS (hook + format + caption pattern, in voice)

Ten concrete post patterns covering the trip arc (content §8.1 three-phase: Before / During / After). Each has a hook (from content §9.1 library, Caples headline doctrine), a format (content §9.4), and a caption pattern in the operator's voice (content §9.3, calm-authority register, no em-dashes, complete thoughts, no hype). UNKNOWN slots are bracketed and resolve from the 7 intake blanks. Discipline rule on every important post: write 12-25 hooks, ship the strongest, never the first (content §9.5).

EVERY post below serves recognition (compound stack) AND drives toward access (an opened room or the funnel that opens one). None are vanity.

**Phase A · BEFORE (coil the spring, plant the loop, pre-warm the rooms)**

POST 1 · The decision (Pillar 3, open the loop).
- Hook (story/inciting-incident shape): "[Time marker], I decided to [GOAL] in [DEST]. Here is what is actually at stake."
- Format: text-only or single-frame proof. Opens on the becoming, not the destination-as-scenery (brandvisual §7.2; corrects the-meantime-001 law).
- Caption pattern: control line (the decision, flat and declarative). Insight (why now, the real stake, operator register). Impact (what changes if it works, what breaks if it does not, this is the open-loop question). Soft tag, no CTA yet.
- Access job: plants the question that makes people watch; the people watching are the first rooms.

POST 2 · The construct (Pillar 1, state the one sentence).
- Hook (systems-reframe): "This is not a trip. It is [the one-sentence construct]."
- Format: carousel, each slide one subhead (content §9.4). Slide 1 is the hook.
- Caption: short, declarative, no hype. States what the campaign is in one sentence (brandvisual §4.1 construct filter). Ends on the open loop.
- Access job: anyone who self-selects into the construct is a qualified room.

POST 3 · The pre-warm (Pillar 5, warm the people at the destination).
- Hook (selector, Caples +20% response): "[WHO] in [DEST]: [specific outcome they want]."
- Format: story with single CTA, or DM lane (content §10.5).
- Caption: Mom-Test framing, talk about their problem not the pitch (content §10.5). One low-friction ask: a reply, a "want me to swing by," a micro-offer.
- Access job: this is the literal room-opener, sent before arrival so a meeting is set on the ground.

**Phase B · DURING (one authored beat per day, escalating)**

POST 4 · Day-1 build (Pillar 1, the work begins).
- Hook (how-to, Caples' most durable): "How to [outcome WHO wants] without [the thing they dread]."
- Format: short video, structure from content §9.2 (hook in 1-2s, one insight, cause-effect chain, reality-check turn, soft CTA / next-day tag).
- Caption pattern A: control (what got built today). Insight (the one move that mattered). Impact (why it matters to [WHO]). Soft CTA to the list.
- Access job: the build is the proof a real person needs to grant the next room.

POST 5 · The first room (Pillar 2, the access receipt, honest).
- Hook (news/announcing): "[Something real that happened in a room today]."
- Format: short video or single-frame at the [THRESHOLD], with consent.
- Caption: field-note plain, withholding (no grade/gesture/key, brandvisual §7.6). States what moved, traceable to the campaign, no overclaim. This is the receipt as content.
- Access job: this IS the load-bearing proof; it also signals to other prospects that rooms open here.

POST 6 · The read (Pillar 3, taste on something observed).
- Hook (reality-check): "Most people [do the obvious thing in DEST/in this work]. Here is where it breaks."
- Format: text-only (the cheapest face-free format).
- Caption: skeleton B, one reality-check line + (optional) one visual, no CTA or one micro-ask.
- Access job: positions the operator-layer judgment that makes a room worth opening.

POST 7 · The object (Pillar 4, the running motif).
- Hook: none needed; the image carries it. The [OBJECT] at a [THRESHOLD], state advanced one notch.
- Format: single-frame proof (Shot C).
- Caption: one short field-note line, never captioned as meaningful (brandvisual §7.4 image system, handle with invisibility).
- Access job: pure recognition; compounds the campaign into one thing people remember and refer rooms to.

POST 8 · The midpoint escalation (Pillar 1, raise the stakes).
- Hook (curiosity + self-interest fused): "The [surprising thing] about [GOAL/DEST] that [WHO] keeps missing."
- Format: carousel, one insight per slide, the progressive-complication beat (brandvisual §7.3, a harder action than Day 1, never a flat repeat).
- Caption: pattern A, control-insight-impact, advances the open loop toward its hardest point.
- Access job: shows the operator handling the hard thing, the exact evidence a higher-tier room wants.

**Phase C · AFTER (the harvest, the payoff, the close)**

POST 9 · The payoff (Pillar 2, resolve the open loop, the Key Image).
- Hook (story-turn): "[Time marker] in [DEST]. [The stake]. Then [the turn]."
- Format: short video or the single Key Image (brandvisual §7.5, the [OBJECT] at the decisive [THRESHOLD]). Penultimate beat should carry the OPPOSITE charge of this one so the finale lands full.
- Caption: pattern A, resolves the Day-1 question honestly. States what the trip actually produced, including the opened room and the receipt, no hype, no crown.
- Access job: the resolution is the case-study spine; it converts watchers who waited the whole arc.

POST 10 · The hand-raise / harvest (Pillar 5, the close).
- Hook (self-interest direct): "If you have [a real input and real taste], here is what I just proved you can build around it."
- Format: text or carousel; single strong CTA to the off-platform list / a call (content §7.6 list ownership, §10.6 trip-bounded deadline is real not manufactured).
- Caption: pattern A. Control (what the trip proved). Insight (the offer in one line, the meal not the kitchen, no guaranteed outcomes, OFFER_PROTOTYPE guardrails). Impact (what it could do for them). One CTA. Reference the market only lightly: campaign packages are a thing people pay for. The offer stands on the execution and the opened room just shown, NOT on any one anecdote.
- Access job: this is the funnel mouth; it turns the whole arc's earned attention into booked conversations.

Caption HALT on all ten (content §9.3 NORTH_STAR_FOUR_GATE): ship only if clear, structured, sounds like real field experience, an operator would respect it. No em-dashes. No motivational filler, no "so basically," no "it is important to understand that." No hype words. No crown.

UNKNOWN across all ten: [GOAL], [DEST], [WHO], [OFFER], [OBJECT]/[THRESHOLD], [SUBJECT], plus one real proof point per audience (content FACTS #11) and the single dread/pain [WHO] has (content FACTS #12).

## PART 6 · DEAD-SIMPLE INTAKE CARD (fill these 7, the v0 goes concrete)

Hand this to the operator. Seven fields. Filling them resolves every UNKNOWN above. Keep answers to one line each.

```

1. DEST       : where is the trip (city/region/country)

  - WARDROBE + CLIMATE : pieces you have + destination climate (sets the capsule + lane)
  - AUDIENCE : current following/list size + platform split (sets post routing + ratio)

  - DREAD : the one pain WHO has that your offer removes (sets the how-to hooks)
```

THE ONE RULE on the card, in voice: every day of this trip, the question is not "what do I post." It is "what room did I open, and can I prove it traces to this campaign." The posts are the byproduct. The room is the point.
