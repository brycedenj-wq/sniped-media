# TRIP CAMPAIGN V0 (concrete, built around the unknowns)

This is the runnable v0 of the trip campaign. It is built now, not deferred to "drop the facts." Every determinable thing is concrete (rules, patterns, copy shapes, shot rigs, the daily loop, the proof spine). The only blanks are real trip facts (place, dates, names, the one offer, the object), each marked UNKNOWN with the exact field name. Answer the nine facts in `TRIP_FACT_INTAKE_CARD.md` and every UNKNOWN below resolves to concrete in one pass.

Constraints in force: zero spend, zero generation, zero posting, no naming, no crown, no em-dashes, no hype. Face-independent (real models, characters, world faces, wardrobe, objects, places, POV, hands, body, and the operator's voice, taste, and story are all allowed; his face is never the load-bearing reason it works).

The single load-bearing proof of this whole campaign is an OPENED ROOM plus an ACCESS RECEIPT. Pretty content is not enough. If no room opens and the dashboard does not move, the campaign collapses into a content package. Every part below points at the room.

## How the unknowns resolve

The nine facts on the intake card map one-to-one onto the brackets in the parts:

- DESTINATION  ->  [DEST], [PLACE]: sets the cliche-to-avoid list, the location daylight rig, the venue/scene targets, the climate that picks the wardrobe lane.
- DATES / DAYS  ->  [DATES], [DATES/DAYS]: sets the number of daily-runbook loops, the trip-bounded deadline, the warmest-first window.
- PURPOSE / GOAL  ->  [GOAL]: sets the thesis sentence, the open-loop question, the how-to hooks.
- OFFER / OUTCOME  ->  [OFFER]: sets Post 10, the cohort framing, the proof set the trip must hit.
- TARGET PEOPLE / BRANDS / VENUES  ->  [WHO], [NAMES], [TARGET]: sets the roster, the fit screen inputs, the cohort split, the cold-access scene.
- CURRENT AUDIENCE  ->  audience size / platform split: sets the post routing, the pillar ratio, the TIER 2-4 numeric thresholds.
- PUBLIC BANNER  ->  the construct sentence: sets Post 2 and the one-line frame.
- BUDGET CEILING  ->  the capsule size, the kit-on-hand rigs, any approval-gated spend cap.
- HARD NO'S  ->  the must-not-wear list, the forbidden-cliche list, the off-limits claims.

Until those land, the determinable scaffold stands as built and the brackets stay UNKNOWN, not invented.

## The one rule, in voice

Every day of this trip, the question is not "what do I post." It is "what room did I open, and can I prove it traces to this campaign." The posts are the byproduct. The room is the point. The cold-access ladder (§7) is the plan for the day no room moved, so the proof leg has a real path, not a hope.
