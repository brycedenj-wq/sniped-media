# LA TARGET CATEGORIES · the first 25 (types, not named entities)

The first 25 LA target CATEGORIES the discovery method screens against. These are types, not named people, brands, or venues. No named entities appear here; named targets need operator approval and live discovery (`APPROVAL_LIST.md` gate a, gate e). Each category is a screen for a buyer signature, not a contact list.

For each: the category, the buyer signature (the moment they have coming), why the campaign machine moves it, and one line on where to find them in LA. They feed the fit screen and roster in `LA_TARGET_DISCOVERY_ROSTER_METHOD.md` Steps 1, 3, 4. The offer they are screened against is the 10k Campaign Universe Buildout (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` who-this-serves). Per-target slots stay as variables until discovery fills them: `{TARGET}`, `{THEIR_THING}`, `{THEIR_MOMENT}`.

All entries assume the LA-resident reachability rule: presence is given, the screen is for a dated moment inside the 14-day sprint (`TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V3.md` §A.2; `LA_TARGET_DISCOVERY_ROSTER_METHOD.md` Step 4).

## GROUP 1 · ARTISTS / MUSIC (5)

1. INDIE MUSICIAN WITH A DROP
- Buyer signature: a single, EP, or album with a set release date inside the window, and no campaign wrapped around it.
- Why the machine moves it: a drop is a dated moment with a built-in audience and no machine; the OS builds concept, rollout, visuals, and outreach around the release instead of one flat post.
- Where in LA: music-night tags, listening-session spaces, recording-studio districts, label-night flyers (`LA_TARGET_DISCOVERY_ROSTER_METHOD.md` music scene).

2. RAPPER WITH AN LA SHOW
- Buyer signature: a booked LA show inside the window, building toward it with flyers but no campaign.
- Why the machine moves it: the show is the trigger; the OS turns one date into a sequenced run (pre-show, show, post-show proof) the artist owns.
- Where in LA: small-and-mid venue rosters, show-announce posts, local hip-hop tags.

3. DJ WITH A RESIDENCY
- Buyer signature: a recurring residency night, a recognizable sound, and zero brand system around it.
- Why the machine moves it: a residency is a repeatable moment; the OS builds a continuity system (world, object, place-edge) that makes every night read as one body of work.
- Where in LA: residency-night listings, club and lounge rosters, DJ-scene tags.

4. TOURING ARTIST ROUTING THROUGH LA
- Buyer signature: a tour with an LA date inside the window, a real audience, and a generic tour rollout.
- Why the machine moves it: a tour stop is a high-stakes dated moment; the OS builds the LA-leg campaign and the proof dashboard that justifies the next city.
- Where in LA: tour-routing posts, venue booking calendars, management-office clusters.

5. ARTIST CREATIVE DIRECTOR
- Buyer signature: the person already shaping an artist's visual world, stretched thin, with a moment coming they cannot fully build.
- Why the machine moves it: they have taste and decision capacity but no machine; the OS is the build arm they can hand an input to.
- Where in LA: artist-team credits, studio-district collaborators, creative-direction tags.

## HOW THESE FEED THE ROSTER

Each category maps to a scene_type and a cohort in `la_target_roster.csv` (`LA_TARGET_DISCOVERY_ROSTER_METHOD.md` Step 7). Categories 1 through 24 are mostly moment_holder or under_built cohorts; category 25 is the venue_scene_gatekeeper cohort. Discovery (Step 3) finds real candidates inside each category; the fit screen (Step 4) sorts them FIT/HOLD/PASS; warmest-first (Step 6) ranks them. No category becomes a named target until the operator approves the real names live discovery produces (`APPROVAL_LIST.md` gate a).
