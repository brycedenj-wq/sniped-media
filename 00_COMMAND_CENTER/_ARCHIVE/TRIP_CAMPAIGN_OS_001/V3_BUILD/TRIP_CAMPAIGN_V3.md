# TRIP CAMPAIGN V3 · THE LA PROOF SPRINT (master)

This is V3, the LA master. V2 (`V2_BUILD/TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V2.md`) built the campaign as a variable system, where `{DEST}`, `{DATES}`, and `{AUDIENCE}` were swappable tokens that resolved on drop-in. V3 drops the first two of those tokens to a real value and holds the third honest. `{DEST}` = Los Angeles. `{DATES}` = a rolling 14-day proof sprint, Day 1 = today. `{AUDIENCE}` = still UNKNOWN, and that unknown is the whole reason the strategy looks the way it does.

The name changed too. V2 called this a trip. V3 does not. The operator LIVES in LA. There is no arrival day, no flight, no trip-bounded deadline that ends with leaving. LA is not a place the operator visits to make content. LA is the first proof cell, and the operator is a resident inside it. That single fact changes the machine from a trip campaign into a standing proof cell, and §A spells out what that buys.

The job has not changed and is stated everywhere, raw: this is not travel content. This is proving the 10k Campaign Universe Buildout offer by opening a real room in LA (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof section; `V2_BUILD/TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V2.md` thesis).

Constraints in force: zero spend, zero generation, zero posting, no naming (real names logged locally only, never invented), no crown, no em-dashes, no hype. Face-independent: real people, characters, world faces, wardrobe, objects, places, POV, hands, body, and the operator's voice, taste, and story are allowed; the operator's face is never the load-bearing reason it works (`V2_BUILD/TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V2.md` constraints line).

## PART A · THE LA THESIS (resident, not visitor)

### A.1 · The LA-specific campaign thesis

The proof cell is a wedge, not the product. It converts the operator's standing presence in one real city into one opened room, real relationships, and a proof artifact, on the lane the operator already owns (`V2_BUILD/TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V2.md` §B.1; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` commercial thesis).

The offer is locked and baked into every line: Campaign Universe Buildout. Take any real input a person brings (an artist with a drop, a brand with a launch, a founder with a world) and build the whole machine around it, concept through proof dashboard, handed over built and owned. 10k proven floor, 25k entertained, 50k+ anchor. Trade scope, never price. BASEPLATE is parked, not active (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` 10k/25k/higher-tier sections; `V2_BUILD/TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V2.md` §B.6).

Thesis sentence, LA-concrete, with the per-target slots still as variables until discovery fills them:

> In LA, the operator is the one who can build the whole campaign machine around a real input, so `{TARGET}` (an LA artist, brand, or founder with `{THEIR_MOMENT}` coming) gets `{THEIR_THING}` turned into a built, owned campaign machine, and that opened room becomes the access receipt that proves the offer.

Three properties the thesis holds, each gating the copy: outcome-first (lead with the target's result, never the operator or the city), in-lane and scope-traded (10k floor, trade scope not price), face-independent (the access receipt, not a face, is the load-bearing proof) (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` tier sections, trade-scope-not-price).

### A.2 · Why LA-resident changes the game, from a trip to a standing proof cell

A trip campaign has a clock that runs out and a target set bounded by who happens to be reachable in a short window. A resident does not have that clock. This is the single biggest change in V3, and it rewrites the math in five places:

1. NO ARRIVAL, NO DEPARTURE. V2's runbook ran D-14 (pre-warm) to D+7 (post-trip) around an arrival day. There is no arrival. The 14-day sprint is a focus window, not a presence window. If a room does not open by Day 14, the operator does not leave LA, so the honest stop is "this sprint did not fire," not "the trip failed." The proof cell stays open; the next sprint starts the next day with a warmer runway.

2. THE WINDOW IS A CHOICE, NOT A CONSTRAINT. V2's `{DATES}` bounded which targets were reachable. In LA, almost every target is physically reachable; the 14 days bound how many the operator works, not who is in range. So the fit screen's REACHABLE-IN-WINDOW gate stops meaning "are they in town" and starts meaning "do they have a dated moment inside the next 14 days" (see `LA_TARGET_DISCOVERY_ROSTER_METHOD.md` Step 4).

3. WARM RUNS DEEPER. A visitor pre-warms cold accounts for two weeks. A resident already shares physical scenes, mutual follows, and repeat venues with the LA target set. The warmest-first sequence gets a real already-warm-in-location bucket that a trip never had (`LA_TARGET_DISCOVERY_ROSTER_METHOD.md` Step 6).

4. PHYSICAL PRESENCE IS FREE AND REPEATABLE. The cold-access ladder's rung 1 (be where the scene gathers) costs a visitor a planned outing; it costs a resident a normal week. The operator can return to the same `{THRESHOLD}` across multiple sprints, which is exactly what builds the place-edge recognition the pillars want (§A.3 pillar 2).

5. THE STOP IS HONEST, NOT TERMINAL. Because there is no departure, a "did not fire" sprint is a real result that feeds the next sprint, not a write-off. The honesty gate stays load-bearing (§A.4); it just no longer carries a plane ticket behind it.

Net: LA-resident turns a one-shot trip into a repeatable cell. The 14-day sprint is the first run of a machine that can run again, warmer each time, without spend.

### A.3 · The five-pillar content role: content DOCUMENTS proof for a low audience

The five pillars carry from V2 unchanged in shape (`V2_BUILD/TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V2.md` §B.2). What V3 changes is the JOB of content, and it changes it hard. Because `{AUDIENCE}` is UNKNOWN, reach is not the strategy and never was. The play is access-first and manual: find rooms, target real people, make value-first contact, create receipts, and let content DOCUMENT the proof. Reach is not the strategy; the opened room is. Content here does not chase reach; it records that a room opened so the proof is legible later (`PROOF_DASHBOARD.md`; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof-cell link).

Each pillar still does two jobs, recognition and access, but the access job leads and the documentation job replaces the reach job:

1. THE BUILT-FOR-THEM TEARDOWN. A short, specific frame of where `{THEIR_THING}` is leaving reach on the table, built before any ask. Access: it is the value-first micro-offer that earns the meeting. Documentation: the teardown itself is a receipt artifact, proof the operator thinks in campaigns (`ACCESS_RECEIPT_PLAN.md` value-first micro-offer).
2. THE PLACE-EDGE SERIES. The work returns to `{THRESHOLD}` in LA, the same place-edge across the sprint and across future sprints. Access: being physically at the `{THRESHOLD}` where the scene gathers is rung 1 of the cold-access ladder. Documentation: a repeated frame makes a scattered set read as one body of work, even to an audience of a few.
3. THE RUNNING-OBJECT THREAD. `{OBJECT}` recurs across assets as the continuity anchor. Access: a tangible built piece (the object, or a one-pager carrying it) is the show-dont-ask walk-in, rung 2. Documentation: the object is the through-line nobody can copy cheaply.
4. THE OUTCOME-FIRST PROOF NOTE. Each asset states the target's outcome, never the operator's bio. Access: it is the language that converts a warm reply toward a paid step. Documentation: it reads as a campaign brain, not a portfolio.
5. THE HONEST-RECEIPT POST (post-sprint only, approval-gated). What actually moved, with the access receipt as the spine. Access: it is the case-study asset that turns the prototype into a sellable offer. Documentation: proof, not vibes (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof-cell link).

Pillar ratio and post routing stay UNKNOWN until `{AUDIENCE}` drops, because the mix depends on platforms and warmth. The point holds without the ratio: posts are the byproduct, the room is the point, and with an unknown audience the only honest thing content can do is document that the room opened. Nothing posts without the operator (`APPROVAL_LIST.md` gate c).

### A.4 · The honesty gate and the ladder trigger (LA-tuned)

Each working day of the sprint ends with the same honesty gate: did a room open, logged with its trace, or did no room move (`ACCESS_RECEIPT_PLAN.md` honest-stop). The cold-access ladder fires when the gate reports no room moved for two days running, OR the mid-sprint mark (Day 7 of the 14) is reached with zero rooms opened. If the full ladder runs and no room opens by Day 14, that is logged as "this sprint did not fire," and because the operator lives in LA, the proof cell stays open and the next sprint starts warmer. Never fabricate a room. Never post a fake receipt. The honesty is part of the moat (`ACCESS_RECEIPT_PLAN.md` honest-stop; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof section).

## PART B · WHAT CHANGED, V2 TO V3

| Dimension | V2 | V3 |
|---|---|---|
| `{DEST}` | a swappable variable, UNKNOWN | Los Angeles, concrete. Scenes, channels, and target categories are LA-real (`LA_TARGET_DISCOVERY_ROSTER_METHOD.md`, `LA_TARGET_CATEGORIES_25.md`) |
| `{DATES}` | a swappable variable, UNKNOWN | a rolling 14-day proof sprint, Day 1 = today; a focus window, not a presence window |
| `{AUDIENCE}` | a swappable variable, UNKNOWN | still UNKNOWN, and now load-bearing for the strategy: unknown audience is exactly why the play is access-first, not post-and-hope |
| Frame | a trip (arrival to departure) | a standing proof cell (the operator lives in LA, no clock runs out) |
| Reachability gate | "are they physically in town during the window" | "do they have a dated moment inside the next 14 days" (almost everyone is reachable) |
| Target discovery | a destination-agnostic procedure | the same procedure made LA-concrete, runnable today, plus 25 seeded LA target categories (`LA_TARGET_DISCOVERY_ROSTER_METHOD.md`, `LA_TARGET_CATEGORIES_25.md`) |
| Dashboard | reach/response/cash tiers, numbers UNKNOWN on `{AUDIENCE}` | reach tier de-emphasized, response/access tier leads, with a sane operator-adjustable access-count default for the 14 days (`PROOF_DASHBOARD.md`) |
| Stop | "access leg did not fire," trip over | "this sprint did not fire," cell stays open, next sprint runs warmer |

The room-opening machine, the warmest-first sequence, the cold-access ladder, the proof-dashboard structure, the access receipt as load-bearing proof, and the approval boundary are all unchanged. V3 makes the place and the clock real and tunes the strategy to a low, unknown audience. It does not change the machine.

## THE ONE RULE, IN VOICE

Every day, the question is not what to post. It is what room got opened in LA, and whether it traces to this campaign. The posts are the byproduct. The room is the point. The audience is unknown, so reach cannot be the proof; the opened room is the proof. The cold-access ladder is the plan for the day no room moved, so the proof leg has a real path, not a hope. And because the operator lives here, a sprint that does not fire is not a trip that failed; it is a cell that stays open and runs again, warmer.
