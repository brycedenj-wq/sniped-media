# LA TARGET DISCOVERY ROSTER METHOD · runnable today

The V2 discovery method (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md`) made LA-concrete. V2 was a destination-agnostic procedure waiting on `{DEST}`. `{DEST}` is now Los Angeles, so this file stops being a procedure and becomes a thing the operator can run today to produce a target roster. The buyer type does not change (an LA artist, brand, or founder with a moment coming, in-lane, can say yes, cares about the outcome); what this file fills in is WHERE that type physically gathers in LA and the LA-specific channels that surface them (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` Step 1; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` who-this-serves).

The job this serves: open ONE real room in LA and prove the 10k Campaign Universe Buildout offer. This method finds the person whose room is worth opening. Posts are the byproduct (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof section; `TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V3.md` thesis).

Constraints in force: zero spend, zero generation, zero posting, no naming (real names logged locally only, never invented), no crown, no em-dashes, no hype. Face-independent. Nothing sends without the operator, and no cold contact happens until the operator approves the named list this method produces (`APPROVAL_LIST.md` gate a).

---

## STEP 0 · Inputs (all resolved for LA except audience)

- `{DEST}` = Los Angeles. Resolved. Sets the geography and every sourcing channel below.
- `{DATES}` = a rolling 14-day proof sprint, Day 1 = today. Resolved. Sets the trigger-moment window (Step 4) and the access-count target (`PROOF_DASHBOARD.md`).
- `{AUDIENCE}` = UNKNOWN. Improves warmth ranking when it lands (Step 6); the method runs without it, it just cannot pre-rank the already-warm bucket off the operator's graph until the operator supplies who already engages.

Because the operator LIVES in LA, the reachability question changes from "are they in town" to "do they have a dated moment inside the next 14 days." Almost every LA target is physically reachable; the window screens for a moment, not for presence (`TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V3.md` §A.2).

## STEP 1 · The LA scenes and where each buyer type gathers (online and physical)

LA is not one scene; it is many adjacent ones with their own physical clusters and online graphs. Map the buyer type to the LA scene it lives in. These are scene TYPES and neighborhood clusters, not named venues. Named venues need operator approval and live discovery (`APPROVAL_LIST.md` gate e).

- MUSIC / TOUR scene. Physical: small-and-mid live venues, recording-studio districts, listening-session spaces, residency nights, label and management offices clustered on the Westside and in Hollywood. Online: artist and tour-routing posts, show-announce graphics, label-night flyers, local music tags. Buyer type: indie musicians with a drop, rappers with an LA show, DJs with a residency, touring artists routing through LA, artist creative directors.
- FASHION / PRODUCT scene. Physical: showrooms and design studios in the Arts District and on the Eastside, pop-up retail corridors, sample-sale spaces, photo studios. Online: drop-announce posts, lookbook teasers, brand launch countdowns, streetwear and DTC tags. Buyer type: emerging streetwear brands with a drop, DTC brands with a launch, sneaker and accessory micro-brands, object/jewelry/eyewear designers, designers prepping a show or lookbook.
- CREATOR / EXPERT scene. Physical: creator houses and content studios, podcast studios, coworking and member clubs, workshop and cohort spaces. Online: launch-countdown content, "season 2" announce posts, book and course pre-sale posts, cohort-open posts. Buyer type: creators leveling to a real launch, podcasters launching a season, authors with a book, experts with a cohort or course.
- FOUNDER / TECH scene. Physical: accelerator and incubator demo spaces, founder-dinner circuits, coworking hubs, venture and studio offices clustered on the Westside and in the tech corridors. Online: launch announce posts, raise and announce posts, demo-day calendars, founder communities. Buyer type: startup founders launching a product/app/fund, founders raising or announcing, web3 projects with a mint (screened hard for legitimacy, see Step 4).
- HOSPITALITY / PHYSICAL scene. Physical: new restaurants and bars in pre-open or soft-open, nightlife and event-promoter circuits, fitness and wellness studios opening, pop-up and retail activations with a dated window. Online: opening-soon posts, promoter event pages, studio launch posts, activation countdowns. Buyer type: new restaurants/bars opening, nightlife and event promoters, fitness/wellness studios launching, pop-up and retail activations with a dated window.
- ART / CULTURE scene. Physical: galleries with opening nights, festival and event-organizer offices, film and TV-adjacent production clusters. Online: show-opening posts, festival lineups, premiere announce posts. Buyer type: gallery shows opening, festival and event organizers, film and TV-adjacent creators with a premiere.
- VENUE / GATEKEEPER scene. Physical: the studios, creative spaces, member clubs, and venues whose operator can grant a capture or refer a `{TARGET}`. Online: the venue's own roster and booking posts. Buyer type: studios, creative spaces, and scene gatekeepers whose room is the access point.

Output of Step 1: a short list of named LA scenes and neighborhood clusters (logged locally), each becoming a candidate `{THRESHOLD}` and a cold-access-ladder rung-1 location. The full 25-category breakdown of the buyer types above is in `LA_TARGET_CATEGORIES_25.md`.

## STEP 2 · The LA cliche-to-avoid pass

Before sourcing, rule out the postcard version of LA so the work stays Only-Them, not tourist (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` Step 2; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` why-this-beats-an-agency). Cut the obvious LA tells: palm-tree-and-sunset backdrops, the sign-on-the-hill shot, freeway-at-golden-hour b-roll, beach-pier montage, "city of dreams" framing. The Only-Them test applies to place as well as person: if the angle could be any LA tourist's, it is cut. This keeps `{SUBJECT}` / `{OBJECT}` / `{THRESHOLD}` specific to the real scene the target lives in, not the brochure.

## STEP 3 · The LA sourcing channels (run all that apply)

Each channel produces raw candidates. Log every candidate to the roster CSV before screening; screen in Step 4. All of these are zero-spend, read-only research; none is a send (sends are gated, `APPROVAL_LIST.md` gate b).

1. GEOTAGS. LA and neighborhood-level location tags on the operator's platforms (the Arts District, the specific venue tags, the studio-district tags), pulling accounts posting recent in-lane work from those places.
2. NEIGHBORHOOD-SCENE TAGS. Scene-and-neighborhood-specific tags, not the generic city tag. The streetwear-drop tags, the local-music-night tags, the founder-community tags, the gallery-opening tags. A scene tag beats a city tag for fit.
3. EVENT CALENDARS IN THE 14-DAY WINDOW. Venue listings, show schedules, gallery opening calendars, demo-day and founder-dinner calendars, pop-up and activation windows, restaurant soft-open notices that fall inside the next 14 days. An event in the window is a `{THEIR_MOMENT}` candidate, and in LA the window screens for a dated moment, not for presence.
4. FOUNDER / CREATOR COMMUNITIES. LA accelerator and incubator cohorts, creator houses, content studios, member clubs, studio collectives, founder-dinner circuits.
5. VENUE ROSTERS. Who is booked, showing, launching, or opening at the LA venues during the 14 days. The booking IS the trigger moment.
6. MUTUAL-FOLLOW GRAPH. LA accounts who already share follows with the operator's `{AUDIENCE}`, the warmest cold targets. In LA, mutual-follow often overlaps with shared physical scenes, which makes these warmer than a visitor's mutuals.
7. WARM-NODE REFERRALS. Ask the operator's existing warm LA contacts: who do you know in LA with a moment coming in the next two weeks. As a resident the operator has a real warm-node graph here, not a cold city; a referral path is the highest-warmth source.

Output of Step 3: a raw candidate list, every row tagged with the channel it came from.

## STEP 4 · The fit screen (FIT / HOLD / PASS, run on every candidate)

Five gates, from V2's fit screen plus the offer's client-fit fields, LA-tuned on the reachability gate (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` Step 4; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` client-fit screen). Each candidate scores FIT, HOLD, or PASS.

| Gate | Question | Fail = |
|---|---|---|
| IN-LANE | Does the machine actually serve `{THEIR_THING}` (a real input with taste behind it)? | PASS |
| CAN SAY YES | Decision capacity, or can refer one move to a decision-maker? | HOLD if referrer-only, PASS if neither |
| CARES ABOUT OUTCOME | Low self-orientation, wants the moment built, not cheap content? | PASS (price-shopper) |
| REACHABLE IN WINDOW (LA-tuned) | Do they have a dated moment inside the next 14 days? (Presence is assumed in LA; the gate screens for a moment, not for being in town) | HOLD (LA-reachable but no moment in 14 days) |
| HAS A TRIGGER MOMENT | Is there a `{THEIR_MOMENT}` (a launch, drop, tour, show, opening) on the calendar? | HOLD if none yet, PASS if no plausible moment ever |

Web3-mint hard screen: any web3 project with a mint scores PASS on CARES ABOUT OUTCOME unless it clears a legitimacy check first (real named team, real product, no rug or pump pattern, a real moment not a token-pump). Legitimacy screening is a real bar here, not a formality (`LA_TARGET_CATEGORIES_25.md` founders/tech group).

Verdict rule: FIT = all five pass. HOLD = one or two recoverable gaps (no dated moment yet, referrer-only, moment outside the 14 days but real). PASS = in-lane fails, or price-shopper, or no plausible moment, or a failed legitimacy screen. A PASS is removed before any outreach, never discounted later.

## STEP 5 · Per-target capture (Only-Them, fill for every FIT and HOLD)

For each surviving target, capture the fields that make the outreach real and impossible to copy-paste (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` Step 5; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` Only-Them test):

- ONLY-THEM FACT: one fact true for this target and no one else, in LA, in this window. Their specific drop date, their specific venue night, the specific gap in their specific rollout. This is what makes the reason-to-meet impossible to copy-paste.
- `{THEIR_THING}`: their real input (the launch, drop, tour, world, idea).
- `{THEIR_MOMENT}`: the dated moment that creates the trigger, with its date inside the 14-day window where possible.
- The value-first micro-offer hook: the one specific free piece worth building for `{THEIR_THING}` (a teardown of where their LA rollout leaves reach on the table, a frame for `{THEIR_MOMENT}`, a single biggest-gap map). The full micro-offer is built only on the operator's go (`ACCESS_RECEIPT_PLAN.md` value-first micro-offer; `APPROVAL_LIST.md` gate b).

No Only-Them fact, no FIT. A target with no fact specific to them is a generic contact, not a screened target.

---

## STEP 6 · Warmest-first ranking

Rank the FIT and HOLD targets so warm rooms get worked first and the cold-access ladder is spent only when warm rooms do not move (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` Step 6; `ACCESS_RECEIPT_PLAN.md` warmest-first sequence). Rank order:

1. ALREADY-WARM IN LA. Already engaging the operator's `{AUDIENCE}` AND sharing a physical LA scene or repeat venue with the operator. As a resident this bucket is real, not theoretical. Cheapest rooms, touch first.
2. PRE-WARMABLE. Reachable for one to two weeks of real engagement before any cold touch, inside the sprint.
3. COLD-BUT-FIT. Fit-screened, no warmth yet, reached with an Only-Them reason-to-meet.

Within each tier, rank by trigger-moment proximity to the 14-day window. A `{THEIR_MOMENT}` inside the next 14 days outranks one outside it. Until `{AUDIENCE}` drops, the already-warm bucket cannot be auto-ranked off the operator's graph; the operator supplies the warm names and the rest of the ranking runs.

## STEP 7 · Output, the LA roster CSV

One file, `la_target_roster.csv`, created when the operator approves running discovery. Do not pre-populate with invented rows; no named targets exist until the operator confirms real ones from live discovery (`APPROVAL_LIST.md` gate a). Exact column schema (carries V2's schema, adds an LA neighborhood field):

```
target_id,name_or_placeholder,channel_source,scene_type,la_neighborhood_cluster,cohort,decision_capacity,in_lane,can_say_yes,cares_outcome,reachable_in_window,has_trigger_moment,fit_verdict,warmth_tier,only_them_fact,their_thing,their_moment,micro_offer_hook,outreach_status
```

- target_id: T001, T002, ...
- name_or_placeholder: the real name (logged locally only, never posted) or `{TARGET}` placeholder until real.
- channel_source: geotag | neighborhood_scene_tag | event_calendar | founder_community | venue_roster | mutual_follow | warm_referral
- scene_type: music_tour | fashion_product | creator_expert | founder_tech | hospitality_physical | art_culture | venue_gatekeeper
- la_neighborhood_cluster: the LA cluster the target lives in (logged locally; categories not named venues unless operator-approved)
- cohort: moment_holder | under_built | venue_scene_gatekeeper (mirrors the dashboard cohort field, `PROOF_DASHBOARD.md` rooms.csv)
- decision_capacity: decision_maker | referrer_only | unknown
- in_lane / can_say_yes / cares_outcome / reachable_in_window / has_trigger_moment: yes | no | hold
- fit_verdict: fit | hold | pass
- warmth_tier: already_warm | pre_warmable | cold_but_fit
- only_them_fact: one line, the fact true only for them
- their_thing: their real input
- their_moment: the dated trigger moment (date inside the 14-day window where possible)
- micro_offer_hook: the one specific value-first piece worth building for them
- outreach_status: not_started | pre_warming | touched | replied | room_opened (links to `PROOF_DASHBOARD.md` outreach.csv and rooms.csv)

A row is roster-ready only when fit_verdict is fit or hold AND only_them_fact, their_thing, and a micro_offer_hook are all filled. A row missing the Only-Them fact is a contact, not a screened target.

## HOW TO RUN IT TODAY

1. Run Step 1 (LA scenes) and Step 2 (LA cliche pass).
2. Run Step 3 across every applicable LA channel, logging raw candidates (read-only research, zero spend, no sends).
3. Run Step 4 fit screen on each; drop PASS; apply the web3 legitimacy hard screen.
4. Run Step 5 Only-Them capture on every FIT and HOLD.
5. Run Step 6 warmest-first ranking.
6. Write `la_target_roster.csv` with the Step 7 schema.
7. Surface the warmest-first roster to the operator for approval. No cold contact, no send, no posting until the operator approves the named list (`APPROVAL_LIST.md` gate a, gate b). Then hand the approved warmest-first roster to the access sequence (`ACCESS_RECEIPT_PLAN.md` warmest-first sequence).
