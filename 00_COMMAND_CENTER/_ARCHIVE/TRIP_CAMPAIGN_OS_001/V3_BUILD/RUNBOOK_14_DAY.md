# RUNBOOK · 14-DAY LA PROOF SPRINT (V3)

Day 1 = today. This is the execution half of TRIP CAMPAIGN V3, the LA PROOF SPRINT. It restructures the V2 date-relative runbook (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md`) into absolute sprint days, Day 1 through Day 14, starting now.

This is not travel content. This is proving the 10k Campaign Universe Buildout offer by opening a real room in LA. The access move is the load-bearing step every single day. Posts are the byproduct that documents the proof, never the goal (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof section; `V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` line 5).

## LOCKED LA CONTEXT (what changed from V2)

- DEST = Los Angeles. The operator lives here. There is NO pre-trip travel window. V2's PHASE 1 (D-14 to D-1 pre-trip runway) and PHASE 3 (D+1 to D+7 post-trip) collapse into one standing 14-day sprint that runs from where the operator already is (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` PHASE 1, PHASE 2, PHASE 3).
- DATES = a rolling 14-day proof sprint. Day 1 = today, starting now. No waiting, no warm runway to land into, because the operator is already on the ground. Discovery and first contact front-load into Days 1 to 3 instead of running across a two-week pre-trip window.
- AUDIENCE = UNKNOWN. The sprint stays ACCESS-FIRST and manual: find rooms, target real people, brands, and venues, contact value-first, create receipts, let content document the proof. This is not post-and-hope. Because AUDIENCE is unknown, the dashboard's tier 2 to 4 numeric thresholds stay UNKNOWN; the headline floor (one real opened room with a valid receipt) does not depend on audience size (`V2_BUILD/PROOF_DASHBOARD.md` HEADLINE METRIC, WHAT IS UNKNOWN).

## THE JOB, EVERY DAY

Open ONE real room and prove the 10k Campaign Universe Buildout offer. The access move (send the value-first micro-offer on the operator's go, take the meeting, make the intro, ask for the capture, move a warm reply toward a paid step) is the load-bearing daily step. One built piece of the real campaign artifact and at most one documentation post follow as the byproduct (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` daily loop; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof section).

A room is opened only when a real person, in the real world, takes a real next step traceable to the campaign. A like, a follow, a generic "keep in touch," or a hallway hello with no next step does not count (`V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` line 5).

## CONSTRAINTS IN FORCE (whole sprint)

Zero spend until the operator approves. Zero generation, zero posting, no naming in any public surface (real names logged locally only, placeholders public), no crown, no em-dashes, no hype words. Face-independent, this is BJ-face-independent work. Nothing sends, posts, generates, or names without the operator's explicit go (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` line 7; `V2_BUILD/THREE_FACTS_BEFORE_ACTION.md` THE BOUNDARY).

Operator-approval gates are marked **[GATE]** at every day where something would send, post, or generate. Condition-met is eligible, not authorized.

## PHASE A · BUILD THE BOARD (Days 1 to 3)

Front-loaded discovery and first contact. There is no warm runway to inherit, so Days 1 to 3 build the target board and start the first touches at once. The work is to land Day 4 with a fit-screened roster, a locked offer one-pager, drafted value-first micro-offers, and a live receipt log, plus first contact already warming.

### Day 1 (today) · Run the LA discovery method, open the receipt log

- ACCESS MOVE (load-bearing): begin the LA discovery pass. Run `TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` Steps 1 and 2 for Los Angeles: map the LA scenes the buyer type gathers in (music/tour, launch/brand, founder/creator, venue/gatekeeper) and run the cliche-to-avoid pass so the work stays specific to the real LA scene, not the postcard version (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` STEP 1, STEP 2).
- Run Step 3 candidate-sourcing across every channel that applies to LA: geotags, local scene hashtags, event calendars inside the next 14 days, founder and creator communities, venue rosters, mutual-follow graph, warm-node referrals. Log every raw candidate with its channel tag (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` STEP 3).
- Create the receipt log now: start `rooms.csv` with the schema from the dashboard, and create `target_roster.csv` with the Step 7 schema. Do not pre-populate with invented rows (`V2_BUILD/PROOF_DASHBOARD.md` CSV SCHEMA A; `V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` STEP 7).
- BYPRODUCT POST: none today. Discovery first.
- END-OF-DAY HONESTY GATE: did a real room move today, logged with its trace? On Day 1 the answer is almost always no, and that is correct; the board is being built. Log "no room moved, building the board."

### Day 2 · Fit-screen, capture Only-Them facts, rank warmest-first

- ACCESS MOVE (load-bearing): run the fit screen (`TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` STEP 4) on every Day-1 candidate. Score each FIT, HOLD, or PASS. Drop every PASS now, never discount it later. For each surviving FIT and HOLD, capture the Only-Them fact, `{THEIR_THING}`, `{THEIR_MOMENT}` (a real LA moment inside the 14-day window where possible), and the one value-first micro-offer hook worth building for them (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` STEP 4, STEP 5).
- Rank warmest-first: already-warm-in-LA, then pre-warmable, then cold-but-fit; within each tier rank by trigger-moment proximity to the sprint window (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` STEP 6).
- Write the ranked `target_roster.csv`. A row is roster-ready only when fit_verdict is fit or hold AND the Only-Them fact, their_thing, and a micro_offer_hook are all filled. A row missing the Only-Them fact is a contact, not a screened target (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` STEP 7).
- FIRST WARM TOUCH (no pitch): on the warmest already-warm-in-LA targets, open real engagement (real comments, real reads on their work, no pitch). Because there is no pre-trip runway, the warm window compresses into Days 2 and 3 (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` D-13 to D-8 logic, compressed).
- BYPRODUCT POST: none yet, or one build-in-progress note held for the operator. **[GATE]** if anything would publish.
- END-OF-DAY HONESTY GATE: did a real room move? Likely no. Log "no room moved, board ranked, warm touches opened."

### Day 3 · Lock the offer one-pager, draft micro-offers, send first value-first touches

- ACCESS MOVE (load-bearing): lock the offer one-pager for the 10k Campaign Universe Buildout (the show-dont-ask artifact: the one-pager, a phone-ready cut, or the frame of `{THEIR_THING}` already started) so it can be carried into any LA room from Day 4 on (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` D-1 pre-stage logic).
- Draft (do not send) the value-first micro-offer for each top target, built for their `{THEIR_THING}`: a teardown of where their rollout leaves reach on the table, a frame for `{THEIR_MOMENT}`, or a single biggest-gap map (`V2_BUILD/TARGET_DISCOVERY_METHOD.md` STEP 5; `V2_BUILD/OUTREACH_10.md` angles).
- **[GATE]** On the operator's go only: send the first value-first touches to the warmest pre-warmable targets. Copy gate per touch: single CTA, under 80 words, no link, plain text, outcome-first, no hype, no em-dashes. Log each to `outreach.csv` with sent_approved = yes only after the operator's go (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` D-7 to D-2 logic; `V2_BUILD/PROOF_DASHBOARD.md` CSV SCHEMA B).
- A positive reply gets a response inside 30 minutes (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` line 29).
- BYPRODUCT POST: at most one documentation post of the build, **[GATE]** operator go to publish.
- END-OF-DAY HONESTY GATE: did a real room move? If a positive reply is already moving toward a set meeting, that is progress, not yet a room. Log honestly.

## PHASE B · THE DAILY ACCESS LOOP (Days 4 to 10)

The standing daily loop. The access move is the load-bearing step. Run the same loop each day; the targets and the warmth path change. This collapses V2's on-ground loop (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` PHASE 2) into the live sprint, with no arrival day because the operator is already in LA.

### The loop, run Day 4 through Day 10

1. NAME THE ONE ROOM. Pick today's warmest unworked target from `target_roster.csv`. Name the one room you intend to open today and the one value-first micro-offer built for their `{THEIR_THING}`. One named target, not a vague hope (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` daily loop step 1).
2. DO THE ACCESS MOVE, LOG THE RECEIPT. **[GATE]** Do the access move on the operator's go: send the micro-offer, take the meeting, make the intro, ask for the capture (consent required), or move a warm reply toward a paid step. Respond to any positive reply inside 30 minutes. The moment a room opens, fill one block of `ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` and log the matching `rooms.csv` row the same day, with its campaign trace. No trace, no valid receipt (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` daily loop step 2; `V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` THE COPY-PASTE BLOCK).
3. BUILD ONE PIECE, ONE POST. Build one real piece of the campaign artifact for a target's `{THEIR_THING}` (the real machine, not a mockup). Produce at most one documentation post, **[GATE]** operator go to publish. The post exists to draw a touch that becomes a room, never for reach (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` daily loop step 3; `V2_BUILD/POSTS_10.md`).
4. END-OF-DAY HONESTY GATE. Did a real room open today, logged with its trace? If yes, log it. If no, log "no room moved" and check the escalation trigger below (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` daily loop step 4).

### The 2-day-no-room escalation trigger (binds to the honesty gate, no new metric)

The cold-access ladder fires when the gate reports either:

- NO ROOM MOVED FOR TWO DAYS RUNNING, or
- THE SPRINT MIDPOINT REACHED (end of Day 7) WITH ZERO ROOMS OPENED.

Either fires the ladder. Do not wait for Day 14. The ladder is detailed in PHASE C and may fire any time from Day 5 onward if the trigger trips (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` escalation; `V1_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_PLAN.md` §4).

Guardrails every day: value-first only, no mass-DM, no fabrication, consent for any capture, face-independent, nothing posts or sends without the operator.

## PHASE C · ESCALATION AND FOLLOW-UP (Days 11 to 13)

If rooms have moved, these days deepen and follow up. If rooms have not moved (the 2-day trigger has fired, or fired earlier), the cold-access ladder runs in order. The ladder can fire any time from Day 5 on; Days 11 to 13 are the latest it should run before the close.

### The cold-access ladder (run in order, `V1_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_PLAN.md` §4)

1. GO WHERE THE SCENE GATHERS. Be physically at the LA threshold, venue, or event the target is in. LA is the operator's home ground, so this rung is low-cost and should fire early when warm rooms stall (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` escalation rung 1).
2. THE SHOW-DONT-ASK WALK-IN. Carry the built artifact (the offer one-pager or the started frame of their `{THEIR_THING}`) into the room. Show, do not ask (rung 2).
3. CAPTURE THE MICRO-CONTACT, SAME-DAY FOLLOW-UP. After any in-person contact, follow up the same day while it is warm (rung 3).
4. DOWNGRADE THE ASK, NEVER THE PRICE. A 10-minute coffee, a referral. A referral to a real decision-maker IS an opened room and logs with its trace; that is scope-trade applied to access, never a discount (rung 4; `V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` EXAMPLE RECEIPT 2).
5. THE HONEST STOP. If the full ladder runs and no room opens, that is a real result, logged honestly, never fabricated (rung 5).

### Days 11 to 13 work

- ACCESS MOVE (load-bearing): for each opened room, deliver the value promised and move to the committed next paid step (proposal to send, follow-up call booked, scope to scope). For rooms not yet opened, run the next ladder rung. Same-day follow-ups on every warm in-person contact (`V2_BUILD/DAY_BY_DAY_RUNBOOK.md` D+3 to D+4 payoff logic, brought inside the sprint).
- **[GATE]** Draft the honest-receipt documentation post (the case-study asset) and any proposal, operator go to send or publish. Hold the floor, trade scope not price (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` trade-scope-not-price).
- END-OF-DAY HONESTY GATE each day, same as PHASE B.

## PHASE D · HARVEST AND HONEST CLOSE (Day 14)

The last sprint day. Harvest, reconcile, decide continue or rotate.

### Day 14 work

- ACCESS MOVE (load-bearing): close any open loop. Send the last same-day follow-ups, confirm any committed next paid step, run the final ladder rung if a room is still cold and worth one more move.
- RECONCILE THE DASHBOARD. Reconcile `rooms.csv`, `outreach.csv`, and `content.csv`. Confirm every receipt has a real campaign trace; a row without a trace is a contact, downgraded, not a receipt. content.csv feeds outreach.csv feeds rooms.csv; the headline reads off rooms.csv (`V2_BUILD/PROOF_DASHBOARD.md` HOW THE TIERS CONNECT).
- HEADLINE READ. At least one real opened room with a valid receipt = the offer carries past prototype on this LA cell, never crowned beyond that. Zero rooms opened with a valid receipt = "access leg did not fire," logged honestly with the R000 honest-stop block; the offer is not yet proven and the next attempt needs a warmer runway or a different target set (`V2_BUILD/PROOF_DASHBOARD.md` HEADLINE METRIC; `V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` THE HONEST-STOP RULE).
- **[GATE]** Assemble the built campaign artifact as the portfolio piece (concept through proof dashboard), operator go for any public surface.
- DECIDE CONTINUE OR ROTATE. If a room opened and a paid step is committed, continue the LA cell into a next sprint or roll the won room into delivery. If the access leg did not fire, decide: warmer runway, different target set, or rotate to a different proof cell. Log the decision. Never fabricate a room to clear the headline; the honesty is part of the moat (`V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` THE HONEST-STOP RULE).

## OPERATOR-APPROVAL GATES (where they sit)

Approval lives at the boundary, never mid-loop. The hard **[GATE]** days and steps:

- Day 3: first value-first sends, and any build-documentation post.
- Days 4 to 10 (every day): step 2 the access move (any send), and step 3 the documentation post.
- Days 11 to 13: the honest-receipt post and any proposal send.
- Day 14: any public surface for the portfolio artifact.

Everything inside discovery, fit-screening, drafting, ranking, and local logging runs without a gate; nothing leaves the operator's machine without one. Condition-met is eligible, not authorized (`V2_BUILD/THREE_FACTS_BEFORE_ACTION.md` THE BOUNDARY).
