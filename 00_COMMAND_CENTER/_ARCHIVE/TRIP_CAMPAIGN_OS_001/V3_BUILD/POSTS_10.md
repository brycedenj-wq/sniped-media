# TRIP CAMPAIGN OS 001 · V3 · LA PROOF SPRINT · FIRST 10 POSTS / SCRIPTS

V3 is the LA cut. The destination is no longer a token. It is Los Angeles, and I live here, so this is not travel content. LA is the first proof cell. The job is to open one real room in LA and prove the offer on the ground.

The window is a rolling 14-day proof sprint. Day 1 is today. The arc opens a loop early, documents the build day by day, logs any real room honestly, and closes on what actually happened by Day 14.

The audience is assumed small. So these ten are proof-documentation for the few right people, not broadcast for reach. They earn the right rooms by showing the access work and the receipts straight. No post here is written as if a crowd is watching.

The offer is locked and written concrete. Campaign Universe Buildout, 10k floor. Bring me the idea, the world, the concert, the artist, the product, the script. If there is real taste or a real spark behind it, the OS builds the whole campaign around it. The outcome is a real moment turned into a built, owned campaign machine.

The buyer is one of three. An LA artist or act with a moment coming. A brand with a launch. A founder with a world and no machine. Taste yes, machine no.

Value-first holds throughout. The free, specific value drop is a teardown or a map of the single biggest gap in how their thing is being rolled out. The micro-offer is the CTA. The meeting is the pivot after they engage.

---

## VARIABLE KEY

LA, the 14-day sprint, the offer, the buyer, and the micro-offer are written concrete. Only these stay as tokens, since no names are invented.

- `{TARGET}` : a single screened person, brand, or venue.
- `{THEIR_THING}` : the target's real input. Their idea, world, concert, product, or launch.
- `{THEIR_MOMENT}` : the target's dated moment. The launch, show, or drop with a date on it.

PILLAR KEY: BUILD (proof of the work) · ROOM (the access receipt, honest-only) · READ (taste and judgment) · OBJECT (the running motif) · HAND-RAISE (the close).

## STANDING NOTES
- The five pillars repeat for recognition: BUILD, ROOM, READ, OBJECT, HAND-RAISE. If a post is not one of the five, it does not ship.
- Post 5 and Post 9 (ROOM) are honest-only. No opened room means no post, never a staged receipt. A failed access leg is a real result, logged as such, not faked.
- LA is written concrete throughout. The operator lives here, so this reads as local proof work, not a travel diary.
- Audience is assumed small. Every post is proof-documentation for the few right people. None chases volume or speaks to a crowd.
- Face-independent throughout. Recognition rides the object, the place, and the voice. A face is allowed and supporting, never the load-bearing reason a post works.
- The friend-deal anecdote appears as at most one light market line in Post 10. It is not the proof.
- Tokens kept: `{TARGET}`, `{THEIR_THING}`, `{THEIR_MOMENT}`. These are reserved for named or screened inputs and are used heavily in the outreach file. The posts speak to the room broadly, so LA, the 14-day sprint, the day index, the offer, the buyer, and the micro-offer are all written concrete.
- No spend, no generation, no posting, no naming, no crown.
