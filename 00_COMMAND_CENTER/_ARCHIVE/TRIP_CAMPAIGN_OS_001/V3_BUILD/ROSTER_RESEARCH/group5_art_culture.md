# Group 5 · Art / Culture Roster Research

Research date: 2026-06-26
Window targeted: late June 2026 through about September 2026
Method: live public web search (WebSearch) then page confirmation (WebFetch). Read-only public discovery. No contact, no login, zero spend.

Offer being fit-screened (NOT pitched here): "Campaign Universe Buildout," a 10k service that builds a whole owned campaign around one real input for someone with real taste and a real moment but no campaign machine.

FIT rule: emerging artist / smaller gallery / independent organizer / indie filmmaker with a real dated moment and no marketing machine = FIT. Blue-chip galleries (David Kordansky, Pace, Lisson, Hauser and Wirth) and major-studio premieres = PASS or HOLD (they already have a machine).

Honest note: several of the biggest, most-promoted LA shows this window are blue-chip and are marked PASS on purpose. Fewer real FIT targets beats a padded list.

Categories in this group:
- 22 = gallery show or solo artist exhibition opening (dated)
- 23 = festival / event organizer with a dated festival or event
- 24 = film / TV-adjacent creator with a dated premiere / screening / release
