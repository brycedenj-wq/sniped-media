## Honest framing before the list

The single hardest rule to satisfy was "dated moment quoted verbatim from the source with the date shown." Two patterns emerged:

1. Brand-OWN dated drops are rarely printed publicly. Emerging LA streetwear and underground brands (Badfriend, Vale Forever) sell out through community channels and Instagram, not dated calendars. Their sites show "Summer 2026 collection" or "new styles each month" but not a public hard date. These are honest INFERRED or WEAK, not CONFIDENT.
2. EVENT-anchored dated moments are abundant and verifiable (sample sales, warehouse sales, jewelry shows, fashion week, craft fairs). These carry exact verbatim dates and name real brands, so they are CONFIDENT, but several involve brands that are large/established (PASS or HOLD on fit) rather than under-built.

Where a real entity has a real moment but the public date is soft, it is marked WEAK and kept honest. Fewer real beats more fake.

The offer ("Campaign Universe Buildout," 10k, whole owned campaign around one real input) fits EMERGING / under-built LA brands with a real dated moment and no marketing machine. Brands with a full machine are PASS or HOLD.

- A clean July-pop-up for a single emerging LA brand with its OWN published hard date in the window was not found in public sources within scope. The closest brand-own dated moments are seasonal ("Summer 2026," "new styles each month").
- The CONFIDENT dated moments are mostly EVENTS (sample/warehouse sales, jewelry show, craft fair, fashion week). Several of those name large brands, so they are PASS/HOLD on fit even though the date is rock solid.
- The best FIT-on-profile targets (Vale Forever, O-M Ceramic, Made Some, AKILA) are real, LA, and under-built or independent, but their public dated moment is soft, hence WEAK or INFERRED.
