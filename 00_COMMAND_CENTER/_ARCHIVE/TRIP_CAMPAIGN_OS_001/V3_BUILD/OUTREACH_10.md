# TRIP CAMPAIGN OS 001 · V3 · LA PROOF SPRINT · FIRST 10 DM / COLD-EMAIL MESSAGES

V3 is the LA cut of the outreach set. The destination is no longer a token. It is Los Angeles, and the operator lives here, so LA is referenced naturally as the place this is happening. Not a trip. The first proof cell.

The window is a rolling 14-day proof sprint. Day 1 is today. Messages reference it as this week, the next two weeks, or right now, never a date token.

The offer is locked and written concrete. Campaign Universe Buildout, 10k floor. You bring the real input with real taste behind it, and the OS builds the whole campaign around it. The outcome sold is a real moment turned into a built, owned campaign machine.

Every message is value-first. The first touch leads with a free, specific value drop, a teardown or a map of the single biggest gap in how their thing is being rolled out, not a cold ask to meet. The micro-offer is the CTA. The meeting is the pivot after they engage. Every message drives toward an opened room in LA.

Three cohorts. COHORT A is the moment-holder, an LA artist or act or brand with a dated moment coming. COHORT B is the under-built, real taste and real input, no machine, doing every job alone. COHORT C is the venue or scene gatekeeper, whose room is the access point.

---

## VARIABLE KEY

LA, the 14-day sprint, the offer, the micro-offer, and the cohorts are written concrete. Only these stay as tokens, since no names are invented.

- `{TARGET}` : the single screened person, brand, or venue this message goes to.
- `{THEIR_THING}` : the target's real input. Their idea, world, concert, product, or launch.
- `{THEIR_MOMENT}` : the target's dated moment. The launch, show, or drop with a date on it.

Copy gate held on all ten: under 80 words each, single CTA, no link, plain text. Zero em-dashes and zero look-alikes, zero hype words, no bible or spec voice tells. Every message ends on terminal punctuation. Operator voice, raw, plain, declarative, calm authority. None send until the operator approves.

## STANDING NOTES
- Every message is value-first. The micro-offer (a free teardown or a map of the single biggest gap in how their thing is being rolled out) is the CTA. The meeting is the pivot after they engage, never the cold ask.
- Mix held: DMs (1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9) run short. Cold emails (5, 6, 10) carry a subject line and one more sentence of context. Each stays under 80 words.
- Single CTA each, no link, plain text. The cohort and channel are labeled on every message.
- LA is referenced naturally throughout (in LA, here, this week, this city) because the operator is local. The 14-day sprint reads as this week or the next two weeks, never a date token.
- Angle 10 writes about the operational problem the moment creates (keeping it producing after the date), not the trigger itself.
- The friend-deal anecdote and any market reference are kept out of outreach entirely. The value drop and the offer carry it.
- Face-independent. The built work and the value drop open the room, never the operator's face.
- Tokens kept: `{TARGET}`, `{THEIR_THING}`, `{THEIR_MOMENT}`. Everything about LA, the sprint, the offer, the micro-offer, the cohorts, and the proof cell is written concrete.
- No spend, no generation, no posting, no naming, no crown.
