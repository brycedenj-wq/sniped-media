# ACCESS RECEIPT PLAN · LA

The opened room plus its receipt is the single load-bearing proof of the whole campaign. It is the one thing a creative director, an agency, and an AI operator cannot show (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof section; `V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md`). This file is the LA-concrete plan for opening that room and logging that receipt. The fillable copy-paste block lives in `V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md`; this plan points to it and does not duplicate it.

The job, raw: this is not travel content. This is proving the 10k Campaign Universe Buildout offer by opening a real room in LA (`TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V3.md` thesis; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof-cell link). Because the audience is unknown, the access receipt is the proof, not reach. The play is access-first and manual, not post-and-hope.

Constraints in force: zero spend, zero generation, zero posting, no naming in any public surface (real names logged locally only), no crown, no em-dashes, no hype. Face-independent. Nothing sends, posts, or spends without the operator (`APPROVAL_LIST.md`).

---

## 1 · WHAT COUNTS AS A ROOM IN LA

A room is opened when a real person, in the real world or a real direct channel, in LA, inside this sprint, takes a real next step traceable to the campaign. The four room types (unchanged from V2, `V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md`):

- A MEETING TAKEN. A real working conversation about building `{THEIR_THING}`, in person or scheduled, that traces to the campaign.
- AN INTRO MADE. A gatekeeper or warm node connects the operator to a real decision-maker `{TARGET}`. A referral to a real decision-maker IS an opened room (scope-trade applied to access, never a discount, `V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` example 2).
- A CAPTURE GRANTED. A venue, studio, or `{TARGET}` grants the operator real access to capture, with consent logged.
- A CONVERSATION TOWARD A PAID STEP. A real exchange that moves toward a scoped 10k (or higher tier) proposal.

What does NOT count: a like, a follow, a generic "keep in touch," a hallway hello with no next step, or any room with no campaign trace. No trace, no valid receipt (`V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` validity rule). In LA specifically, a chance run-in at a shared scene only counts if it produced a real next step traced to the campaign; proximity alone is not a room.

## 2 · THE WARMEST-FIRST LA SEQUENCE

Work warm before cold; spend the cold-access ladder only when warm rooms do not move (`V2_BUILD/TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V2.md` §B.3; `LA_TARGET_DISCOVERY_ROSTER_METHOD.md` Step 6). Order:

1. ALREADY-WARM IN LA. Targets already engaging the operator's `{AUDIENCE}` and sharing a physical LA scene or repeat venue. As a resident this bucket is real. Touch first; these are the cheapest rooms.
2. PRE-WARMABLE. Targets reachable for one to two weeks of real engagement before any cold touch, inside the sprint.
3. COLD-BUT-FIT. Fit-screened, no warmth yet; reached with an Only-Them reason-to-meet, and only after the warm tiers have been worked.

Within each tier, work the targets whose `{THEIR_MOMENT}` falls soonest inside the 14-day window first; a sooner moment is a hotter trigger. No tier is touched without an operator-approved roster (`APPROVAL_LIST.md` gate a).

---

## 3 · THE VALUE-FIRST MICRO-OFFER (earns the meeting)

Each `{TARGET}` gets a free, specific piece built for `{THEIR_THING}`, delivered before any ask. The micro-offer is the CTA; the meeting is the pivot (`V2_BUILD/TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V2.md` §B.3; pillar 1). It is the built-for-them teardown: a short, specific frame of where `{THEIR_THING}` is leaving reach on the table around `{THEIR_MOMENT}`, built before any ask.

Rules: it is built for one target, not a template; it leads with the target's outcome, never the operator's bio; it carries the Only-Them fact so it cannot be copy-pasted; it points at the target's dated moment. The full micro-offer is built only on the operator's go (`APPROVAL_LIST.md` gate b). The ask never appears inside the micro-offer; the micro-offer earns the right to a conversation, and the conversation is where the ask lives.

## 4 · THE 5-RUNG COLD-ACCESS LADDER (LA-concrete)

Carried from V2 (`V2_BUILD/TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V2.md` §B.3; `V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` example 2), made LA-concrete. The ladder fires only on the trigger in §5 below, after warm rooms have been worked.

- RUNG 1 · BE WHERE THE LA SCENE PHYSICALLY GATHERS. Go to the real LA scene cluster the target lives in (the studio district, the venue, the showroom corridor, the opening night), not the tourist LA. As a resident this costs a normal week, not a planned trip, and the operator can return to the same `{THRESHOLD}` across sprints to build place-edge recognition (`TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V3.md` §A.2; `LA_TARGET_DISCOVERY_ROSTER_METHOD.md` Step 1).
- RUNG 2 · THE SHOW-DONT-ASK WALK-IN. Lead with a tangible built piece (the `{OBJECT}`, or a one-pager carrying the built-for-them frame), not a pitch. Show the work; do not ask for the room. The built piece is the reason the conversation starts.
- RUNG 3 · CAPTURE THE MICRO-CONTACT, SAME-DAY FOLLOW-UP. Get one real micro-contact (a name, a handle, a yes-to-a-follow-up) and follow up the same day with the Only-Them frame. Same-day, because in a live LA scene the moment cools fast.
- RUNG 4 · DOWNGRADE THE ASK, NEVER THE PRICE. If the full meeting is not available, trade scope of access: ask for an intro, a smaller capture, a shorter conversation. A referral to a real decision-maker is still an opened room. The 10k floor holds; access scope flexes (`OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` trade-scope-not-price).
- RUNG 5 · THE HONEST STOP. If the ladder runs and no room opens, log it honestly and stop. Never fabricate a room. Never post a fake receipt (§6).

---

## 5 · THE LADDER TRIGGER AND THE SPRINT CLOCK

The cold-access ladder fires when the honesty gate reports no room moved for two days running, OR the mid-sprint mark (Day 7 of 14) is reached with zero rooms opened (`TRIP_CAMPAIGN_V3.md` §A.4). Each working day ends with the same honesty gate: did a room open, logged with its trace, or did no room move. Because the operator lives in LA, the Day 14 stop is "this sprint did not fire," not "the trip failed"; the proof cell stays open and the next sprint starts warmer.

---

## 6 · THE RECEIPT SPEC

Every opened room logs one receipt the same day it opens, using the fillable copy-paste block in `V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md`. The receipt is VALID only when ROOM TYPE, WHO, DATE, and CAMPAIGN TRACE are all real and filled. A block missing the trace is a contact, not a receipt. Each receipt block maps to one `rooms.csv` row (`PROOF_DASHBOARD.md` schema A; `V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` field-to-CSV map).

The honest-stop rule is load-bearing: if the full ladder runs and no room opens by Day 14, log it as a real result ("this sprint did not fire"), not a content problem, and do not log a room that did not open. Never post a fake receipt. The honesty is part of the moat; a faked receipt destroys the one thing competitors cannot show (`V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` honest-stop rule; `OFFER_PROTOTYPE.md` proof section).

Use the fillable block: see `V2_BUILD/ACCESS_RECEIPT_TEMPLATE.md` for the copy-paste receipt, the two worked examples, and the did-not-fire entry. This plan does not duplicate the block; it points at the one source of truth so there is no drift.
