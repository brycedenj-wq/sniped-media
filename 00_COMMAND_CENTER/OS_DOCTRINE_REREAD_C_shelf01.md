### BOOK 1: Save The Cat! (Blake Snyder, 2005)

**Coverage line:** 9/9 segments read, full book captured from logline doctrine through marketplace strategy and glossary.

**1-line thesis:** Story structure is commerce; a clear ironic logline, genre fluency, and 15-beat architecture are not artistic choices but mechanical laws that determine whether an idea survives the marketplace.

**Densest frameworks/lessons:**

1. **The Logline Law (4 components):** Irony + compelling mental picture + audience/cost clarity + killer title. All four must fire simultaneously. Any failure collapses the pitch. Test against strangers before writing a word.
2. **Genre as structural navigation:** 10 proven story types (Monster in the House, Golden Fleece, Out of the Bottle, Buddy Love, etc.). Know your genre's rules before you break them. Ignorance of genre guarantees drift.
3. **BS2 (Blake Snyder Beat Sheet):** 15 beats mapped to fixed page numbers in a 110-page script. Catalyst page 12, Act 2 break page 25, Midpoint page 55 (false victory or false defeat), All Is Lost page 75 (whiff of death), Break Into Three page 85, Final Image page 110 (proof of transformation).
4. **The Set-Up's Six Things That Need Fixing:** Plant all character flaws in the first 10 pages. These become call-backs, running gags, and the hero's transformation proof. If you don't plant them, you can't pay them off.
5. **Hero proactivity law:** Hero leads, never gets dragged. No passive questioning. Hero seeks clues, others ask him. Cover the character names in your dialogue; you should know who's speaking from voice alone.
6. **Primal substrate:** All sophisticated plots run on caveman drives underneath: survival, hunger, sex, protecting family, fear of death. Strip your pitch to primal urgency or international audiences won't feel it.
7. **All Is Lost whiff of death:** Something literally or symbolically dies at page 75 (mentor, goldfish, old self). This clears the ground for synthesis. The hero cannot arrive at Break Into Three without first losing the old world.
8. **Outreach hierarchy (Segment 8):** In-person beats phone beats query letter beats email. Referral beats cold outreach by orders of magnitude. "Keep turning the crank" is the only career strategy.

**SNIPED application:**

- Every SNIPED deliverable (Direction Stack book, Card system, KOTS institutional frame, composite environment rotation, VIB pitch) needs to pass the 4-component logline test before shipping. If it can't be stated in one sentence with irony and a mental picture, it is unfinished.
- Direction Stack book architecture maps directly to BS2: opening image (the myth of the candid photograph), catalyst (introduction of the constructed image paradigm), midpoint (false mastery: "I understand the theory"), All Is Lost (confronting the difficulty of execution), final image (proof of reader transformation via case studies).
- SNIPED's core irony is already built: AI-powered world-building + identity lock. That is the itch. The positioning just needs to state it in one sentence.
- The primal substrate check: SNIPED's trust story reduces to protection of cultural memory and survival of craft tradition against dissolution. Current framing may be too cerebral. Audiences feel the primal layer before they absorb the argument.
- VIB outreach follows Snyder's hierarchy already (warm > cold). The referral loop doctrine (one warm intro > 100 cold emails) is validated here as career physics, not preference.
- "Bad Guy Badder" check: SNIPED's implicit antagonist (careless AI deployment, commodity studio photography) must be cast as equally capable, not weaker. If SNIPED uses AI and the antagonist uses AI, the differentiation must be intentionality and craft execution, not weaponry.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:**

- **Reinforces** "hardest to say no to" rule: Snyder's logline discipline is the mechanism. Irony is non-negotiable; polished copy without irony fails.
- **Reinforces** "repetition over novelty": Snyder's "Give Me The Same Thing Only Different" principle (templates repeated with fresh twists) is the structural argument for running the architecture SNIPED already has.
- **Mild challenge to** "full engagement before direction": Snyder argues that holding off writing until logline is locked produces sharper decisions. The OS should apply this before Direction Stack prose begins: lock the logline (one sentence, four components) before drafting chapters.
- **New addition:** The "Six Things That Need Fixing" framework has no direct OS equivalent. Deserves a slot in Direction Stack pre-writing and in client onboarding (what six beliefs does the founder carry that the work needs to fix?).

### BOOK 2: Published (Chandler Bolt, 2016)

**1-line thesis:** The book is not the product; it is the leverage lever that compresses authority, audience, and backend revenue into a single proof object, and anyone can ship one in 90 days using backward-design discipline.

**Densest frameworks/lessons:**

1. **Five purpose archetypes:** Passion Project, Money/Business Growth, Build Authority, Grow Network, Share Your Story. Weak purposes (vague bucket-list, "sounds fun") collapse under pressure. Strong purposes sustain momentum through the "Crisis of Meaning" valley post-draft.
2. **Pre-writing funnel (15-min free-write, 30-min mind map, structured outline):** Not an outline-first process. Free-write releases what's already in your head; mind map organizes chaos; outline sequences chapters. Together they collapse "do I have a book?" ambiguity before committing to prose.
3. **Parkinson's Law applied to book sprints:** Shorter deadline = higher focus. Open-ended timelines produce low output. 30-day sprint with daily hour-blocks (15 min chapter prep + 10 min outline review + 35-90 min writing) is the operational unit.
4. **Pain pills vs. vitamins titling:** People act to remove pain, not prevent it. Title must surface the biggest pain point the book solves. Subtitle is the specificity bomb (so niche it feels uncomfortable). "Obvious" titles sell. Clever ones die.

6. **Four launch strategies:** Free (entry level, no audience, momentum play), $0.99 (established niche, 500+ email list), Traditional (media-intensive, high budget), Free-Plus-Shipping (advanced, credit-card commitment, upsell funnel). No hybrid. Commit to one lane.
7. **Value Ladder (3-tier product stack):** Teach (book, cheapest, scalable) → Show (video course, mid-price) → Do (done-for-you premium service, high delivery). Funnel users through via trust accumulation. LTV must exceed CTAC + COGS for any funnel to work.
8. **Review push mechanics (3-stage + autoresponder):** Pre-launch personal outreach + embargo → launch-day direct ask with link + goal + rationale → post-launch re-ask to non-compliers. Autoresponder: immediate welcome → day-2 feedback nudge → week-1 stats and gratitude. Gary Vee principle embedded: give generously before the ask. One review outperforms one sale in long-run algorithmic value.

### BOOK 3: Gemini PHOTO YAP.docx

**Coverage line:** 4/4 segments read, full doc captured: AI-generated photo critique system across 10+ images, portfolio synthesis, Lightroom/Evoto workflow mechanics, color physics, and SNIPED Hero Playbook distillation.

**1-line thesis:** Elite commercial portraiture is a physics engineering problem, not an aesthetic one; in-camera primacy, histogram mastery, color optics, and repeatable pose architecture are the levers, and post-production is cleanup, not salvation.

**Densest frameworks/lessons:**

1. **8-criterion analysis matrix:** Composition, lighting, color palette, subject pose/presence, depth/dimensionality, edit/grading, emotional weight, uniqueness. Scored 1-10 per image. Each criterion isolates a single production variable. Actionable as a repeatable QA gate before any hero image is released.
2. **In-camera primacy doctrine:** If concept, direction, and lighting are correct in-camera, retouching is production work. Bad in-camera decisions cannot be fixed in post. "Fix-in-post" mentality is categorically rejected.
3. **Pose micro-rules:** Crop between joints, never at them (mid-limb slicing). Chin slightly forward and down (jawline definition). Hands need a specific task. Off-camera gaze vs. direct eye contact is a narrative tension toggle, not a stylistic accident.
4. **Three Pillars of Digital Depth (Segment 4):** Atmospheric depth (Lens Blur 15-20, before grain, mimics medium-format optical drop-off). Luminance depth (linear gradient floor-to-waist at -0.50 exposure, fakes inverse-square light law). Color depth (warm advances, cool recedes; Expansive Matrix = cool bg + warm subject for 3D separation; Claustrophobic Matrix = warm on warm for 2D poster flatness).
5. **Lightroom panel hierarchy as sacred structure:** Exposure → Contrast → Highlights/Shadows/Whites/Blacks → Tone Curve → HSL → Color Grading → Calibration → Effects (grain). Jumping panels produces inconsistent results. Flow is non-negotiable.
6. **Grain as biological optic hack:** Global subtle grain (Amount 15, Size 20, Roughness 50) glues pixels. Local heavy grain on background (Amount 45) forces viewer eye to subject because human vision seeks the sharpest element. Museum grit + commercial polish without plastic-mask effect.
7. **Elite color physics rule:** Background color cannot be painted in post; it must engineer the physics of light bounce. If a wall glows neon, that light must spill onto the subject's shadowed edge (edge masks, linear gradients, exposure +0.25 to +0.30, matching hue and saturation). Faking color without spill = cheap composite recognized by brain instantly.
8. **Evoto at 85%, not 100%:** 15% original background bleed-through anchors subject and hides AI clipping. Full opacity = floating sticker. Evoto handles skin cleanup only; Lightroom builds color and depth first.

**SNIPED application:**

- The 8-criterion matrix is now the official QA gate for every SNIPED hero image before release. Score each criterion; if uniqueness or emotional weight scores below 6, the image does not ship as a hero post.
- In-camera primacy doctrine is already locked in SNIPED; this doc operationalizes it at the physics level (histogram, HSL, color spill math). These are the specific levers, not just the principle.
- The SNIPED Hero Playbook (Segment 4) is literally titled for SNIPED: S-curve tone, lifted blacks, Orange HSL (+5 hue, -10 saturation, +15 luminance), Blue Primary +30 saturation, global grain 15/20/50, local background grain 45/25/50. This is the locked preset formula.
- Black wardrobe power matrices (High-Anxiety: Blood Red/Crimson; Luxury: Emerald/Teal; Void: Charcoal/Black-on-Black) give SNIPED a decision tree for background selection when subject wears black, which is SNIPED's most common wardrobe anchor.
- "The Final 20% Principle" (editing is a solved problem; lock preset forever, redirect 100% energy upstream to capture) is the enforcement mechanism for "repetition over novelty" applied specifically to post-production.
- Portfolio synthesis (Segment 2: 5 strengths, 2 growth areas) validated the monochromatic/commercial-studio lane but flagged emotional weight and narrative depth as inconsistent. The "in-between capture" directive (Avedon/Frank methodology: shoot during transitions and exhaustion, not formal poses) is the fix. Apply to every future shoot.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:**

- **Reinforces** "strongest photograph != most processed" with specific mechanics: pore-level preservation, black point honesty, destructive edits only when concept-driven.
- **Reinforces** quiet luxury editorial restraint: matte lifted blacks (film wash), Vibrance +15 not saturation crush, Adobe Portrait not Color profile. Teal/orange explicitly absent.
- **Reinforces** connected toolchain default: Lightroom-first, Evoto-cleanup. Reversing the pipeline melts subjects into backgrounds.
- **Promotes** to locked doctrine: The 8-criterion matrix should be formalized as an evaluation gate skill. Currently no single OS document consolidates these criteria as a named gate.
- **New addition:** Three Pillars of Digital Depth (Atmospheric/Luminance/Color) has no prior OS equivalent. This is the physics framework for composite background integration and should be added to the composite environment rotation workflow.
- **New addition:** Wardrobe-Lighting-Skin Tone testing framework (three-axis QA before hero release) is a direct addition to the pre-shoot prep and post-shoot same-day protocols.
- **Mild contradiction with** current edit register bifurcation: The doc emphasizes that psychological/documentary energy (unguarded, kinetic) is the highest-value signal, but SNIPED's current output shows strong commercial/rehearsed polish. Both are valid in different registers, but the "in-between capture" must be built into shoot-day protocol explicitly or it won't happen.

### BOOK 4: kling 3.00.docx

**Coverage line:** 5/5 segments read, full doc captured: Kling 3.0 multi-shot workflows, asset-first production, viral YouTube engineering, faceless authority channel playbook, cinematic prompt architecture, element tagging, LoRA training, and platform consolidation.

**1-line thesis:** AI video production is now a directing and asset-management discipline, not a generation discipline; character consistency, multi-shot narrative decomposition, batch automation, and element tagging are the operator's craft, and platform consolidation beats tool proliferation.

**Densest frameworks/lessons:**

1. **Asset-first production:** Build 4-7 angle character sheets (front, back, profile, 3/4, emotion variants) before generating a single video. Store in Elements. Tag with "@" in all subsequent prompts. This is the one-time setup cost that enables infinite variation without identity drift.
2. **Multi-shot narrative sequencing:** Decompose storyboards into 3-second shots before touching the tool. Embed transitions in prompts. End of Clip A becomes the start frame for Clip B (bridge-frame workflow). Narrative continuity is engineered at script stage, not fixed in post.
3. **Grid-to-video workflow:** Create a 3x3 grid image of the desired sequence. Upload as visual storyboard. Kling deconstructs the grid into 9 sequential shots with absolute visual consistency. Eliminates manual per-shot prompting.
4. **Motion Transfer (Kling O1/2.6):** Upload a static character image plus a reference video. Motion Control maps the video's dynamics onto the static asset frame by frame. Decouples performance from identity. Eliminates green-screen, VFX, and rotoscoping costs.
5. **5-Part Cinematic Structure for prompting:** Subject, Action, Environment, Lighting/Atmosphere, Camera Movement. Toggle Professional Mode, use negative prompts (blurry, distorted, unrealistic hands, glitchy), keep Creativity Slider at Relevance. This is the quality floor enforcement mechanism.
6. **Conflict Arc for retention engineering:** Hook → Rising Action → Conflict (unexpected reversal) → Comeback → Payoff. The unexpected reversal post-rising-action measurably boosts viewer retention over linear storytelling. Data-driven ideation: hunt high-comment-count outliers in target videos (specific items or mechanics), integrate into own content to guarantee community interest.
7. **Two-pass generation:** Low-resolution discovery pass for brainstorming + high-fidelity refinement using polished start frames. Failed frames go to external image tools, get refined, come back as re-imported start frames.
8. **Platform consolidation over tool proliferation:** Single aggregator subscription (OpenArt, Higgsfield, Weavy, Freepik) beats 5+ individual tool subscriptions. Workflow serialization (saved "boards") reduces iterative overhead. Multi-model orchestration routes tasks to best available model without subscription fatigue.

**SNIPED application:**

- Composite Environment Rotation directly maps to element tagging + Kling's cinematic prompt matrix (Genre-Scene-Subject-Action-Camera-Audio). The 7 locked environments (Brutalist Monument, Industrial Minimal, etc.) become the element library. Each shoot produces tagged assets usable across infinite future sequences.

- LoRA training on 10+ high-quality 1080p source videos produces a persistent character model that locks identity while allowing wardrobe and environment variation. This is the technical execution of edit register bifurcation applied to video.
- Conflict Arc framework applies to Direction Stack Chapter Cards: what assumption does the viewer bring to the image, and where does the image break it? The reversal is not narrative in a video sense; it is the visual irony (quiet restraint surprising someone who expected loud commercial photography).

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:**
