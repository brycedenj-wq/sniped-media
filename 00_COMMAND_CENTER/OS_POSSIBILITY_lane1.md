## 10. What could go wrong

These are the real failure modes, grounded in doctrine, not hedging:

**1. Proof stalls at zero because the offer never reaches the market.** The "build internal-tight before market" rule (OS_MASTER_DOCTRINE.md, Locked Truth 10) is protective but has a failure mode: indefinite internal polish delays the first real conversation. PROOF_LOOPS_30_60_90.md opened with all rows at UNTESTED. If they stay UNTESTED at 60 days, the system is running in theory only.

**2. Social norms get contaminated before warming completes.** Trust Architecture (OS_MASTER_DOCTRINE.md §3): "Once money introduced into social relationship, social norm collapses irreversibly." If a VIB DM jumps to sales language before warming is complete, the contact is burned, not recoverable. The LinkedIn Comment Doctrine (5-10/day, soft opener, no emojis, 3-window cadence) is protection against this, but it requires discipline.

**3. Pricing anchor gets set wrong on the first conversation.** Anchor Sequencing doctrine: "Sequence: context frame > price > proof. Anchor high; present premium option first." If Bryce names $500 or trades scope before naming price in a discovery call, the anchor is permanently low for that prospect and their referral network.

**4. Case study gap persists and VIB conversion collapses.** The VIB DM structure requires one subtle proof signal. Without a case study, the proof leg of the DM is weak. If the first few VIBs go out without a named outcome reference, conversion rate suffers and the outreach system cannot calibrate.

**5. Direction Stack intellectual property leaks before monetization.** The Direction Stack is described as "method (internal) + positioning device (serialized content) + product later (book after 3-5 paid Resets)." If LinkedIn content reveals the full methodology before the product is built and priced, the serialization window closes. IP succession is flagged in OS_MASTER_DOCTRINE.md §8 contradictions as unaddressed.

**6. Category design gap allows a competitor to name the category first.** Play Bigger doctrine: category kings capture 76-93% of market cap. "Category name not yet coined, available, or locked" is named as "the single highest-leverage unfinished task before Direction Stack launch." A competitor who coins the category first captures the frame.

**7. Scene-density thinking collapses into audience-growth behavior under pressure.** Scene-density vs. audience-growth (OS_MASTER_DOCTRINE.md §1, confirmed from the intel pile): every shoot and relationship asks "does this thicken the scene?" Under revenue pressure, the instinct is to cast widely or pitch anyone with a following. This is the exact opposite of what compounds.
