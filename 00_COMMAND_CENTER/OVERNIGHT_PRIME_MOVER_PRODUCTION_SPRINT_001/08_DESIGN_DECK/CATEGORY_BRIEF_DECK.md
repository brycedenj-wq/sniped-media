# THE CATEGORY BRIEF

### SOLE HOUSE · The Repositioning Deck

**A 72-Hour AI-Native Campaign House**

Internal demo build. Faceless by construction: no operator identity, no client face, no shoot. Board-ready sales deck, 15 slides. Every slide carries a Vault Room visual direction note so the deck can be rendered against the locked world without further art direction.

World lock: The Vault Room. Palette: Ink black `#0A0A0B`, Bone white `#EDE8DD`, SOLE brass `#A8843C`, Concrete grey `#6B6B6E`, Vault shadow `#1C1C1E`. Type: high-contrast didone display (engraved nameplate) over a quiet grotesque body. The recurring composition is always the single object under one shaft of hard top-light.

---

## SLIDE 01 · COVER

**Slide title:** The Sole Claim

**Headline copy:**
We do not make you better. We make you the only one.

**Body / support copy:**
SOLE HOUSE. A 72-hour repositioning house.
One claim. One world. One name the market cannot copy.

**Visual direction (Vault Room):**
Full Ink black field. Dead center, a single brass Singular Seal embossed into the surface, lit by one hard shaft of top-light so the engraved numeral 1 (a single unbroken vertical stroke inside a vault-door ring) catches and the rest of the medallion falls into Vault shadow. The wordmark SOLE set small in didone caps at the lower third, letter-spaced wide, in Bone white. No tagline clutter, no subhead stack. The cover should feel acquired and archived, not launched. One object, one light, one name.

## SLIDE 02 · THE COMMODITIZATION PROBLEM

**Slide title:** AI Flattened Your Category

**Headline copy:**
"Good enough" is now infinite, instant, and free.

**Body / support copy:**
Every competitor in your market just got the same tools you have. The same copy engine, the same site builder, the same brand-in-a-box. The work that used to separate you is now a button anyone can press. You are not losing to a better firm. You are losing to a market where everyone looks identical and the only visible difference left is price.

**Visual direction (Vault Room):**
A long polished stone floor receding into Vault shadow. On it, a row of identical plinths stretching to the vanishing point, each holding an identical grey object, all lit evenly and flatly in Concrete grey. No single shaft of light, deliberately: this is the one slide where the SOLE light is absent. The flatness is the point. Bone white headline floats top-left. The sameness should feel oppressive, like a warehouse of interchangeable units.

## SLIDE 03 · THE COST OF BEING INVISIBLE

**Slide title:** Invisible Is Expensive

**Headline copy:**
When the market cannot tell you apart, it sorts you by price. And price is a race you lose slowly.

**Body / support copy:**
You are competent. You are profitable. You are also indistinguishable, and that is the quiet emergency. Indistinct businesses defend margin by discounting, chase every lead because none are loyal, and watch "the client went with the cheaper option" become a recurring line in the quarterly review. The cost is not one lost deal. It is the permanent ceiling on what you can charge and the permanent floor under your anxiety. The pain here is status, not cash. And status does not recover on its own.

**Visual direction (Vault Room):**
A single object on a plinth under the shaft of light, but the light is dimming frame-right, with a redaction bar (graphic motif) sliding across the object, partially obscuring it. Long shadow stretching away. Concrete grey dominant, brass barely present. The composition should feel like something valuable being archived into obscurity. Headline in Bone white, the phrase "race you lose slowly" set in brass for emphasis.
