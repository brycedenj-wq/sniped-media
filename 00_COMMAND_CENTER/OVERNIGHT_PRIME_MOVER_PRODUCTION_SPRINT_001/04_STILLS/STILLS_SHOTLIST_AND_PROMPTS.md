# SOLE HOUSE · THE STILLS SHOT LIST + GENERATION PROMPTS

**Purpose:** Operator-ready generation sheet for the Vault Room world bible. Consumed directly by Higgsfield (image plates) and Blender (hero-object renders). Every prompt below is ready to paste.

**World lock:** The Vault Room. A vast, dim, monolithic interior between a private bank vault, a brutalist cathedral, and a museum after hours. One object under one shaft of hard top-light. The client is always the one object under the light.

**Palette lock (paste into every prompt):** ink black ground #0A0A0B, bone white light #EDE8DD, SOLE brass #A8843C as the single struck metal, concrete grey #6B6B6E connective neutral, vault shadow charcoal #1C1C1E. No teal, no orange, no SaaS gradients, no modern-minimal startup white.

**Register lock:** photographic editorial, large-format stills, Roversi and Meisel restraint, Calvin Klein 90s austerity, Aman hotel materiality, museum vitrine lighting, Swiss private-bank lobby, heist-film title-sequence gravity. Everything looks acquired, archived, singular.

**Faceless constraint (non-negotiable):** no real people, no operator identity, no client face. Where a human presence is needed, it is a fully synthetic figure, silhouetted or back-lit to pure shadow, never a recognizable face, never a real likeness. Default to objects and architecture over figures.

**The Singular Seal:** a struck brass medallion engraved with the numeral 1 rendered as a single unbroken vertical stroke, set inside a circular vault-door ring. Watermarked into stills, embossed on the deck cover, struck into the final film frame.

**Aspect lock:** generate every still at 16:9 as the master. Re-generate (do not crop) a 4:5 variant for the deck, the landing, and vertical placements. The 4:5 note under each shot says what to recompose so the subject and the hard top-light survive the taller frame.

## BLENDER HERO-OBJECT RENDERS (2)

These are the precision Singular Seal renders. Build the medallion once as a clean parametric mesh, render two presentations. Use these for the deck cover emboss, the film sign-off, and the Sovereign-tier physical-object reference. They must out-resolve any Higgsfield approximation of the Seal.

**Geometry (build once):** A disc 60mm diameter, 6mm thick. Outer raised vault-door ring with concentric machined steps and shallow radial spokes. A single unbroken vertical stroke (the numeral 1) recessed and engraved dead center, full height of the inner field, crisp 0.8mm chamfered edges. No serifs, no foot, no flag. One stroke only.

### B1. THE MEDALLION ON THE PLINTH (hero render)
**Role:** Deck cover hero, film sign-off frame, master beauty render.
**Material notes:** Warm aged brass, base color #A8843C, metallic 1.0, roughness 0.28 on polished faces and 0.5 on the engraved recesses for contrast. Subtle anisotropy along the machined ring steps. A faint procedural micro-scratch and edge-wear mask so it reads struck and handled, not CGI-clean. Tiny brass fingerprint-free patina darkening in the engraved valley of the vertical stroke.
**Lighting notes:** One hard area light directly overhead as the key (the shaft of top-light), small enough to throw a crisp specular line along the ring and the stroke. One very low brass-bounce fill from below to keep the recesses from going pure black. HDRI off or near-black so the background reads ink black #0A0A0B. Plinth is dark matte concrete (#6B6B6E, roughness 0.9). Camera at a slight 15-degree high angle so the emboss depth reads.
**Output:** 16:9 master at 4K, plus a top-down 1:1 flat-on variant (no perspective) for the deck cover emboss and the watermark alpha. Render with a clean alpha channel so the medallion can be composited over any Vault Room plate.

### B2. THE STRIKE FRAME (motion-ready render)
**Role:** Film final-frame sign-off, landing locked-to-live strike, the ceremonial beat.
**Material notes:** Same brass material as B1, but add a hot bone white #EDE8DD specular glint racing along the top rim to read as the instant of the strike. Slightly increase the polished-face contrast so the rim flares.
**Lighting notes:** Same overhead hard key, but add one tight rim light from camera-left at brass color to flare the edge at the strike moment. Background pure ink black #0A0A0B, no plinth (the medallion floats centered for the sign-off). Render a 3-frame micro-sequence (rim glint travelling across the rim) so After Effects can time the strike, or a single hero frame plus the alpha.
**Output:** 16:9 at 4K centered with generous black margin for the wordmark below, clean alpha. Match the framing of still #10 (THE STRIKE) and still #14 (THE LOCKED LANDING) so Blender and Higgsfield versions are interchangeable in the cut.
