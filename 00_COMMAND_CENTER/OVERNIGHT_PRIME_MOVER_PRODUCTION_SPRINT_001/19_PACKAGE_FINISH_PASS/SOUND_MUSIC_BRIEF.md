# SOUND + MUSIC BRIEF , SOVRA/SOLE manifesto

> Audio stack gate = BLOCK on one required item: a music (melody) engine. ElevenLabs (VO + SFX) PASS. This brief is the exact blocker + the spec to execute the moment a music engine is approved. VO + SFX + room-tone are built this pass (free tier); MUSIC is the only held layer.

## EXACT BLOCKER (logged, not hand-waved)
- ElevenLabs does VO and SFX, NOT melody/score. A scored bed needs a paid engine (Suno / Udio) OR a licensed library cue (Musicbed / Artlist). No paid audio subscription is approved yet -> music generation is HELD. The film therefore ships as a VO + SFX + room-tone master, music pending.

## Built this pass (free tier)
- **VO**: full manifesto read (see VOICE_SCRIPT_V3_TAGGED.md).
- **Seal-strike SFX**: one percussive die-stamp (deep thud + bright metallic ring, fast decay). The locked SOVRA audio mark.
- **Room-tone bed**: deep, cold, reverberant vault ambience under the full runtime (generated via ElevenLabs text_to_sound_effects, free tier). This is sound design, not music.

## Music spec (execute on approval)
- One austere register: a low sustained drone (cello/contrabass + sub), almost no melody, a single slow swell into the claim (beat 4), and a sub-drop fused with the Seal strike (beat 6). Silence is part of the score. No risers, no trailer hits, no percussion loops.
- Length: 92s bed + a 30/15/6 conform. Key: minor, static. Tempo: implied, ~50-60 bpm feel.
- Engine recommendation: **Suno (instrumental mode)** for a custom SOVRA drone (fast, cheap, on-register), with a **licensed library cue as the zero-AI-provenance fallback** for buyer-facing use.
- Reference: the held-breath before a vault opens; a private-bank lobby at night; the low end of a Johann Johannsson / heist-film score, stripped of melody.
