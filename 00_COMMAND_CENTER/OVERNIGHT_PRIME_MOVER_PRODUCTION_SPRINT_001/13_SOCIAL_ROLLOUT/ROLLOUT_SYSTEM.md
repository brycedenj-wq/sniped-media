## 0. VOICE + GUARDRAILS

**The house speaks, never a person.** First-person plural ("we"), or no person at all (the verdict states itself). No founder bio, no "hey guys," no face on camera, no behind-the-scenes of a human. The account is the House: an institution that issues verdicts and strikes a seal.

**Register:** private bank, heist-film title card, museum after hours. Short declarative sentences. No exclamation points. No emojis. No hashparty. One or two precise hashtags maximum, and only where the platform rewards them.

**The four-word spine, repeated until it is owned:** *We do not make you better. We make you the only one.*

**Hard nos:** no em-dashes, no SaaS gradient language, no "level up / unlock / game-changer," no process bragging about AI tools (the stack is invisible plumbing), no teal/orange, no stock-startup optimism. The product is status, not software.

**Faceless-safe construction:** every frame is a Vault Room plate, a hero object under the shaft of light, a Seal, or pure typography on `#0A0A0B`. If a concept would require a human face to land, it is cut.
