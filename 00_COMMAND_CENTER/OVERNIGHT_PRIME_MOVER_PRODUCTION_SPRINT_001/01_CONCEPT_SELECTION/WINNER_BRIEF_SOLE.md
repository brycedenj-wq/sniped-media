**The Vault Room.** A single ownable world: a vast, dim, monolithic interior somewhere between a private bank vault, a brutalist cathedral, and a museum after hours. The client is the one object under the light.

**Palette (hex):**

| Role | Color | Hex |

| Bone white (light) | warm off-white | `#EDE8DD` |

No teal/orange. No SaaS gradients. No modern-minimal startup white.

**Motifs:** a shaft of hard top-light striking one object on a plinth. Long shadows, polished stone floors. Embossed not printed surfaces. Vault-door circular geometry. Archive drawers. Redaction bars as a graphic device. The single object under the light is always the recurring composition.

**Type:** a high-contrast didone display (engraved nameplate register) paired with a quiet grotesque for body.

**References:** Roversi and Meisel restraint, the lobby of a Swiss private bank, Aman hotel materiality, the title sequence of a heist film, Calvin Klein 90s campaign austerity, museum vitrine lighting. Everything looks acquired, archived, singular.

**Risk discipline:** the Vault Room is the moat. Visual drift is the primary failure mode, so the world is pre-rigged with locked templates and the Seal. Only the claim and copy vary per client.
