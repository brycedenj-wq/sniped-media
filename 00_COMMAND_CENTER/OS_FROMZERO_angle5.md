Vendors in this space have large marketing budgets and almost nothing worth spending them on that is credible. Sponsorship of a well-produced insider YouTube episode can command $5,000-$20,000 from a single vendor once the audience is proven (500-2,000 niche subscribers is enough in this domain; the buyers are capital allocators, not consumers).
