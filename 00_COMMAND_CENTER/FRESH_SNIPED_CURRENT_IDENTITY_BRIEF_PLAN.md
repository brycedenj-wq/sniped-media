## 7. Proof-loop structure + 30/60/90 test plan (the core of the brief · operator fills the specifics)

The brief replaces "ingest more" with "run reps and measure." A proof loop is: **hypothesis -> smallest real test -> observed signal -> keep/kill/iterate.** The 30/60/90 frame (targets are the operator's to set; the plan defines the shape, not the content):

| Horizon | Purpose | What would count as proof (examples · operator sets the real ones) |
|---|---|---|
| **30 days** | cheapest real-world signal | shipped one concrete artifact/offer to a real audience; had N real conversations with the actual target buyer; one paid or near-paid signal |
| **60 days** | repeatability | the 30-day signal repeated (not a fluke); a second cohort/test; an early pattern in who responds and why |
| **90 days** | direction-worthy evidence | enough repeated proof to *consider* (not lock) canonizing one direction; clear keep/kill calls on the rest; the held identity material reviewed against real evidence, not theory |

**Rule:** a direction is not canonized by argument or by corpus depth · it is canonized only after the proof loop produces repeated real-world evidence. Until then it stays a live option. This is the BJJ day-one principle made operational.
