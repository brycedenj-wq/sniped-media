## 1. Current architecture problem
The tension is four-sided: (a) BASEPLATE cannot be reduced to photography (locked guardrail); (b) SNIPED has genuine creative/camera equity (brand, Direction Stack, the Reset offer, LA cultural access); (c) BJ still has the camera, taste, connects, and desire, all real advantages; (d) the camera is both a faster money lever (founder portraits) and a status/access lever (the LA scene), so it cannot be discarded. The trap is letting these collide: photography visible under BASEPLATE reduces the infra thesis to "a guy with a camera"; killing SNIPED wastes a real edge and the C4 fallback; running both as one brand confuses two different buyers. The fix is structural separation, not a choice between them.

## 4. Best architecture
- **One legal parent, two public brands.** Baseplate, LLC is the holding company (it already owns the Direction Stack IP). Under it: BASEPLATE = the infra operator brand (baseplate.systems/hq), and SNIPED Media = the camera/creative/founder-presence brand (snipedmedia.com).
- **SNIPED stays SEPARATE from BASEPLATE.** Different buyers, different aesthetics, different domains. No fold, ever.
- **SNIPED is PARKED but defined and public-capable**, not retired. SNIPED Media remains the photography/email identity and the founder-presence lane (the C4 fallback).
- **Photography under BASEPLATE happens only as the dossier instrument**, never as a branded photography service. BASEPLATE's word is "proof," not "photos."
- **No redirects between the two brands.** snipedmedia.com stays on the camera lane; baseplate domains stay on infra. snipedmedia.com is never pointed at BASEPLATE.

## 5. Money path (SNIPED)
- **Now:** $0 from SNIPED by choice. Focus is the BASEPLATE internal system. SNIPED is parked.
- **30 days:** still parked unless debt forces it. Cashflow escape valve: if near-term cash is needed, run a deliberate, time-boxed batch of Reset founder portraits ($1,500) to the warm network, NOT as a focus-replacement, capped so it does not eat the BASEPLATE reps.
- **90 days:** at the BASEPLATE proof gate, if infra is converting, keep SNIPED parked; if infra stalls (the C4 trigger), activate SNIPED's founder-presence lane as the live cash+proof engine.
- **1 year:** if activated, SNIPED could be a premium founder-presence + cultural-content brand with a retainer layer (the high-ceiling cultural/status path).
- **Offers (when activated):** founder portrait / Reset ($1,500), operator presence package, editorial/content day, Direction-Stack-guided shoot, recurring founder content retainer (later), creative production for select clients (later). School/event: decline (low ceiling, off-thesis).

## 6. Relationship to BASEPLATE
- **SNIPED can do what BASEPLATE can't:** consumer/founder-facing creative, personal presence, status/cultural plays, IG mythology, AI-forward art. All of which would reduce BASEPLATE if done under it.
- **BASEPLATE can do what SNIPED can't:** B2B infra trust, capability proof, the operator-layer thesis, the AI-Ops/network endgame.
- **Where the camera supports BASEPLATE:** as the instrument inside the Capability Dossier (real, security-safe imagery of a firm's people/work). Internal, not branded.
- **Where it must stay separate:** the brand, the domain, the audience, the public positioning. An infra buyer never sees a SNIPED portfolio or an IG mythology link.
- **Avoiding reduction:** BASEPLATE markets "proof," media is the instrument; the word "photography" never headlines BASEPLATE.
- **Avoiding buyer confusion:** never cross the domains; never send a staffing-firm owner to snipedmedia.com; never put founder-portrait offers on the BASEPLATE surface.

## 7. Role of Direction Stack
Still valuable. It is a method (a directing diagnostic), not a brand. Homes: (a) it is SNIPED's product/method for the camera lane (the founder-presence shoots run on it, and it can become a SNIPED product/book later); (b) it is the internal production standard inside BASEPLATE's dossier (the same diagnostic discipline produces the dossier's imagery). IP is held by Baseplate, LLC (the parent). It is never publicly branded on BASEPLATE (that would reduce the infra brand to a photography method). One method, two private uses, one owner.

## 8. Role of AI visual tools
- **SNIPED is the safe home for creative AI experiments** (the IG mythology/aesthetic engine, per the platform-split doctrine). SNIPED can publish AI-forward creative openly as art/concepting, clearly authored, never faked as a real client outcome.
- **BASEPLATE uses AI for concepting/atmosphere only, never as client evidence.** The dossier proof is always real, photographed, client-approved.
- **The line (both brands):** AI may build atmosphere, concepts, and mythology; AI may never be presented as real proof of a real client's people/work/outcomes. Higgsfield/Kling = SNIPED's playground + internal concepting; never BASEPLATE client evidence.

## 9. Decision tree (which lane owns a project)
- **BASEPLATE** if: buyer = an infra firm (staffing/sub), outcome = a hire or a bid, deliverable = capability proof.
- **SNIPED** if: buyer = a founder/operator/individual, outcome = personal presence/status/brand, deliverable = portraits/creative/content.
- **BJ personal brand** if: it is BJ's own named-author content/thought leadership (this feeds BASEPLATE's distribution layer and can feed SNIPED's cultural brand; the author is the person, not either company brand).
- **Decline** if: low-margin commodity (school/event), off-thesis, or it would cross/confuse the lanes or reduce BASEPLATE.

## 10. Final recommendation
**PARK SNIPED FOR 30 DAYS** as a defined, separate camera/creative/founder-presence brand under Baseplate, LLC, ready to activate, not retired and not folded. Reasons: the current objective is the BASEPLATE internal system + proof; splitting focus now would delay it (overbuild/avoidance risk), and folding would reduce BASEPLATE (forbidden). SNIPED is preserved as the C4 fallback and a deliberate cashflow escape valve (the Reset), with a clear trigger at the 30-day BASEPLATE gate: activate SNIPED's founder-presence lane if infra stalls or if debt forces cash; otherwise keep it parked. This kills nothing emotionally, keeps nothing from nostalgia, honors the camera/taste/Direction-Stack advantage, and protects BASEPLATE from reduction.
