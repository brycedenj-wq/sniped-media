## 1. Purpose and scope
Govern how every reply, call, objection, proposal, payment, delivery, referral, AI-Ops pull, media signal, and pivot signal gets recorded, so decisions run on evidence. Scope is the logging discipline itself: what counts, the schema, who logs, the review cadence, and the pivot rules. The proof tracker (PROOF_LOOPS_30_60_90) is the scoreboard; this SOP is the rulebook for filling it honestly.

## 2. Why proof logging exists
Because memory lies and vibes inflate. The OS warns that a system that never meets a paying customer is hollow no matter how polished. The proof log is the only thing that converts "sound thinking" into "a real business," and the only thing that can honestly kill or keep the thesis. It exists to stop two failures: counting praise as progress, and continuing a dead direction from ego.

## 3. What counts as proof
- **Money committed:** a paid deposit, a paid Pilot, or a signed paid LOI.
- **Access granted:** a non-secure shoot approved, an intro made, a bid-or-role-use commitment.
- **Repeatable demand:** a second buyer, an inbound, a referral.
- **System pull:** a buyer asking, unprompted, for more than the dossier (recruiting/bid/documentation/reporting).
- **A named, concrete anchor on every one:** a specific role or bid, not "we hire sometimes."
Real proof = a verifiable action by the buyer (paid, granted access, referred), tied to a named role/bid.

## 4. What does NOT count as proof
- "Looks great," "send me info," "we'll keep you in mind," "circle back next quarter."
- A booked call with no outcome.
- A sent proposal with no payment.
- Compliments, LinkedIn likes, encouragement from friends/peers.
- BJ's own confidence or a polished artifact.
- Anything fabricated. Interest is not proof; only buyer action is.

## 5. Proof categories
- **Reply proof:** a real reply from a decision-maker (positive/objection/neutral). Logged, but a reply alone is Yellow until it becomes a call or commitment.
- **Call proof:** a discovery call held; logged with the qualification outcome (G/Y/R).
- **Proposal proof:** a proposal sent to a qualified buyer; Yellow until paid.
- **Payment proof:** deposit or Pilot paid, or signed paid LOI. This is Green, the core proof (T1/T2 pass).
- **Delivery proof:** a dossier delivered and client-approved; the artifact behind a paid engagement.
- **Referral proof:** a buyer refers another, or inbound arrives. Repeatability signal (60-day).
- **AI-Ops / system-pull proof:** unprompted request for systems beyond the dossier. Logged as a thesis signal, not acted on yet.
- **Media / intelligence pull proof:** interest in BJ's perspective/content, "where do you post," candidate-facing distribution interest. Logged as a distribution-layer signal.
- **C4 / founder fallback proof:** signals that fit/energy points to founders/operators over infra procurement. Logged toward the 30-day infra-vs-C4 gate.
- **Red-flag / kill proof:** repeated "won't pay a premium," "we win on price only," "can't show anything," or 0 paid after the threshold. Logged as evidence toward revise/kill.

## 6. Green / Yellow / Red definitions
- **Green:** buyer action taken, money committed or access granted, tied to a named role/bid. Real proof.
- **Yellow:** genuine interest but no action yet (reply, call held, proposal sent, "not now" with a real trigger). Track and follow up; not proof.
- **Red:** disqualified or negative (no decision-maker, price-only, nothing showable, treats it as headshots, hard no). Evidence toward revise/kill; do not chase.

## 7. Proof entry schema (one block per real interaction)
```
- Date:
- Source: (warm/network, LinkedIn, Instantly, referral, inbound)
- Segment: (staffing / sub / founder-C4)
- Company:
- Contact / Title:
- Channel: (email / LinkedIn DM / call)
- Action taken: (what BJ did)
- Response: (what the buyer said/did)
- Buyer pain: (the real problem named)
- Named role or bid: (specific, or "none yet")
- Economic value: (placement $ / bid $ if known)
- Security constraint: (what can/can't be shown)
- Outcome: (paid / access / proposal / nurture / disqualified)
- Next step:
- R/Y/G rating:
- Thesis signal: (infra pull / AI-Ops pull / media pull / C4 / kill)
- Artifact affected: (which playbook this informs, if any)
- Follow-up date:
- Notes:
```
Fill every field honestly; "none yet" and "unknown" are valid and useful. The schema is the discipline; vague entries defeat the log.

## 8. When each artifact logs proof
- **Outbound Activation Playbook:** logs every send and every reply (reply proof, R/Y/G).
- **Admin Lead Research SOP:** admin does NOT judge proof; admin enters data BJ dictates and flags any inbound reply to BJ. No interpretation.
- **Discovery + Qualification Playbook:** logs every call held (call proof + qualification outcome + thesis signal).
- **Proposal + Invoice Playbook:** logs proposal sent (proposal proof) and payment (payment proof, the Green/T1-T2 trigger).
- **Sample Dossier Build Playbook:** logs a delivered, client-approved dossier (delivery proof) and whether it moved the outcome when known.

## 9. Admin logging role
Admin enters factual data into the tracker (the section 7 fields BJ provides), keeps the sheet clean, dedupes, and flags any prospect reply to BJ immediately. Admin never rates R/Y/G, never interprets a thesis signal, never decides what counts as proof. Data entry and hygiene only.

## 10. BJ logging role
BJ logs the judgment: the R/Y/G rating, the thesis signal, the outcome, the next step, and the honest read. BJ logs every real interaction the same day (no memory, only the log). BJ owns the keep/kill/iterate calls at the checkpoints.

## 11. Daily proof review
At end of day: log every interaction from the day; update the pipeline number (sends out, replies, calls booked, proposals live, dollars committed); set follow-up dates. 5 minutes. The anti-hiding check: a day of building with zero real contact attempts is a flag, not progress.

## 12. Weekly proof review
A short pass: tally the week (contacts, replies, calls, proposals, paid); identify the converting angle (or the bottleneck per the deliverability/response data); decide one message/segment adjustment only if a real pattern shows; move H1/H2 status; write a one-line week summary in PROOF_LOOPS.

## 13. 30-day checkpoint (~ 2026-06-25)
Cheapest-signal review. Question: did real conversations and offers happen, did anyone pay/grant access? First read on T1 (staffing) and T2 (subs), and the first infra-vs-C4 signal. Apply the pivot rules (section 16) only against logged evidence.

## 14. 60-day checkpoint (~ 2026-07-25)
Repeatability review. Question: does the signal repeat, any inbound/referral, any AI-Ops or media pull? Looking for second-order demand and a delivery workflow clear enough to repeat.

## 15. 90-day checkpoint (~ 2026-08-24)
Direction-worthy-evidence review. Question: real case studies, retained demand, a buyer pulling for systems? Decide infra-vs-C4 against evidence, not theory. Even here, "consider," not "lock," per the proof-loop doctrine.

## 16. Pivot rules (evidence only, never ego)
- **Continue BASEPLATE infra:** T1 or T2 passes (a paid Pilot/LOI) and rooms open. Keep running.
- **Revise the wedge:** interest but no payment after ~5 real conversations per segment, or the dossier is consistently not what moves the decision. Revise the offer/segment/price once, deliberately, logged.
- **Test C4 / founders fallback:** if the fit/energy evidence points to founders over infra procurement, or infra stays Red after the threshold. Test, do not abandon infra on a whim.
- **Park AI-Ops:** keep it parked unless a real system-pull signal repeats AND the employment/IP attorney check clears anything touching data-center operations.
- **Continue without platform/network language:** always, until the network is earned by data and demand. No public platform/registry claim regardless of momentum.
Two or more kill-signals sustained after real reps = revise (likely toward C4 or a narrower layer). No pivot without logged evidence; no continuing a dead thesis from ego.

## 17. AI-Ops / system-pull logging
When a buyer asks, unprompted, for more than the dossier (recruiting/bid/documentation/reporting systems), log it: date, company, what they asked, how concrete. Tag thesis signal = "AI-Ops pull." Do not scope or sell it (later layer), and never anything touching data-center operations without the attorney check. A repeating pull is evidence the wedge-to-AI-Ops path is real, surfaced at the 30/60/90 gates.

## 18. Named-author media / intelligence logging
When a buyer signals interest in BJ's perspective/content, asks "where do you post," or wants candidate-facing distribution, log it: tag thesis signal = "media pull." It informs when to start the named-author media seed (BJ-timed, internal-first). Do not pivot a call to it; just record the pull.

## 19. False positives (the things that feel like proof but are not)
- Enthusiasm with no named role/bid and no money ("this is amazing, let's talk soon").
- A booked call that no-shows or goes nowhere.
- A marketing person who loves it but is not the decision-maker.
- A "yes in principle" with no invoice paid.
- Peer/friend encouragement.
- A polished sample dossier (it is an illustration, not proof of demand).
Each gets logged as Yellow (or Red), never Green. Calling a false positive "proof" is how a dead thesis survives too long.

## 20. No-fake-proof rules
Never invent a pilot, a client, a testimonial, a case study, or a metric. Never log praise as payment. Never inflate "interest" into "proof." Never round a "maybe" up to a "yes." The log's only value is its honesty; a single fabricated entry makes the whole scoreboard worthless and would destroy a procurement buyer's trust if it ever surfaced. If unsure, log it lower (Yellow over Green).

## 21. Where proof lives
The single source of truth is PROOF_LOOPS_30_60_90 (the proof-entry blocks in its log section + the 30/60/90 tables). A mirror tracking sheet (Pixieset Studio Manager or a simple Google Sheet with the section 7 fields) can hold the operational pipeline, but PROOF_LOOPS is canonical. No proof lives only in a chat or BJ's head.

## 22. How to update PROOF_LOOPS_30_60_90
- Add a Proof entry (section 7 schema) per real interaction under the relevant hypothesis (H1/H2) + the "infra-buyer pull (staffing)/(subs)" line.
- Move H1/H2 from UNTESTED to TESTING on the first real call; toward KEEP/KILL/ITERATE as signals land.
- Check the 30/60/90 boxes as targets are met.
- Write the weekly one-line summary.
- This SOP does NOT edit PROOF_LOOPS now; it defines how BJ does it when real interactions begin.

## 23. What not to log
Employer/confidential information; the employer's customers/vendors/employees obtained via inside knowledge; facility-sensitive details; client-confidential names/projects under NDA; PII without consent; anything that violates the security-safe or employer-clean guardrails. The proof log records BASEPLATE's own market interactions, not protected information.

## 24. What not to do
No fabricated entries. No counting praise/interest as proof. No inflating Yellow to Green. No pivot without logged evidence. No continuing a Red thesis from ego. No logging employer/confidential or facility-sensitive data. No platform/registry language creeping in on momentum. No reducing the offer to "photos" in the notes. No automation of the log before there is real volume to justify it.

## 25. Guardrails
No fake proof, invented pilots, fake testimonials, or inflated interest. No "looks good" counted as payment proof. No platform/registry/network public claim. No photography-only reduction (the dossier is the deliverable). No employer/confidential information; no targeting employer customers/vendors/employees via inside knowledge; no facility-sensitive details. No automation before proof. No pivot without evidence; no continuing a dead thesis from ego. Old SNIPED/BASEPLATE material is historical evidence, not law. Bible held.
