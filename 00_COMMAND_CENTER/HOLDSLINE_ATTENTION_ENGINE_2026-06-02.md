# HOLDS LINE: THE ATTENTION ENGINE
### One operating document for THE FIELD TEE · 001

The garment is quiet. The campaign carries the heat. This document keeps the heat on-brand instead of generic-AI, and turns it into a waitlist and a preorder.

Two engines, one rule.

- **PROOF ENGINE = real.** Canon R6 Mark II. The garment, fabric macro, fit-on-body, the after-wash measurement, the founder's face, real wearers. Never AI.
- **ATTENTION ENGINE = AI.** Higgsfield stack. The campaign world, the house-model avatar, impossible environments, motion ads, surreal product films, content volume. Openly stylized.

The one rule that holds the whole thing together: **AI builds the room. The camera makes the receipt.** Museum Room Theory: you do not need a new message, you need a new room. The garment is the object. The AI world is the all-white museum room that makes a $48 tee read as studied. The proof always posts first. The myth only amplifies what the proof already earned.

Everything below is held against four standards, in order: **STOP** a scroll, **FEEL** like one brand, never **BREAK** trust, **DRIVE** a waitlist or preorder.

The system is pulled straight from the live site: palette bone `#f4f1ea`, ecru `#e9e3d6`, ink `#1c1a17`, stone `#6f6962`; Inter type; wordmark "Holds Line / Made in Los Angeles"; the "220 / 20" spec logic; the tag format "The Field Tee · 001."

## 1. THE AI-NATIVE CONTENT WORLD

### 1.1 The governing split (do not violate)

| Engine | Medium | Owns | Hard line |
|---|---|---|---|
| PROOF | Real (R6 Mark II) | The garment, fabric macro, fit-on-body, after-wash proof, the founder, real faces | Never AI-generate the product. The product-page hero is shot, never rendered. The after-wash proof is a real ruler on real cloth, forever. |
| ATTENTION | AI (Higgsfield) | Campaign world, house-model avatar, impossible environments, motion ads, surreal product films, volume | Never let AI touch a falsifiable claim. AI builds the room, not the receipt. |

### 1.2 The cast (a repertory company, not casting roulette)

Consistency across posts is the single biggest tell of "real brand vs random AI," so the cast is small and fixed. Four roles on rotation.

- **FIELD (the AI house-model).** A single, consistent AI avatar trained once via Higgsfield Soul ID, reused across the whole campaign. Never named on-camera. A face, not a personality. No voice, no lore, no "AI influencer" bit. The moment she has a backstory, the brand becomes the faceless-AI anti-pattern. Casting brief for her source frames: mid-to-late 20s, androgynous-leaning, lived-in not glossy, real pores, slightly asymmetric, neutral resting expression. She wears the tee like a uniform, not a costume. She lives in the impossible environments the R6 cannot reach. She **never** stands next to a falsifiable claim, never holds a ruler, never appears in the after-wash shot.
- **THE FOUNDER (real, the trust anchor).** Shot on R6 or native phone. Build-in-public, founder POV, the hand that washes the tee 20 times, the voice that says "I got tired of tees that turn into crop tops, so I'm fixing it." FIELD carries volume; the founder carries belief.
- **THE REAL WEARERS (real, the proof of belonging).** Candid, R6, real LA settings, different bodies and ages, minimal styling. Their job is to break the "everything here is AI" spell at least once per content cluster. Candid posture only.
- **THE GARMENT (the true protagonist).** The one thing in every post regardless of engine. Treat it like a face: consistent lighting, consistent color, consistent hang. When the cloth drifts post-to-post, the brand dies even if every face is perfect.

**The cast rule:** every content cluster (a week's batch) contains at least one founder appearance and one real-wearer appearance. The avatar never outnumbers the real by more than 2 to 1 in any week. Attention requires the avatar; trust requires the real face. The mix is the brand.

### 1.3 The world: THE QUIET FIELD (one world, four rooms)

Authorship comes from constrained world-building, not novelty. HOLDS LINE gets ONE world with a tight rotation inside it, never a free-for-all. A clean, depopulated, editorial near-future. Daylight and concrete. Restrained-surreal: one impossible thing per frame, never ten. Never tryhard-rich. No yachts, no marble lobbies played straight, no luxury cosplay.

**The palette is the world** (locked from the live site). Every frame, real or AI, lives in these five values:

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Bone | `#f4f1ea` | Primary ground. Walls, light, paper, negative space. |
| Ecru | `#e9e3d6` | Secondary ground. Warm shadow, fabric, plaster. |
| Ink | `#1c1a17` | Type, deepest value, the garment. Never pure `#000`. |
| Stone | `#6f6962` | Mid-tone. Concrete, fog, spec-mark text, shadow. |
| Line | `rgba(28,26,23,.14)` | Hairline rules, frame edges, the grid. |

No statement color. No teal/orange. No oversaturation. Every render is graded back into this five-value set in Adobe MCP before it ships. This is the strongest "one brand" signal there is, because the color is identical whether a frame was shot on R6 or generated by FIELD.

**The four rooms** (one per content chapter, never mixed within a chapter):

| Room | Visual traits | Use case | Engine |
|---|---|---|---|
| **THE ROOM** | One plain wall, one window, bone light, a single shadow. The default. | Founder POV, real fit-checks, quiet product film | R6 first, FIELD for impossible versions |
| **THE SLAB** | Brutalist concrete, daylight, monumental scale, one figure | Hero escalation, avatar editorial | FIELD (AI reaches scale R6 cannot) |
| **THE VOID** | Fog-grey to ecru gradient, no horizon, tee or figure floating in still air | Opacity/fabric mythology, identity heroes | FIELD + AI motion |
| **THE BLOCK** | A real, plain LA street corner, daylight, no landmarks | Real-wearer fit-checks, "uniform in the wild" | R6 only |

THE BLOCK is intentionally R6-only. The world needs one environment that is provably real, or the whole thing floats off into AI. Three constructed worlds, one undeniable street: that contrast is itself the brand statement. The clothes are real, the world is myth.

### 1.4 The visual language

**Type (locked from the live site).** Inter, weights 300/400/500/600. Wordmark `Holds Line` at `.36em` tracking, uppercase, 600. Subline `Made in Los Angeles` at 9px, `.3em`, 400. Spec text: 11px, `.32em`, uppercase, stone. Headlines: 400 weight, negative tracking, one bold word, lots of negative space. No second typeface. No display font. No serif flourish.

**The spec-mark motif (the brand's one piece of iconography).** The `220 / 20` mark (GSM / wash-count). It earns its place because it is functional, not decorative. Forms: `220 / 20` · `220 GSM · 20-WASH` · `THE FIELD TEE · 001`. Stamped, never glowing, never embossed-3D. Reads like a quartermaster's stamp, not a logo. Placement: bottom-left or a hairline-ruled caption bar, stone on bone, one per frame max. Motion: it does not animate in. It appears as a single hard cut, like a stamp hitting paper. Never a swoosh, never a fade-up-with-bloom. **Serialize everything:** `001`, `002`, `003`. Each post becomes a self-contained, numbered, branded artifact that survives being clipped or reposted. Attribution survives the clip.

**Composition (anti-"centered-void").** Never center the subject in an empty frame; that is the #1 AI composition tell. Rule of thirds, asymmetry, intentional negative space. One light source, directional, soft. Adobe Neutral foundation, lifted blacks (creamy, never crushed), rolled highlights (creamy, never paper-white `#fff`). Real lens behavior, plausible depth of field, no fake bokeh balls, no HDR. One impossible thing per surreal frame.

**Grade and finish (every frame, real or AI).** Adobe MCP pass: grade into the five-value palette, global saturation under 8, film grain ~15, sharpening ~35. The grain is the great equalizer: it makes the AI render and the camera frame share one surface so the seam disappears.

### 1.5 Motion grammar

Slow, quiet, deliberate. The opacity test is slow. The fold is slow. The reveal waits. Continuous takes over cuts where possible (the stretch-and-recover in one unbroken shot reads honest; rapid cuts read as hiding something). One move per shot: the camera does one thing, FIELD does one thing. The tee behaves like heavy cloth: 220 GSM does not flutter like silk; if AI renders it floating weightlessly, reject it. Spec-mark cuts hard, never blooms. 9:16 primary, reframe to 4:5 and 1:1. Audio, when used, is restrained: room tone, cloth sound, one quiet note. No epic-trailer swell.

### 1.6 The campaign rules (what makes this ONE world, not random AI posts)

1. One palette, always. Grade everything back into the five hexes.
2. One cast. FIELD is one `reference_id`, reused. The avatar never outnumbers the real by more than 2 to 1 per week.
3. One world, four rooms. One per chapter.
4. One spec-mark motif. `220 / 20`, stamped, functional, stone-on-bone.
5. Serialize everything. Document, not post.
6. AI builds the room, the camera makes the receipt. The proof posts before the myth.
7. Real and AI welded, not alternated randomly. Use the `media_upload` bridge so real cloth seeds the AI world.
8. The avatar has no lore.
9. Restraint over volume, every time. The Loewe test: would this sit in a Loewe campaign, or a YouTube preset pack?
10. Never name the aesthetic. No "quiet luxury," "old money," "elevated" on-camera. The restraint shows; it is never announced.
11. Pre-screen before posting. Every cut through `virality_predictor`.
12. The garment is consistent across every post.

## 2. THE CONTENT VOLUME SYSTEM

### 2.1 The honest ceiling for one operator + the AI stack

Volume is set by what AI can sustain at quality, anchored by a thin real spine that refreshes on a slow cadence.

| Surface | Per week | Quality bar | Source mix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feed posts | 4 to 5 | STOP + FEEL (every one portfolio-grade) | hybrid-led |
| Reels | 4 to 6 | STOP (hook-screened before posting) | AI-led, real-anchored |
| Stories | 15 to 25 frames | low-polish, high-trust | real-led |

Roughly 9 to 11 polished feed/reel pieces plus a running story layer, per week, indefinitely, from one person. That number is reachable only because real capture is **batched, not daily**. If the operator tries to shoot every piece real, the ceiling drops to about 3 polished pieces a week and the brand stalls.

### 2.2 The weekly cadence (fixed, repeating)

Repetition over novelty: the same week run 50 times builds the room far more than 50 different content plans.

- **Monday: PROOF.** 1 feed + 1 reel, real-anchored. Opacity hold-to-light, after-wash measurement, fabric macro. The week opens on honesty, not hype.
- **Tuesday: WORLD.** 1 feed + 1 reel, AI-led. The impossible editorial, surreal product film, avatar editorial. Pre-screened.
- **Wednesday: FIT / WEAR.** 1 feed + 1 reel, hybrid. Real garment on a real body graded into a built world, or a clean fit check.
- **Thursday: FOUNDER / BUILD.** 1 reel, native phone, founder voice. The preorder model stated plainly. No AI. Must read human.
- **Friday: HOOK / HUNT.** 1 feed + 1 reel, AI-led or hybrid, aimed at the "perfect white tee hunt." The most shareable, save-bait slot. Top candidate for the $30/day desktop boost if it lands organically.
- **Saturday: light.** 1 reel, a reframe/remix of the week's strongest asset. No new production.
- **Stories: every active day.** Behind-the-glass, polls, the wash test running live, DMs answered on screen, the waitlist count climbing.

### 2.3 The batching method: two day-types, decoupled

The whole system rests on decoupling shoot days from render days so the camera never gates volume.

**SHOOT DAY (real, R6, every 3 to 4 weeks).** One disciplined session feeds 3 to 4 weeks. Captured in priority order so the trust assets survive even a short day:

1. The after-wash measurement proof (day-one vs 20-wash, ruler in frame). The single most important asset in the brand.
2. Fabric macro: weave, hand, opacity hold-to-light.
3. The garment flat and on a stand (product frames + AI reference plates).
4. Fit-on-body in 2 to 3 real settings, real person, candid.
5. Motion clips: stretch-and-recover pull test, hold-to-light, slow fold. Continuous takes.
6. 5 to 20 clean face frames of the real model (the Soul ID training set, so the AI house-model traces to a real person who actually wore the tee).

Yield: the full product-page set, plus 30 to 50 real frames and 8 to 12 motion clips.

**RENDER DAY (AI, one day a week, desk-bound).** The volume engine. Generate campaign-world plates (`generate_image`, `nano_banana_pro` for hero/text, `soul_2` for editorial). Build surreal product films and motion ads (`generate_video`: `seedance_2_0` reference-driven, `kling3_0` multi-shot, `marketing_studio_video` for ads). Run hybrids via `media_upload` + `media_confirm`. Use the trained Soul ID (`text2image_soul_v2`, `soul_cinema_studio`) for the recurring face. Grade everything in Adobe MCP. `reframe` every asset to 9:16 / 4:5 / 1:1. Pre-screen every cut with `virality_predictor` at the desk; weak hooks die there, never posted.

**Two short edit-and-schedule blocks, ~1 hour each.** Caption, sequence, schedule. Founder POV is shot native on the phone the morning it posts.

Realistic labor footprint: one render day, two short edit blocks, phone-native founder/story moments, plus one shoot day every 3 to 4 weeks. That produces 9 to 11 polished pieces a week.

### 2.4 The AI / real / hybrid split, and why

| Surface | AI | Hybrid | Real | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Feed** | ~35% | ~45% | ~20% | The portfolio. Mostly hybrid: a real garment or proof frame inside a built world, one grade. Pure-AI is the impossible-world flex; pure-real holds the proof posts. |
| **Reels** | ~55% | ~30% | ~15% | The attention engine and volume driver. AI carries the load. Real reels are the pull-test and founder voice, which cannot be faked. |
| **Stories** | ~15% | ~15% | ~70% | The trust layer. Human, immediate, unproduced. AI shows up only as quick world-teasers for the next drop. |

Stated plainly: **trust requires proof, attention requires world.** Proof lives where the buyer verifies (stories, proof posts, product page) and is almost entirely real. Attention lives where the algorithm rewards spectacle (reels, campaign feed) and is mostly AI. Hybrid is the bridge that keeps the two from feeling like two different brands.

## 3. THE QUALITY GATE (the anti-slop firewall)

A finished render is not a shippable render. The buyer for a $48 honest tee reads fake instantly, and one slop post poisons the trust the whole brand is selling.

### 3.1 The PASS criteria (an AI asset ships only if all are true)

- It beats its source visually. If the render does not beat an honest R6 frame of the same idea, the R6 frame ships instead. The strongest image is not the most processed one.
- The skin reads human (pores, texture, asymmetry, warm not orange).
- The fabric reads like 220 GSM cloth: heavy hang, real weave, real weight in motion. Not plasticky shimmer.
- The hands are right (five fingers, correct joints, plausible grip).
- The light is motivated and directional, one source, Adobe Neutral grade.
- It lives in the palette and one of the four rooms, spec-mark present and correct.
- FIELD is recognizably the same person as the last 10 posts.
- The composition is intentional, asymmetric, purposeful negative space.
- It passes the Loewe test, not the preset-pack test.
- It cleared `virality_predictor` above the hook and retention floor.

### 3.2 The kill-list (reject if ANY is present)

| # | Tell | Why it kills HOLDS LINE |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plastic over-rendered skin | This buyer reads fake skin in under a second; signals the whole brand is fake, including the guarantee |
| 2 | Melting / wrong hands | Instant disqualifier, screenshotted and mocked |
| 3 | Fake-looking fabric (shimmery, weightless, glowing opacity) | The cloth IS the product. Fake fabric on the one thing the brand engineers is fatal |
| 4 | Generic stock-AI lighting (flat, sourceless glow) | Reads as Midjourney default, breaks the editorial register |
| 5 | Inconsistent character across posts | The biggest "random AI" tell. Use the Soul ID `reference_id` every time |
| 6 | Uncanny motion (morphing faces, wrong flutter, AI-flythrough) | One uncanny frame undoes the film |
| 7 | Over-saturation, any teal/orange, a "vibe" grade | Loud = cheap = AI in this market |
| 8 | The centered-void default | Reads as "nobody directed this" |
| 9 | Garbled text / fake spec-mark | Generate text in `gpt_image_2` / `nano_banana_pro` or set it in Adobe, never trust a raw render's letters |
| 10 | The tryhard-rich frame (yacht, supercar, marble cosplay) | Off-lane |
| 11 | AI making a real claim (a render standing in for proof, a fake ruler) | The unforgivable one. Faking the falsifiable claim destroys the moat permanently |
| 12 | Render that loses to its source | Completion is not quality. Ship the real frame |

### 3.3 The gate procedure (run on every AI asset, in order)

```
RENDER READY
  → PASS 1 · TELLS (mechanical, binary)
      scan the kill-list. ANY tell → REJECT.
        reject path: regenerate (fix prompt/seed/reference), OR
        swap for a real R6 frame, OR kill the post.
      closest attention: hands, skin, fabric, FIELD's identity vs the last 10, the spec-mark text.
  → PASS 2 · STANDARDS (taste, judged)
      STOP   does the first frame stop a thumb?
      FEEL   palette + cast + world + spec-mark correct? same brand as yesterday?
      TRUST  is anything falsifiable being faked? is real-where-promised respected?
      DRIVE  is there a clear path to a signup/preorder, or is it just pretty?
      any NO → REJECT or rework.
  → BEAT-THE-SOURCE CHECK
      does this beat an honest R6 frame of the same idea? NO → ship the R6 frame.
  → virality_predictor
      below hook/retention/creative floor → rework the hook or cut, do not post.
  → GRADE + FINISH (Adobe MCP)
      grade into 5-value palette · grain ~15 · sharpen ~35 · unify with real frames.
  → SERIALIZE
      apply spec-mark, chapter number. attribution survives the clip.
  → SHIP
```

An asset that is beautiful but fails TRUST is rejected harder than an asset that is plain but honest. The honest plain frame beats the gorgeous fake every time, because the brand's entire moat is a falsifiable promise.

## 4. THE REAL-PHOTO R6 ANCHOR

The brand is never faceless and never fake because reality holds five fixed positions. Everything else can be AI. These never can.

### 4.1 What is ALWAYS real (the non-negotiable spine)

1. **The after-wash measurement proof.** Day-one vs 20-wash, same tee, ruler in frame. The entire angle is falsifiable; this shot makes the written guarantee visible. R6, honest, never staged, never AI. Faking it kills the moat permanently. There is no render good enough to be worth that.
2. **The product itself on the product page.** Hero on body, fabric macro, opacity hold-to-light. Garment hand is the quality signal; this buyer reads AI fabric as fake. The page hero is photographed, never generated.
3. **The fit-on-body.** Real person, real posture, real settings. Resolves the fit-uncertainty complaint.
4. **Founder voice.** Native phone, unproduced. Build-in-public and preorder honesty.
5. **The face that anchors the AI.** The real model shot on R6 is the Soul ID training set. Even the recurring AI house-model traces back to a real person in the real garment. The world can be impossible; the face is earned.

### 4.2 The must-be-real vs AI-is-fine sheet (the firewall, locked)

| MUST BE REAL (R6 + founder + manufacturer) | AI IS FINE (no quality drop) |
|---|---|
| The tee on camera, for the product page | Campaign-world environments (THE SLAB, THE VOID) |
| Fabric macro, weave, opacity hold-to-light | Landing-page hero backgrounds, mood plates |
| Fit-on-body proof in real settings | Motion mood films, surreal product films |
| **The after-wash measurement shot (sacred, never AI, never staged)** | Color grade, grain, finishing |
| Real faces, real wearers, candid | FIELD (world-building only, never the product page) |
| The founder, the voice, the build-in-public | Copy drafts, format ideation, hook variants |
| Neck construction, finishing, relabel, packaging | Reframe to 9:16 / 4:5 / 1:1, volume variants |

### 4.3 How one shoot day feeds a month

One shoot day produces ~30 to 50 real frames and 8 to 12 motion clips.

- **Cutdowns (real → many).** One pull-test clip → Monday proof reel + story sticker + 1:1 feed loop + 4:5 ad cut via `reframe`. The after-wash reveal → standalone proof reel + product-page block + story poll + carousel frame. The fold/pack clip → ASMR retention reel + QC story.
- **Hybrids (real frame → AI world).** A clean fit-on-body frame goes in via `media_upload`; render day builds 5 to 8 campaign worlds around it. One real body, a month of impossible environments, same real garment.
- **Soul-ID reuse (real face → consistent campaign).** The 5 to 20 face frames train one Soul Character; `text2image_soul_v2` and `soul_cinema_studio` then produce a month of identity-faithful editorial in worlds no camera could reach.
- **Grade-locked reuse (one look across everything).** Every cutdown, hybrid, and render is graded to the one neutral lane in Adobe MCP. That single grade is why a month of mixed output reads as one brand.

The math: 5 spine captures → ~5 cutdown reels, 3 proof/feed posts, 8+ hybrid frames, a week of ad variants, a full month of consistent AI house-model editorial. The camera works one day; the stack works thirty.

## 6. THE HIGGSFIELD WORKFLOW

### 6.1 The standing pipeline (every job)

```
DEFINE THE JOB
  → pick world (1 of the 4 rooms, ONE per chapter)
  → pick engine: PROOF (R6) / ATTENTION (AI) / HYBRID (R6 frame bridged into AI world)
  → generate (named model/skill below)
  → reframe to 9:16 / 4:5 / 1:1
  → grade + grain + finish (Adobe MCP)
  → virality_predictor pre-screen (gate: post or recut)
  → post / route to waitlist or preorder
```

**Job notes that carry the rules:**

- **Soul ID (the recurring house-model):** train once on 5 to 20 photos of one face → returns a reusable `reference_id` → feeds the try-on, surreal motion, and ad variations. Character consistency is where most AI fails and where audiences instantly read fake; Soul ID is the one tool that closes that gap. A consistent AI face must be disclosed as a non-real model (Section 7).
- **Virtual try-on:** an ATTENTION asset only. It styles the look and the world. It is never the fit proof, never the measurement proof. A try-on render on a product page is a lie about the product.
- **UGC ads:** "Pack a Field Tee order with me" = Unboxing preset. "Why this one and not the cheap one" = Product Review preset. The avatar speaks the world; the claim it makes stays honest. No avatar saying "I washed this 20 times."
- **Surreal motion:** surreal does not mean random. A Field Tee in an impossible concrete monument is on-brand; a neon cyberpunk tee is not. The world is impossible; the taste is constrained.

### 6.3 The hybrid bridge (the most important mechanical move)

```
SHOOT real frame on R6 (fabric macro, weave, opacity hold-to-light, or garment on body)
  → media_upload  (push the real R6 frame into Higgsfield)
  → media_confirm (confirm it as a reference/input)
  → generate_image (nano_banana_pro or soul_2) USING the confirmed R6 frame as reference
      → AI builds ONLY the world around the real garment
  → Adobe MCP: harmonize grade so real frame and AI world share one light
  → generate_video (seedance_2_0, reference-driven so the real garment shape holds)
  → reframe → virality_predictor → post
```

The garment hand, weave, and opacity are photographed real. AI only constructs the surrounding world. This is the bridge for ATTENTION assets, not a license to put an AI world behind a product-page proof shot. The bridge produces gorgeous campaign frames; it does not produce evidence.

### 6.4 The virality predictor gate (pre-post screen on every cut)

`virality_predictor` runs before posting on every video asset and reads hook strength (first 2 to 3 seconds), attention, retention, distraction risk, and creative score. Decision rule:

- Strong hook + strong retention → ship, and it is a candidate for the $30/day LA boost.
- Strong everything but weak hook → recut the first 2 seconds only (swap the Marketing Studio hook), do not rebuild.
- Weak retention/creative → kill the cut. Do not post hoping the algorithm saves it.

## 7. THE ETHICAL / TRUST LINE

The brand's entire moat is a falsifiable claim: opaque, square, same length after 20 washes, guaranteed in writing with the measurements. Falsifiable is what makes it ownable. It is also what makes it fragile. A faked proof on a falsifiable claim is not a marketing slip, it is the product being a lie. The trust line is load-bearing.

**The governing principle:** the product page, the wash test, the measurements, and the fit-on-real-body proof stay real and are never AI. The campaign world is openly stylized and reads as made, not as a fake real place.

### 7.1 What can be AI with zero issue

Campaign environments and impossible worlds. Surreal motion, mood films, transitions, atmospheric plates. Landing-page background and hero backdrop (the world behind a real product shot, not the product shot itself). Color grade, grain, finishing, generative expand of a background. The recurring AI house-model as a stylistic figure in the campaign world (disclosed). Copy drafts, hook ideation, ad-variation volume. UGC ad delivery where the avatar carries vibe and the claims it speaks are honest and non-proof.

### 7.2 What must be disclosed

- **The AI house-model.** When a non-real person presents the brand, the brand says so, plainly, not in fine print. A standing convention works: a small consistent "AI-generated, not a real model" mark, or a stated brand fact: "HOLDS LINE uses a digital house-model for campaign imagery; all fit and wash proof is shot on real people." Stating it plainly turns a risk into a signal of integrity.
- **The campaign world is openly made.** It should read as stylized, not pretend to be a real place. The surreal register does this automatically. The danger is the photoreal-but-fake world that tries to pass as a documentary location.
- **AI motion ads.** Standard platform AI-content labeling where required. Comply, don't dodge.

### 7.3 The hard wall (never AI, never AI-adjacent, never AI-backgrounded)

| Proof asset | Why it is real-only |
|---|---|
| The tee on the product page | Garment hand is the quality signal; AI fabric reads fake instantly |
| Fabric macro (weave, 220 GSM hand) | The differentiator is the cloth. Faking it forfeits the differentiator |
| Opacity hold-to-light | The #1-complaint proof. Must be a real backlit garment |
| **The 20-wash measurement shot** | The single most important asset. Faking this kills the moat forever. Never staged, never AI'd |
| Fit-on-real-body | A virtual try-on is styling, not fit |
| The measurements / the written guarantee | Numbers from a real wash test. No generated number ever appears |
| Preorder terms | A promise with money attached. Real, plain, honest |

### 7.4 The house-model: honest by design

The AI house-model never appears in a proof context, and the brand never implies it is a real customer or wearer.

- House-model in a surreal void editorial: fine, disclosed, openly stylized. That is the campaign.
- House-model in a UGC ad saying "I love how this fits me" while implying a real testimonial: not fine. It borrows credibility it does not have.
- Fit, comfort, and wash testimony come from real people on the R6, or not at all.

HOLDS LINE gets both: an AI house-model for reach AND an openly-real proof engine for trust, as long as the two never trade clothes.

### 7.5 The trust line as positioning, not just ethics

A growing market camp reads AI as cheap-looking clip art, and brands using real capture now make a point of saying so. HOLDS LINE turns the wall into a brand statement:

> The campaign is built. The proof is shot. The world is AI and we say so. The tee, the fit, and the 20-wash measurement are real, on a real body, on a real camera, because the guarantee is real.

Honest, ownable, and no competitor running fake-real merch photography can say it. It makes the falsifiable claim more believable, not less, which is the only reason the waitlist and deposits convert.

## 8. THE MONEY PATH + PER-TYPE CTAs

### 8.1 The path: world → waitlist → preorder → sale

```
ATTENTION (AI world + hybrids)  →  pulls cold eyes, builds brand-feel, no claim
        ↓
TRUST (real proof)              →  converts attention into belief in the after-state claim
        ↓
WAITLIST (soft signal)          →  futureuniform-site · email capture
        ↓   [GATE 1: 300 signups]
PREORDER (hard signal)          →  Shopify + PreOrder Now/Timesact · deposit
        ↓   [GATE 2: ~40 preorders · GATE 3: >=10% waitlist→preorder]
MANUFACTURE financed by deposits → first run ships → run two prices a warm list
```

**Threshold logic (set before launch, never moved after):**

- **GATE 1 · 300 waitlist emails** before the Shopify preorder page gets built. Below 300, the angle is not pulling: re-angle, do not spend on commerce.
- **GATE 2 · ~40 paid preorders** covers a 50-unit financed run (~$900 at ~$18 landed). At or above 40, place the run.
- **GATE 3 · waitlist→preorder >=10%.** Below it, the page is the problem (price or proof), not the traffic. Fix the page, retest once. Two misses kills the angle.

How the engines split the work: ATTENTION (AI) is volume and reach to fill the top of the funnel. PROOF (real) is conversion: it turns a follower into a believer in the falsifiable claim, which is what moves someone to the waitlist. AI gets attention to the page; proof gets the email; proof + the written guarantee gets the deposit.

### 8.2 The exact CTA per content type

Each type has one job and one CTA. AI posts must NOT make the falsifiable claim, so their CTA points to world and waitlist, never to "guaranteed." All CTAs route to the one live page until the Shopify preorder page exists.

| Content type | Job | Exact CTA (verbatim) | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI world post** | Top-of-funnel reach, zero claim | Caption: "The uniform is real. The drop is coming. Link in bio to get in first." / End card: "Join the waitlist →" | Bio link → waitlist. Never says "guaranteed." |
| **Real proof post** | Convert attention to belief, carry the claim | Caption: "Opaque, square, same length after 20 washes. In writing. Get the drop and the guarantee: link in bio." / On-screen: "Get the guarantee →" | Bio link → waitlist (pre-300) / preorder (post-300). Carries the claim because it is the proof. |
| **Hybrid post** | Bridge world to proof, name the split | Caption: "Real tee, built world. The proof you can measure, the drop you can join. Link in bio." / On-screen: "See the proof. Join the drop →" | Bio link → waitlist. Earns trust by naming the real/AI line, then routes. |
| **Story** | Highest-intent, lowest-friction capture | Sticker: "Tap to join the waitlist" (pre-300) → "Tap to preorder the Field Tee" (post-300). Plus poll on world stories: "Would you wear this? Yes / Show me the proof" | Link sticker → waitlist, then preorder. The poll re-routes "show me the proof" tappers straight to a proof post. |

**CTA escalation (same audience, three asks getting harder):**

1. **Pre-300 signups:** every type ends at the waitlist. AI and hybrid carry the soft ask ("join the drop"); proof posts carry the claim ("get the guarantee"). The job is volume of emails.
2. **At 300 (GATE 1 clears):** build the Shopify preorder page. Flip proof-post and story CTAs to "Preorder the Field Tee →" with the deposit. AI posts keep pointing to the bio link (now the preorder page) but still never make the claim.
3. **Post-preorder-launch:** proof posts close ("Preorder. Run financed by deposits, ship by [date]."), AI posts keep filling the top, hybrids carry the "real proof / built world" bridge that makes the deposit feel safe.

The standing rule that protects the moat: the written guarantee and the measurement proof only ever appear attached to real-shot assets. An AI post can build desire and point to the drop. It can never be the thing that makes the falsifiable promise.

## 9. THE 7-DAY EXECUTION PLAN

The goal of the first 7 days is not volume. It is to stand up the spine (one real shoot, the trained Soul ID, the first proof posts live) and read whether the content world pulls before spending a dollar on commerce or boosts. Proof first, always.

The plan assumes the wash test is already running (see THE FIRST REAL MOVE), because Day 2 cannot shoot the measurement proof without the washed tee in hand.

### Day 1 (desk) · GENERATE the empty world

Build the world so it is ready to receive real frames. Roughly half a day at the desk.

1. Lock the five-hex palette and the four rooms into a one-page reference sheet (the grading target for every asset).
2. Generate the first 3 AI campaign-world plates: `generate_image` with `nano_banana_pro`. The tee in THE VOID (post 11 plate), the tee on a pedestal in THE SLAB (post 16), the fog-plane poster (post 13). No faces yet. FIELD does not exist until the real face is shot on Day 2.
3. Grade those 3 plates into the five-value palette in Adobe MCP. Confirm they read as one world.
4. Draft all 30 captions and hooks, em-dash-free, with the per-type CTAs from Section 8.2 pre-written into each.
5. Stand up the waitlist capture on `futureuniform-site`, send a test signup, and confirm the email actually lands in the list.

Do NOT generate FIELD on Day 1. The avatar cannot precede the real face that anchors it.

### Day 2 (one real R6 session) · SHOOT the spine

One disciplined session, the most important day of the week. Capture in this exact priority order so the trust assets exist even if the day gets cut short. The minimum that makes the week safe is captures 1 through 6.

1. **The after-wash measurement proof.** Day-one tee vs the 20-wash tee, ruler in frame, same length, flat-lay, identical framing both tees. The asset the whole brand stands on.
2. **Opacity hold-to-light.** Backlit, hand behind fabric, one continuous take.
3. **Fabric macro.** Weave, 220 GSM hand, neck and hem construction.
4. **Fit-on-body** in THE ROOM and THE BLOCK. Real person, candid, minimal styling.
5. **Motion.** Stretch-and-recover pull test, slow fold (ASMR). Continuous takes.
6. **5 to 20 clean face frames** of the real model, even light, neutral expression, for Soul ID training.
7. **Founder POV**, native phone: the "crop tops" line and the preorder model stated plainly.

Same day: dump cards to SSD then HDD before the laptop closes. Tag the measurement frames first.

### Day 3 · POST 1 goes live, stories open

- **POST 1 (real, opacity test reel).** Hook: "Stop buying tees you can see through." CTA → waitlist. The brand opens on the differentiator made visible, never on hype.
- **Stories.** Behind-the-glass of the Day 2 shoot, the wash test referenced, "ask me anything about the guarantee." Waitlist sticker on every frame.

### Day 4 · POST 3, the signature asset

- **POST 3 (real, after-wash carousel).** Hook: "Watch what happens after 20 washes." Slide 3 is the written guarantee. Carries the proof CTA ("Get the guarantee → link in bio"). This is the conversion engine of the week.
- **Stories.** Poll: "Guess how much length it loses after 20 washes." Re-route the curious to post 3.

### Day 5 · POST 5, then render and train

- **POST 5 (real, founder POV reel).** Hook: "I got tired of tees that turn into crop tops." Belonging plus trust, native phone.
- **RENDER + TRAIN (desk).** Train Soul ID on the Day 2 face frames; FIELD now exists. Build the first hybrids from the Day 2 real frames via `media_upload` + `media_confirm` (post 21, post 22). Run both through `virality_predictor`.

### Day 6 · first myth post, first bridge

- **POST 11 (AI, the void product film).** Hook: "A uniform for a world that doesn't exist yet." The first myth post, only after three proof posts (1, 3, 5) have banked trust. CTA → waitlist, no claim. Pre-screened.
- **POST 21 (hybrid, real opacity test in an AI gallery).** Hook: "The see-through test, in a room built for it." Proves the bridge works on a live feed.

### Day 7 · close the loop, read the signal

- **POST 8 (real, pull test) + story poll.** Hook: "Pull it. Watch it go back."
- **Read the 7-day signal** against the table below and make the kill / hold / scale call.

By end of Day 7: 4 real proof posts (1, 3, 5, 8), 1 AI world post (11), 1 hybrid (21) live; FIELD trained; the hybrid pipeline proven; a running story layer; the waitlist collecting.

### How to MEASURE whether the world is pulling

Watch these signals across the 7 days. The mix matters more than any single number.

| Signal | What it tells you | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Saves** | The post is reference-worthy. The strongest organic intent signal for this category. | Proof + hybrid posts especially |
| **Shares** | It travels. The broadcast mechanic working. The white-tee-hunt and proof posts should lead here. | All types |
| **Watch-time / retention** | The hook and payoff held. Cross-check against the `virality_predictor` pre-score. | Reels |
| **Profile taps** | A single post converted a scroll into brand curiosity. The bridge from post to bio link. | All types |
| **Waitlist signups** | The actual conversion. The only signal that proves the world drives money, not just attention. | The live page |
| **Waitlist source** | Which post type drove the email: proof, AI, or hybrid. Tells you what to scale. | UTM / link tracking |

**The kill / scale read (end of Day 7, and again weekly):**

- **SCALE** if proof posts are driving saves and waitlist signups, and at least one AI or hybrid post cleared its `virality_predictor` hook floor and pulled profile taps. The world is pulling: keep the weekly cadence, and route the single best-scoring cut to the $30/day desktop LA boost (10-mile radius). Boost only what already scores organically.
- **HOLD AND RE-ANGLE** if attention is fine (taps, watch-time) but waitlist signups are flat. The page or the proof framing is the problem, not the traffic. Fix the page copy and the guarantee presentation, retest once.
- **KILL THE FORMAT, NOT THE BRAND** if a specific AI post type reads as slop (low saves, low taps, fast scroll-away despite a clean pre-score). Pull that format, lean harder on proof and hybrid, regenerate.
- The hard commerce gate still governs: do not build the Shopify preorder page until 300 waitlist emails (GATE 1). The 7-day read tells you whether 300 is reachable on this angle or whether the angle needs to change first.
