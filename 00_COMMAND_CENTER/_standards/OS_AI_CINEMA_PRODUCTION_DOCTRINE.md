# OS AI CINEMA PRODUCTION DOCTRINE (LOCKED 2026-06-08)

**Status: STANDING DOCTRINE. The OS defaults to this method for ALL AI film, commercial, brand story, social rollout, and high-end visual production.** The old way (freehand prompts, ship-when-complete, self-judged "looks good") is RETIRED. Supersedes ad-hoc generation habits. Composes with [[higgsfield-production-doctrine]] (`OS_HIGGSFIELD_PRODUCTION_DOCTRINE.md`), os-face-lock, os-quality-gates, and the [[second-model-gemini-lane]].

## The 10 commandments
1. **Watch and hostile-review before trusting any edit or AI film.** Run `/watch` on the actual cut and a Gemini hostile pass before believing it works. No "I think it's good" without seeing it and being attacked on it.
2. **Higgsfield / Seedance / Nano Banana are a production STACK, not random generators.** Treat them like a crew with roles, not a slot machine.
3. **Lock identity/world first with stills, then animate only approved frames.** Cheap stills (~2cr) gate the expensive video (~22-45cr). The lock is the look.
4. **Use `banana-pro-director`** for character/source-image and scene-plate discipline (base → 6-panel sheet → plates).
5. **Use `cinema-worldbuilder`** for Seedance motion direction, scene continuity, camera language, shot grammar (5 cinema modes, diegetic audio only).
6. **Use the 3x3 cinematic contact sheet and the 9-scene sequence methods BEFORE spending heavy video credits** (`HIGGSFIELD_REUSABLE_MEGA_PROMPTS.md`).
7. **Final emotional films get a proper owned music route (Suno or equivalent).** ElevenLabs scratch SFX swells are NOT final music. Scratch is allowed only as a labeled placeholder, never in a client-ready cut.
8. **Complete is not excellent. 7/10 is not done. 9/10 is the working floor.** Do not call anything excellent because it finished rendering.
9. **Gemini is the hostile critic, not the final judge.** Accept evidence-backed notes; reject tone-deaf notes that violate the brief's intended tone. Cite which were accepted/rejected and why.
10. **Every production ends with the full proof packet:** proof files, QA, gate score, accepted/rejected critique, and the exact next-lift named.

## The mandatory production loop (every AI film runs this)
1. Brief read → emotional truth → concept(s) → pick winner (STORY_GATE 9/9).
2. Lock identity/world stills (banana-pro-director) → storyboard → operator sign-off before heavy spend.
3. Animate approved frames only (cinema-worldbuilder / Seedance; non-Seedance route like Kling 3.0 when the filter blocks a shot).
4. Assemble → owned music (Suno/equivalent) → grade → captions → title.
5. **`/watch` the cut** (see it and hear it) → note watch-based issues.
6. **Gemini hostile pass** → reconcile against brief (accept/reject with evidence).
7. **Score against the gate. 9/10 is the floor for client-ready.** Below that, fix the two highest-leverage issues and re-score.
8. Ship the proof packet (point 10) or name+accept the remaining gap explicitly.

## What "client-ready" requires
A cut is client-ready ONLY when it clears the 9/10 gate, OR the remaining gap is explicitly named and the operator accepts it in writing. Never call a 7 "ready."
