# MAX_CAPABILITY_MODE (LOCKED 2026-06-08)

The default mode for serious work. Use the full registry intelligently, pull cross-domain skills, do not settle for good-enough, do not compress quality, do not skip proof, target 10/10.

## Default ON for
client work, film/video, campaign work, photo/composite, editing/retouching, websites, decks, strategy, pricing, new business ideas, and anything the operator says is important.

## What MAX mode means
- Use the full 78-skill registry intelligently (consider all, route the relevant, integrate their contribution). NOT dump-everything-into-context.
- Activate cross-domain skills (the `cross_domain` map). A film also gets story, editing, sound, brand, audience, QA, platform mastering, toolchain.
- Do not settle for "good enough." Do not compress quality. Do not skip proof.
- 10/10 is the target. Do not call 9/10 the goal when 10/10 is possible. If 10/10 is blocked, NAME the blocker and the path to remove it (in the OS_RECEIPT).
- Resolve cross-skill conflicts by the authority hierarchy; synthesize the best from the union.

## MAX mode vs EMERGENCY mode (do not confuse them)
| | MAX_CAPABILITY | EMERGENCY (compressed) |
|---|---|---|
| scope | full | reduced (fewer deliverables, simpler cut, tighter brief, fewer revisions, faster route) |
| quality / taste | ceiling | SAME ceiling on the protected core |
| review / proof | full | minimum QA still runs; never-relax gates hold |
| skills | full registry + cross-domain | the subset that protects the core |
| label | can reach "final" | "sendable (emergency, named gaps)", rarely "final" |

Emergency means LESS SCOPE, not weaker taste, lazy edit, skipped review, app-looking work, fake final, or low quality. Compressed is not careless. If the core outcome cannot be protected even at reduced scope, decline or reset scope.

## The non-negotiables (both modes)
identity/likeness correctness, legal/usage/release, os-vision-reject-gate hard fails, brand integrity on the protected core, honest labeling. These never relax.

## How it fires
The conductor (`os_activate.py`) flags serious tasks and emits the MAX banner + cross-domain pull + receipt requirement automatically. The Stop gate enforces the receipt. Operator can force MAX on anything by saying "max" / "go all out" / "whole OS" (serious keywords). Companion: [[master-os-conductor]].
