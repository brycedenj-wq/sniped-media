## Entry rule and laws
Every AI film/commercial/brand-story/visual task activates this stack first via `OS_ACTIVATION_INDEX.json` (scoped activation, never the whole corpus). Laws: stills before motion (animate only approved frames); diegetic audio in prompts, owned music in finishing; every shot has the subject act and read muted; 9/10 is the floor, not the goal.

Updated by: when the doctrines are revised (locked, operator-approved per AGENTS.md) or new film skills register in OS_ACTIVATION_INDEX.json.
