## 1. Master content thesis
**"Read the made world."** A faceless cross-platform engine that takes anything already made (a famous object, a brand, a campaign, a creative or cultural system) and runs it through one disciplined craft lens to expose the single move that makes it work or fail, then asks the audience which version they would actually buy, wear, or use. The durable premise is the LENS plus the SIGNAL-PROBE, not any one subject, so it never runs out of material and every post is also a market test.

**Lane + authority:** the design-literacy of culture, a faceless "editor of made things and made systems." Authority is from the operator's real strengths in the packet: systems-thinking-as-creative, design/aesthetic taste, AI-native production, and a builder's eye. It is employer-safe (only public, external objects and culture are read, never workplace/engineering material) and faceless (no face, ever; the work is the argument). **Why not Greek/HBCU as the default:** the rescope explicitly bars picking one world and bars Greek/HBCU as the default; THE YARD AFTER DARK is permitted only as at most ONE flagged-risky concept-capsule hypothesis, gated behind a respect-read, never the engine's lane.

> **Caveat (from verify):** the LA-creative / founder / coach access pools the autopsies and capsules assume are operator-asserted (from the packet), not yet verified. The engine treats "who is actually here" as a signal to confirm in the first 30 days, not a given.

## 2. Three repeatable content formats
1. **THE TEARDOWN (Object → 3% → New Object).** Faceless short: a famous object isolated on white, the single 3% intervention named in quotes, both the old and new reading held live, an infinite-loop ending, and a one-line signal probe ("which version would you actually buy/wear/use"). Cadence 5/wk, batchable in one OS authoring session. Tests WHICH CATEGORY the audience pulls toward (apparel / objects / type-and-graphics / spaces / tools).
2. **PUT THE NOUN IN QUOTES (System Autopsy).** Faceless systems-thinking short that takes a public creative/cultural/operational system, puts its label "in quotes," and exposes the one load-bearing mechanic. Cadence 3/wk. Tests WHICH AUDIENCE is gathering (founder / creative / coach / operator) and WHICH PROBLEM repeats. Employer context is a hard off-limit.
3. **THE READYMADE DROP (Concept Capsule, signal-priced).** A weekly "what if this were real" capsule: one finished-looking concept object/world fragment, process-as-product (sketch → variant grid → final), a one-line premise, and a no-commerce signal probe ("reply DROP if you'd want this"). Cadence 1/wk + 1 variant-grid repurpose. Tests DEMAND INTENSITY and physical-vs-digital FORM. No paywall, no presale, no rails.

All three are fixed templates the OS fills (no bespoke build per post), faceless, and authored no-spend; only the weekly hero visual is spend-gated (Higgsfield, operator-approved).

## 4. How one asset becomes all platform versions
LAW: one weekly HERO source asset becomes every platform version, no bespoke rebuild.
1. **Author the source packet** (OS, no spend, READY): hook, script, on-white plate spec, caption, loop, signal probe, tags.
2. **Produce the one visual** (spend-gated, operator-approved): the on-white plate / variant grid generated once via Higgsfield, or kept as an on-white spec render if no spend is authorized.
3. **Cut the 9:16 master once** (vertical 1080x1920, VO or text-on-screen + plate + loop).
4. **Reframe, do not rebuild**: derive 4:5 and 1:1 for IG, 2:3 Pins for Pinterest from the one master.
5. **Derive the text cuts** from the same packet: a Threads post (hook + one line), a LinkedIn POV, the email issue.
6. **Build the carousel / process-spread** from the variant grid (already produced).
7. **Tag + log every cut** into the DEMAND_SIGNAL_LEDGER (category tag + audience tag + platform).
NET: 1 authored packet + 1 visual → 1 vertical master → 3 short-video posts + 1 carousel + 1 Threads post + 1 LinkedIn POV + Pins + 1 email issue.

## 6. What signals decide the product
| signal | threshold | what it implies |
|---|---|---|
| Strong-intent comment rate ("where do I buy / make this real") | a single category clears it repeatedly | that category is a candidate product-territory |
| Save-to-reach ratio by category | >=3% for >=3 posts in a category | private intent (costly action), real pull |
| Readymade Drop waitlist-interest reply rate | >=8% of reached accounts give a costly reply | closest no-spend read to "would you actually buy" |
| Inbound DM intensity + buyer archetype | >=10 unsolicited "can I commission / buy" | reveals WHO the future customer is |
| Format-pull split (physical vs digital intent) | one side clearly exceeds the other | decides the product's FORM |
| Hero durability (does the produced hero out-pull) | hero >=2x the strong-intent of cheap posts | confirms production investment tracks demand |
| Repeat-engager rate (same accounts return) | >=20 named accounts acting repeatedly | a true re-pointable audience vs a spike |

## 7. Five product surfaces that could emerge (none crowned)
1. **Signal-priced concept capsule → limited physical drop.** Triggered by apparel/objects category clearing save + waitlist thresholds. Owned good, payment follows proof (waitlist before any production).
2. **"Read the made world" licensed teardown series / brand work.** Triggered by save-to-reach + repeat-engager from titled accounts. Retainer or per-piece licensing.
3. **Owned digital system / tool product.** Triggered by the format-pull split favoring digital-system intent. Productized access (the made SYSTEM, not the method/ingredients).
4. **Authored concept-design commission lane.** Triggered by inbound DM intensity (>=10 "can you make this"). High-anchor commissions priced on value.
5. **Curated drop membership / standing audience surface.** Triggered by the repeat-engager threshold. Low-friction recurring access.
Each is an owned output (the meal), never ingredient resale. Signal ranks them; the operator decides; nothing is crowned here.

## 8. 30-day test plan
- **Phase 0 (Days 1-3): instrument before posting.** Stand up the DEMAND_SIGNAL_LEDGER (durable file), define the category + audience tags, write the first 2-week batch of angles. **Operator approves the batch of angles before any posting (approval at the boundary, per OS orchestration law 4).**
- **Phase 1 (Days 4-14): baseline + run all three instruments.** Teardown 5x/wk, Autopsy 3x/wk, one Readymade Drop/wk, tagging every post. Manual posting. Get a clean cross-category, cross-audience baseline.
- **Phase 2 (Days 15-25): probe the leading category harder.** Point a sharper signal-probe at the top 1 (max 2) categories from Phase 1. Operator approves the sharpened batch before posting.
- **Phase 3 (Days 26-30): reconcile signal → ranked hypothesis (NOT a crown).** Score every category and archetype against the 7 signals + thresholds, produce a ranked product-hypothesis list, and bring it to the operator for the next decision. No product is crowned by the engine.

## 9. Kill criteria
- KILL THE FORMAT (not the engine): any format whose strong-signal rate never clears ~1% across Phase 1.
- KILL A CATEGORY HYPOTHESIS: high weak signal (likes/reach) but fails the strong-intent + save thresholds.
- KILL THE PREMATURE PRODUCT: if NO category clears its strong-intent + save + waitlist thresholds in 30 days, do not launch a product; iterate the engine.
- KILL THE GREEK/HBCU HYPOTHESIS-SUBJECT on first respect-read failure (it appears at most once, gated).
- KILL ON EMPLOYER/IDENTITY EXPOSURE: any format/subject drifting toward engineering/employer/facility material.
- KILL THE BESPOKE-LABOR DRIFT: if the engine can no longer be authored by the OS in a single batchable session.
- KILL A WAITLIST PROBE THAT WON'T COMMIT: soft interest that evaporates when a real (still-gated) commitment is asked.
- KILL THE INGREDIENT-RESALE TEMPTATION: if a surface's only monetization is selling the method/SREF/doctrine, drop it.
