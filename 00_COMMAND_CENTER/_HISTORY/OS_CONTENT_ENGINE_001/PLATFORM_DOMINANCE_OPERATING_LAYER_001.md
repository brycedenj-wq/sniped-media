## 4. "Fire video" in measurable terms
A video is fire only if it clears ALL:
- **Hook:** the scroll stops inside 1 second (the contradiction/novelty is visible before any text).
- **Retention:** average view duration at or above the platform bar (>=70% completion on a <=15s, >=50% on a 30s).
- **Rewatch:** replay/loop rate measurably above the account median (the seamless loop does its job).
- **Save:** save-to-reach at or above ~3% (a costly, private intent signal).
- **Share:** share rate above the account median (it gave someone a reason to send it).
- **Comment:** the comment trigger fires (a binary or a named-object reply, easy and costly to answer).
- **Clip-survival:** it still reads and re-attributes when reposted without sound or context.
- **The DM test:** at least one stranger asks "where can I get this / can you make this."
Views and likes alone do NOT qualify a video as fire.

## 5. "Second to none visuals" in measurable terms
- Object reads at a 120px thumbnail.
- Zero AI tells at 200% zoom (no warp, melted edges, extra geometry, gloss haze).
- Material truth (believable specular roll-off, real edges; not plastic-AI).
- One disciplined color; everything else neutral (no teal/orange, no neon).
- At least ~55% intentional negative space on the hero.
- A 4K master exists (1k generation upscaled, or rendered at 4K).
- It beats an honest camera frame of the same subject, not merely completes the task.
- It passes the External Visual Proof Gate (a named human signs off; Claude is not the final visual authority).

## 7. Daily/weekly volume without manual burnout
- Fixed formats (the same instrument re-pointed at new subjects), so no bespoke build per post.
- Batch authoring: one OS session writes the week's packet; the tool pipeline does the production heavy-lifting.
- Build-once-reframe: one hero plate -> all ratios via crop, no re-generation per platform.
- A durable content calendar + backlog so reruns skip rediscovery.
- 1 hero + supports per week; manual posting is reduced to copy/crop/paste (~10-40 min/day, no auto-poster verified).
- Per-batch credit caps protect spend; spend only on the hero, supports stay no-spend/spec.
- Burnout guard: if the week can no longer be authored in one batchable session, the format is too bespoke, simplify it.

## 9. How money appears WITHOUT prematurely selling products
Order: attention -> trust -> visual proof -> demand capture -> INBOUND money. Money shows up as a response to proof, never as an early push:
- Free value first (GaryVee jab); the email list is the owned asset, grown weekly.
- Money enters ONLY through inbound signal: a DM asking to commission/license/buy, waitlist interest, a sponsor/partner inbound.
- No payment rails are built until a real yes (proof before price).
- Never boost/advertise to manufacture demand; ads only amplify a post already producing product signal.

## 10. How a product graduates (only after repeated signal)
A product surface graduates from "hypothesis" to "scope a real offer" ONLY when it clears, across 2 to 4 weeks: the named-reply pull threshold AND >=10 inbound "make this real" asks AND a repeat-engager base (>=20 named accounts). Then, and only then, scope the offer with the Hormozi value equation, payment follows proof. The surfaces and their triggers:
- **Commissioned visual concepts** <- inbound "can you design X like this" DMs.
- **Brand content systems** <- brands asking for the look/format applied to them.
- **Licensing** <- a brand wants the aesthetic/asset licensed.
- **Paid campaign work** <- inbound from founders/brands who saw the proof.
- **Physical drops** <- a category clears save + waitlist + named-reply pull (make-to-order, waitlist-first, no inventory risk).
- **Memberships** <- a repeat-engager base wants more/early access.
- **Tools** <- the audience repeatedly asks how it is made (a self-serve product, only if it does not become ingredient-resale of the OS).
- **World / IP** <- the world itself accrues audience and licensable equity.
Graduation is permission to scope an offer, NOT a crown. Nothing is built before its trigger fires.
