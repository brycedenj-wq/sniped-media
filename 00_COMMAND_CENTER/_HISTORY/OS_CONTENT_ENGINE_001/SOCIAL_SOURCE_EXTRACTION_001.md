## THE THREE THESES

- **V1 Kallaway:** social platforms post-2020 are pure media-distribution engines that match any video to any viewer, so growth and money come from feeding the algorithm a militantly single-topic, narrowly-targeted signal on ONE hero platform, then funneling that attention off the low-value media layer into owned products.
- **V2 Kallaway:** the algorithm is a matchmaker that judges a fresh post on a ~200-person non-follower sample test; you win by making the algo's job easy (narrow topic + fixed avatar so it builds a confident fit score) and making that sample engage hard (watch time, engagement rate, watch-time session share). Great videos to one avatar across a narrow topic band, repeated, is the only lever.
- **V3 GaryVee:** platforms are interest-graph distribution engines, not follower-graph; any individual who relentlessly produces native organic content can reach high-propensity-interest strangers for free and compound that attention into a personal brand, the only durable arbitrage.

### EXTENDS the doctrine (adds mechanism it lacked; 11 items, the load-bearing ones)
- **The ~200-person sample cascade:** on post, the platform shows the video to ~200 predicted-best-fit people (mostly non-followers); positive data cascades 200 -> 2,000 -> 20,000 -> 200,000; neutral RE-samples another ~200; negative shuts the push almost immediately. Decay = audience exhaustion, not a penalty (V2 02:23, 03:04, 03:18, 03:50, 04:39). This is the mechanical WHY behind the §8 view-velocity instrumentation.
- **Topic is read primarily from the spoken transcript + caption** (plus full multimodal: CV on visuals + audio + transcript + metadata -> one topic map -> fit score). Beautiful silent visuals classify as nothing (V1 04:19; V2 01:49, 02:23). Direct add for a faceless visual engine: every clip needs scripted/VO topic language + keyword captions.
- **Watch-time SESSION SHARE:** a hidden metric = what % of a viewer's in-session minutes were on YOUR videos. Implies producing binge-able clusters/series (V2 09:34, 09:57). Absent from the current 21-field ledger.
- **The four horsemen content-unit spec:** (1) relevant to the avatar's problem, (2) non-obvious + implementable, (3) high absorption, (4) short implementation distance. The same four turn viewers into buyers (V2 10:51, 12:09). An upstream content-value spec the doctrine lacks.
- **Off-avatar viral is a multi-post drag:** even one viral hit to the wrong audience poisons the fit-score sample for the next several posts; re-anchor immediately (V2 07:44, 08:43).
- **Outlier-mining research:** build a set of in-niche winning channels, filter by outlier score, library the winners' transcript/hook/mechanics, remix into avatar-matched concepts BEFORE producing (V2 12:53).
- **Comment-driving levers:** contrarian stance, confident superlative framing, anchor to cult-loved named brands/people, drive emotion; plus a dual-use CTA that harvests a demand-validated topic backlog (V2 13:18, 14:42, 14:58, 15:20, 17:07). Note: V2 itself flags the enemy/contrarian lever as an employer-safety risk.
- **Interest-graph targeting is literal:** first viewers are chosen by propensity-of-interest in the actual objects depicted in-frame (banana + rum-and-coke example); the in-frame object IS a targeting token (V3 00:15, 00:53). Add to prop/shot selection.
