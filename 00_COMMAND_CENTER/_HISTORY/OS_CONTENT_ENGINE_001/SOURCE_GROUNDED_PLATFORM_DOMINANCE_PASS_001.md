## 2. Platform laws
- **TikTok:** widest discovery island. Audience-matching: ~30-50 same-topic videos to train the algo. Hook in 1-3s (delta 5-20x). Enters the board on Day 5 as discovery, not Day 1.
- **Instagram:** the curiosity/taste platform. The grid reads as ONE body of work; every tile passes the 4-question SNIPED Visual Standard.
- **YouTube Shorts:** seed deliberately as AEO/GEO answer-data (feeds LLMs, becomes the named answer in ~4 years). Cheap secondary cut.
- **YouTube long:** the trust + citation depth engine (25-min at 8-10min AVD; Kallaway trust math ~400 content-minute blocks to a $10k buyer). Evergreen. Later phase.
- **LinkedIn:** the conversion island and the single most underpriced attention for this operator (decision-makers, low creator density). **KILL-LINE [FIX 5]:** if LinkedIn does not produce comments-from-titled-accounts + discovery interest within ~3-4 weeks of consistent posting, treat the underpriced-island thesis as unproven and re-weight to the island that is actually returning signal; do not keep feeding it on faith.
- **Threads:** the one platform that runs from Day 1 (~15 min/day) as awareness + a free hook lab. Replies > likes, text-only ok.
- **Email:** the ONLY owned channel and the real conversion asset (~60% open vs ~3% social). Rented platforms ramp INTO it.
- **Site:** a durable demand-capture surface, not the crown; built for AI-citation (schema, metadata); deferred until a lane is proven.
- **Backend:** three-layer order: Infrastructure (verified identity, portfolio, 2FA) -> Systems (production + lead capture) -> Offer. Built when signal warrants.

## 3. Visual laws (undeniable, not decent)
- Every grid frame passes the 4-question SNIPED Visual Standard (color block real, pose architected, skin as the only contrast, no AI tells).
- Identity is captured-real, never AI-freehand: the world/background is the cheap swappable AI layer; the real face/skin is masked back in.
- Measure the world, do not eyeball it (sample real color anchors and hit them).
- Hard-negative every text/signage in any AI plate (the #1 AI tell); win the still first.
- A still plus a push-in is NOT a film shot (Push-In Law): narrative video carries authored START-to-END motion, first 10 seconds earn the watch.
- Document-don't-create is the default texture (rough real capture beats cinematic polish ~10:1 in views). **Note [FIX from red-team]:** rough = INTENTIONAL authenticity, not lazy; it still passes the standard.
- Consistency of SYSTEM not sameness of subject (different palettes, one texture signature).
- External eyes before client-safe (Claude is not the final visual authority).
- Visual proof is evidence (before/after, case study, real result), not a portfolio flex.

## 4. Volume law
Volume is the discovery mechanism, not the goal. Per concept produce 5-30 variations (5-10 low-stakes, 10-20 core, 20-30 a major push) and let the market pick. Run the 7x7: ONE core capture/day (voice note, on-set footage, edit screen-rec, client debrief) fans into 7-8 platform-native cutdowns via the AI processing prompt; the capture is not the content, the cutdowns are. One Operator Kit shoot = ~20-25 pieces over 3-4 weeks; one 10-min voice note = 7+ pieces. Volume comes from REPURPOSING, never from creating more. Cadence once up: 1-3 unique core pieces/day + cutdowns across the chosen islands (GaryVee's 4-15/day bar tuned DOWN for a solo operator, per the Attention Stack and the too-hard red-team). Batch covers 80% (Sunday 90-min plan+capture), real-time 20%, never all real-time (burnout by week 6). Manual vs automated: the OS automates the fan-out (transcribe, cut, caption, reformat); the human does the one capture + the taste pass + posting (no verified auto-poster, so posting is manual).

## 5. Money law
Money comes from attention, in sequence, no offer crowned early: attention -> trust -> visual proof -> demand capture -> offer. Content is ALL GIVE; the ASK lives in the bio and a separate paid layer, never inside the content (95/5 value-to-ask). First capture is OWNED attention: convert views to an email list via a lead magnet (you can monetize traffic without an offer, never an offer without traffic). The offer is a deferred decision revealed from signal: mine comments + DMs as the focus group, read view-velocity, let the densest validated lane name the offer (service retainer, micro-offer, commission, or licensed authored work). Mid-funnel mechanic replaces crown-then-advertise: a piece that proves itself organically earns a push-down (add a CTA / demand capture); only proven pieces get spend. Pricing, when it comes: value-based 3-option proposals after discovery (series_2), never single-price, never good/better/best naming.

## 9. Red-team (where this becomes mid / generic / too hard / too product-first / too story-first / too manual)
- **Mid:** document-don't-create misread as low-effort. Guard: rough is intentional authenticity, still passes the visual standard.
- **Generic:** if the lane is broad ("I do visuals") it is a continent not a niche and gets a blended algo score. Guard: the pre-Day-1 content-game choice + one complex problem.
- **Too hard / unsustainable solo:** GaryVee's 4-15+ posts/day is agency scale; a solo operator who copies it burns out by week 6. Guard: tuned cadence, batch 80%, automate the fan-out.
- **Too product-first:** the $10k-then-multiply trigger and the micro-offer tempt an early crown. Guard: the offer is deferred to signal; no crown before the graduation bar.
- **Too story-first:** over-weighting the film/first-10-pages gate becomes a film-studio bet that burns Higgsfield spend before signal. Guard: story is a gate for narrative pieces, not the business.
- **Too manual:** if every cut is hand-made, volume dies and the operator is the bottleneck. Guard: automate transcribe/cut/caption/reformat; the human does capture + taste + posting.
- **Authority-tax impatience:** authority is a ~10-year tax with a ~2-3yr arbitrage window; do not judge the plan dead on 2-week numbers (instrument, do not over-read).
- **Tool-readiness over-claim:** Premiere was UNVERIFIED then verified this session; Adobe cloud PARTIAL + URL friction; Blender LIVE but situational; Figma/AE conditional; Higgsfield spend-gated. State readiness honestly per asset.
- **Legal-stage drift:** own-channel posting is editorial (no release); the moment a frame SELLS something it is commercial and needs a release, and raw AI is not ownable. Activate release/contract machinery only at the commercial stage.
