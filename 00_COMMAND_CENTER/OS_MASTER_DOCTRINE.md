### Edit Moves

**v3 LUXURY Visual Lock:** Adobe Neutral base, Meisel/Roversi/Mert and Marcus lane, quiet luxury editorial restraint. No teal/orange. One preset, one 10-step develop backbone, one Hero Finish ceiling.

**Dual-Lane Grading:**
- Lane A editorial (warm WB, lifted blacks, shadows +15-25, Red/Blue +5-10, grain 8-15): heroes, IG, campaigns
- Lane B clean product (neutral WB, linear tone curve, near-zero grain, true garment color): web, e-commerce
- Skin is master in both: melanin-rich protection outranks stylistic mood. Green/ashy/grey renders are grading failures.

**Lifetime Editing System:** Layer 1 (Correction: Exposure + WB per-image) separate from Layer 2 (Style: Tone Curve, HSL, preset). Never mix layers. Complexion Compensation: Exposure > Highlights > HSL Orange > Color Grading.

**Three-Tool Pipeline:** Lightroom is spine (catalog, develop, masks). Evoto is skin specialist (frequency sep, dodge/burn, color match, backdrop optimization). Photoshop is surgeon for Track B environmental compositing only. Most frames live entirely in Lightroom.

**B&W Card Dual-Register:** Card locked: masthead #F5EFE6, Playfair Black, -1 sat / +0.08 contrast / +0.02 exposure. B&W Mix: Orange +40 (primary skin control for dark complexions), Red +25, Yellow +15, Green -20, Blue -30. Export: JPEG sRGB 1350px IG / TIF ProPhoto 300ppi print.

**Evoto Scope:** Skin/backdrop/gentle body only. Not compositing, color grading, or identity work. Mid-pipeline only between LR color and LR Hero Finish. AI Retain Shadows = single most critical setting. Opacity 85-95% for gradients, 70-85% for environments. Texture dial 40-80%; 100% produces visible artifacts.
