## 3. Proof

### 01 · Functional + integrated testing coverage
PhaseLine's roster owns Level 4 functional and Level 5 integrated systems testing on hyperscale builds. Engineers arrive with scripted-test discipline, an operator's reading of what FAT and SAT outputs mean for the program, and the bench credibility to defend a scripted-test result with the owner team in the room. Placements include lead CxA roles, controls-integration leads, and commissioning managers carrying scripted Level 4 and 5 scopes.

### 02 · Schedule + hand-off discipline
PhaseLine matches operators to specific schedule gates · first-energization readiness, integrated systems test, owner hand-off · and selects for the ability to close the gate, not just attend the meeting. The firm builds back from the program's milestone calendar; the operator placed is matched to the gate they have to land.

### 03 · Critical-facilities standards literacy
The roster carries fluency in the standards programs hire against:
- Uptime Tier I through IV classifications and the operator implications of each.
- ASHRAE TC 9.9 thermal classes (A1 through A4) and the cooling design they imply.
- NFPA 70E (electrical safety) and NFPA 72 (fire alarm and signaling), and the commissioning coordination both require.
- IEEE 1584 (arc-flash) and IEEE 3007 (mission-critical power distribution) on the controls and integration side.
- BMS and EPMS integration points and the control-system commissioning that ties them together.

Operators work in the standard, not around it.
