### BOOK 1: Prompt Template - Self Criticism (Basic)-3.pdf

**Coverage:** 1/1 segments, complete.

**Thesis:** A structured self-criticism loop (generate, evaluate against criteria, identify gaps, suggest one iterative improvement) is a lightweight but powerful quality gate for any AI-assisted output before it ships.

**Densest Frameworks:**

1. **Three-point evaluation pattern:** validate existing quality, identify gaps, suggest one iterative improvement. Forces specificity over vague revision.
2. **Self-criticism loop:** generate first pass, then prompt AI to evaluate against specific criteria (clarity, completeness, fit, tone, actionability). Second-pass critique is a distinct prompt, not an edit.
3. **Tagging taxonomy (SE / SR / COVE):** Strategic-Execution, Sales-Revenue, Coverage-Verification. Signals the *type* of output being evaluated, calibrating what "good" means per context.
4. **CTA and hashtag check (Marketing gate):** message clarity, engagement, missing CTAs, suggested effectiveness boost. Checklists for specific output categories.
5. **Documentation reorganization gate:** flow, examples, simplified complexity. A process doc eval that mirrors Direction Stack's own SOP logic.
6. **"One iterative improvement" constraint:** prevents revision bloat; forces prioritization of the highest-leverage change.

**SNIPED Application:**
- Self-criticism loop is the missing gap between first draft and ship for: brand system copy, LinkedIn positioning language, case study accuracy, Cultural Doc clarity, caption drafts.
- The three-point pattern maps directly to SNIPED's "hardest to say no to" doctrine: if you cannot specify what gap remains after critique, you are not ready to ship.
- The tagging system (SE / SR / COVE) can be adapted as a quality-gate classifier before any output exits SNIPED's production pipeline.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine:**
- **Extends** the "hardest to say no to" doctrine (LOCKED 2026-06-02) with a concrete operational mechanism: the self-criticism prompt is *how* you verify you've cleared that bar.
- **Extends** "maximum by default" rule: max depth requires a verification pass, not just a generation pass. The critique loop is the proof step.
- **Weakness flagged:** the template provides no standard for what constitutes a "weak" vs. "strong" critique, and no audience-specificity layer. For SNIPED's premium lane, the evaluation criteria must be hardened against the buyer's actual decision context (founder psychology, status signaling logic, trust equation). Bare-template version is a starting point, not the finished gate.

### BOOK 2: The Color Purple Collection (Walker, Alice) - The Color Purple + The Temple of My Familiar + Possessing the Secret of Joy

**Coverage:** 35/35 segments, complete.

**Thesis:** Three interlocked novels constitute a complete theology of lineage, witness, and resistance: identity emerges from inside inherited suffering and creative making, not from institutional naming or outsider observation; resistance is not a tactic but the foundational condition for joy; the photograph, the letter, the painting, the song, the bell, and the pants exist as a single family of technologies for holding what patriarchy, colonialism, and slavery tried to erase.

**Densest Frameworks:**

1. **Lineage-from-Inside vs. Lineage-Tourism:** The work documents FROM INSIDE five nested lineages (Black church, Black Southern family, diasporic African, queer, maker-artist). Every character who observes from outside (Nettie as missionary, Suwelo in his library, the museum diorama, the Sorbonne) misses the truth. Every character who enters the lineage by living it (Celie making pants, Nzingha apprenticing to elders, Evelyn naming Dura's murder, Walker claiming the whole continent) produces authority.

2. **Repetition as Architecture, Not Limitation:** Celie's epistolary repetition (same household, deepening tone), Hal and Lissie painting side by side across decades, Fanny returning to Africa twice, Ola driving the same car until it is taken, Bessie Head at her typewriter in the desert every day. The architecture is built; the work is reps.

3. **Testimony as Spiritual and Political Act:** Celie's "Dear God" letters, Nettie's intercepted letters, Lissie's invisible-ink letter, Evelyn's courtroom naming of Dura's murder, Tashi's execution banner. Each is a speech act that resists erasure and regenerates identity for the speaker before any audience receives it.

4. **Photography/Image as Identity Corroboration and Witness:** Shug Avery's photo as proof another life exists. Hal's "interior portraits" where each paints the other's infinite inner space (not the surface face). Henry Laytrum's 30-year photo archive of Lissie as 13 different women. M'Sukta's photograph radiating "health, richness, intelligence, and shine" contra colonial narrative. The ancient fertility idols as counter-archive to the blank-faced doll. Photography does not create selfhood; it surfaces and validates what is already real.

5. **Craft as Prayer (Maker Identity Over Content Identity):** Celie's pants business. Zede's grandmother striking a bell to check soul-alignment before creating priestly feather-capes. Arveyda's bread. Ola's plays. Lissie's paintings. Carlotta's bell chimes. Mr. ____'s shirt (made to pair with Celie's pants). Making is the spiritual practice; the maker identity precedes any institutional category.

6. **Possibility Engine as Operating Logic:** Miss Lissie as 13 different women. Fanny refusing to collapse into "wife" or "professor" or "African." Bryce as "operator/possibility engine, not any one output." Every character who accepts institutional naming (Suwelo refusing the shopping cart, Nzingha's rejection at the Sorbonne, Tashi under trial) loses something fundamental. Every character who refuses it, even at cost, finds "oddly free."

7. **Cosmological Justification as Harm Technology:** The Dogon myth (clitoris as termite hill excised by God), the termite-queen metaphor (winged queen clipped to immobile reproduction tube), coded riddles as multi-generational institutional transmission. Patriarchy does not require force alone; it requires creation myths. Any system that embeds harm in cosmology (including branding systems and positioning frameworks) must be interrogated at the level of its foundational premises, not just its outputs.

8. **Scene Density as Community Survival Strategy:** Fanny-Suwelo-Arveyda-Carlotta's house with dual wings and ceremonial center. Sofia's family of six brothers and sisters as collective defense. The banquet house culture ("All those at the banquet!"). Ola's compound with women's painted adobe walls. "In a house where two men cared for her, Lissie recovered." Density of witnesses, not breadth of audience, is the survival mechanism.

**SNIPED Application:**

The Color Purple Collection is the most complete single corpus validation of the SNIPED OS doctrine already on disk. Specific applications:

- **Lineage Doctrine:** Walker's architecture is the doctrine's literary proof. Every character who works from inside their lineage produces authority. Every outsider produces flatness or harm. Bryce's five lineages are not heritage claims; they are epistemological requirements for the work to be real.
- **Direction Stack methodology:** "Taken vs. made" images (Berger/Dyer) lives throughout the text. Shug's photo, Lissie's interior portraits, the ancient idols vs. the blank doll. The Direction Stack's insistence on "made" over "taken" is Walker's insistence made operational.
- **Repetition over Novelty (LOCKED):** The entire Walker corpus is this doctrine in narrative form. The Cathedral exists; run the office.
- **Possibility Engine protection:** Miss Lissie as 13 women, Fanny refusing categories, Carlotta as "female impersonator" recovering embodiment, Nzingha holding father's and mother's legacies simultaneously. Do not crown a lane. Identity emerges from proof.
- **Edit Register Bifurcation (LOCKED):** Tashi's body (identity untouched even through mutilation's aftermath), hair as variable (end of novel), the edit register between "who she is" and "how she is styled." Validated at narrative depth.
- **Scene-Density Thinking:** Every surviving community in Walker is tight, specific, and layered. No community survives breadth. SNIPED's LA cluster-building mirrors this.
- **Maximum-by-Default:** Walker writes at maximum creative depth in every sentence. No tier offer. Just ship the best.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine:**

- **Strengthens** Repetition over Novelty. Walker's own creative practice is the proof: Tashi appeared in The Color Purple, survived into Temple, and drove Possessing the Secret of Joy. The same character, 20 years of commitment. Reps compound.

SNIPED's authority comes from inside-lineage proof, not from positioning as instructor.
