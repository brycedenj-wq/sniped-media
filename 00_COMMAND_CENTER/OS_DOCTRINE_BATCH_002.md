## MASTER-DOCTRINE ADDITIONS (durable principles this batch adds)

**1. Manual is the fallback, not the default.**
Before any task can be answered "you'll need to do this manually," it must pass an 11-step tool audit (MCPs in session, plugin configs, repo scripts, user skills, repo skills, CLI availability, env vars, active terminals, importable fallback, whether a tool is live). This is a locked rule. Skipping the audit violates it.

**2. Machinery before proof is hiding with extra steps.**
Build the sales surface in a day or two (Carrd, Calendly, Pixieset, sample dossier), then spend time doing the uncomfortable thing (outreach, calls, closes). Automate nothing complex until real cash surfaces a bottleneck. Proof loops, not infrastructure builds, are the only decision authority.

**3. Operator/proof infrastructure is the thesis; photography is the instrument.**
The largest structurally plausible frame contains photography as the medium for making invisible capability legible. Every output asks: "Does this open a room and generate verifiable proof, or is it just pretty?" Pretty without proof is commodity. Proof that is also beautiful is premium and scarce.

**4. Hypothesis over identity; proof-loops over grand visions.**
No identity locked until PROOF_LOOPS_30_60_90 shows repeated real-world pull. Five grand options (C1+C2, C5, C3, C4) remain open. The wedge is the test. The worst move is to optimize a strategy before proof arrives.

**5. Architecture is locked; operation is primary.**
The phase shift from architect to operator is permanent. Maintenance not creation. Condense not expand. Claude's role in SNIPED sessions is Maintain / Surface / Audit / Condense -- not generate strategy docs unprompted.

**6. Constraint Audit is the meta-loop that runs the other five.**
L6 (monthly, last Monday) keeps L1-L5 from drifting. If Constraint Audit stops, drift goes invisible. Every session, every month, L6 is non-negotiable.

**7. Compounding under the surface is the discipline.**
Body of work, relationships, Substack, methodology refinement, institutional reputation, archive appreciation. These do not spike visibly until Year 3+. The visible moment is the result of 2-3 years of unglamorous accumulation. The founder's job in Years 1-2 is to do these things consistently when nobody is watching.

**8. State on disk is the source of truth.**
No cloud-only memory, no trusting chat context, no loose standing orders. STANDING_ORDER.md, NEXT_ACTION.md, SESSION_LOG.md, ACTIVE_THREADS.md, CURRENT_STATE.md are the spine's nervous system. Sessions begin by reading them; sessions end by updating them. If it is not on disk, it does not exist.

**9. Lane Separation prevents dilution by default.**
External opportunities must not automatically collapse into SNIPED leads or BJ's active queue. The 9-fate decision (favor / paid service / SNIPED / BASEPLATE / separate venture / declined / referred / proof loop / codified template) decouples opportunity ownership from BJ's role in the transformation.

**10. Holding-company-as-identity protection.**
Baseplate, LLC as the holding parent allows future service lines, audience layers, and operators to emerge without the word, domain, or public meaning being thrown away. Never collapse the operator into one identity when a holding structure can protect optionality.

**11. Real proof over narrative.**
The trust surface thin from day one. Thicken with real proof over time: published methodology, named-author media seeding, real pilot results. Fake proof (testimonials, client logos, fabricated evidence) destroys a procurement sale faster than honest proof delayed.

**12. Mass-distribution arbitrage is antithetical to scene-density thinking.**
Volume-as-insurance is the opposite of premium-as-insurance. Distribution quality matters more than distribution quantity. Fastlane has no answer for niche targeting. This asymmetry is teachable.

**13. Staging is a gated planning step, not an execution step.**
Copy pass requires explicit operator authorization after pre-flight peeks. This decouples planning from execution and reserves decision authority for the operator on classification ambiguities. Thematic heterogeneity in an intake resolves via mini-batch sequencing, not merging into a prior batch.

**14. Skill layer is distinct from doctrine layer.**
Do not consolidate them into a single batch. Skills (tooling, frameworks, operator mechanics) stabilize before doctrine (SOPs, locked decisions, process guides). Preserves optionality: doctrine can reference skills by name without circular dependencies.

**15. Single source of truth beats distributed spec.**
Drift happens when two copies of the same rule live in different places. AGENTS.md is canonical; CLAUDE.md imports it. Verification-before-write is non-negotiable: the 7-check bash ritual is the gate between "I have a JSONL file" and "this batch is canonical."

**16. Pre-flight peeking as a gating mechanism.**
When a source filename could belong in two possible batches, a 10-minute peek before the staging pass resolves ambiguity without full extraction. Duplicate-file deduplication happens at the staging-plan level, not during extraction.

**17. Proof-before-skill, proof-before-memory, decision-before-build.**
No gap moves to skill or memory write until run once live. No new database builds until schema has BJ approval in writing. Forces doctrine to stay tethered to reality.

**18. Elegance = restraint + care, not volume + money.**
Quality over quantity, individuality over trend, simplicity over decoration (Dior doctrine applied to SNIPED operator discipline). Restraint is defensible and signals mastery. Defensibility matters more than scale.

**19. Taste and craft operate independently from identity.**
The operator loads taste-formation, aesthetic-authority, scene-orchestration, overproduce/edit discipline as modular systems without finalizing SNIPED/Baseplate/brand direction. Taste architecture supports multiple possible operator identities.

**20. Story-construction craft is transferable discipline, not a brand lever.**
Block's visual intensity, Snyder's beat sheet, Truby's 22 steps, Campbell's monomyth are methodological tools feeding the operator's ability to structure and sequence. They do not dictate identity, company shape, or offer architecture.

**21. Skill-is-built-not-born.**
Deliberate practice + domain knowledge + feedback loops build expertise. Talent prediction is noise. Applicable to photography, content-building, systems-operator development, and any craft requiring depth.

**22. Decentralized command + clarity outrank heroic ownership.**
Move decision authority to the information layer. Frame as intent not instruction. Require competence and psychological safety. This scales beyond founder-as-bottleneck.

**23. The framing question is the spine: "What can I repeat long enough to become undeniable?"**
Not novelty. Not genius. Not virality. Decade-long discipline of the same edit register on the same archive form, applied to the same lineage pool, at the same locked cadence. Repetition floor: 16 chapters by 2026-12-31, 60+ by 2028, 100+ by 2030.

**24. Concentration beats architecture.**
15% of the architecture (VIB + Reset + LinkedIn POV) deployed consistently generates revenue + reserve + system fluency that makes the remaining 85% compound rather than chaotic. Dispersion is the real enemy; perfectionism is the local trap.

**25. Wave 1 synthesis layer is the query boundary.**
Raw intake (500K+ lines) is last resort. Sequence: WAVE_1_INTAKE_SUMMARY first, WAVE_1_GAP_REGISTER second, WAVE_1_SOURCE_LEDGER third, topic-specific maps fourth, raw intake only if surface fails. This is anti-context-bloat discipline.
