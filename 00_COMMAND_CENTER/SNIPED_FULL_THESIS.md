## 3. Core SNIPED category
SNIPED is NOT a photography business. It is a **status image-making and visual-authority practice** for LA founders, operators, and creatives, with a **cultural-documentation ceiling**. The product is visual authority for serious people whose presence sits below the level of what they have built. Photography is the instrument, not the category. Operator-coded, not artist-coded.

## 4. One-sentence thesis
SNIPED is a status image-making and visual-authority practice for LA founders, operators, and creatives that compounds over a decade into a cultural-documentation body of work; photography is the instrument, not the category.

## 5. One-paragraph thesis
SNIPED makes serious people and culture legible and elevated through directed images. The commercial engine is visual authority for founders and operators whose presence sits below the level of what they have built (the Reset, then the Operator Kit, then the Brand System), proven now through creatives and models. ## 10. Offer ladder (the locked spine, surfaced)
- **Reset ($1,500):** Tier 1. 1 look, 1 location, ~45 min, 20 retouched in 5 business days, $750 non-refundable deposit, $80/image gallery upsell with 48-hour expiry. The only offer pitched in cold outreach. The cash wedge.
- **Operator Kit ($3-8K):** Tier 2. Multi-look, broader system, 12-month commercial license. Pitched at Day 30 to delivered Reset clients only.
- **Brand System ($10K+):** Tier 3. Full visual identity engineering. Not pitched until 3 Operator Kits close.
- **Strategic free (with return mechanics):** Collab (1 hero, SNIPED keeps rights), Community (cultural pillars, returns trust/access/referrals), Access (event coverage for downstream intros).
- **Content/media layer:** the campaign, LinkedIn POV, Substack (Phase B+). Iron Maiden cadence.
- **Direction Stack product layer:** the book ("A Recovery Manual for Photographers," 57 pages, 10 protocols), held private until a Q3 2026 launch, after 3-5 Resets close.
- **Parked layers:** the paid cultural-documentation layer (relationship-built first); any course/training (later).
