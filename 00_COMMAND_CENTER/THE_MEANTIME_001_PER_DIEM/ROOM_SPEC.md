One real-feeling room, defined once. Frames 1-5 live here and every prompt inherits this spec verbatim. Frames 6-7 use the satellite spaces below. This is the continuity law (os-world-bible discipline at minimum scale).

## The room
Mid-tier business hotel, North American chain register, deliberately unplaceable. 12th-floor feel. Clean but tired. Generic wear, observed, never dramatized.

- **Layout:** entry corridor past bathroom; queen bed on the right wall; desk against the window wall; dresser with flatscreen TV facing the bed; single armchair in the corner; ironing board deployed near the closet.
- **Palette:** beige walls, navy-and-taupe geometric carpet (low pile, corporate pattern), white bedding with a navy runner folded at the foot, dark walnut-laminate furniture. Blackout curtains in heavy oatmeal weave with a sheer behind.
- **Wear level:** scuff at the door's kickplate, faint ring stains on the nightstand, iron-shine on the board's cover, TV remote worn glossy. Nothing broken, nothing new.
- **Anonymity law:** zero brand marks anywhere. Hotel art is a generic abstract print, out of focus whenever visible. Key card is blank white. Phone, notepad, pen are unbranded.

Frame law: ONE source dominates per frame, a second may support, never three.
