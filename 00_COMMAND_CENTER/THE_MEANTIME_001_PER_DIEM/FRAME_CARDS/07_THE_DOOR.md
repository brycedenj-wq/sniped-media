# 07 · THE DOOR
**Register:** threshold
**Subject:** the room's door from the corridor at 1am: warm lamp light leaking under the door's bottom gap, a shadow interrupting the leak mid-crossing. Someone is in there, building.
**Composition:** centered or near-centered door, corridor walls converging slightly; the light-leak is the brightest element in a dim frame; the shadow's interruption is the event.
**Light law:** dimmed corridor sconces (warm, weak) + the under-door leak (warm, alive). Two warm sources, leak dominates.
**Insider detail:** the gap-light at an hour when every other door on the corridor is dark.
**Must prove:** the closer. The permanent series law: every chapter of THE MEANTIME ends on a threshold not yet walked through. Sequencing weight: must end the arc, not repeat frame 3.
