## DOCTRINE ENTRIES (deduped)

**D-01: Cold Email Rule of One**
One reader, one idea, one offer, one action per email. Email 1 is 80 words max (ideal under 70). Plain text only. No links in body. No images, attachments, pricing, emoji, or em-dashes.

**D-02: Sending Domain Lock**
Five sending domains permanently assigned to cold email. admin@snipedmedia.com killed for cold outreach (0% reply, damages primary business email). Sending architecture is infrastructure debt; do not touch.

**D-03: ICP Waterfall + 4-of-4 Gate**
Assistant sourcing uses strict 4-of-4 ICP criteria (LA-based + active poster + visual gap + revenue/funding). Tier 1 = 3+ trigger events. No exceptions. Tier hierarchy drives personalization depth, not volume.

**D-04: Founder / Non-Founder Split**
LinkedIn VIB lane serves Tier 0 CRM founders (low volume, high precision). Cold email via Super Search serves non-founder tech operators (VP/Director/C-Suite at 21-500-person firms). Zero cannibalization rule. The two tracks never share leads.

**D-05: Loom Audit as Front-End Default**
5-minute prospect-specific Loom (BJ master audio + Higgsfield-generated reference visuals + prospect LinkedIn photo). Hybrid-operator stance: BJ voice (human) + AI world-construction (Higgsfield). NOT a free consult. NOT a brochure. This is the only front-end offer for cold email C1.

**D-06: Volume-Throttle-Before-Scale**
Start cold email at 200/day; validate math at 5 audit requests/day (25/week) before scaling to 500/day. Option A is the default. Prevents capacity blowout on Ren's Loom production.

**D-07: Quarterly TAM Refresh (Non-Optional)**
Re-sequence entire TAM every 90 days with fresh enrichment and a different offer angle. Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4 each use angle A/B/C/D. Prevents list exhaustion. Same list, four rotation cycles = system reps, not new strategy.

**D-08: VIB as Sole Cold Outreach Asset**
Single Figma-built PNG (1920x1280px, #1A1A1A background). Prospect photo (unedited, from LinkedIn) on left. SNIPED demographic-matched reference on right. No logo, watermark, animation, or URL on asset. Footer references Direction Stack only. DM script mandatory per send (lives in VIB_caption_library.md).

**D-09: VIB Pacing Discipline**
6 VIBs/week. Mon evening production (3 VIBs in 25-min target). Tue/Wed/Thu 7:30-8:30 AM send (one per day). Day 5 follow-up (Script D, Loom) only for Series A+ founders or strong-connection non-responders. Never a third message in thread. 90-day recontact window per prospect.

**D-10: Discovery Call Binary Close**
Three valid close outcomes only: Commitment (book date), Advancement (specific follow-up within 7 days), Clean Rejection. No zombie leads. Price is fixed ($1,500 floor). Scope is flexible. Mom Test discipline on all calls (past behavior + specifics, not hypotheticals).

**D-11: Reply SLA Structure**
Positive replies: 2-hour SLA (30-min target). Questions: 4-hour SLA. Deflections/negatives: 24-hour SLA. Unibox checked 3x daily (9 AM, 1 PM, 5 PM). Ren handles replies 1-7. Escalation to BJ via Slack on ambiguous, hostile, pricing, or out-of-spec threads.

**D-12: Pricing Floor Non-Negotiable at All Levels**
$1,500 Reset floor holds at Ren level, BJ level, and in all DM reply scripts. Scope flexes (12-frame hero vs 6-frame essentials). No discount language. No free strategy call. Price is not pre-negotiated in DM.

**D-13: Visual Gap Diagnosis as Sourcing Gate**
Asymmetry between a founder's visual register and their operational sophistication is the primary ICP signal. False positives waste VIB production. Monthly calibration sampling with BJ feedback is mandatory. Claude is approved for research enrichment and gap drafting, not DM/email composition or strategy.

**D-14: 5-Beat Shoot Sequence (Reset Portrait)**
Nine-minute beats: architecture > mechanics > energy > rotation > variation. Single variables at a time. Thought Narration unlocks presence. Show one frame on camera-back (trust deposit, not gallery review). 4-hour Phase 1 ingest after wrap. 5-business-day delivery clock starts from shoot end.

**D-15: Pre-Shutter 5-Point Gate (Posing)**
SHAPE (silhouette) + DIRECTION (where eyes/body point) + STORY (emotion/role). Build-a-pose: feet/weight > hips > spine > arms > face micro-cues > energy cue. Never silent during shoot. Energy management = trust signal = premium price justification.

**D-16: Dual-Lane Grading**
Lane A (editorial mood): warm WB, lifted blacks, shadows +15-25, Red+Blue calibration +5-10, grain 8-15. Lane B (product accuracy): neutral WB, linear tone curve, near-zero grain, garment colors true to physical. Identity and skin texture protected in both lanes. Adobe Neutral base profile throughout.

**D-17: B&W Card Dual-Register**
HERO posts are full v3 LUXURY color (the moment). Chapter Card images render B&W (the document). B&W via saturation -100 in Lightroom or Figma filter. Apparatus layer stays color. Doctrine-clean via Aperture/LIFE/Magnum tradition.

**D-18: Composite 11-Gate QA**
Gates 1-8 = LITE/INTERNAL tier. Gates 9-11 (DOF/lens match, perspective/camera-height match, directional color bleed) = CEILING/CLIENT-READY. Subject identity is LOCK POINT post-grade. Gate 8 is hard reject: AI smear, warped geometry, barcode sky, melted plants. Proof bundle mandatory (hero, hero with text, 100% hair/edge crop, 100% feet/shadow crop, before/after, QA scorecard).

**D-19: Empty-Prompt-First (Generative Fill)**
Default is empty prompt for Generative Fill. Escalate to minimal descriptive only on failure. 3 outputs per generation; pick variant passing 5 checkpoints (no seam, tonal match within 5%, no hallucinated objects, no texture loops, maintained DoF). After 6 failed attempts, stop and escalate to manual clone/heal or accept documentary context.

**D-20: Seedream Version Picker**
Seedream 5.0 Lite: background plate generation, color palette enforcement (HEX direct to output), environment compositing. Seedream 4.5: face-preserving composites. Nano Banana Pro: identity-preserving subject compositing (best-in-class likeness hold). Never use any Seedream version for client deliverables or subject face generation.

**D-21: Higgsfield Use Matrix**
Higgsfield approved for: IG hero composite (lite lane), carousel multi-frame consistency, BTS UGC frames, Op Kit/Brand System hero shots, pre-vis briefs for shoots, content trend research. Higgsfield banned for: client deliverables, subject face/identity generation, replacing Portfolio anchor composites.

**D-22: Hook Library Maintenance**
80+ production-ready hooks organized by content surface. Banned hook register (9 patterns, including any "I"-opener and "hot take"). Quarterly refresh: retire hooks used 3+ times in 90 days, target 60-100 active hooks in rotation.

**D-23: Reference Pool Requirement for VIB**
12-16 reference frames pre-categorized by gender/age/skin tone/register/setting required before any VIB deployment. Demographic mismatch = abort. Prospect photo rule: unedited, direct from LinkedIn. Export format: PNG only (JPG compresses skin tones unevenly).

**D-24: 5-Pass Cull Discipline**
Reject > Technical > Lane/Select > Best-in-Cluster > Standout. Reduces ~300 RAWs to ~50. Gate 1: curator culls to 50. Gate 2: client picks favorites from proof set. Gate 3: hero retouch + recolor locked after picks. No hero retouch pre-Gate 2 (anti-pattern: over-editing before client selection).

**D-25: Photography Vault as Technical Reference Layer**
Lighting patterns (8 named), multi-light setups (13), posing diagrams (6) = vocabulary only. Direction Stack Protocol = grammar layer (which pattern for which founder). SNIPED's edge is the selection and sequencing methodology, not the patterns themselves. Lighting patterns are NOT LinkedIn content (creator-tier positioning hazard).

**D-26: Photoshop Reference Image Collapse**
Photoshop 2026 Generative Fill + Reference Image collapses Path A (fast) and Path B (controlled) to 80% of Path B quality at 20% of the time. Reference image library: 3-5 images per environment, saved to `/SNIPED_OS/05_PRODUCTION/_composite_references/`. Decision now depends on control/iteration preference, not quality gap.

**D-27: Claude Phase Stack**
Phase 1: Opus 4.7 (strategic), Sonnet 4.6 (content velocity/tactical), Haiku 4.5 (batch low-stakes). Phase B+ unlocks: Cowork (Monday cockpit, VIB prep, Constraint Audit reminder), Computer Use (Lightroom imports, Pixieset uploads, Notion entry, Higgsfield pipelines). Defer: Claude for Excel, Claude Design, Enterprise plans.

**D-28: Director Not Photographer Reframe**
On-set work is direction/energy architecture. Subjects are directed, not posed. Director confidence is the primary trust signal to premium buyers. Energy management = the actual premium product. SHAPE + DIRECTION + STORY gate before every shutter.

**D-29: Evoto Color-Blend Path (Efficient Chapter Production)**
For chapters with limited source pool (2-look shoot), backdrop-swap via color blend mode preserves original studio lighting and avoids full Rejuar composite bandwidth. Valid efficiency trade. Rejuar remains available for advanced composites, not required.

**D-30: LinkedIn Comment Pacing**
5-10 comments/day on LA founder posts (Tier 0 CRM only). Search: "LA founder" past 24 hrs, latest sort. Never global "founder" feeds. Spread across 3 posting windows (9 AM, 1 PM, 5 PM). Format: soft opener + specific post-language callback + slightly imperfect tone. 1-2 sentences max. No emojis, no corporate polish.
