## ONE-SENTENCE THESIS
A synthetic aristocrat-heir of a foreclosed hunt-country dynasty is catalogued and sold off, object by object, while still standing in her own house, tagged as **LOT 00**, the most valuable and most for-sale thing in the estate. Inherited untouchable luxury collides with total liquidation. She holds the body grammar of a portrait sitter who owns the room, while a kraft auction-tag on her wrist says she is inventory. ## VISUAL LAWS (gate-enforceable)
1. **The tag is omnipresent or the frame is killed.** Every hero frame contains at least one visible kraft lot-tag. If the auction-tag intrusion is absent, the frame reverts to generic-premium aristocrat and is rejected at gate. NON-NEGOTIABLE. [CERT diagnosis: restraint on an empty synthetic reads as stock unless broken by the signature intrusion]
2. **Scale-violence required.** The figure must be in measurable scale-conflict with the space in every hero frame: dwarfed, hemmed, crated-in, or hammer-threatened. A frame where she is comfortably, proportionally placed in a pretty room is a FAIL even if technically beautiful. [CERT diagnosis: conflict makes a frame alive]
3. **One punctum per frame, do-not-clean.** Each hero carries exactly one unplanned, slightly-wrong detail the pipeline is forbidden to retouch out: a tag-string frayed, a glove seam split, a crate-nail catching light, an off-fall of the unworn glove. The do-not-clean list overrides the generator's smoothing instinct. [PROV Barthes punctum; the wound is the tell]
4. **Restraint is the floor the distortion breaks, not the identity.** Adobe-Neutral quiet-luxury base is the resting state. The signature is what VIOLATES it (the tag, the scale-violence, the red). Restraint alone = stock. [CERT visual_direction_luxury_editorial used as base, not as the whole move]
5. **Lot numbers climb chapter to chapter.** Visible lot numbers in-frame escalate across the body of work as the estate empties. A readable countdown of dispossession. The world has a memory and a direction. [PROV worldbuilding: meaning compounds; CERT scene-density memory]
6. **Every frame is a deposit into the tag.** No standalone pretty pictures. Each image must add to the lot-tag language or it does not ship. [CERT advertising: compound the symbol or erode it]

## LOCATION GRAMMAR (the constrained rotation that COMPOUNDS)

1. **The tack room** · auction-tagged saddles on racks, bridles tagged, the leather smell you can almost read. The most intimate room: the working heart of the dynasty, tagged.
