- Algorithm vs. Intuition Rule: Trust intuition in high-validity environments with extensive practice + feedback. Use algorithm otherwise. Default to algorithm when uncertain.
- Risk Policy Framework: Broad framing (treat each decision as one of many) reduces loss aversion. Define rule in advance, apply uniformly.
- Organizational Debiasing Protocol: 9-step (common vocabulary, premortems, reference-class forecasting, structured evaluation, reframing, broad framing, slowdown, devil's advocate, process-quality review).
