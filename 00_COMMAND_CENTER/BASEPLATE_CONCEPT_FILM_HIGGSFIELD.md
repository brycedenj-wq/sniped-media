## Film spec (paste at top of the Higgsfield project)
**6 shots, ~60s total, 16:9 cinematic primary. 9:16 social cut derived from Shots 1, 3, 4 (signing only), 6.** Working title: "The Operator."

## Pipeline (per Higgsfield Operator Playbook order)
1. **Soul ID first** if BJ wants to be in it: train on 20-30 of his own photos (varied angle/light). Tag `@op`. Otherwise use an archetypal operator. Soul ID stays BASEPLATE-only, never crosses to SNIPED.

4. Feed the previous scene's clip as context for continuity. Lock start/end frames.

## MASTER STYLE block (reuse in every prompt)
> Cinematic anamorphic, large-format look. Palette locked: onyx black `#0F0F0F`, concrete grey `#8C8C8C`, single accent of blueprint blue `#0055FF`. Industrial precision, architectural brutalism, data-center-schematic mood. Low-key restrained lighting, deep shadow, soft volumetric haze. Quiet-luxury editorial, premium, serious. Subtle 35mm grain. NEGATIVE: no teal-and-orange grading, no cartoon, no 3D-render look, no stock-corporate vibe, no logos, no text artifacts, no warm skin glow, no shallow-dof bokeh-blur fashion-portrait look, no servers / racks / cabling / equipment labels / signage.

## Visual thesis (the through-line)
BASEPLATE is the operating layer underneath the physical-AI buildout. The film visualizes "underneath / foundational" through three repeating motifs:
1. The operator stands on or near STRUCTURE (columns, beam intersections, footings, foundation slabs), never near TECH (servers, racks).
2. A literal **industrial baseplate** (the engineering object: a machined steel mounting slab, ~12 by 18 inches, four bolt holes, brushed surface, soft fill light) appears in Shot 3 (the desk) and Shot 5 (the result-object).
3. Blueprint blue is the only chromatic signal in an otherwise onyx/concrete world. The blue is the operator's eye; everything else is grey.

## Operator styling (locked, applies to `@op` everywhere)
- Heavy work jacket, technical fabric, no logos, no patches, no badges.
- Charcoal, deep grey, or black only. No color in clothing anywhere.
- Sleeves rolled in Shots 1 and 3 (working state). Jacket on in Shots 2 and 4 (moving and meeting state).
- No watch, no rings, no chain, no visible brand marks. Restraint signals seniority.

## Sound spec
- **No VO.** Atmosphere carries.
- Low industrial ambient score from Shot 1 to Shot 5. One rising note enters at Shot 4 (the trust beat) and decays back into ambient at the signing of Shot 5.
- Wordmark hold in Shot 6 is silent for the final 2 seconds before the file ends.

## SHOT 1 · THE OPERATOR, PRE-DAWN
> 1 shot, 10s, 16:9. `@op` is the character reference. [MASTER STYLE]. Composition: wide, a lone man at a minimal steel desk in a vast dark industrial space, one screen glowing faint blueprint-blue on his face. Subject: still, focused, calm, sleeves rolled, no watch. Camera move: slow dolly-in, eye level. Mood: quiet command before the world wakes. 0-5s push in slowly, 5-10s settle on his face lit by the screen. Camera IS one slow dolly-in. Camera is NOT cutting, NOT zooming, NOT orbiting.

## SHOT 2 · THE FOUNDATION
> 1 shot, 10s, 16:9. [MASTER STYLE]. `@op` jacket on, walking past a row of massive structural columns and exposed footings, blueprint-blue task lights running along beam intersections. Cavernous concrete and steel, no servers, no equipment, no signage, no logos. Subject: `@op` small against the architecture, walking unhurried, sense of belonging. Camera move: low tracking push following behind him, single continuous move. Mood: this is what holds it all up, he stands on the foundation. Camera IS tracking forward low. Camera is NOT cutting, NOT craning. NEGATIVE: no racks, no equipment, no cables, no fluorescent overhead, keep it architectural and abstract.

## SHOT 3 · THE CRAFT
> 1 shot, 10s, 16:9. [MASTER STYLE]. Composition: tight insert, the desk surface in soft fill light. In foreground: a machined steel industrial baseplate (mounting slab, brushed surface, four visible bolt holes, ~12 by 18 inches). In middle ground: deliberate hands at work, editor's precision. In background: a screen with a clean image being composed, a proof document taking shape, blueprint-blue UI accents. Camera move: slow lateral slide left to right across the desk, ending with the baseplate in the foreground, hands at center, screen at back. Mood: the eye at work, the brand made physical, nothing rushed. Camera IS a slow lateral slide. Camera is NOT zooming, NOT shaking. NEGATIVE: no warm tones on hands, no over-stylized macro lighting.

## SHOT 4 · THE ROOM (trust beat, Kling 3.0)
> 1 shot, 10s, 16:9. `@op` character reference, jacket on. [MASTER STYLE] plus one warm key light cutting the cool. Composition: two men across a concrete table, shallow depth. Subject: `@op` calm, listening, weight back, not selling. The other man is older, infrastructure-firm principal, has seen every pitch. His trust shift reads in micro-expression: his eyes settling on the operator and staying. NO leaning forward, NO smiling, NO nod yet. Camera move: slow dolly-in on the second man's face as the moment registers, single continuous move. Mood: relief, recognition, "this is our guy." Camera IS one slow dolly-in. Camera is NOT cutting between faces, NOT zooming. Natural micro-head movement only. NEGATIVE: no handshake, no leaning in, no smile, no obvious body-language tell, no corporate-trust-cliche.

## SHOT 5 · THE YES + THE PROOF
> 1 shot, 10s, 16:9. [MASTER STYLE]. Composition: overhead insert, the concrete table. A finished dossier rests at center. Beside it, the same machined steel baseplate from Shot 3 sits in the upper third of frame as a quiet anchor. A pen enters frame from screen-right. The principal's hand signs a single line. Blueprint-blue accent under the signed line glows once and stays. The signing IS the yes; there is no handshake. Camera move: slow vertical lift, single continuous move. Mood: done deal, quiet weight, the first piece of evidence, the flywheel starts. Camera IS a slow vertical lift. Camera is NOT rotating, NOT cutting. NEGATIVE: no handshake, no celebration, no fast cut to faces.

## SHOT 6 · THE WORDMARK
> 1 shot, 10s, 16:9. Pure onyx black `#0F0F0F`. The word **BASEPLATE** resolves in clean industrial type (Space Grotesk or equivalent, restrained tracking), centered. A single blueprint-blue `#0055FF` underline draws in left to right beneath the wordmark. Hold 2 seconds. Then a small mono credit reveals beneath: `Bryceden Jones, operator.` Optional one-line tagline below that on the same beat: `The operating layer underneath.` (Tagline is reversible; render with and without and pick.) Hold to fade. Mood: foundational, permanent. Camera IS static. NEGATIVE: no extra text, no tagline clutter, no logo lock-up, no client list, no CTA, no URL.
