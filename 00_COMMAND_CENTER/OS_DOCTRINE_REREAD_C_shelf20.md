### BOOK 1: Blair Enns - Pricing Creativity (2018, RockBench)

**Coverage:** 9/9 segments, complete.

**Thesis:** Value is entirely subjective; creative firms that price on labor theory cap their own profit, while those who price on client risk absorption and desired future state unlock an order-of-magnitude higher margin.

**Densest Frameworks/Lessons:**

1. **Subjective Value Theory (1870s paradigm):** Value lives in the buyer's perception, never in the work itself. Nike logo ($35) vs. Pepsi logo ($1M) proves this: same output type, 50,000x price difference, explained almost entirely by risk exposure.

2. **Producer-Marketer Spectrum:** Two mutually exclusive cultures. Efficiency (producer) = price taker, caps at $150-200k AGI/FTE. Innovation (marketer) = price maker, opens path to $250-300k+. You cannot run both simultaneously at the cultural level.

3. **Three Pricing Models (Inputs < Outputs < Value):** Restaurant loyalty app: $75k (time + materials) / $90k (fixed deliverable) / $200k (desired future state). The inversion is complete: start with client challenge, decide price, constrain cost, THEN design product.

4. **Six Rules + Four-Step Value Conversation:**
   - Rule 1: Price the client, not the job (subjective value per buyer)
   - Rule 2: Offer three options, never one (extremeness aversion shifts choice right)
   - Rule 3: Anchor high (Kahneman/Tversky: high anchor lifts all estimates 20-38%)
   - Rule 4: Say price orally before showing proposal (prevents deferral, surfaces objections early)
   - Rule 5: Master the value conversation (Three-Year Question, Steps 1-4, senior buyer only)
   - Rule 6: Limit unpaid proposals to one page (proposal = visual prop for oral pitch, not persuasion artifact)
   - Value conversation steps: commit client to desired future state > metrics of success > agree value of success > offer pricing guidance as range

5. **Risk-as-Insurance Premium:** Two definitions of "premium" collapse into one. Drucker: all profit comes from risk. Clients hire you to absorb risk they carry in the marketplace. Pricing options should be framed by risk transfer level, not service tier.

6. **Cognitive Biases as Choice Architecture:** Anchoring, loss aversion, social influence, certainty bias, scarcity, confirmation bias. Confirmation bias is most powerful and least noticed. Counter it by challenging the senior client, not validating their existing view.

7. **Negotiation Doctrine:** Don't overinvest (time, money, emotion). Stop negotiating when you've won. Pre-determine concessions. Neuter procurement before they arrive. Use "Positive No," "Ask How," and "Yes, But" as alternatives to capitulating.

8. **Self-Esteem Multiplier:** PP = M x A x SE. Self-esteem is the gating variable. Controlled optionality (healthy pipeline, excess capacity, cash reserves) compensates when foundational self-worth wavers. This is not a soft insight; it is operational architecture.

**SNIPED Application:**

- $1,500 floor + three-option architecture (Proof Project / Brand System / Full Runway) maps exactly to Enns' Rule 2/3 system. Lead verbally with the highest anchor. Never lead with the floor.
- "Price the client, not the job" validates per-founder pricing. KOTS, VIBs, and named founders each see different risk profiles and different value from the same deliverable.
- Three-Year Question replaces "what do you need" in VIB discovery. Gets to wants (subjective value). Creates The Flip from vendor to expert before price is discussed.
- Bundling doctrine: all SNIPED packages end in three zeros. Never itemize. Prevents formula decoding.
- Policy-as-lever: formalize one-page spec sheets for Direction Stack, composite packages, Cultural Doc cadence. "That's the policy" ends most negotiation loops.
- Diagnostic-sale (paid first phase) for complex engagements: e.g., "Cultural Audit at $3k, then Brand System options at $8-15k." Signals confidence, gates discovery behind commitment.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine:**

- PROMOTES: intel_pricing_logic.md (Blair Enns pricing playbook) is validated and expanded. Three-option architecture gets full mechanics now (anchor uptake measurement: >20% on anchor = underpriced; always-low = gap too large).
- ADDS: Rule 4 (oral price before written proposal) was not previously operationalized as a doctrine. Gap identified: current VIB DM flow may be skipping this step. Needs to be locked as outreach protocol.
- ADDS: Procurement-neutralization tactic not previously captured. Relevant when SNIPED's client relationships involve a second-layer gatekeeper (studio managers, marketing leads, etc.).
- ADDS: Two Stages of Success (Dan Sullivan). Stage 1 = hard work + saying yes. Stage 2 = innovation + saying no. Cannot reach next level using Stage 1 tools. Directly validates "Repetition > Novelty" lock.
- ADDS: "Weird Double Thank-You" as the pricing north star. Both parties mean it. Aligns with Trust Equation (S denominator near zero) and Win Without Pitching doctrine.
- ADDS: Lease/license model for Direction Stack recurring revenue. "Strategy by the Month" as GE Power-by-the-Hour equivalent.

### BOOK 2: Gary C. Halbert - The Boron Letters (2013)

**Coverage:** 6/6 segments, complete.

**Thesis:** Clarity, selling power, and life performance are downstream from physical state, market study discipline, and working at brutal volume. The practitioner who studies what people actually buy (wallet behavior, not survey data) and customizes with precision will outperform every generalist.

**Densest Frameworks/Lessons:**

1. **Positive Addiction Doctrine:** Road work (jog/run 1hr, 6 days) creates real neurochemical addiction. Physical state gates mental clarity. "How you feel affects how you think" is not wellness language; it is causal business infrastructure.

2. **Starving Crowd > Superior Product:** The market-first sequencing law. A guy with a new product can't always find a hot market. A guy who found a hot market can always find a product. Wallet behavior is truth. Survey data is noise.

3. **List Progression Logic (DM Market Ladder):** Cold to hot in deliberate sequence: phone book > zip codes > professional lists > mail-order buyers > repeat buyers > big-ticket repeat buyers > RECENT big-ticket repeat buyers > your own satisfied customer list. Your own list is the ultimate moat.

4. **Recency / Frequency / Unit of Sale:** Three evaluation metrics for any list or buyer segment. Recency dominates. 30-90 day hotline buyers outperform all. Timing window into a market matters as much as the market itself.

5. **AIDA + HALT + Nugget Notes:**
   - AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action): governs every sales letter, every DM, every outreach sequence. Close gets 25%+ of all word count; it is not a throwaway.
   - HALT: Never decide when Hungry, Angry, Lonely, or Tired. This is an executive decision gate, not personal development advice.
   - Nugget Notes: Research assembly via starred fragments. Let subconscious work after. Breakthrough arrives after you set work aside.

6. **Customization Inverts Scarcity:** Segment by profession + geography + problem. Break product into universal core + customizable sections. Cost of customization online is near zero. Competitors skip it. Easy differentiation that compounds.

7. **A-Pile vs. B-Pile Envelope Doctrine:** Personal = always opened. Commercial = conditional. Design everything (cold email subject lines, cold DMs) for A-Pile. First-class stamp, no teaser, hand-written feel. One bad subject line halves all orders.

8. **Believability via Specificity:** "77.6% of car owners" beats "most." Parenthetical micro-details create ambient credibility without being noticed as technique. Exact names, exact outcomes, exact context in all case studies and positioning language.

9. **State Management as Business Infrastructure:** Weakness is visible. When off, work invisible (solo output, no relationship exposure). When hot, amplify output. Time-sense test (guess current time without looking; off by 10+ min = you're compromised). Pool cue story: know which fights require holding and which cost more than they return.

10. **Market-First 7-Step MO Formula:** Find hot market > find/create product > create promotion > test small (1-5k) > analyze results > scale to 20-100k if profitable > roll with cash flow.

**SNIPED Application:**

- Scene-density strategy IS Halbert's market-first law applied to cultural geography. LA Black founder and creator circles are the hot market. SNIPED's offering is designed inside the market, not imposed on it.
- Recency timing: Founders at inflection (post-raise, rebrand, scale-up, IPO prep) are the 30-90 day hotline equivalent. VIB outreach timing should exploit these windows, not spray broadly.
- Client list as moat: SNIPED's warm network (past clients, Pearl referrals, VIB relationships) IS the ultimate list. Cold outreach (Instantly, Super Search) is cold list equivalent; always lower conversion than own list.
- A-Pile doctrine for cold DM/email: personal salutation, time-stamped, specific founder mention, no "services brochure" feel. Subject line = outer envelope = critical gate for the whole sequence.
- AIDA applied to Direction Stack book launch: Attention (single strongest image), Interest (proof of craft + lineage), Desire (internal state of working inside sacred lineage), Action (pre-order link, exact URL, no ambiguity).
- Specificity as credibility: SNIPED case studies must name exactly: founder, context, specific proof outcome. Not "helped a LA founder" but "worked with [founder] during their Series A transition on [specific deliverable]."
- HALT gate: Bryce operates on limited hours (field engineer + founder). High-consequence decisions (pricing, client acceptance, partnership terms) should be HALT-checked before committing.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine:**

- ADDS: HALT decision gate. Not previously captured as a named protocol. Recommend adding to session-start/end protocol (feedback_execution_mode.md).
- ADDS: State management visibility doctrine. "When off, work invisible" is a concrete operational decision not previously codified.
- ADDS: Recency timing as a prospect-segmentation criterion. Current VIB/outreach doctrine tracks WHO to contact; Halbert adds WHEN (inflection-point window sensitivity).
- PROMOTES: "Old work informs, current proof decides" doctrine validated by Halbert's observation vs. survey distinction. Wallet behavior (actual bookings, referral patterns) over stated positioning.
- ALIGNS: "Repetition > Novelty" lock confirmed by Halbert's intentional repetition method and "going over the Barzini business" principle.
- ALIGNS: Proof over packaging, build internal-tight before market: validated by test-then-scale framework and Halbert's 5-step creation workflow.

### BOOK 3: Gemini Nano Banana Prompting Guru (3/3 segs)

**Coverage:** 3/3 segments, complete.

**Thesis:** AI image and video production has shifted from creative novelty to precision engineering. The monetizable skill is not generation; it is workflow orchestration via reference-anchored, constraint-based prompt architecture that produces consistent, reproducible output at near-zero marginal cost per variation.

**Densest Frameworks/Lessons:**

1. **6-Part Prompt Architecture:** Subject + Action + Environment + Art Style + Lighting + Camera Specs (85mm, film stock, f-stop). Technical vocabulary (lens, lighting direction, material texture) carries 80% of the photorealism bridge. Vague aesthetics carry almost nothing.

2. **Reference Density as Entropy Control:** Multi-reference feeding (3-14 images) plus annotation-driven editing replaces prompt gambling. More reference images = less hallucination. Character Reference Sheet (CRS, multi-angle: front/side/3/4/back) is generated BEFORE any narrative content. It is infrastructure, not an artifact.

3. **Constraint Prompting as Primary Lever:** All high-quality edits are deterministic: "Keep X identical, change Y." Action word (Add/Change/Make/Remove/Replace) + specific element + desired effect + detail. Specificity > creativity in all edit instructions.

4. **Three-State Workflow (Awakening Pattern):** Negative/Empty State > Positive State > Video Morph. Environment is generated once, subjects are swapped at near-zero marginal cost. One composite environment + 7 subject variations = 7 deliverable campaigns from one prompt investment.

5. **Gemini Gems as Creative Director Layer:** Gems preprocess vague intent into 100-word technical prompt recipes (camera specs, lighting, texture). Decouples intent (Gemini) from execution (Nano Banana 2). Enables 3-word request > full production-ready prompt without manual expansion.

6. **Operator Playbook Pattern:** Every framework ends in a 5-7 step standardized runbook (Define > Lock > Expand > Frame > Animate/Sequence). Operator playbooks, not model mastery, are the monetizable output. Reproducibility at scale is the service moat.

7. **AI Filmmaking: Controlled Synthesis, Not Generation:** "AI filmmaking is no longer about generation; it is about controlled synthesis and workflow orchestration." Multi-reference feeding (up to 14 per prompt) + start/end frame prompting in Kling 3.0 prevents temporal distortion in video. Morph-Transition Design (overlapping motion vectors) enables seamless scene cuts.

8. **Metaprompting at Scale:** LLMs (Claude, ChatGPT) used as prompt architects: they expand scene descriptions into high-specificity production prompts. Decouples prompt quality from manual labor. JSON-prompt standardization converts prompt engineering into system engineering.

**SNIPED Application:**

- CRS framework directly upgrades SNIPED Composite Environment Rotation. Current approach: one reference image per chapter. Upgraded approach: CRS-driven multi-angle anchor sheet per environment before any generation. Prevents subject drift across the 7-environment rotation.
- Constraint prompting for identity preservation: "Keep BJ's face and build identical, change environment to Brutalist Monument" scales to 70+ variations across 7 environments with identity locked.
- Gems as Creative Director Layer: build a Gem pre-configured with SNIPED's visual DNA (Meisel/Roversi lane, Adobe Neutral base, no teal-orange) to auto-expand vague shoot concepts into production-ready composite prompts. One-time setup, infinite sessions.
- Three-State Workflow (empty > full > morph) maps to SNIPED Chapter Card cadence: environment (B&W, document register) > hero in environment (full color, moment register) > reveal video. Documented in same workflow.
- Temporal aging and flashback skills (line 1512) directly unlock Direction Stack Chapter Card archive content and lineage documentation sequences.
- JSON prompt architecture enables batch carousel generation: Claude Project + Brand System doc + 30-image Nano Banana library = carousel engine. Current SNIPED carousel output is manual assembly. This replaces it with a repeatable pipeline.
- Friction point: Faceless AI influencer framing in segment 3 contradicts Lineage Doctrine. That section is not applicable. Filter: SNIPED's use of AI is world-construction and composite expansion, never identity replacement or persona fabrication.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine:**

- ADDS: CRS (Character Reference Sheet) as production infrastructure doctrine. Should be added to composite_environment_rotation_v1.md as a mandatory pre-generation step.
- ADDS: Metaprompting (LLM-as-prompt-architect) as a workflow upgrade. Not previously captured. Enables consistent visual DNA without manual prompt crafting per session.
- ADDS: JSON prompt standardization as system-engineering layer. Routes composite generation from ad-hoc to repeatable and auditable.
- ALIGNS: Hybrid-operator stance (AI for world-construction, not identity) is confirmed by constraint-prompting methodology. The Nano Banana workflow proves that identity IS preservable under generative expansion. Strengthens the Lineage Doctrine operational foundation.
- CONTRADICTS (partial): Faceless AI influencer monetization framing in segment 3 directly conflicts with Lineage Doctrine. Flag and exclude. Do not import that lane.
- PROMOTES: Connected toolchain default (feedback_connected_toolchain_default.md) is directly validated. Antigravity + Claude Code + Nano Banana + Kling chain replaces specialized hire overhead.

### EVALUATION GATES

1. **Pricing Proposal Gate (pre-send):** Was price stated orally before this document was prepared? Does the proposal show only three options (no more, no fewer)? Does the anchor option frame risk-transfer language explicitly? Are all prices round numbers ending in three zeros? Is scope bundled (no itemization)? Pass all five or revise before sending.

2. **Outreach A-Pile Gate (pre-send):** Personal salutation with founder's name? Time/date specificity in opener? No service-brochure language? Single specific next action with no ambiguity? Zero generic capability claims? Pass all five or rewrite.

3. **Composite Consistency Gate (pre-generation):** Is a CRS (multi-angle: front/side/3/4/back) generated and validated before any environment composite? Are constraint prompts structured as "Keep X identical, change Y"? Are camera specs (lens, film stock, lighting direction) explicit in prompt? Pass all three or generate CRS first.

4. **HALT Decision Gate (pre-commit):** Not hungry? Not angry? Not lonely? Not tired? Any fail = defer decision, recover state first, then return.

5. **Case Study Specificity Gate (pre-publish):** Does the case study name: specific founder/client, specific inflection context, specific deliverable, specific measurable outcome? No vague "helped a founder" language passes. All four elements present or revise.

6. **Offer Anchor Gate (pre-conversation):** Is the highest-tier offer the first number spoken in the pricing conversation? Is the anchor framed as risk absorption, not feature list? Any no = sequence is inverted; reset before continuing.

### DECISION UPGRADES

1. **Pricing sequence:** Default is now anchor-first, oral, then written. Not price-list-first. Not itemized-scope-first. Enns Rule 4 + Rule 3 sequencing becomes the operating standard for every new client pricing conversation.

2. **Discovery question:** Replace "what do you need" with Enns' Three-Year Question. "It's three years from today and you're really happy with the progress you've made. What's happened to make you so happy?" This is the wants-based discovery opener. Do not modify the framing.

3. **Prospect timing:** Before any VIB outreach, check the recency trigger (Halbert's hotline-buyer principle). Has this founder just raised, rebranded, scaled up, or shipped something significant? Timing into a window of inflection multiplies conversion. Not time-sensitive outreach = lower tier queue.

4. **Composite workflow:** No composite session starts without a validated CRS. The CRS is generated first, reviewed for identity lock, then environment composite proceeds. This is a production protocol, not a preference.

5. **State management:** On any "off" day (HALT diagnostic fires, time-sense test fails by 10+ min), work invisible. Solo output, no relationship exposure, no client-facing decisions. Re-emerge when state is restored. This is not optional; it is competitive infrastructure.

6. **Negotiation default:** When a client brings procurement late, recognize you have already won the substantive decision. Do not re-open pricing. Neuter procurement pre-emptively in verbal close conversation: "When they call asking for price cuts, I'll say no. Can I refer them to you if needed?"

7. **List/outreach priority order:** Own list (past clients, warm referrals, VIB warm touches) > targeted cold list (inflection-triggered, occupation + geography segmented) > broad cold (spray). Never invert this hierarchy. Marketing effort and personalization depth scale with list temperature.
