### BOOK 1: Rob Meyerson, "Brand Naming: The Complete Guide" (2021)

**Coverage:** 7/7 segments, full read confirmed.

**1-line thesis:** A name is a strategic asset with a longer lifespan than any other brand decision; rigorous process (brief, volume generation, screening, legal, launch) beats inspiration every time.

**Densest frameworks and lessons:**

1. **9-Factor Framework (3 domains):** Strategic (Meaningful, Adaptable, Distinctive), Creative (Memorable, Sounds Good, Looks Good), Technical (Legally Available, Linguistically Viable, Easy to Spell/Pronounce). Every SNIPED product name must clear all three domains, not just the creative one.

2. **Approach x Construct 2D Classification:** Approach axis = Descriptive to Suggestive to Abstract. Construct axis = Real-word / Compound / Coined. Coined words win on trademark clearance, domain availability, and distinctiveness. Descriptive names are rigid and pigeon-hole companies that evolve. SNIPED's naming should bias toward Suggestive + Coined.

3. **Sound Symbolism (Phonosemantics):** Hard consonants (k, t) + bright vowels = sharp/edgy. Soft consonants (m, l) + rounded vowels = smooth/curved. Tonality is constructible, not accidental. Kodak's k-sounds = power. Olay's soft sounds = smooth skin. Apply to every SNIPED product name.

4. **7-Step Professional Process:** Brief (approved by all decision-makers before generation) > Generate 100+ names > Shortlist > Trademark prescreen > Linguistic check > Present > Full legal search > Select. No shortcuts. Process beats Scrabble.

5. **Conceptual Spelunking (Cave Model):** Navigate concept-space via hypernym (up/parent), hyponym (down/child), allonym (across/sibling). Maps laterally to SNIPED's Direction Stack territory exploration.

6. **20 Name Generation Techniques:** Field trips, misdirection (pretend you're naming a fighter jet), bad-ideas exercises, off-brief exploration, sleep/subconscious, word combiners (Kombinator), etymology rabbit holes, other-language words. Quantity first (hundreds), cull ruthlessly after.

7. **Domain Subordination Rule:** Name quality beats domain perfection. Tesla launched as teslamotors.com. Dropbox launched as getdropbox.com. Solve the name for meaning and personality; domain strategy follows. AFTERLIGHT failed the .com gate; SEAMLIGHT passed. But Meyerson clarifies: the gate should be "no viable domain path exists," not "exact .com unavailable."

8. **Launch Simultaneity + Commitment Doctrine:** Name + logo + website + messaging release together. Staggered rollouts dilute impact. New names feel awkward for weeks; steadfast commitment is mandatory. Backtracking = brand failure signal.

**SNIPED application:**

- SEAMLIGHT, Direction Stack book title, KOTS institutional naming, and any future sub-brand all need a full brief (approved by BJ before generation) + 100+ candidates + screened-then-presented process, not a "here are three names, pick one" flow.
- Lineage Doctrine requires the linguistic check scope to include Black church / HBCU / Southern athletic registers, not just generic US English.
- The "coined words win" rule is already locked in OS memory (feedback_name_availability_gate.md). This book explains the mechanism: easier trademark clearance, domain acquisition, and distinctiveness in a crowded field.
- Presentation psychology: always brief stakeholders on the process and candidate count before showing names. Positive feedback first. Use mockups (business card, product context), not naked text.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:**

- **Strengthens** the name availability gate (coined > real-word is now mechanically justified, not just a heuristic).
- **Refines** the domain gate: the rule is "no viable domain path," not "must have exact .com." This is a minor loosening. Many permanent brands launched on alternative domains; exact .com obsession produces tortured names (Tezzly, Slydshr).
- **No contradictions** detected.

### BOOK 2: Robert Greene, "The Laws of Human Nature" (2019)

**Coverage:** 40/40 segments, full read confirmed.

**1-line thesis:** Human behavior follows predictable psychological patterns rooted in evolutionary wiring, childhood formation, and social dynamics; mastering these patterns in yourself and others is the foundation of power, creativity, and authentic authority.

**Densest frameworks and lessons:**

1. **18 Laws as a Decision Taxonomy:** Irrationality, Narcissism, Role-playing, Compulsive Behavior, Covetousness, Shortsightedness, Defensiveness, Self-sabotage, Repression, Envy, Grandiosity, Gender Rigidity, Aimlessness, Conformity, Fickleness, Aggression, Generational Myopia, Death Denial. Each is a predictable trap with a corresponding counter-strategy. The OS should treat these as a decision checklist, not just character study.

2. **Law of Narcissism + Empathy Stack:** Healthy narcissism (strong self + outward channel) vs. deep narcissism (relationship destruction). Four empathic skill components: Empathic Attitude (assume ignorance of others, reject snap judgments), Visceral Empathy (read mood via body language before content), Analytic Empathy (gather data on early years, values, authority responses), Feedback Loop (direct and indirect confirmation). The VIB method is operationally the Empathic Skill Stack applied to outreach.

3. **Nonverbal Communication as Primary Language:** 65%+ of human communication is nonverbal; people capture only 5%. The "second language" is physical, not intellectual. Key categories: dislike/like cues (microexpressions, feet direction, torso angle, genuine vs. fake smile via eye muscles), dominance/submission (space, eye contact, interruption, stride), deception (controlled zones vs. uncontrolled zone mismatches, emphasis timing, too-pat narratives). Baseline + deviation is the detection model.

4. **Law of Defensiveness + Universal Self-Opinions:** Every person defends three core beliefs: "I am autonomous," "I am intelligent in my own way," "I am basically good." Directly challenging any of them triggers total resistance. Confirming them dissolves it. Johnson's 11-year Senate playbook is the operational manual: observe obsessively, identify key allies, mirror their values, share intelligence as currency, confirm their self-opinion without appearing calculating.

5. **Shadow Integration (Jungian):** Everyone has repressed traits that leak under stress, in contradictory behavior, vehement denials, overidealization, and projection. Six Shadow release signs. Integration (laugh at flaws, show the dark side consciously) produces authenticity and power. Suppression produces volcanic eruption (Nixon). SNIPED's hybrid-operator AI stance and lineage-from-inside approach are Shadow integration moves, not compromises.

6. **Law of Covetousness / Desire Object Mechanics:** Absence beats presence. Transgression + mystery + scarcity = irresistible covetousness. Open-ended work (Shakespeare, Chekhov) invites interpretation; explicit instruction kills engagement. Chanel's subliminal perfume launch is the operational model. Social proof (others being seen desiring the object) activates competitive desire.

7. **Law of Grandiosity + Post-Success Reset:** After any win, analyze: elements of luck present, role of mentors/timing. Raise vigilance. Wipe slate clean per project. Pay less attention to applause as it grows louder. Don't believe bigger is better. Protect allies. Eisner fired Katzenberg (the person actually generating the profits) to soothe ego bruising, and created a funded competitor (DreamWorks) in the process.

8. **Law of Generational Myopia / Zeitgeist Detection:** Four-generation historical cycle (Revolutionary > Order > Pragmatist > Crisis), each ~22 years. Each generation absorbs decisive events ages 10-18 and forms collective spirit. Detect the zeitgeist by: reading what the young mock (old idols becoming ridiculous = spell broken), fashion/style shifts, socializing pattern changes. Ride the tide (Danton), don't fight it with old symbols (Louis XVI).

**SNIPED application:**

- VIB outreach architecture is now fully grounded: use Empathic Skill Stack (analytic + visceral), confirm the founder's self-opinion (autonomous, intelligent, good), share intelligence as currency before asking for anything, never challenge their self-image directly.
- Founder vetting gate: use character-over-credentials (Greene's pattern-over-incident rule), toxic type detection (8 archetypes listed in segment 9), and nonverbal baseline + deviation in discovery calls.
- Scene density thinking is Danton's model applied: embed in 3-4 LA cultural circles, feel the tide before articulating it, give voice to what founders feel but can't name. The anti-novelty lock is tide-riding, not arbitrary restraint.
- Shadow integration validates the hybrid AI stance: show the method (world-building AI + analog identity preservation), don't hide it. The shown method is the moat, not the hidden secret.
- Grandiosity guard: slot Greene's post-success reset protocol into the annual Constraint Audit. After Championship book, after named-client win, after any proof moment: externalize attribution, protect allies, do not overreach into new domains.
- Chanel's desire mechanics validate the B&W Card dual-register, selective visibility, and strategic absence in content cadence. Drop, then disappear. Don't over-explain. Captions should close the loop less, not more.
- Mortality awareness (O'Connor, segment 38-39) operationalizes the execution governor: scene density over audience growth, reps over novelty, craft depth over platform volume. Every production day is finite. Run the office.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:**

- **Strengthens** scene-density thinking: Greene's Danton case provides the mechanism (feel the tide in the cultural cluster, then amplify it back). Not just a strategic preference but a proven historical path.
- **Strengthens** the Shadow integration rationale for the hybrid AI stance. "Show the method" is not a vulnerability; it is the authority move.
- **Refines** outreach doctrine: the Rubin model (deference + ask juniors' input + listen with absorption + phrase as "just one opinion") is a superior LinkedIn comment pattern to anything previously documented. Integrate into linkedin_comment_doctrine_v1.md.
- **Introduces tension** with "Withdrawal/Absence" (Law of Covetousness) vs. "Scene Density" (show up regularly in tight community). Resolution: withdraw from mass media and broadcast channels; show up intensely in the tight cluster. Both rules hold simultaneously at different scales.
- **No contradictions** with existing locked doctrine.

### EVALUATION GATES

**Naming gates (additions to existing name availability gate):**
- Approach gate: Does the name fall into descriptive territory? If yes, flag rigidity risk and push toward suggestive or abstract.
- Tonality gate: Map phonemes (hard vs. soft consonants, vowel brightness) to intended brand personality. Mismatch = reject or revise.
- Turducken gate: Is the name trying to pack more than 2 meanings? If yes, reject. New names can say one or two things.
- Trendstamp gate: Does the name end in -ly, -ify, -ster, or rely on current naming convention? If yes, flag as timestamp risk.
- Linguistic disaster gate: Before presentation, route any coined/foreign-root name through native-speaker checks across at least 3 languages relevant to the audience.

**Character/relationship gates:**
- Toxic type detection gate: Before any partnership or onboarding, score the person against Greene's 8 archetypes. Any Drama Magnet, Big Talker, or Passive-Aggressive Charmer signal = structural clarity required before proceeding.
- Grandiosity gate: After any win, run the post-success attribution audit before committing to next expansion.
- Deception gate: When a narrative feels "too pat" or "too seamless," activate Columbo protocol (don't confront, encourage more talking, observe freeze-response to unexpected question).
