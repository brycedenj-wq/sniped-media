### Source (single source · Discourses on Livy · 12 chunks)

- **Discourses on Livy (Niccolo Machiavelli, Ninian Hill Thomson tr., Dover)** · pdf · 143,936 words · 12 (incl 1 synthesis):
  - **Power (3):** healthy conflict makes good laws (the Roman tumults · "wise laws in these very tumults"); a people is "wiser and more constant than a prince" (the broad base as guardian of liberty); how liberty is lost to ambition, inequality, and the idle gentry ("to live as gentlemen").
  - **Operator-process (1):** the right to accuse vs calumny ("the right to accuse" as a lawful release valve; "Calumny is as hurtful" when grievance has no accountable outlet).
  - **Operator-doctrine (2):** return to first beginnings ("back to their first beginnings" · periodic renewal before drift compounds); the synthesis (the institution-design operating pattern + the optionality guardrail).
  - **Leadership (1):** the founder "must stand alone" to establish order, then hand off to institutions (founding and sustaining demand opposite things).
  - **Strategy (2):** adapt to the times or fall ("he does not change with them"); conspiracies usually fail ("danger at three stages" · asymmetric downside of covert schemes).
  - **Culture (1):** shared belief as cohesion ("making use of religion to keep the minds" · instrumental, honest).
  - **Systems-thinking (1):** institutions decay without renewal ("in progress of time this excellence" corrupts · compounding entropy).
  - **Ethics (1):** ends-and-means realism read honestly ("excused rather than blamed" · analysis of how founding power behaves, explicitly NOT an endorsement of cruelty or manipulation).
