Strassler, ed.)** · pdf · 187,008 words · 4: command + calculated risk; clemency as strategy (clementia that made his position credible); control your own narrative (the Commentaries as self-account, named frankly as propaganda); the bond with his soldiers (loyalty through shared stake).
