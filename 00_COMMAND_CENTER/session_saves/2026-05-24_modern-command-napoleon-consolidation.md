### Source (single source · Napoleon: A Life · 14 chunks)

- **Napoleon: A Life (Andrew Roberts)** · epub · 385,251 words · 14 (incl 1 synthesis):
  - **Leadership (4):** make ordinary people feel capable of extraordinary deeds (the army followed him after Moscow); leading from the front (le petit caporal, shared risk); lead by recognition ("it is with such baubles that men are led", the Legion d'Honneur); energy and compartmentalized focus ("drawers in a cupboard").
  - **Operator-process (3):** command of detail ("one of the most unrelenting micromanagers in history"); logistics and supply discipline ("three minutes without gunpowder", an army marches on its feet); build durable institutions (the Code Napoleon · "My true glory ... is my Civil Code" · "Is this fair? Is this useful?").
  - **Strategy (1):** the operational art, concentrate force on the decisive point ("attacks must not be disseminated, but concentrated").
  - **Founder-psychology (1):** the self-made Corsican outsider / autodidact ("read constantly, especially history books") who reinvented himself and embodied careers-open-to-talent.
  - **Culture (1):** self-myth and narrative control, the bulletins (he "didn't consider himself to be on oath"), the St Helena memoir, echoing Caesar's Commentaries.
  - **Systems-thinking (1):** the Continental System as a self-defeating policy that boomeranged ("protectionism that brought him down", his own army in "uniforms made in Halifax and Leeds").
  - **Power (1):** the appetite that would not relinquish, the Cincinnatus pose vs Washington's actual relinquishment ("more glory than is necessary"), "insatiable hubris", Russia 1812 as the culminating point. Cautionary.
  - **Ethics (1):** the human cost, read honestly, the wars and the dead, the "unprecedentedly efficient surveillance system" and censorship, the 1802 reintroduction of slavery. Cautionary, not glorified.
  - **Synthesis (1 · 014 · operator-doctrine):** the modern-command operating pattern (energy + system + tempo + recognition + durable institutions) + the over-reach counter-lesson + the optionality guardrail.
