### Included sources (per-source Culture Code 2 / Leadership in Turbulent Times 1 / Team of Rivals 1 / Extreme Ownership 2 / Dichotomy 1 / Measure What Matters 2 / Radical Candor 2 / Turn the Ship Around! 2 / High Output Management 3)

- **The Culture Code (Daniel Coyle)** · epub · 63,281 words · 2: belonging cues and psychological safety drive team performance over talent; the leader builds trust by leading the vulnerability loop (going first, admitting fallibility).
- **Leadership in Turbulent Times (Doris Kearns Goodwin)** · epub · 195,517 words · 1: leadership is developed through adversity, not innate, and the mode must match the moment (transformational / crisis / turnaround / visionary).
- **Team of Rivals (Doris Kearns Goodwin)** · azw3 · 90,717 words · 1: assemble your strongest rivals rather than loyalists and lead them through emotional strengths (empathy, self-control, absorbing blame, sharing credit, magnanimity).
- **Extreme Ownership (Jocko Willink & Leif Babin)** · mobi · 84,758 words · 2: the leader owns everything (no one to blame); no bad teams only bad leaders; decentralized command and the Laws of Combat (Cover and Move, Simple, Prioritize and Execute, Decentralized Command).
- **The Dichotomy of Leadership (Jocko Willink & Leif Babin)** · epub · 96,028 words · 1: leadership is a balance between opposing forces (own yet empower, aggressive yet cautious, lead yet follow); most failure is one principle taken too far.
- **Measure What Matters (John Doerr)** · epub · 71,642 words · 2: OKRs pair objectives with measurable key results; focus and commit to the few priorities; stretch for amazing.
- **Radical Candor (Kim Scott)** · epub · 93,384 words · 2: care personally AND challenge directly; the failure modes are ruinous empathy (most common), obnoxious aggression, and manipulative insincerity.
- **Turn the Ship Around! (L. David Marquet)** · epub · 65,697 words · 2: leader-leader replaces leader-follower; move decision authority to where the information is and shift to intent-based language ("I intend to..."), built on competence and clarity.
- **High Output Management (Andrew S. Grove)** · pdf · 68,084 words · 2 + 1 synthesis (016): a manager's output is the output of the units they influence, so maximize leverage; make decisions via free discussion, then a clear decision, then full support.
- **Synthesis (016 · operator-doctrine):** the leadership/operator-management toolkit (felt safety, situational leadership, ownership + decentralization, OKRs + focus, care + challenge, authority-to-information, managerial leverage) + the optionality guardrail · attributed to High Output Management.
