### Included sources (per-source The Beautiful Fall 4 / The Chiffon Trenches 3 / Dior by Dior 3 + synthesis / The Little Dictionary of Fashion 2 · incl 1 synthesis)

- **The Beautiful Fall (Alicia Drake)** · mobi · 169,922 words · 4: the dense 1970s Paris fashion scene as a creative resource (designers do not create in a vacuum; the milieu of stimulation and fascination); the designer-as-star (from dressmaker to symbolic figure, puissant style arbiter, the myth and mystery of Paris couture); the Lagerfeld vs Saint Laurent rivalry and its two opposed temperaments (armored prolific discipline vs tormented genius); aesthetic authority as an implicit, self-enforcing standard (image as the governing idea).
- **The Chiffon Trenches (Andre Leon Talley)** · epub · 87,847 words · 3: taste built from outside through obsessive study (the segregated South, the library, a hefty diet of fashion glossies, taste as trainable not inherited); editorial status and access earned by apprenticeship and proximity to the gatekeepers (the unpaid, highly selective Vreeland apprenticeship, then becoming a gatekeeper himself); loyalty, dignity, and the human cost beneath the glamour (the chiffon trenches; loyalty as a noble human endeavor).
- **Dior by Dior (Christian Dior)** · pdf · 73,252 words · 3 + 1 synthesis (013): craft justifies the visible effect (an ethereal appearance is only achieved by elaborate workmanship; dresses constructed like buildings); reconciling personality and discipline and editing ruthlessly (overproduce toiles, then destroy most so the collection coheres); reading the cultural moment from conviction over the dictates of commerce (the New Look as a reaction to the ration-book era).
- **The Little Dictionary of Fashion (Christian Dior)** · epub · 18,733 words · 2: elegance is not money (simplicity, good taste and grooming; elegance as distinction, naturalness, care, and simplicity, with care the most important); quality over quantity and individuality over slavish trend (buy little but good; no elegant woman follows fashion slavishly).
- **Synthesis (013 · operator-doctrine):** the fashion/luxury culture and taste-systems operator toolkit (taste-as-trainable, scene-as-input, aesthetic-standard-as-discipline, craft-beneath-the-effect, overproduce-then-edit, quality-and-individuality) + the optionality guardrail · attributed to Dior by Dior.
