- **Synthesis (011 · operator-doctrine):** the expertise/creative-skill operator toolkit (trained perception, image literacy, creative receptivity, self-doubt management, deliberate practice, mental representations, the motivation multiplier) + the optionality guardrail · attributed to Peak.
