### Included sources (per-source Value-Based Fees 2 / Million Dollar Consulting 2 + synthesis / The McKinsey Way 2 / Managing the Professional Service Firm 2 / Getting Naked 2 / The Advantage 2 / Flawless Consulting 2)

- **Value-Based Fees (Alan Weiss)** · pdf · 81,605 words · 2: price the perceived value and outcome, not the time (time-billing anchors the buyer on cost and penalizes speed); reach conceptual agreement on objectives, measures, and value with the economic buyer before the fee or proposal.
- **Million Dollar Consulting (Alan Weiss)** · pdf · 143,350 words · 2 + 1 synthesis (015): build market gravity (a field of attraction that pulls prospects to you) rather than chasing; the work exists to improve the client's condition, and the durable asset is the trusting relationship.
- **The McKinsey Way (Ethan M. Rasiel)** · pdf · 42,414 words · 2: MECE (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive) problem decomposition; the hypothesis-driven, fact-based method; don't boil the ocean (80/20) and solve the right problem.
- **Managing the Professional Service Firm (David H. Maister)** · pdf · 126,173 words · 2: leverage, the finders/minders/grinders ratio, determines the economics; the real equity is the people's goodwill, skill, and reputation, balanced across service / people / financial goals.
- **Getting Naked (Patrick Lencioni)** · pdf · 47,421 words · 2: vulnerability builds client loyalty; shed the three fears (losing the business, being embarrassed, feeling inferior) so you serve honestly and put the client's interest first.
- **The Advantage (Patrick Lencioni)** · pdf · 58,648 words · 2: organizational health (minimal politics and confusion) beats raw smarts as the single greatest success factor; the four disciplines (cohesive team, create clarity, overcommunicate, reinforce) and great meetings.
- **Flawless Consulting (Peter Block)** · epub · 107,028 words · 2: contract explicitly and aim for the collaborative (50/50) role rather than expert or pair-of-hands; be authentic (name what you experience) and read resistance as a signal of an underlying concern.
- **Synthesis (015 · operator-doctrine):** the consulting/service operator toolkit (value-pricing, MECE diagnosis, leverage economics, vulnerability-based trust, clarity, authentic delivery) + the optionality guardrail · attributed to Million Dollar Consulting.
