- **The Inevitable (Kevin Kelly)** · epub · 108,050 words · 4 + 1 synthesis (016): the 12 technological forces as trajectories not products; flowing (free copies push value to the un-copyable generatives: immediacy, authenticity, embodiment, patronage); accessing and sharing (access beats ownership, cooperation compounds); filtering (in abundance, attention is the scarce resource).

- **The Long Tail (Chris Anderson)** · epub · 75,305 words · 4: "selling less of more" (aggregated niches rival the head); the three forces (democratize production, democratize distribution, connect supply and demand); the tyranny of the hit ends, filters become the new tastemakers; "make everything available" + "help me find it."
- **Free: The Future of a Radical Price (Chris Anderson)** · pdf · 74,656 words · 3: the economics of abundance ("marginal cost" toward zero) and "cross-subsidies" (the kinds of free); free buys "attention and reputation" (the gift economy); "free is not" a universal answer, "waste is good" only with a paid layer (conditional).
- **The Great Online Game (Packy McCormick)** · pdf · 4,343 words · 1: "The Great Online Game" / "Win the Internet" · building in public compounds reputation and status into real-world opportunity (a short capstone essay, not padded).
