### Option 2 locked and executed (per-source Truby 5 / Campbell 4 / Snyder 3 / Block 3 · incl 1 synthesis)

- **The Anatomy of Story (John Truby)** · pdf · 126,225 words · 4 + 1 synthesis (015): the story as an organic body with a single designing principle (story process + original execution); desire (the surface goal the audience tracks) vs need / weakness (the hidden change that makes payoff possible); the opponent defined structurally as a competitor for the same goal; theme as a moral vision argued through the character web, not stated.
- **The Hero with a Thousand Faces (Joseph Campbell)** · epub · 142,097 words · 4: the monomyth (a hero ventures forth; departure-initiation-return; returns to bestow boons); the call to adventure and the refusal of the call; the road of trials and the ultimate boon (earned through ordeal); the return / master of the two worlds / freedom to live (the hardest stage). Held strictly as cultural and narrative pattern study, NOT faith or spirituality.
- **Save the Cat! (Blake Snyder)** · pdf · 59,787 words · 3: the logline / one-line (irony, a vivid mental picture, a killer title, "what is it?"); the ten primal genres and "the same thing, only different"; the fifteen-beat sheet and the save-the-cat likability beat (earn rooting interest early).
- **The Visual Story (Bruce Block)** · pdf · 61,118 words · 3: the seven basic visual components (space, line, shape, tone, color, movement, rhythm); contrast and affinity as the key to visual intensity (contrast raises, affinity lowers); the screen is never empty, so plan visual progressions that escalate with the story's build. Translated into practical visual-structure patterns for BJ's visual/operator work.
- **Synthesis (015 · operator-doctrine):** the operator toolkit (structural coherence around a designing principle + a recognizable arc + premise clarity inside a familiar category + deliberate visual intensity matched to the build) + the optionality guardrail · attributed to The Anatomy of Story.
