### Sources (per-source attribution · Herodotus 5 / Thucydides 6 / Arrian 4 / Engels 3)

- **The Landmark Herodotus: The Histories (Herodotus · Robert B. Strassler, ed.)** · epub · 437,842 words · 4 + 1 synthesis (018): custom is king of all (read a scene's norms from inside · nomos); call no man fortunate until he is dead (the impermanence of fortune); the ambiguous oracle (Croesus heard "destroy a great empire" as he wished · hearing what you want to hear); free men outfight subjects (ownership as a force multiplier).
- **The Landmark Thucydides: The Peloponnesian War (Thucydides · Robert B. Strassler, ed.)** · epub · 352,171 words · 5 + 1 synthesis (017): the real cause (the growth of Athens and the alarm it inspired in Sparta made war inevitable · the Thucydides Trap); the Melian dialogue (the strong do what they can · power realism read honestly, with the slaughter of Melos as its moral cost); Pericles' bounded strategy (attempt no new conquests · the discipline of not over-reaching); the Sicilian expedition (catastrophic over-extension far from base); stasis at Corcyra (conflict inverts values · words change their ordinary meaning).
- **The Campaigns of Alexander (Arrian)** · azw3 · 132,066 words · 4: rapidity was all in all (speed and tempo as a weapon); leading from the front (the bond earned by visibly sharing risk); cutting the Gordian knot (refuse the inherited framing); hubris and the failure of self-mastery (insatiable appetite for fame · the Hyphasis limit · "self-restraint is a noble thing"), read as cautionary.
- **Alexander the Great and the Logistics of the Macedonian Army (Donald W. Engels)** · pdf · 75,419 words · 3: supply is the basis of strategy (logistics underwrites everything); the transport eats its own load (the carrying-capacity limit on reach); provisions arranged in advance (intelligence and preparation before the march).
- **Synthesis (017, 018):** the ancient-history operating pattern (read the scene, see power clearly, hold discipline against over-reach, build the supply base first · strategy) + the pattern-library discipline and the optionality guardrail (operator-doctrine).
