> The 18 startup docs (from the Essential Docs carousel, source: Ramon Lyons social post , third-party, [PROV]) turned into operating infrastructure, NOT a pile of forms. The OS does NOT make you build all 18 now. It STAGES them by the certified `payment-follows-proof` + `company-of-one` doctrine: legal/entity structure follows proof, never blocks it. [CERTIFIED doctrine] · every legal item is **[LEGAL-REVIEW-NEEDED]** , these are template STUBS and decision gates, NOT legal advice and NOT final.

## The staging rule (why most are "later")
- **NOW** = anything the active proof loop actually requires (the form collecting emails) or that protects owned IP cheaply.
- **STAGED** = built only when proof/revenue/team/funding justify it. [CERTIFIED: payment-follows-proof; company-of-one , stay solo until forced].
- Nothing here crowns a lane or forms an entity prematurely.
