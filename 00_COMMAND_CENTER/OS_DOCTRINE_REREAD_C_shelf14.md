### BOOK 1: Alex Hormozi, *$100M Leads* (2023)

**Coverage line:** 10/10 segments, full read.

**1-line thesis:** Every business problem is a lead-volume problem; engaged leads (not contacts) are the only real advertising output, and the Core Four channels plus four lead-getter types compound into an exponential machine when run at sufficient volume for a sufficient duration.

**Densest frameworks/lessons:**

1. **Engaged Lead Definition.** An engaged lead is someone who shows interest, not someone whose contact data you hold. All advertising outputs (traffic, clicks, followers) are false proxies; the only true output is an engaged lead.

2. **Core Four Channels.** Warm outreach (1:1, warm), content posting (1:many, warm), cold outreach (1:1, cold), paid ads (1:many, cold). Every business uses some combination; mastery of all four compounds faster than any single channel.

3. **Lead Magnet Architecture (7-step build).** Narrow problem + next-problem reveal + multi-format delivery + headline testing + value-bomb (give secrets, sell implementation) + clear CTA with urgency/scarcity/reason anchors. CAC reduction logic: lead magnet lowers friction, filters quality, produces 3x cheaper customers.

4. **Hook/Retain/Reward Content Unit.** Every piece of content must hook (get attention via labels, yes-questions, conflict, unusual), retain (lists, steps, stories, unresolved tension), and reward (satisfy the headline promise completely). Applies to short-form once; long-form chains multiple units.

5. **Value Equation (4 levers).** Dream outcome / perceived likelihood / time delay / effort and sacrifice. Optimize all four in every offer and all four in every ad. Show value through multiple stakeholder perspectives (self, spouse, boss, rivals) and past-present-future timelines to multiply perceived value.

6. **Give:Ask Ratio + "Give Until They Ask" Doctrine.** Minimum 3:1 give-to-ask; growing platforms need much higher. Hormozi's finding: the moment you start asking for money is the moment you decide to slow down growth. The best customers come to you asking how to pay.

7. **LTGP:CAC Ratio + Client-Financed Acquisition.** Target 3:1 LTGP to CAC minimum. Design offers so acquisition cost is paid back within the 30-day credit-card float window. Recycle cash immediately into next customer. This eliminates external funding dependency.

8. **$100M Roadmap (7 Levels) + More-Better-New Sequencing.** L1 warm outreach, L2 max personal capacity, L3 hire to scale, L4 25%+ referral rate, L5 multi-platform, L6 autonomous executive team. Scale via More (Rule of 100), then Better (constraint testing), then New (new platform/method). Never jump to New before exhausting More and Better.

9. **Referral Goodwill Equation + 7 Ask Mechanics.** Goodwill = Value minus Price. Referrals start with product excellence; asking amplifies. Seven ask tactics: one-sided, two-sided, at point of purchase, as negotiation chip, referral events, ongoing programs, unlockable bonuses.

10. **Affiliate Architecture (6-step + Whisper/Tease/Shout launch sequence).** Map ecosystem, create affiliate offer, qualify via certification investment (10-20% of annual earnings), tiered payouts (25/50/100% CAC at tiers 1/2/3), launch via frequency escalation, integrate via bundling/white-label/standalone. Compounding: affiliates getting you affiliates eliminates paid ads entirely at scale.

**SNIPED application:**

- Direction Stack book is the lead magnet (narrow problem: scene authority for LA founders; next problem it reveals: sustained proof and distribution).
- VIB DM outreach is warm outreach, operationalized via ACA (Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask). Run at Rule-of-100 volume before claiming channel failure.
- Give:Ask ratio protects SNIPED's growth curve. Cultural Docs, Chapter Cards, essays, and case studies are the give layer. Premature monetization signals slow down trust compounding.
- LTGP:CAC logic justifies premium pricing ($1,500 Reset floor) and frames the print/workshop/licensing upsell stack as the breakeven mechanism.
- Affiliate lane (L4-L5 horizon): LA photographer peers and collaborators become revenue-sharing referral partners via tiered payout and methodology certification. SNIPED's composite environment rotation v1 is the certification product.
- Hormozi's "give secrets, sell implementation" is SNIPED's entire positioning architecture: Direction Stack teaches the method openly; BJ's judgment, relationships, and execution are the paid offer.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:**

- REINFORCES: Repetition over Novelty (Hormozi posted 2x/week for 4 years before Top 100), scene-density over audience breadth (puddles-to-oceans = scene-density thinking), payment follows proof (Goodwill first, then ask).
- UPGRADES: Referral handling protocol now has a formal 7-ask tactical menu and a tiered payout architecture, not just a floor price and scope flex. Add the "ask at point of purchase" and "referral as negotiation chip" tactics to the Pearl-network doctrine.
- WEAKENS: No specific doctrine contradicted. The pricing-velocity ladder (free, then progressive discounts) is the one structural divergence from SNIPED's premium-floor positioning - but Hormozi's own caveat (premium brands skip discounting; the velocity principle is the pattern, not the price) resolves the tension.
- NEW rule candidate: "Open To Goal" replaces time-boxing on volume activities. Not "I DMed 10 founders today" but "I DM until I hit X engaged responses." Outcome commitment, not activity commitment.

### BOOK 2: Ethan Rasiel, *The McKinsey Way* (1999)

**Coverage line:** 7/7 segments, full read including index and author bio.

**1-line thesis:** Premium consulting at scale rests on three interdependent disciplines: fact-based, MECE-structured, hypothesis-driven problem-solving; ruthless scope and communication discipline; and integrity-as-brand-moat, all executed through small, high-caliber teams that prioritize client ownership of solutions over consultant credit.

**Densest frameworks/lessons:**

1. **MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive).** All problem framing, categorization, and deliverable design must be MECE. Maximum 3-5 top-line categories. Overlap and gaps in structure are thinking failures, not formatting failures.

2. **Initial Hypothesis + Issue Tree.** Enter every engagement with a testable hypothesis. Branch it downward into 2-3 levels of sub-issues. Define the analyses needed to prove or disprove each branch. Adjust hypothesis as facts arrive; never force facts into a predetermined answer.

3. **"The Problem Is Not Always the Problem."** Clients mislabel their pain. Diagnose the real problem before solving the stated one. Example: division needs "expansion" may actually need closure. This is the diagnostic discipline that separates strategic advice from order-taking.

4. **80/20 Rule operationalized.** Identify the 20% of inputs driving 80% of results. Sort every data set (sales, cost, performance) for clumps. Concentrate resources on the 20%. Anti-perfectionism for the 80%.

5. **Elevator Test.** Compress 6 months of work into a 30-second explanation: issues, top 3 recommendations, payoffs, skip data. If you can't pass the elevator test, you don't understand your work well enough to sell it.

6. **Prewiring as political strategy.** No surprises in the formal presentation. Every stakeholder (especially opponents) is briefed privately beforehand. The formal meeting is performance, not discovery. Removes shock, allows private negotiation, ensures the room is controlled before you enter.

7. **Marketing over selling.** McKinsey waits for the phone to ring. The firm made being known a systems problem: published books, a free quarterly journal, partner expertise positioning, board seats, alumni activation, follow-up with past clients. No cold calls, no ads. The positioning did the selling.

8. **3Ds Training Protocol (Document-Demonstrate-Duplicate).** Write down every step. Walk through the checklist. Watch them do it following the checklist. Adjust the checklist when they slow or stop. Praise direction-following, not results. One piece of feedback per session. This is replication-grade training.

9. **Client team triage.** Two liability types: useless (incompetent/unwilling) and hostile (protecting a faction's interests). Useless: isolate to noncritical work. Hostile: quarantine sensitive information, identify their patron's agenda, weaponize it in the final sale.

10. **Implementation is half.** Recommendations are worthless without execution. Require a detailed implementation plan (what, when, by whom) at the lowest possible detail level, with specific responsibility owners and requirements so clear a fool understands them. Post-delivery engagement determines long-term reputation.

**SNIPED application:**

- MECE applies directly to Direction Stack architecture (5 lanes must not overlap), Composite Environment Rotation (7 non-overlapping environments), and Card system dual-register (B&W vs color is a MECE separation by design).
- IH + Issue Tree is the consulting frame for every new founder brief. Enter with a testable hypothesis about their scene-density problem, not a blank page or generic proposal.
- Prewiring translates to pre-briefing key stakeholders (dad in KOTS, lead founder in VIB cohort, institutional gatekeeper) before formal direction reveals. Removes shock, converts presentation into confirmation.
- "Marketing over selling" validates SNIPED's entire positioning architecture. Direction Stack, Cultural Docs, LinkedIn POV essays, and Chapter Cards are the McKinsey Quarterly. The phone should ring because the work is known, not because BJ cold-pitched.
- 3Ds protocol is how to build the first retoucher or production assistant hire. Not "here's the vibe," but documented checklist, demonstrated execution, duplicated in front of you. Adjust checklist at every failure point.
- Elevator test applies to SNIPED's positioning phrase bank. Every founder conversation, every DM, every caption should compress to a 30-second elevator test pass.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:**

- REINFORCES: Build internal-tight before market (solution-implementation fit), proof over packaging (facts bridge credibility gap), hospitality layer (implementation as the second half of work), Company of One (high-caliber small teams beat large budgets).
- UPGRADES: Client triage (useless vs. hostile) is a new operational distinction not currently in the OS. Add this to casting call doctrine and institutional deal-handling. Hostile gatekeepers require different tactics than incompetent ones.
- ADDS: "Don't accept 'I have no idea'" as an OS rule. When any collaborator, dad, or client says they have no idea, treat it as a challenge, not a stop. Chisel with pointed questions until an educated model emerges.
- NEW rule candidate: "Coordinator force multiplier" - one high-trust ops coordinator with clear instructions, initiative room, and evaluation advocacy unlocks 36 hours of operator time per week. The coordinator manual does not yet exist in SNIPED_OS and is a gap.
- WEAKENS nothing in current doctrine. McKinsey's client-dependent frame (they solve for billable external engagements) is the one structural difference; SNIPED is founder-operator building the firm, not consultant. Adapt accordingly: "client" = founder, institutional partner, cultural collaborator.

### BOOK 3: *Claude Cowork Genius* (video transcript compilation)

**Coverage line:** 10/10 segments, full read.

**1-line thesis:** Claude Cowork is a shift from AI-as-generator to AI-as-employee, enabled by a persistent context architecture (Context/Projects/Output folders, global instructions, skills, connectors, plugins, and scheduled tasks) that compounds capability across sessions, eliminating the re-explanation problem and converting recurring workflows into autonomous background operations.

**Densest frameworks/lessons:**

1. **Three-Mode Hierarchy.** Chat (stateless brainstorm), Cowork (local computer access, file operations, agentic execution), Code (multi-agent scripting, remote routines, persistent even when computer is off). Mastery flows left to right. Cowork is the on-ramp; Code is the destination.

2. **Persistent Context Architecture (5-layer stack).** Global instructions > project-level instructions > session-level instructions > skill definition > task prompt. Each layer narrows scope. Fewer repetitions, less context loss, more consistent outputs. The three foundational files: about_me.md, brand_voice.md, working_preferences.md.

3. **Task Repetition Maturity Ladder.** One-off request, folder-based multi-session work, project with soft and hard memory, skill-wrapped workflow, scheduled skill (automated daily/weekly). Maturity grows left to right; each step reduces operator cognitive load.

4. **Skills as Repeatable Playbooks.** A skill is a markdown file that documents repeatable steps. Auto-triggered when prompt matches description field. Skills can invoke other skills (composable). Seven-step complex workflows are the threshold for wrapping as a skill. Test-refine cycle before production handoff is mandatory.

5. **Plugin = Opinionated Container.** Bundles skills + connectors into one installable package triggered via slash command. Build only if work repeats weekly or more, involves multi-step complexity, and is client-facing or brand-consistency-sensitive. Customizing official plugins (5 minutes) beats building from scratch.

6. **Connector-First over Browser Automation.** Pre-built connectors (Gmail, Drive, Notion, Airtable, Slack) are faster and safer than browser extension access. Browser automation grants full account access and is a risk surface. Granular permission controls (read-only, block delete) are the safe default.

7. **Sub-Agents for Bulk Parallel Work.** Sub-agents allow N simultaneous tasks instead of sequential. Sweet spot: 100-200 item batches. Context window stays clean (summaries only, not full history). Higher token cost; Max plan needed. Beyond 200 items, use Zapier/Make.com instead.

8. **Remote Routines (Code-level).** Routines run on Anthropic servers, not local machine. Survive computer sleep/off. GitHub and API triggers supported. This is the true 24/7 autopilot layer. Local scheduled tasks require computer awake; remote routines do not.

9. **Memory Duality.** Hard memory: manually managed markdown files (learnings.md, memory.md) at project level. Soft memory: Claude's automatic 24-hour sweep capturing contextual nuance. Combined: no loss of conversation nuance plus persistent institutional knowledge.

10. **Whisper/Tease/Shout Launch Sequence (from Hormozi overlap).** Pre-launch frequency escalation for content and affiliate activation. Frequency schedules are themselves automatable via scheduled tasks. This segment operationalizes the launch mechanic from Book 1 into a toolchain workflow.

**SNIPED application:**

- SNIPED_OS folder architecture (00_BRIEF, 05_PRODUCTION, 03_OUTREACH) already mirrors Context/Projects/Output. The missing piece is encoding the doctrine files (THE_SPINE, CANONICAL_TRUTHS, Composite Environment Rotation, Casting Doctrine, LinkedIn Comment Doctrine) as project-level instructions in a single Claude Project, so every session auto-loads full operating context without pasting MEMORY.md.
- Skill candidates (see Capability Harvest below) convert existing SNIPED SOPs into auto-triggered playbooks.
- Sub-agent workflows for bulk work: 50+ VIB DM drafts in one session, 30+ image portfolio reviews for HERO vs Card assignment, scene-density audit across 90 days of output.
- Remote routines for 24/7 SNIPED operations: weekly LinkedIn comment engine, Monday briefing on cast opportunities, Friday delivery deadline alerts, scene-density metric tracking.
- Plugin blueprint: SNIPED_OUTBOUND_SALES_PROCESS (lead enrichment + VIB qualification + DM drafting + follow-up sequencing) containerized and triggered via /sniped-outbound command. Shareable via GitHub link for future team or affiliate training.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:**

- REINFORCES: Connected toolchain default (connector-first over browser automation is the exact priority order), execution governor (tool-first routing), repetition over novelty (skills remove the re-explanation problem and make repetition default behavior), full-OS synthesis (hard+soft memory across sessions = corpus-level continuity).
- UPGRADES: Session-start protocol should now include a carryover prompt that snapshots state, findings, and next steps at session end, then loads that snapshot at the next session start. This makes the existing CURRENT_STATE + ACTIVE_THREADS + SESSION_LOG protocol tool-native rather than manual.
- ADDS: Eval-driven skill quality loop. Every skill update must be tested against a rubric (output quality, tone match, format compliance). If performance declines, revert. This is a new discipline not yet in any existing SNIPED SOP.
- NEW rule candidate: Plugin-wrapping threshold test. Before encoding any workflow as a skill or plugin, ask: does it repeat weekly or more? Does it have multi-step complexity? Is it client-facing or brand-consistency-sensitive? If 2 of 3, build the plugin. If fewer, stay with manual or one-off prompt.

### EVALUATION GATES

1. **Engaged Lead Gate (Hormozi):** Before any content or outreach ships, ask: does this produce an engaged lead (someone showing interest) or just a contact/impression? If the latter, redesign the CTA or hook.

2. **MECE Gate (McKinsey):** Before finalizing any framework, system, or offer structure (Direction Stack lanes, composite environments, tier definitions), verify: are categories mutually exclusive? Are they collectively exhaustive? Overlap or gap = thinking failure, not formatting issue.

3. **Elevator Test Gate (McKinsey):** Before any client presentation, investor briefing, or institutional pitch, compress to 30-second pass. If it fails, the work isn't understood well enough to sell.

4. **Hook/Retain/Reward Gate (Hormozi):** Every Chapter Card, LinkedIn post, and Cultural Doc excerpt must pass all three components. Fail on any = revise before scheduling.

5. **Give:Ask Ratio Gate (Hormozi):** Before any monetization ask (DM conversion, offer reveal, paid product push), audit the preceding content ratio. Minimum 3:1 give-to-ask in any 30-day window.

6. **Skill Plugin-Worthiness Gate (Cowork):** Does this workflow repeat weekly or more? Does it involve multi-step complexity? Is it client-facing or brand-consistency-sensitive? If 2 of 3, build the skill/plugin. If fewer, stay manual.

7. **Eval-Driven Skill Regression Gate (Cowork):** Every skill update must be tested against a rubric before production deployment. If quality score drops vs. prior version, revert. Never ship a degraded skill.

8. **Prewiring Gate (McKinsey):** Before any formal presentation to a multi-stakeholder audience (institutional partner, founding cohort, brand client), verify every key stakeholder (especially potential opponents) has been briefed privately. If not, delay the formal presentation.

9. **"Real Problem" Diagnostic Gate (McKinsey):** Before designing any solution, ask: is the stated problem actually the problem? Run one round of IH + Issue Tree decomposition to verify. Do not solve the labeled pain without at least one diagnostic pass.

10. **Goodwill-Before-Ask Gate (Hormozi Referral).** Before triggering any referral ask, confirm goodwill is high: did the last deliverable exceed expectations? Is value minus price positive in the client's felt experience? If not, build goodwill first; asking before goodwill amplifies nothing.

### DECISION UPGRADES

- **Volume commitment over time-boxing:** Replace "I spent X hours on outreach today" with "I hit X engaged responses today." Outcome commitment (Open To Goal) outperforms activity tracking when volume is the constraint.
- **Puddle-first platform sequencing:** Don't enter a new platform until the current one is maxed. Scene-density thinking applied to distribution: depth per cluster before breadth across platforms. LA founder LinkedIn before IG before YouTube before paid ads.
- **Affiliate readiness check before adding platforms:** The affiliate lane (L4 roadmap) requires 25%+ referral rate first. Don't pursue affiliate architecture until referral rate proves product quality. Current phase: L1-L2.
- **Agency-as-school not agency-as-service:** When SNIPED needs a new platform skill (YouTube SEO, TikTok distribution, paid ad optimization), hire the elite agency explicitly to teach the skill in 6 months, then build internal capability. Never hire an agency indefinitely without a skill-transfer clause.
- **Useless vs. hostile triage before any institutional negotiation:** Before entering any multi-stakeholder deal (KOTS school committee, brand partnership, institutional client), identify which blockers are useless (incompetent, manageable) vs. hostile (protecting a faction, require political upstream work). Different tactics; do not apply the same approach.
- **Connector-first over manual-first for any recurring cross-app task:** The moment a task crosses two or more platforms (Gmail to Notion, Drive to Airtable, Instantly to CRM), route through connector/MCP before considering manual. Manual is the fallback after toolchain audit returns no path.
- **Certification investment as affiliate filter:** Any affiliate, referral partner, or methodology licensee must invest (time or money) proportional to expected earnings. Low-cost onboarding produces low-commitment affiliates. The filter is the feature.
