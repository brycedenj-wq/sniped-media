## DOCTRINE ENTRIES (deduped)

**DE-01: Promotion/Evidence Bifurcation**
Canonical OS engines (cold email, discovery call, close flow, LinkedIn warming, follow-up cadence, messaging angles, VIB production SOP) are PROMOTED. Sent DMs, old prospect lists, and past campaigns are EVIDENCE that the SOP works. Evidence proves the rule; the rule is promoted. Evidence is never treated as the SOP itself.

**DE-02: VIB as Conditional Capability (Not Default)**
VIB (Visual Identity Bridge / frame-comparison asset) is a high-warrant, conditional production capability. Decision gate: does this prospect warrant a custom video/frame asset? If no, ship cold email. VIB-as-default is neutralized. The SOP is promoted; specific sent VIBs are evidence.

**DE-03: 3-Email Diagnostic-Reframe-Giveaway Sequence**
Cold outreach structure: Email 1 (diagnostic offer) + Email 2, Day 3 (surface reframe, company-name personalization inserted here only, names 3 concrete deployment surfaces) + Email 3, Day 7 (give-away exit, low-friction, no pressure, Direction Stack diagnostic). Post-Email 3: 90-day suppression, CRM tag "warm-but-no-reply," trigger-event re-engage via LinkedIn DM.

**DE-04: 90-Day Re-Engage Protocol (Trigger-Gated)**
Dormant prospects move to "Re-engage 90d" in Notion Pipeline DB. Reactivation sends ONLY on fresh triggers (new role, funding, publication, keynote, company milestone). No blind check-ins. Cold VIB non-converts get a new comparison frame, not a generic note. If no trigger fires, prospect sits another 90 days.

**DE-05: Co-Founder Pair Pattern**
Both co-founders from the same company may be messaged within 48 hours using different deployment contexts to avoid appearing coordinated. Paired prospect pattern allows a single shoot to serve a dual booking pipeline if both convert. Not yet formally codified; validated by evidence.

**DE-06: Referral Ask Protocol**
Trigger: 30-45 days post-delivery, after testimonial received AND photos deployed AND social signal visible. Ask for two named founders by criteria (visual presence below operational level). Draft intro language for referrer (zero-friction). Do not follow up on declined or silent referral asks. Referrals enter Notion at "Engaged" stage, not "Target."

**DE-07: Direction Stack Dual Production Job**
Direction Stack operates as: (1) on-set real-time subject diagnosis + live correction, and (2) per-prospect audit deliverable for outreach. Two distinct revenue surfaces: shoot-day directing + audit/consultation. The diagnostic system and the book launch (Batch 7 money item) are separate scopes.

**DE-08: Photoshop Reject Gates as Binary Doctrine**
Reject gates are not guidelines or taste calls. Five global locked anti-patterns apply across ALL techniques before technique-specific gates: plastic skin / identity drift / over-processed register / cheap AI tells / anti-Avedon polish. Execute technique correctly, THEN apply reject gates. Reject gates disqualify output even when technique execution is correct.

**DE-09: Proof vs. Portfolio Reframe**
Portfolio = art showcase. Proof = business-problem-solving system. Proof deployment is hierarchical: website first, then outreach, then content. Proof feeds the money engine (case studies become sales justification). Inventory is sorted around what SOLVES problems, not what looks best.

**DE-10: Delivery Email Lifecycle as 9-Step State Machine**
Not ad-hoc follow-up. Fixed sequence: pre-shoot brief (24h out), booking confirmation (Stripe post-deposit), Day 0 delivery, Day 7 testimonial ask, Day 10 gentle-ask fallback, Day 19 window-close reminder (conditional: opened but not upgraded), Day 30 Op Kit pitch, Day 90 re-engagement, referral ask (Day 30-45). Variants per product (Reset, Sprint, Op Kit, Brand System).

**DE-11: Product-Specific Upgrade Window Timing**
Reset: 14-day upgrade window, send Day 19 reminder. Op Kit: 7-day window, send Day 5 reminder. Sprint: no upgrade window, skip reminder entirely. Upgrade: $60 (Proof to Hero), $30 (Proof to Select), with 5-pack discounts.

**DE-12: No-Show / Late Reschedule Policy**
First reschedule: free within 14-day window. Second reschedule: $150 charge. Third: deposit forfeited. Late-arrival lane (15-30 min): hold studio, prompt action. No-show lane (60+ min): deposit protection, binary offer (reschedule or studio credit with 12-month window). Notion dual-write: Pipeline notes + Shoots status.

**DE-13: Operator Direction Protocols (Locked Suite)**
10 protocols. Locked Shoulders (Protocol 02): drop + 15-20 degree angle, registration problem not posture. Soft Jawline (Protocol 05): "tongue to roof of mouth, teeth lightly touching," jaw engagement is the structural foundation of facial definition, upstream of smile/no-smile. Protocol 09: Good Mechanics, Zero Presence. Protocol 10: Full Psychological Shutdown. Scripts are crew-facing, not client-facing.

**DE-14: Demographic Gate in VIB Outreach**
SOP Step 3 mandate: skip target rather than send a mismatched reference pool photo. Demographic alignment (age band, register, studio context) is non-negotiable before deploying VIB. Strongest photo rule: never send an incomplete or mismatched asset.

**DE-15: Two-Lane Grading Architecture**
Each shoot carries named color-graded presets: Lane A (editorial) and Lane B (product). Saved once per brand, applied via batch preset during pipeline. Color-label tagging at cull time determines which lane preset applies. No override at grade time. Three manual human gates lock progression.

**DE-16: Figma MCP Tool Spine for VIB**
Official Figma MCP (mcp__plugin_figma_figma__*) is the validated tool for VIB design automation. VIB master file architecture: single master file + per-prospect duplication. Do not over-engineer with multi-page workflows or component variant generation in Phase 1.

**DE-17: Content Engine Multiplier (Stateless)**
1 shoot produces 5+ deployable outputs (LinkedIn + IG) via reusable, date-independent engines. Calendar retirement: 30-day calendars are replaced by stateless engines. Hook library is a palette, not a mandate. Caption depth is craft-dependent. LinkedIn POV angle selection requires founder intent per VIB targeting.

**DE-18: AI Identity Boundary (Hard Constraint)**
AI for world-construction (plates, environments, creative engine): YES. AI for subject identity (face, body, hair): NO. No credit spend without per-batch approval. Applies across all 8 AI/motion engines in the OS.

**DE-19: Gate-8 Composite QA Framework**
6-axis scoring: lighting, grounding, edge, color marriage, artifact scan, brand fit. Each axis scored 0-10. Gate-8 pass threshold: all axes >= 8 + no reject. Grounding doctrine: heels flush to surface, cast shadow from silhouette base (not detached blob), contact shadow plate-matched. Secondary environments (WEST/BEACH) only rebuild after flagship passes.

**DE-20: Threads Hard No**
Threads photographer culture is hobby-leaning, engagement-bait heavy, mobile-first. No premium fashion photography distribution stack exists on Threads in 2026. SNIPED platform routing: LinkedIn + Instagram only. Threads is locked Hard NO, consistent with TikTok refusal.

**DE-21: Proof-First Referral Trigger**
Send referral ask only after: testimonial received AND photos deployed AND social signal is visible. The ask is never sent from a vacuum. Warm intros skip the cold VIB step entirely; they enter at a different stage.

**DE-22: Conditional Day-19 Upgrade Send**
Send Day-19 window-close email ONLY if client opened the gallery AND has not yet purchased an upgrade. Reference two specific proofs by deployment use case (not generic gallery links). Pixieset built-in reminder is primary; this email is personalization override layer.

## MASTER-DOCTRINE ADDITIONS

**MDA-1: Promotion/Evidence Bifurcation is Structural**
The OS formally separates canonical reusable engines from proof-point examples (sent DMs, old campaigns). This is structural, not tonal. It is the architecture that enables delegation and repeatability. Old framing ("here is the DM that worked") treated evidence as the SOP. New frame: the rule is promoted; the example proves the rule works.

**MDA-2: Reject Gates as Binary OS Mechanic**
"Reject gates" is a new explicit OS mechanic, distinct from "guidelines" or "taste." Observable failure modes become binary gates. Five global anti-patterns are the floor; technique-specific gates populate above them as tutorial consumption cycles run. Every photoshop ceiling technique inherits the same gate template.

**MDA-3: Proof Loop as Leverage Architecture**
Proof inventory, proof sorting, proof deployment, portfolio strategy reframe, and case-study structure form a 5-step loop that closes the output-to-money cycle. This operationalizes the empire leverage thesis: work becomes leverage via proof systems, not just via doing the work.

**MDA-4: VIB Diagnostic Naming is Visible to Prospect**
Protocol failure naming (Protocol 07, Protocol 09, Protocol 10) is embedded in cold copy sent to prospects, not just held for internal review. This makes the diagnostic system a positioning signal, not a backstage tool. Candidate for expansion into POSITIONING_PHRASES doc.

**MDA-5: Delivery Email Lifecycle is a State Machine**
9-template delivery lifecycle is a state machine, not a collection of standalone templates. Each template is a state with a trigger condition, send logic, and Notion state mutation. Conditional send logic (Day 19: opened but not upgraded) and product-variant timing (Reset/Sprint/Op Kit) are embedded.

**MDA-6: Perennial Seller Patience is a System Gate**
90-day suppression + trigger-event re-engage is not just patience; it is an operational system gate. Most cold prospects convert in 6 months, not 7 days. The "warm-but-no-reply" CRM tag and trigger-event re-engage are the structural implementation of this.
