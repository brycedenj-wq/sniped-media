## DOCTRINE ENTRIES (deduped)

**Copy Architecture**
- Headline formula hierarchy for premium positioning: Proof-Focused + Differentiation-Focused over generic Outcome. Proof-first signals selectivity; outcome-first signals commodity.
- Page structure default for SNIPED properties: Varied Engaging (narrative arc + objection handling). Compact for ad-driven funnels. Enterprise for KOTS institutional positioning.
- Problem-section leads with empathy + recognition, not feature list. Aligns with hit mechanics (exposure + story before broadcast).
- Seven-sweep editing discipline (Clarity, Voice/Tone, So What, Prove It, Specificity, Heightened Emotion, Zero Risk). Sweeps are sequential; each sweep returns to verify no new issues introduced.
- "So What" test: every assertion must answer "why should I care?" Features without consequences are dead weight.
- Proof is non-negotiable. Vague claims ("industry-leading," "trusted by thousands") without evidence erode trust. Specific numbers ("2,847 teams," "save 4 hours weekly") outperform round numbers and vague superlatives.
- One primary CTA per page. Button copy communicates value, not action ("Start Free Trial" over "Sign Up"). CTA repeated at decision points, visible above fold.
- Risk reversal near CTAs (money-back, free trial, cancel anytime, social proof placement, privacy assurance) reduces barrier.
- Awareness staging (Schwartz: Unaware, Problem Aware, Solution Aware, Product Aware, Most Aware): headline posture and opening strategy must match the awareness level of the specific traffic source.
- Rule of One: one reader, one idea, one offer, one action per communication. Competing CTAs collapse conversion.
- Big Idea criteria: easy to understand, easy to believe, counterintuitive or interesting.
- Voice of Customer proof stacking ranked by intensity and frequency beats generic claims.
- Plain English wins on trust, accessibility, and conversion. Substitution hierarchy: delete, shorten, simplify.
- Filler phrases ("at the end of the day," "obviously," "the fact of the matter is") delete without loss.
- AI-tell transitions to avoid: "That being said," "It's worth noting that," "At its core," "In today's digital landscape," "This begs the question," "Let's delve into," "In conclusion" as opener, "Moreover" and "Furthermore" when repeated.
- Tone varies by audience. Over-signposting erodes trust. Front-loading key info in transitions aids skimming.
- Human voice is a competitive signal. Rejected transitions (AI tells) are friction; natural ones are invisible.

**CRO and Conversion Architecture**
- Test prioritization sequence: revenue influence first, then conversion rate, then engagement.
- Phase sequencing: Phase 1 = brand-positioning messaging tests; Phase 2 = form friction; Phase 3 = pricing psychology.
- Message-match (ad-to-page consistency), single-focus design (nav removal), and repetition are landing page fundamentals.
- Pricing pages require multiple format explorations (annual vs monthly, tier count, plan presentation) plus objection handling.
- Luxury editorial restraint constrains visual-format tests: illustration/video/GIF testing must preserve restraint, never overstimulation.

**Web Design and UX**
- Pre-design question gate (WHO/WHAT/WHY/TONE/CONSTRAINTS/DIFFERENTIATION) before any visual work begins.
- Typography is primary differentiator. Never Inter/Roboto for marketing. Type choice is identity.
- Anti-generic rules: purple gradients, stock hero photos, centered-everything, gray-on-white+blue buttons = template clones. If a site could be a template, it needs more design.
- Whitespace is premium signal. Less content, more breathing room = higher perceived value.
- Motion carries semantic load: every animation must answer "what information does this convey?" Decorative motion is waste.
- Color is not information alone. Red fields must have icon + message. Dark mode uses dark gray, not pure black.
- Spacing system uses 4px or 8px base unit consistently.
- 320px minimum responsive constraint. No horizontal scroll at any breakpoint.
- Typography readability floor: 1.5-1.8 line-height, 16px minimum body, 65ch max line width, semantic heading hierarchy.
- Touch targets: 44x44px minimum. Animations: 150-300ms.
- Pre-delivery verification grid is mandatory before shipping any web surface (11 sections, 141 checkpoints including dark mode toggle, WCAG 4.5:1 contrast, CLS < 0.1, single h1, Open Graph, canonical URL, real mobile testing).
- Strongest design is not most feature-full. Verification catches over-animation, under-tested forms, missed accessibility.
- Empty states over blank slate: guidance + next action prevents bounce.
- Form validation timing: inline as-you-type, not only on submit.

**Compositing and Platform Mastering**
- Prime directive: subject's graded TIFF/PNG (color, skin, contrast, identity) is LOCKED. All work is environment integration only.
- 8-gate QA protocol (G1 biological reality, G2 relight to plate, G3 two-shadow grounding, G4 surface interaction, G5 transparent/reflective objects, G6 kill cutout tell, G7 sensor match, G8 artifact rejection).
- Client-ready threshold: every axis at least 8/10 on scorecard + zero Gate-8 rejects. No shortcuts.
- Required proof per hero: final no-text hero, final text/drop-card, 100% hair/edge crop, 100% feet/shadow crop, before/after, QA scorecard.
- Platform mastering is re-composition per surface, never flat resize. Subject grade locked; only output/export/aspect changes.
- 5 platform crops: story 9:16, feed 4:5, square 1:1, deck 16:9, proof studio frame. Each has specific safe-area rules.
- Skin-drift numeric check: interior body RGB alpha-eroded 25px, source vs composite, mean RGB + delta. Uniform delta on R/G/B (brightness only) = PASS. Uneven/hue/temp shift = FAIL.
- Color leads conversion when built on brand identity. B&W is editorial/prestige sidecar, not default.
- A pretty AI plate is not a concept. Brand fit fails if the environment competes with subject or is generic plate with no drop logic.
- Concept discipline rule: environment must serve subject, not compete with it.
- After any phase changing a hero or status: update SESSION_STATE.md + git commit with one-line milestone message.

**Strategic Frameworks**
- Counter-Positioning: a challenger's business model the incumbent finds attractive standalone but cannot adopt due to collateral damage (Milk = NPV-negative, History's Slave = cognitive bias/sunk-cost, Job Security = internal politics). Capitulation Point: timeline when incumbent finally adopts despite damage. Price low early to make incumbent underestimate profitability and delay response.
- Create-Destroy stress-test: generate strategy via Kernel (Diagnosis + Guiding Policy + Coherent Action), then destroy it systematically, rebuild from genuinely different diagnosis, repeat 2-3 rounds minimum. Minimum deliverables: progression log V1 through final, vulnerability register per version, surviving strategy with robustness rationale, risk watch-list.
- Consultative selling (Diagnostic-first): position as "Doctor of Selling," not pitcher. Four Ps problem mapping (Person, Pain, Promise, Price). Five Barriers to Purchase proactively addressed (cost ROI, social proof, fit proof, cost-of-inaction, process friction). Damaging admission raises credibility. Ascension Model: low-ticket entry, mid-ticket deepening, high-ticket capture.
- Professional discipline (Turning Pro): highest-resistance work is most important work. Amateurs identify with outcomes; professionals identify with process and show up daily regardless of mood or inspiration. Technique is foundational; creativity flows through mastered craft. Final-phase intensification is expected.
- Boardroom methodology: seats canon thinkers mapped to corpus intel docs, requires mandatory objection from every advisor, surfaces real disagreement as the signal, tests recommendation against north-star thesis verbatim. Unanimous agreement signals a poorly seated board. Board recommends; operator commits.

9. **Pre-delivery web checklist is an institutional gate, not a recommendation.** Every page ships against the 141-point verification grid.

10. **Composite QA threshold:** Every axis 8/10 minimum + zero Gate-8 rejects. "Internal-only" status for anything below. Nothing shipped client-ready without proof crops.

11. **Platform mastering is re-composition, never flat resize.** One export resized across platforms fails the quality gate by default.

12. **Color leads conversion; B&W is prestige sidecar.** B&W default only if brand concept explicitly specifies it. Test sharpening + contrast before considering B&W fallback.

## CONTRADICTIONS FLAGGED

1. **CRO testing vs. quiet luxury restraint:** The CRO experiments doc recommends video/GIF/illustration format testing on hero sections as standard practice. SNIPED's visual direction (Meisel/Roversi/Mert and Marcus lane, restraint over volume) constrains which tests are permissible. These are not irreconcilable but require an explicit filter layer: CRO test hypotheses must pass the quiet luxury restraint gate before execution. No current SNIPED doc establishes this filter explicitly. Gap.

2. **"Maximum-by-default" rule vs. "Company of One" constraint:** Batch 017 adds Consultative Selling (multi-step discovery + close), Create-Destroy (minimum 2-3 cycles), Boardroom (multi-advisor seating), and brand-validation-machine (12-step full validation) as standard operating methodologies. Each is labor-intensive. The Company of One doctrine (right-size, not scale; resilience over scale) and operating constraint rule (design for limited hours, leverage-first, never assume full-time founder availability) create friction when multiple intensive methodologies are activated simultaneously. No current doc establishes a sequencing rule for when full methodology applies vs. when leverage-abbreviated versions are permissible.

3. **Proof-first copy gate vs. pre-proof phase:** Seven-sweep Sweep 4 (Prove It) requires social proof, testimonials, case studies, guarantees, and review scores as non-negotiable copy elements. SNIPED is in a pre-proof phase where some of these assets do not yet exist at the volume the sweep implies. Applying the sweep literally would stall copy production. The sweep must be run with honest gap-surfacing (state the proof that exists, flag what is pending) rather than blocking shipment.

4. **creative-resistance-turning-pro skill "When to Use" section:** Lines 25-26 contain corrupted/mismatched guidance ("When building AI-powered workflows, automations, or systems that need to operate reliably at scale") unrelated to the skill's actual purpose (creative resistance and professional discipline).
