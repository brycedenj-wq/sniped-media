### Photography Methodology (Art Series Masters)

**Richard Avedon:** Subtraction as aesthetic law. Methodology over moment (portable studio, fixed crop, fixed distance). Duration as tool: shoot until subject runs out of self, not until you have something good. Body as graphic shape first. Anti-flattering as ethical position. Duration is the highest-leverage borrow for SNIPED's narrative-weight gap. Frame: "after the thing" timing vs. directing into pose.

**Robert Frank:** Six-point refusal method (hide faces, compose graphically, restrain to near-binary B&W, available light only, narrative from absence, body as social type). The Sequencing Principle: "The Americans" works as a book, not 83 singles. Order + repetition + adjacency accumulate meaning. Peripheral framing and anonymity as punctuation. Three-tier face policy: face-sculpted on commercial / face-incidental 1 frame per 12 on personal / face-hidden on one annual found-footage project. B&W is occasional, not default (burns color discipline).

**Ernst Haas:** Color as primary structure (found red wall, waited for red shirt). Motion blur as compositional language (1/15 shutter on bullfighters). Abstraction from real surfaces. Lyrical not deadpan. Fragmentation over completeness. Scoring: Composition 9 / Color 8-10 / Uniqueness 9; low on Pose (abstraction) and Depth (surface-first). Three borrowable moves: (1) saturated-field-with-one-punctuation; (2) found-painting eye applied to backdrops (stop treating backdrop as inert); (3) slow-shutter frame on personal work only.

**Fred Herzog:** Pre-framed color-block geometry. Shot on Kodachrome in shadowless late-afternoon light. Humans as scale/punctuation, not subject. Constructed frames before occupants entered. Shadowless warmth. Scoring: Composition 10 / Color 10 / Uniqueness 10. Three borrows: (1) pre-frame geometry before subject enters; (2) doomed-mundane vernacular material (old signs, weathered type) as cultural anchor; (3) ambient warm light as primary, no strobe. One-line: build frame first, ambient light, vernacular cultural anchor. Solves depth + emotional weight + uniqueness simultaneously.

**Graciela Iturbide:** Kinship through duration (9 months+ immersion). Scoring: 9.1 signature/narrative/composition/pose. Four traits: subject collision (animal + human, traditional + modern), narrative through duration, geometric anchors, pose binary (full-face or full-back-turned, refuses three-quarter). B&W strips color to force eye toward shape/ritual. Grain and print density signal object-weight.

Applied to SNIPED: Duration principle confirms Avedon diagnosis. Subject collision as styling upgrade (one disruptive element in locked frame without animals). Full-back-turned as personal project series. Extended-shoot protocol: all shoots add 15-20 min unstructured buffer after "final shot."

**Joel Meyerowitz:** Light as protagonist (not tool). Depth via atmospheric layering (3-5 planes separated by haze). Restrained found color (arrives without palette lock). Patience across visits (same location, different hours/days). Held level composition. No post fingerprint. Scoring: depth 8.4, color 8.8, lighting 8.6, pose 6.0 (traded intentionally). Fixes named SNIPED weaknesses: depth (atmospheric plane insertion), narrative (patience across visits), color (found-color pre-lock walk). "Atmosphere as visible subject" refines visual doctrine from light-based structure to light-atmosphere-space structure.

**Stephen Shore:** Democratic observation + large-format rigor. Edge-to-edge depth from view-camera physics. Locked-tripod method (frame first, subject second). Found color via Kodachrome restraint. Five pillars: composition, signature, depth, edit/grading, color at 9-10. Depth via spatial layering (foreground/midground/background all sharp, all doing work). Key borrow: walk back, add foreground, add background, compose with three planes. Lock studio frame on tripod before subject enters.

**William Eggleston:** Color as content, not decoration ("The Red Ceiling isn't a photo of a room; it's a photo of red"). Found over designed. Dye-transfer as method integral to aesthetic. Architectural depth without rim light (three-plane through foreground intrusion, layered planes). Off-center snapshot composition with formal rigor. Borrowable: architectural depth at locations (doorways, hallways, layered windows), found-color recognition rehearsal on personal work, one-frame decisiveness as calibration tool. Non-borrows: subject matter, print density, incidental light mode for client work, off-center asymmetric on commercial work.

**Annie Leibovitz (via Art Series skill):** Concept-first portraiture (idea exists before camera). Signature via premise (famous body + absurd/mythological idea). Narrative from art-historical quotation. Body integrated with environment. Edit finish: saturated, expensive, magazine-gloss. Trade-off: depth sacrificed for integrated composition (5.9 avg). Scoring: 8.8 narrative, 8.6 signature, 8.5 pose, 8.3 composition. Borrowable: concept-first reflex before locking palette, art-historical reference as directing tool. Non-borrows: painterly warm grade (contradicts quiet luxury editorial register), absurd premise without A-list access, full-production crew scale.

**Cross-Study Synthesis (load-bearing):** Avedon, Frank, Iturbide, and Meyerowitz all independently surface narrative weight as SNIPED's recurring audit weakness, approaching from different angles (duration / refusal / subject collision / atmospheric patience). The dual-path solution: concept-first + duration-collision runs simultaneously on same shoot. Both Leibovitz and Iturbide accepted depth trade-off (5.9 and 6.9 avg) to achieve narrative. Depth is not a signature for either; narrative is. This validates SNIPED's stated depth gap as secondary to the narrative problem.

**5. Slow-shutter Haas borrow: personal only vs production integration**

The Haas distillation explicitly states "slow-shutter frame on personal work" and "don't deliver to client." This is clean. But the framing also says it addresses "narrative-weight flatness."
