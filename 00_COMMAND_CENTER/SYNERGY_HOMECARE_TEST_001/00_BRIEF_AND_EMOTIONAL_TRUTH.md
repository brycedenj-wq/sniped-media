# Synergy HomeCare of Cape Fear · Brief Read + Emotional Truth

**Client:** Synergy HomeCare of Cape Fear (Wilmington, NC). National brand, locally owned and run.
**Commissioner:** FMO Media. This is a paid production-partner vetting test ("AI Production Vetting").
**Source brief:** `~/Downloads/SYNERGY_CREATIVE_TEST_DIRECTIVE_Distribution.pdf` (verified on disk, 2026-06-07).
**What they grade:** quality · what you do with creative freedom · turnaround speed. Deliberately under-briefed on purpose.

---

## What they literally asked for
- One short test piece, 30 to 60 seconds. Go to whatever length is cleanest.
- **Vertical 9:16 is the priority** (that is where this client lives) plus a **YouTube-ready cut**.
- Two audiences. Serve both in one piece, or make two short pieces. My call.
  1. Seniors and their families: what it feels like to be cared for here.
  2. Caregivers and recruits: what it feels like to work here.
- Concept is ours. No script, no storyboard handed over.
- AI is welcome and expected (avatars, voice, cinematic b-roll, music you own). They care about the result, not the method.
- Do not sweat brand compliance or logo placement. Use the name and the feeling. Move them.
- Speed is part of the signal. Send the first instinct fast.
- Deliverable back: just the piece.
- Spec is paid: if they use it, they pay before it goes live.

## What the brief actually wants (the read under the words)
They are not buying a healthcare ad. They are auditing a storyteller. The phrase that matters most: "something that makes a room go quiet." The whole test is whether we can find real feeling in an under-briefed brief and execute it fast. Over-production loses. Heart wins. They said it plainly: "heart is what's missing in this industry."

## The emotional truth (per side)

**Families.** The buyer is almost always the adult child, the daughter or son who is "scared, stretched thin, and lying awake at 2 a.m. wondering if they're failing their mom." The decision carries guilt ("I should be doing this myself"), fear, and relief at once. The target feeling: **the moment the weight lifts.** The door opens, the bag is set down, someone competent and warm is now in the home. You can exhale. The brief's own pass/fail test: *if a viewer feels "you're not failing, you're carrying a lot," you've hit it.*

**Caregivers.** This is hard, low-status, underpaid work almost everywhere. Caregiver supply is the single biggest constraint on the company's growth, so recruiting is an audience, not an afterthought. The target feeling: **this is where the work means something. Not a shift. A calling that's finally respected.**

## The one insight that unlocks both sides
The two emotional truths are **the same moment seen from two sides.** The caregiver walking through the door is the exact instant the weight lifts for the family. The hand held that lets the daughter finally let go is the same hand that makes the caregiver's work a calling. One doorway. One song remembered. One hand. Two meanings. That hinge lets a single film serve both audiences honestly, and lets the same asset bank be recut into a family cut and a caregiver cut for "more places to put the story to work" (a stated client want).

## Register and guardrails (from THE HOW)
- Real, unfiltered, human. Small moments beat big production: a hand held, eye contact, a memory-care client recognizing a song, a discharge-day homecoming, an overnight-to-morning kitchen handoff.
- **Avoid stock-footage gloss and "corporate healthcare" sheen.** It reads as fake here and they tune it out. This is the single biggest failure mode for this category and the easiest one for AI to fall into.
- Local texture (optional but it is the difference between generic and theirs): coastal North Carolina, Cape Fear region, river and beach, small-city warmth, veteran and church community.
- AI used to increase emotional truth, not spectacle. No AI flex.

## Format classification (per Commercial Craft Benchmark V2)
This is an **emotional brand film** with a **social-teaser hook discipline** on the vertical cut. Closest profile: luxury manifesto (slow, motivated holds, score-led, space and silence, payoff = one held hero with contrast >= 2.2x) but with the hook landing inside the first 3 seconds because it lives on a feed. ASL target 3.5 to 6.5s, the emotional hero beats (the song recognition, the exhale, the hand) are the longest holds. Speed is never the goal here; motivation and payoff are.
