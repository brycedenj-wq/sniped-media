# Synergy HomeCare · Three Concept Options

All three obey the same guardrails: real over glossy, small moments over production, the 2 a.m. adult child as the family emotional center, caregiver dignity as the recruiting center, coastal NC texture. Each is scored against the brief's own pass test and the STORY_GATE.

---

## Concept A · "The Door" (one film, both audiences, shared-moment hinge) · RECOMMENDED

**Logline.** A daughter has carried her mother alone since 2 a.m. for a long time. One morning a door opens, and the same warmth that lets her finally exhale is the caregiver's reason for doing the work.

**Spine (but / therefore).** 2 a.m., a daughter awake and afraid she is failing her mom. Therefore she has been carrying it alone (pill organizer, missed calls, the drive across town, asleep at the kitchen table). But one morning the door opens and a caregiver steps in and sets the bag down. Therefore the house changes: coffee, an old song on the radio, the mother's face remembering every word, a hand held. Cut to the daughter, somewhere in her own life, and for the first time her shoulders drop. She can breathe. Button: "You're not failing. You're carrying a lot. Let us carry it with you."

**Why it serves both.** The caregiver's face when the mother recognizes the song is the "this work means something" beat. The single hand held is both the daughter letting go and the caregiver's calling. One asset bank recuts into a family cut and a caregiver cut.

**Risk.** Trying to say too much. Mitigated by letting silence and one VO line carry, not narrating both sides.

---

## Concept B · "2 a.m." (family-first, single emotional spine)

**Logline.** A portrait of the worry that lives at 2 a.m., resolved by the morning the help arrives.

**Spine.** Hold long on the daughter awake, the ceiling, the phone, intercut with her mother alone across town. Morning: the door, the bag, coffee, the song, mom smiling. The daughter, at work or at her own kid's game, finally present because she knows. Lands hard on "you're not failing."

**Why it could win.** Purest emotional center, most likely to make a room go quiet, simplest to shoot fast.
**Why not.** Underserves the caregiver recruiting audience, which the brief flags as the single biggest growth constraint. Leaves half the assignment on the table.

---

## Concept C · "The Calling" (caregiver-first, recruiting spine)

**Logline.** Everywhere else it was a shift. Here it is the reason you started.

**Spine.** Follow one caregiver through a real day: the overnight-to-morning kitchen handoff, learning how Eleanor likes her coffee, the song she remembers, the hand held, the small dignities. Reveal: this is not a shift, it is a calling that is finally respected. Recruiting CTA.

**Why it could win.** Directly attacks the growth bottleneck (caregiver supply). Distinct from every family-side senior-care ad in the market.
**Why not.** Underserves the family buyer, who is the revenue audience. As a single test piece it reads as a recruiting spot, not a brand film.

---

## Decision

**Winner: Concept A · "The Door."** Reasons:

1. **It answers the whole assignment, not half.** The brief offers one piece for both audiences and explicitly names caregiver recruiting as a real audience. A is the only option that serves both honestly because the two emotional truths are literally the same moment from two sides. B and C each leave an audience behind.
2. **It hits the brief's own pass test directly.** The button is built from the line they handed us ("you're not failing, you're carrying a lot"). The exhale beat is the literal "moment the weight lifts" they described.
3. **It uses every named small moment** (door opening, bag set down, song recognition, hand held, kitchen) as structure, not decoration. That is the register they said wins.
4. **It gives them more mileage** (a stated want): one asset bank produces the master film plus a family cut and a caregiver cut, which proves production-partner thinking, not just a one-off.
5. **It is fast.** AI-generated, shared asset bank, one grade, one music bed. First instinct, shipped fast, which is part of what they are watching.

The single test deliverable is the unified master film (one 9:16 plus one YouTube cut). The family cut and caregiver cut are specced as the bonus that shows the operating system, not required for the first send.
