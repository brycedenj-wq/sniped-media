# Gemini Review Bundle · "The Door" v1 · Synergy HomeCare of Cape Fear

## The brief (what the client wants)
FMO Media is vetting a production partner with one test piece for Synergy HomeCare of Cape Fear, a senior in-home care company in Wilmington NC. Two audiences: (1) FAMILIES, specifically the adult child who is "scared, stretched thin, lying awake at 2 a.m. wondering if they're failing their mom"; the target feeling is the moment the weight lifts, "you can exhale," and the explicit pass test is: if a viewer feels "you're not failing, you're carrying a lot," it worked. (2) CAREGIVERS/recruits; target feeling "this is where the work means something. Not a shift. A calling that's finally respected." Register demanded: real, unfiltered, human, small moments over big production. The brief explicitly warns: AVOID stock-footage gloss and "corporate healthcare" sheen, it reads as fake and the audience tunes out. Local texture welcome (coastal NC, Cape Fear). AI is welcome. 30-60s, vertical 9:16 priority + a YouTube cut.

## The concept
One film serves BOTH audiences via a shared-moment hinge: the family's relief and the caregiver's calling are the same doorway, the same remembered song, the same hand held. Button line: "You're not failing. You're carrying a lot. Let us carry it with you."

## Production method (for your judgment of believability)
All people are AI-generated (Higgsfield nano_banana_pro stills, identity-locked across shots, then Seedance 2.0 image-to-video at 720p, upscaled to 1080). Warm documentary grade, film grain, vignette applied uniformly in post. Eleanor's recognition (22.8-28.8) and some inserts are slow pushes on stills.

## Known limitations (already disclosed, do not waste the review re-finding these)
1. MUSIC is a scratch bed: sequenced from owned ElevenLabs piano/string SFX swells, not a composed score (the Music API was unavailable). This is known-weak.
2. 720p clips upscaled to 1080; the separate 16:9 YouTube cut is a centered-with-blurred-fill conform, not natively framed wide.
3. Eleanor's recognition + the exhale are partly slow pushes on stills (a video content filter false-flagged those motion generations).
