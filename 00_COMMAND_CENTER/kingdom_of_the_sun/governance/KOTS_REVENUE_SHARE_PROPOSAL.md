## The proposal (one page, for the committee)

**Request:** a defined hosting partnership that returns a portion of Kingdom of the Sun revenue to the Vanguard basketball program, separate from the Kingdom operating account.

**Why it is reasonable:**

1. **This is a Vanguard / Marion County event.** The Kingdom carries the school's name and runs in the school's house. An event of this scale is an institution, and the host institution sharing in it is normal, not exceptional.
2. **Vanguard provides the venue and the operation.** The gym, the facilities, the setup and staffing, and the home environment that makes the tournament what it is.
3. **The host team is a primary draw.** Vanguard on opening night brings the biggest local crowd of the event. That attendance benefits the whole tournament and its sponsors.
4. **The host program currently shares in none of it.** As an example of the gap: concessions alone reportedly cleared in the range of $8,000 last year, and the basketball program received nothing from it. A program that anchors an event generating that kind of revenue should not also have to fundraise from scratch for its own uniforms and travel.
5. **Hosting fees and revenue splits are standard.** Across high school and travel tournaments, the host venue and host program are customarily compensated. It is unusual for the host program to be the one party that does not share in the event it anchors.
6. **The money stays in the family.** Funds returned to the Vanguard program reinvest in the students who represent the Kingdom's name, uniforms, travel, facilities, development, which raises the quality and profile of the host team, which raises the tournament.

**Two simple structures (pick one):**

- **Option A · Flat hosting fee.** A fixed annual amount paid from Kingdom revenue to the Vanguard basketball program for providing and operating the venue.
- **Option B · Revenue share.** A defined percentage of net Kingdom revenue (gate, concessions, sponsorship, or a named subset) directed to the program each year.

*Illustrative scale, for the committee to set: on a tournament that grosses around $25,000, a hosting return of roughly $5,000 to the Vanguard program (about 20%) would be a reasonable floor, with the figure reviewed as the event grows.*

**What it is not:** this is not a claim on the tournament's independence or its mission. The Kingdom remains its own entity. This is a standard hosting arrangement that keeps the host program healthy and invested for the long run.

**Suggested next step:** the committee sets the structure and figure for the 2026 cycle, reviewed annually.
