# Kingdom Media Production · The Number

> Built 2026-06-01. Internal, for BJ to set the figure he brings to the committee. These are planning estimates, real numbers depend on the trip, sales volume, and sponsorship. BJ sets the final asks.

---

## The strategy in one line

**Ask the committee for a modest production fee. Earn the real money from families and sponsors.** Most of your income does not come out of the committee's pocket, which is exactly why your ask fits right in next to everyone else getting paid.

---

## 1. Break-even first (what being there costs you)

You're flying in from LA over the holidays. Cover this before anything counts as "paid":

| Item | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Round-trip flight (LAX → Orlando, holiday) | ~$550 |
| Hotel, 6 nights @ ~$150 | ~$900 |
| Rental car, ~6 days | ~$400 |
| Food / incidentals | ~$400 |
| **Trip cost (break-even)** | **~$2,250** |

Your production fee has to clear this first.

## 2. The committee ask (the number to bring)

This is the part the committee votes on. Keep it clean and defensible.

| | Amount | Framing |
|---|---|---|
| **Production fee** | **Open at $5,000, settle ~$4,000-4,500** | "Covers my travel from Los Angeles plus four days running the complete media production: photography, video, the website, and all content." |
| **Builder retainer** (website, annual) | **$1,500 / yr** | "Keeps the official site live and updated year-round." |
| **Committee-funded total** | **~$6,000 / yr** | The only part that touches the tournament budget |

Why this lands: it's clearly more than a local day-rate photographer because you fly in and run *everything* (photo + video + site + archive), but it's a single line the committee can approve without it competing with anyone's stipend. Open at $5,000 so there's room to settle around $4,000-4,500.

---

## 3. Your full take (the real picture, Year 1)

The fee is the floor. Here's the stack, and most of it never hits the committee budget:

| Line | Who pays | Conservative | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Production fee | Committee | $4,000 | $5,000 |
| Builder retainer | Committee | $1,500 | $2,500 |
| Photo sales (families/players) | Families | $1,500 | $3,500 |
| Video / highlight reels (recruiting tape) | Families | $2,000 | $5,000 |
| Championship recap + prints | Families/fans | $500 | $1,500 |
| Sponsor media add-ons | Sponsors | $500 | $2,000 |
| **Year 1 total** | | **~$10,000** | **~$19,500** |

Roughly **$5,500-7,500 from the committee, the rest ($4,500-12,000) earned directly** from families and sponsors, money that does not exist today and competes with no one.

The math behind the consumer lines: 16 teams, ~190 players. If even a third of families buy a photo package and you sell 15-25 highlight reels, those two lines alone clear $3,500-8,500. That's the upside that grows every year as the field gets bigger, which you're also building.

---

## 4. Why it fits the committee

- Your committee ask (~$6k) is one modest line, not a budget-buster.
- The bulk of your income comes from **new money** (family photo/video sales, sponsor media) that the tournament has never captured.
- You're not taking from anyone's stipend, you're adding a revenue and quality layer that didn't exist.
- You fly in, run the whole production, and leave behind a website + archive that lifts the event every year. That's worth more than the ask.

---

## 5. The one-line ask to bring

> "For the media, I'm asking $4,500 to cover my travel from LA and run the full production, photo, video, the website, and content, plus a $1,500 retainer to keep the site live year-round. Everything beyond that I earn directly from photo and video sales to families, so it's not on the tournament's budget."

## Notes
- Adjust the $4,500 up or down to land just at or above what the tournament director makes, given you're doing far more.
- If the committee budget is tight, hold the fee at $3,500 (still clears the trip + a bit) and lean harder on the consumer lines, your real upside is there anyway.
