**Your role:** you're the one who can get these coaches on the phone and extend the invites. Tell me who you're going after and I'll track every one from invite to confirmed.

## 2. Revenue Share Proposal
**What it is:** the ask for the **Vanguard basketball program to get a cut of Kingdom revenue** for hosting. The money stays separate from the Kingdom account.

**Your role:** review it with me, then get it to the **principal so she can fight for it with the committee** on our behalf. I just need your okay and her name to drop in.

---

## 3. Booster Club Framework
**What it is:** the basics for standing up the booster club, roles, how the money works, and the hard rule that **booster money and Kingdom money never touch** (separate accounts, separate books).

**Your role:** once you lock in your president, hand her this. It's her starting playbook.
