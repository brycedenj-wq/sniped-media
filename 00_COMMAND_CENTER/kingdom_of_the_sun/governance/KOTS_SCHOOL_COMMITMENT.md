# Vanguard School Commitment · Run the Kingdom as an Institution, Not a Solo Project

> DRAFT for BJ, then Coach Jones, to bring to the Principal. Internal. This is the keystone: it takes the weight off one person and puts it on the school, where an event this size belongs. Pair it with `KOTS_REVENUE_SHARE_PROPOSAL.md` (the money side of the same idea).

---

## The one true thing

The Kingdom of the Sun is a 53-year national tournament. It is a **Vanguard High School / Marion County Public Schools** event. It is not a one-coach production, and it cannot be run like one. Right now too much of it sits on Coach Jones alone, on top of coaching, a full-time job, and building a program. That is not sustainable, and it is not how an institution of this size should operate.

The fix is not "work harder." It is "stand up the structure that should already be around it."

---

## Two committees, not one

- **The Kingdom committee (external).** Already exists. Runs the tournament itself: field, bracket, officials, the event.
- **The school committee (internal).** Does not exist yet. This is the gap. It is the Vanguard / Marion County side that provides the institutional backing: facilities, staffing, administration, finance, and the school's share of the revenue.

Coach Jones should not be the external organizer *and* the entire internal school operation. He runs basketball. The school runs the school's part.

---

## What the internal committee covers (roles to fill from school + district staff)

- **Administration sponsor:** the Principal (or an assistant principal) as the school's named owner of the event.
- **Athletics liaison:** the AD (the head football coach) for facilities scheduling, staffing, district coordination.
- **Facilities / custodial:** setup, breakdown, gym readiness across four days.
- **Finance / bookkeeping:** tracks gate, concessions, and the school's revenue share through the school account (not out of a coach's pocket).
- **Concessions lead:** runs food ops and vendor relationships (see the food sponsorship play).
- **Volunteer / security coordinator:** SRO presence, ticketing, crowd, working with school clubs and the booster club for volunteers.

Lean on the resources that already exist: the school, the district, and the Y network. The point is delegation, not addition.

---

## What Coach Jones asks the Principal for

1. **Name the event as a school event,** with administrative backing on record.
2. **Assign two or three school-side point people** (athletics, facilities, finance) so it is not all on one person.
3. **Formalize the school's revenue share** so the program reinvests in the kids who carry the tournament's name (full case in `KOTS_REVENUE_SHARE_PROPOSAL.md`).
4. **Provide facilities and staffing support** through the school and district, not volunteers begged for at the last minute.

## What the school gets

- A flagship community event run at a professional level (the website and media operation already prove the standard).
- A revenue line the school is currently leaving on the table.
- Prestige, enrollment halo, and community goodwill from a nationally known tournament that carries the Vanguard name.

---

## The ask, in one line (for Coach Jones to the Principal)

> "The Kingdom is a Vanguard and Marion County event, and it has outgrown what one coach can carry. I need the school behind it: a few named point people, facilities and finance support, and a formal revenue share for the program. I am not asking the school to do more work than it should. I am asking it to own the part that was always the school's to own."
