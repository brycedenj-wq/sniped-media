## The role, in one line

BJ runs the **entire media production** for the Kingdom of the Sun: photography, video, the website, the content system, and the archive. It is his show, year over year. He determines how it operates and how it makes money.

## Scope (what BJ would own, if approved)

- **Official photography execution** per the shot list, galleries, and delivery structure.
- **The website / digital** (`../site/`): the build, upkeep, the live bracket/score phases, the recap pages.
- **The content system**: the game-week calendar, the social templates, the recap package.
- **The archive**: capturing and maintaining the permanent record that feeds the legacy layer.

## What the tournament owns (not BJ)

- The tournament brand, accounts, sponsor relationships, and money structure.
- The decision on whether photos may be sold to families.
- The authority to approve the role and set the pay.

## Compensation (placeholder, gated)

- A **flat production fee** that clears the December trip (set with the committee) is the floor, plus the consumer and sponsor revenue lines that are BJ's to run.
- Full model in `KOTS_MEDIA_MONETIZATION.md`: production fee + photo sales + video packages + sponsor media + builder retainer + licensing.
- BJ sets the numbers. The operation is his.

## Why a defined role helps the tournament (not a pitch, a fact)

A single accountable media lead means consistent quality, one archive that compounds, sponsor proof that actually gets delivered, and a website that improves every year instead of restarting. The alternative (ad-hoc volunteers each December) is why 50 years of history never made it online.
