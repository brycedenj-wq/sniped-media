# Vanguard Basketball · Season Social Playbook (student-run)

> A full-season how-to for the student who runs the basketball page. Follow this and the page stays consistent and good all season without anyone babysitting it. When in doubt: keep it clean, spell names right, post fewer but better.

---

## Your job (the whole season)

Run the page all season to: show off the players (helps them get recruited), get people to the games, and document the year. You are the program's voice online. Treat it like it matters, because to these players and families, it does.

---

## The look (learn this once)

- **Colors:** school colors, kept clean. Navy, gold, white work.
- **Vibe:** real program, not a meme account. Clean and confident. Less is more.
- **Don'ts:** no messy filters, no clutter, no blurry photos, no emoji spam. One clean photo beats five okay ones.

---

## Weekly rhythm (non-tournament weeks)

Aim for **3-5 posts a week**, plus stories on game days:

| When | Post |
|---|---|
| Game day morning | Matchup graphic: "Tonight · [Team] vs [Opponent], [time], [place]" |
| During the game | Score updates + a photo or two (stories) |
| After the game | Final score graphic + 2-3 best photos (feed) |
| Midweek | A player spotlight, a practice clip, or a throwback |
| Announcements | Schedule, senior night, tryouts, big news as they come |

---

## Content types (rotate these so it's not repetitive)

- **Matchups** (before games)
- **Final scores** (after games)
- **Player spotlights** (one player, a good photo, a sentence or two, tag them)
- **Action photos** (the best shots from games)
- **Milestones** (1,000 points, big wins, records)
- **Behind the scenes** (practice, locker room energy, team moments)
- **Announcements** (schedule drops, senior night, community events)
- **Throwbacks** (old wins, alumni, program history)

---

## Game-day checklist (every game)

1. Post the matchup in the morning
2. Bring a charged phone, shoot landscape for feed photos
3. Get: action shots, the bench, the crowd, the scoreboard
4. Post score updates to stories during
5. Post the final + best 2-3 photos after
6. Double-check the score and all names before posting

---

## Captions (short, every time)

- Matchup: `Tonight. [Team] vs [Opponent]. [Time] · [Place]. Come out.`
- Final (win): `W. [Team] [score], [Opponent] [score]. [record].`
- Player spotlight: `[Name], [grade] · [position]. [one line].`
- Milestone: one clean line, let the moment carry it.
- Always tag the players and the opponent if you can.

**First comment hashtags:** `#VanguardBasketball #Knights #OcalaFL #HighSchoolHoops`

---

## The Kingdom of the Sun (the season's biggest week)

In late December, Vanguard hosts the Kingdom of the Sun. **This is the biggest content week of the season.** Post heavy, every day, December 28-31: matchups, scores, the dunk and 3-point contests, the champion. BJ runs the pro coverage that week, your job is to keep the page active and tag everything. Ask BJ for the good photos and the history posts.

---

## Three rules you never break
1. **Spell names right.** Check every player and team name.
2. **Post fewer, better.** One great photo wins.
3. **When unsure, ask first.** Send it to [BJ / the coach] before posting anything you're not sure about.
