# Kingdom of the Sun · Sponsor Media Deliverables Map

> What photo / video / social assets each sponsor receives after the event. This is the **fulfillment** side of the sponsor system (`../sponsorship/`) and the engine of renewals. Owner: Kingdom; executed by BJ if approved.
>
> **Gated:** anything promising **broadcast** placement waits on NFHS confirmation. Crown on deliverables waits on the brand going official (use heritage mark or text until then).

---

## What each asset is
- **Signage / activation photos:** their banner, court-apron, scorer's table, or booth, cleanly shot and legible (captured per the shot list).
- **Named-contest coverage:** photos/clips of the dunk or 3-point contest they titled, branded.
- **Social feature:** a dedicated tournament-account post thanking/featuring the sponsor, tagged.
- **Recap-video feature:** their logo or named slot in the post-event recap reel.
- **Broadcast-proof still:** a frame showing their brand in the NFHS broadcast context (gated).
- **Logo-on-site:** confirmation their logo ran on the tournament website for the cycle.
- **Branded recap:** their tier's slice of the post-event recap (numbers + their proof).

---

## Delivery
- Bundled **within one week** of the event, per sponsor, in `03_DELIVERY/sponsors/<sponsor-key>/`.
- Paired with the recap (see `KOTS_POST_EVENT_RECAP.md`) and a short note: "here is your year, hold your spot for next year."
- Logged in `KOTS_MEDIA_TRACKER.csv` (captured → edited → delivered, per sponsor).

---

## Why this is the renewal engine
A sponsor who receives clean proof of their brand on a national stream, with real numbers, renews without being re-sold. The deliverable **is** the renewal pitch. Fulfilling it well is the single highest-leverage media habit the tournament has.
