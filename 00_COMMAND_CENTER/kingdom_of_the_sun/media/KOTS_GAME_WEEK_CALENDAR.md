# Kingdom of the Sun · Game-Week Content Calendar (Dec 27-31, 2026)

> The repeatable content rhythm for tournament week. Owner: Kingdom social, fed by official photography + NFHS. Reusable each year, shift dates only.

---

## Cadence template (every game day)

| Slot | Post | Platform |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Today's slate + tip-off times + the **NFHS watch link** | IG story, X, FB |
| Pre-game | Matchup graphics, team spotlights | IG feed, X |
| In-game | Live score updates, a few action stills | X (fast), IG story |
| Post-game | Result graphic + a small photo set | IG feed, X, FB |
| Nightly | Day recap reel + gallery-live note | IG reel, FB |

Platforms by job: **X** = fast live scores. **IG** = the mythology and the photography (feed + stories + reels). **Facebook** = parents and the local community.

---

## Day by day

### Sun · Dec 27 · Load-in & Arrivals
- **Capture:** teams arriving, venue prep, Jim Haley Court detail, the bracket board, BTS setup.
- **Post:** "It begins tomorrow" hype + the full bracket + the watch-link announce. Push every invited program's tag.
- **Sponsor:** photograph signage as it goes up (first sponsor-proof frames).

### Mon · Dec 28 · Opening Round
- **Capture:** all opening-round games (action, benches, crowd), team intros, first contest energy.
- **Post:** morning slate + watch link; per-session photo sets; nightly day-1 recap reel.
- **Archive:** flag any standout frames for the permanent set.

### Tue · Dec 29 · Quarterfinals
- **Capture:** quarterfinal action, coach intensity, crowd reactions, sponsor activations in full swing.
- **Post:** matchup graphics, live scores, QF photo galleries, "who's still alive" bracket update.

### Wed · Dec 30 · Semifinals + Dunk & 3-Point Contest
- **The biggest content night.** Capture: semis, then the **dunk and 3-point contests** (peak reel material), crowd, lights.
- **Post:** heavy reels and stories; contest clips are the week's most shareable content. Tag contest sponsors.

### Thu · Dec 31 · Championship + Awards
- **Peak capture:** the championship game, the buzzer, the trophy, the net, the **MVP and All-Tournament team**, the confetti, families.
- **Post:** champion crowned graphic + photo set, MVP feature, all-tournament team, "53rd champion in the books." This is the week's hero post.
- **Archive:** the champion, MVP, all-tournament, and trophy frames go straight to `04_ARCHIVE_2026/` and feed the site champions roll for next year.

### Post-week · early January
- Recap rollout (see `KOTS_POST_EVENT_RECAP.md`), gallery delivery, sponsor proof sets, archive close-out.

## Lead-up (before game week, no gate)

The promo engine starts in the fall, not in December. Match the real KOS cadence:

- **Late September:** field announcement ("Your 2026 Kingdom of the Sun Field," call out the out-of-state programs).
- **Early October:** bracket release (tease it the day before, "Bracket Release Tomorrow 9am," then drop the full bracket with the dates).
- **October to early December:** steady drumbeat: team spotlights as the field confirms, "the original since 1974" legacy posts, alumni-in-the-NBA throwbacks, sponsor welcome posts.
- **~Dec 1:** open the media credential request window (form + QR, ~3-week window).
- **Final 30 / 14 / 7 / 3 / 1 days out:** countdown posts, watch-link pre-save, daily hype into game week.
- Pull legacy content (champions, alumni-in-the-NBA throwbacks) from `../legacy/` to fill the long October-to-December runway.
