# Kingdom of the Sun · Post-Event Recap Package

> The wrap-up that closes the year and sets up the next one. Built from all five rails. Owner: Kingdom; executed by BJ if approved. Reusable template, refilled each year.

---

## What the recap contains

1. **The numbers**
   - Attendance / gate (across four days)
   - **NFHS stream views** (the headline reach number, pull from NFHS)
   - Social reach + top posts (impressions, engagement)
   - Teams, states represented, games played

2. **The result**
   - The 53rd champion + final score
   - MVP + All-Tournament team
   - Notable games, upsets, records touched

3. **The photos**
   - The hero set: champion, trophy, MVP, the moment
   - A best-of gallery link

4. **Sponsor proof**
   - Per-sponsor activation proof (ties to `KOTS_SPONSOR_MEDIA_DELIVERABLES.md`)
   - "Your brand ran here, in front of this many" with their stills + the reach number

5. **Watch / replay**
   - NFHS replay links for the marquee games
   - The recap reel

6. **Legacy / history tie-in**
   - "The 53rd chapter" framing: this year's champion added to the 50-year roll
   - An alumni/history note (e.g., this year's field vs the tournament's NBA lineage)
   - Updates the site champions roll for next year

---

## Formats (one recap, several surfaces)

| Surface | Form | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| **Site** | A `/recap-2026` page (champion, numbers, gallery, replay) | Public, SEO, the permanent record |
| **Sponsor one-pager** | A clean PDF/deck per sponsor, their proof + the numbers | Sponsors (renewal) |
| **Social series** | A recap reel + carousel + the champion hero post | Public |
| **Archive entry** | The year folded into `04_ARCHIVE_2026/` and `../legacy/` | Internal, compounding |

---

## Timing
- **Champion hero post:** night of Dec 31.
- **Social recap series:** first 3-5 days of January.
- **Sponsor recaps + media deliverables:** within one week.
- **Site recap page + champions-roll update:** within two weeks.
- **Archive close-out:** within two weeks.

---

## The compounding move
Every recap feeds the next year: the champions roll grows, the alumni story deepens, the sponsor proof drives renewals, and the archive thickens the legacy layer. The recap is not the end of the cycle, it is the seed of the next one.
