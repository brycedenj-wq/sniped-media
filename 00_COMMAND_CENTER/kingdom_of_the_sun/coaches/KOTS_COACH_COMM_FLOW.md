# Kingdom of the Sun · Coach Communication Flow

> Invitation-only. This is relationship and confirmation management, not registration. Every message treats the coach as an invited guest of a 53-year national event, not an applicant.
>
> **Owner: Lane A (dad / committee / tournament director).** All drafts below are starting points.

---

## 1. The flow maps to the 8 stages

Each communication moves a program one stage forward. Send the next one only when the prior milestone is in.

| Stage target | Communication | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Invited → Verbal | A. Formal invitation | Field is set, invite window opens |
| Verbal → Confirmed | B. Confirmation lock | Coach said yes verbally |
| Confirmed → Roster | C. Roster request | Written confirmation in |
| Roster → Travel | D. Travel info request | Roster received |
| Travel → Hotel | E. Hotel info request | Travel known (often sent together with D) |
| Hotel → Media | F. Media assets request | Logistics settled |
| Media → Fully ready | G. Final-week confirm | All assets in, event near |

---

## 2. Cadence

- **Invite window:** summer / early fall. Named teams first, final 6 as soon as chosen. Holiday-tournament schedules fill early; late invites lose teams.
- **Confirmation deadline:** set one hard date. A verbal that is not written by the deadline is treated as open and the spot can move.
- **Readiness waves:** roster, travel, hotel, and media collected across fall into December.
- **Reminders:** one nudge per missing item every 7 to 10 days. Coaches are busy in-season; make each ask one tap to answer.
- **Final week:** confirm every team reads Fully Ready. Anything short is the call list.

## 3. Templates (DRAFTS, Lane A sends)

### A. Formal invitation
> Coach [name], on behalf of the Kingdom of the Sun, I am inviting [school] to the 53rd Annual, December 28-31, 2026 at Vanguard High School in Ocala. This is the original national holiday tournament, going on 53 years, and every game streams live on NFHS Network. We would be proud to have your program in the field. Can I count you in? Happy to talk through the details by phone.

### B. Confirmation lock
> Coach [name], glad to have [school] in. To lock your spot in the bracket I need a written confirmation by [deadline date]. Just reply "confirmed" and we are set. After that I will send the roster, travel, and hotel details.

### C. Roster request
> Coach [name], we are building the program and broadcast materials. Please send your official roster (player names, numbers, grade, height) by [date]. This is what the announcer reads and what goes on the site, so accuracy helps your kids get seen.

### D. Travel info request
> Coach [name], quick logistics: what day are you arriving, how many in your traveling party, and are you coming by bus or flying in? This helps us plan meals and schedule.

### E. Hotel info request
> Coach [name], where is your team staying for the tournament? If you need help with a hotel block in the Ocala area, let me know and I will point you to our options.

### F. Media assets request
> Coach [name], last piece: please send your team logo and a current team photo (and player headshots if you have them). These go on the tournament website, program, and broadcast graphics. The better your assets, the better your program looks on a national stream.

### G. Final-week confirm
> Coach [name], we are a week out. Confirming [school] is set: roster in, arriving [day], staying at [hotel], assets received. Anything you still need from us? Looking forward to having you in Ocala.

## 4. Out-of-state and long-travel note

Peachtree Ridge (GA) and Tallahassee Godby travel furthest. Flag hotel and travel earlier for them, and confirm their arrival day first, since they anchor the most logistics risk in the field.
