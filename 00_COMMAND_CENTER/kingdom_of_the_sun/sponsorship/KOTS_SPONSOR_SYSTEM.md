## 1. Why this tournament is sellable (the asset, in one read)

The 53rd Annual Kingdom of the Sun is not a local rec event. It is the original national high school holiday tournament: founded 1974, 51-plus years of history, 71 state champions, programs from 25-plus states plus Canada and the Bahamas, and NBA alumni including Dwight Howard, Christian Laettner, Jamal Mashburn, and Kerry Kittles. December 28-31, 2026 at Vanguard, 16 invited programs, four days.

What changed the sponsor math: **every game streams on NFHS Network** (the Vanguard gym already has the camera). That turns a gym audience into a measurable digital one. Sponsors are not buying a banner in Ocala, they are buying four days of national-history brand association with trackable streaming reach plus a new website as standing digital real estate.

That is the pitch spine. Everything below is the inventory that spine lets us sell.

## 8. How the system runs, year over year

1. **Pre-season:** refresh the prospect list, reset tracker stages, confirm tiers + pricing with dad.
2. **Outreach window:** run the sequence in `KOTS_SPONSOR_OUTREACH_FLOW.md`, warm network first.
3. **Close + activate:** collect commitments, deliver inventory (signage, site logos, program, contest naming).
4. **During event:** fulfill (PA reads, stream, on-site), capture proof (photos of activated signage, stream numbers).
5. **Post-event:** send each sponsor a recap with their activation proof and streaming reach. This is the renewal hook. Mark renewals in the tracker.
6. **Renew:** returning sponsors get first right of refusal on their tier before new prospects are offered it.

The renewal recap is the highest-leverage habit in the whole system. A sponsor who sees their logo on a national stream with real numbers renews without being re-sold.
