# Kingdom of the Sun · Sponsor Outreach Flow

> **DRAFT SYSTEM. No message here is sent until dad confirms authority to sell (System doc section 6).** This is the sequence, the segments, and the templates. Build now, fire later.

---

## 1. Segments (who we approach, in priority order)

Warm before cold. The whole sequence runs warm network first, because the tournament has 53 years of relationships it has never once monetized.

1. **Warm / network (highest yield)** · dad's contacts, committee members, and tournament alumni. These convert fastest and anchor the Crown and Court tiers.
2. **Local Ocala / Marion County** · auto dealers, banks and credit unions, health systems, QSR franchises, local retailers, Chamber members. This fills the Community and Sideline tiers and builds the logo wall.
3. **Regional Florida** · Publix-tier retailers, regional brands with a youth-sports giving program.
4. **National HS-sports brands** · Gatorade, Wilson, Spalding, athletic retailers. Slowest, longest lead, usually in-kind before cash. Plant year 1, harvest year 2.

---

## 2. The sequence (per prospect)

Five touches, owner noted. Dad's authority fronts the ask; BJ builds and executes the materials.

| Step | Action | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warm intro or first contact | A (dad / committee) | A named person opens the door |
| 2 | Send the one-pager | B builds, A sends | Personalize the tier to the prospect |
| 3 | Conversation / call | A (dad) | Diagnose fit, not pitch hard |
| 4 | Tailored proposal (tier + price) | B builds, A approves | Only after a real conversation |
| 5 | Close + activation details | A + B | Collect logo, payment terms, inventory list |

After close: log in the tracker, deliver inventory, fulfill at the event, then send the renewal recap (the highest-leverage step in the system).

---

## 3. Cadence

- Open the window roughly 90 days out (late September / October for a December event).
- Warm segment first, two weeks.
- Local segment next, rolling.
- One follow-up per prospect if no reply within 7 to 10 days, then a final check-in. Three touches, then park to next year. Never chase.

## 4. Templates (DRAFTS, not for send)

### A. Warm intro request (BJ or dad to a connector)
> Quick one. We are lining up sponsors for the 53rd Annual Kingdom of the Sun this December at Vanguard. Every game streams on NFHS Network this year, so there is real reach behind it. You know [prospect] · would you be open to a quick intro? I will keep it short and make it easy for them to say yes.

### B. First outreach (after a warm intro)
> Hi [name], [connector] suggested I reach out. The Kingdom of the Sun is the original national high school holiday tournament, going on 53 years, December 28-31 at Vanguard. This year every game streams live on NFHS Network. We have a few partnership spots open and I think [brand] would be a strong fit. Mind if I send a one-page overview?

### C. One-pager send
> Thanks [name]. Attached is the overview. The short version: 53 years of history, NBA alumni, 16 programs over four days, and live national streaming, with year-round placement on our new site. Tiers run from a local-business level up to full presenting rights. Happy to find the spot that fits [brand]. Open to a quick call this week?

### D. Follow-up (no reply, day 7-10)
> Hi [name], circling back on the Kingdom of the Sun partnership. Spots are filling and I wanted to make sure [brand] had first look before we offer the tier elsewhere. Worth a quick call?

### E. Renewal recap (post-event, the renewal engine)
> [name], thank you for backing the Kingdom of the Sun this year. Attached is your recap: your logo on the broadcast, on-site, and on the site, plus this year's streaming numbers. We would love to hold your spot for next year before we open it up. Want me to pencil you in?

## 5. Owner discipline (Lane separation)

- **Dad / committee (Lane A)** front every ask and every price. The authority and the relationships are theirs.
- **BJ (Lane B)** builds the materials, runs the tracker, owns the website and digital placements as builder of record, and owns the photo gallery and highlight sponsor inventory tied to his media role.
- BJ does not send a tournament sponsor ask, or quote a tournament price, without dad's explicit go on that prospect. The website digital inventory is the one lane BJ can run more directly, still under dad's authority.
