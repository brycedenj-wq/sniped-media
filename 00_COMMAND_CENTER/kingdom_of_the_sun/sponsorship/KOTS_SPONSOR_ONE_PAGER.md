## The Original. Since 1974.

The Kingdom of the Sun is the original national high school holiday basketball tournament. Fifty-three years. 71 state champions. Programs from more than 25 states, Canada, and the Bahamas. Alumni who reached the NBA: Dwight Howard, Christian Laettner, Jamal Mashburn, Kerry Kittles, and more.

**December 28-31, 2026 · Vanguard High School · Ocala, Florida · 16 invited programs.**

This year, every game streams live on **NFHS Network**, turning four days of basketball into measurable national reach. Plus a new tournament website that carries your brand year-round.

When you sponsor the Kingdom of the Sun, you are not buying a banner. You are putting your name on 53 years of history, in front of a national audience, on a stage that produced NBA players.
