### BOOK 1: Prompt Template - Thought Generation-2.pdf

**Coverage:** 1/1 segments · complete

**Thesis:** Chain of Thought and Tree of Thought prompt patterns decompose multi-step tasks into numbered reasoning scaffolds, forcing sequential "show your work" logic rather than direct-to-conclusion generation.

**Densest Frameworks / Lessons:**

1. **CoT (linear):** Task description → numbered steps (3-4) → output expectation. Best for single-path problems (affordability checks, campaign planning, data analysis).
2. **ThoT (branching):** Same scaffold but steps generate multiple solution branches, then evaluate feasibility. Best for ambiguous or multi-option problems (supply chain delays, creative narratives).
3. **Step 1 Identify / Step 2 Generate / Step 3 Evaluate / Step 4 Recommend:** Universal 4-step scaffold transferable to any multi-step OS task.
4. **Feedback analysis template (Example 5):** Summarize positives → identify complaints → propose improvements → justify effectiveness. Directly maps to VIB debrief and post-shoot review workflows.
5. **Pedagogical limit:** Document is accessibility-grade, not strategic depth. No failure modes, refinement loops, or adversarial prompting patterns shown.

**SNIPED Application:**

The 4-step scaffold is immediately usable inside SNIPED OS skill prompts (pre-shoot prep, caption writing, VIB debrief, Notion CRM update). Example 5 (customer feedback) maps to post-delivery retro. No new strategic frameworks; existing OS docs (Direction Stack, Trust Equation) are deeper on substance.

**UPDATES / CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine:**

None. Consistent with OS toolchain default (route to Claude Code with structured prompts). Confirms existing practice. Adds no doctrine. Shelf weight: low.

### BOOK 2: How to Market a Book: Overperform in a Crowded Market (Fayet / Reedsy, 2021)

**Coverage:** 10/10 segments · complete

**Thesis:** In a supply-saturated market, visibility is earned not by doing everything but by locking product fundamentals (niche fit, packaging, owned audience) before deploying focused, measurable distribution channels, then compounding via catalog architecture.

**Densest Frameworks / Lessons:**

1. **Reader-Centric Inversion:** Flip from "author finds readers" (push) to "reader finds book" (pull). Position where readers already search and browse. Direct parallel: SNIPED does not find clients; founders already searching for something that signals what they want to become.

2. **Four Fundamentals Before Tactics:** (a) Know target market precisely, (b) product matching or exceeding expectations, (c) platform mechanics literacy, (d) owned email list. No tactics until all four are locked. Violating this wastes every downstream dollar.

3. **Conversion Layer Stack:** Cover (hire pro, A/B test via ad CTR) → Blurb (copywriting, not storytelling; conversion-first) → Review volume + review quality (social proof signal) → A+ Content (mobile = above-blurb, rich media). Each layer solves for reader psychology at point of decision, not author ego.

4. **Series / Catalog Architecture as Revenue Multiplier:** Same marketing effort, 5-10x revenue difference. 1 standalone × 1,000 readers = $350-4,200. 10-book series × 1,000 readers = ~$21,000. Applied outside books: Direction Stack (1 shoot) vs. multi-chapter portfolio arc (5-8 chapters) = same sales + relationship effort, compounding return.

5. **Also Boughts Purity (Amazon AIRS contamination risk):** Amazon's internal recommendation engine learns from co-purchase signals. One wrong-audience promotion poisons the recommendation graph for months. Applies to any algorithmic platform: wrong audience early-on contaminates the algorithm's model of who your work is for.

6. **Word-of-Mouth Silent Majority:** Vocal reviewers are the minority; the true sales drivers are non-reviewing readers who recommend privately. Optimize for quiet recommendability, not public praise metrics. Inglis case: ads = 25%, WoM = 75% of sales.

7. **Read-Through (RT) Lifetime Value Math:** Series profitability requires RT accounting. Book 1 at $0.99 appears negative in isolation; with 45-60% RT at $2.99-$4.99 downstream, lifetime value returns 280%+ ROI. Ad spend decisions must account for full catalog value, not single-sale ACoS.

8. **Boat Metaphor (Non-Negotiable Foundation):** The boat = niche fit, cover, blurb (packaging). Sails = metadata/SEO (passive). Paddles = manual outreach. Thrusters = paid ads. Weak boat, all tactics fail. Strong boat, each layer multiplies.

**SNIPED Application:**

Direction Stack book launch is the primary activation zone. Apply in order:

- Lock the niche (not "photography book" - narrow to creative methodology for Black founders and visual-identity-first operators).
- Nail the blurb before any channel deploy. Blurb is copywriting, conversion-first. Every word answers: "What changes for the reader who buys this?"
- Seed launch with clean audience (VIB network, LA founder scene, lineage circles) before any broad ad targeting. Amazon AIRS contamination risk is real; wrong audience early = months of damage.
- Wide distribution (Apple Books, Kobo) over KU exclusivity. Direction Stack is premium methodology, not genre fiction volume-play. Apple readers are price-insensitive (iOS signal); Kobo rewards exclusivity with human merchandising attention.
- Preorders only on Apple / Kobo (count twice). Amazon preorders are net-negative unless series momentum justifies.
- Build email list as the owned audience layer before launch, not after. Email is the only channel immune to algorithm volatility.
- RT math applies to SNIPED's multi-product arc: Direction Stack book → Cultural Doc → Workshop → Consulting. Client or reader who enters at book 1 and moves through the arc is worth modeling at full lifetime value.
- Newsletter swaps (trust-transfer distribution) via LA founder voices are higher-ROI than cold ads at launch. Relationship-first, in-scene, same lineage.

**UPDATES / CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine:**

- **REINFORCES** Scene-Density Thinking: depth inside specific LA cultural circles beats breadth. Fayet confirms cluster-first distribution is superior to mass-broadcast.
- **REINFORCES** Perennial Seller logic: Fayet's wide distribution = 12+ month commitment, slow proof-building. Not a blitz.
- **REINFORCES** Repetition over Novelty: test one channel at a time (30 days), measure, then add. Scatter = failure.
- **REINFORCES** Silent Majority doctrine: vocal praise is a lagging indicator; silent recommenders drive compounding.
- **NEW ADDITION to OS:** Also Boughts Purity as contamination doctrine. Platform recommendation graphs are model-poisonable. First-audience purity matters on any algorithmic surface (IG, Amazon, LinkedIn). SNIPED must seed every new platform with the RIGHT cultural signal audience first.
- **NEW ADDITION to OS:** Read-Through Lifetime Value math as a calculation discipline. SNIPED currently evaluates offers at single-sale level. Multi-product arc requires RT-style modeling (what percentage of book buyers become workshop buyers? What does that change about what to spend on acquisition?).
- **WEAKENS** assumption that paid ads are early-stage tools. Fayet is clear: ads amplify a solid foundation; pre-foundation ad spend is waste. SNIPED's existing paid-boost doctrine ($30/day desktop 10-mile LA) is only valid AFTER boat is solid.

### EVALUATION GATES

1. **Boat Gate (pre-launch mandatory):** Niche specificity confirmed? Blurb is conversion-copy, not story-copy? Seed audience is clean (correct cultural signal, no contamination risk)? Email capture live? If any gate fails, no tactic deploys.

2. **Also Boughts Purity Gate:** First 50-100 buyers (or followers/interactions) are from the RIGHT audience cluster. Platform recommendation model is seeded clean. If broad promo runs before this, pause and assess contamination risk.

3. **RT Gate (before scaling paid acquisition):** Has a read-through (or equivalent: client continuation, workshop conversion) model been built for the multi-product arc? If not, acquisition cost decisions are incomplete.

4. **Channel Mono-Focus Gate:** Is only one distribution/marketing channel being tested right now? If more than two are active simultaneously, one must be paused. Scatter = zero attribution, wasted effort.

5. **Blurb Conversion Gate:** Does every product/service description answer "what specifically changes for the buyer?" If it reads like internal narrative (accurate to the maker's view), it fails. Rewrite for the buyer's decision point.

### DECISION UPGRADES

- **Direction Stack book: Wide over KU.** Premium methodology hybrid (not genre fiction volume). Apple / Kobo human curation rewards exclusivity. KU exclusivity locks into Amazon dependency and forces price promotions that contaminate audience signal. Decision: Wide.
- **Pre-launch order:** Blurb lock → seed audience → email capture → street team (20-30) → preorder (Apple/Kobo only) → Amazon release day. Not ads first.
- **Newsletter swaps before paid ads** for initial launch velocity. LA founder voices (10-15) + internal SNIPED list = tighter trust-transfer distribution. Paid ads amplify, not initiate.
- **Paid ad timing:** Only after boat is solid and seed audience is clean. Then: Amazon Advertising for higher-priced nonfiction ($14.99-$19.99 range); BookBub only for discounted/free promotional windows; Facebook for retargeting and list-building at scale.
- **Multi-product RT modeling required before any single-sale ACoS target is set.** If Direction Stack book feeds a workshop → consulting arc, the true acquisition value of one book buyer is 3-5x the book price. Changes what you can spend to acquire.

### DOCTRINE / MEMORY UPGRADES

**PROMOTE to OS doctrine (new rules):**

- **Also Boughts Purity Doctrine:** On any algorithmic platform (Amazon, IG, LinkedIn, TikTok), the first audience you attract becomes the model the platform uses to find more like them. Seed wrong, and the algorithm finds more wrong. Seed with precision inside your scene, then scale. No broad promo before seed audience is locked.

- **Conversion-Copy Doctrine:** Any product description, caption, landing page, or blurb is conversion-copy, not narrative copy. It answers the buyer's question ("what changes for me?"), not the maker's question ("what is this?"). Internal accuracy is insufficient. Buyer psychology governs.

- **RT Lifetime Value Discipline:** No acquisition cost decision is valid without a read-through (or equivalent conversion arc) model. Single-sale math is systematically misleading. Calculate: acquisition cost vs. full lifecycle value (book → workshop → consulting, or shoot → retainer → referral). This changes pricing, spending, and selectivity decisions.

**WEAKENED assumptions:**

- Paid ads as early-stage tools: Fayet is definitive. Ads amplify foundations. Pre-foundation ad spend wastes every dollar. SNIPED's $30/day paid boost is only valid after niche, packaging, and seed audience are locked.
- "More channels = more reach" assumption: Fayet and SNIPED doctrine now aligned. More channels before mastering one = no attribution, diluted effort, failed measurement.

**CONTRADICTIONS resolved:**

- Fayet's KU thesis (fiction volume play) does not apply to Direction Stack (premium methodology). No tension once genre-function distinction is drawn.
- Fayet's "social media optional; email mandatory" is consistent with existing OS leverage logic (owned audience over rented platform). Not a contradiction; reinforces the rule.
