## MASTER-DOCTRINE ADDITIONS

**1. Proof-Log Discipline (new, from EXECUTION_RUN_30DAY_CAPABILITY_DOSSIER + THESIS_PRESSURE_TEST_C1C2)**
Every interaction becomes data. REAL PROOF = money + named outcome + access. FAKE INTEREST = praise, "send info," no door opened. Proof-log entry is mandatory for every send, reply, call, objection, and paid outcome. The kill/iterate decision must be grounded in logged evidence, not vibes. This replaces generic B2B sales SOP for active beachhead sprints.

**2. Room-Access as Unlock (new, from EXECUTION_RUN_30DAY_CAPABILITY_DOSSIER)**
Calls booked and interest expressed are NOT success metrics. Success = PAID (Pilot or LOI) + ROOM OPENED (access granted, named bid/role commitment secured). This distinction governs every close/kill/iterate decision.

**3. RED/YELLOW/GREEN Gate (new, from EXECUTION_RUN_30DAY_CAPABILITY_DOSSIER + THESIS_PRESSURE_TEST_C1C2)**
GREEN = 1+ paid + 1+ room opened → run 60-day repeatability. YELLOW = strong verbal pull but unpaid, or access without payment → change one lever, re-run. RED = 0 paid + 0 rooms after 15 conversations → kill current framing, pivot to C4 (founder/operator presence). This is the decision gate for all beachhead sprints.

**4. C1/C2 Thesis Update (new, from TRUE_BILLION_DOLLAR_THESIS + THESIS_PRESSURE_TEST_C1C2)**
C1 survives narrowed: SMB supply chain (staffing + specialty subs), owner discretion, short cycles, outcome-anchored offer. Proof unlocks each layer; layers are not interchangeable. Kill criteria: 11 failure modes; 2+ sustained = revise toward C4.

**5. Orwell-vs-Huxley Operator Warning (new, from LITERARY_CANON_DYSTOPIAN)**
Orwell warned of oppression by external force (boot + fear). Huxley warned of oppression by pleasure and comfort (soma + control). AI systems tend toward Huxley's vector (engagement optimization, comfort delivery, belief engineering). Operator builds with brakes on; comfort-as-control and propaganda-in-democracy are the anti-patterns to SNIPED hospitality and dignity.
