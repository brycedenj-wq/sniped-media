## DOCTRINE ENTRIES (Deduped, Cross-Skill Principles)

**D-001: Incentives Govern Behavior, Not Intent**
Policy works or fails based on what it makes profitable, cheap, or easy for the actual decision-maker. Second- and third-order consequences are where most policies fail. Apply the Sowell Test: compared to what? At what cost? What evidence?
Sources: economic-incentive-policy-analysis, industry-dynamics-assessment

**D-002: Sequence Is Non-Negotiable**
Workflow order matters absolutely. Whether commissioning a data center, setting up Meta Business Suite, or building a knowledge system, skipping steps or wrong sequencing causes downstream failure. Gated progression beats parallel execution on complex systems.
Sources: server-farm-commissioning, meta-business-infrastructure-setup, second-brain-CODE-PARA

**D-003: Outside View Before Inside View**
Anchor to base rates before case-specific reasoning. Reference class first, then adjust for the particulars of your situation. Overconfidence comes from skipping the outside view. Applies to forecasting, market evaluation, and negotiation.
Sources: superforecasting-workflow, signal-vs-noise-bayesian, fermi-estimation

**D-004: Specialist Positioning Over Generalist Range**
Premium pricing requires a narrow, defensible position. "Who can I serve better than anyone else?" is the forcing question. Generalist skills cannot command premium rates; specialist systems can. Applies equally to photography, consulting, and knowledge work.
Sources: photography-business-system, seven-powers-assessment, strategy-kernel-development

**D-005: Past Behavior Over Future Promises**
Real validation comes from what people have already done, not what they say they will do. Compliments are noise; commitments (time, reputation, money) are signal. A meeting without a clear next step is waste.
Sources: mom-test-customer-conversation, signal-vs-noise-bayesian

**D-006: Inversion as Mandatory Risk Layer**
Before committing to any plan, list all ways to guarantee the opposite outcome. Fail-mode enumeration (premortem, Munger inversion) consistently surfaces risks that forward-planning misses. Run it before execution, not after.
Sources: munger-two-track-analysis, premortem-analysis

**D-007: Governing Thought First, Evidence Second**
All communication, strategic or operational, must lead with the single main takeaway. Evidence, arguments, and supporting detail follow the governing thought. This is the Pyramid Principle applied universally.
Sources: pyramid-structured-communication, success-message-design

**D-008: Organize by Use, Not by Topic**
Information and assets should be organized by where you will actually use them, not by subject matter. Actionability is the organizational criterion. Applies to knowledge management, production asset libraries, and OS structure.
Sources: second-brain-CODE-PARA

**D-009: Cheap Levers Before Expensive Ones**
Exhaust price increases, upsells, and frequency gains before spending on customer acquisition. The four-lever revenue framework inverts the default startup reflex. Acquisition is last resort, not first instinct.
Sources: revenue-growth-diagnostic, market-evaluation-scorecard

**D-010: Feedback Loops Calibrate Everything**
Predictions without tracking degrade into overconfidence. Outputs without measurement drift from standards. Commit to measuring actuals against predictions over time, both for forecasting (Brier scores) and content analytics (follows gained, not vanity views).
Sources: signal-vs-noise-bayesian, superforecasting-workflow, social-media-content-strategy

**D-011: Commitment Escalation Is a Bias, Not a Strategy**
Doubling down on a direction because you are already invested is a Track 2 misjudgment (Munger). Decisions should be re-evaluated from first principles each time, especially when new evidence contradicts the original thesis.
Sources: munger-two-track-analysis, premortem-analysis

**D-012: Structure Proves Logic, Not Just Arrangement**
Whether building a strategy kernel, a pyramid document, or a posing sequence, every element must logically support the governing whole. If any element could be removed without weakening the structure, it is not load-bearing and should be cut.
Sources: pyramid-structured-communication, strategy-kernel-development, professional-portrait-direction

**D-013: Multi-Framework Synthesis Over Single-Lens Analysis**
Complex decisions require multiple frameworks firing in sequence. The framework orchestrator principle: diagnose with one lens, evaluate with another, check for bias with a third. Contradictions between frameworks are the most valuable output.
Sources: framework-orchestrator, munger-two-track-analysis

**D-014: Curse of Knowledge Is the Communication Blocker**
Expertise blinds you to what a listener actually hears. Every communication artifact must be built for the receiver's context, not the sender's. Use concrete sensory equivalents, not abstract claims.
Sources: success-message-design, pyramid-structured-communication

**D-015: Black Swans Are Discovered in Person, Not by Email**
Hidden information that changes the entire deal dynamic is almost never revealed through formal channels. Face time, unguarded moments, and casual conversation are where the real intelligence surfaces.
Sources: negotiation-leverage-black-swan

## MASTER-DOCTRINE ADDITIONS

**ADD-001: Sowell Test Gate**
Before shipping any policy, pricing, or program decision: "Compared to what? At what cost? What evidence?" This is a mandatory three-question gate, not a rhetorical check.

**ADD-002: Premortem as Standard Pre-Launch Protocol**
Every shoot, book chapter, content vertical launch, or business lane activation requires a premortem. Failure mode enumeration must happen before the execution calendar locks, not after the first problem surfaces.

**ADD-003: Four-Lever Revenue Priority Order**
LOCKED priority: Price increase first > Purchase frequency second > Transaction size third > Customer acquisition last. Any plan that leads with acquisition without exhausting the other three levers requires explicit justification.

**ADD-004: Commitment vs. Compliment Discipline**
In every sales, partnership, or validation conversation: a meeting ends with a specific next step or an explicit rejection. Positive sentiment without commitment is not signal. This applies to VIB outreach, casting calls, and brand partnership conversations.

**ADD-005: Bayesian Update Discipline**
Every major forecast (revenue, audience growth, launch outcome) must have a documented prior, evidence log, and posterior. Beliefs update incrementally; do not wait for certainty before adjusting direction.

## CONTRADICTIONS FLAGGED

**C-001: Artifact 25 is Not a Skill**
Artifact 25 in the batch is a clarification message ("The file path you referenced doesn't exist..."), not a distilled skill. The batch claims 30 artifacts but contains only 29 genuine skill distillations plus one navigation error. The inventory above marks this as flagged. Operator must confirm which skill was intended for slot 25 or accept a 29-skill batch.

**C-002: EPMS and Server-Farm Skills Have No SNIPED Application**
Skills 2 (epms-site-analysis) and 23 (server-farm-commissioning) are explicitly noted as "not directly applicable" to SNIPED in their own distillations. However, the server-farm skill's gated-verification doctrine (no stage advancement with open deficiencies) has orthogonal value for production management. Both should be archived rather than kept in the active skill roster unless SNIPED expands into infrastructure advisory.

**C-003: Lean Transformation 4P vs. Execution Governor**
The lean-transformation-diagnostic emphasizes that leadership must embody philosophy, not just mandate tools. The SNIPED Execution Governor currently operates as a rules-based routing system (STANDING_ORDER + NEXT_ACTION). If the OS is treated as a tool mandate without the underlying Philosophy and Problem Solving pillars engaged daily, the 4P diagnostic predicts stall. This is a live tension worth tracking: is SNIPED running the OS as a philosophy or as a checklist?

**C-004: Hoshin Kanri Catchball vs. Company of One Constraint**
Hoshin Kanri's value comes from two-way negotiation between levels. SNIPED is a one-person operator. The catchball mechanism must be adapted: Bryce sets True North, vendors and collaborators provide feasibility feedback upward, then the plan locks. The skill assumes multi-level organizational hierarchy that does not exist as described. Using it unmodified risks over-engineering a solo operator context.

**C-005: Freelance Platform Optimization vs. Win Without Pitching**
The freelance-platform-optimization skill explicitly builds toward Fiverr/Upwork gig infrastructure and consultation-first monetization. Win Without Pitching doctrine positions SNIPED as a practitioner who does not audition or compete on platforms. These are not directly contradictory (platform could be a lead-gen channel, not the positioning home), but they sit in tension. Operator should define whether any platform presence is acceptable or whether it undermines the positioned-authority lane.
