Different content categories have different maturity levels. Tech and AI is mature — bar is high, every visual must be tight. Dentistry is low maturity — even a grainy phone shot

LinkedIn posts, IG Stories, YouTube descriptions sometimes — engagement gets nuked. Platforms don’t want users leaving. Trial Reels abused as a hack (mass spam) — patched. Now
you’ll get banned/throttled. Must integrate into broader strategy. Mixed-topic accounts on Instagram — accountbased embeddings mean IG assigns ONE topic to your whole account. If
you post lifestyle/kitchen/travel, the algorithm never gives you a clean fit and you stagnate. Confused-fit-score videos (after a viral hit to wrong audience) — next several videos

realize they’re watching content they don’t need to watch, leave. Long sentences that the brain can’t parse in chunks → comprehension drops → bounce. Vague hooks with zero topic
context (“guys this is crazy”) → instant bounce on the steep early retention drop-off. WHAT KILLS CULT FANDOM Talking about yourself before earning the right (your morning routine,
