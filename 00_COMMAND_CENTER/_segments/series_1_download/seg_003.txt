works, such as anthologies and collages of public domain materials, as long as the human author contributed creativity through selection, arrangement, and ordering. The principle
has always been clear: without human authorship, there’s no copyright. This standard extends to Gen-AI. However, advancements in software now enable users to exert greater control
over AI-generated imagery. Some tools even allow users to “paint” with AI, defining spaces and directing the AI to fill them based on specific prompts. If anyone is interested,
