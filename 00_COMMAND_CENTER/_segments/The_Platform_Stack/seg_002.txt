XI: Meta Advertising Fundamentals Meta advertising is one of the highest-leverage activities available to a business with product-market fit. It is also the place where the most
money is wasted by businesses that do not have it. This part covers the fundamentals. Account structure, campaign architecture, audience strategy, creative frameworks, and the
ban-prevention discipline that keeps the account alive. 11.1 Before running ads A prerequisite check. Running ads without the following pieces in place wastes money. Verified
identity and Business Portfolio. From Part VII. Pixel installed on your website. Tracks visitor behavior. Required for audience building and conversion tracking. Conversion events
configured. Purchase, Lead, AddToCart, ViewContent, whatever matters to your funnel. Set these up in Events Manager. Product catalog (for e-commerce). From Part IX. A tested

campaign structure Level What It Is Decision Ad Account The billing and reporting container. One ad account per business entity. Create with the correct timezone and currency
(permanent). Campaign The objective container. One campaign per marketing objective. Choose objective: Sales, Leads, Traffic, Engagement, Awareness. Most businesses use Sales or
Leads. Ad Set The audience and placement container. One ad set per audience or targeting approach. Set budget, schedule, and placements here. Ad The creative container. The actual
image, video, or copy. Multiple ads per ad set for testing. Keep ad count per set under 6 for clean data. 11.3 Campaign objectives The objective tells Meta what optimization target
to aim for. Choose carefully. Sales. Optimizes for purchases. Use when your conversion event (Purchase) is set up correctly and you are driving to a checkout. Leads. Optimizes for
form submissions, lead magnet downloads, or Lead Ads. Use for B2B or service businesses where leads precede sales. Traffic. Optimizes for link clicks. Use when you are driving to
content, not a conversion event. Cheaper than Sales but also lower-intent. Engagement. Optimizes for Page likes, post engagement, or Messenger conversations. Use for top-of-funnel
content amplification. Awareness. Optimizes for reach and impressions. Use only for brand-building with a specific budget allocated to awareness. 11.4 Audience strategy The
audience decision is often more important than the creative. A great ad to the wrong audience performs worse than a mediocre ad to the right audience. Advantage+ versus manual
Advantage+ is Meta's AI-driven audience tool. It allows Meta to find the audience dynamically based on the creative and the conversion data. For most e-commerce and most
direct-response businesses, Advantage+ now outperforms manually-targeted ad sets. Start with Advantage+ Shopping campaigns for e-commerce. Start with Advantage+ for Leads if your
conversion event is well-tracked. Use manual targeting when you need specific audience exclusions, when testing a very specific segment, or when Advantage+ is not yet producing
acceptable results. Custom audiences Audiences built from your own data. These are some of the highest-performing audiences available on Meta. Website visitors. Retarget people who
visited your site but did not convert. Customer list. Upload existing customers. Retarget for repeat business. Or exclude them from acquisition campaigns. Engagement audiences.
People who interacted with your Facebook Page, Instagram profile, Reels, or ads. Video viewers. People who watched a certain percentage of a specific video. Build a warmer audience
over time. Lead form openers. People who opened a Lead Ad form. Retarget with a specific follow-up creative. Lookalike audiences Audiences built from a seed audience. Meta finds
people similar to your existing customers, engagers, or high-value segments. 1 percent lookalike of your customer list. Most similar. Smaller audience. Often the highest-performing
lookalike. 3 to 5 percent lookalike. Broader. Larger audience. Slightly less similar but still strong. 10 percent lookalike. Broadest. Use for scaling campaigns that have exhausted
narrower audiences. Exclusion audiences As important as inclusion. Exclusions prevent waste. Exclude existing customers from acquisition campaigns. You do not want to pay to
re-acquire people who have already bought. Exclude recent purchasers from retargeting. A customer who bought yesterday does not need a retargeting ad today. Exclude recent leads
from lead campaigns. Same logic. 11.5 Creative frameworks The ad creative is where most campaigns are won or lost. Meta has largely commoditized the technical side of advertising
(Advantage+, automated placements, automated budget optimization). What is left, and what matters most, is the creative. The formats that work Format Strengths Use For Short video
(15 to 30 seconds) Highest engagement format. Works across all placements including Reels and Stories. Almost every direct-response campaign. Default format for 2026. UGC

you sell and why it is for them. Educate or entertain. Ads that feel like content get the watch time and engagement that tells Meta to show them to more people. Lead with the hook.

The causes are predictable. Avoid these specifically. Scaling too aggressively. Going from 20 dollars per day to 300 dollars per day overnight triggers Meta's fraud detection.
Scale by 30 to 50 percent per day, not by 10x. Policy-violating creative. Prohibited content categories: sexualized content, weapons, drugs, before-and-after health claims,
misleading financial promises. Review Meta's Ad Policies directly, not someone else's summary. Misleading landing pages. If the ad promises something the landing page does not
deliver, Meta's automated systems will flag the mismatch. Incomplete business information. Business Portfolio missing fields or with inaccurate info. Verify everything is filled
out correctly. Multiple accounts flagged to the same identity. If a previous account was banned, any new account connected to your identity can be flagged automatically. Payment
method issues. Failed payments, cards that do not match the business name, new payment methods with no history. Meta treats these as fraud signals. Part XII: The Integrated
