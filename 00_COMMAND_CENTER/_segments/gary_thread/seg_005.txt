is designed to prevent stiffness. If it happens, something broke in the process. Policy: no refunds for delivered work. Your contract says "Photographer's total liability shall not
exceed the session fee paid." But a reshoot offer is smart business if the relationship is worth preserving. The script: "I hear you. Let's look at this together. Can you point me
to the specific images where you feel the stiffness? I want to understand what's not landing for you." If it's 1-2 images out of the set, offer to swap them for different selects
from the session at no cost. You shot more than you delivered. There are likely options they haven't seen. If it's the entire set, offer a 30-minute reshoot at no additional
charge. Frame it as: "I want to get this right. Let's do a quick reshoot. I'll adjust the direction based on what we learned from the first session." This costs you 30 minutes and
preserves a client who will either become a repeat customer or a referral source. Losing them over a refund dispute costs you far more. Never offer a refund as the first response.
Always offer a fix. 15. Instagram engagement drops 50% in Week 2. Hold the line. Two weeks of data is not enough to pivot. Algorithm fluctuations happen constantly. A 50% drop
could mean: Instagram is testing your content with a different audience, you posted at a slightly different time, or one post underperformed and dragged the average. What you do:
check which specific post underperformed and why. Was it the caption? The image? The lane? If Archive posts are dying but Frame posts are holding, lean into Frame for Week 3. If
everything dropped equally, the algorithm shifted and it will shift back. What you don't do: change the three-lane system, start posting more frequently to "feed the algorithm," or
go back to the old portfolio-only approach. The narrative shift needs 30-60 days of data before you evaluate it. Two weeks is noise. The only exception: if DMs go to zero and saves
go to zero and follower count drops, that's a content-audience mismatch, not an algorithm shift. But you'd need to see that for 3-4 consecutive weeks before acting on it. 16.
