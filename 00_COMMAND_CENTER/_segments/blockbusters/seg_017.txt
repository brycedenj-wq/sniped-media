It's amazing how surprising life can be. I knew I was in for an adventure when I joined the Harvard Business School as a newly minted PhD in 2003, but I didn't quite expect it to
be [this]{.ePub-I} thrilling. If you had told me back then that ten years later I would have been named a chaired professor, would have had the opportunity to study some of the

[Blockbusters]{.ePub-I} is the culmination of a decade-long exploration of my passion, the worlds of entertainment, media, and sports. When I first toyed with the idea of focusing
