email platform in use. It handles sending infrastructure, lead storage, campaign sequences, reply management (Unibox), and lead scraping via Super Search. Email Infrastructure
Component Detail Sending Domains 5 domains active — each with 5 inboxes = 25 total inboxes Send Limit Per Inbox 30 emails/day Total Max Send Capacity 150 emails/day across healthy
inboxes Warm-Up Running continuously on all domains via Instantly Format Plain text only — no HTML, no images, no links in body Signature Name + website only Deliverability Target
40–60% open rate — below 30% means inbox placement problem Instantly Super Search — Lead Scraping Tool Built into Instantly.ai. Pulls LinkedIn-verified professional data — direct
decision-maker emails by job title, industry, company size, and location. What it can filter by: City (not county — county-level inputs are not supported) Industry / Category Job
Title Company Size (employee range) Keywords Key limitation: Only city-level location inputs — no county, no metro area as a single entry Small suburbs and neighborhoods are not
listed independently — they fall under the parent city (e.g. Eagle Rock is under Los Angeles) Workaround: build a city list for each metro area and enter them as separate pulls or
stacked inputs Pull speed: 200–500 leads per search in roughly 15 minutes. Weekly target is 800–1,500 leads pulled. Campaign Structure in Instantly Campaigns are organized by
niche. Each campaign contains: A 3-email sequence (Day 1, Day 3, Day 7) Multiple A/B variants per sequence Assigned sending accounts from the domain pool Automated follow-up on the
same thread or new thread depending on step Campaigns are numbered sequentially (C1, C2, C3...) and each maps to a specific niche or audience segment. Unibox — Reply Management All
replies across all campaigns and all sending accounts land in one unified inbox inside Instantly called Unibox. Replies are categorized (interested, not interested, auto-reply, out
of office, etc.) and handled from there. Target response time is within 2 hours of any positive reply. 2. Lead Scraping — Options and What's in Use Option 1: Instantly Super Search
(Active — Primary) Preferred method. No middleman, no lag, no handoff. Pull leads directly inside the platform, tag them, upload to campaign, send same day. End-to-end in under an
hour. Output: LinkedIn-verified emails, job titles, company names, locations. Better deliverability than scraped lists. Closer to actual decision makers vs. generic contact pages.
Option 2: Third-Party Scraper / VA (LeadSwift — Retired) Previously used a contractor (Ren) running LeadSwift to pull leads and hand them off via Google Drive CSV. Pulled from
Google Business listings — firm names, phone numbers, front desk emails. Problems with that approach: 24–48 hour lag from pull to send 6 handoff steps before a lead hit a campaign
~20% wrong-fit leads that had to be cleaned manually No control over pace or quality Retired. Instantly Super Search replaced it entirely. Option 3: Upwork VA for Instantly
Operations A contractor can be hired to run the daily pull/tag/upload block inside Instantly. This is an option if the daily process is not running consistently. What a competent
Instantly VA needs to know: Instantly.ai navigation — campaigns, sequences, Unibox, lead upload Super Search filtering — how to set industry, title, location, company size,
keywords Basic lead hygiene — removing junk leads, checking for duplicates before upload CSV formatting for Instantly upload What they should NOT be responsible for: Writing email
copy or icebreakers Strategy or niche decisions LinkedIn activity Reply handling or booking calls Typical cost: $5–10/hr on Upwork. Roughly 5 hrs/week for the pull block. Not
urgent until the daily process proves unsustainable solo. 3. LinkedIn — Setup and Capabilities Account Element Detail Profile URL linkedin.com/in/brycedenjones Connection requests
Can send up to 10/day = 50–70/week without triggering restrictions DMs 2–3/day to accepted connections that are 48+ hours old Content posting 3x/week — managed via Buffer,
scheduled Tue/Wed/Thu 9–10am PT Engagement 5–10 substantive comments/day on prospect and peer posts How LinkedIn Connects to Cold Email LinkedIn runs as Channel 2 in parallel with
cold email. The same leads pulled via Super Search get connection requests sent on LinkedIn. Email hits volume. LinkedIn builds the relationship layer. Both happening
simultaneously compounds over time. 48-hour rule: Do not DM a new connection immediately. Wait 48 hours after they accept. Engage with their content if possible first, then DM.
LinkedIn Content 3 posts per week minimum. All posts use real photos from real shoots — no stock images, no Canva graphics, no text-on-black posts. Captions follow a 4-line
structure: bold claim, insight, system or process, CTA (only 1 in 4 posts). Engagement rule: Comment on 5–10 posts per day in target prospect clusters. Skip promoted posts and any
posts with 500+ comments. Keep comments substantive — not generic. 4. Daily Execution Flow Block Action Time Pull Instantly Super Search — filter by niche, cities, titles, company
