reviews. Core Thesis Evoto AI replaces 80% of manual retouching work with one-click AI tools while preserving natural skin texture. The value isn't that it makes retouching easier.
It makes retouching fast enough to be profitable at volume. A wedding photographer delivering 400 images or a portrait photographer running 3-4 sessions per weekend cannot spend 5
minutes per face in Photoshop. Evoto collapses that to seconds per image with AI that actually understands skin texture, facial structure, and color consistency. The workflow:
shoot, cull, global edit in Lightroom, AI retouch in Evoto, export, deliver. 1. Platform Overview Evoto is a standalone AI photo editor for portrait, wedding, commercial, and
studio photography. It handles retouching, color grading, background replacement, batch processing, and tethered shooting. Available on Windows, macOS, and iPadOS.
Subscription-based with tiered plans. Core Modules AI Portrait Retouching: Skin, face, body, hair, teeth, eyes, makeup. All AI-driven with sliders for intensity control. Color and
Tone: AI Color Match, built-in looks/presets, basic adjustments (exposure, contrast, white balance, HSL), tone curves. Background Tools: AI background removal, replacement, blur,
and sky replacement. Batch Editing: Apply retouching settings and color presets across entire sessions with one click. Evoto Instant: Tethered shooting module. Camera connects to
phone, AI processes images in real time, uploads to cloud. 2. AI Retouching Tools 2.1 Skin Blemish removal: AI detects and removes spots, acne, pimples, and dark circles. One
slider. Works on faces and body skin. Wrinkle removal: Reduces wrinkles while preserving natural skin texture. This is the key differentiator from competitors. Most retouching
software blurs skin when removing wrinkles. Evoto preserves pore-level detail. Skin tone unification: Evens out blotchy skin tones across the face and body. Corrects redness,
sallowness, and uneven sun exposure. Freckle preservation: Retouching intensity can be calibrated to remove blemishes while keeping freckles intact. Most AI retouching tools treat
freckles as blemishes. Evoto distinguishes between them. 2.2 Face Face shaping: Adjust face width, jawline, cheeks, nose, and chin. Subtle adjustments only. If the client notices
you reshaped their face, you went too far. Eye enhancement: Sharpen eyes, add catchlights, reduce red veins, remove glare from glasses. Eyes are the anchor of every portrait. This
tool makes them pop without looking artificial. Teeth whitening: Corrects and whitens teeth. Can remove braces and add natural-looking smiles. Use conservatively. Glowing-white
teeth look fake. Unify face complexion: AI analyzes the entire face and equalizes color/tone across all areas. Fixes patchy foundation, uneven blush, and mixed-lighting color
casts. 2.3 Body, Hair, and Clothing Body adjustments: AI liquify for arms, legs, waist, and posture corrections. Use for fixing unflattering angles and clothing pulls, not for
reshaping the client's body. Hair tools: Color change, detail enhancement, hairline adjustment, and gap filling. Fixes flyaway hairs and windblown mess. Clothing dewrinkle: AI
smooths wrinkles in clothing. Eliminates the need to manually clone-stamp every fabric crease. Makeup adjustment: Adjust eyebrows, eyeliner, eyeshadow, eyelashes, lip color, and
contact lenses. Apply preset makeup looks or fine-tune individual elements. 3. Color and Background Tools 3.1 AI Color Match Select a reference image (your hero edit), and Evoto
matches the color grade across your entire session. This is not a preset. It analyzes the actual color relationships in the reference image and maps them to each target image,
adjusting for different lighting conditions per frame. For multi-location shoots where lighting changed between setups, this is the fastest path to visual consistency. 3.2
Background Tools AI background removal: One-click subject isolation. Clean edge detection around hair and complex outlines. Background replacement: Swap backgrounds with studio
colors, textures, or custom images. Useful for headshot clients who need corporate-appropriate backgrounds from a location shoot. Background blur: Simulate shallow depth of field.
Apply realistic bokeh to busy backgrounds without reshooting. Sky replacement: Swap overcast or blown-out skies with natural alternatives. Edge blending matches horizon lighting.
4. Batch Editing and Presets 4.1 Batch Workflow This is where Evoto becomes a volume tool. The workflow: Edit one image fully (retouching + color + any background work). Save the
settings as a preset (or use AI Color Match with the edited image as reference). Select all remaining images from the session. Apply the preset or color match in batch. Evoto
processes them all with the same retouching intensity and color treatment. Spot-check 10-15% of the batch for edge cases (different lighting, different subject distance, different
skin tones in group shots). Export. A 200-image wedding gallery that took 6 hours of manual retouching collapses to under an hour with this workflow. 4.2 Custom Presets Save your
retouching intensity, color settings, and preferred adjustments as named presets. Create presets for different session types: one for outdoor portraits (lighter retouching, warmer
tones), one for studio headshots (heavier skin cleanup, neutral tones), one for events (minimal retouching, fast delivery). Presets can be shared with other Evoto users if you're
working with a team or second shooter. 5. Tethered Shooting (Evoto Instant) Evoto Instant connects your camera to your phone for real-time tethered shooting with AI processing.
Photos flow from camera to phone to cloud with AI retouching applied automatically. 5.1 Connection Methods Wired (USB/OTG): Direct cable from camera to phone. Type-C phones connect
directly. Older phones need an OTG adapter. Supports one camera at a time. Wireless (FTP): Camera transfers to phone over Wi-Fi via FTP. Supports multiple cameras simultaneously.
Evoto Instant displays the FTP server address and credentials. You configure your camera to connect to that server. You cannot use wired and wireless simultaneously. 5.2 Supported
Cameras Canon, Sony, Nikon, Fujifilm, Panasonic, Leica, and Olympus all support tethered connections. Camera-specific settings vary (Canon and Sony require specific USB connection
modes). Check Evoto's help docs for your specific model's setup steps. 5.3 Why This Matters for Your Workflow On-location headshot sessions where the client wants to see images
immediately. Corporate gigs where a marketing director needs to approve poses in real time. Event setups where you're delivering same-day social media assets. The AI processing
starts the moment the image hits the phone, so by the time you review it with the client, basic retouching is already applied. 6. The Operator Workflow The full pipeline from
shutter click to client delivery: Shoot the session (tethered via Evoto Instant if real-time review is needed). Cull in Lightroom or Narrative Select. Cut ruthlessly. Only selects
move forward. Global edit in Lightroom: exposure, white balance, tone curve, HSL, color grading. Sync across similar images. Export from Lightroom as full-resolution JPEGs or
TIFFs. Import into Evoto. Apply AI retouching: skin, face, eyes, teeth, hair, clothing dewrinkle. Adjust intensity per image type. Apply color match or preset across the batch.
Spot-check 10-15% of the batch. Fix edge cases manually. Export final images. Upload to Pixieset gallery. Apply watermark. Enable print store. Send delivery email. Total time for a
50-image portrait session with this workflow: under 90 minutes from import to delivery-ready files. Without Evoto, the retouching step alone takes that long. The Operating
Principle AI retouching is not cheating. It's removing the bottleneck that keeps photographers from being profitable at volume. The craft is in the direction, the lighting, and the
moment. The retouching is production work. Automate the production. Protect the craft.
