## STORY (client-faithful, do NOT re-narrow)
- LOG LINE (client): A mannequin-still girl in the cherry-print Alma Love suit moves through a luxury Beverly Hills summer day while every object around her quietly betrays her: the speaker dies, the towel is too big, the dog wins, the handcuff glints. She never reacts. The suit survives the chaos, and that is the sell.
- INTENT (client): awkward luxury paparazzi meets deadpan swimwear commercial. Comedy is always the SITUATION, never her face. The suit is the calm, readable, desirable center of a slightly-off world.
- ENGINE: object-betrayal, not "quiet ceremonies." Each beat = the world malfunctions around her; she stays mannequin-still; the suit endures. That tension IS the comedy and the sell.
- OPEN LOOP / HOOK: the lens-wipe reveal (shot 01) opens on a loud cherry suit inhabiting an off-kilter luxury world; the through-line question "why does nothing touch her" pays off at the seated-poster freeze + the brand line.
- TWO-MODE LAW (client, load-bearing): LUXURY (clean Canon, 2-4s holds, suit readable, composed) HARD-ALTERNATES with CHAOS (iPhone 0.5x wide, low aggressive, 0.3-1s inserts, paparazzi). Rhythm: clean, awkward, clean, awkward, clean. Every chaos cluster bracketed by luxury. This is the texture that makes it read as a directed film, not a slideshow.
- BRAND: Alma Love retro-Americana (1970s California editorial, Kodak Portra, warm halation); end card ALMA LOVE / DEADPAN SUMMER / LOUD SUIT. STRANGE DAY. in the suit's own ivory + warm-red.
- RUNTIME: ~38s (inside the client's 30-45s). 19 shots + end card. ASL ~1.9s. Hard cuts only.

## FOUNDATION BUILD DISCIPLINE (the layer v1 lacked; apply during GENERATE/EDIT/FINISH)
1. LOCK THE PRINT OUTSIDE THE GENERATOR: the coral-on-cream cherry feather-fan suit is a real cut-out PNG / locked keystone, composited or inpainted, never freehand-prompted per shot. One locked variable per shot. This is what killed v1 (print drift) and what the keystone-crop method proved.
2. LOCK IDENTITY: one synthetic keystone lead, every on-body frame reference-chained to it (light golden-tan olive skin, short slicked dark copper-brown wet-look crop, strong brow, red lip, DARK large 70s aviator sunglasses, gold HOOP earrings only [no other jewelry], deadpan mannequin-still). NOTE: sunglasses are DARK-lensed 70s aviators, not gold-framed (EDL line 5). v1 failed on cast fragmentation; v2 hero re-check failed 4 of 5; only consistent-keystone beats hold.
3. WIN THE STILL BEFORE MOTION: base -> realism pass (skin-texture restore + film grain + micro-sharpen, crop-verified 100%) -> approve -> only then i2v. Never animate a raw still.
4. TWO GATES, different scopes (do not conflate):
   (a) F8 GARMENT-FIDELITY GATE applies to EVERY product-visible beat (EDL product-critical list 01,03,04,06,07,09b,12,13,14,15,18 must read every locked garment rule correctly or regen; secondary wides/inserts 02,05,08,10,17 are color-family + construction strict, flat-vs-rhinestone + exact dice grade-tolerated). The suit color/construction is never "forgiven."
   (b) HERO PERFECTION BUDGET (the extra realism polish + endpoint-diff + the longest holds) concentrates on the 4 tentpoles = 01 (hook), 09 (trunk back-reveal hero), 12 (palm front hero), 18 (seated poster). Other product beats still pass F8 but get a lighter polish pass; the short chaos macros/transitions (04,05,07,08,11,13,16) are forgiving on polish, never on the no-text / no-melt / product-color rules.
5. CUT ON MOTION PEAKS, not settles (defeats the slideshow read). Authored start->end per shot; endpoint-diff gate kills any take whose only change is framing/scale (no still+push).
6. HANDS/PROPS RISK MANAGEMENT: the chaos macro inserts (04 button, 05 kick, 07 handcuff, 08 leash, 13 top, 16 cigarette) are where v1/v2 melted hands. Keep them SHORT (0.3-1s), frame the prop large + the action slow, retry-only-failed, and cut around any melt frame. If a macro cannot pass after the stop-loss attempts, dropping it is an OPERATOR-APPROVAL call (the 19-beat board is production-locked; the rhythm survives losing one insert, but no chaos beat is auto-cut at build time without sign-off).
7. ONE MEASURED MASTER GRADE: neutral-balanced (cream reads cream, suit reads coral-on-cream vs the REAL photos; do NOT crush green to magenta as v2.1 did); warm 1970s Portra halation; -14 LUFS.
8. DIEGETIC SOUND IS A GATED STAGE: the EDL Sound Plan (per-shot SFX + the broken-then-locked synth music + near-silence under chaos inserts) is built before the hostile watch. No VO.
