## 1. LOGLINE + INTENT

A mannequin-still girl in the cherry-print Alma Love suit moves through a luxury Beverly Hills summer day while every object around her quietly betrays her: the speaker dies, the towel is too big, the dog wins, the handcuff glints. She never reacts. The suit survives the chaos, and that is the sell.

Intent: awkward luxury paparazzi meets deadpan swimwear commercial. The comedy is always the situation, never her face. The suit is the calm, readable, desirable center of a slightly-off world.
