# SWIMSUIT PRODUCT ATLAS · Alma Love cherry bikini
The canonical garment spec, extracted from the REAL shoot (KEN RAWS 144 frames + EVOTO graded selects + 3 macro frames). Product truth only. The world can be stylized; this garment cannot be guessed. Source of truth for EVERY product-visible generation and grade.

## IDENTITY NOTE
The Alma garment is worn by two models in the shoot (matching suits). Confirm by figure + the turquoise/larimar silver pendant on the lead. The necklace and silver cuff bracelets are STYLING PROPS, never reproduce them as part of the swimsuit.

## COLOR (measured, the answer to coral-vs-cherry)
TRUE WARM RED. Not coral, not pink/salmon.
- Print-red anchor (dominant lines): #B84A40 (hue ~5deg, S~48%, L~49%).
- Deepest line cores: #99423E to #A8413A (hue ~3deg).
- Brightest fully-lit lines: #C25C4E to #C46758 (do not go more orange than this).
- Ivory base (warm off-white, NOT pure white): #D5CAC9 highlight down to #CFC1BE mid.
- RULE: keep Green slightly above Blue in the red (G-B ~4-12). If B exceeds G it drifts pink (wrong). If G-B passes ~25 it drifts coral/orange (wrong).
- LIGHTING CAVEAT: the KEN RAWS as-shot files carry a warm/yellow white balance that makes the red read orange (#AC7054, hue ~20deg). That is a white-balance artifact, NOT the dye. The EVOTO grade corrected it. Use the EVOTO grade and the anchors above as color truth, never the raw orange.

## PRINT / FABRIC
Lightweight stretch swim knit. A coral-to-rust feather-fan / dotted-plume swirl motif (fine dot-stippling fanning from darker spines, etched toile feel) printed on the ivory ground. NOT a solid, NOT a flat tropical leaf, NOT a snakeskin. Same print on top, bottom, and all cords.

## TOP (front)
- Classic string-triangle HALTER. Two free-hanging sliding triangle cups, unstructured (no underwire, no molding, no push-up). Cup edges finished with a thin self-fabric rolled bind.
- Deep wide-V open neckline; center is a thin connector where the triangles meet; plunges to mid-sternum.
- Thin spaghetti halter neck-tie runs up the chest to a tie at the nape, PLUS a separate thin underbust/ribcage tie linking the cups across center front and tying at the back.

## CHERRY (the most-failed detail)
- ONE rhinestone cherry pair on the wearer's RIGHT cup only (camera-LEFT in a front-on frame), upper-inner quadrant near the cleavage. LEFT cup is plain print. There is NO front-bottom cherry.
- It is a raised rhinestone-pave applique laid ON TOP of the print (print shows underneath): two red crystal-pave berry balls + a thin curved stem + a small leaf. The stem reads as a muted dark accent (a soft gray-green, NOT a bright emerald; the bright-emerald description was an overstatement corrected by pixel check).

## BOTTOM
- Low-rise, narrow high-cut cheeky front panel (inverted-triangle shield), navel fully bare, high leg openings.
- Side-tie string bottoms: a thin printed cord bow on EACH hip, tied HIGH on the hipbone, with long dangling printed tails down the outer thigh. Tail ends carry small silver metallic aglet/bead caps.

## BACK
- Two ties on top: a higher back-neck halter tie and a lower center-back band tie finishing in a small bow with two long dangling capped tails down the spine.
- Bottom is cheeky-to-brazilian (small tapered back triangle, most of the glutes bare; NOT full coverage, NOT a thin floss-thong).
- ONE paired-cherry rhinestone charm hangs at CENTER-BACK waistband, just below the back-band bow (two red rhinestone-pave berries on a stem). These are bright specular red rhinestone, not flat print.

## MACRO HARDWARE (the dice + cherry, true scale)
- DICE-BEAD STRING ENDS: EXACTLY 3 silver dice cubes stacked per tie tail, on all 4 tails (uniform). Genuine 3D engraved silver cubes (two faces visible at once), bright nickel-silver / chrome (NOT gold). Pips are recessed concave drilled dimples, classic die layout, MIXED/random values per cube (1-6 all appear; bottom cube often reads 6). Fabric threads top-to-bottom through a bore in each cube. Below the lowest cube the tie is whip-wrapped and deliberately FRAYS into a small whitish thread tuft (no clean heat-seal).
- RHINESTONE CHERRY PAIR: rhinestone applique on sheer mesh laid on top of the print; two pave berry balls, muted stem. Measured reddest cherry pixel ~#BC5446 (do not over-saturate past this).
