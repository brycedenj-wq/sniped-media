## 0. Framing (read first)
SNIPED is NOT a photography business. SNIPED is visual authority engineering / status image-making for LA founders, operators, and creatives; photography is the instrument, not the category. The **Reset is the cash wedge**, the **Direction Stack is the hidden method** behind it, the **5/6-model campaign is the proof, distribution, and launch vehicle (and the first cultural-documentation artifact)**, and **cultural documentation is the long ceiling**. This playbook turns the existing creative asset into cash in 14 days, capped, without reducing or delaying BASEPLATE.

## 1. Purpose and scope
Turn SNIPED's existing asset (7 years of reps + the recently-shot 6-model campaign) into near-term cash with hard caps. Scope: one clean offer (the Reset), a warm-network + founder-VIB outreach push, and a 3-5 shoot cap over 14 days. SNIPED stays a separate brand under Baseplate, LLC. Controlled cash engine, not a return to "being a photographer," not a merge with BASEPLATE.

## 2. 14-day activation thesis
The camera is BJ's fastest cash asset in LA, and SNIPED is not at zero. BASEPLATE's internal system is complete and its remaining work is low-time real-world steps, so a capped SNIPED lane funds the runway without meaningfully delaying BASEPLATE. The Reset is the wedge of a visual-authority practice, not a one-off photo gig.

## 3. Primary offer: SNIPED Reset (Founder/Creative Presence Session)
One offer only. A directed session run on the Direction Stack method (the hidden method, named but not given away), in the locked v3 LUXURY editorial register. No giant menu.

## 4. Buyer
LA founders, operators, and creatives in BJ's warm network (and via the campaign's reach) whose presence sits below the level of what they have built. Premium-but-attainable, not commodity headshot shoppers.

## 5. Deliverable (locked spine spec)
A directed session, 1 look, 1 location, ~45 minutes, **20 retouched finals** in the v3 LUXURY register, delivered in a private gallery, formatted for LinkedIn / IG / profile.

## 6. Price (locked spine spec)
- **Reset = $1,500.**
- **$750 non-refundable deposit** to book; balance ($750) on delivery.
- **$80/image gallery upsell** with a 48-hour expiry window (the documented post-delivery upsell).
- **No $750 commodity pricing.** The $750 is the deposit on the $1,500 Reset, not a price floor.
- **Optional first-3 at $1,000** only if velocity is genuinely needed, framed as a time-boxed "this drop" exception, then back to $1,500. A controlled exception, not the price.

## 7. Turnaround
**5 business days** is the locked Reset delivery. **48-hour delivery is an optional speed variant**, not the default (offer it as a premium rush only if it serves a specific buyer).

## 8. Exact scope
One directed session, 1 look, 1 location, ~45 minutes. Direction Stack diagnostic up front. One round of selects. 20 finals retouched in the locked register. Private delivery gallery with the $80/image upsell.

## 9. What is included
The directed session, the direction (Direction Stack method), 20 retouched finals, the private gallery (with the upsell), formats for LinkedIn/IG/profile.

## 10. What is not included
Multiple looks or locations, raw files, unlimited revisions, video, ongoing content. Those are the Operator Kit / Brand System tiers later, not the Reset.

## 11. Payment terms
**$750 non-refundable deposit** plus a booked date starts the work; **balance ($750) on delivery**; **$80/image gallery upsell** (48-hour expiry) after delivery. Run payment through Stripe or Pixieset/HoneyBook. No work begins before the deposit clears and the date is set.

## 12. Why someone buys now
They have watched the campaign dropping (live, real social proof), BJ is opening a few spots "this drop," the work is directed on a real method, and their presence does not match what they have built. The proof is visible; the spot is limited by BJ's real cap (not fake scarcity).

## 16. Operating caps (the integrity mechanism)
- **Time cap:** fixed blocks only (2-3 shoot days/week + a short daily rollout block). BASEPLATE's real-world steps and the domain re-lock keep their protected time.
- **Money goal:** $3,000-5,000 over 14 days from 3-5 Resets.
- **Shoots cap:** 3-5 in 14 days. Hard cap.
- **Rollout cap:** finish the existing campaign. Do NOT shoot a new campaign now.
- **Stop:** at the cap or 14 days, review. **Keep going** only if converting cleanly AND not eating BASEPLATE.

## 17. What to decline
Dating/profile portraits, event/nightlife coverage, school/event photography, actor headshot packages, commodity work, discount-seekers below the floor, and anything that reduces SNIPED to commodity or pulls time from the cap.

## 18. Proof loop
