- **24h loop:** make ONE spec campaign for an imaginary brand; DM 5 small brands "made this for you, want the set?"
- **7-day loop:** land 1-2 paid pilots at $1.5k; deliver; capture a (brand-credited, not you) testimonial.

- **Not yet:** don't hire, don't build a website empire before 3 paid pilots.
