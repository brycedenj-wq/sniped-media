## DOCTRINE ENTRIES (deduped)

**D-01: Spec-first doctrine**
Plan phase is highest-leverage. Execution (clicking through Notion) should be fast once the plan is locked. Specs are the product; database implementation is the last mile.

**D-02: Notion as human-facing dashboard, not brain**
Notion is a READ/WRITE surface for daily action stream and project visibility. It is a mirror, not the primary CRM. Markdown is the substrate. Airtable is the operational record. Drive is the file vault. The OS survives any single tool going down.

**D-03: Incremental activation with reject gates**
Activate 1-2 databases initially. Evaluate after 30 days under real use before activating more. If DB 1 or DB 2 unused for 14+ consecutive days by day 30, archive it and keep markdown only.

**D-04: No-automation mandate for first 30 days**
Manual card-move discipline validates flow before Zapier. Prevents brittle automation on broken process.

**D-05: 3-tier delivery hierarchy (Heroes / Selects / Proofs)**
v1 single-hero model is retired. v2 architecture is: Heroes (10-12, fully retouched), Selects (30-40, color-graded), Proofs (60-100, upgrade-gated). Proofs auto-hide at 14-day expiry. Natural gallery expiry replaces artificial time pressure.

**D-06: Upsell unbundled into three paths**
Path A (Select-to-Hero $60/image), Path B (Proof-to-Select $30/image), Path C (commercial license $250-3K). 14-day window replaces 48-hour artificial scarcity.

**D-07: Sprint sub-tier ($750) is warm-referral-only**
Warm-only, 1 look, 30 min, 5-8 heroes. Fills warm-referral lane without eroding Reset positioning. Cold inbound stays at $1,500 Reset floor.

**D-08: Edit-time ceiling as the scale lever**
3-hour edit ceiling per Reset. Above 4 hours signals retoucher hire moves up. The lever for scaling is Hero count discipline, not speed.

**D-09: 4-pass cull standard (Reject-Pick-Star-Heroes)**
Every shoot runs this sequence. Hero retouch precedence: 12-15 min each. Heroes = SNIPED-grade. Selects = production-quality. Proofs = unlock upsell path.

**D-10: Post-delivery sequence (Day 0/7/30/90)**
Day 0: v2 delivery email with 3-tier reveal. Day 7: testimonial ask (2-3 sentences, protocol correction + business deployment). Day 30: Op Kit pitch on every Reset regardless of conversion expectation. Day 90: TAM refresh on Closed Lost cards.

**D-11: Pitch discipline over expected conversion rate**
Every Reset gets pitched for Op Kit at Day 30. "Pitch discipline > expected conversion." No-silence rule: silence = mark Closed Lost, set Re-engage 90d, do not pursue.

**D-12: 3-option pricing architecture**
Reset Anchor ($1,500) / Op Kit ($3K-8K) / Brand System ($10K+). Anchor high, client picks tier. Floor holds at $1,500. Scope flexes; price does not.

**D-13: Proposal as filter, not presentation**
"What's NOT included" page signals selectivity. Case study slots stay empty until Run 1 + Op Kit work ships. No mock-ups before deposit per WWP Proclamation 9.

**D-14: Hospitality moments are structural, not bolt-on**
Day-60 archival print, quarterly handwritten notes, annual visual register review baked into Op Kit / Brand System scope. Guidara service-vs-hospitality distinction operationalized.

**D-15: Out-of-scope triggers Brand System upsell**
Broadcast, billboard, packaging, exclusive, brand ambassador use = Brand System pricing. Out-of-scope clause in Op Kit MSA establishes Brand System as the next tier.

**D-16: 12-month auto-renewal as recurring revenue mechanism**
Op Kit MSA: 12-month commercial license, auto-renewal at 30-50% of original fee unless Client opts out 30 days before renewal. License renewal date must live in Notion Op Kit Delivered view.

**D-17: Non-refundable deposit as qualification filter**
$750 non-refundable deposit confirms calendar commitment. Calendly stays tentative until deposit clears. Filters tire-kickers by behavioral confirmation.

**D-18: Copyright and likeness architecture**
SNIPED retains full copyright on all work. Client gets non-exclusive perpetual personal-use license. SNIPED gets perpetual worldwide royalty-free likeness license for portfolio/marketing/exhibitions/books.

**D-19: AI composite disclosure gap**
Contracts (Reset MSA, Collab Agreement) are silent on AI compositing in deliverables. If Composite Environment Rotation is applied to Reset galleries or collab heroes, a method-disclosure clause is required before deployment.

**D-20: Presence over mechanics in operator photography**
The goal is not technical correctness; it is operator gravity. Internal cue leads to body response. Photo critiques lead with "What's the operator state?" not "What's the pose?"

**D-21: Duchenne markers as the genuine-smile gate**
Eye-muscle engagement is the physiological signal. Forced smiles fail the operator register (reads nice-guy, not founder gravity). Fix: triggered conversation-state smile OR no-smile variant (Protocol 05, jaw-engaged gravity).

**D-22: Body angle is standing direction order**
15-30 degree offset from camera, head turned back to lens. Run before hand/shoulder positioning. Asymmetry signals intentionality. Square posture = ID-badge reading. Applied doctrine from Protocol 03.

**D-23: Hands are the second registration point**
Tension in hands telegraphs uncertainty. Three solutions: crop tight shoulders-up, hand on shoulder, hands in pockets or lapels. Images where hands show grip/tension fail the "strongest photograph" gate. Protocol 01.

**D-24: Transition Freeze as a trust-damage protocol**
Profile photos capturing motion between intentional frames (half-smile, mid-turn, in-motion hands) are processed as "screenshots," not portraits. Root cause: reuse of event-photography burst frames. Reset is the only fix. Protocol 08.

**D-25: VIB (Visual Identity Brief) mechanics**
Side-by-side diagnostic comparing current visual register to aspirational register. Wardrobe continuity across panels keeps the conversation on REGISTER, not styling. Failure mode checklist: no generic compliments, no multi-CTA, under 80 words, no em-dashes.

**D-26: Protocol classification drives outreach scripting**
Protocol 09 (Good Mechanics, Zero Presence) is the diagnostic label for cold outreach to operators whose photos are technically correct but lack contextual authority. Script B (cold with trigger event) vs Script C (Loom when event is less than 14 days imminent).

**D-27: Loom-gated follow-up sequence**
Email 1 contains no pitch. Pitch is deferred to Loom + Email 2. No company-name personalization at volume (fragility at scale). Merge variables: first name + company size only. 70-90 words, plain text, one CTA.

**D-28: Direction Stack audio voice doctrine**
Lowercase casual register. Talk like showing a friend something on a phone. No apologizing, no hedging, no "if I may." Stay in field. Land on status, not utility. Slight imperfection signals human-authored.

**D-29: Automation ceiling and the human-stylus 5% boundary**
Gates 1-6, 8-9 on hero composites are automatable via physics (ceiling_v3.py). Gate 5 (acrylic heel refraction, toe rendering, contact shadow, re-grain) cannot be automated. Requires hand Photoshop. ~15 min for a trained retoucher. Phone-scale credibility is the pass criterion.

**D-30: Platform mastering is a distinct step from composite approval**
Each platform (Story, Feed, Square, Deck, Proof) requires intentional recompose or crop. Not one flat master exported everywhere. Subject color grading locks at composite stage. Skin-drift numeric test is mandatory: uniform delta = PASS, uneven delta = FAIL.

**D-31: Proof-only gate on client delivery**
No high-res, no finals, no retouch, no heroes, no internal files sent at proof stage. Client confirm required before hero pass. TEST vs REAL separation enforced via instance folder isolation.

**D-32: Lane manifest CSV pattern**
Yellow/Green/Purple + A/B split (Lane A: warm editorial, Lane B: neutral/product) across folder contract: 02_selects to 04_graded to 03_client_review. Repeatable architecture across all future shoots.

**D-33: Dual-register content rule**
HERO posts remain full v3 luxury color (moment). Chapter Cards render B&W (document). Not interchangeable. Apparatus stays color per Aperture/LIFE/Magnum tradition.

**D-34: Evaluation-then-activation discipline for creative concepts**
Route > taste-check > lineage-check > ecosystem-check > size/kill > verdict (world/product/campaign/film/content-system/nothing). Default verdict = NOTHING until gates pass.

**D-35: LinkedIn POV calibration (locked)**
2 posts per week, Tuesday/Thursday, 200-400 word target, editorial voice, methodology-first not pitch-first. Named-client case study loop. Caption length 150-300 words optimal per live scrape data.

**D-36: Shadow-test gate is mandatory**
Before revenue activation on any warm opportunity: market-eval + pre-launch validation scorecard. No breadth outreach until scene-density cluster meets threshold.
