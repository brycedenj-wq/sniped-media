# AXIS , CONCEPT + SYMBOL SYSTEM (the depth the first run lacked)

## The idea (not a mood: a tension)
AXIS is the last keeper of a house that remembers what its family chose to forget. The house is not a building; it is an archive of inheritance. He moves through it cataloguing what the bloodline buried. Tension: to keep the house he must read what it remembers about him.

## The symbol system (this is what was missing)
- MARK: a single embossed sigil , a meridian line bisecting a square (the "AXIS" line). Appears as a blind deboss, never colored.
- RECURRING OBJECT: an oxblood-edged ledger (the house's memory). The ONE oxblood accent per frame is always this object or its trace.
- MOTIF: a sealed aperture (a doorway that remembers). The arch is not decor; it is a threshold between what is kept and what is forgotten.
- GESTURE: a gloved hand opening/closing the ledger.

## Color law (single accent = the ledger)
Charcoal / bone / graphite neutral. Oxblood appears ONLY on the ledger edge or wax seal. Nothing else carries color. This makes the symbol legible.

## Copy system (tied to image, not mood)
- Title: AXIS
- Line: THE HOUSE REMEMBERS
- Symbol caption: "the ledger keeps what the family buried"
- Chapter mark: I , THE KEEPER
- No em-dashes. In-world voice. Each line must point at something we SEE (the ledger, the seal, the threshold).

## Shot taxonomy required (kills the repetition)
1. beauty/face (tension, eye contact)
2. still-life: the oxblood ledger on bone surface
3. detail/macro: the deboss mark + wax seal
4. wide environmental: figure dwarfed (Blender-true)
5. gesture: gloved hand on the ledger
6. threshold: figure at the sealed aperture
