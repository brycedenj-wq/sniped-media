### BOOK 1: Prompt Template - In Context-2.pdf

**Coverage:** 1/1 segments complete.

**Thesis:** Few-shot prompting is a pattern-replication engine: show two domain-matched examples before any task and the model replicates the structure, tone, and format without further instruction.

**Densest Frameworks/Lessons:**

1. Minimum-two-example threshold: one example sets a tone; two establish a pattern the model generalizes from.
2. Task instruction placed after examples (not before) primes in-context learning more reliably.
3. Domain-matching is mandatory: customer-service examples must match customer-service register; analysis examples must match analysis register. Cross-domain examples break replication.
4. Identical structural template works across domains: context → example 1 → example 2 → task. The container is stable; only the content swaps.
5. Pattern works for tone, length, vocabulary register, and output format simultaneously.
6. No chain-of-thought or explicit instruction needed when examples carry enough format signal.

**SNIPED Application:**
Direction Stack shot sequences, caption voice, LinkedIn comment register, and VIB outreach DMs can all be few-shot-templated internally. Compress 2 known-good examples of each into a master prompt stub per output type. Team members or subagents run the stubs; output voice stays consistent without constant founder review. Also useful for casting call messaging (tone is very specific) and post-shoot delivery language.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine:**
No contradiction. Additive only. Extends the "connected toolchain default" and "max-by-default" rules: internal few-shot templates are a low-build, high-leverage production accelerant. Does NOT promote any new framework. Acts as an implementation primitive underneath existing workflows.

### BOOK 2: Machiavelli, Discourses on Livy (Dover, 2007) - 21/21 segments complete

**Coverage:** Full text read. All three books (Rome's foundations, expansion/empire, military doctrine), including conspiracy chapter, renewal theory, and final institutional mechanics.

**Thesis:** Durable institutions are built by founders who create self-renewing structures, not by rulers who substitute personal virtue for architecture; and the highest governance art is knowing when to use fraud, force, patience, or humanity based on the structural moment, not preference.

**Densest Frameworks/Lessons (8 most load-bearing):**

1. **Founding Concentration, Distributed Maintenance.** One founder sets the architecture under near-absolute authority; once set, the multitude maintains it better than the individual. Single-mode governance past initialization = tyranny.

2. **Institutional Renewal Rhythm.** 10-year maximum between visible enforcement events (laws executed, examples made) or corruption spreads. Florence refreshed every 5 years. Missing renewal cycles = rot, not stability.

3. **No Middle Path.** The middle course is consistently the most dangerous option in Machiavelli: neither integrating nor destroying enemies, neither surrendering fully nor seizing power, neither releasing nor executing captives. Every case study vindicates extremity over moderation in crisis moments.

4. **Corruption as Irreversible State Change.** Light corruption is treatable via reform or exemplary punishment. Total corruption requires a single virtuous dictator (temporary fix) or collapse. Once the people are corrupted, no law works because corrupted people will weaponize good institutions for bad ends.

5. **Pressure-Release vs. Suppression.** Formalized accusation channels (tribunes, legal impeachment) prevent violent rupture. Calumny (private rumor without accountability) destroys careers and institutions. Every organization needs a mechanism for formal grievance distinct from private faction.

6. **Merit Over Credential, Necessity as Accelerant.** Merit-based systems open ranks faster than comfort-based systems. Necessity (closed doors, no escape) generates maximum commitment. Opening escape routes saps resolve. Casting doctrine and production discipline are the organizational expression of this.

7. **Intelligence Timing is Determinant.** In mutual stalemate, the side that learns the opponent's true condition first wins. Command art = divining intent before the opponent reveals it. Information advantage converts comparable situations into decisive victories.

8. **Property Seizure Creates Permanent Grievance.** Depriving people of an advantage they already possess (distributed spoils, promised equity, earned credit) triggers daily-renewed resentment. Denying future hypothetical gain is forgettable; clawing back actual possession is permanent poison.

**Additional frameworks worth noting:** Alliance entrapment via apparent equality (Rome/Latin model), Religion-as-compliance-apparatus (internalized rules bypass enforcement cost), Reputation via distinctive personal action (singular acts outweigh accumulated ordinary achievement), Discipline over spectacle (Rome's three-division depth vs. flat-line modern armies), Fraud doctrine (fraud against trusting parties never glorious; feints against distrusting opponents are legitimate), Momentum collapses deliberation (audacity + presence outperforms permission-seeking), Household culture cascades generationally (education stamps family/institutional ethos), Coerced agreements are null at first pressure relief.

**SNIPED Application:**

- SNIPED_OS is Machiavelli's "founding concentration, distributed maintenance" model. Bryce is in initialization mode; the architecture (CANONICAL_TRUTHS, THE_SPINE, Direction Stack, casting doctrine) is the foundation. Execution reps compound it; new frameworks violate it.

- The three-engine structure (Revenue/Audience/Reputation) parallels Rome's three-division military depth (hastati/principes/triarii). If one front breaks, the others absorb and renew. Flat single-product businesses collapse on first defeat.
- Casting call doctrine (two-strike rule, 48-hr confirms, wardrobe gate) is the "closed door" that triggers maximum commitment. Open-door casting produces the Oddeschi collapse.
- Quiet luxury editorial restraint maps directly to authority-via-restraint doctrine: rulers perceived as minimally ambitious toward dominion are most readily surrendered to. The less SNIPED signals hunger for clients, the more it reads as authoritative.

### EVALUATION GATES

1. **Middle-Path Gate:** Before finalizing any partnership, pricing, or creative compromise: is this full integration, full exclusion, or the dangerous middle? The dangerous middle is always flagged for explicit review.

2. **Property-Seizure Gate (Spoils Gate):** Before any compensation or deliverable structure: have we committed to anything we might need to reverse? If yes, the commitment is either honored upfront or renegotiated before signing.

3. **Renewal-Deficit Gate:** Monthly: which doctrine has not been demonstrated by an actual rep in the past 10 months? Not documented. Demonstrated.

4. **Intel-Timing Gate:** Before any major pitch or VIB approach: what is the target's current condition (capacity, recent activity, distress signals)? Never pitch into the unknown.

5. **Corruption-Threshold Gate (Team Scaling):** Before bringing on any contractor or team member: is this role structured with visible advancement, public accountability, and no private-patron pathway? If private loyalty could accumulate, the structure is flagged.

6. **Few-Shot Quality Gate:** Before any batch output (captions, DMs, briefs) from a template: do both examples in the stub match domain tone/format exactly? If not, the stub is rebuilt before running.

### DECISION UPGRADES

1. **Hiring and team structure:** Rotation-mandatory titles, time-bounded authority, no secret mentoring lanes. Rome's solution to ingratitude was many captains with mutual-check structure, not one loyal lieutenant. SNIPED's equivalent: plural operators with public accountability.

2. **When to enforce vs. temporize:** Machiavelli gives a precise rule: temporize only on slow-burn disorders that can die naturally. Force-cure only when the disorder is acute and your capability to cure is clear. Half-hearted force accelerates the harm. Applies to casting cuts, client disputes, creative direction conflicts.

3. **Pitch and outreach momentum:** Create conditions where delay costs more than acceptance (Machiavelli's hardihood doctrine). Compressed offer timelines, surprise presence (event appearances, warm intro drops), and urgency framing collapse deliberation. Do not wait for permission to enter a scene.

4. **Partnership vetting:** Character locks across time. Past behavior is predictive at the organizational level. Validate partner track record in analogous situations before trusting novel commitments.

5. **Creative scope discipline:** Defend only strategic territory. Abandon non-critical fronts without reputation loss. The pitched battle (Direction Stack book, lineage-anchored work) is worth full force. Skirmishes (random commissions, low-signal collabs) should be declined or won with certainty, not attempted with doubt.

6. **Novel counsel framing:** When introducing a new process or framework inside SNIPED, frame it as one perspective, not sole advocacy. Let the system own the decision. Safety paradox: quiet counsel gains credibility when other approaches fail; confident sole-advocacy loses it.
