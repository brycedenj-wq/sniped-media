# WORLD THESIS , MERIDIAN HOUSE
A monumental, quiet world of bone-white plaster and raw concrete, raking north light, one solitary figure (AXIS) who keeps a house that remembers what the family buried. The ledger (oxblood-edged, wax-sealed) is the house's memory; the arch is a threshold between kept and forgotten. Restraint over spectacle. The pitch to a brand: this is the depth and consistency we build for YOUR world , a complete visual system, not a pretty room.
Color law: charcoal/bone/graphite neutral; oxblood is the ONLY accent and it lives on the ledger/seal.
