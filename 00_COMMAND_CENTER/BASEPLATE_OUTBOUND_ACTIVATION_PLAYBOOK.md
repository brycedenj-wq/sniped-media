## 1. Purpose and scope
Turn the outbound docs into one runnable engine: how a stranger becomes a lead, a reply, a booked call. Scope is lead-finding and first contact only. The call, proposal, and delivery live in their own artifacts (#3, #4, #6). This is internal system-building, not execution.

## 2. Coverage vs activation
The outbound method was already "covered" (chunked across docs). This playbook is the activation: the assembled, runnable version. Each piece pulls the best source on its topic (ICP from WEDGE_OFFER_SPEC, infrastructure from The_Outbound_Stack, sequence from the Instantly doc), so the system encodes the best available craft, not generic cold-email advice.

## 3. Phased execution
- **Phase 1 (warm/manual proof):** the first market action when BJ chooses to go. BJ personally sends a small batch of targeted, warm LinkedIn DMs + direct emails to owners on the admin list, books calls, learns what lands. No infrastructure needed. Defined and runnable now; **not triggered by writing this doc.**
- **Phase 2 (Instantly cold-engine setup, in parallel):** the documented setup to stand up cold infrastructure (domains, inboxes, warmup) while Phase 1 runs. **This doc buys no domains and launches no campaigns.** It defines the procedure so the engine is ready when proof justifies volume. Phase 2 never blocks Phase 1.
- **Phase 3 (scale):** only after Phase 1 produces real reply/call proof. Cold volume turns on once the message is proven to convert, not before.

## 4. Money-first activation ranking
1. Warm/manual outreach (Phase 1): fastest to a real conversation, zero infra, BJ-run.
2. Admin lead list (Artifact #2): fills the funnel, delegable.
3. Cold-engine setup (Phase 2): leverage, warms in the background.
4. Scale (Phase 3): only on proof.

## 5. ICP search criteria (from WEDGE_OFFER_SPEC §3)
- **Segment A, critical-facilities staffing/recruiting firms (lead first):** titles Owner / Founder / President / VP Recruiting / Head of Talent / Head of Employer Brand; size ~10-150; placing data-center / critical-facilities techs, commissioning agents, MEP roles; one placement worth $20-45K.
- **Segment B, specialty subcontractors (parallel):** titles Owner / President / VP Business Development / Preconstruction / Estimator-Proposal lead; size ~15-300; electrical, mechanical, commissioning, cooling, networking, field service; bidding into data-center / mission-critical work.
- **Geo:** LA/SoCal first, then dense buildout metros (Northern VA, Phoenix, Dallas, Atlanta, Columbus).

## 6. Instantly Super Search filter plan
- Title contains: owner, founder, president, "VP recruiting", talent, "business development", preconstruction, estimator.
- Industry: Staffing & Recruiting; Construction (electrical/mechanical); Facilities Services.
- Keywords: "data center", "critical facilities", "mission critical", "commissioning", "MEP", "electrical contractor", "mechanical contractor".
- Company size: 10-300. Geo as above. Verified-email only. Export one campaign list per segment.

## 7. Lead list structure (one row per firm)
Company · Decision-maker name · Title · LinkedIn URL · Verified email · Email provider (Gmail/Outlook, for provider-matching) · Location · Segment (staffing/sub) · Urgency signal · Source · Notes.

## 8. First 100 lead plan
Super Search builds ~100 (≈70 staffing / 30 subs) to admin enriches each row and tags provider + urgency to split into Gmail-recipient and Outlook-recipient pools. The list exists as inventory; it is only fed to the cold engine in Phase 3, after Phase 1 proof and after warmup.

## 9. First 25 manual/warm outreach plan
BJ personally (no infra, no warmup): 25 warm LinkedIn DMs to owners on the admin's first batch, plus a handful from his normal email. This is Phase 1, the cheapest real signal. Runnable the moment BJ decides to go; this doc does not start it.

## 10. Email sequence (3-email; The_Outbound_Stack architecture + WEDGE_OFFER_SPEC §9 copy)
Plain text, lowercase 2-3 word subject, outcome-first, under 80 characters per sentence, human tone.
- **E1 (day 1):** value-prop opener. Staffing subj "your reqs"; body = WEDGE §9 staffing email 1 (an open role costs you weekly, I build the dossier that makes top techs choose you, pays for itself on one hire, worth 15 min). Subs subj "your bid"; body = WEDGE §9 subs email 1 (when you and three subs look identical on paper, price decides; I make you the credible one).
- **E2 (day 3):** context + free micro-offer (a quick "candidate-view teardown" of how the firm shows up), no new pitch.
- **E3 (day 6):** low-friction status-alignment close ("worth 15 minutes, or not a priority right now?") + Calendly link.

## 11. Follow-up sequence
WEDGE_OFFER_SPEC §9 follow-ups, spaced 48-72 hours, max 3 touches, then stop. Break-up = "I will leave the door open; when a hard bid or a stuck req comes up, that is the moment this earns its keep."

## 12. LinkedIn pairing
For warm targets: connect + DM (WEDGE §9 DM) in parallel with email. LinkedIn is channel 1 for warm, Instantly is channel 2 for volume. No public posting required (posting is Artifact #7, BJ-timed).

## 13. Reply classification (to action)
- **Positive** ("interested / send the example / book"): BJ books the call within 2 hours.
- **Objection** ("we have marketing" / "send info" / "security"): BJ answers with WEDGE §7 objection lines, offers the 60-second sample.
- **Neutral / not now:** log Yellow, set a follow-up date.
- **Negative / unsubscribe:** remove, never re-contact.
- **Bounce:** admin removes (keep bounce under 3%).

## 14. Proof-log update rules (PROOF_LOOPS §6 + WEDGE §12)
One Proof entry per real conversation under H1/H2 + "infra-buyer pull (staffing)/(subs)"; fields: price, paid y/n, named role/bid, room-opened y/n. Real proof = money committed + a named role/bid. "Looks great, send info" = fake interest, logged Yellow. Every send and reply is logged the same day.

## 15. BJ handoff rules
The instant any reply lands or a call books, it is BJ's. Every conversation, pitch, objection, and close is BJ, non-delegable. Admin never touches a live thread.

## 16. Admin stop rules
Admin stops at: list building, enrichment, provider/urgency tagging, CRM hygiene, proof-log data entry, sample-asset organization. Admin never contacts, DMs, emails, pitches, or writes copy. Full scope in Artifact #2.

## 17. Daily cadence (when running; from CONTROL_ROOM §6)
BJ 45-60 min: send the day's warm quota, reply to all inbound within 2 hours, run booked calls with the WEDGE §10 script, send proposals within 24 hours, log everything. Admin: enrich the next batch, CRM hygiene. Body/BJJ and day-job performance stay non-negotiable.

## 18. Quality guardrails
Verified emails only, decision-makers only, plain-text human copy, no spam-trigger words, cap 30 sends per inbox per day, provider-match Gmail to Gmail. Deliverability targets: open 40-60%, reply 3-5%, positive >1%, bounce <3%, spam <0.1%. Kill a segment if positive-reply stays under 1% after real volume. Never fabricate proof.

## 19. Do-not-contact rules
- The employer's (Serverfarm's) own clients, vendors, or employees.
- Anyone reached via inside knowledge or any employer list.
- Generic info@ / sales@ addresses (decision-makers only).
- Bounced or unsubscribed contacts.

## 20. Proof gates (from WEDGE_OFFER_SPEC §12)
- **T1 (staffing):** at least 1 staffing firm pays a Pilot or signs a paid LOI. Fail: 0 paid after 5 real conversations.
- **T2 (subs):** at least 1 sub pays a Pilot or a concrete LOI tied to a live bid.
- **T3 (room-opening):** at least 1 decision-maker shown the sample says "this would help me win [bid] / fill [role]" AND grants a non-secure shoot, intro, or bid-use commitment.
Compliments without money or access = not proof.

## 21. Do-not-overbuild rule
No n8n, no Twilio/RetellAI callers, no AI agents, no extra CRM beyond Pixieset Studio Manager, no cold volume before warm proof. Cold infrastructure is leverage, not proof. The only thing that validates the engine is replies, calls, and paid pilots.
