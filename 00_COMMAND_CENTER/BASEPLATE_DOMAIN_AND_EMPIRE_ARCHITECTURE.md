## 6. Domain strategy

**Naming/domain decision (OS_NAME_FOUNDRY_PASS, re-locked 2026-05-27): KEEP BASEPLATE, flagship primary = `baseplateworks.com`.** The name stays. A re-locked name pass live-checked ~190 names (dictionary, coined, structural-architecture, fortress, the `-plate` lineage, joinery, power/data-center jargon, and metaphor fields) and found only four weak standalone `.com`s open (mountplate, loadplane, ballasto, smeltco). No standalone beats BASEPLATE, and the OS naming canon (MAYA, Made-to-Stick, the brand-name-vs-register tension principle) independently confirms BASEPLATE as the best name. The exact-match `baseplate.com`/`.co` are taken by third parties (1997 / 2018). So the flagship is a BASEPLATE-qualified `.com`, with the wordmark held as BASEPLATE: `baseplateworks.com`.

**Domain standard (locked):**
- `.com` first.
- `.co` acceptable only if exceptional.
- `.systems`, `.studio`, `.press`, `.build`, and other alternate TLDs are NOT acceptable as the flagship primary. They may be redirects or experiments only.

- **Flagship primary:** `baseplateworks.com` (a BASEPLATE-compound `.com`; the public wordmark stays BASEPLATE, the qualifier lives only in the URL). Register it; set it as the flagship and the canonical. Chosen over the other available compounds (`baseplatehq.com`, `baseplategroup.com`, `baseplateindustries.com`, `baseplateops.com`, `baseplatefoundry.com`) because "works" reads operator/industrial with no SaaS tell, where "hq/ops" read SaaS-y and "group/industries" read heavier or longer. It keeps the BASEPLATE name and satisfies the `.com` standard without a weak alt-TLD flagship.
- **Canonical URL:** `https://baseplateworks.com/`.
- **baseplate.systems:** demoted to an optional owned redirect only (301 to `baseplateworks.com`), NOT the flagship, never marketed. Registering it is optional.
- **baseplate.studio:** keep it (owned), 301-redirect to `baseplateworks.com`, never marketed. Creative-coded; never shown to an infra buyer.
- **Do not chase `baseplate.com` / `.co` / `.build`** from their current owners pre-revenue (taken since 1997 / 2018 / 2024; costly and unnecessary).
- **snipedmedia.com / sniped.media:** do NOT redirect to the infra site. Keep for the photography track and working email.
- **Personal domain:** not required now. Named-author presence lives on LinkedIn + the publication under Baseplate. Reserve the personal name only if cheap and available.
- **Future media/network domain:** not yet. Earned, not pre-bought.
- **Do not buy:** clothing domains, `.press`, platform/registry/"verified-operator" domains, or a spread of speculative TLDs. One clean flagship (`baseplateworks.com`) is the whole job.

- **Too small / service-coded:** a "photographer/agency" framing caps it at lifestyle income.
- **Too vague / platform-coded:** claiming "platform/registry/network" now reads as vapor to a procurement buyer and destroys trust.
- **Too media/photography-coded:** `.studio`, "Media, LLC," charcoal+gold editorial, or photography language re-triggers the reduction. (The live risk.)

- **Too many domains:** `.studio` + `.co` + `.press` + sniped + personal, all live, confuses buyers and dilutes trust.
- **Confusing the buyer:** a "media studio" lander promises creative; a "platform" lander promises software. Either mismatch kills the dossier sale.
- **Confusing future systems:** the committed docs already disagree (entity name; palette across two bibles; old BASEPLATE = photography holding co). Unresolved, every future routing decision inherits the contradiction. This lock record exists to end that.
