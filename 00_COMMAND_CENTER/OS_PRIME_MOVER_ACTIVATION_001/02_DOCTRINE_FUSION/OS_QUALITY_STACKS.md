# OS QUALITY STACKS , what "excellent" means per domain, and the gate that enforces it
| domain | doctrine loaded | excellent | gate (auto) | self-correct |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| copy | Ogilvy/Hopkins/Schwartz, positioning, WWP | complete thought, one big idea, in-world, no hype | os_doctrine:copy (deterministic) | fix strips hype/em-dash; fragments+bible flagged for rewrite |
| visual/image | luxury-editorial, photo theory, strongest-photo | beats a camera frame, one-color discipline | os_postproduction_gate + os_doctrine:visual | regenerate/grade until SHIP |
| video/motion | photo theory, dual-register | intent per frame, color holds in motion | os_motion_qa + os_doctrine:motion | re-shoot/re-cut |
| editing | pacing/edit (video-use hard rules) | rhythm, clean audio, caption-safe | os_motion_qa + video-use rules | re-pace |
| sound | restraint, deliberate hits | room tone + one hit, not wallpaper | taste gate | re-brief |
| layout/design | environment rotation, the kit | owned, legible, intentional, no filler | os_doctrine:layout | rebuild |
| offer/pricing | pricing logic, new luxury, status, WWP | values not cost, scarcity/anchor, proof-first | os_doctrine:pricing_offer | reframe |
| trust/client | trust equation, hospitality, hardest-to-say-no | premium, hard to say no, privacy-clean | os_doctrine:trust + os_privacy_gate | re-cut the package |
| world/3D | lineage, scene density, status | ownable mark, specific, faceless, real geometry | os_world + os_blender_gate | re-spec |
| safety | possibility-engine, payment-follows-proof | no leak, faceless, nothing irreversible | os_privacy_gate | block + flag |
The rule: a domain's output cannot ship until its stack passes. "Good enough" is caught there and then.
