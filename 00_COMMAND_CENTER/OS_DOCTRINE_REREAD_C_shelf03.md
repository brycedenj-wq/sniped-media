### BOOK 1: To Kill a Mockingbird (Harper Lee)

**Coverage line:** 15/15 segments, full novel end-to-end.

**1-line thesis:** Moral authority is built from the inside of a scene, demonstrated through invisible consistency, and proven by what you do when no audience is watching.

**Densest frameworks and lessons:**

1. **Lineage as operating architecture.** Simon Finch to Atticus to Scout. Identity is relational and inherited before choice. The work documents from within the lineage, not from tourist distance. The novel opens with genealogical depth for a reason: context precedes character.

2. **Reputation is collective myth, not personal claim.** Boo Radley is a phantom constructed by town consensus. He never asserts himself. SNIPED parallel: the apparatus (Card system, composite environments, dual-register) installs identity the same way. Repetition over time writes the legend.

3. **Scene density over breadth.** Maycomb is 20 miles from Finch's Landing, same size for 100 years. The novel thickens this one cluster for 300 pages. Not a state, not a region. One county. This is the exact argument for SNIPED's LA founder circle strategy.

4. **Invisible acts carry the most weight.** Boo's gifts (soap dolls, pennies, the blanket, the sewn pants) are given with zero audience. Atticus's vigil at the jail is not performed. The work that no one watches is the work that proves character.

5. **Trust via particularity, not universality.** Scout breaks the mob by naming Cunningham's son, referencing his entailment. Generic appeals to humanity fail. Hyper-specific, scene-rooted human contact works. This is the VIB method's theoretical spine.

6. **Both/and framing: loss plus progress coexist.** Miss Maudie reframes Tom's conviction as a baby-step because the jury deliberated for hours rather than minutes. Defeat plus moral advance are not mutually exclusive. The principle decouples from the win condition.

7. **Mercy as the deeper law.** Sheriff Tate's "Ewell fell on his own knife" is not corruption; it is the narrative that honors the actual hero without destroying him. Exposure is not always justice. Visibility can destroy the innocent.

8. **Code-switching as load management, not dishonesty.** Calpurnia's dual register (Blackstone formal English vs. church dialect) is validated by the text: "It's not necessary to tell all you know." World varies; identity holds. This is SNIPED's LinkedIn vs. Instagram split stated as a lived doctrine.

**SNIPED application:** The novel is the single best literary proof of the Lineage Doctrine, scene-density thinking, invisible-work-as-moat, and the dual-register rule. It validates trust mechanics (T = C+R+I / S) via Atticus's courtroom behavior, hospitality layer via Boo's gifts and Maudie's cakes, and the photography-as-witness thesis via the "made vs. taken" distinction (Berger). The mockingbird principle extends beyond innocence to the destructive power of exposure itself: not every truth should be broadcast.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:** Confirms lineage doctrine, scene-density, repetition over novelty, dual-register, proof over packaging. Adds one nuance not explicit in existing memory: **mercy can supersede transparency**. The current OS defaults to "proof on disk = real." The novel argues that some proof should remain invisible to protect the prover. This does not contradict the OS but adds a calibration: not all proof is for publication. File this under proof-handling discretion.

### BOOK 2: Made to Stick (Chip Heath and Dan Heath)

**Coverage line:** 14/14 segments, full book plus index.

**1-line thesis:** Ideas survive and spread not because they are true but because they are simple, unexpected, concrete, credible, emotional, and narrative-shaped; the Curse of Knowledge is the primary enemy of communication.

**Densest frameworks and lessons:**

1. **SUCCESs framework (6 principles).** Simple (core plus compact, not just short), Unexpected (schema violation plus post-dictable resolution), Concrete (sensory hooks, Velcro memory theory), Credible (antiauthority beats expert, Sinatra Test beats credentials), Emotional (identity appeal beats self-interest, Mother Teresa effect, higher Maslow layers), Stories (flight simulators, three plot types: Challenge, Connection, Creativity).

2. **The Curse of Knowledge.** Once you know something, you cannot un-know it. Experts mistake "nuanced" for "accurate." The tapper/listener experiment: tappers predict 50% success, achieve 2.5%. Experts bury the lead because they cannot recall what it is like not to know. Every SNIPED external artifact is at risk of this. "Lineage doctrine," "scene density," "apparatus layer" are insider shorthand that outsiders cannot simulate mentally.

3. **Commander's Intent as simple core.** One crystalline north star guides 1,000 decisions without supervision. Southwest's "THE low-fare airline." Hoover Adams's "Names, names, and names." Herb Kelleher's core resolves flight attendant chicken-salad decisions instantly. SNIPED needs this per quarter, per lane.

4. **Generative metaphor beats sound bite.** Disney's "cast members" generates behavior guidance across auditions, costumes, breaks, and street-sweeper training. "Sandwich artists" generates nothing. The metaphor must predict downstream behavior in situations you haven't discussed yet.

5. **Gap theory of curiosity.** Curiosity equals knowledge gaps plus pain. Open the gap before closing it. Question-first beats fact-first. Mystery arcs sustain attention longer than one-time surprise. This is the architecture for the B&W Card episodic release: audience doesn't know the 5 lineages, discovery arc builds engagement across posts.

6. **Story as cognitive simulation.** Readers build spatial mental models. Event-simulation (replay how a problem arose) outperforms outcome-simulation (visualize victory). 63% of audience remembers stories; 5% remembers statistics. Stories embed expertise invisibly and defeat the Curse of Knowledge. The Jared/Subway case passes all six SUCCESs tests; "7 Under 6" passes none.

7. **Identity over isolated self-interest.** James March's two decision models: calculate consequences vs. ask "What do people like me do?" The second model dominates. "Don't Mess with Texas" worked via identity (Texans don't litter), not health messaging. 73% message recall in months; 72% litter decline in 5 years. SNIPED's buyer is not buying photography; they are buying confirmation of who they are.

8. **Spotting beats creating.** Three universal plot templates (Challenge, Connection, Creativity) are lenses for recognizing sticky ideas already present in the world. Train your eye (Core Idea Glasses) to spot them in daily work. Jared didn't require creative genius; it required defenders at each handoff point.

**SNIPED application:** Made to Stick is the operating manual for every SNIPED external artifact. Positioning language must pass the Curse of Knowledge gate. Case studies must be story-first, not stat-first. LinkedIn copy must activate identity appeal ("founders like you document their lineage"), not feature appeal. The dual-register rule (HERO color moment vs. Card B&W document) is a concrete/abstract split the book validates. The Sinatra Test applies to composite proof: show the hardest-case founder (AI-skeptic, high-value brand) convinced by the process, and credibility radiates across every other use case. The "three whys" drill converts abstract mission language into visceral emotional core.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:** No contradiction. Confirms Repetition over Novelty (Velcro memory), scene density (Saddleback Sam persona as decision filter for 50k members), proof over packaging (testable credentials beat authority claims). Adds two sharpenings: (1) Analytics-first messaging actively suppresses emotion (Carnegie Mellon charity study: stats + story = less donation than story alone). SNIPED must not lead with metrics even when they're strong. (2) Story sequence controls belief, not just story content (jury verdict study: 31% vs. 78% conviction with identical facts, different narrative order). The order in which SNIPED tells a story matters as much as the content.

### BOOK 3: Your First 1000 Copies (Tim Grahl)

**Coverage line:** 5/5 segments (segment 5 was endnotes only, no content).

**1-line thesis:** Obscurity is harder to monetize than imperfection; build an owned permission asset (email list) first, then deliver relentless helpfulness through it, and conversion follows without manipulation.

**Densest frameworks and lessons:**

1. **Permission beats reach.** Email list is the moat. 1% social read rate vs. near-100% email read rate. You own email; platforms own your social following and can block export or algorithm-throttle reach. The "holey bucket" problem: traffic arrives but no mechanism retains it without a permission vessel.

2. **5-phase connection system.** Permission (email list, RSS, owned channel) → Content (utility plus voice, evergreen assets) → Outreach (amplification via one-to-one influencer contact and one-to-many fan broadcasting) → Sell (direct ask, story-as-disarmer, co-creation, auto-responder) → System (feedback loop, analytics north star: total book sales).

3. **Specific offer language drives signup.** Jean Chatzky's "Jean tells you what the week's headlines mean for YOUR wallet" produced 332% signup lift over "news and updates." Concrete benefit plus named recipient plus explicit transformation beats vague brand language.

4. **Evergreen flagship content compounds.** Chris Guillebeau's "A Brief Guide to World Domination": 100k+ downloads. Manifesto-level assets work while you sleep. Chase news cycles and burn out. Create assets that compound over time.

5. **Empathy first in outreach.** Five assumption checks before contact: time, busyness, rejection, silence, promises. Help first, ask second. Zig Ziglar principle. "Let's meet" (selfish) vs. "interview request" (service). Fans receive one-to-many communication; influencers receive one-to-one. Social media is a booster, not a driver. Email lists convert at 10-to-1 over Twitter for the same audience size.

6. **Free sample plus cliffhanger combo.** Kindle 10% sample model. Release first chapter free, end at narrative cliffhanger, route to a stripped-down CTA page (testimonials, bio, buy link only; no sidebar links or extra images during the ask). The one-two punch.

7. **Auto-responder as onboarding engine.** New subscribers don't know your body of work. Pre-built email sequence (weeks or months in advance) ensures all subscribers meet you the same way. Natural vehicle for backlist promotion and identity establishment.

8. **Enthusiasm as proof of belief.** Daniel Pink dinner story: animated belief created pre-commitment 9 months before release. Readers detect conviction. Hedge language ("maybe," "could," "try") is a sales killer. Be your own fan.

**SNIPED application:** The Direction Stack book launch needs the Grahl architecture from day one: email list as the core asset (not IG follower count), specific-win-named offer language, free first chapter ending at a cliffhanger, auto-responder sequence introducing Bryce plus methodology plus one case study, and direct ask via story-first pattern (before stats). The LinkedIn-to-email funnel is the permission stack: LinkedIn comment → warm DM → email signup → book pre-order. Review generation SOP should target lineage-adjacent voices (HBCU intellectual, LA Black founder cluster), not strangers. Multi-asset repurposing: one shoot yields Chapter Card, HERO post, LinkedIn essay, email excerpt, and future book chapter. The "first 1000 copies" philosophy is scene-density thinking applied to book publishing: dense 1,000 versus thin 10,000.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:** No contradiction. Confirms Repetition over Novelty, scene-density over broadcast reach, proof over packaging, system over novelty. Adds one structural piece absent from the OS: **the auto-responder onboarding sequence as a scalable hospitality system**. The Hospitality Layer intel (Guidara) addresses client touchpoints; Grahl addresses subscriber onboarding at scale. These are different problems. The auto-responder is the mechanism for delivering hospitality to 500 new subscribers without Bryce writing 500 personal emails. This should be built before the Direction Stack book launches, not after.

### BOOK 4: My Editing Wavee Forever (internal methodology doc)

**Coverage line:** 2/2 segments, complete Lightroom architecture for dark skin on Canon EOS RP.

**1-line thesis:** Melanin-intentional editing is physics first and aesthetics second; the Canon EOS RP's sensor biases are a known problem requiring systematic correction, not taste.

**Densest frameworks and lessons:**

1. **Calibration as correction, not style.** Canon EOS RP biases red/magenta in shadows and produces notable shadow noise. Camera Calibration corrects this before any creative decisions: Shadows Tint -2 to -5, Red Primary Hue +15 to +25, Green Primary Hue -10 to -20, Blue Primary Hue -15 to -30. Not preference. Physics.

2. **Specular highlights are the 3D anchor.** For dark skin, the brightest specular reflections (not shadows) define shape and dimensionality. Global contrast crushes shadows immediately on dark complexions. Correct approach: lift the black floor off zero (Tone Curve Output 20 at Input 0), raise Shadows slider (+10 to +35 depending on preset), and isolate specular highlights with a dedicated mask (Whites +5 to +15).

3. **Four-preset architecture with distinct dynamic range logic.** Controlled Studio (Contrast +5, Highlights -10 to -15, Shadows +10 to +15), Uncontrolled Natural (Contrast -5 to 0, Highlights -40 to -50, Shadows +25 to +35), Editorial/Lifestyle (Contrast -15, Blacks +25 to +35 for analog fade), Timeless B&W (Contrast +15 to +20, Blacks -15 to -20). Values are load-bearing, not approximate.

4. **HSL as surgical scalpel.** Instead of crushing global contrast, target specific channels: Orange -8 to -10 fights skin oversaturation, Yellow controls midtone brightness without color shift, Green/Aqua separates subject from foliage. B&W preset replaces HSL with B&W Mix: Orange +15 to +25 lifts subject from muddy gray, Blue -30 to -45 creates sky drama.

5. **Color Grading follows physical reality.** Shadows in outdoor light are blue (ambient sky), so push shadows toward teal/cyan. Highlights mimic sunlight (golden yellow/orange). Midtones stay near neutral or very slight warm (Saturation 5-8 max) where skin lives. Heavy color cast on midtones kills skin tone. This is science, not mood-boarding.

6. **AI mask plus manual mask layering.** AI Preset masks lock three moves: Facial Skin (Texture -8 to -10, Clarity 0), Eyes (Exposure +0.10 to +0.15, Texture +15, Clarity +5 to +10), Background (Exposure -0.15 to -0.20, Texture -10 to -15). Manual masks add surgical per-image control: Specular Pop (subject-intersect-luminance), Face Pop (radial gradient, Exposure +0.10, Temp +2 to +4), Contrast Sandwich (linear edge gradients for organic vignette).

7. **Sharpening is masking-dependent.** Studio: Amount 40, Masking 60-70 (aggressive mask protects skin). Field: Amount 45-50, Masking 50-60. Editorial: Amount 20-25, Masking 80 (grain does texture work). B&W: Amount 60, Masking 30-40 (texture must show). Over-sharpening on melanin without a mask produces crunchy, dry, overworked skin.

8. **Export cascade is data-sacred.** Lightroom to Evoto: TIFF 16-bit ProPhoto or Adobe RGB, ZIP compression, no output sharpening (Evoto needs clean data for 3D face geometry mapping; sharpening before handoff causes posterization banding). Final delivery: master TIFF 16-bit Adobe RGB (print/archive), client JPG 100% sRGB 3000-4000px (web), IG JPG 100% sRGB exactly 1350px (bypasses IG server compression algorithm).

**SNIPED application:** This document is the technical backbone of the Production OS. Every Composite Environment Rotation chapter (all 7) must be graded through this exact four-preset architecture, not aesthetic approximation. The B&W Card dual-register rule finds its technical specification here: the Timeless B&W preset is the Card engine. The 3-pass culling SOP (garbage purge → hero selection → cohesion grid check) operationalizes the "40 bangers not 300 frames" standard. The EOS RP to R6 Mark II upgrade path is rationalized and removes the gear excuse from the execution governor. Evoto integration as Stage 7-10 in the pipeline operationalizes leverage logic (code plus media removes labor).

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS existing doctrine:** No contradiction with visual direction (Adobe Neutral foundation confirmed, restraint over volume confirmed, no teal/orange confirmed). Adds three anti-patterns worth flagging explicitly: (1) Luminance NR plus Film Grain is a conflict: software blurring noise while simultaneously adding grain produces muddy pixel structure; Editorial preset must set Luminance NR to 0 and let grain own the texture. (2) Clarity is dangerous on color skin; only safe in B&W preset. (3) EF-S 24mm on full-frame drops to 10MP effective resolution; this lens should be sold, not kept. These are not new principles but they are now documented with enough specificity to serve as a hard gate in the production workflow.

**1. sniped-curse-of-knowledge-gate**
A recurring evaluation gate run on every SNIPED external artifact (positioning copy, LinkedIn post, DM template, case study, chapter card caption) before it ships. Checks for: insider jargon without sensory grounding, abstract claims without concrete proof anchors, leads buried past sentence two, missing emotional hook, no story or transformation arc, identity appeal absent. **2. sniped-success-checklist**

Generates the 5-7 email onboarding sequence for new LinkedIn connections and email subscribers. Outputs: sequenced email drafts with subject lines, cadence schedule, and CTA per email. Builds the hospitality-at-scale layer before Direction Stack book launch.

An eval gate for any edited image leaving the SNIPED pipeline. A field intelligence tool. Input: a founder's LinkedIn post, a shoot-day event, a client outcome, a casting moment. ### EVALUATION GATES

**Curse of Knowledge gate (mandatory before any external SNIPED artifact ships):**
- Does the first sentence contain insider language without a sensory anchor?
- Is the core message in the first two sentences or buried?
- Can a non-SNIPED founder simulate the idea mentally from this copy alone?
- Does the copy trigger identity appeal or just feature appeal?
- Is there a concrete example or proof point within the first 100 words?

**SUCCESs gate (for any idea or content piece):**
- Simple: Is there one core, stated compactly?
- Unexpected: Does it violate a schema and resolve it post-dictably?
- Concrete: Are there sensory details or human-action anchors?
- Credible: Is there a Sinatra Test case, vivid detail, or antiauthority proof?
- Emotional: Does it activate identity appeal or higher-Maslow aspiration?
- Story: Does it have a protagonist, obstacle, and transformation?

**Lightroom preset audit gate (before any image leaves Production OS):**
- Was the correct preset applied for the shooting condition (Studio/Natural/Editorial/B&W)?
- Is Calibration correction applied (Shadows Tint, Red/Green/Blue Primary Hue)?
- Is Clarity set to 0 or negative on color skin presets?
- Is Luminance NR set to 0 if Film Grain is active (no conflict)?
- Is export spec correct for destination (TIFF 16-bit for Evoto, JPG sRGB 1350px for IG)?
- Did the image pass 3-pass cull before reaching edit?

**Story quality gate (before any case study or cultural doc chapter ships):**
- Is the plot type identified (Challenge/Connection/Creativity)?
- Is there a protagonist with a specific obstacle (not a generic "challenge")?
- Is the transformation concrete and observable (not "improved brand")?
- Does it pass the Jared test: could this survive as a story without any stats?
- Is narrative order optimized for conviction (problem before resolution, not simultaneous)?

**Auto-responder enthusiasm gate (before any email in the sequence ships):**
- Does the email contain hedge language ("maybe," "could," "try")?
- Is the ask direct and present in the email?
- Does Bryce's voice convey he believes in the work?
- Is there one concrete proof point (image, case study snippet, specific outcome)?
