### BOOK 1: Prompt Template - Self Criticism (Advanced)-2.pdf

**Coverage:** 1/1 segs complete.

**Thesis:** Embedding self-criticism loops directly into LLM prompts (S2A, RaR, RE2 patterns) raises output quality without adding human review cycles.

**Densest Frameworks/Lessons:**

1. **S2A (Strategy to Arguments):** Generate output, then force the model to articulate reasoning behind each option. Converts a list into a justified set. Apply when batching taglines, positioning statements, or caption options.

2. **RaR (Rephrase and Refine):** Ask the model to rewrite toward named criteria (brand voice, clarity, tone). The rewrite IS the quality gate. Apply on email drafts, DM templates, LinkedIn copy.

3. **RE2 (Re-read and Verify):** Force re-reading of the output against a checklist (accuracy, narrative coherence, metric validity). The model catches its own hallucinations before delivery. Apply on Cultural Doc drafts, case study claims, positioning docs.

4. **Output-first, critique-second structure:** The sequence matters. Output first (unfiltered), then critique layer. This preserves generativity while enforcing quality. Reversing it (filter first) collapses creative range.

5. **Criteria embedding:** Quality criteria must be named explicitly in the prompt (brand voice, value alignment, narrative coherence). Vague prompts produce vague self-criticism. Specificity in the gate = specificity in the output.

**SNIPED Application:**

- S2A deploys on every tagline/caption batch for composite environment Chapter Cards. Force the model to explain which card concept maps to which Lineage Doctrine root, not just generate options.
- RaR deploys on every outbound DM draft, VIB outreach copy, partnership announcement. Named criteria: "does this read as a founder peer or a vendor?"
- RE2 deploys on Cultural Doc drafts before export. Gate: "does every factual claim trace to a direct source or lived observation? does the narrative align with the Direction Stack thesis?"
- Operationalizes the Maximum-by-default rule without a manual tier-review pass.

**UPDATES/CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine:**

- Reinforces Maximum-by-default rule (LOCKED 2026-05-12): these patterns ARE the mechanism for default-maximum output.
- Adds operational specificity to Hardest-to-say-no-to rule (LOCKED 2026-06-02): hardest-to-reject outputs require explicit named criteria in the self-criticism loop. Cannot just demand "best output." Must name what "best" means in context.

### BOOK 2: SparkNotes 1984 (Orwell) - Complete 4-Segment Read

**Coverage:** 4/4 segs complete. Segments 1-3 carry doctrine weight. Segment 4 (essay scaffold/study guide) is reference tier.

**Thesis:** Total control over a population requires simultaneous command of the body (pain), language (thought boundaries), history (narrative baseline), and hope (false promise directed at the victim's own trap). Remove any one layer and the system weakens, but no single layer is sufficient alone.

**Densest Frameworks/Lessons:**

1. **Multi-modal control architecture:** Psychological + physical + informational + technological + linguistic control systems reinforce each other. Dismantle one, others remain. The lesson: any system (institutional, market, cultural) that wants to hold territory must hold multiple vectors simultaneously. The inverse: resilience requires multi-modal independence, not single-vector strength.

2. **"Who controls the past controls the future. Who controls the present controls the past."** Documentary authority = future authority. This is not metaphor. Whoever holds the archive of what a culture was decides what it is allowed to become. Applied directly: Direction Stack + Cultural Doc + Lineage Doctrine are a documentary authority play, not a branding exercise.

3. **Newspeak: language reduction collapses thought.** Remove the words for a concept and the concept ceases to be thinkable. "Crimethink" replaces the entire Declaration of Independence. The inverse: specificity of language preserves the full range of what is imaginable. Generic positioning language ("premium photography," "elevated visuals") is market-level Newspeak. It compresses differentiation out of existence.

4. **Hope as the most insidious control vector.** The Party does not crush resistance immediately. It cultivates false hope (via O'Brien, the Brotherhood, "the place where there is no darkness") to intensify psychological damage when the trap closes. The lesson: naive trust in false liberators is more dangerous than recognized oppression. Applied adversarially: identify when an institutional promise (platform, accelerator, network) is directing behavior toward its own benefit.

5. **Physical presence overrides psychological resistance.** Pain (somatic control) breaks ideological conviction faster and more completely than language control alone. The inverse: the body's presence in work (real subject, real skin texture, live encounter) resists algorithmic compression more than any language argument. "Show up in the room" beats "explain the work online."

6. **Psychology over technology (model student essay, Seg 4).** The Party would survive without telescreens. It would collapse without self-policing citizens. Visible tools (surveillance tech) are the surface layer. Invisible psychology (doublethink, groupthink, paranoia) is the structural layer. Parallel: visible AI tools (compositing, background generation) are the surface. Invisible craft (cultural authority, scene-density, body-documentation) is what sustains the operator position.

7. **Betrayal gambit: self-preservation overrides moral conviction.** Room 101 forces sacrifice of the person loved most. Proves that self-love is stronger than ideology. Applied to SNIPED: any client relationship or partnership structure that requires the client to sacrifice their self-interest to maintain the relationship is fragile. Premium positioning works because it aligns vendor interest with client self-interest (proof of status, not extraction).

8. **Conversion without martyrs.** Historical tyrannies created martyrs (Inquisition, Stalin show trials). The Party's innovation: convert enemies socially, erase them while alive. The inverse: SNIPED's scene-density + documentary continuity means the work is harder to erase without creating a visible gap. Dense-archive presence resists non-person status.

**SNIPED Application:**

- **Lineage Doctrine IS the anti-Newspeak move.** Preserve specificity, depth, and historical memory against flattening language and commercial erasure. Every Cultural Doc entry, every Chapter Card caption, every Direction Stack chapter title should resist compression to market category.

- **Anti-identity-AI stance clarified.** Refusing to apply AI to client identity (face, body, skin texture) is a documentary integrity move. The Party erases specificity to maintain control. Commercial AI vending erases specificity to maximize throughput. Same structural move, different context. SNIPED's refusal is the inverse: preserve the specific body as the irreducible fact of the work.
- **Trust Equation inversion.** Orwell's Party maximizes self-orientation (S) to maximize control. Maister's Trust Equation: maximize S = minimize trust. SNIPED's premium lane depends on visibly low S (building culture, not extracting from it). This validates the Trust Equation intel as a structural counterargument to commercial AI positioning.
