# LOT 00 , the reference standard
The proven one: a synthetic aristocrat-heir of a foreclosed estate, auctioned as Lot 00 with everything she owns. Kraft auction tag = the mark; red = for sale. This is the asset that first passed the full chain to gate SHIP at 4K.
## What it does better than DEED
- A human figure carries more immediate emotional pull than an empty records room.
- The auction-of-a-person premise has sharper status-betrayal charge.
- It is the most battle-tested (already SHIP, already a full kit, already a 4K hero).
## What DEED does better than LOT 00
- A cleaner, more drawable single mark (a stamp vs a paper tag).
- A built-in ending (finite parcels) that LOT 00 lacks.
- A more obvious physical-product money path (numbered certificates) and a clearer B2B licensing lane.
- Faceless with zero figure-likeness risk (LOT 00 still renders a body).
