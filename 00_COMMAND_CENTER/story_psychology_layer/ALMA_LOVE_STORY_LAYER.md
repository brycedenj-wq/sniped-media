# ALMA LOVE - Story Layer Application (no cut yet)

The story/psychology layer applied to Alma Love. This governs the NEXT edit; it is not a cut. Cards cited from STORY_INTELLIGENCE_CARDS.json.

## Story premise (char_flawed_protagonist + char_world_rules_then_break)
A composed, mannequin-still woman moves through a luxury Beverly Hills summer day, and the expensive world keeps malfunctioning around her (a speaker she has to drag and kick, props that fight her). Her governing trait: unbreakable poise. The swimsuit is her armor and the one thing that always looks intentional. **The suit survives the chaos. That is the sell.**

## Viewer psychology (story_emotional_target + psy_status_new_luxury)
- First 3s target feeling: **amused intrigue + expensive calm** ("wait, what is she doing... and she looks rich").
- Status signal activated: Beverly Hills / Mercedes / composure transferred onto an affordable swimsuit = the trade-up (Silverstein).

## Commercial tension (story_but_therefore + psy_withhold_reveal)
The drama is poise vs. chaos. She arrives dragging a speaker (context) BUT it fails her (conflict) THEREFORE she ignores it and the suit holds the frame (resolution) BUT the world interrupts again (new loop) THEREFORE she ends frozen as the brand image (payoff). The clean product is WITHHELD behind teases (dangle, blur) until the trunk reveal.

## Character / world rules
- She never reacts emotionally (deadpan). Comedy is situational, never her face.
- Canon = the luxury baseline (clean, composed). iPhone = the chaos/comedy/POV energy. The edit NEEDS both - establish luxury, then break it.
- The suit is always the brightest, cleanest, most-intentional thing on screen (story_visual_focal_light).
