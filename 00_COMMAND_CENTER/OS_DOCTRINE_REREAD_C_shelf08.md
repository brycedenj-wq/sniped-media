### $100M MONEY MODELS (Alex Hormozi, 2025)

**Coverage**: 7/7 segments, full book

**Thesis**: A business is a deliberate sequence of offers (Attraction, Upsell, Downsell, Continuity) engineered so first-30-day cash from customers exceeds acquisition cost, creating a self-funding growth loop without external capital.

**Densest Frameworks / Lessons**:

1. **4-Layer Revenue Stack**: Attraction (thin/free, get the customer) → Upsell (solve next problem at point of need) → Downsell (restructure, never reprice the same thing) → Continuity (recurring safety net). Any single layer is fine; all four together is 8-27x growth.

2. **30-Day Payback Rule**: Cash velocity beats margin. If customer acquisition cost isn't recovered in 30 days or less, the business is illiquid regardless of LTV math. Credit card interest-free window is the operating loop.

3. **Friction Removal Cascade (7-step payment plan)**: Cost objection = payment structure objection, not product value rejection. Reward prepay → 3rd-party financing → 50/50 split → desire check (1-10, must be 8+) → 3-installment → weekly-spread → free trial. Never discount the same offering.

4. **Weekly Billing Lever**: 13 billing cycles per year vs. 12 months = +8.3% revenue, +41% annual profit on 20% margins. Zero product change. Most underused tactic in service businesses.

5. **Feature Downsell Architecture**: Lower price by removing value, never discounting identical product. Customers re-evaluate original offer when they see what they lose. Remove highest-value features first to incentivize upgrade back.

6. **Rollover Upsell**: Apply prior spend as credit toward higher-tier offer. Works for lapsed clients, upset clients, competitor poaching. 4x rule: upsell at minimum 4x the credit given.

7. **Phased Money Model Activation**: Stage I (Attraction) funds Stage II (Upsell/Downsell) funds Stage III (Continuity). Launching full model day one causes operational collapse. Patient sequencing is the doctrine.

8. **Continuity Math**: One-time $1k at 10% conversion = $10k. $50/mo recurring at 40% conversion, 20-month duration = $40k over time + 4x more customers for upsell base.

**SNIPED Application**:
- SNIPED currently has no documented Attraction/Upsell/Downsell/Continuity sequence. The offer stack is project-based and single-layer.
- Attraction: a free or thin-margin offer that reveals SNIPED's value at the moment of founder need (e.g., free positioning audit, free first composite comp, free Cultural Doc sampler).
- Upsell: at booking, present the next problem the founder now realizes they have (LinkedIn narrative assets, quarterly brand refresh, social distribution architecture).
- Downsell: when $5k Brand System gets a no, never lower the price. Offer feature-reduced tier (Composite Only, DIY Guidance) or payment plan (half VIB confirmation, half on delivery).
- Continuity: quarterly narrative refresh subscription, annual Cultural Doc drop at $199/mo, annual print club with waived-fee structure.
- 30-day rule governs launch timing: pricing must recover acquisition cost in 30 days, not 6 months.
- Weekly billing (not monthly) on any subscription product adds 8.3% revenue at zero additional work.

**UPDATES / CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine**:
- Confirms "Payment Follows Proof" (2026-06-03) but adds specificity: proof enables offer sequencing, not just entity structure.
- Confirms "Pricing Creativity logic" (Blair Enns 3-option architecture) but Hormozi's 4-layer stack is more granular. Enns governs premium positioning; Hormozi governs cash-flow architecture. Both needed.
- Weakens any assumption that a high-ticket single offer is sufficient. The real money is in what follows it.
- Adds new doctrine candidate: **Offer Stack Sequencing** (no existing OS doc covers this in Hormozi's granularity).

### BUILDING A STORYBRAND (Donald Miller, 2017)

**Coverage**: 8/8 segments, full book

**Thesis**: Customers do not buy the best products; they buy the ones they can understand fastest. Positioning the brand as the guide (not the hero) and the customer as the hero of their own story unlocks 4-20x revenue lifts from the same audience and the same channels.

**Densest Frameworks / Lessons**:

1. **SB7 Framework**: Character (customer with a want) → Problem (external/internal/philosophical three levels) → Guide (brand with empathy + authority) → Plan (3-6 steps, reduces cognitive load) → CTA (direct + transitional) → Failure State (moderate fear, Kahneman 2-3x loss-aversion) → Success Vision (specific, aspirational, named transformation).

2. **Three-Level Problem Taxonomy**: External (what you sell) → Internal (what customers actually buy, the emotional frustration) → Philosophical (why it morally matters, the injustice being corrected). Brands that only address the external level leave most of the purchase motivation untouched.

3. **Grunt Test**: Can a stranger answer in 5 seconds: what do you offer / how does it improve my life / how do I buy it? If not, the message is noise.

4. **Guide Positioning (Empathy + Authority)**: Amy Cuddy's two trust questions: "Can I trust?" (empathy) and "Can I respect?" (authority). Four authority signals: testimonials (max 3, brief), statistics, awards, recognizable client logos. Empathy signals: "We understand how it feels to..."

5. **Loss Aversion as Primary Motivator**: Kahneman/Prospect Theory: 2-3x more motivated by avoiding a $100 loss than gaining $100. Failure state framing (moderate dosage) outperforms gain framing in all tested contexts.

6. **Transitional CTA as Revenue Engine**: StoryBrand grew multi-million-dollar revenue in year 2 with zero paid marketing via a free PDF ("5 Things Your Website Should Include"). Free gated asset → email list → 3:1 nurture-to-offer drip → referral system. This compound is the actual growth machine.

7. **One-Liner Formula**: Character (who is customer) + Problem (their struggle) + Plan (how you solve it) + Success (life after). Compressed to one repeatable sentence. Teach to staff, test on strangers, deploy across every surface.

8. **Narrative Void in Organizations**: 1 in 5 employees engaged (Gallup). Root cause is absence of a unifying story. BrandScript deployed internally turns teams into aligned sales forces. Case study: 5% to 30% growth, same people, three-year implementation.

**SNIPED Application**:
- SNIPED's external problem is stated well (premium photography). Internal problem (fear of cultural invisibility, imposter syndrome in premium markets) is underexplored in copy. Philosophical problem (hyphenated identity deserves architectural respect, not dilution) is documented in OS but absent from market-facing surfaces.
- VIB outreach currently leads with SNIPED capability ("we do X"). Should lead with founder's internal problem ("we understand how it feels to build something that reflects your community, not dilutes it").
- All CTAs on LinkedIn, email, and website need audit: are they bold, prominent, and repeated? StoryBrand evidence: passive selling communicates product doubt.
- SNIPED needs a written Agreement Plan: what fears does SNIPED explicitly counter? (Identity integrity, no AI-generated faces, no commodity delivery, named process). This is the trust architecture in a single artifact.
- Transitional CTA ladder: free Cultural Doc sampler or Apparatus gallery → gated methodology PDF → direct "schedule heritage shoot" / "get custom brand strategy."
- The one-liner does not currently exist for SNIPED in deployable form. Needs to be built, tested on strangers, and deployed across LinkedIn comments, emails, and cold outreach.

**UPDATES / CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine**:
- Confirms "Trust + Authority Mechanics" (intel doc) but adds the Amy Cuddy two-question sequence and the explicit failure state dosage rule (moderate, not high or none).
- Confirms "Positioning Language Patterns" but adds the philosophical-problem layer as a missing unlock. Current positioning phrases are strong on external and moderate on internal but light on philosophical.
- Confirms "Repetition > Novelty" (locked 2026-05-12) with a narrative-mechanics justification: story repetition is the mechanism, not just discipline.
- Adds new doctrine candidate: **SB7 Spine** (one-liner + agreement plan + CTA ladder + failure state dosage) as a single distillable artifact.

### MOODBOARDING DOC OG.docx (Curated Video Transcript Anthology)

**Coverage**: 13/13 segments, full doc

**Thesis**: A mood board is not a collection of pretty pictures. It is a pre-production alignment artifact, a stakeholder communication device, a direction contract, and an iterative creative hypothesis. Its quality is the quality of the shoot.

**Densest Frameworks / Lessons**:

1. **5-Section Shoot Structure (Lindsay Adler standard)**: Title Page (emotional hook, ego-play, authority) → Lighting Page (5 consistent references, not 2) → Styling/Hair/Makeup (specificity over full-face examples) → Poses Page (energy, not cliche) → Vibes Page (overall mood collage). On-set: physical copies at 3 crew stations (makeup, wardrobe, main light). Send to editor as tonal direction doc. Credit every image. Sign every page.

2. **Vibe Board + Tactical Board Dual System**: Vibe boards (feeling, archetype, emotional direction) are separate from tactical boards (lighting, typography, layout, product photography, social media specifics). One umbrella vibe + multiple granular tactical boards = stakeholder alignment without surprise rejections late in process.

3. **5-Bucket Shoot Planning Grid**: Setting (location refs, ceiling height, light condition) → Action (what subject does, not just poses) → Angle (perspective, camera position) → Lighting (visual quality reference, not gear spec) → Edit/Post (color direction, collage intent, composite plan). Bonus 6th bucket: BTS capture strategy (tactical vs. conceptual vs. premium gimbal-planned b-roll).

4. **Composite Reference as Talent Director**: Show the AI-generated or prior composite to talent before the shot. The Viking/Foxy example: disengaged performer became fully committed character the moment they saw the visual reference. This is show-don't-tell direction without verbal friction.

5. **Midjourney Parameter Control**: Stylize (S) value = accessibility lever between moodboard inheritance (high S) and prompt override (low S, 15-60 range). Successful outputs feed back into source board immediately. Nested Discord shortcuts (/MB) compress full environment test suites to single keystrokes. Aspect ratio diversity in boards prevents locked-frame output bias.

6. **Personality Slider + Keyword Sprint (Brand Strategy Layer)**: Before gathering references, identify brand position on multiple axes (elite/mass, serious/playful). Expand 4-5 brand keywords into nested feelings (e.g., "magical" → playful design + energy/aura + sense of wonder). Build visual collection around nested feelings, not surface aesthetic.

7. **FrameSet Tool Architecture**: Commercial/editorial database filterable by director, cinematographer, colorist, lens, camera, color temperature, saturation. References tagged by credits double as equipment education. Drag-and-drop reorganization creates a narrative viewing sequence for crew before shoot day.

8. **Reference Curation as Career Asset**: Organized references (board buckets by recurring theme, Sunday night 45-min review cycles, intentional pin curation) compound over time. The photographer with the organized reference system beats the talented-but-disorganized peer at scale.

**SNIPED Application**:
- Composite Environment Rotation (locked 2026-05-12) needs a dedicated mood board per environment, not a text description. Each of the 7 environments (Brutalist Monument / Industrial Minimal / Monochromatic Void / Sculptural Gallery / Cinematic Urban / Organic Surreal / Futurist Editorial) gets a Lindsay Adler-standard 5-page board with 5+ lighting references, pose energy references, styling direction, and an explicitly labeled vibes page.
- B&W Card Dual-Register: Tip 6 (text-heavy boards train text output) enables consistent typography on Chapter Card graphics. Feed successful card text treatments back into Midjourney moodboard before next chapter generation.
- Edit Register Bifurcation (identity stable, styling variable): segmentation mask workflow in Nano Banana + "keep character exactly same" prompt operationalizes the doctrine in AI generation.
- Casting call doctrine already has 24-hr confirmation + wardrobe photo requirement. Mood boards are the missing upstream alignment artifact that locks crew before shoot day, not during.
- Track B composite pipeline: nested Discord shortcuts (/MB) turn 7-environment testing from manual to automated. One keystroke runs all aspect ratios and stylize variations.
- VIB method: send founders a mood board preview before shoot as proof-of-professionalism and to reduce on-set friction. This is a pre-sell trust artifact before a single shot fires.

**UPDATES / CONTRADICTS Existing Doctrine**:
- Confirms "Composite Environment Rotation v1" (locked 2026-05-12) but adds the missing operationalization layer: each environment needs a committed mood board, not just a label.
- Confirms "Strongest Photograph != Most Processed" via the authenticity doctrine in the AI generation section ("reveal best version of existing, not artificial creation"). Alignment is tight.
- Confirms "Edit Register Bifurcation" and provides the AI prompt architecture to enforce it (segmentation masks, "keep character exactly same").
- Adds new doctrine candidate: **Mood Board as Direction Contract** (pre-shoot, on-set, and post-production). No existing OS doc treats mood boards as a system; they are referenced but not formalized.
- Weakens any assumption that a text brief alone communicates shoot direction. Visual reference is non-negotiable for crew alignment, composite consistency, and talent embodiment.

### EVALUATION GATES

1. **Offer Readiness Gate**: Before any offer is presented to a founder/client, verify: (a) Attraction layer exists and recovers cost in 30 days. (b) At least one Upsell is architected and timed to the hyper-buying window. (c) A Feature Downsell or Payment Plan path exists for the no. (d) Continuity layer is documented even if not yet active. Fail any of these = offer is incomplete.

2. **Message Clarity Gate (Grunt Test)**: Before publishing any copy (LinkedIn, website, email, DM), test against three questions: What do you offer? How does it improve my life? How do I buy it? If a stranger cannot answer in 5 seconds, copy fails. Mandatory before any outreach push.

3. **Failure State Dosage Gate**: Every piece of outward-facing copy audited for failure-state level. Score: 0 (no stakes named), 1 (moderate, correct), 2 (excessive fear, counterproductive). Only score 1 ships.

4. **Mood Board Completeness Gate**: Before any shoot day, verify mood board contains: Title/concept hook, Lighting page with 5+ consistent references, Styling/hair/makeup with feature-specific callouts, Poses page (energy-driven, not cliche), Vibes page. Physical copies at 3 crew stations. Editor copy prepared. Fail = shoot not cleared.

5. **Three-Level Problem Gate**: Before shipping any positioning asset, verify the copy addresses all three problem levels: External (what's offered), Internal (emotional frustration, what they actually buy), Philosophical (why it morally matters). Two out of three is not passing.

6. **Offer Rejection Diagnosis Gate**: When a client or founder says no, run the diagnosis before moving on. No = want mismatch / price / payment friction / product fit / timing. Identify which layer broke before presenting the next move. Undiagnosed no = wasted downsell.

### DECISION UPGRADES

1. **Pricing architecture**: Never lower price on the same offering. Instead, choose between Feature Downsell (remove value, lower price), Payment Plan (same price, restructured timing), or Rollover (apply prior credit toward higher tier). These are three distinct tools; conflating them destroys trust.

2. **Continuity sequencing**: Do not lead with subscriptions or retainers. Attraction and Upsell stages must fund and prove before Continuity is layered. Premature continuity offering undercuts perceived value of the primary offer.

3. **Copy orientation**: Every first-contact surface (LinkedIn headline, email subject, DM opener) should name the customer's internal problem or aspirational identity, not SNIPED's capabilities. The guide does not announce themselves; they acknowledge the hero.

4. **Talent direction protocol**: Showing a composite reference or mood board to talent before the shot is a direction decision, not a nice-to-have. It removes verbal ambiguity, activates character embodiment, and produces more usable frames per hour.

5. **Billing cadence**: Any subscription or membership product should anchor annual billing first, then downgrade to quarterly, then monthly. Annual = 2% monthly churn; monthly = 10.7%. This single decision has compound effects on retention math.

6. **One-liner before outreach**: A deployable one-liner (SB7-compliant, stranger-tested) must exist before any outreach push begins. Without it, individual messaging variation fragments the positioning over time.
