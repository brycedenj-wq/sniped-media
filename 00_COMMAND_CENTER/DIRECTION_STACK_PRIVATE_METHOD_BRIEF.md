## 1. Purpose and scope
Stop sitting on the IP without distracting from paid work. Define how the Direction Stack is used privately now to premium-ize the Reset, give the founder VIB its language, seed a few content tastes, and serve as a quiet production standard, while the public book launch waits for proof. Scope is the private use; the public launch is a deferred, separate plan.

## 2. What Direction Stack is
A directing method/diagnostic: 10 protocols (6 mechanical: Claw Hands, Locked Shoulders, Squared, Pinned Arms, Soft Jawline, Spinal Collapse; 4 psychological: Forced Smile, Transition Freeze, Good Mechanics Zero Presence, Full Shutdown), articulated as a 57-page book ("A Recovery Manual for Photographers"). The book is the articulation; the method is the asset. IP held by Baseplate, LLC.

## 3. What stays parked
The full public/paid book launch (the Q3 2026 window, on an Iron Maiden posture to a built audience), any course/training product, a public free lead magnet of the full method, and any release that gives away all 10 protocols. None of these happen now.

## 4. What activates now
The private method behind the Reset; the protocol diagnostic as sales/VIB language; a few content tastes (one protocol at a time); an optional short client-only leave-behind for delivered Resets; and the private production standard inside BASEPLATE dossier work. All private or partial, none public-launch.

## 5. Why the public book launch waits
Perennial-seller logic: a book launched to no audience and no proof flops and burns the asset. The spine scopes the launch to Q3 2026, after 3-5 Resets close (proof-of-work first), built to a faithful following on an Iron Maiden timescale. Launching early also risks "photographer selling a PDF," which weakens the premium mystique. So the method is used now; the book ships later, to a built audience, on proof.

## 6. Private method role
The Direction Stack is the hidden engine of every SNIPED shoot: the diagnostic that decides direction, the reason the work is better than a generic shoot. It is named as the method ("directed on the Direction Stack") but its full contents stay private. It is what makes the practice a method, not just a camera.

## 7. SNIPED Reset role
The Reset is "directed on the Direction Stack method." That single framing premium-izes the Reset with zero book launch. The method is the closing tool and the differentiator on the discovery call; a delivered Reset can include the short leave-behind as a bonus (per the spine's Reset-bonus use). The method justifies the $1,500 without selling anything extra.

## 8. VIB / founder diagnostic role
The Visual Infrastructure Board and founder outreach speak in protocol language: name the specific protocol failure visible in the prospect's current image (e.g., "locked shoulders, squared, reads defensive") without altering their photo (the VIB hard rule). The diagnostic is the authority; it earns the conversation. No pricing in the first DM; no AI-render of the prospect's photo.

## 9. Operator Kit / Brand System role
The method is the depth that justifies the tier jump: the Operator Kit applies the full diagnostic across multiple looks/contexts; the Brand System engineers a complete visual identity on the method. The client leave-behind (section 10) lives at the Operator Kit tier and up.

## 10. Client leave-behind role
A short, client-only leave-behind (a tight one to three pages, not the 57-page book) given to delivered clients: the relevant protocols applied to their session, as a premium artifact they keep. Private to clients, never public, never the full manual. Optional, used when it strengthens the relationship/referral.

## 11. Content taste role
Teach ONE protocol at a time as content (a carousel/story/post on "Claw Hands" or "Forced Smile"), a taste that demonstrates the method and the eye. Never the full 10. A few high-signal pieces, not a daily treadmill. The public gets tastes; clients get the application; the book (later) gets the full articulation.

## 12. Campaign rollout role
The campaign captions and BTS can reference the directing method (a protocol mentioned, the "why" behind a frame) in the lineage/editorial register, pulling from the campaign copy that already exists in prior sessions (with attribution). The method is the through-line that makes the campaign read as authored work, not a photo dump.

## 13. BASEPLATE private dossier role
Inside BASEPLATE, the same diagnostic discipline produces the dossier's imagery (the production standard that makes a firm's people read credible and composed). Private and internal only. The method improves the dossier without "photography" or "Direction Stack" ever headlining the infra brand.

## 14. What never appears on public BASEPLATE
The Direction Stack name, the protocols, the book, the "directed" framing, any photography-method language. On BASEPLATE these would reduce the infra brand to a photography method. The method is invisible on the BASEPLATE surface; it lives inside production only.

## 15. AI visual tool relationship

The VIB hard rule holds: never AI-render a prospect's photo. Kling/Higgsfield are reserved for post-conversion delivery moments, not the pitch. On BASEPLATE, AI is concept/atmosphere only, never evidence.
