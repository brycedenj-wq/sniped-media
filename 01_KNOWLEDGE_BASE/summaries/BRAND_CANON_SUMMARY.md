## Key signal installed

1. **The Brand Gap (Neumeier):** a brand is a person's gut feeling, not what you say but what they say; the brand gap between left-brain strategy and right-brain creativity, bridged by the charismatic brand (no substitute) and by aesthetics as the language of feeling; the five disciplines (differentiate via who-you-are / what-you-do / why-it-matters, collaborate, innovate, validate, cultivate).
2. **Designing Brand Identity (Wheeler):** brand identity as tangible recognition infrastructure that fuels recognition and amplifies differentiation; the brand-identity ideals (vision, meaning, authenticity, coherence, flexibility, commitment, value, differentiation, durability) as evaluation criteria; the managed process (research, strategy, identity, touchpoints, managing assets) and the primacy of touchpoints; positioning and the big idea as the foundation under the design.
3. **Identity Designed (Airey):** visual identity as the instrument of recognition and distinction in saturated markets; consistency as consistently-distinctive (not sameness); the creative brief as the structural framework that disciplines the work and overrules subjective preference.
4. **Brand Naming (Meyerson):** you do not have a brand until you have a name; the name as the number-one touchpoint; the naming process (brief, generate hundreds, narrow, trademark/linguistic screen, commit).
5. **Hello, My Name Is Awesome (Watkins):** the SMILE/SCRATCH test for sticky names; names land through familiar concepts and emotional resonance, not linguistic engineering.
6. **Synthesis (015):** the brand-as-perception operator toolkit (shape perception, build recognition systems, settle positioning, name with craft) + the optionality guardrail.
