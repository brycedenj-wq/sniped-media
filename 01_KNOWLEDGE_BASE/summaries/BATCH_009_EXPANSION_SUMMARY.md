## Key signal installed

1. **Negotiation as tactical empathy** (Voss): label emotions, mirror, run the accusation audit, chase "that's right" not "yes", use calibrated how/what questions, Ackerman staging, hunt the Black Swan. "No deal is better than a bad deal."
2. **Challenger positioning** (Morgan): ideas-led not budget-led; the Lighthouse Identity; Sacrifice + Overcommitment to concentrate force; intelligent naivety and thought leadership of the consumer.
3. **Category design** (Play Bigger): design the category you can lead; the category king takes the majority of the economics; lead with a Point of View; co-evolve the Magic Triangle (product/company/category); stage a Lightning Strike; condition the market.
4. **Tribe building** (Godin): a tribe is a shared interest plus a way to connect; leadership (creating change) is not management; lead the smallest viable tribe and give them tools.
5. **Jobs to Be Done** (Christensen): customers hire products to make progress in a circumstance; the job has functional, social, and emotional dimensions; circumstance beats customer attributes; integrate the offer and the organisation around the job.
6. **The synthesis** (021-022): a thinking sequence (job to be done to POV/category to challenger identity to tribe to the deal), used as option-generators that preserve optionality, never as a mandate to lock a category, niche, identity, or offer ladder.
