## Key signal installed

1. **The Lean Startup (Ries):** validated learning (progress = learning, not output); the MVP + build-measure-learn loop (with actionable, not vanity, metrics); pivot or persevere (the evidence-based strategic inflection · runway = pivots remaining); the engine of growth (sticky/viral/paid · tune its governing variables).
2. **The Hard Thing About Hard Things (Horowitz):** the Struggle (focus and make the best move when there are "no good moves"); wartime vs peacetime CEO (match the mode to the moment); take care of "the people, the products, and the profits, in that order" + the right kind of ambition; CEOs should "tell it like it is" (candor builds the trust crises require); "only lead bullets" / "no silver bullets" (no shortcut to a better product, do the hard work).
3. **The Founder's Dilemmas (Wasserman):** Rich versus King (the wealth-versus-control trade-off · most can't have both); the Three Rs (relationships, roles, and rewards · interlocked, decide early); equity splits and the cost of the quick handshake (early structure compounds · the Pandora case); solo vs team and when to found (career, market, and personal factors); founders' biases (passion, optimism, and conflict avoidance breed shortsighted decisions).
4. **Synthesis (015):** the build-under-uncertainty toolkit (learn fast, decide on evidence, lead with candor and hard work, structure partnerships deliberately, know your Rich-vs-King stance) + the optionality guardrail.
