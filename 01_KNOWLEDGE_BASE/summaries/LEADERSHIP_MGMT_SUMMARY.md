## Key signal installed

1. **The Culture Code (Coyle):** belonging cues and psychological safety drive team performance over talent; the leader builds trust by going first into the vulnerability loop, not by projecting invulnerability.
2. **Leadership in Turbulent Times (Goodwin):** leadership is developed through adversity, not innate, and the mode must match the moment (transformational / crisis / turnaround / visionary).
3. **Team of Rivals (Goodwin):** assemble your strongest rivals rather than loyalists and lead them through emotional strengths (empathy, self-control, absorbing blame, sharing credit, magnanimity toward dissent).
4. **Extreme Ownership (Willink & Babin):** the leader owns everything (no one to blame); no bad teams only bad leaders; decentralized command and the Laws of Combat (Cover and Move, Simple, Prioritize and Execute, Decentralized Command).
5. **The Dichotomy of Leadership (Willink & Babin):** leadership is a balance between opposing forces (own yet empower, aggressive yet cautious, lead yet follow); most failure is one principle taken too far.
6. **Measure What Matters (Doerr):** OKRs pair objectives with measurable key results; focus and commit to a few priorities; stretch for amazing.
7. **Radical Candor (Scott):** care personally AND challenge directly; the failure modes are ruinous empathy (most common), obnoxious aggression, and manipulative insincerity.
8. **Turn the Ship Around! (Marquet):** leader-leader replaces leader-follower; move decision authority to where the information is and shift to intent-based language ("I intend to..."), built on competence and clarity.
9. **High Output Management (Grove):** a manager's output is the output of the units they influence, so maximize leverage; make decisions via free discussion, then a clear decision, then full support.
10. **Synthesis (016):** the leadership/operator-management toolkit (felt safety, situational leadership, ownership + decentralization, OKRs + focus, care + challenge, authority-to-information, managerial leverage) + the optionality guardrail · attributed to High Output Management.
