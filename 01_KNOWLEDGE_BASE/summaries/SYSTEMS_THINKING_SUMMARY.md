## Key signal installed

1. **The Checklist Manifesto (Gawande):** most failure in complex work is ineptitude (we know but fail to apply), not ignorance, so a short checklist of critical easily-missed steps with pause points catches lapses; DO-CONFIRM vs READ-DO, and the checklist as a team-communication / shared-responsibility discipline.
2. **Understanding Media (McLuhan):** the medium is the message (form shapes us more than content); media as extensions of man and as an invisible environment that reorganizes the whole system; hot vs cool media (participation) and the global village.
3. **Thinking in Systems (Meadows):** stocks, flows, and feedback loops so a system's behavior comes from its structure not external events; leverage points (intervene at rules/goals/paradigm, not parameters); bounded rationality, delays, and why we misread systems.
4. **The Fifth Discipline (Senge):** the laws of systems (the harder you push the harder it pushes back; shifting the burden); the learning organization and its five disciplines (personal mastery, mental models, shared vision, team learning, integrated by systems thinking as the fifth); surfacing and testing mental models.
5. **Synthesis (012):** the systems-literacy operator toolkit (checklist reliability, medium-as-environment, structure-drives-behavior, leverage-point thinking, bounded-rationality caution, examined mental models) + the optionality guardrail · attributed to Thinking in Systems.
