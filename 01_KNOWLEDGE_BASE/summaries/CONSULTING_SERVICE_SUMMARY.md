## Key signal installed

1. **Value-Based Fees (Weiss):** price expertise against perceived value and outcome, not time (time-billing anchors the buyer on cost and penalizes speed); reach conceptual agreement on objectives, measures, and value with the economic buyer before the fee or proposal.
2. **Million Dollar Consulting (Weiss):** build market gravity (a field of attraction that pulls prospects to you) rather than chasing; the work exists to improve the client's condition, and the durable asset is the trusting relationship, not the transaction.
3. **The McKinsey Way (Rasiel):** MECE (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive) problem decomposition; the hypothesis-driven, fact-based method; don't boil the ocean (80/20) and confirm you are solving the right problem.
4. **Managing the Professional Service Firm (Maister):** leverage, the finders/minders/grinders ratio, determines the economics; the real equity is the people's goodwill, skill, and reputation, balanced across service / people-satisfaction / financial goals.
5. **Getting Naked (Lencioni):** vulnerability builds client loyalty; shed the three fears (losing the business, being embarrassed, feeling inferior) so you serve honestly and put the client's interest first.
6. **The Advantage (Lencioni):** organizational health (minimal politics and confusion) beats raw smarts as the single greatest success factor; the four disciplines, cohesive team, create clarity, overcommunicate clarity, reinforce clarity, and great meetings.
7. **Flawless Consulting (Block):** contract explicitly and aim for the collaborative (50/50) role rather than expert or pair-of-hands; be authentic (name what you experience) and read resistance as a signal of an underlying concern.
8. **Synthesis (015):** the consulting/service operator toolkit (value-pricing, MECE diagnosis, leverage economics, vulnerability-based trust, clarity, authentic delivery) + the optionality guardrail · attributed to Million Dollar Consulting.
