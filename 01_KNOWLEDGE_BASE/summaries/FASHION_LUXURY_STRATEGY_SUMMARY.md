## Key signal installed

1. **The Luxury Strategy (Kapferer):** luxury is superlative not comparative (refuse positioning against rivals); the anti-laws of luxury marketing (don't pander, dominate the client, communicate to non-targets, keep non-enthusiasts out); scarcity and the price architecture (make it difficult to buy, don't respond to rising demand, raise prices over time; luxury sets the price); advertising builds the dream not the sale; craft, origin, and time (don't relocate factories; the work that justifies the myth).
2. **Deluxe (Thomas):** the democratization of luxury into a mass-produced, democratic status symbol (the handbag of the season, entry-level perfume/cosmetics as a piece of the dream); going public forces short-term profit and erodes craft (outsourcing, inferior materials, false made-in-Europe); the surviving handcraft beneath the volume; manufactured desire and its shadow (counterfeiting).
3. **The End of Fashion (Agins):** the end of the trickle-down/aspirational system (fashion off the pedestal); the commoditization of fashion (Consumer Reports debunking; taste and value decoupled from price; "it's fashionable to pay less"); image is the form and marketing is the function (the designer-as-brand; perception as the real product).
4. **Synthesis (013):** the luxury/fashion symbolic-value operator toolkit (how status, scarcity, and the dream are engineered; the craft-versus-commoditization tradeoff; the image-as-product shift) + the optionality guardrail.
