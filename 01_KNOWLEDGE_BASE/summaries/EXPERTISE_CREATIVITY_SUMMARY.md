## Key signal installed

1. **Ways of Seeing (Berger):** seeing comes before words and is a trained, active, relational act shaped by what we know (perception is a craft); images carry ideology (men act and women appear), reproduction detaches meaning, and publicity is the manufacturing of glamour.
2. **The Creative Act (Rubin):** creativity is trained receptivity (everyone is a creator; the artist as antenna tuning to what's available); make for your own standard and treat awareness as the practice; work with permanent self-doubt by lowering the stakes.
3. **Peak (Ericsson & Pool):** purposeful and deliberate practice (specific goals, full focus, feedback, just past the comfort zone); mental representations are what expertise actually is; skill is built through adaptability, not bestowed by innate talent.
4. **Talent Is Overrated (Colvin):** talent is overrated as an explanation of great performance; sustained deliberate practice plus domain knowledge is what really separates top performers; the multiplier effect grows the intrinsic motivation that sustains the practice.
5. **Synthesis (011):** the expertise/creative-skill operator toolkit (trained perception, image literacy, creative receptivity, self-doubt management, deliberate practice, mental representations, the motivation multiplier) + the optionality guardrail · attributed to Peak.
