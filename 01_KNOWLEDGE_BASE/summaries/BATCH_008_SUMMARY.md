## Key signal installed

1. **Cheap prediction, expensive judgment** (Prediction Machines, Power and Prediction): AI is a drop in the cost of prediction; value migrates to the human judgment, data, and action that complement it. Own the complements, rent the model.
2. **Point solutions vs system solutions** (Power and Prediction): quick bolt-on wins versus organization-redesign transformations. SNIPED's two-tier opportunity taxonomy.
3. **Augmentation, not replacement** (Only Humans Need Apply, Human + Machine, Co-Intelligence, Second Machine Age): the winning unit is human-plus-machine in a deliberate middle. The intellectual backbone of SNIPED's hybrid-operator stance.
4. **The five steps** (Only Humans): step up, step aside, step in, step narrowly, step forward; SNIPED is the step-forward builder.
5. **Practical AI use** (Co-Intelligence): always invite AI to the table, the Jagged Frontier, human-in-the-loop, treat-it-like-a-person, assume-it-is-the-worst-AI-you-will-use.
6. **Digital operating models** (Competing in the Age of AI): the AI factory, scale/scope/learning without diminishing returns, operating-model collision.
7. **The macro stakes** (Life 3.0, The Coming Wave): substrate-independence, alignment, containment, asymmetry, the curious-but-responsible stance.
8. **Ownership and community** (Read Write Own, The Network State): read/write/own eras, protocol vs corporate networks, community-first then territory, founder-led, a moral innovation as the founding asset.
9. **The operator/agency HOW** (AI Edge course, sniped_os, youtube skool): the implementation gap is the business; find-your-edge backwards approach; four-part ICP; one-liner formula; back-office-first; volume-and-rules opportunity screen; DMAIC discipline; ROI + adoption.
10. **The AlphaGo milestone** (AI Changed Everything): machines reached intuition and produced genuinely creative moves (move 37); learning-from-experience beats hand-coded rules.
