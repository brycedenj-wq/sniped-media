## Key signal installed

1. **Diagnosis (systems-thinking):** post-scale decline is systemic and incremental (the loose-thread / commoditization drift), not one failure. Name the root cause before fixing.
2. **Founder return (founder-psychology):** re-taking hands-on daily control to repair what you built; founder identity as both fuel and hazard.
3. **Standards reset (operator-process):** closing all 7,100 US stores to retrain espresso; a costly, visible act that signals a real quality reset, not a memo.
4. **Brand-trust repair (brand):** restoring the experience (the romance, the third place, craft and aroma); brand lives in texture, not the logo.
5. **Transformation Agenda (strategy):** a named, forward-leaning framework sequenced from the burning platform; honesty about problems plus concrete action.
6. **Focus by subtraction (operator-doctrine):** slowing openings, closing ~600 underperformers; the discipline of saying no to the growth reflex.
7. **Innovate from the core (commercial-architecture):** VIA and the rewards platform; new revenue only when relevant to the core and values ("Why not?").
8. **Plan B (operator-process):** ~$400M permanent structural cost cuts via Lean and rebuilt unit economics, while protecting customer-facing investment.
9. **Cultural repair (culture):** New Orleans leadership conference; reconnecting partners in person after cuts; trust as load-bearing infrastructure.
10. **Accountability + resilience (founder-psychology):** owning missteps publicly while holding conviction through the first quarterly loss and media scrutiny.
11. **Conscience (ethics):** fighting for the company's life without losing its soul; protecting health care, ethical sourcing, and community even while cutting.
12. **The synthesis (012):** the repeatable repair sequence (diagnose root cause, re-take control, reset standards, focus by subtraction, structural economics, cultural/trust repair, core-adjacent innovation, protected values) + the explicit optionality guardrail.
