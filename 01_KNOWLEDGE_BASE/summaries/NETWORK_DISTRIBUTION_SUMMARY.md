## Key signal installed

1. **The Inevitable (Kelly):** the 12 forces are trajectories not products (read the current, not the company); flowing means free copies push value to the un-copyable generatives (immediacy, authenticity, embodiment, patronage); access beats ownership and sharing compounds; in abundance, attention/filtering is the scarce resource.
2. **New Rules (Kelly):** increasing returns + the law of plenitude (value explodes with membership); follow the free (generosity as a distribution engine); seek opportunities not efficiencies, let go at the top.
3. **The Long Tail (Anderson):** selling less of more (aggregated niches rival the head); the three forces (democratized production, democratized distribution, connected supply/demand via filters); hits-to-niches with filters as the new tastemakers; "make everything available and help me find it."
4. **Free (Anderson):** the economics of abundance (marginal cost toward zero) and the kinds of free (cross-subsidy, three-party, freemium, gift); free as a distribution strategy that buys attention and reputation; free is conditional, not a default (it needs a paid layer and near-zero marginal cost).
5. **The Great Online Game (McCormick):** building in public compounds reputation and status into real-world opportunity; the online game and offline outcomes converge for consistent players.
6. **Synthesis (016):** the connected-economy distribution toolkit (charge for the un-copyable, give away to earn attention/reputation, design free as cross-subsidy, aggregate niches with navigation, let public work compound) + the optionality guardrail.
