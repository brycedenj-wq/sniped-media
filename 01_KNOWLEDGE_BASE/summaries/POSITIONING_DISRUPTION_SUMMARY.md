## Key signal installed

1. **Crossing the Chasm (Moore):** the adoption life cycle and the chasm (visionaries buy on vision, pragmatists buy on references); target one winnable beachhead niche (D-Day) and dominate before broadening; the whole product (complete the surrounding solution the pragmatist needs); the bowling alley (expand segment by segment on references).
2. **The Innovator's Dilemma (Christensen):** sustaining vs disruptive technologies (disruptors are worse on the old metric, better on a new one); value networks and resource allocation (good management is structurally blind to disruption; listening to current customers steers you away from it); low-end and new-market footholds; performance oversupply (when you overshoot, competition shifts to convenience/reliability/price).
3. **The Mom Test (Fitzpatrick):** ask about their life, not your idea (opinions are worthless, past behavior is data); avoid bad data (deflect compliments, anchor fluff to specifics, measure commitment and advancement).
4. **Synthesis (011):** the toolkit (pick and fully serve a beachhead, validate with the Mom Test, find a low-end/new-market opening on an undervalued dimension) + the optionality guardrail.
