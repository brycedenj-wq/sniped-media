## Key signal installed

1. **The business of hip-hop** (Big Payback): a scene became global culture; built by entrepreneurs/hustlers, not institutions; ownership-vs-being-owned; distribution as the leverage point; credibility as currency; crossover without losing the base; exploitation/appropriation cautions; the artist-as-brand/mogul.
2. **Craft and the producer's discipline** (Dilla Time): the third-path rhythmic invention; mastery through obsessive private practice; constraints producing the signature; influence without fame; the Detroit scene/mentors; document-from-inside; feel over precision.
3. **Self-authorship and voice** (Decoded): rap as layered poetry; re-create yourself; the hustler's transferable logic; the persona/mask; Marcy-as-origin-material; the recording as deliberate transformation.
4. **Transformation and discipline** (Gucci Mane): deliberate post-prison reinvention; prolific output as the comeback engine; honest accounting; family inheritance; Atlanta scene-building; the name as a built asset.
5. **Persona and resilience** (Hurricanes): persona-construction; the authenticity (image-vs-fact) question; the birth of the artist; transcending setbacks; taking a name and mythology.
6. **The ownership arc** (Empire State of Mind): street corner to corner office; building a notorious brand; equity over fees; diversification on one brand; the come-up grounded in Marcy.
7. **Image-making as power** (Supreme Models · light): the visual archive as cultural memory; being-first as inheritance; image as authorship and self-definition.
8. **The synthesis** (041-045): self-authorship is the through-line; ownership over being owned; persona is a built asset; craft discipline + reps make the legend; document from inside the lineage.
