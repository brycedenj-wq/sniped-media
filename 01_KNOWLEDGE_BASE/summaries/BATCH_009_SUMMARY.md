## Key signal installed

1. **Advertising is salesmanship; test everything; be specific** (Hopkins): the direct-response bedrock.
2. **Channel mass desire; meet awareness + sophistication stages** (Schwartz): the market-state targeting model.
3. **The copy craft toolkit** (Bly, Sullivan, Whitman): headlines, AIDA, features-to-benefits, idea-first, write-to-one-person, the Life-Force 8, emotion-then-logic.
4. **The persuasion canon** (Cialdini Influence + Pre-Suasion): the six principles, reciprocation/social-proof/authority/liking/scarcity, and pre-suasion priming, with the ethics gate.
5. **Why ideas spread** (Berger STEPPS, Heath SUCCESs): social currency, triggers, high-arousal emotion, simplicity, curiosity gaps, the curse of knowledge.
6. **Behavioural + psycho-logic** (Shotton, Sutherland): tested biases, the pratfall effect, distinctiveness, perceived value, costly signalling, the contrarian edge.
7. **Positioning** (Trout, Dunford, Godin): own a differentiating idea, positioning-as-context, the five components, smallest viable market, people-like-us, be remarkable.
8. **Offer + lead architecture** (Hormozi): the Grand Slam Offer, the Value Equation, value-stack + guarantees, the Core Four, lead magnets, compounding.
9. **Story-led clarity** (Miller StoryBrand): customer-as-hero / brand-as-guide, the SB7 spine, three-level problem, clarity beats cleverness.
10. **The synthesis** (073-076): sell the outcome to a specific person with proof; persuasion is dual-use (apply with low self-orientation); differentiation + irresistible offer escapes price; make work remarkable and shareable for the smallest viable market.
