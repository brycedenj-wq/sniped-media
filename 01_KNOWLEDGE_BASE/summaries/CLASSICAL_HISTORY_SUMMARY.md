## Key signal installed

1. **Herodotus:** custom is king of all (read a scene's norms from inside · nomos, not nature, governs a people); call no man fortunate until he is dead (the impermanence of fortune · great cities become small); the ambiguous oracle (Croesus heard "destroy a great empire" as he wished and destroyed his own · hearing what you want to hear); free men outfight subjects (ownership as a force multiplier).
2. **Thucydides:** the real cause (the growth of Athens and the alarm it inspired in Sparta made war inevitable · the Thucydides Trap); the Melian dialogue (the strong do what they can and the weak suffer what they must · power realism read honestly, with the slaughter of Melos as its moral cost); Pericles' bounded strategy (attempt no new conquests, expose the city to no hazards · the successors abandoned the discipline); the Sicilian expedition (catastrophic over-extension far from base); stasis at Corcyra (conflict inverts values · words change their ordinary meaning · war as a rough master).
3. **Arrian:** rapidity was all in all (speed and tempo as a weapon); leading from the front (the bond earned by visibly sharing risk); cutting the Gordian knot (refusing a problem's inherited framing · "I have undone it"); hubris and the failure of self-mastery (insatiable appetite for fame · the Persian dress and proskynesis that alienated his men · the Hyphasis limit · "self-restraint is a noble thing"), read as cautionary.
4. **Engels:** supply is the basis of strategy (logistics underwrites every bold move · the rapid marches required a highly efficient logistic organization); the transport eats its own load (pack animals consume their cargo over distance · a systemic carrying-capacity limit on reach); provisions arranged in advance (intelligence and preparation secured before the march, not during).
5. **Synthesis (017, 018):** the ancient-history operating pattern (read the scene, see power clearly, hold discipline against over-reach, build the supply base first) + the pattern-library discipline and the optionality guardrail.
