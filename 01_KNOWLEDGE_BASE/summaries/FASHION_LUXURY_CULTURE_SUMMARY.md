## Key signal installed

1. **The Beautiful Fall (Drake):** the scene as a creative resource (a dense milieu of stimulation and fascination; designers do not create in a vacuum); the designer-as-star (from dressmaker to puissant style arbiter and symbolic figure, drawing on the myth and mystery of Paris couture); rivalry and two temperaments (Lagerfeld's armored prolific discipline vs Saint Laurent's tormented genius, the rivalry as an engine); aesthetic authority as an implicit, self-enforcing standard (image as the governing idea; no one entered impeccably-dressed by command, only by understanding).
2. **The Chiffon Trenches (Talley):** taste formation from outside (built in the segregated South through library study and a hefty diet of fashion glossies, taste as trainable, not inherited); editorial status and access (the unpaid, highly selective Vreeland apprenticeship; access earned by proximity to gatekeepers, then converted into one's own authority); loyalty, dignity, and the human cost (the chiffon trenches; loyalty as a noble human endeavor; guarding self-respect in a status world that can withdraw belonging).
3. **Dior by Dior (Dior):** craft justifies the effect (an ethereal appearance is only achieved by elaborate workmanship; dresses constructed like buildings); reconciling personality and discipline and editing ruthlessly (overproduce toiles, then destroy most so the collection coheres); reading the moment from conviction (the New Look as a reaction to the ration-book era; rejecting the dictates of commerce, which he swears would have no chance of surviving).
4. **The Little Dictionary of Fashion (Dior):** elegance is not money (simplicity, good taste and grooming; elegance as distinction, naturalness, care, and simplicity, with care the most important); quality over quantity and individuality over slavish trend (buy little but good; no elegant woman follows fashion slavishly).
5. **Synthesis (013):** the fashion/luxury culture and taste-systems operator toolkit (taste-as-trainable, scene-as-input, aesthetic-standard-as-discipline, craft-beneath-the-effect, overproduce-then-edit, quality-and-individuality) + the optionality guardrail · attributed to Dior by Dior.
