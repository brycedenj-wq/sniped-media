## Key signal installed

1. **The Prince (Machiavelli):** feared vs loved (be feared without being hated); the fox and the lion (cunning + force); rely on your own arms not mercenaries (own the core capability); realpolitik read honestly (effects over intention, hard moves at once / benefits over time) explicitly as analysis, NOT endorsement.
2. **On War (Clausewitz):** war as the continuation of politics by other means (means serve the end); friction ("everything is simple, but the simplest thing is difficult"); the center of gravity (concentrate force on the decisive point); the culminating point + the strength of the defensive (know when an advance over-extends).
3. **Meditations (Marcus Aurelius):** the dichotomy of control (govern your judgments, not externals); duty and the present (do the work in front of you); the inner citadel (nothing external can compel your response); memento mori (impermanence as clarity and proportion). Read as SECULAR operator-discipline, not faith.
4. **Landmark Caesar (Strassler):** command, audacity, and calculated risk; clemency as strategy (clementia that made his position credible); control your own narrative (the Commentaries as self-account, read frankly as propaganda); the bond with his soldiers (loyalty earned through shared stake).
5. **Synthesis (017, 018):** the classical-strategy operating pattern (see clearly, concentrate force, govern yourself, act decisively with restraint) + the pattern-library discipline and the optionality guardrail.
