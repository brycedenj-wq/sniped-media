## Key signal installed

1. **The Goal (Goldratt):** know the goal and its three measures ("the goal is to make money" · throughput, inventory, operational expense); dependent events + statistical fluctuations (the chain underperforms its parts); the constraint governs throughput ("an hour lost at a bottleneck"); the five focusing steps (identify, exploit, subordinate, elevate, repeat).
2. **Reengineering (Hammer/Champy):** "fundamental rethinking and radical redesign" for dramatic (not incremental) improvement; process orientation ("organized around outcomes" · "Don't Automate, Obliterate"); the clean-sheet test ("blowing up the old" · redesign from the outcome backward).
3. **The E-Myth Revisited (Gerber):** work ON your business, not IN it ("Working On Your Business" · "rather than in it"); the Fatal Assumption (being good at the craft is not running the business · Technician/Manager/Entrepreneur); the franchise prototype (build it as if it must run without you · a thinking tool, NOT a directive to franchise).
4. **Built to Sell (Warrillow):** owner-independence ("a sellable business" runs "without you" · a job vs an asset); specialize and productize (one teachable, repeatable service · "say no to other work"); recurring revenue on a standardized process (the durable operating backbone).
5. **Synthesis (014):** the operations toolkit (find the constraint, redesign from the outcome backward, work ON the system, build owner-independence and recurring revenue) + the optionality guardrail.
