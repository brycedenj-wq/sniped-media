## Key signal installed

1. **The leadership craft:** make ordinary people feel capable of extraordinary deeds (the army followed him even after Moscow); leading from the front (le petit caporal, shared risk); lead by recognition ("it is with such baubles that men are led", the Legion d'Honneur as a motivational system); energy and compartmentalized focus ("drawers in a cupboard", whole attention to one thing at a time).
2. **The operator machine:** command of detail ("one of the most unrelenting micromanagers in history"); logistics and supply discipline ("three minutes without gunpowder", an army marches on its feet); build durable institutions ("My true glory ... is my Civil Code", tested against "Is this fair? Is this useful?", "masses of granite as anchors").
3. **The operational art:** concentrate force on the decisive point ("attacks must not be disseminated, but concentrated"), the spine Clausewitz would later theorize.
4. **The self-made outsider:** the Corsican arriviste who made himself through relentless self-education ("read constantly, especially history books") and serial reinvention, embodying careers-open-to-talent.
5. **Self-myth and narrative control:** the bulletins (he "didn't consider himself to be on oath"), the press, the St Helena memoir that burnished the legend, echoing Caesar's Commentaries.
6. **The cautionary collapse (read against HISTORICAL_BIOGRAPHY + CLASSICAL_HISTORY):** the self-defeating Continental System ("protectionism that brought him down", his own army in "uniforms made in Halifax and Leeds"); the appetite that would not relinquish (the Cincinnatus pose vs Washington's actual relinquishment; "more glory than is necessary"; "insatiable hubris"; Russia 1812 as the culminating point); the honest human cost (the wars, the "unprecedentedly efficient surveillance system" and censorship, the 1802 reintroduction of slavery).
7. **Synthesis (014):** the modern-command operating pattern (energy + system + tempo + recognition + durable institutions) paired with its over-reach counter-lesson and the optionality guardrail.
