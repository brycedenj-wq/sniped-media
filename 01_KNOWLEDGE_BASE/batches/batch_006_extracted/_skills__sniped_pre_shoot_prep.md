# SNIPED Pre-Shoot Prep Skill

The day-of-shoot launch protocol. Output target: zero surprises on shoot day.

## MANDATORY READING

1. `/Users/sniper/Downloads/    SNIPED_OS/05_PRODUCTION/checklist_pre_shoot_day_of.md` · the locked checklist
2. `/Users/sniper/Downloads/    SNIPED_OS/05_PRODUCTION/SOP_reset_shoot_day.md` (if Reset)
3. `/Users/sniper/Downloads/    SNIPED_OS/05_PRODUCTION/SOP_strategic_free.md` (if FreeCollab / Cultural Doc)

## INVOKE WHEN
- Shoot is today or tomorrow
- "What do I need to prep"
- After a new shoot books
- Building shoot kit / location plan

## OUTPUT
Run the locked checklist categorically:
1. **Gear** · camera bodies, lenses, batteries, cards, lighting kit, backup
2. **Location** · address confirmed, parking, contact onsite, alt indoor if outdoor weather
3. **Talent** · client confirmed time, MUA confirmed (if Sun shoot), wardrobe brought / staged
4. **Direction Stack notes** · the diagnostic answers from intake, the chapter / pose direction
5. **Shot list** · the locked frame targets (8-12 Heroes for Reset, more for higher tiers)
6. **Contingencies** · what if it rains, what if MUA cancels, what if client late

## REFUSE
- Skipping the gear check ("I'll just bring what I always bring")
- Same-day prep with no day-before pass
- Ignoring weather / logistics contingencies
