# Mom Test Customer Conversation

**Skill ID:** 4.1  
**Category:** Customer Discovery & Market Validation  
**Source Document(s):** CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY_FRAMEWORKS.docx

## Purpose

Design and execute customer conversations that produce reliable data by talking about the customer's life (not your idea), anchoring to past behavior (not future promises), and listening more than talking.

## When to Use

When validating business ideas, new features, or market opportunities. When you need real data from customers rather than opinions.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Choose a focused, findable customer segment. If you can't describe who they are and where to find them, slice finer.
2. Decide your Big 3 learning goals. Include at least one question that terrifies you.
3. Decide ideal next steps and commitments before the meeting. Know what you'll push for.
4. Do desk research first. Don't waste a conversation on questions Google can answer.
5. Frame the meeting using VFWPA: Vision (half-sentence, no product mention), Framing (where you're at), Weakness (your specific gap), Pedestal (why they specifically can help), Ask (ask for help, not a meeting).
6. During the conversation: Ask only questions that pass the Mom Test. Deflect compliments immediately. Anchor fluff to specifics ('When's the last time that happened?'). Dig beneath feature requests ('Why do you want that?').
7. Press for commitment at the end: Time, reputation, or money. If you leave without a clear next step or rejection, the meeting was pointless.
8. After the conversation: Review notes with the team. Transfer to permanent storage. Update beliefs and plans. Decide the next 3 big questions.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- Conversation notes using signal symbols (emotions, pains, goals, obstacles, workarounds, money signals, follow-ups)
- A commitment outcome (specific next step or explicit rejection)
- Updated belief log (what changed based on the conversation)
- Refined Big 3 questions for the next batch
