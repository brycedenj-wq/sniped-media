## THE FRAME (the 3 named playbooks)
1. **Gress** · real subject + AI background + Photoshop assembly. SNIPED's Track B is this playbook.
2. **Petty** · "vending machine" framing · AI generates options, photographer curates. SNIPED uses this for IG content variation, not client work.
3. **Northrup** · pre-viz upgrade · AI generates the mood board / pre-vis, real shoot executes. SNIPED uses this for shoot planning.

The lane SNIPED OCCUPIES:
- Gress-style real-subject composite for IG creative engine (Track B)
- Anti-identity-AI on client work (per `sniped-ai-sentiment`)
- Methodology + real photography as the named premium tier

What SNIPED REFUSES:
- Full AI-generated subjects (Petty's vending machine on faces)
- AI-generated portfolios with no real shoot (some competitors do this · SNIPED doesn't)

## OUTPUT

- Copying competitor positioning directly (the moat is being different in this lane)
- Recommending the Petty vending machine for client subjects
- Generic "use AI to create more content" framing without lane specificity
