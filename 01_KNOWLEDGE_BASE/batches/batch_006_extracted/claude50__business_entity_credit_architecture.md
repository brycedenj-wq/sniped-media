# Business Entity & Credit Architecture

**Skill ID:** 7.3  
**Category:** Business Operations & Finance  
**Source Document(s):** business_finance_extraction.docx

## Purpose

Set up the legal, financial, and credit infrastructure for a new business following the Entity Separation Framework and Business Credit Independence Model.

## When to Use

When evaluating business operations, financial structure, or revenue growth opportunities.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Form the LLC: File with your state, obtain EIN from IRS, establish the legal separation between personal and business.
2. Set up business banking: Open business checking/savings at 3 banks that pull from different credit bureaus (Chase/Experian, US Bank/TransUnion, Truist/Equifax).
3. Deposit consistently: $50-$100 weekly for 3-6 months to build banking relationships.
4. Build business credit in stages: Start with starter vendors (net-30 accounts), progress to retail/fleet credit, then Visa/Mastercard/Amex, then auto/equipment financing.
5. Optimize personal credit in parallel: Keep utilization under 10%, maintain 8+ year credit age, space inquiries 90 days apart.
6. Evaluate S-Corp election: Once revenue justifies it, elect S-Corp status to eliminate self-employment tax on distributions.
7. Implement tax architecture: Document all deductible expenses (travel, technology, professional services, meals) with business purpose.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- LLC formation checklist with all required filings
- Business credit building timeline (staged sequence)
- Banking relationship strategy (3-bank approach with bureau coverage)
- Tax deduction tracking system
