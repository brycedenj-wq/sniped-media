## Section 2 · The single constraint (15 min)

**The question:** what is the ONE bottleneck that, if cleared, makes 3 other problems disappear next month?

Run the bottleneck through this filter:

| Layer | Symptom | Bottleneck candidate |
|---|---|---|
| Top of funnel | Low VIB count or low reply rate | Targeting · script · cadence |
| Middle of funnel | Replies but no Discovery calls | Reply scripts · objection handling |
| Conversion | Discovery calls but no Reset bookings | Pricing · positioning · close discipline |
| Delivery | Resets delivered late or under-quality | Edit-hour bottleneck · gear · hire timing |
| Upsell | Reset clients not buying upgrades | Pixieset config · email sequence · price |
| Repeat | No Op Kit conversions | Day-30 message · Reset case studies · pitch quality |
| Audience | Content posted but no inbound | Cadence · format · platform mix |
| Operations | Manual work eating hours | Templates · automations · delegation |
| Founder | Drift / avoidance / motion-not-progress | Cockpit constraint · 2-min rule · drift correctives |
