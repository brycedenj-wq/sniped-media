# Chapter Intake v1

Locked 2026-05-12. The 4-mode subject intake system that feeds every SNIPED chapter rollout. Built for speed first, depth second. The system never blocks on intake · there is always an escape valve (Mode 4 silent).

Companion doc to `/05_PRODUCTION/chapter_rollout_doctrine_v1.md`.

---

## THE FOUR MODES

| Mode | Time for subject | Use frequency | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mode 1 · Ultra-Light DM** | 90 seconds | 80% of chapters | Casting-call collaborators, creative portraits, fast chapters, default |
| **Mode 2 · Expanded DM** | 3 minutes | 15-20% of chapters | Stronger subjects, returning collaborators, Structure B/C chapters |
| **Mode 3 · Full Form** | 5 minutes | <5% of chapters | Lineage subjects, multi-session productions, anyone you're building a longer relationship with, founders considered for future Level 2 features |
| **Mode 4 · Silent** | 0 seconds | Escape valve | Same-day chapters, no-intake-time situations, environmental world-building, self-shoots |

**Default:** Mode 1. Escalate to Mode 2/3 only when the chapter warrants it. Use Mode 4 when speed > intake.

---

## MODE 1 · ULTRA-LIGHT DM (the default · paste-and-send)

Sent via IG DM or text. 3 core questions. Use for 80% of chapters.

**Paste-and-send template:**

```
Hey [name] · locked in for the shoot. Couple quick things before we run so the chapter lands right.

1. Three words for how you want this chapter to feel?
2. Any colors, words, or themes you want avoided? (wardrobe, props, captions · anything)
3. Cool with me posting the chapter closer as a collab post on both our grids?

That's it · 2 mins. Sending you the wardrobe + location details right after.
```

That's the message. Send as-is. Adapt the first sentence to match your voice with that specific subject if needed.

**Subject's expected reply time:** 90 seconds.

---

## MODE 2 · EXPANDED DM (5 questions · for stronger subjects)

For subjects with more cluster value, return collaborators, or chapters you want deeper material for.

**Paste-and-send template:**

```
Hey [name] · pumped for the shoot. Couple quick things before we run so the chapter lands right.

1. Three words for how you want this chapter to feel?
2. Anything you want these images to communicate about you specifically?
3. Any colors, words, or themes you want avoided?
4. Any words you do NOT want used when posting (titles, descriptors, anything that misreads you)?
5. Cool with me posting the chapter closer as a collab post on both our grids?

One bonus if you have time · one sentence about where you are in life right now (helps me edit with context, won't be quoted publicly).

Takes 3 mins max. Sending wardrobe + location details after.
```

## MODE 3 · FULL FORM (Google Form or Notion · 13 questions · for high-effort chapters)

Hosted as a Google Form or Notion form. Sent at least 48 hours before the shoot. Used when the subject is a return collaborator, a lineage subject, or anyone you're building a longer relationship with.

### THE SNIPED CHAPTER INTAKE

**Form intro copy:**

> Quick intake before our shoot. Takes about 5 minutes. The answers shape the chapter direction, the editorial register, and what gets said in the post copy. Nothing here gets shared publicly without your approval.

**Section 1 · Basics (30 sec)**

1. What first name do you want on the chapter?
2. What's your IG handle for tagging and collab post invite?
3. Any other handles or credits you want named? (work account, brand, project)

**Section 2 · The chapter's feel (90 sec)**

4. Three words for how you want this chapter to feel.
5. Anything you want these images to communicate about you specifically? (1-2 sentences)
6. Is there a personal moment, season, or chapter of life you want this shoot to reflect? (Optional · examples: just moved to LA, finished a project, new role, anything you'd want documented)

**Section 3 · Visual register (60 sec)**

7. Favorite color, if you have one. (Doesn't dictate the edit · just signal for wardrobe/props)
8. Any colors you do NOT want featured.
9. Music, film, or fashion references that point at what you want. (Optional · one or two)

**Section 4 · Voice and approvals (60 sec)**

10. One sentence about where you are in life right now. (Used as edit context · not quoted publicly unless you approve.)
11. Any words or phrases you do NOT want used when posting? (Examples: "model," "boss babe," "muse," anything that misreads you)
12. Approval preference before posting · pick one:
    - Just send me the cover/closer to approve before posting
    - Send me everything to approve before posting
    - Trust your call, just tag me
    - Want to talk through it first

**Section 5 · The chapter closer (30 sec)**

13. The chapter closes with an editorial-style closer post (Chapter Card) tagged as a collab on both our grids. You good with that?
    - Yes
    - Yes but want to see it first
    - Not sure · let's discuss

End. 5 minutes max.

## MODE 4 · SILENT (no intake · escape valve)

For: same-day or short-notice chapters, casting-call collaborators where there's no time for intake, environmental world-building shoots where the subject is incidental, self-shoots, or any chapter where intake friction would kill momentum.

**The constraint when running silent:**
- The chapter's editorial argument is decided by photographer, not subject
- The Chapter Card's cover line is photographer-side editorial (not pulled from intake)
- The caption stays restrained (no personal claims about the subject, no quoted material)
- The subject's first name and IG handle are still credited
- If subject approval is needed before posting, ask via DM after the edit: "Cool to post these tomorrow?"

**This is the escape valve. The system never blocks on intake.** Approximately 5% of chapters run silent · but it's available for any chapter where the alternative would be missing the rhythm.

## INTAKE ANSWER → CHAPTER DECISION MAPPING

Each intake field drives a specific decision in the rollout:

| Intake field | Drives |
|---|---|
| Q1 · Preferred name | The `[FirstName]` in every caption (`Yae · 01`) and in the Chapter Card masthead chapter line |
| Q2 · IG handle | Collab post invite target + first-comment credit |
| Q3 · Other credits | First-comment credit block |
| Q4 · Three words for the chapter | HERO environment choice from the 7-environment rotation · maps feel words to environments (see Section below) |
| Q5 · What images should communicate | Direction Stack protocol selection at the shoot · which of the 10 protocols to deploy |
| Q6 · Personal moment | Edit context only · informs which frames carry the chapter's editorial argument · NEVER quoted in post copy without explicit Q12 approval |
| Q7 · Favorite color | Honored loosely in wardrobe styling · does NOT override locked v3 LUXURY grade |
| Q8 · Avoided colors | Hard ban from wardrobe, props, environment |
| Q9 · References | Mood reference only · informs environment selection · does NOT change the SNIPED visual register |
| Q10 · One sentence about where you are | Edit context · never quoted in caption without explicit Q12 approval |
| Q11 · Banned words | Hard ban from captions, first-comment credits, and any SNIPED-side copy about the subject |
| Q12 · Approval preference | Workflow gate · whether the Card and captions go through subject review before posting |
| Q13 · Collab post acceptance | Card collab invite default · if "not sure," ship Card as SNIPED solo instead, follow up after to gauge comfort for next chapter |

## THREE-WORD → ENVIRONMENT MAPPING (quick reference for HERO planning)

When the subject answers Q4 with three words, those words guide the HERO environment choice from the 7-environment rotation:

| Subject's feel words map to → | Environment |
|---|---|
| quiet · grounded · deliberate · composed · severe · concrete | Brutalist Monument |
| clean · sharp · engineered · methodical · precise · technical | Industrial Minimal |
| still · alone · internal · weighted · focused · quiet | Monochromatic Void |
| considered · slow · observed · refined · structural · curated | Sculptural Gallery |
| moving · present · lived-in · urban · alive · streetwise | Cinematic Urban |
| open · expansive · surreal · dreamlike · atmospheric · poetic | Organic Surreal |
| forward · technical · future · constructed · optic · cinematic-tech | Futurist Editorial |

This is a guide, not a rule. The photographer's read of the subject still leads. The intake just informs.

## FORM DISCIPLINE RULES

1. **Send the intake at least 48 hours before the shoot** for Mode 2 and Mode 3. The answers inform shoot direction. Mode 1 DM can be sent anytime in the 24-48 hour window.
2. **Read the answers before the shoot.** Bring 2-3 relevant phrases from the intake to mention during the shoot (signals you read it, builds trust).
3. **Treat the intake as confidential to SNIPED.** Do NOT share answers between subjects.
4. **If a subject doesn't fill out the form, the chapter still proceeds** · default to Mode 4 silent and write more conservative captions.
5. **Q6, Q10, Q11 are confidentiality-protected** · they inform edit decisions but never appear in public-facing copy without explicit Q12 approval.
6. **Q13 controls collab post default** · if subject is "not sure," ship Card as SNIPED solo and gauge comfort for the next chapter.

## THE SILENT-MODE FALLBACK FOR CARDS

The Chapter Card's cover line is built from whatever intake material is available:

| Available material | Cover line source |
|---|---|
| Full form intake answers (Mode 3) | Pull from Q4 (three words) and Q10 (one sentence) |
| Expanded DM intake (Mode 2) | Pull from the three-word answer + Q4 communicate answer |
| Ultra-light DM intake (Mode 1) | Pull from the three-word answer |
| Silent mode (Mode 4) | Photographer-side editorial decision based on what the photography produced |

The Chapter Card never breaks because intake didn't happen. The Card adapts to whatever input level was used. The system absorbs intake variance.

- HERO environment likely: Industrial Minimal

**Example C · Subject Q4 = "moving, present, alive":**
- Cover line draft candidates: `Year one in the city.` / `Present tense.` / `She moves.`
- HERO environment likely: Cinematic Urban

**Example D · Subject Q4 = "open, surreal, atmospheric":**
- Cover line draft candidates: `The afternoon she stayed.` / `Slow burn.` / `Atmosphere study.`
- HERO environment likely: Organic Surreal

Cover lines are always drafted fresh per chapter · these are just shape examples.

## INTAKE STORAGE

Per-chapter intake answers are saved to:

```
/SNIPED_OS/_archive/chapters/CH##_[firstname]/intake.md
```

Format:

```markdown
# CH## · [FirstName] · Intake

Mode: [1 / 2 / 3 / 4]
Date sent: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Date received: [YYYY-MM-DD]

## Q1 · Preferred name
[Answer]

## Q2 · IG handle
[Answer]

[... etc ...]

## Edit notes
[Photographer's own notes from reading the intake · what to bring to the shoot, what to avoid, what register to hold]
```

The intake file gets read at the start of the edit pass. Confidential answers (Q6, Q10, Q11) inform decisions but stay private.
