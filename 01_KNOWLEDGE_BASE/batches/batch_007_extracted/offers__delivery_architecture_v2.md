# Delivery Architecture v2 · Premium Curated Abundance

**The 3-tier delivery model that fits the actual production scale of how SNIPED shoots, while protecting edit hours, the brand, and the upsell economics.**

This is a v2 OVERRIDE document. Where it conflicts with v1 (Reset MSA Section 2 · SOP_capture_to_delivery Phase 3 · SOP_strategic_free deliverables · SOP_post_delivery upsell · pixieset_config gallery template), this one wins from this date forward.

The 1-hero framing in v1 was calibrated for hypothetical TFP scenarios at install. The 6-shoot weekend reality (real direction, multiple looks, tethered workflow, real production energy) made it operationally false. v2 corrects.

---

## 0 · The principle

**Premium curated abundance, not manufactured scarcity.**

A SNIPED session produces real volume because the methodology produces real volume. Pretending the deliverable is one image when the experience produces 80-300 strong frames creates an integrity gap. v2 closes that gap.

The discipline is not in delivering less. It is in **edit-tiering** the delivery so the polished work stays editorially polished, the wider gallery shows production reality, and the upsell path is value-clear, not arbitrarily gated.

---

## 1 · The three edit tiers (the operational core)

Every frame from a shoot lives in exactly one of these tiers.

| Tier | Edit work | Time per frame | Purpose | Quality signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HERO** | Lightroom base + Evoto clinical retouch + Photoshop final pass | 10-15 min | Editorial deployment · LinkedIn header · press · deck · paid licensing | SNIPED-grade · the published work |
| **SELECT** | Lightroom base + color grade + crop. **No Evoto retouch.** | 1-2 min | Personal social · phone use · texting friends · personal portfolio | Production-quality · not editorial-final |
| **PROOF** | Lightroom batch preset only · all picks pass through this | 15-30 sec | Gallery navigation · client selection · upsell discovery | Web-only · not for deployment |

**The economics of the tiering** (per typical Reset shoot, ~120-180 raw frames captured):

```
~120-180 raws
        ↓ AI cull + manual final pick (15 min)
  60-100 picks
        ↓ Lightroom batch preset (15-30 sec each · all picks)
  60-100 PROOFS
        ↓ Promote 30-50 to SELECT tier (1-2 min each)
  30-50 SELECTS
        ↓ Promote 8-12 to HERO tier (10-15 min each)
  8-12 HEROES
```

**Total edit time per Reset: 2-3.5 hours.** Down from 5-7 in the v1 spec. This is the leverage win that makes 2-3 Resets/month sustainable on a field-engineer schedule.

## 3 · The Sprint · NEW sub-tier (formalized)

The user's market reality requires a paid-but-accessible tier for the warm-network / dancer / model / referral lane that doesn't have $1,500 budget.

**The Sprint · $750 · 1 look · 30-min on-camera · 5-8 heroes + 20-30 selects · 3-day delivery**

Critical positioning rules:
- **Never pitched cold.** Reset remains the only offer pitched in cold VIB outreach. Sprint is offered ONLY to warm intros / referrals / network-driven inquiries that genuinely cannot do $1,500.
- **Triggered by external context:** dance recital window · family event · Tuskegee alumni network · Sasha-referral lane · Pearl's aesthetics network. The reduced price is the relationship, not the work.
- **Same Direction Stack diagnostic on set.** The methodology doesn't change. The volume + price changes.
- **Same edit-tier discipline.** Heroes are still SNIPED-grade. The price reduction is in scope (1 look · 30 min · fewer heroes), not quality.
- **No discount language.** It's "the Sprint package · $750." Not "the Reset, but $750." Different SKU.

**Why this works:**
- Maintains offer-ladder discipline (Reset stays $1,500 cold; Sprint is warm-only)
- Captures revenue from the relationship-network lane that wouldn't book at $1,500
- Builds Reputation Engine referral nodes (every Sprint client is also a future Reset referrer)
- Edit math is favorable: $750 / 2 hr edit = $375/hr · comparable to Reset's $/hr

**Why this could break (warning):**
- If Sprint pricing leaks to the LinkedIn cold outreach lane, Reset positioning erodes
- If Sprint heroes are produced at lower quality than Reset heroes, brand bleeds
- If Sprint book-out rate exceeds Reset book-out rate, SNIPED becomes a $750 photographer in market perception

**Operational rule:** the Sprint is offered only when the inquiry comes through a warm referral path documented in Notion CRM. Cold inbound is always quoted at Reset $1,500. If a cold inquiry asks "do you do anything cheaper," the answer is **scope flex on Reset, not Sprint**.

## 4 · The upsell architecture · v2

The v1 upsell ($80/image · 48-hr window) was calibrated for the 1-hero model. v2 unbundles the upsell across the three tiers.

### 4.1 The three upsell paths

**Path A · Select-to-Hero upgrade** (highest volume)

Client wants a specific Select promoted to Hero quality (full Evoto retouch + Photoshop). They mark the frame in Pixieset; SNIPED produces the Hero version.

- **Price:** $60 per single upgrade · **Bundle: 5 upgrades for $250** (saves $50) · 10 upgrades for $450 (saves $150)
- **Window:** 14 days from gallery delivery (matches gallery expiry)
- **Why this works:** the Select already exists; the upgrade is just the additional retouch labor. $60/image at 10-15 min edit = $240/hr for the upsell labor.
- **Conversion expectation:** 30-50% of Reset clients buy at least one upgrade · average 2-4 upgrades · average upsell revenue $120-240/Reset

**Path B · Additional Selects** (rare; gallery is usually generous enough)

Client wants Proofs promoted to Selects (color-graded). Less common because Selects pool is already 30-50 frames.

- **Price:** $30 per single Select promotion · Bundle 5 for $120
- **Window:** 14 days
- **When to expect this:** family events where the client wants more group coverage · institutional documentation where the institution wants their own use of more frames

**Path C · Commercial license upgrade** (founder / brand context only · highest margin)

Client wants to use Hero or Select frames for paid advertising / branded campaigns / commercial deployment beyond the personal/internal-use license included.

- **Price:** scoped per use · $250-1,000 per Hero for 12-month limited campaign use · $1,500-3,000 per Hero for unlimited 12-month use
- **Window:** any time within 24 months of original shoot
- **When this triggers:** Reset client gets press · founder portrait used in a brand campaign · institutional photo licensed by sponsor brand
- **Critical:** v1's "personal and internal business use only" license language stays in Reset MSA. Any commercial use outside that requires explicit license upgrade. This is documented in `/02_CONTRACTS/02_reset_msa.md` Section 5.

### 4.2 The Pixieset gallery structure (v2 · three sub-collections)

Replace the v1 two-sub-collection structure (Selections · Additional) with three:

**Sub-collection 1 · `Heroes`**
- Visibility: visible to client at gallery delivery
- Download enabled: yes (high-res JPG, included)
- Store enabled: no (these are the contracted tier)
- Display order: SNIPED's curated order (strongest 3-5 frames first)

**Sub-collection 2 · `Selects`**
- Visibility: visible to client
- Download enabled: yes (high-res JPG, included)
- Store enabled: yes (Path B · upgrade-to-Hero pricing displayed inline)
- Display order: SNIPED's order

**Sub-collection 3 · `Proofs · upgrade gallery`**
- Visibility: visible to client (with banner: "Available for upgrade · 14 days")
- Download enabled: NO (purchase first to access)
- Store enabled: yes (Path A · Select-to-Hero or Proof-to-Select pricing)
- Display order: SNIPED's order
- Watermarked: light SNIPED watermark on Proofs only (not Heroes/Selects)

**Window mechanic:** all three sub-collections live for 14 days. The Proofs sub-collection becomes hidden after 14 days; Heroes + Selects stay accessible for the full gallery window. The 48-hour artificial scarcity is GONE; the natural gallery expiry is the only time pressure.

## 6 · The editing workflow · v2

Replaces SOP_capture_to_delivery.md Phase 3.

### Phase 3 · Day 1-2 · Cull + 3-tier edit pass

**Pass 1 · AI Cull (10-15 min)**
- Open Lightroom Classic. Import raws.
- Run **Lightroom AI Cull** (the built-in AI culling tool, or Aftershoot if integrated)
- Trust the AI for the elimination layer (closed eyes, OOF, blurred). Manual override for any AI-rejected frame that BJ recognizes as a keeper.

**Pass 2 · Manual final pick (10-15 min)**
- Filter to AI picks. Star-rate ⭐⭐⭐ for the 60-100 strongest frames.
- These are the **picks** that pass through the rest of the pipeline.

**Pass 3 · Proof grade · batch preset (15-30 sec across all picks)**
- Apply the SNIPED batch preset to all 60-100 picks
- Auto-WB off, Camera Standard profile, lens corrections on, baseline tone curve
- Output: all picks are now Proof-grade (gallery-ready, navigation-ready)

**Pass 4 · Select promotion (1-2 min × 30-40 frames = 45-60 min)**
- From the 60-100 proofs, promote 30-40 to Select tier
- Manual color tone refinement, crop, exposure adjustment
- HSL tweaks for the locked color block (saturate dominant tonal family)
- Output: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ rating, ready for Pixieset Selects sub-collection

**Pass 5 · Hero promotion (10-15 min × 8-12 frames = 80-180 min)**
- From the 30-40 Selects, promote 8-12 to Hero tier
- Lightroom export to TIFF → Evoto clinical retouch → Photoshop final pass (only as needed)
- Output: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ rating, the editorial-grade frames

**Total per Reset: ~2-3.5 hours edit time.** Distributed over Day 1 and Day 2 of the SLA, leaves Days 3-4 as buffer for travel/engineering/life.

### Edit-time tracking

Track per-shoot edit time in Notion Shoots DB (Edit hours actual field). Moving 8-week average should stay below **3 hours/Reset**. If it climbs:

- Above 3 hrs: review Pass 4 + 5 efficiency. Are you over-retouching? Tighten the SNIPED Evoto preset to be more aggressive default.
- Above 4 hrs: real bottleneck signal. Trigger for retoucher hire moves up.
- Above 5 hrs: SLA at risk. Pause new Reset bookings for 2 weeks and reset the workflow.
