# Email · Referral ask (sent Day 30-45 after delivery)

**Trigger:** Reset client has confirmed satisfaction (testimonial received) AND is at the 30-45 day mark where the photos have been deployed and the social signal has happened.

**Send via:** the same channel used to book.

**Subject:** `Quick question · two specific founders`

**Body:**

```
[First name],

The Reset shipped last month. The [LINKEDIN HEADER / PRESS COVER / DECK PORTRAIT] has been running well from what I can see externally.

One quick ask · narrowly framed:

Are there two founders or operators in your network who would benefit from the same Direction Stack diagnostic? Specifically: someone whose visual presence on LinkedIn is sitting below the operational level of their business, and who you would actually feel good introducing to me.

Not a "blast my newsletter" ask. Just two named introductions, made personally if it lands.

I will draft the intro language so it is zero-friction on your end · just confirm and forward.

· BJ
```

---

## When they say yes

Send the intro template within 24 hours (not next week):

```
[First name],

Thanks. Here is intro language you can edit and forward. Two versions depending on style:

Short (for a quick LinkedIn DM):
"[Referee first name] · meet BJ at Sniped Media. He runs a 10-protocol diagnostic on set, the Reset is $1,500, and the work is operator-coded not artist-coded. I went through it last month · take the call if your visual presence on LinkedIn is something you have been thinking about."

Longer (for an email forward):
[2-3 sentence version with their specific protocol observation about the referee]

Whichever feels right. Or write your own. Either works.

· BJ
```

---

## When they refer

- Send the new prospect a warm-intro DM within 48 hours (not next week).
- Open with: "[Referrer first name] connected us · they mentioned [SPECIFIC CONTEXT]."
- Skip the cold VIB step. Go straight to: "Worth a 10-min conversation if the gap is interesting?"
- Calendly Discovery link inline.

---

## When they decline / go silent

Do nothing. Do not follow up on a referral ask. The relationship is the asset; pressing erodes it.
