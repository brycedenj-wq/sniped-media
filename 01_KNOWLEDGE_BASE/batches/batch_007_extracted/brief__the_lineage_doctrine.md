# THE LINEAGE DOCTRINE

Locked 2026-05-12. The ethical and authorial foundation of SNIPED's cultural documentation work.

## The single rule

**The work documents from inside the lineage, not from outside it.**

This is the line. It is what separates SNIPED's body of work from anthropology, exploitation, vanity, and creator-extraction. It is the irreplaceable element. AI cannot generate it. Competitor photographers cannot copy it.

## Why this matters (the operational defense)

### 1. The ethical defense against extractive documentation

Photography in Black cultural environments has a fraught history. "Black trauma porn" extracts pain for editorial credit. "Black joy" performative flattens subjects into a saleable category. "Anthropology" frames subjects as specimens.

SNIPED's lineage immunity: BJ shows up at Emmanuel 33rd because he came up in Florida HBCU + Southern Black church culture. The documentation is participation, not observation.

The discipline that follows:
- Multi-visit relationships before any serious documentation
- Subject's name + context + permissions ALWAYS in captions
- Reciprocity ledger (framed prints, paid family portraits, copies of essays, institution's own use rights)
- Refusal of single-visit cultural tourism regardless of visual potential
- The 10-year test for cultural documentation: "Would the subject, 10 years from now, looking back at this frame and caption, feel honored? If no, don't publish."

### 2. The authorship moat against AI

AI can generate convincing Black portraits. It cannot generate a 10-year-and-growing relationship with a specific bishop in a specific COGIC church. It cannot generate the multi-year coach-athlete arc. It cannot generate the Tuskegee alumni network depth.

The Year-10 analog set (Khalil Joseph · Bradford Young · Teju Cole · Carrie Mae Weems · Dawoud Bey) operates the same defense. Each one is unleavable because their authorship is structurally inseparable from their lived lineage.

### 3. The cluster-depth advantage (per Hit Makers · "Audience of My Audience")

Lineage-based work compounds in specific clusters. A photographer who documents Bishop Peters' church for 5 years isn't competing with all LA photographers · he's the only photographer at all in that specific cluster.

The cluster is small. The cluster is deep. The cluster is trusted. The cluster transfers.

A founder in the LA Black tech ecosystem who attends Emmanuel 33rd doesn't need to be sold SNIPED. They've seen the work in the church. The lineage IS the marketing.

### 4. The cultural-recognition arc

Year 10 vision: the named visual documentarian of LA's emerging Black founder/operator/artist/athlete culture.

This title is granted by the culture, not claimed by the photographer. The grant requires:
- Multi-year consistent presence (5-10 years minimum)
- Documentation that the documented endorse (not just tolerate)
- Body of work that becomes a primary cultural document (not just a photo archive)
- Institutional placement (gallery, monograph, museum collection)

None of these are achievable by a photographer documenting from outside the lineage. All are achievable by one documenting from inside.

## How to apply the doctrine

### When considering a cultural documentation engagement

Five questions, in order. If any answer is no, decline the engagement.

1. **Lineage connection.** Does this subject / institution / community sit inside one of the 5 lineages? If it's adjacent, what's the multi-visit relationship arc that would deepen it?
2. **Reciprocity mechanic.** What does SNIPED owe back to the documented party? Frame prints. Copies of the essay. Family portraits at the event. The institution's own use rights. Pay subjects whose work directly produces commercial revenue. The reciprocity is named BEFORE the shoot.
3. **Multi-year intent.** Is this a one-shoot extraction or a multi-year arc? Single-visit cultural documentation produces extractive work regardless of visual quality. Decline if there's no return path.
4. **Editorial dignity default.** Will the frames be made with the same composed-severe-architectural register the commercial Reset receives? Or will they slip into "Black joy performative" or "anthropological distance"? If the latter, decline.
5. **The 10-year subject test.** Would the subject, 10 years from now, feel honored by this frame and caption? If no, decline.

### When considering an Art Series subject

The Art Series is canon-anchored personal practice (9 photographer studies). The lineage doctrine applies:
- The subject should sit inside one of the 5 lineages
- The photographer being studied (Avedon, Eggleston, etc.) provides the methodological lens
- The subject + the lineage + the photographer's lesson combine into the frame
- An Art Series frame of a random LA model is weaker than one of a specific lineage-connected subject because the lineage carries half the meaning

### When refusing work

Three patterns trigger doctrine-based refusal:
1. **Tourist documentation** · "I want to shoot at a Black church to add diversity to my portfolio" · refused
2. **Extractive lookbook** · "Brand wants Black culture as a backdrop for their product" · refused
3. **Trauma-extractive editorial** · "Magazine wants documentation of Black struggle for an issue" · refused

The refusal is not about the subject's race. It's about whether the work is being done from inside or outside the lineage. A wealthy white founder photographed in his DTLA lab is also inside SNIPED's lineage if the engineering/operator/Series-A-tech-LA-cluster fits. A Black model photographed for a streetwear lookbook with no relationship is OUTSIDE the lineage.

## What the doctrine does NOT mean

- It does not mean SNIPED only photographs Black subjects. The lineage includes the engineering training / LA founder cluster / DTLA operator culture, which spans demographics.
- It does not mean SNIPED's commercial work is restricted by lineage. Reset clients can come from anywhere. The lineage rule applies to cultural documentation work, not paid commercial work.
- It does not mean other photographers should be discouraged from documenting Black culture. The doctrine is SNIPED's authorial defense, not a gatekeeping claim on the culture.
- It does not mean visual quality is secondary to lineage authenticity. Both are required. A lineage-connected shoot that produces weak frames doesn't ship; a non-lineage shoot that would produce strong frames is refused.
