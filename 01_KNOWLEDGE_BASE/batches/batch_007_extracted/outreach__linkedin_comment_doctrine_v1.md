# LinkedIn Comment Execution Doctrine v1

Source: LinkedIn execution chat debrief. Migrated to SNIPED_OS as canonical reference 2026-05-12.

This doc is the daily LinkedIn comment execution playbook. Use it to brief any chat (Claude Code, ChatGPT, Gemini) on the format and pace. Drop the Tier 0 CRM list into the chat alongside this doc.

---

## Purpose

Daily LinkedIn comment execution. Paste a feed dump, get back ready-to-post comments on founder content. Target: 5-10 comments/day, LA founders only, audit CTA pattern. Designed to surface SNIPED in the comments of CRM Tier 0 founders so that when a VIB DM lands, the profile already feels familiar.

---

## Locked context

- **Offer:** The Reset · $1,500 · 1 look · 20 final images · 5-day turnaround · 50% non-refundable deposit
- **CTA pattern:** "DM 'audit' if you want me to look at yours"
- **Audience:** LA founders. Never photographers.
- **Tier:** Tier 0 only. Founders in CRM.
- **Pace:** 5-10 comments per day, spread across 2-3 sessions for natural feed presence
- **Burn rules:** No commenting on photographer accounts. No global feeds.
- **Reply protocol:** Same-day reply to DMs from Board sends

---

## The format that works

For each post:
1. Soft opener (`yeah` / `honestly` / `this is where` / `curious how` / `this part`)
2. Reference a specific word or phrase from the original post
3. Slightly broken sentence structure (not polished, not corporate)
4. 1-2 sentences max
5. Observational or slightly contrarian
6. No emojis, no corporate tone

The eye should read: "person who saw the post and had an actual thought" not "person reading from a script."

---

## Comment tone rules (locked)

- Sounds like "I read your post and had a thought" not "here's my take"
- Mid-conversation feel, not tweet-quote feel
- No "great post" energy
- No AI-clean structure
- Slight imperfection is the goal
- NO em dashes (lifetime ban applies)

---

## What worked

- **Pre-built bank of 20+ comments** ready to drop spread over 2-3 days. More natural than burning all in one session.
- **2 comment options per post** lets you pick which one matches your voice that day.
- **Soft openers** (`yeah` / `honestly` / `this part`) killed the AI-clean tone.
- **Calling out the post's own language directly** (one word from their post) made it feel real.

## What failed in prior sessions

### 1. Wasted cycles on global feeds
Searching "founder" alone pulled LATAM, India, EU posts. Burnt time on irrelevant content.

**Fix:** Search `LA founder` or `Los Angeles startup`, past 24 hours, latest sort.

### 2. Commenting outside CRM Tier 0
Was commenting on whatever LA founder showed up in feed.

**Fix:** Lead next chat with a list of Tier 0 names and only paste their posts.

### 3. Recycled top match feed
Same posts kept appearing across sessions (Jake Hurwitz, Laura Nelson, Krishna Lakamsani showed up 3x).

**Fix:** Switch to "Latest" + "Past 24 hours" every session, not "Top Match Past Month".

### 4. Slow start every chat
Spent first 3-5 messages calibrating tone before format clicked.

**Fix:** Paste this doc into new chat first message.

### 5. Bloated responses early
Started with multi-paragraph insights, took 3 corrections to get to tight format.

**Fix:** Lock format in first message.

### 6. Image-search assist missing
Sometimes screenshots didn't fully capture comment context.

**Fix:** Paste raw text dumps when possible.

### 7. No tracking of which posts were actually commented on
Lost track across sessions.

**Fix:** End each session with "comments dropped today: [list]" so next chat knows what's burnt.

```
**[Name] - [Brief topic]**

```

No insight summaries. No "what the post is about." No multi-paragraph analysis. Just name + 2 comment options. 10-second read max.

## Optimization checklist for the next campaign

1. **Lead with CRM list.** First message in any new chat should paste Tier 0 names so the chat only works on those founders' posts.
2. **Tighter search.** Use "LA founder" or "Los Angeles startup" not "founder".
3. **Spread the posting.** 3-5 per day is more natural than 10 in one burst. Posting at 9am, 1pm, 5pm beats posting all 10 at noon.
4. **Track session count.** 5-10 per day target. Log what's dropped at end of session.
5. **Keep the soft-opener bank handy.** `yeah` / `honestly` / `curious how` / `this part` / `the X part hits` / `interesting that` / `this is where`
