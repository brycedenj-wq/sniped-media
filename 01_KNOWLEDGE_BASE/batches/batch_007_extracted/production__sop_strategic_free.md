# SOP · Strategic Free Shoot

**Three categories: Collab Free · Community Free · Access Free. Each with a defined return mechanic and a primary engine assignment per CANONICAL_TRUTHS.md Truth 5.**

| Category | Builds | Primary engine fed |
|---|---|---|
| **Collab Free** | Portfolio + audience + network (1 hero for collaborator's portfolio · SNIPED retains commercial license · the visual asset itself becomes IG/Carrd content) | **Audience Engine** |
| **Community Free** | Trust + reputation + cultural proof (named-subject institutional documentation · the church/coach/pastor/community-pillar network) | **Reputation Engine** |
| **Access Free** | Venues + promoters + artists + status (event coverage in exchange for downstream commercial introductions and access continuity) | **Reputation Engine** |

Do not collapse them into one bucket. Each has distinct return mechanics, contracts, CRM tagging, and engine attribution. Track in Notion under the `Lane` field with the engine implied by the category.

The free trap (named in the Operating Brief): doing a free shoot, getting tired, and just sending 30 photos because it's easier than running the upsell system. This SOP exists to prevent that.

## Ceiling, not quota (v1.1 spine correction)

The 4-per-month cap (1/week across all three categories combined) is the **maximum**, not a target to hit. Free work exists for leverage / access / community positioning **only**.

**Default answer to "should I take this free shoot" is NO unless ALL of the following are true:**
1. There is a written return mechanic before booking ("I want to be there" is not a return mechanic)
2. Current paid pipeline allows the time (3+ Resets booked in the month → free drops to 1 or 0)
3. Edit-bottleneck queue is not in the red (no SLA risks on paid Resets)
4. The shoot fits one of the three categories with a clearly named conversion lane

**When to actively decrease free volume:**
- Reset pipeline fills (3+ booked in current month) → free drops to 1/month
- Edit-hours moving average crosses 5 hours per Reset → free pauses entirely
- Quarterly review: any free-shoot category that didn't produce measurable return in 60+ days → category gets paused

**The discipline is the lever.** Photographers stuck at $250/month are usually photographers who said yes to free work that never returned. SNIPED's growth depends on saying no often.

## Pre-shoot · category determination

Before agreeing to any free shoot, name the category. If you can't, decline.

### Collab Free
**Use case:** TFP-equivalent. Models, creators, network warm-up shoots, demographic-fit Art Series collaborators (e.g., a model who fits an Avedon-canon study).
**Return mechanic:** copyright + commercial license retained by SNIPED. Model gets 1 hero image for personal/non-commercial portfolio. Pixieset upsell available at $80/image for 48 hours.
**Contract:** Collab Agreement (`/02_CONTRACTS/01_collab_agreement.md`)
**Volume cap:** maximum 1 per week. More than that and free shoots displace paid shoots.

### Community Free
**Use case:** documenting cultural pillars. Coaches, pastors, neighborhood institutions, church functions, community gatherings. Examples already in the loop: Emmanuel 33rd COGIC services, broader COGIC / Black church network in LA via the Bishop Peters relationship, Eagle Rock community events.
**Return mechanic:** trust capital, repeat access, downstream referrals. **Not** monetized directly via Pixieset upsell; the structured upsell happens at delivery to the broader community (family portraits, individual portraits) at the event itself.
**Contract:** Community Release (one-page, simpler than Collab Agreement; for events with multiple subjects, the host signs on behalf of the gathering).
**Volume cap:** 1-2 per month, with strategic clustering (the church network is one cluster; promoter relationships are another).

### Access Free
**Use case:** event coverage in exchange for downstream commercial introductions. Live Nation, promoter relationships, festival/concert work. Examples: 1500-day birthday concert (already shot), summer LA event season.
**Return mechanic:** explicit commercial-conversion clause in the engagement letter. The free coverage produces archival assets; the commercial introduction (sponsor brand, artist label, venue marketing arm) produces the paid follow-up.
**Contract:** Access Engagement Letter (one-pager naming the introduction expected and the commercial license terms when introduction converts).
**Volume cap:** 1-2 per month during summer/event-season window. Off-season: by exception only.

Updated conversation:

> "Strategic free shoots deliver [N] fully retouched Heroes within 21 days plus a wider gallery of color-graded Selects. The gallery includes a 14-day upgrade window if you want more frames moved into Hero tier. Same model as my paid clients, with reduced included counts."

Specifically per shoot category (read `/01_OFFERS/delivery_architecture_v2.md` Section 2):
- **Collab Free:** 3-5 Heroes + 15-25 Selects + 30-50 Proofs (1 hr edit time)
- **Community Free (institutional):** 5-10 named-subject Heroes + 30-50 institutional-grade frames + 60-100 Selects (2-3 hr edit time)

The 1-hour-edit cap on Collab Free is the leverage discipline. Above that and the free shoot is displacing paid work.

If the model / collaborator balks at this scope: this isn't the right shoot. Walk away. The discipline is the **edit-time cap**, not single-image scarcity.

## Delivery

### Collab Free
- 1 hero retouched + uploaded to Pixieset within 21 days (slower SLA than Reset; free shoots are not on the 5-day clock)
- Gallery includes 10-15 additional images priced at $80/image for 48 hours
- Run the same Day 0 / Day 1 / Day 2 upsell sequence as Reset (per `SOP_post_delivery.md`)
- Send the model the credit ask: "@snipedmedia tag on Instagram, Sniped Media credit on other platforms"
- Notion: Shoots DB · Type = `Free Collab` · Cash collected = upsell revenue only
- Mark in Outreach DB if the model becomes a Pipeline candidate (referral source, repeat collaborator)

### Community Free (event-based)
- Hero gallery delivered to the host/leader within 14 days (church bishop, coach, etc.)
- The host gallery is for the institution's use (newsletter, social, internal materials)
- **The structured upsell happens at the event:** at the banquet/service, distribute a one-page card with a Pixieset link to a separate gallery for family/individual portraits priced at standard rates ($150-300 per family for a quick portrait, $300-500 for a 15-min headshot block during the event)
- Notion: Shoots DB · Type = `Free Community` · Cash collected = portrait upsells from event
- Mark each family/individual portrait engagement as a separate Pipeline card if they show interest in further work

### Access Free (event/promoter)
- Selected archive (50-100 frames) delivered to the promoter/artist within 14 days, watermarked, low-res
- Hi-res delivery is gated on commercial license activation
- **The conversion mechanic is the introduction:** within 7 days of the event, follow up with the promoter/artist for the named commercial introduction (sponsor marketing director, artist label, venue marketing). The introduction is what was paid for, not the photos.
- When the introduction lands, run the introduced contact through the standard outbound flow: VIB diagnostic of their visual gap, propose a paid engagement.
- Notion: Shoots DB · Type = `Free Access` · Cash collected = commercial conversions traced back to the event

## The free-trap escape (operational discipline)

Three bright-line rules that prevent free shoots from cannibalizing the paid business:

1. **Volume cap.** Maximum 4 free shoots per month total across all 3 categories. If you're booking more, you're substituting cheap energy for high-value energy.
2. **Hard 1-hero boundary on Collab Free.** Even if the model is loved, even if the shoot felt great, the deliverable is one image. The system enforces; you don't have to argue.
3. **Named return mechanic before booking.** If you can't articulate the specific return on this free shoot in one sentence, the shoot doesn't book. "Church event → access to the broader COGIC network for paid family portraits" is a return mechanic. "It'll be fun" is not.

## Tracking

Notion Shoots DB · view `Free shoot ledger`:
- Group by Type (Free Collab / Free Community / Free Access)
- Sum Cash collected per category
- Monthly review (last Monday): is each category producing the named return? If a category goes 60+ days with no measurable return, the category gets paused.

The discipline is the test. Free shoots that don't compound are not free; they cost the hours that should have gone to paid Resets.
