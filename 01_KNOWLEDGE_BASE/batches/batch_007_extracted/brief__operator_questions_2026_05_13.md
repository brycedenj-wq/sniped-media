## TASTE

**Which images still feel alive 6 months later?**
The early Linnea frames from late 2025 (locked the v3 LUXURY register's emotional target). Frames where the subject's stillness reads as authority, not posing. The Yae HERO · pending tonight's revision · IF Rejuar's shadow physics + edge integration land clean.

**Which edits already feel dated?**
The pre-v3 LUXURY presets (cinematic teal/orange register · retired). The rough Lightroom AI-background-mask comps from May casting weekend · those were preview, not finals. Anything pre-Adobe-Neutral foundation.

**Which references actually influence me vs impress me?**
INFLUENCE: Roversi, Meisel, Mert & Marcus, Aperture monographs, LIFE archives, Magnum, Berger, Dyer. IMPRESS: cinematic AI compositors on IG (Tadder, jpwphoto). They look incredible. They are not the lane.

**What visual mistakes do I repeat?**
Letting hair flyaways slide. Defaulting to centered comp when asymmetric crop is stronger. Over-shooting one wardrobe look without enough variation in pose register. Skin retouch going too smooth on identity texture · the locked rule (identity untouched · skin texture stays) was written because this was the repeat mistake.

**What aesthetic shortcuts make work feel fake instantly?**
Heavy teal/orange split tone. AI-generated faces in composites. Skin-smoothing that erases freckles, pores, scars. Overlit beauty register on what should be editorial. Heavy AI background generation without lighting integration. Stock-photo composite tells.

**What does "premium" ACTUALLY mean in my work?**
Restraint. Negative space. The subject's own register being the loudest element. B&W discipline on Cards. Paper-warm masthead with brass colophon. Skin tones holding without color cast. Captions that name subject + chapter and refuse explanation. Single hero subject per frame.

**What do I remove when I want something to feel expensive?**
Saturation. Marketing copy. Multiple subjects. Engagement bait. Hashtags. Filters layered on top of the v3 LUXURY edit. Any visible attempt to convince the viewer. Photographer-centered language.

## THE MOST IMPORTANT QUESTION

**What can I repeat long enough to become undeniable?**

**The chapter system.** Bi-weekly chapters at v3 LUXURY register, named subjects, sealed Cards, breath days between every post.

Repetition floor:
- **16 chapters by 2026-12-31** (100Q audit Q24 lock)
- **60+ chapters by 2028-12-31**
- **100+ chapters by 2030-12-31**

The repetition is:
- One edit register (v3 LUXURY · decade-locked · Lock 7)
- One archive form (the Cards · B&W · serial-numbered)
- One subject pool (operator-coded · 5 lineages)
- One direction method (Direction Stack)
- One cadence (bi-weekly minimum · breath days between posts)
- One archive (SNIPED · numbered · named · sealed)

**Repetition with taste = the moat.** Not novelty. Not genius. Not virality. The decade-long discipline of the same operator-coded register on the same archive form, applied to the same lineage pool, at the same locked cadence.

The work doesn't become undeniable because of any one chapter. It becomes undeniable because chapter 47 looks like chapter 03 and chapter 03 was already canon.
