## The 12 Canonical Truths

### Truth 1 · SNIPED has three engines, not two

SNIPED is not only a client-service photography business. The operating system runs three distinct engines, each with its own mechanics, metrics, and surfaces:

- **Revenue Engine** · paid shoots · Reset · Sprint · Operator Kit · Brand System · Pixieset upsell · the cash flow layer
- **Audience Engine** · Instagram · BTS · studio culture · recognizable visual language · LinkedIn POV · the cultural gravity / discovery layer
- **Reputation Engine** · community work · artists · promoters · LA access · cultural documentation · founder/operator trust · the named-figure layer

The previous "Engine A · Engine B" framing flattened Audience and Reputation. They are related but distinct: Audience is what someone sees scrolling; Reputation is what someone says about SNIPED when SNIPED isn't in the room.

All three engines run simultaneously. Their relative weights shift across phases (Phase 1 lean override prioritizes Revenue; Phase B+ expands Audience and Reputation).

### Truth 2 · Instagram is not optional

LinkedIn = business authority. Decision-makers scroll LinkedIn when buying B2B services. The VIB → Reset funnel runs there.

Instagram = cultural gravity. Status signal. Taste archive. Discovery engine. Proof of motion. Community magnet.

IG is not a portfolio dump. IG is a media property. The grid is the body of work. Stories are the texture. Carousels are the depth. Both LinkedIn and Instagram are co-equal primary surfaces with different functions.

### Truth 3 · The lane is premium and accessible, not expensive and inaccessible

The positioning: high-standard, culturally tapped-in, accessible enough to build community, premium enough to command respect.

Pricing reflects this:
- The Reset at $1,500 (the cold quote)
- The Sprint at $750 (warm-network only)
- Op Kit $3-8K, Brand System $10K+
- Free shoots categorized strategically

The lane is NOT $5,000 portrait sessions for celebrities. The lane is also NOT $300 commodity headshots. Premium that the LA founder/operator/creative community can actually afford while signaling SNIPED operates at the operational level of their business.

### Truth 4 · Delivery philosophy is controlled abundance

Not one-image scarcity (that misrepresents the production scale). Not raw-dump chaos (that destroys edit hours and brand standard). The model:

- Curated proof gallery (60-100 frames, batch-graded)
- Limited Hero edits (10-12 for Reset, full clinical retouch)
- Selects (30-40 color-graded, social-utility tier)
- Upgrade paths (Select-to-Hero $60, etc, 14-day window)
- No unlimited editing · no infinite revisions

Spec: `/01_OFFERS/delivery_architecture_v2.md`. This is the v2 architecture that replaced the early "1 hero only" v1 framing.

### Truth 5 · Free work stays separated · three categories with distinct outputs

| Category | Builds | Engine fed |
|---|---|---|
| **Collab Free** | Portfolio + audience + network | Audience Engine (primarily) |
| **Community Free** | Trust + reputation + cultural proof | Reputation Engine (primarily) |
| **Access Free** | Venues + promoters + artists + status | Reputation Engine (primarily) |

Do not collapse them into one bucket. Each has distinct return mechanics, contracts, and CRM tagging. Spec: `/05_PRODUCTION/SOP_strategic_free.md`.

### Truth 6 · The goal is becoming known, not just booking clients

Booking clients is the Revenue Engine output. Becoming known is the Reputation Engine output. They are connected (clients become referrers; reputation creates inbound clients) but they are not the same outcome.

The 10-year vision is being the named visual documentarian of LA's emerging Black founder/operator/artist culture (per `/00_BRIEF/REVERSE_ROADMAP.md`). That outcome is reputation-driven. The Phase 1 revenue focus is in service of becoming known, not at odds with it: revenue funds the time spent on Reputation Engine work.

The question that runs through every decision: "How does SNIPED become a recognizable name in LA creative/operator culture?"

### Truth 7 · Long game vision · short cycle execution

Strategically patient. Operationally fast.

The 10-year arc is real. The next-90-day execution is real. They reinforce: long-arc patience prevents wrong-game thinking; short-cycle execution prevents fragmentation and slow drift.

If a decision feels like it requires 6 months to test, it's a Phase B/C decision. If a decision can ship this week, it ships this week.

### Truth 8 · The engineering job is a constraint AND leverage

Constraint:
- Limited weekly hours (10-12 hr/wk SNIPED)
- Travel weeks (1-2/month)
- Burnout risk if recovery is neglected

Leverage:
- **Income floor** · no panic-pricing · holding the $1,500 Reset is sustainable because rent isn't at risk
- **Daily laptop access** · continuous low-friction work in pockets across the day
- **AI stack fluency** · Claude/Gemini/ChatGPT advantage is real · most LA photographers don't run AI as a force multiplier
- **Systems thinking** · engineering background IS positioning leverage (operator-coded, not artist-coded)
- **Technical comfort** · Notion · Stripe · Pixieset · automation tooling are not friction
- **Possible outsourcing later** · revenue + cash reserve enable hires when the time comes

Design around BOTH the constraint and the leverage. The job is funding mechanism + operational discipline trainer + identity differentiator. Don't apologize for it; integrate it.

### Truth 10 · Art Series matters but is not Phase 1 revenue · do not let it become productive avoidance

Art Series (the 9-photographer canon) is important for authorship and legitimacy. It is NOT a Phase 1 revenue mechanism.

Specific drift risk: "Art Series feels meaningful · I should work on it" when the actual highest-leverage move is sending VIB #1 or editing a paid Reset. This is **productive avoidance** · a drift symptom that disguises avoidance as serious creative work.

Phase 1: zero Art Series frames shipped (per LEAN_EXECUTION_AUDIT). Phase B+: 1 frame per 2 weeks. Phase C+: full series cadence.

If you find yourself reaching for Art Series concept work mid-week when paid Reset edit is overdue, name it as productive avoidance. The corrective is the 2-min decision rule and the Cockpit Constraint.

### Truth 11 · Direction Stack is authority asset, not just a book

The Direction Stack methodology supports:
- **Pricing** · "10-protocol diagnostic on set" justifies $1,500 vs commodity $300 headshots
- **Founder positioning** · operator-coded methodology vs artist-coded improvisation
- **LinkedIn POV** · 10 protocols × multiple angles = ongoing content series
- **Visual doctrine** · the methodology IS the aesthetic
- **Premium perception** · methodology = serious practice = serious price
- **Methodology** · the discipline that runs on every shoot

The book is one deployment of the asset. The 5-page interim PDF (this week's deliverable) is another. The LinkedIn POV bank is another. The Q3 public launch will be another. The 12-protocol diagnostic on set is the same asset deployed differently.

### Truth 12 · The Minimum Viable Media Machine is a single canonical loop

```
Shoot → Curate → Deliver → Post → Follow up → Extract proof → Log in CRM → Reuse into LinkedIn/IG → Repeat
```

Every system in the workspace either reinforces this loop or is decoration. The audit question for any new artifact, system, or workflow: "Does this strengthen one of the 9 stages above, or does it add complexity?"

The 9 stages mapped to engines:

| Stage | Engine |
|---|---|
| Shoot | Revenue (paid) or Reputation (free strategic) |
| Curate | Production discipline (cross-engine) |
| Deliver | Revenue (paid) or Reputation (free) |
| Post | Audience |
| Follow up | Revenue (Day-7 testimonial · Day-30 Op Kit) + Reputation (relationship maintenance) |
| Extract proof | Audience (case study, testimonial) + Revenue (next sale ammo) |
| Log in CRM | Operations (Notion) |
| Reuse into LinkedIn/IG | Audience (multiplies the shoot's outputs) |
| Repeat | The compound discipline |

This loop is the spine. Every Cockpit week is one or more cycles through it. Every artifact in the workspace either supports a stage or it doesn't belong.
