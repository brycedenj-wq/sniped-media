# Caption templates · LinkedIn + IG + Reel + Carousel

**This is the caption library for content production. Pull a scaffold, fill in the variables, post. No caption gets written from scratch.**

The scaffolds enforce voice consistency. The hook library at `/07_CONTENT/hook_library.md` provides openers. The LinkedIn POV bank at `/07_CONTENT/linkedin_pov_bank.md` provides 40 finished posts (Series A/B/C/D).

This doc covers the underlying scaffolds the bank was built from + the surfaces the bank does not cover (IG carousel, Reel, Stories, BTS).

---

## Universal scaffold

Every caption follows this skeleton:

```
HOOK · 1 line · the protocol or the insight named in 8 words or fewer

CONTEXT · 3-5 lines · why it matters · who it applies to · when it shows up

DEPLOYMENT · 1-2 lines · where the buyer will see this in their reality

CTA or close · 1 line · either "DM SNIPED for the diagnostic" / "the gallery is at snipedmedia.com" / a single middle-dot
```

Length cap: LinkedIn 4-8 lines · IG 100-200 words · Reel description 1-3 lines · Stories none.

```
[HOOK · subject-named or case-study-led, e.g., "Shot [SUBJECT] last week in [DTLA / LOCATION]."]
```

## IG Carousel scaffold (8 frames + caption)

### Carousel structure (visual)

| Frame | Content | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero opening · the strongest single image · 1080×1350 portrait | The Reel-thumbnail-equivalent. This is what stops the scroll. |
| 2-3 | Context · 1 image + 1 protocol-named text frame | Text frame: 3-5 lines max, big type, on matte black |
| 4-5 | Process · 2 BTS or alternate-angle frames | Demystifies the methodology |
| 6-7 | Output variations · same subject, different deployment crops | Shows the editorial range |
| 8 | Closing card · matte black + one-line CTA + SNIPED logotype small | Locked template (one PSD/Figma file) |

### Carousel caption scaffold

```
[HOOK · the post is about · 1 line]

[3-5 line short essay: the protocol, the subject, the deployment context. Treat it like a LinkedIn POV but slightly shorter and slightly more visual-first.]

[Subject permission line if applicable: "Shot with [NAME]'s permission. [Their company] · [Their role]."]

[CTA: "Diagnostic on snipedmedia.com." OR "·" OR "DM for the brief."]

[Hashtags · NO hashtag spam. 5-8 max, all relevant: #LAFounder #DTLAStudio #DirectionStack #Editorial #FounderPortrait + 2-3 specific to the post]
```

Stories: minimal text. Stickers carry information.

| Sticker | When | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Location · DTLA | Every shoot | Always |
| Music · low-BPM instrumental | Every shoot | Same rotation as Reel music · brand consistency |
| Subject mention sticker | When subject has given permission | Always tag |
| Poll sticker · "A or B?" | Once or twice per shoot | Mid-shoot, on a wardrobe or crop choice |

Story text overlay: max 5 words. "Setting up." "Beat 1." "Last frame." Done.

## What never goes in a caption

- "Excited to share..." (lazy opener, signals creator-economy register)
- "Swipe through!" (the platform shows the carousel without instruction)
- "Available for bookings!" (use the CTA · "DM for the diagnostic")
- Generic motivational content (Mondays, hustle, grind, etc.)
- Emojis (one or two for emphasis is fine, more than that signals casual creator vs operator)
- "DM me!" without context (always pair with what they DM you for)
- Hashtag dumps (max 8, all relevant)
- Overpromise language ("will change your business," "guaranteed results")
- Self-deprecation ("I am still learning," "not a real photographer")

## Voice check (before posting)

Three filters:
1. **Could a generic LA photographer have posted this?** If yes, kill or rewrite. Specificity is the moat.
2. **Does the protocol or methodology name appear?** For LinkedIn POV: yes always. For IG: usually. For Reel: yes if it fits.
3. **Is the CTA aligned with where the reader actually goes next?** LinkedIn: usually DM. IG carousel: usually Carrd OR DM. Reel: usually Carrd. Stories: no CTA needed.

If a draft fails any filter, do not post · adjust or kill.
