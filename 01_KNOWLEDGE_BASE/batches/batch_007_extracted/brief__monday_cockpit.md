## 🔄 How this doc cycles

- **Monday AM:** new Cockpit page created (duplicate this template) · 15 min to fill in
- **Daily:** glance at the Cockpit · move active SLA tasks · check Constraint progress
- **Friday/Sunday:** Friday wrap section completed · Cockpit is "closed" for the week
- **Last Monday of the month:** the prior 4 Cockpits are reviewed during Constraint Audit

Old Cockpits are archived in Notion (one parent page · 4-week rolling visible · older collapsed). Pattern recognition across 8-12 weeks is the input to monthly Constraint identification.

---

## ⚠️ When the Cockpit isn't filled in

If you find yourself entering a week WITHOUT a filled Cockpit, that itself is a drift signal. Three possible causes:

1. **You're avoiding the question of what this week's work is** (drift symptom 6.2 · emotionally avoiding)
2. **You're overwhelmed and operating on autopilot** (drift symptom 6.7 · engineering creep · burnout precursor)
3. **The Cockpit habit hasn't installed yet** (Months 1-2 reality; build the habit by reps)

The corrective is the same in all three: fill in the Cockpit now. Even partially. Even if you spend 30 minutes instead of 15. The cost of not having a Cockpit is a week of fragmented motion; the cost of filling it is 15-30 minutes.

This is the moment the architecture and the operations re-sync.
