# Pre-shoot day-of checklist (printable · mobile-friendly · keep on phone)

**The 60-minute pre-arrival window before the client walks in. Run this list every shoot. No exceptions.**

Print to PDF · keep on phone for offline · or screen-grab as a checklist photo. Do not run this from memory.

---

## 18 hours before · the night before

- [ ] Battery charge: 2x camera batteries on the charger
- [ ] Memory cards: 2x cards formatted, in the bag (DO NOT format the previous shoot's cards · those still need to be archived first)
- [ ] Lens cleaning: front + rear elements wiped, sensor checked for visible dust
- [ ] Wardrobe confirmed with client (reply received in writing · screenshot in `/00_BRIEF/`)
- [ ] Location confirmed (key, code, parking, contact)
- [ ] Pre-shoot brief sent 24h ago + read receipt or reply confirming receipt
- [ ] Moodboard / shot list / call sheet on phone (in Notion or local PDF)
- [ ] Music playlist queued (low-BPM instrumental, 60-90 min runtime)
- [ ] Studio access logistics confirmed for arrival time
- [ ] Backup laptop charged (in case primary fails)
- [ ] Clear the calendar 2 hours before + 4 hours after (no engineering calls, no other obligations)

---

## 90 minutes before · departure

- [ ] Camera bag: body + 2 lenses + 2 batteries + 2 cards + cleaning kit + grey card
- [ ] Lighting bag: 2 strobes (or 1 strobe + 1 continuous) + modifiers + stands + sandbags
- [ ] Tether cable + laptop + power brick
- [ ] Backup card reader (do not rely on laptop's built-in slot)
- [ ] Hard drive (the SSD working drive · for same-day ingest)
- [ ] Phone tripod or holder for BTS capture
- [ ] Water bottle + protein bar (you will not eat for 2-3 hours)
- [ ] Mask/wipes/hand sanitizer (subjects appreciate it; do not skip)
- [ ] Wallet + studio access card / key
- [ ] Phone fully charged + power bank in the bag

---

## 60 minutes before · on location

- [ ] Confirm studio entry / parking
- [ ] Walk the space · note window light direction, ambient color, any obstructions
- [ ] Set up lighting · per the agreed look (one of three working setups: window+bedsheet · two-strobe+beauty dish · key+fill+rim)
- [ ] Test on a stand-in (chair with coat, your own face on self-timer · 5 frames)
- [ ] Check exposure histogram on the test frames · adjust before client is in the chair
- [ ] Confirm the camera is set: ISO, shutter, aperture, white balance, drive mode, focus mode
- [ ] Tether laptop ready (Lightroom open, tether folder set to today's shoot folder)
- [ ] Music playing softly · low volume
- [ ] Water for the subject in view (small gesture, big trust signal)
- [ ] Mirror near set if possible (subjects shoot looser when they can self-check)
- [ ] Phone on silent · airplane mode if possible (notifications kill the room's energy)
- [ ] Notebook / phone for quick notes during the shoot (which protocol surfaced, which beat moved well)

---

## 10 minutes before · client about to arrive

- [ ] Close all unrelated browser tabs / messages on laptop
- [ ] Phone in a drawer or face-down · if used for BTS, hand it to a collaborator
- [ ] Bathroom break (you will not get one in the next 60 minutes)
- [ ] Final breath · review the protocol you are looking to surface today
- [ ] One look in the mirror · you are about to be in front of someone who is paying $1,500 for your eye

---

## When the client arrives · the warm-up (5-10 min)

- [ ] Greet at the door, not the studio entrance · the walk to the set is the social warm-up
- [ ] Address the insecurity question first, conversationally:
  > "Before we get into it: anything specifically you are self-conscious about that I should mind on the day?"
  Whatever they say, take it seriously. The shoot mitigates around it. Trust-builder.
- [ ] Walk them through the set · show them where they will stand, where the camera is, where the lights are. Demystify the room.
- [ ] 7-second physical warm-up: "Before we shoot, shake the legs out, roll the shoulders, take a breath. The first three poses always feel weird; that is fine."
- [ ] Frame the next 45 minutes:
  > "We are going to run through the 10-protocol direction. Mechanics first, energy second. I will talk you through the body · chin here, shoulders here, hands here · then we move to where the eyes go. The session goes fast. Trust the calls."

---

## On camera · 45 minutes · 5 nine-minute beats

(Detail in `/05_PRODUCTION/SOP_reset_shoot_day.md` · this is the at-a-glance reminder.)

- Beat 1 (0-9 min): mechanical setup · pose vocabulary, posture, hands · slower
- Beat 2 (9-18 min): mechanical refinement · the protocols that surface · faster cadence
- Beat 3 (18-27 min): the protocol correction · the "this is the frame" moment
- Beat 4 (27-36 min): wardrobe / pose reset · second register
- Beat 5 (36-45 min): the in-between · the looser moment · narrative-weight frame

Time check at the start of each beat. Stay on rhythm.

---

## Shoot wrap · last 5 minutes

- [ ] Final frame: subject's nod / agreement that the session feels complete
- [ ] Verbal close: "We are at frame [N]. The cull will happen tomorrow, gallery in 5 business days. I will email a Reset summary tonight."
- [ ] Walk them out · do not let them wander
- [ ] Ask the casual referral question on the way out (NOT the formal Day-30 referral ask):
  > "If this lands, who is one founder in your network who would benefit from the same diagnostic?"
- [ ] Note the answer (or lack of) in `/00_BRIEF/post_shoot_notes.md` immediately after they leave
