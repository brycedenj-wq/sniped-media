**Trigger:** 24 hours before shoot. Calendly auto-send (Phase B+) or manual paste (Phase 1).

**Subject line options (pick one):**
- `Sniped Reset · tomorrow · 1 of 2 things to read`
- `Tomorrow's Reset · wardrobe + parking`
- `Reset tomorrow at [TIME] · brief inside`

**Body:**


## Wardrobe block options (paste the relevant one)

**Default · monochromatic register:**
> Monochromatic, dark tones. Black, charcoal, navy. Avoid logos, busy patterns, three-piece suits unless they are already your visual identity. Solid colors, well-fitted, single layer. Bring two options if you are unsure.

**Founder / executive register:**
> Executive register. Solid color shirt or knit, well-fitted jacket optional. Avoid wide collars, busy patterns, anything boxy. Bring two options. Ironed or steamed before arrival.

**Creative / operator register:**
> Creative register. Texture is fine (wool, linen, knit), pattern is risky (avoid). Earth tones or monochrome only. The neckline matters more than the brand. Bring two options.

**Color-block (Series-A founder, press cycle):**
> One bold color tee or knit + neutral jacket OR vice versa. The graphic frame is the point. No prints, no logos, no slogans. Bring two color options.
