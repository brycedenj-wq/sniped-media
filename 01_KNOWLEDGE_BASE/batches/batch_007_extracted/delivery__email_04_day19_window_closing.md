# Email · Day 19 · Upgrade window closing in 24h

**Trigger:** Day 19 after delivery (5 days into the 14-day upgrade window remaining = Day 13 of window). Pixieset built-in reminder fires automatically. This is the manual override / personalization layer.

**Send only if:** the client opened the gallery but has not made an upgrade purchase yet. If they bought, skip. If they never opened the gallery, escalate by phone/text instead of another email.

**Subject:** `Re: Sniped · 24 hours left on the upgrade window`

**Body:**

```
[First name],

Quick check-in. The 14-day upgrade window on your Reset gallery closes tomorrow at [TIME].

If you have already grabbed any upgrades, ignore this. If not, the gallery is here: [PIXIESET LINK]

Specifically [REFERENCE 2 OF THE STRONGEST PROOFS BY DEPLOYMENT USE CASE]:
- The [LOW-KEY EYE-DIRECT] · works for [USE CASE: press cover, deck, etc.]
- The [HALF-TURN WITH NEGATIVE SPACE] · works for [USE CASE]

$60 to upgrade either to a full-retouch Hero. After tomorrow [TIME], the upgrade tier closes (Heroes + Selects you already have stay yours, Proofs are removed from the gallery).

· BJ
```

---

## Variants

**For Op Kit:** the upgrade window is shorter (7 days from delivery, not 14). Adjust subject and timing accordingly. Send on Day 5, not Day 19.

**For Sprint:** Sprint does NOT have an upgrade window. Skip this email entirely; the Sprint gallery just closes at Day 14.
