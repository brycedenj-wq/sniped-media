# SOP · Discovery to Close (BJ-side)

The post-VIB playbook. Picks up where `SOP_VIB_production.md` ends (a reply received) and runs through close.

This SOP folds in the still-valid working bits from the v2 Founder Outreach System (archived at `_archive_v2_2026-05-07/`). The Ambient Audit method is retired. The discovery call script, objection handling, and follow-up cadence are preserved because they're consistent with the locked offer and the WWP / Trust Equation memory.

---

## 1. When a reply lands

**Reply same-day. Same hour if you can.** Cold leads go cold fast.

Goal: get them on a 15-min discovery call. Don't try to close in DMs.

### Reply template

```
Glad you liked it.

Quick 15 min call this week to walk through the session and lock the date?
Calendly here: [link]
```

That's the whole reply. Don't add credibility paragraphs. Don't list past clients. Don't explain SNIPED beyond what the VIB already showed.

Update Notion (or Excel) Outreach card: Status → Replied. Mark date.

## 2. Discovery call · 15 min script

### Min 0-3 · Diagnose
- "Walk me through where you are right now. Are you raising, launching, speaking, anything coming up?"
- "What's the use case for these photos? LinkedIn, press, deck, website?"
- Listen for: trigger event confirmation, urgency, who else they need to look good for.

### Min 3-7 · Solution
- "Here's how I'd shoot this. 45 min, one location, one look. I direct posture, framing, expression. You don't have to know what to do."
- "You get 20 final retouched images in 5 business days. Ready for LinkedIn, press, decks, website."
- Mention SNIPED Visual Standard briefly: "Color-blocked, editorial, commercial-grade. Not the corporate-headshot look."

### Min 7-10 · Close
- "It's $1,500. 50% retainer to lock the date, 50% on shoot day. Want to lock a date?"
- Hand them the calendar. Don't wait. Lock the date on the call.

### Min 10-15 · Logistics
- Confirm location (DTLA studio), parking, wardrobe (1-2 outfits)
- Send invoice for retainer immediately after the call (Stripe link)
- Send prep doc with location pin, what to bring, what to expect

### Update CRM after the call
- Status → Call Booked (if date locked)
- Status → Closed Lost (if hard no)
- Status → Replied with Next Action = follow-up (if "let me think about it")

## 3. Objection handling

### "It's a bit out of budget right now."
"Totally fair. The session is $1,500 because it's a system that delivers commercial-grade founder photos in a week, not a $300 headshot. Want to lock a date for next month instead, or check back in when the timing's better?"

**Do not lower the price. Move the date or move on.**

### "Can I see more examples?"
"Yep, [Carrd URL] has the most recent work. Anything specific you're trying to feel out before locking the date?"

### "Can we do it without the deposit?"
"Deposit is non-negotiable. It's how I make sure the date is real. If something comes up before the shoot we can move the date but the deposit holds."

### "What if I don't like the photos?"
"You'll see proofs the same day. We pick the strongest 20 together. Retouch is dialed in based on your call. Not happy with the final delivery? I'll redo the retouch on any image until you are. The shoot itself isn't refundable but the result is."

### "Can I get a discount if I refer someone?"
"I do referral credits. $200 off your next session for every referral who books. Doesn't reduce this session's price though."

### "Why so expensive vs. a regular headshot photographer?"
This is the buyer-education moment. Use the Sarah Petty handle from `intel_ai_photographer_market.md`:
"AI is going to replace photographers who act like vending machines · the $300 headshot photographer who shows up, takes 50 frames in 15 minutes, hands you a Dropbox. SNIPED is the methodology layer · I direct, the studio is built for this, the system delivers commercial-grade. Different product."

## 5. Dream client spec work (1/week)

Once a week, pick 1 LA founder you really want to shoot. Not just qualified · DREAM client tier. Build a fuller treatment:

- VIB (standard 2-panel) PLUS
- A 1-page mood board with location, wardrobe, lighting reference (Figma)
- Custom DM with subject line referencing their company or moment

Send via LinkedIn DM. Don't pitch the price. Pitch the version:
"Wanted to send this whether you ever buy or not."

Why: 1 dream client closed at $1,500 + Op Kit upsell at $4K = $5.5K from one relationship. Worth the extra hour.

Tracked separately in Notion (or CRM): tag `dream-client-spec` so you can measure conversion specifically.

## 7. Op Kit / Brand System upsell (Day 30 of delivery · NOT cold)

DO NOT pitch Op Kit ($3-8K) or Brand System ($10K+) in cold outreach. Reset only.

The upsell sequence:
- **Day 5:** Reset gallery delivered
- **Day 7:** 48-hour gallery upsell window closes
- **Day 30:** BJ sends a follow-up: "How is the photography landing? What's the next milestone?" (the Op Kit pitch moment per `/06_DELIVERY/SOP_post_delivery.md` Day 30 sequence)

Op Kit upsells convert at ~30% on satisfied Reset clients. The sequence is in `/06_DELIVERY/SOP_post_delivery.md`. Run it. Don't improvise.

## 8. Metrics to track (BJ-side · weekly)

| Metric | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| VIBs sent | 3-10/wk | VIB SOP |
| Reply rate | 25-40% Tier 1 / 10-20% Tier 2 | Notion or CRM |
| Discovery calls booked | 1-2/wk | Calendly |
| Discovery calls held | ~70% of booked | Calendly |
| Closes (deposit collected) | 0.5-1/wk Phase 1 | Stripe |
| Revenue collected | $750-$1,500/wk Phase 1 baseline | Stripe |

If reply rate falls below 10% across 5+ consecutive sends, the issue is trigger selection, not script. Re-segment leads.

If reply rate is healthy but no positive replies, the issue is the script's CTA or the protocol-deployment-context bridge. Test Script B variants.

If positive replies don't convert to discovery calls, the issue is booking flow friction. Test reducing Calendly clicks.

---

## 9. The single rule

**Same-day reply. Lock the date on the call. Refuse the discount.** Speed of reply, certainty of close, and held price are the three levers that move Phase 1 revenue. The VIB does the credibility work; the discovery call does the closing work; the post-delivery sequence does the upsell work. Each stage is a runbook, not improvisation.
