# SNIPED Video Philosophy & Motion Doctrine

**The motion language that fits the SNIPED doctrine. Recognizable, operator-coded, body-of-work-grade. Not generic creator reels.**

This doc directs the editor (and BJ when self-editing). It is conformant with the content philosophy at `/07_CONTENT/sniped_content_philosophy.md` · the visual + cultural doctrine that governs stills also governs motion. Same severity. Same restraint. Same locked aesthetic.

---

## 0 · The premise (what SNIPED video is NOT)

These patterns are common in creator video. SNIPED actively refuses them.

**Not a creator reel.** No "quick cuts to drum hits." No "POV: you're..." titles. No "Day in the life of a photographer." No "rate this 1-10." No talking head with text-on-screen reaction format.

**Not influencer behind-the-scenes.** No staged "candid" moments. No camera flipped to the photographer's face mid-shoot for personality content. No "trust me bro" voice-of-the-creator.

**Not a tutorial.** Not "watch how I edit this in Lightroom." Not "5 lighting setups every photographer needs." The methodology is taught in the book + Substack essays + LinkedIn POVs, not in algorithm-bait video.

**Not a cinematic reel set to lo-fi beats.** No fake film grain. No teal-and-orange grade. No drone establishing shots that exist for their own sake.

**Not a music video imitation.** Even though Khalil Joseph is a reference, SNIPED video is not music video. It is documentary motion in the service of a body of work.

**Not vertical-only thinking.** A SNIPED video that doesn't also work landscape (for Carrd embed, Substack, YouTube) is incomplete. Format flexibility is part of the spec.

**Never edited to inflate emotion that isn't earned.** No swelling music over a still. No slow-motion on a casual moment. No "moment of triumph" register applied to ordinary work.

## 1 · The motion language (what SNIPED video IS)

**Documentary motion in the service of a body of work.** The camera moves only when the subject's relationship to space justifies it. Cuts happen at moments of natural transition (the held breath, the turn of the head, the ambient pause), not at musical hits.

The closest contemporary references:
- **Khalil Joseph** · *Until the Quiet Comes* · *m.A.A.d.* · *Lemonade* · Black music video as cultural document. The reference for tonal weight + pacing + the refusal of explanation.
- **Bradford Young (DP)** · *Selma* · *Arrival* · *Solo: A Star Wars Story* · naturalistic light on Black skin · grounded camera movement · environmental dignity.
- **Barry Jenkins / James Laxton (DP)** · *Moonlight* · *If Beale Street Could Talk* · tonal restraint · the refusal of overexposure · long held shots of stillness.
- **Hiro Murai (Atlanta director)** · atmospheric Black urban contemporary · the held mood · color and music doing structural work.
- **Khalik Allah** · *Field N!ggas* · *Black Mother* · documentary aesthetic ethics · subject + voiceover in unsynchronized layers · the camera as participation.
- **Steve McQueen** · *12 Years a Slave* · *Small Axe* · austerity · the long take of stillness · refusal of cutting away too soon.
- **Wong Kar-wai (Christopher Doyle DP)** · saturated urban color · used selectively for studio environment shots, never for documentary.
- **Hou Hsiao-hsien** · pacing discipline · the long take · the refusal of the algorithm's tempo.

These references aren't applied wholesale. Each one teaches a specific discipline that imports into SNIPED's motion language:

| Reference | Imports |
|---|---|
| Khalil Joseph | Pacing weight · the refusal to explain · music as structure not decoration |
| Bradford Young | Light on Black skin · grounded camera · environmental dignity |
| Barry Jenkins / Laxton | Held shots of stillness · tonal restraint · the long look |
| Hiro Murai | Atmospheric mood · color doing structural work |
| Khalik Allah | Documentary ethics · subject + sound layered · camera as participation |
| Steve McQueen | Austerity · refusal to cut early · earned stillness |
| Wong Kar-wai / Doyle | Saturated urban color (selective use) |
| Hou Hsiao-hsien | Pacing discipline · refusal of algorithmic tempo |

What you are NOT borrowing from any of these: tickets to "art film" pretension. The SNIPED video is operator-coded; the references are tools, not aesthetics to imitate wholesale.

## 2 · The six SNIPED video formats

Each format has its own register, length, and platform target. **Same motion language across all six.** Different deployment.

### Format 1 · BTS Cut (the Reset shoot day documented)
- **Length:** 30-60 seconds
- **Platform:** IG Reels (vertical primary, landscape cutdown for Carrd)
- **Cadence:** 1-2 per month from active Reset shoots
- **Purpose:** humanize the operator without becoming the operator's content. Show methodology in operation.
- **Content:** lighting setup · client arriving + warming up · the 90-second on-set opener · a single direction call ("chin forward, hold") · the captured frame on the camera back. **No talking-head BJ. No styled fake-candid.** The studio in working state.
- **Editor brief:** documentary-grade, observational, never staged. Cut at natural transitions. Hold the frame longer than feels comfortable.

### Format 2 · The Founder Profile Cut (Reset client mini-portrait video)
- **Length:** 60-90 seconds
- **Platform:** IG Reels + LinkedIn + Carrd embed (all formats produced)
- **Cadence:** 1 per quarter, only with explicit client consent
- **Purpose:** founder portrait extended into motion. The Op Kit-grade deliverable rendered as small documentary.
- **Content:** subject in their workspace OR studio · 2-3 environmental shots establishing context · one held portrait moment (the still equivalent moves) · subject voice-over (their own words about their work, NOT about SNIPED) · 1-2 cutaway details (hands on a product, eyes catching window light) · close on a final composed frame.
- **Editor brief:** Bradford Young register. Light on subject must respect skin tone. Background out of focus. Voice and image unsynchronized; the voice carries the content while the image holds composure.

### Format 3 · Cultural Documentation Essay (Reputation Engine · the compounding archive work)
- **Length:** 90 seconds (social-friendly cutdown) + 3-5 minute long form (Substack/YouTube)
- **Platform:** Substack (primary) + LinkedIn + IG cutdown + eventual YouTube
- **Cadence:** 1 per quarter (paired with the quarterly thematic mini-essay from `cultural_documentation_thesis.md`)
- **Purpose:** the cultural documentation thesis rendered in motion. Body-of-work-grade. The asset that compounds.
- **Content:** institution / cultural moment / subject treated with editorial dignity · BJ's voiceover OR subject voiceover (chosen per piece) · ambient sound critical · stills + motion intercut · text on screen minimal (institution name, year, location, captioned subject names)
- **Editor brief:** Khalil Joseph + Khalik Allah register. Sound design carries 50% of the weight. Long held moments. Refusal of the algorithm's tempo. Watch *Until the Quiet Comes* before editing the first one of these.

### Format 4 · Direction Stack POV Cut (LinkedIn authority video, used selectively)
- **Length:** 30-45 seconds
- **Platform:** LinkedIn (primary) + IG cutdown
- **Cadence:** 1 per month maximum (the LinkedIn POV bank is mostly stills + caption; video is the rare deployment)
- **Purpose:** methodology made visible in motion. Used when a single still cannot capture the protocol correction in time.
- **Content:** before/after sequence (subject in old register · cut · subject in corrected register) · BJ voiceover naming the protocol + the deployment context (~30 seconds of script) · text on screen for the protocol number and name (ONLY the protocol; not every word)
- **Editor brief:** clinical, structured, no music or minimal music. The voice carries the content. The protocol name appears as a single text cue, not a Kapwing-style every-word lyric video.

### Format 5 · Process / Studio Atmosphere Loop (the studio-as-character register)
- **Length:** 10-20 seconds
- **Platform:** IG Stories + cutdown to LinkedIn occasionally
- **Cadence:** opportunistic, not scheduled
- **Purpose:** studio atmosphere documentation. Light through DTLA window. Modifier swap. Camera body. Print spread. The texture of the working environment.
- **Content:** single locked frame OR slow push-in OR static observation. No subject typically. Pure environmental.
- **Editor brief:** Wong Kar-wai · saturated color when the light allows. Single sustained shot, no cuts.

### Format 6 · Annual Documentary Essay (Year 2+ format, not Phase 1)
- **Length:** 8-15 minutes
- **Platform:** YouTube primary, with shorter cutdowns to Substack/LinkedIn/IG
- **Cadence:** 1 per year, anchored to the annual archive review
- **Purpose:** the body-of-work argument made in long form. Year 2+ when there's enough work to argue.
- **Content:** the year's cultural documentation woven with founder portrait stills, archival frames, voiceover essay (BJ's own writing read aloud), institutional voice when warranted.
- **Editor brief:** Khalik Allah meets Bradford Young. The pace is the message. Watch *Black Mother* and *Field N!ggas* as direct references.

## 3 · Editing language (per element)

### 3.1 Cuts

- **Cut at natural transitions, not at musical hits.** The held breath. The turn of the head. The ambient pause. The doorway crossing. The light change.
- **Hold the shot longer than feels comfortable.** A 4-second held portrait will read as cinematic where a 2-second held portrait reads as a creator reel.
- **Match cuts allowed.** Hand to hand, eye to eye, shape to shape. Aesthetic alignment between cuts is a SNIPED signature.
- **Jump cuts banned in commercial work.** Allowed in cultural documentation when the editorial intent is rhythm or compression of time.
- **No whip pans.** No fake-handheld jitter applied in post. If the camera moved, it moved on set.

### 3.2 Color

- **Locked color discipline same as stills.** Monochromatic / strict color-block. Wardrobe + backdrop + ambient consistent.
- **Grade reference:** Bradford Young (Selma, Arrival) for naturalistic Black skin. Wong Kar-wai (In the Mood for Love) for saturated urban environment. Barry Jenkins (Moonlight) for the refusal-of-overexposure principle.
- **No teal-and-orange.** No Sedona dust. No fake film emulation. No Kodak 250D LUT. No "cinematic" preset that's recognizable as a preset.
- **The grade serves the subject's skin first.** Background can be technically imperfect; subject's skin tone must be true.

### 3.3 Motion (camera motion + post motion)

- **Camera motion only when the subject's relationship to space justifies it.** A push-in earns its place when the subject is doing something the close-up reveals; otherwise, hold static.
- **Slow over fast.** Drift over jolt. Considered over reactive.
- **No speed ramps.** No artificial slow-mo on a casual moment. If a moment was slow, it was slow.
- **No reverse motion.** No boomerangs. Never.
- **No stutter / glitch effects.** Even when the trend is hot.

### 3.4 Type / text on screen

- **Minimal.** A single protocol name. A single subject name. A single date or institution. NEVER every spoken word as a typographic element.
- **Typography:** Inter (system) · 12-32pt depending on context · #F2F2F2 white on darker backgrounds, #1A1A1A charcoal on lighter · letter-spacing tight (-0.01em on display, 0 on body)
- **Animation:** static or single fade. No bounce. No spin. No "kinetic typography."
- **Position:** lower-third or upper-third, never center-screen unless the entire frame is composed for it.

### 3.5 Aspect / framing

- **Vertical (9:16) for IG Reels / Stories / TikTok cutdowns.** Composed vertical, not landscape-cropped.
- **Square (1:1) for IG carousel embeds + LinkedIn flexibility.**
- **Landscape (16:9) for Carrd, Substack, YouTube, LinkedIn long-form.**
- **A SNIPED video must work in at least 2 of these aspects.** Compose for both vertical and landscape from the start.
- **Crop discipline:** never crop a subject's eyes off-frame (the eyes are the anchor). Never crop at a joint (cropping rule from stills applies).

## 4 · Pacing per format

| Format | Average shot length | Total length | Cuts per minute |
|---|---|---|---|
| BTS Cut | 2-4 seconds | 30-60 sec | 12-20 |
| Founder Profile Cut | 3-5 seconds | 60-90 sec | 12-20 |
| Cultural Documentation Essay | 4-8 seconds | 90 sec / 3-5 min | 8-12 |
| Direction Stack POV Cut | 2-3 seconds | 30-45 sec | 15-22 |
| Process / Studio Atmosphere Loop | 10-20 seconds | 10-20 sec | 0-2 |
| Annual Documentary Essay | 5-10 seconds | 8-15 min | 6-10 |

**The slow formats (Cultural Documentation Essay, Annual) are SNIPED-distinctive.** Most photographers' reels run 25-40 cuts per minute. SNIPED's signature is the refusal of that tempo.

## 5 · Transitions doctrine

Allowed:
- **Hard cut** (default, 90% of cuts)
- **Match cut** (subject motion → subject motion · light direction → light direction · shape → shape)
- **Single cross-dissolve** for time-passage in documentary essays only (max 1 per piece)
- **Single cut to black** at end of pieces, optional, holds for 0.5-1 sec before final card

Banned:
- **Whip pan transitions** (TikTok/reel staple)
- **Zoom blur transitions** (creator default)
- **Wipe transitions** of any kind
- **Iris in/out** (faux-cinematic)
- **Lens flare transitions** (fake J.J. Abrams)
- **Shake / glitch / RGB-split transitions**
- **Speed ramp into cut** (Vine-coded, dated)
- **Tunnel / portal transitions** (CapCut-coded)

If the editor reaches for any banned transition, the answer is **hard cut.**

## 6 · Sound design direction

Sound carries 30-50% of the weight in SNIPED video. This is not optional polish.

### 6.1 Music

- **No lo-fi beats.** No royalty-free moody-piano. No "epic cinematic trailer" stings.
- **Ambient, instrumental, restrained.** When music is used, it stays at -20 dB (well below dialogue/voice). It supports; it does not lead.
- **Reference palette:** Sault, Common's recent work, Floating Points, Ben LaMar Gay, Sons of Kemet (UK jazz), Steve Reich (Music for 18 Musicians sustained register), Pharoah Sanders late work, parts of D'Angelo's *Voodoo*. Music that operates at SNIPED's tempo: slow, considered, structural.
- **License music properly.** Soundstripe / Musicbed / Artlist / direct-license. Never YouTube Audio Library tracks (instantly recognizable).
- **Some pieces use NO music.** Cultural Documentation Essays often work better with ambient + voiceover only. Allow that.

### 6.2 Voiceover

- **BJ's voice (when used) is operator-coded.** Slow tempo. Embedded truths (per `SOCIAL_MEDIA_3_0_REFERENCE.txt`'s confidence rules). No upspeak. No vocal fry. Recorded in a treated space (DTLA studio, with a dynamic mic + soft surfaces).
- **Subject voice (when used) is unscripted.** A 2-3 minute interview captured at the shoot, edited to a 30-60 second selection. The subject's own words about their work. NEVER about SNIPED.
- **Voiceover and image are intentionally desynchronized.** The voice runs across multiple shots; the image holds independent composition. This is the Khalik Allah technique. Refuses the music-video lockstep.

### 6.3 Ambient sound

- **Captured on shoot, layered in edit.** The studio's HVAC. The DTLA street outside. The church's quiet between hymns. The subject's chair shifting. The ambient becomes the temporal anchor.
- **Cleaned but not removed.** Dialogue editing should remove audible problems (clipping, distinct talking outside the frame) but preserve the room's actual sonic texture.

### 6.4 Silence

- **Earned silence is a signature.** A 2-second pause at the start of a piece. A 1-second pause at a cut. A held shot with no music for 4+ seconds. The refusal to fill silence is operator-coded and signals confidence.
- **Algorithm note:** silence reduces algorithmic distribution on TikTok/Reels because watch-completion drops slightly. SNIPED video accepts this trade. The work that compounds is not optimized for the next 72 hours.

## 7 · BTS structure spec (Format 1, deployable)

A BTS cut runs 30-60 seconds and follows this structural template. The editor can deviate when the source footage demands, but should default here.

```
0:00-0:03 · Studio establishing · light on the wall, modifier in frame, no subject
0:03-0:08 · Subject arriving · walking in, greeting, ambient sound only
0:08-0:18 · Methodology in operation · BJ directing (single line audible: "chin forward, hold") · subject in pose · light hitting
0:18-0:28 · The captured moment · camera back showing the frame · the held still
0:28-0:40 · One detail · hands on a prop, eyes catching window, the subject's micro-laugh between takes
0:40-0:55 · The exit · subject walking out, BJ's hands on the camera, the studio in post-shoot quiet
0:55-1:00 · Single text card · subject first name · year · institution OR "The Reset · Sniped Media"
```

**Music:** ambient or none. If music, starts at 0:08, exits at 0:50, never crescendos.
**Voice:** the single direction call ("chin forward, hold") is the only spoken word. No BJ voiceover.
**Color:** locked monochromatic per the shoot's palette. No grade applied beyond the still grade.

## 8 · Documentary structure spec (Format 3, the body of work)

A Cultural Documentation Essay runs 90 seconds (social cutdown) or 3-5 minutes (Substack/YouTube). The structural template:

```
0:00-0:08 · Place establishing · institution exterior, light on the building, ambient sound
0:08-0:25 · Context montage · 3-4 shots establishing the institution / community / moment · ambient sound + subject's voice begins (low register, unsynchronized to image)
0:25-1:00 · The encounter · BJ's documentation on site · subject(s) in environment · institutional ritual / activity captured · voice continues with stills intercut
1:00-1:30 · The held portrait · 2-3 portrait stills with longer hold time · voice rises (quote, prayer fragment, conversation excerpt)
1:30-2:30 · Methodology + observation · BJ voiceover (own essay text, ~150-200 words) over montage · institution + subject + light
2:30-3:00 · The text card · subject name(s) · institution · date · the piece's essay link
3:00-3:30 · Final hold · single still or single sustained shot · ambient sound only · slow fade
```

For 90-second cutdowns: compress the middle, keep the establishing + the held portrait + the final card.

**Music:** ambient instrumental at -22 dB to -28 dB. Often no music in the first 25 seconds. Music exits at the text card.
**Voice:** subject's voice + BJ's essay voice layered. Never the same person on the same beat.
**Pace:** 4-8 seconds per shot average. Refusal of the algorithm's tempo is the SNIPED signature here.
**Color:** Bradford Young naturalistic on Black skin. Tonal restraint. No grade that calls attention to itself.

This format will look "slow" to viewers trained on creator-economy reels. That is the point. The work is for the body-of-work archive, not the algorithm.

## 9 · Social clip philosophy (the LinkedIn / IG cutdown layer)

When a longer piece (Founder Profile, Documentation Essay) gets cutdown for LinkedIn or IG, the cutdown follows these rules:

- **The cutdown is not a teaser.** It is a self-contained piece that happens to share footage with the longer piece. No "watch the full version" CTAs.
- **Cut to length, then trim 10%.** A 60-sec target should be edited to 60 then trimmed to 54. Tightness is the editorial discipline.
- **The hook is a held shot, not a cut sequence.** The first 1-2 seconds is a single composed frame that earns the watch. Refuse the multi-cut "hook" pattern.
- **Captions burned in for LinkedIn (sound-off viewing default).** Captions OFF for IG Reels/Stories (sound-on default with a strong audio choice).
- **End on a single text card OR a single hero still.** Not on a CTA, not on a hashtag, not on "follow for more."

## 11 · Motion + stills coexistence

The relationship between SNIPED stills and SNIPED video is structural, not parallel.

**Stills are the SNIPED standard. Video is the secondary documentary register.**

| Decision | Rule |
|---|---|
| Which is the deliverable? | Stills, primarily. Video is supplementary. |
| Which earns more brand authority? | Stills. The body of work is the still archive. Video documents how the stills were made. |
| Which is the AI-proof asset? | Stills (when produced with operator methodology). Video is more imitable. |
| Production source | Same shoot day. Video is captured incidentally during stills production. NOT a separate video shoot. |
| Editor's brief | "Extract 30-90 seconds of strongest natural footage from this shoot day's B-roll." Not "produce a video." |
| When stills and video appear together (Carrd, IG carousel, Substack) | Still is the primary frame; video is the supporting motion. The video should make you want to see the still. |
| When the still and the video disagree (different subject moment, different mood) | The still wins. Re-cut the video around the still's moment. |

**The exception:** Cultural Documentation Essays (Format 3) and the Annual Documentary Essay (Format 6) are video-primary deliverables paired with still cutaways. These are Year 2+ formats and the only ones where video carries the lead.

## 13 · Production discipline (how this survives constraints)

The motion language is opinionated, but it must survive a field engineer's schedule + travel + limited time. The disciplines that make it survivable:

### 13.1 Capture by default
- Every shoot day produces video B-roll automatically. The phone records ambient. An assistant (or a tripod-mounted second body) captures wide context shots.
- **Capture is mandatory; producing is optional.** Many shoots produce no video deliverable. That's fine. The footage exists in the archive for later use.

### 13.2 Editor does the heavy lift
- Once the editor is briefed and trusted, BJ's job is selection and approval, not editing.
- Editor cycle: 1 week per piece minimum. BJ briefs Mon, editor delivers round 1 by Fri, BJ reviews Sat, editor delivers final by Wed of week 2.
- **Cap: 2 active editor projects at any time.** More than that and review quality drops.

### 13.3 One video per shoot maximum (initially)
- Phase 1: a Reset shoot produces stills + maybe one BTS cut. Not a BTS cut + a founder profile cut + a process loop + a tease. One.
- Discipline prevents over-production from displacing the next paid shoot.

### 13.4 Master edit + cutdown logic
- One master edit per piece, then platform-specific cutdowns.
- Example: Founder Profile is shot at 90 sec landscape. Cutdowns: 60 sec vertical for Reels, 30 sec landscape for LinkedIn, 15 sec square for IG carousel embed.
- The cutdowns are mechanical, not creative. The creative work is the master.

### 13.5 Travel-week protocol
- No new video projects briefed during travel weeks.
- Editor work continues during travel; BJ reviews on the road only if the laptop + headphones came along.
- A delayed delivery is acceptable; a sloppy delivery is not.

### 13.6 Footage archive discipline
- All raw video stored in `~/SNIPED/video/raw/YYYY-MM-DD_[shoot]/`
- 2-backup rule (local SSD + Drive)
- Master edits archived in `~/SNIPED/video/master_edits/YYYY-MM-DD_[piece-name]/`
- Platform cutdowns in `~/SNIPED/video/cutdowns/YYYY-MM-DD_[piece-name]_[platform]/`

## 14 · Per-platform format specifications (deployable)

Each platform gets its own cutdown spec. These are the technical specs the editor delivers against.

### IG Reels (vertical primary)
- Aspect: 9:16 (1080×1920)
- Length: 15-60 sec
- Codec: H.264, MP4, 30 fps (the platform doesn't reward higher), 8-10 Mbps
- Audio: AAC 48kHz stereo
- Captions: NOT burned in (Reels prefer auto-captioning); subject names burned in
- Cover frame: a strong still from second 0-1, vertically composed

### IG Stories
- Aspect: 9:16 (1080×1920)
- Length: 15 sec max per segment, multi-segment OK
- Codec: same as Reels
- Captions: optional, brief if used
- Lower-third + name + date OK

### LinkedIn (square or landscape)
- Aspect: 1:1 (1080×1080) OR 16:9 (1920×1080)
- Length: 30-90 sec ideal (LinkedIn favors short)
- Codec: H.264, MP4, 30 fps, 10-15 Mbps
- Captions: BURNED IN (LinkedIn defaults to muted playback)
- Cover frame: a still that earns the click

### Carrd embed (landscape)
- Aspect: 16:9 (1920×1080)
- Length: 60-90 sec
- Codec: H.264, MP4, 30 fps, 12 Mbps
- Captions: NOT burned in (Carrd visitor is engaged)
- Hosted on Vimeo embed (no autoplay, no related-videos)

### Substack embed (landscape)
- Aspect: 16:9 (1920×1080)
- Length: 90 sec - 5 min
- Codec: same as Carrd
- Captions: closed-caption track recommended
- Hosted on Vimeo or YouTube unlisted

### YouTube long-form (Year 2+)
- Aspect: 16:9 (3840×2160 4K source, deliverable at 1920×1080 1080p)
- Length: 8-15 min
- Codec: H.264, MP4, 24 fps (24 fps for cinematic register), 16-20 Mbps
- Captions: closed-caption track + chapter markers
- Cover frame: composed YouTube thumbnail (separate design pass)

### TikTok (cross-post mechanical)
- Aspect: 9:16 (1080×1920)
- Length: 15-60 sec (same as IG Reels)
- Codec: same as Reels
- Captions: optional
- **Cross-post mechanical only.** Same file as IG Reels. Not optimized for TikTok separately.

## 15 · The 10-year video test

Same as the still 10-year test, applied to motion:

**Will this video, viewed in 2036, still represent SNIPED Media?**

If it has fake film grain, teal-and-orange, kinetic typography, drum-hit cuts, or any of the patterns in Section 10's "Never Happens" list · the answer is no, and the video shouldn't be cut.

If it has held shots, locked color, ambient sound, operator-coded restraint, and editorial dignity · the answer is yes, and the video joins the body of work.

The test is the filter. The filter is the discipline. The discipline is what makes SNIPED video recognizable across a decade.
