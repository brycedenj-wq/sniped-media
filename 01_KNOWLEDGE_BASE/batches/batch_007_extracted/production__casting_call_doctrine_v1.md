# Casting Call Doctrine v1

Source: May 1-3 2026 production campaign retrospective. Migrated to SNIPED_OS as canonical reference 2026-05-12.

This doc is the operating doctrine for every future casting call shoot. Read before Sunday 5/17 shoot. Read before any future multi-model production block.

## What this campaign was (May 1-3, 2026)

3-day production block at DTLA studio (2715 S Main St). Three palette days (white/cream Friday, white/cream + cream/gold Saturday, orange-on-orange Sunday). Casting funnel built from cold start in 60 hours via IG DMs, IG story link sticker, FB casting groups, FB MUA group, Threads.

Target: 8-9 hero shoots + HASS friends + 3 MUAs.

Actual results:

- Saturday: 1 shoot (Jada delivered, Jade ghosted, HASS canceled both)
- Sunday: 3 confirmed (Danika 10 AM, Atiya 12 PM, Emilia 2 PM + Hermine MUA)

Final count: 5-6 hero shoots out of 9 planned. ~55-65% conversion rate from confirmed to shot.

## What worked · KEEP AND SCALE

### 1. The casting form as the funnel anchor

`snipedmedia.com/casting` was the single source of truth. Asked identity, availability, wardrobe per palette. 19 form submissions in 60 hours of distribution. The form lets you triage at scale instead of negotiating each candidate one DM at a time. **KEEP. The form is the foundation.**

### 2. Multi-channel distribution (LOCKED CHANNELS · 2026-05-13)

The canonical 5-channel casting distribution playbook. Validated May 1-3 2026 campaign · 19 form submissions in 60 hours. **No LinkedIn.** LinkedIn is reserved for VIB founder outreach + Reset client lane, NOT casting.

| # | Channel | Notes from last campaign |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **IG cold DMs** | Direct outreach to potential subjects from target list. Used for tier-1 candidates seen organically. |
| 2 | **IG Story link sticker** | Casting form link on Stories. Captures warm audience who already follows SNIPED. |
| 3 | **FB casting groups** | LA-specific casting / model / creative groups. Broad reach. |
| 4 | **FB MUA group** | **Pulled 3 leads in 2 hours** · also the channel for MUA recruitment (dual-purpose · find subjects AND find MUAs). |
| 5 | **Threads casting post** | **Surprise winner · 906 views and 5+ replies** in last campaign. Post early in the casting window. |

**LOCKED:** This is the 5-channel set for every future casting call. Do NOT expand to TikTok, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, or any other platform without explicit doctrine review. The 5 channels above are the validated set. Run all 5 in parallel for every casting window.

**Form URL · single source of truth:** `snipedmedia.com/casting`

**KEEP MULTI-CHANNEL.** Don't lean on one platform.

### 3. The lock-in DM template

Studio address + call time + wrap time + concept + wardrobe + hair + makeup + MUA + release + finals timeline + moodboard reference + "Reply YES to confirm" + Bryceden signoff. Production-grade. Reads professional. **KEEP THE TEMPLATE.**

### 4. Operator voice across all messages

No em dashes, no buzzwords, no corporate tone, lowercase casual where appropriate, direct and warm. Confirmed models replied with "Bet" or "YES" or hearts. The voice built trust. **KEEP THE VOICE.**

### 5. The moodboard webpage system

`snipedmedia.com/moodboard/[palette]` with 9 sections (wash, concept, palette, texture, lighting, direction, wardrobe, team direction, contact). Direction Stack-anchored, Protocol citations, single-page scroll-snap. Much better than Pinterest because it's branded, controlled, and signals operator-level production. **KEEP. This is a differentiator.**

### 6. Warm future-roster for graceful exits

Tara (Friday cancel), Daria (Sunday cancel), Erica (late MUA reply) all got "no friction, future-yes, see you next round" messages. Relationships intact. They'll come back. **KEEP THIS DOCTRINE.** Burning a model over one cancel is amateur thinking.

### 7. Return-engagement tier

Svedey (shot once before, ran late but apologized) preserved for May 15 without re-pitch. Created a roster category between "active casting" and "future-roster." **KEEP THIS.** Build the return-engagement bench deliberately.

### 8. MUA direction per model

Sunday plan: brief Hermine on three distinct registers (Danika polish, Atiya texture, Emilia graphic) within one palette family. Skin is the contrast point, makeup varies the emotional register, the series reads as a series instead of three same-y portraits. **KEEP THIS.** Editorial thinking, not snapshot thinking.

### 9. Studio-as-call-sheet logic

Moodboard IS the call sheet. Don't promise a separate PDF. One asset, one source of truth, accessible from any phone. **KEEP THIS.**

## What broke · FIX FOR NEXT CAMPAIGN

### Failure 1 · 3 ghosts out of 8-9 confirmed (35% drop rate)

Tara (day-of cancel with reason), Jade (ghosted Saturday entirely), HASS friends (canceled both). 35% drop rate on confirmed lock-ins is too high.

**Root cause:** Trade casting. No skin in the game. Models can cancel without consequence. The lock-in DM signals professionalism but doesn't create commitment cost.

**Fix:**
- Require a 24-hour pre-shoot confirmation reply. "Confirm tomorrow's call by 8 PM tonight or your slot opens to backup." Forces accountability.
- Build a deposit mechanism for non-trade tier. Even $50 refundable on arrival creates skin in the game. Add as soon as paid clients enter.
- Two-strike future-roster rule. First no-show is one strike with warm future-yes. Second no-show is permanent removal. Tracked in CRM.
- Pre-stage 2 backup models per slot day-of, not the night before.

### Failure 2 · MUA chain almost collapsed

Aiym ghosted entirely (Friday MUA original plan). Knarik shifted from Saturday to Friday last minute. Brittany Smith pulled from FB for Saturday. Multiple cascading reshuffles.

**Root cause:** No locked-in MUA confirmation cycle. MUAs treated more casually than models even though they're harder to replace.

**Fix:**
- MUA confirms 48 hours before, not day before. Higher bar for the team than the talent.
- Build a 5-deep MUA bench, not 3. Bench should include 2 IATSE-credentialed, 2 editorial commercial, 1 utility.
- Pre-pay or formalize the trade. Even a coffee/lunch budget shows the MUA they're being treated as a paid pro in training, not free labor.
- Build the MUA roster separately and earlier than the model roster. MUAs are the bottleneck, not the talent.

### Failure 3 · Saturday was structurally weakest

Saturday was the "studio bonus day" that didn't exist on the casting form. Backfilled with off-form DMs. 1 of 3 shoots executed.

**Root cause:** The casting funnel only asked Friday and Sunday. Saturday was bolted on after the fact. Models pitched Saturday-only via DM had no funnel commitment.

**Fix:**
- Decide all production days BEFORE building the form.
- If a day genuinely needs to be added late, push to the next campaign instead of compromising the funnel discipline.

### Failure 4 · Wardrobe partial vs full was under-enforced

"Partial orange" or "partial white" got accepted from form submissions. On the day, partial often meant "one piece I think might work."

**Root cause:** The form asked the question but didn't validate the answer. No photo of the wardrobe pieces required.

**Fix:**
- Add a "submit photos of your wardrobe options" step before locking in. Required.
- Reject "partial" candidates if photos don't show at least 2 clean palette options.
- For "full wardrobe" candidates, optional photo submission but encouraged.

### Failure 5 · No same-day confirmation discipline

Drops clustered in the 12-24 hour pre-shoot window (Tara 7 AM Friday, Daria Saturday afternoon, Jade Saturday morning).

**Root cause:** No structured check-in 24 hours and 12 hours out. Models knew they could ghost without notice.

**Fix:**
- 24 hours before call: "see you tomorrow at X, reply with thumbs up to confirm" check-in via DM. Required reply within 4 hours or backup gets pinged.
- 12 hours before call: optional softer "moodboard reminder, see you in the morning" touch.
- If 24-hour check-in goes unanswered, slot opens to backup automatically. Don't chase.

### Failure 6 · No backup activation protocol

When Tara dropped Friday morning, scrambled. Daria backfilled (accepted). When Jade ghosted Saturday, didn't even attempt backfill (too late). Sunday backfill worked only because we got 24+ hours notice.

**Root cause:** No pre-staged backup roster. Backfills were ad-hoc per slot.

**Fix:**
- Build a "tier 2 backup" list per day BEFORE the shoot week. 2-3 models per palette day on standby. They get a "you're standby for [day], slot might open" message Wednesday.
- Standby models keep the day clear. If activated by Friday noon at latest, they shoot. If not, they release.
- Pay the standby a small kill fee (free print, $25, etc) for holding the day.

### Failure 7 · HASS friends double-cancel

External (non-funnel) bookings via friends. Both canceled Saturday. No call sheet sent because it was casual. No moodboard sent because it was "friends, they'll show up."

**Root cause:** Treating friends as guaranteed instead of as production.

**Fix:**
- Same lock-in DM template for friends. Studio address, call time, wrap, concept, wardrobe, MUA, moodboard.
- Same 24-hour confirmation check-in.
- Same two-strike rule. Friends don't get a pass on production discipline.

### Failure 8 · No visible production deposit (even symbolic)

Models had zero financial cost to cancel.

**Root cause:** Trade-only doctrine means trade-only commitment.

**Fix:**
- Once Reset clients land, every paid client is non-refundable 50% deposit on day of booking. Already locked in offer doctrine.
- For trade models, introduce a "creative commitment letter." One paragraph they sign acknowledging the production schedule is tight and dropping last-minute affects the team. Soft commitment but signed paperwork creates a different psychological frame than a DM.

## What was learned but not yet executed

### Direction Stack-anchored brief works

The Sunday MUA brief (three distinct emotional registers: Danika polish, Atiya texture, Emilia graphic) is editorial thinking. Most photographers give every model the same makeup. This breaks the series into three frames that argue with each other. **NEXT CAMPAIGN: do this for every shoot day, not just Sunday.** Build the model-to-register map BEFORE the moodboard goes live.

### The casting funnel produces tiers, not flat data

After 19 submissions you naturally clustered into A (must-shoot), B (backup), C (future-roster). **Tier classification should happen the moment a submission comes in, not after triage.** Add a tier field to the CRM and tag on receipt.

### Aesthetic alignment per model matters

Some models read SNIPED immediately (Yae · agency-trained editorial). Some need to be pushed against type (Emilia · lifestyle/glamour grid pushed into high-fashion graphic). Some already operating in the register. **Tag models by "natural fit" vs "stretch fit"** to plan direction accordingly.

### The "must-shoot" model deserves the highest backup priority

Jada was must-shoot. She delivered. If she'd ghosted, the whole Saturday collapses. **Must-shoot tier should have 2 backup models specifically pre-staged for that slot**, not generic standby.

## Operating doctrine for next campaign

### Pre-production (Week -2)
- Define ALL production days BEFORE building the casting form
- Lock all palettes BEFORE building the moodboard system
- Identify must-shoot models from prior conversations, reach out 2 weeks ahead, lock them first
- Identify all MUA candidates 2 weeks ahead, lock the bench

### Casting (Week -1)
- Launch the form Monday, close by Wednesday EOD
- Distribute across IG DMs, IG story, FB groups, Threads, optional X
- Require wardrobe photos for all "partial" submissions
- Triage Wednesday night, lock tier-A by Thursday noon

### Lock-in (Week -1, Thursday-Friday)
- Send lock-in DMs by Thursday afternoon
- Send moodboard links Thursday evening with lock-in
- Future-roster everyone else Friday morning
- MUA confirmations by Friday EOD

### Pre-shoot (24-48 hours before each shoot)
- 48 hours before · MUA brief with model-specific direction
- 24 hours before · model check-in DM, required reply within 4 hours
- 12 hours before · soft moodboard reminder + studio reminder
- Standby backups on alert at 24-hour mark

### Shoot day
- Arrive 90 min before first call
- Test frame before first model arrives
- MUA arrives 60 min before first model
- 90-min slots with 30-min buffers between
- Card dump + light audit during buffers
- Hard wrap at scheduled time, no exceptions

### Post-shoot (same day)
- Card dump backup before leaving
- Quick cull, mark hero frames
- One BTS clip per shoot for content harvest
- End-of-day voice note for content engine

### Post-week (Monday-Friday)
- Edit hero frames first, deliver finals within 7 days
- Send each model their finals with a thank-you
- Tag and post on grid
- Update CRM tiers based on shoot day performance
- Content harvest into 7x7 multi-platform workflow

## CRM build priority for next campaign

Build a tracking system before next campaign starts. Fields per contact:
- IG handle, real name, email, phone
- Source channel (IG DM, IG story, FB group, Threads, referral, direct)
- Form submission date (or "no form" if external)
- Tier (A, B, C, return, future, off-roster)
- Shoot history (date, palette, performance score)
- No-show history (count + reasons)
- Wardrobe confirmed (palette and pieces)
- MUA preference if relevant
- Last touch date
- Last touch type (lock, future-roster, return ping)

Ren manages this. Update after every message and every shoot.

## Voice and DM templates

### 24-hour confirmation check-in

```
Hey [name], confirming tomorrow's call.

[Day] [Date], [time] AM/PM at 2715 S Main St.

Reply YES to confirm by [time] tonight or the
slot opens to backup.

Bryceden
```

### Standby pre-stage

```
Hey [name], standby for [day] [date].

You're tier-2 backup for the slot, in case a
primary drops. I'll confirm by Friday noon if
you're activated.

If you don't hear back by noon Friday, you're
released for that day, no obligation. If
activated, full call sheet drops same day.

Free shoot for holding the day either way.

Bryceden
```

### Two-strike close (after second no-show)

```
Hey [name], appreciate the interest in shooting
with SNIPED but the production schedule needs
reliable team. After [first incident date] and
[second incident date], the rotation isn't a fit
right now.

If anything changes on your end I'm reachable,
but I won't be actively pitching slots.

Bryceden
```

### Wardrobe photo request

```
Hey [name], before I lock you in for [palette]
day, send 2-3 photos of the wardrobe pieces you
plan to bring (just phone snaps on a hanger or
on you, doesn't need to be styled).

Helps me confirm the palette read before shoot
day so we don't have surprises.

Bryceden
```
