                         Defining your ICP
How to use this document

This worksheet helps you define exactly WHO you're selling to and WHY you can win them
as customers.

A proper ICP has four components:

        ●​      Firmographics - Who they are (objective facts)
        ●​      The Specific Scenario - What situation they're in
        ●​      The Expensive Problem - The costly problem you solve
        ●​      Why You Can Reach Them - Your competitive advantage

Be honest as you can when filling this out. A well-defined ICP beats a vague one every time.
If you can't answer something, that's a red flag. Either you need to do more research or pick
a different ICP.

Before you start: Complete the "Finding Your Edge" assessment first. Use what you learned
about your experience, network, and advantages to inform your ICP choices.

Remember
Your ICP will evolve. That's normal. You might:

        ●​ Start broad and narrow down once you see which subset converts best
        ●​ Discover a different (more expensive) problem your ICP has
        ●​ Find a related ICP that's easier to reach

The goal isn't perfection on day one. The goal is to be specific enough to test and
learn.

A tight ICP that you can reach beats a perfect ICP you can't access.
