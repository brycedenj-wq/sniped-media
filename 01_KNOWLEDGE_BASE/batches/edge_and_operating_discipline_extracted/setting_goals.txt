                     Setting Your Goals
Time Required: 60 - 90 min every 3 months

How to use this document
This worksheet helps you set goals you'll actually achieve and build the reflection habit that
separates successful founders from everyone else. Most entrepreneurs set goals that are
either too vague ("grow my business") or too ambitious ("get 1,000 customers in a month").
Both lead to frustration and burnout.This framework has four components:

        ●​      SMART Goals - Setting clear, achievable targets
        ●​      Goal Hierarchy - Breaking big visions into weekly actions
        ●​      Reality-Testing - Making sure your goals are grounded
        ●​      Weekly Reflection - The system that makes it all work

Be honest as you fill this out. A realistic goal you achieve beats an ambitious goal you miss
every time. If you can't answer something, that's a signal to do more research or adjust your
goal.​




Section 1: Your SMART Goals

How Many Goals?

Start with 3 goals maximum for this quarter (3 months). More than 3 splits your focus
and reduces your chances of success.


Goal #1

S - Specific: What exactly will you achieve?

Not "grow revenue" or "get customers." Be precise.

❌ Bad: "Grow my business"
✅ Good: "Acquire 50 paying B2B customers in the accounting sector"
