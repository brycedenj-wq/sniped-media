Prompt Template - Self-Criticism (Advanced)
Customer Retention Strategy (S2A)
Task: Develop a customer retention strategy for a subscription-based business.
Prompt:
"Carefully consider all aspects of customer retention, including loyalty programmes,
personalised communication, and pricing strategies.
1. List three specific strategies to retain customers.
2. Explain the reasoning behind each strategy, focusing on how it will address customer
needs and improve retention rates."

---

Partnership Announcement Email (RaR)
Task: Write an email to announce a partnership with a major brand.
Prompt:
"Rephrase the email to ensure it:
1. Highlights the value of the partnership clearly and concisely.
2. Uses an engaging tone to excite the audience.
3. Aligns with the professional voice of the brand."

---

Annual Report Summary (RE2)
Task: Summarise the company’s annual performance in a report.
Prompt:
"Re-read your summary and confirm:
1. All major achievements and challenges are included.
2. Key metrics, such as revenue growth and market expansion, are accurately represented.
3. The summary aligns with the original data and provides a clear narrative."

---

Marketing Tagline for a New Product (S2A)
Task: Create a tagline for an eco-friendly cleaning product.
Prompt:
"Think deeply about the product's unique benefits, target audience, and competitive edge.
1. Generate three tagline options.
2. Explain the thought process behind each option and why it would appeal to customers."

Employee Handbook Section (RaR)
Task: Write a section of an employee handbook about the hybrid work policy.
Prompt:

"Rephrase the policy to make it:
1. Clear and easy to understand for all employees.
2. Positive and motivational in tone.
3. Aligned with the company’s values and goals."
