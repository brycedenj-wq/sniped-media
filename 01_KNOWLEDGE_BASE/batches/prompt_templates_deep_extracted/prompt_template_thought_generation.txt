Prompt Template - Thought Generation
Logical Reasoning (CoT)
Task: Solve a problem step by step.
Prompt:
"John has £50 and wants to buy three items costing £15, £20, and £10 each. Does he have
enough money? Break down your reasoning step by step:
1. Calculate the total cost of the items.
2. Compare the total cost with the amount John has.
3. Determine if John can afford all the items."

---

Planning a Marketing Campaign (CoT)
Task: Plan a campaign for a product launch.
Prompt:
"Step by step, outline a marketing campaign for a new electric bike.
1. Identify the target audience.
2. Decide on the key selling points to highlight (e.g., eco-friendliness, cost savings).
3. Choose three promotional channels and explain why they are effective for this audience.
4. Create a brief tagline or slogan for the campaign."

---

Problem-Solving in Business (ThoT)
Task: Handle a supply chain issue.
Prompt:
"Step 1: Identify the main problem (e.g., delays in raw material deliveries).
Step 2: Suggest two short-term solutions to minimise impact.
Step 3: Propose one long-term strategy to prevent future delays.
Step 4: Evaluate the feasibility of each solution and recommend the best approach."

---

Creative Story Writing (ThoT)
Task: Create a short story introduction with a twist.
Prompt:
"Step 1: Introduce the main character (e.g., a detective with a secret).
Step 2: Set the scene (e.g., a bustling city at midnight).
Step 3: Add a twist where the detective discovers a clue that implicates their closest ally.
Step 4: Conclude with a suspenseful cliffhanger."

---

Data Analysis and Insights (CoT)
Task: Analyse customer feedback data for insights.
Prompt:
"Step by step, analyse the following customer feedback data:
1. Summarise the key positive comments.
2. Highlight the most common complaints.
3. Suggest two actionable steps the company can take to improve customer satisfaction.
4. Explain why these steps are likely to be effective."
