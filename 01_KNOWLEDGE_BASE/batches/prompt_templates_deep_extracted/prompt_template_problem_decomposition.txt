Prompt Template - Problem Decomposition
Marketing Strategy Development (LtM)
Task: Create a marketing strategy for a new online course platform.
Prompt:
"Step by step, build a marketing strategy:
1. Start with identifying the target audience.
2. Outline the key features and benefits to highlight.
3. Choose three effective promotional channels (e.g., social media, email, webinars).
4. Develop a timeline for the campaign and suggest metrics to measure success."

---

Customer Feedback Analysis (PaS)
Task: Analyse customer feedback to identify improvement areas.
Prompt:
"Plan first:
1. Categorise feedback into three groups: positive, neutral, and negative.
2. Highlight the most common themes in each group.
3. Identify areas needing improvement.
Then execute:
1. Summarise the feedback analysis.
2. Suggest two specific improvements for the business."

---

Launching a New Product (PoTh)
Task: Plan the launch of a new skincare product.
Prompt:
"Follow these structured steps:
1. Define the target market (e.g., age, gender, skin concerns).
2. Identify three key selling points of the product.
3. Create a launch timeline with pre-launch, launch day, and post-launch activities.
4. Suggest two promotional strategies and explain why they are effective."

---

Workflow Optimisation (LtM)
Task: Optimise the workflow for processing online orders.
Prompt:
"Start with the simplest tasks and move to the more complex:
1. Identify the steps involved in the current order process.
2. Suggest improvements for reducing time spent on each step.
3. Recommend automation tools to streamline the process.

4. Create a new, optimised workflow for the team."

---

Financial Planning for a Business Expansion (PaS)
Task: Develop a financial plan for expanding into a new market.
Prompt:
"Plan first:
1. Determine the costs of expansion (e.g., marketing, hiring, operational setup).
2. Identify potential revenue streams from the new market.
3. Consider risks and mitigation strategies.
Then execute:
1. Create a budget based on the costs and revenue projections.
2. Suggest three ways to secure funding for the expansion."
