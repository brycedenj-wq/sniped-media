Prompt Template - Self-Criticism (Basic)
Product Launch Plan (SE)
Task: Develop a product launch plan for a new mobile app.
Prompt:
"Review the product launch plan you just created and evaluate:
1. Does it clearly define the target audience?
2. Are the marketing channels appropriate for reaching this audience?
3. Suggest two ways to make the plan more impactful."

---

Sales Pitch for a Service (SR)
Task: Write a sales pitch for a cloud storage service.
Prompt:
"Refine the sales pitch you just wrote by:
1. Making the language more persuasive and customer-focused.
2. Adding specific benefits like 'increased productivity' or 'cost savings.'
3. Simplifying technical jargon for a broader audience."

---

Quarterly Business Report (COVE)
Task: Summarise the highlights of a quarterly business report.
Prompt:
"Cross-check the summary you wrote for accuracy by:
1. Ensuring all key metrics match the data provided.
2. Confirming that no major achievements or challenges are missing.
3. Revising the summary to align with the original report’s tone and focus."

---

Marketing Campaign Draft (SE)
Task: Create a draft for a social media marketing campaign.
Prompt:
"Evaluate the draft of your marketing campaign by:
1. Checking if the key message is clear and engaging.
2. Identifying any missing elements, such as a call to action or hashtags.
3. Suggesting one improvement to enhance its effectiveness."

---

Process Documentation (SR)
Task: Document a process for onboarding new employees.
Prompt:

"Refine the onboarding process documentation by:
1. Reorganising steps to improve the flow of information.
2. Adding examples or templates to make it more actionable.
3. Simplifying complex instructions to make them easier to follow."
