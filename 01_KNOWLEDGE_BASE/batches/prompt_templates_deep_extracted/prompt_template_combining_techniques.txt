Prompt Template - Combining Techniques
                   (Advanced)
Comprehensive Marketing Plan
Task: Develop a detailed marketing plan for launching a new AI-powered software.
Prompt:
1. CoT: "Outline the marketing plan step by step, starting with identifying the target audience,
followed by creating key messaging, and then selecting promotional channels."
2. Problem Decomposition: "Focus on the 'Key Messaging' section. Break it down into three
main points highlighting the software's benefits (e.g., cost savings, efficiency, innovation)."
3. Self-Criticism: "Review the entire plan to ensure it aligns with the product's goals and
resonates with the target audience. Suggest improvements if needed."

---

Customer Feedback Improvement Plan
Task: Analyse customer feedback and propose an improvement plan for a retail business.
Prompt:
1. Problem Decomposition: "Categorise customer feedback into three groups: product
quality, customer service, and delivery experience."
2. CoT: "For each category, explain step by step how improvements can be made, supported
by specific examples."
3. Self-Criticism: "Cross-check the improvement plan against the feedback data. Ensure all
major issues have been addressed and propose refinements if necessary."

---

Employee Training Programme
Task: Design a training programme for onboarding new employees in a customer service
role.
Prompt:
1. Problem Decomposition: "Create an outline of the training programme with three modules:
company policies, customer interaction best practices, and technical tools training."
2. CoT: "For the 'Customer Interaction' module, explain step by step how to handle customer
complaints effectively."
3. Self-Criticism: "Re-read the training programme outline to ensure it is comprehensive and
easy to follow. Suggest improvements if any gaps are found."

---

Strategic Business Expansion Plan
Task: Develop a business expansion strategy for entering a new international market.
Prompt:

1. CoT: "Step by step, outline a plan for expanding into the new market. Include market
research, product localisation, and marketing strategy."
2. Problem Decomposition: "Focus on the 'Product Localisation' step. Break it into sub-steps
such as adapting the product to local regulations, cultural preferences, and language."
3. Self-Criticism: "Evaluate the expansion plan for feasibility. Ensure it accounts for potential
risks and propose adjustments if needed."

---

Quarterly Sales Analysis
Task: Analyse quarterly sales data and present insights to stakeholders.
Prompt:
1. Problem Decomposition: "Break the analysis into three steps: summarising sales
performance, identifying trends, and highlighting key areas for improvement."
2. CoT: "For the trend analysis, explain step by step why certain quarters performed better or
worse, considering factors like seasonality and product launches."
3. Self-Criticism: "Cross-check the insights against the raw sales data to ensure accuracy
and completeness. Refine the presentation to highlight the most compelling points."
