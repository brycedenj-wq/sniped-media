Prompt Template - In Context Learning
Content Creation:
Task: Write a social media post for a tech company about AI trends.
Few-Shot Prompt:
"Here are examples of posts:
1. 'AI is revolutionising the workplace! Discover how automation can save your team hours
every week.'
2. 'Stay ahead of the curve with AI-driven insights. Transform your business today.'
Now, write a post highlighting how AI improves decision-making in business."

---

Customer Service:
Task: Respond to a customer complaint about a defective product.
Few-Shot Prompt:
"Here are examples of polite responses:
1. 'Dear [Customer Name], we are truly sorry to hear about the defective product. We’ll issue
a replacement immediately.'
2. 'Dear [Customer Name], thank you for bringing this to our attention. We’ve initiated a
refund and will work to prevent this issue in the future.'
Now, write a response to a customer complaining about late delivery."

---

Data Analysis:
Task: Summarise a dataset and identify trends.
Few-Shot Prompt:
"Here are examples of summaries:
1. 'Q1 sales increased by 15% due to a product launch, while Q2 stabilised at 10% growth.'
2. 'The data shows a 20% decline in Q3 revenue, primarily due to seasonal factors.'
Now, summarise the following data and highlight one key trend:
- Q1: $50,000
- Q2: $60,000
- Q3: $40,000
- Q4: $55,000."

---

Education:
Task: Create a lesson plan for teaching a complex topic.
Few-Shot Prompt:
"Here are examples of lesson plan formats:

1. 'Topic: The Water Cycle. Step 1: Introduce key terms (evaporation, condensation). Step 2:
Show a diagram of the water cycle. Step 3: Conduct a hands-on activity using water vapour.'
2. 'Topic: Fractions. Step 1: Explain what a fraction is. Step 2: Use visual aids like pie charts.
Step 3: Practice with examples on the board.'
Now, create a lesson plan to teach students about photosynthesis."
