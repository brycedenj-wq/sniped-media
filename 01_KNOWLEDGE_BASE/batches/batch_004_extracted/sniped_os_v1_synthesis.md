## Section 1 · The SNIPED Visual Identity System

### 1.1 The Recognizability Test (locked)

A SNIPED frame, at thumbnail size, reads as SNIPED if all seven signatures hold:
1. **Color discipline** · monochromatic or strict color-block, locked before shoot
2. **Architectural pose** · A-frame base, body 35-45° off-axis, chin forward and slightly down, hands with task
3. **Rim light always** · the silhouette is the moat
4. **Clinical retouch** · skin unified, pore detail preserved, no plastic smoothing
5. **Graphic over atmospheric** · shapes and color blocks carry the frame
6. **Subject in command** · eyes committed before the smile
7. **Severity as default** · warmth earns its place, never the baseline

A frame that fails 4 of 7 is not SNIPED. **This is the editorial standard for HERO tier.**

## Section 2 · Composite Workflow Architecture

### 2.1 The 7-Environment Rotation (locked 2026-05-12)

The single most distinctive system in the SNIPED OS for AI composite work:

| Environment | Role | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| Brutalist Monument | Power · isolation · scale | Main hero escalation |
| Industrial Minimal | Realism · grounded cool | Chapter bridge |
| Monochromatic Void | Timelessness · silhouette focus | Identity hero |
| Sculptural Gallery | Cultural elevation · canonization | Artifact / legacy |
| Cinematic Urban | LA mythology · scene realism | Operator-coded chapters |
| Organic Surreal | Emotional softness · dream-state | Creative/fashion lane |
| Futurist Editorial | Future mythology · conceptual | RARE hero events only |

The single rule: **one environment per chapter, don't mix.** This is the constraint that creates recognizable authorship. Most AI composite artists fail because they chase novelty (new style every image). SNIPED's instinct is constrained world-building.

### 2.2 The Tool Stack (revised 2026-05-12)

**For plate generation (background only · NEVER subject):**
- Higgsfield Soul · atmospheric environments (Brutalist Monument, Cinematic Urban)
- Seedream 5.0 Lite · HEX-precise palette + camera cheat codes
- Nano Banana Pro (via Gemini) · narrative urban + likeness-preserving variants
- Adobe Firefly · in-Photoshop Generative Fill with Reference Image (Path A default)

**For composite assembly:**
- Photoshop · Generative Fill (Path A · fastest) OR layer composite (Path B · register match)
- Topaz Photo AI · plate upscale (Standard model 4x · 16-bit TIFF output)
- Neural Filter Harmonize · color/lighting unification at 40-50%

**For motion (Phase B+):**
- Higgsfield Marketing Studio · UGC reels
- Kling AI · post-conversion client surprise asset
- Seedance 2 · stills-to-video pipeline

### 2.3 The Camp B Rule (absolute)

AI tools generate INPUTS (backgrounds, moodboards, plates, reference). AI tools NEVER generate the subject's face, body, hair, or identity.

This is structural, not preferential. It is the moat against AI commoditization in 5 years. The Berger photo theory (Suit and the Photograph) underpins it: the photograph's value is in its specificity to the photographed subject in time. AI-generated faces have no such specificity.

The hybrid-operator stance (locked in memory `intel_ai_sentiment`) is: AI for world-construction yes, AI for identity no.

### 2.4 Track B Creative Push (the composite pipeline)

The 5-step Gress playbook executed:
1. Lightroom prep (crop 16:9, WB pass, subject mask polish, exposure lift, export TIFF)
2. Evoto skin pass (light touch, no body, no D&B)
3. Plate generation (one environment from the 7-rotation, NO subject in prompt)
4. Topaz upscale (4x Standard)
5. Photoshop assembly (Smart Object subject, AI subject mask, plate as background, Harmonize 40-50%, edge lights, grain unify, final color wash)

Time budget per first frame: 60-90 min. Once muscle memory locks: 30-45 min.

**Distinctive:**

- The HEX palette locked across all 7 environments (visual continuity)
- The "background replacement, not subject regeneration" doctrine
- The two-path architecture (Path A fast / Path B register-match) for different use cases

## Section 3 · Lightroom / Editing Philosophy

### 3.1 The Locked Catalog Architecture

One master catalog per year. `SNIPED_2026.lrcat`. All shoots, all Smart Collections, single backup target.

**Why this matters:** most photographers fragment by per-shoot catalogs and lose global search + cross-shoot pattern recognition + the ability to run "all heroes of 2026" as a single view. SNIPED rejects fragmentation.

### 3.2 The 5-Pass Cull (compressed via assisted culling)

- **Pass 0:** Lightroom AI auto-rejects (0 manual minutes · saves 30-45 min vs. manual)
- **Pass 1:** Reject refinement (5-8 min)
- **Pass 2:** Pick pass (10-15 min)
- **Pass 3:** Star rating (10-15 min) · 3=Proof, 4=Select, 5=Hero candidate
- **Pass 4:** Color label (2-3 min) · Red=Photoshop, Yellow=Evoto, Blue=VIB pool, Purple=case study, Green=delivered

Target: 15-25 min per Reset. The compression is the leverage.

### 3.3 The 6-Mask Kit (locked, still active across v1 → v3)

1. AI Subject Mask (Sharpening +20, NR -10)
2. AI Background Mask + grain (35/25/50)
3. Fake Rim (Subject intersected with Linear Gradient · Exposure +0.30 Highlights +15 Whites +10)
4. Skin Glow via Point Color (sample skin midtone · Sat -5 to -10 · Lum +10 to +15)
5. BG Smooth (Texture -50, Clarity -30, Sharpness -50)
6. BG Color Shift (rescue only)

**Point Color** is identified in the legacy doc as the highest-leverage underused tool. **Radial Gradient** is flagged as the trap mask most operators overuse.

### 3.4 The Develop Workflow (per Hero)

1. Base layer via `SNIPED_LOCKED_LOOK` import preset (Adobe Neutral v3 LUXURY now, was Adobe Portrait v1)
2. Per-image adjustments in locked order (do not skip, do not jump)
3. AI mask stack (5-6 masks max · resist adding more)
4. Generative Remove for in-Lightroom cleanup
5. Decision tree: Yellow → Evoto, Red → Photoshop
6. Evoto round-trip (skin clinical, pore detail preserved)
7. Apply `SNIPED_HERO_FINISH` preset
8. Export TIFF master + JPG deliverable via locked export presets
9. Topaz Photo AI sharpen on JPG only
10. Color label → Green (Heroes Live)

Per Hero target: 12-15 min. If a Hero exceeds 25 min: stop, ship or downgrade to Select. Time-cap discipline > perfection.

### 3.5 Critical Craft Discoveries (locked across v1 → v3)

- **No global grain.** Grain only on background mask. Evoto buries Lightroom global grain anyway.
- **Hard light without fill or rim is the trap.** Build rim or kicker into the setup BEFORE the first frame.
- **The floating full-body problem.** Three fixes: keep 5-10% floor visible, paint contact shadow, or crop mid-thigh. Never crop on the joint.
- **Atmospheric depth is the recurring gap.** Add atmospheric plane between subject and backdrop. The audit consistently scores depth lower than color/signature.
- **The post pipeline has a ceiling.** Raws at 38-52/80 ceiling out at 63-65/80 after edit. The ceiling lives on set, not in post.
- **BG Smooth mask doesn't fix real wrinkles.** Generative Remove first, PiXimperfect Photoshop fallback.
- **Client edit vs grid edit are different.** Client = clean baseline (color corrected, retouched, no stylization). Grid = full SNIPED stylization.
- **Photoshop is NOT routine.** Evoto + Lightroom Generative Remove handle 90%. Photoshop is last resort using PiXimperfect method.
- **Grid sequence logic.** Color stories diverge while system holds. Don't post two dark moody sets back-to-back.

### 3.6 The Tool Stack (locked Phase 1)

- Lightroom Classic · cull, develop, mask
- Evoto · skin + backdrop (primary retouch · NOT Photoshop)
- Photoshop · Red-label frames only, Track B comp, frequency separation (Brand System)
- Topaz Photo AI · final sharpen on JPG export
- Adobe Firefly · in-Photoshop Generative Fill (utility only)
- Leonardo AI · pre-shoot moodboards (internal only, never delivered)

### 3.7 What's culturally distinctive

**Distinctive:**
- The 12 Smart Collections vocabulary (color labels + stars + keywords compose into auto-views)
- The locked import preset that applies the visual signature on every frame (deviation is intentional, not creation)
- The 12-15 min per Hero discipline (most photographers have no time-cap and spend hours)
- The Evoto-as-primary-retouch (not Photoshop) workflow (most photographers default to Photoshop)
- The v1 → v3 upgrade path (Adobe Portrait retired, Adobe Neutral locked · most photographers don't audit and retire base presets)
