Finding Your Edge!
How to use this document
This assessment helps you figure out where you actually have an edge when starting a new
venture. Instead of guessing or going with what sounds good, we'll dig into your real
advantages. The stuff that makes you different from everyone else trying to do the same
thing.

Be honest as you work through this. The goal isn't to create advantages that don't exist, but
to find the genuine ones you already have.

Section 1: What You Already Have Access To

Section 7: What To Do Next
Don't Do These Things

        ●​      Convince yourself you have advantages you don't really have
        ●​      Pick something just because it's trendy
        ●​      Go after markets where you know nobody
        ●​      Focus on your weakest advantages
        ●​      Ignore whether people actually spend money on this

Good Signs You're On The Right Track

        ●​      You can name specific people who'd pay for what you're thinking about
        ●​      You've seen these expensive problems yourself
        ●​      People already trust your opinion in this area
        ●​      People come to you for advice about this stuff
