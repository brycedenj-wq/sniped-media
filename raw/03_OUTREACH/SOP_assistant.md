## 3. The ICP (locked · 4 of 4 must hit · no exceptions)

A lead must hit ALL FOUR criteria. The previous "3 of 4" allowance from the v2 CRM setup sheet is deprecated. Lock to 4 of 4.

### Criterion 1 · LA-based or LA-frequent
- Based in greater LA, OR
- Lives elsewhere but is in LA monthly for business
- Verify via LinkedIn location, recent post locations, or company HQ

### Criterion 2 · Active poster
- Posted on LinkedIn in the last 7 days
- If last post is older than 14 days, skip. Inactive founders don't see DMs.

### Criterion 3 · Visual gap
The mismatch between the founder's current visual presence and the operational level of their business.

This is the most important criterion. You're not looking for "bad photos" · you're looking for a mismatch between who they are visually and who they actually are operationally.

**Real visual gaps:**
- Founder of $5M+ company using iPhone selfies for high-stakes posts
- Technical/academic founder (PhD aesthetic) running a high-growth company that needs executive presence
- Lifestyle/casual visuals on someone running serious B2B partnerships
- Corporate-safe headshots on someone whose company sells edge / vision / narrative
- Engineering/product-only visuals on a founder who should be the public face of the brand
- Outdated headshot (3+ years old) on someone who just raised, just got press, or just hired execs

**Use this language to describe the gap in the CRM:**
- "Lacks a Power Founder visual set."
- "Needs a [VC Power Player / Visionary Leader / Strategic CEO] visual shift."
- "Missing the Founder as the Face moment."
- "Visual presence is overshadowed by company brand."

**Not a visual gap:**
- Already has professional, recent founder photography
- Posts mostly company content with no personal presence (they may not need a founder image yet)
- CEO of a 100+ employee public company (they have an in-house team)

### Criterion 4 · Has revenue or funding
- Seed to Series B startup, OR
- Solo operator with visible revenue (paid newsletter, agency, consultancy with named clients), OR
- Founder of an established $500K-$5M company
- Skip: pre-revenue, early-stage students, hobby projects, anyone who can't afford $1,500

## 4. Tier system

### Hot tier
All 4 ICP criteria + active trigger event in next 30 days. BJ contacts these first.

### Standard tier
All 4 ICP criteria, no active trigger. BJ contacts these in regular outbound batch.

### Skip tier
Misses 1+ of 4 criteria. Don't add to active CRM. Optional: keep in a separate "Skipped" sheet for monthly review.

## 5. Trigger events (tiebreaker · not requirement)

A strong lead WITHOUT a trigger is still a Standard tier lead. A strong lead WITH a trigger jumps to Hot.

**What counts as a trigger:**
- Funding round announced in last 14 days (seed to Series B)
- Speaking gig coming up in next 30 days
- Podcast tour (recent or upcoming)
- Product launch in next 30 days
- Press push or major feature in last 14 days
- Rebrand or website relaunch
- New executive hire in their company in last 14 days

**Where to find triggers:**
- LinkedIn announcement posts ("Excited to share…", "We've raised…", "Speaking at…")
- LA Business Journal, dot.LA, TechCrunch LA section
- Crunchbase recent funding filter, LA region
- The company's news/press page

## 13. Claude usage (for the assistant)

You use Claude (claude.ai · or whichever AI model BJ designates) for these specific tasks:

### Lead enrichment
Drop a LinkedIn URL into Claude, ask:
> "Profile the person at [URL]. What's their company? What stage? What's their last 14 days of LinkedIn posts about? Any signs of a trigger event (funding, speaking, launch, press, rebrand)?"

Use Claude as a research speed-up. Verify everything Claude says against the actual LinkedIn profile · LLMs hallucinate.

### Visual gap drafting
Once you've identified a candidate, you can ask Claude:
> "Here's a founder: [name, company, role, brief description]. Here's their LinkedIn photo register: [describe what their photos look like]. Their company sells [X]. Draft a one-sentence visual gap statement using language patterns like 'Power Founder,' 'VC Power Player,' 'Visionary Leader,' 'Strategic CEO.' Match the operational level of their business to the visual register they should have."

This speeds up Section 3 visual gap writing. Verify the language reads correct before pasting into CRM.

### Trigger event search
> "Search for recent (last 14 days) news, press, or LinkedIn announcements about [company name] or [founder name]. Looking for: funding round, speaking gig, podcast appearance, product launch, press feature, rebrand, executive hire."

Cross-check Claude's findings against actual sources. Don't trust unverified citations.

### What you do NOT use Claude for

- Writing DMs or emails to send (BJ owns outreach copy)
- Replying to leads (you don't reply at all)
- Generating fake "evidence" of fit (fabrication kills the relationship long-term)
- Pricing conversations
- Anything that simulates BJ's voice or signature work
- Creating visual mocks or VIB-style images (BJ owns visual production)
- Strategic decisions

### Privacy rules with Claude

- Do not paste BJ's personal info, financial info, or memory-layer content into Claude
- Do not share the locked SNIPED_OS docs that aren't on the curated list (Section 12)
- Do not save Claude conversations that contain client lead data outside of your secure workspace
