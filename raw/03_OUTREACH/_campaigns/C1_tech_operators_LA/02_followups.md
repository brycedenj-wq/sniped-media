# Email 2 + Email 3 · Follow-ups

Last updated: 2026-05-12

Same voice rules. Plain text. No em dashes. Lowercase casual subjects. Short. Different angle per follow-up · not the same email worded differently.

---

## Email 2 · Day 3 · Reframe (surface where it matters)

**Subject:** quick follow

```
Hey {{first_name}},

Quick follow on the LinkedIn photo note. The reason I send these specifically to VPs and Directors at LA tech companies is the photo gap shows up most on three surfaces:

- Company hiring posts (your face is on the careers page · 60% of engineering hires check it)
- Press / podcast thumbnails when the company gets coverage
- Sales-side decks where founders use VP headshots as social proof

If any of those are coming up this quarter for {{company_name}}, the read might be useful.

Still happy to record the 5-min Loom if you want it.

· BJ
snipedmedia.com
```

Word count: ~95.

**Why this:** Email 1 was the diagnostic offer. Email 2 names the SPECIFIC SURFACES the gap shows up on. This is the "OK but where does this actually matter for ME" answer. Three concrete surfaces makes the abstract concrete.

Company name is added in Email 2 (not Email 1) because at this point the prospect has already seen one cold email · they're past the "is this a blast?" filter. Personalization here lands as care, not creep.

## Email 3 · Day 7 · Give-away exit (warm the relationship)

**Subject:** the framework anyway

```
Hey {{first_name}},

Last one. If the timing's off, no problem.

Putting the Direction Stack 10-protocol diagnostic on my site this month as a free read · the same framework I'd use on the Loom. Want me to ping you when it's live?

If yes I'll send it over. If not, no worries.

· BJ
snipedmedia.com
```

Word count: ~65.

**Why this:** The give-away exit. Don't ghost · don't push. Offer something with zero ask. Keeps the relationship warm for the 90-day re-engage cycle. Anyone who says "yes ping me" stays on the warm list.

This is the **Perennial Seller logic** at work: most cold prospects don't convert in 7 days. They convert in 6 months when they're suddenly hiring or going on a podcast. The give-away keeps the door open.

---

## What we're NOT doing in follow-ups

- Not increasing the pressure ("circling back," "bumping this up," "didn't hear from you")
- Not asking for the call directly · still keeping the Loom as the path
- Not pitching the Reset price · the Reset doesn't come up until the Loom is delivered
- Not sending the same email reworded · each follow-up has a distinct angle
- Not following past Email 3 · suppressed after that

---

## After Email 3 unanswered

1. Move to suppressed-active list (no more cold sends for 90 days)
2. Tag in CRM as "warm-but-no-reply"
3. Re-engage cycle: at day 90, if they've had a trigger event (funding, hire, press), reach out via LinkedIn DM not email · different channel
4. The Direction Stack public release (per Email 3 promise) → notify the give-away list
