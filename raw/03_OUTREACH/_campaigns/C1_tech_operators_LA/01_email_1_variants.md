# Email 1 · Variants for A/B

Last updated: 2026-05-12

Plain text only. No em dashes. No HTML. No images. No links in body. Signature: name + website.
Word count target: 70-90 words.
Tone: mid-conversation, slight imperfection, no corporate.

## Variant A · "your linkedin photo" (register-gap direct)

**Subject:** your linkedin photo

```
Hey {{first_name}},

I'm BJ, run a portrait studio in DTLA called SNIPED.

Saw your LinkedIn while pulling a list of VPs at LA tech companies hiring this quarter. Your photo's reading 2-3 protocols off from where your role is operating, which matters more on hiring posts and press surfaces than it sounds.

Want me to record a 5-min Loom walking you through the read? No pitch, just the diagnostic.

· BJ
snipedmedia.com
```

Word count: ~72.

**Hook:** "2-3 protocols off" creates curiosity (what is a protocol? off from what?). The mention of hiring posts and press surfaces lands the relevance.

## Variant C · "5 min loom" (offer-first)

**Subject:** 5 min loom for you

```
Hey {{first_name}},

I run SNIPED in DTLA · founder + operator portraits.

Built a 10-protocol diagnostic for LinkedIn photos. Yours is reading Protocol 09 territory · "good mechanics, zero presence." Common at the VP tier, but it shows up specifically on the surfaces where role gravity matters (hiring, press, sales).

Want a 5-min Loom walking through it?

· BJ
snipedmedia.com
```

Word count: ~70.

**Hook:** Naming the specific protocol diagnosis (09) in email 1 is a high-conviction move · it tells them you actually looked. Risk: feels presumptuous to some. Reward: dramatically increases reply rate from people who think "huh, what's that mean."

## A/B test plan

Phase 1 (Week 1): Run all three variants at equal split (~33% each) across the first 2,000-2,500 prospects.

Track:
- Open rate per variant
- Reply rate per variant
- Positive reply rate (audit requested) per variant
- Negative reply rate (unsubscribe / not interested / annoyed) per variant

Cutoff for declaring a winner: 500+ sends per variant with clear separation on positive-reply rate.

Phase 2 (Week 2+): Run winner at 100%. Iterate against the winner with a new variant testing a different pain hook (NOT a different word choice · per the testing framework rule, test offers/ICPs/pains/triggers, not micro-copy).

## What we're NOT doing in email 1

- No company-specific personalization (creates breakage at volume)
- No links in body (deliverability)
- No images, no HTML, no PDF attached (deliverability + reads as marketing blast)
- No long credibility paragraph ("I've shot 50 founders" etc.) · the methodology IS the credibility
- No multi-CTA · one ask, one path
- No premature pricing · the Reset comes up only after audit delivered
- No em dashes (lifetime rule)
