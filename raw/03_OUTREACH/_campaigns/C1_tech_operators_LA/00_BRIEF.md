## ICP (locked)

**LA tech VPs, Directors, and non-founder C-Suite operators.**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Person | VP / Director / non-founder C-Suite (CTO, CPO, COO, CMO, CRO that did NOT found the company) |
| Function | Engineering, Product, Operations, Marketing, Sales, Design |
| Company stage | Series A through pre-IPO (~20-500 employees) |
| Industry | Software · SaaS · Internet · IT · FinTech · Computer Hardware · Information Services |
| Geography | Greater Los Angeles only · city-level: LA, Santa Monica, Culver City, Pasadena, Glendale, Long Beach, El Segundo, Marina del Rey, West Hollywood, Beverly Hills |
| Pool depth | ~30k+ |
| Exclude | Founders / Co-Founders / founding-CEOs (these go to LinkedIn VIB Tier 0 track · zero cannibalization) |
| Exclude | Recruiters, Photographers, Creative Directors (lane conflicts) |

**Why this ICP:** Adjacent to your existing positioning, not a lane shift. Same register/protocol gap on LinkedIn. Income clears the offer easily. Direction Stack diagnostic translates 1:1. Doesn't cannibalize the LinkedIn VIB lane.

## Pain hook (the thing email 1 names)

The **register gap** between role and visual:

- VP / Director / C-Suite role title
- LinkedIn photo reads as IC engineer at a desk, casual founder, or stock-corporate
- Gap shows up specifically on: hiring posts (their photo on the company page), press coverage, board decks, sales-side surfaces, conference speaker thumbnails

The methodology read: most operators at this tier are Protocol 09 (Good Mechanics, Zero Presence) or Protocol 07 (Forced Smile / Emotional Disconnection). Their photo is technically fine, no operator gravity.

## Front-end offer (the no-friction yes)

**5-min "Loom audit" using the Direction Stack 10-protocol framework.**

Production format (per `05_loom_production_workflow.md`):
- BJ-voiced master audio templates (one per protocol · 10 total · recorded once, reused forever)
- Higgsfield-generated prospect-specific visuals (their photo → SNIPED register treatment)
- Assembled in Descript or similar editor
- ~5-10 min Ren-led production time per prospect

The "Loom" stays on the hybrid-operator side of the locked AI stance: BJ's voice anchors the methodology · Higgsfield does world-construction only · no AI avatar of BJ, no AI face replacement.

## Back-end offer (what they buy)

**The Reset** · $1,500 · 1 look · 20 final images · 5-day turnaround · 50% non-refundable deposit.

CTA path:
- Loom delivered → soft Calendly link at end of Loom + at bottom of email reply
- Discovery call → 15-min, runs SOP_discovery_call.md
- Aligned → Reset booking
- Day-7 testimonial → Op Kit pitch at week 2 if conditions are right

## Sequence (3 emails max)

| Email | Day | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Email 1 | Day 0 | Pain hook + offer + soft assumptive CTA |
| Email 2 | Day 3 | Reframe · same offer, different angle (e.g., "saw [trigger event], the photo gap shows up most when [specific surface]") |
| Email 3 | Day 7 | Final touch · "if not now, here's the framework anyway" (give away the Direction Stack scorecard as a thank-you exit · keeps the relationship warm for future) |

After Email 3 unanswered: move to nurture list (90-day re-engage). Don't send a 4th.

## Volume + cadence

- 25 inboxes × 30 emails/day = ~750 emails/day max
- Conservative start: 20 emails/inbox/day × 25 inboxes = 500 emails/day
- Weekly: ~2,500 prospects emailed
- Monthly: ~10,000

Lead pull cadence: 1,500/week from Super Search.
Lead burn cadence: ~2,500/week emailed.
Net: pull more than email, so the backlog grows for tier-tagging discipline. Tier 1 leads get priority placement in the queue.

## Success metrics (don't track vanity)

| Metric | Target | Action if below |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | 40-60% | Below 30%: deliverability issue · pause campaign, audit inbox health |
| Reply rate | 3-7% | Below 2%: copy iteration · test new email 1 hook |
| Positive reply rate (audit requested) | 50%+ of replies | Below 30%: offer is unclear · rework |
| Audit → call booked | 30-50% | Below 20%: Loom isn't converting · refine Loom format |
| Call → Reset close | 20-40% | Below 15%: discovery call SOP refinement · price objections cluster |
| Reset booked / week | Target: 2-4 | Below 1/wk after 3 weeks: ICP wrong, restart |

Phase B trigger reminder: $3K MRR sustained 2 months. Reset at $1,500 × 2/week × 4 weeks = $12k/month. Single Reset/week = $6k/month (Phase B trigger fires in month 2). System-design for 1-2 Resets/week minimum from cold email alone.

## What this campaign is NOT

- Not a substitute for the LinkedIn VIB lane. Different ICP. Both run in parallel.
- Not a place to test offers that aren't the Reset · the Reset is the locked back-end. Front-end Loom is the test variable.
- Not a high-touch personalization play. Volume is the goal. Personalization happens in the Loom (Higgsfield-generated visual), not in email 1.
