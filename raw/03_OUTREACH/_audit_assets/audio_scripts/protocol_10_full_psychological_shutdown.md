# Protocol 10 · Full Psychological Shutdown

**The read:** retreat from the lens, dead-eyed gaze, dated visual, sometimes literally an old photo the operator never replaced. The face is in the frame but the person isn't.

**Most common in:** older operators who've stopped engaging with the photo surface, exec-tier veterans who haven't updated since a 2018 corporate shoot, anyone whose LinkedIn photo is more than 5 years old.

---

## Observation · ~80 sec

```
Protocol 10 · Full Psychological Shutdown. The most severe
diagnosis on the Direction Stack.

What's happening in your photo: the face is technically there,
but the person isn't. The eyes don't register engagement with
the lens. The expression is flat in a specific way that reads
as retreat, not as composed neutrality. The body shows up.
The operator doesn't.

This is different from Protocol 09. Protocol 09 has good
mechanics with no presence. Protocol 10 has presence that's
deliberately withdrawn. The operator showed up to the shoot
and decided not to be there.

Three causes I see most often. One · the photo is dated. Five,
seven, ten years old. The operator's been doing the work for
a decade since this was taken. The photo's frozen in time.
The current operator isn't in it.

Two · the operator dislikes being photographed. Most operators
in this category say something like "I don't do well in front
of cameras" or "I never look good in pictures." Which means
they've armored up. The shutdown is a defensive position.

Three · the operator outgrew the photo and never updated. The
role got bigger. The face on LinkedIn stayed the role they had
when the photo was taken.

All three produce the same read · the person on the page isn't
the person doing the work today.
```

## Implication · ~75 sec

```
The impact of Protocol 10 is severe because the photo is doing
the opposite of what it should be doing.

For a senior operator · VP, C-suite, founder · the photo is
supposed to communicate that the person leading the company
is present and engaged. Protocol 10 communicates absence. The
brain processes it as "this person isn't really here." Which
contradicts the bio claiming they run the company.

On hiring · candidates filter on this without naming it. The
shutdown read translates to "the leadership at this company
isn't really present." Senior candidates pass before they read
the role. They don't want to work for an absent leader.

On press cycle · journalists pick photos that match the story
they're telling. Protocol 10 photos get used for stories about
companies in trouble, leadership transitions, or as B-roll in
broader industry pieces. They don't get used for stories about
companies winning.

On partnerships · the photo is the operator's avatar in
preliminary conversations. Other operators read the avatar
before the meeting. Protocol 10 starts the conversation with
a disadvantage that has to be overcome before substance can
land.

This is the protocol that costs operators the most across the
most surfaces. And it's almost always solvable in a single
shoot.
```

## The Fix · ~75 sec

```
The fix for Protocol 10 is a complete rebuild.

The earlier protocols can be addressed by adjusting one or two
things in a single frame. Protocol 10 requires the whole photo
to be replaced. The shutdown is too foundational to fix in
post-production or with a re-edit.

What that looks like in practice: a 90-minute Reset shoot in
the studio. Start by re-establishing the operator's relationship
with the lens. Most operators who land in Protocol 10 haven't
been deliberately photographed in years. The body forgot how.

We rebuild gradually. Test frames. Direct from neutral to
engaged. Watch the eyes light up · or not · in real time on
the tethered screen. Adjust direction based on what's landing.
Most operators move out of shutdown within the first 20 minutes
of the shoot.

What gets delivered is not the same person putting on a smile.
It's a portrait that catches the actual operator who runs the
company today. Eyes engaged. Jaw active. Body open. Direction
clear. The person on the LinkedIn page becomes the person
behind the role.

This is the highest-value protocol upgrade because it crosses
the largest gap. From "absent operator" to "present operator"
is a register shift that every surface · hiring, press, partner,
board · responds to.

One shoot. New photo. Different operator on the page.
```
