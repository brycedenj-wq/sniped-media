# Master Audio · Universal Intro + Outro

These segments record ONCE. Spliced onto the start and end of every protocol-specific script.

Recording notes: lowercase, conversational, slight imperfection. Don't read like a script. Pause where natural. If you stumble, leave the stumble · sounds human. Per `Direction_Shift` doc: "the content has been too perfect, nobody reads it." The voice needs texture.

---

## INTRO · ~30 sec · universal

```
Hey. BJ from SNIPED. Quick 5-min walkthrough on your LinkedIn
photo here.

I run a portrait studio in DTLA, and I built a diagnostic
framework called the Direction Stack that breaks every operator
photo into one of ten patterns. Ten years of shooting compressed
into ten things I look for. We use it on every client shoot,
and I'll walk you through what I'm seeing on yours.

This is a free read. No pitch attached. Just the diagnostic.
If it lands, the conversation's open. If not, all good.

The protocol I'm seeing on your photo is [PROTOCOL NAME].
Let me walk through what that means.
```

Length when read at conversational pace: ~35-40 seconds.

Recording variants to capture during the session:
- Version A: above, exactly as written
- Version B: same script but slightly more terse (cut the "ten years of shooting" line)
- Version C: same script but BJ adds one sentence of personal texture ("I had this exact gap on my own LinkedIn for years before I built the framework")

Pick the strongest of the three after recording. Default to A unless C lands better.

Length when read at conversational pace: ~25-30 seconds.

## What this voice should NOT do

- Don't read like a presentation. Talk like you're showing a friend something on your phone.
- Don't say "I noticed" or "I observed." Say "I see" or "you're getting" or "what's happening here is."
- Don't apologize. Don't say "if I may." Don't soften with "this might sound harsh." Just say the thing.
- Don't use em dashes (even in voice, the slight gap reads as em dash on transcript).
- Don't promise things you don't know · no "I guarantee this will boost your hiring metrics by X%."
- Don't badmouth their photographer. The photo isn't bad. The protocol is just a specific gap.

## What this voice SHOULD do

- Lowercase casual register. "the protocol that's hitting on yours" not "the protocol I'm observing in your photograph."
- Acknowledge the texture of being in the seat. "everyone has this. I had it for years." Real.
- Name the gap directly. No hedging. "your photo's reading IC engineer not VP. The fix is in three things."
- Stay in the field. Reference specific places the gap shows up (hiring page, press cycle thumbnails, conference speaker slides). Concrete.
- Land on status, not utility. "your role earned a photo that operates at that altitude" beats "your photo could be optimized."

## Production setup

- Quiet room, no echo. Even a closet with clothes works.
- Phone mic in airplane mode is fine. Pro mic better but not required.
- Record in Descript directly so editing/clipping is on the same file.
- One take per script if possible. Don't overproduce.
- Total recording session target: one Saturday morning, 9 AM to 1 PM.
- 10 scripts × ~3-5 min each = ~40 min of audio. Add intro/outro variants + retakes = ~3.5 hr session.
