## THE FRAME
- **Winner-take-most markets** · few hits make most of the money. Photography commercial work, books, content all behave this way.
- **Superstar economics** · the top 1% of any category captures disproportionate value. Named photographers, named clients, named books.
- **Distribution dominates** · a great work without distribution dies. A good work with great distribution dominates.
- **Few big bets > many small ones** · concentrating bet allocation on highest-leverage opportunities wins over diversification in this market.

For SNIPED: don't spray. Pick the 1-2 anchor bets per phase. Direction Stack book launch IS a blockbuster bet (perennial + blockbuster combined). One named LA founder portfolio piece beats 10 medium pieces.

## OUTPUT
- Whether this situation deserves blockbuster allocation or steady-state
- If blockbuster: what the concentrated bet looks like, what distribution leverage exists
- If not: redirect to steady-state Phase 1 cadence

## REFUSE
- Spreading bets thin across 5+ initiatives simultaneously
- "Just do a little bit of everything"
- Big bets without distribution plan (great work + zero distribution = death)
- Blockbuster framing on Phase 1 daily cadence work (overkill)
