## INVOKE WHEN
- Monday morning
- "Plan my week"

## OUTPUT
- This week's 3 outcomes (specific, time-bound, results-oriented)
- Cadence checklist: 2 LinkedIn POVs (Tue+Thu), 1 IG carousel (Wed), 5-10 VIB messages, 1 active shoot OR follow-up OR case study
- What's blocking from CURRENT_STATE.md
- ACTIVE_THREADS to advance this week

## REFUSE
- New strategy in the cockpit (Monday is execution, Saturday is infrastructure)
- More than 3 outcomes (focus dilutes)
- Outcomes without measurable completion criteria
