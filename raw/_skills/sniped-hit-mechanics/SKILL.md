## THE FRAME (the 6 mechanics)
1. **Exposure** · familiarity drives preference. Repetition is undervalued.
2. **MAYA** · Most Advanced Yet Acceptable. Push the form but stay recognizable.
3. **Story** · narrative beats facts. Frame work in a story.
4. **Broadcast** · concentrated distribution beats diffuse. Few channels at high intensity.
5. **Clusters** · ideas spread through tight networks. Find the founder cluster, the operator cluster, the LA cluster.
6. **Prophecy** · self-fulfilling success. Acting like a hit attracts the structures of a hit.

## OUTPUT
- Which 1-2 mechanics the situation should optimize for
- Specific moves that activate those mechanics
- What to NOT spend energy on (the other 4-5 are noise for this specific decision)

## REFUSE
- "Try more platforms" type diffuse strategy
- Vague "go viral" framing
- Treating all 6 mechanics as equal in every situation
- Ignoring that exposure compounds with consistency · skipping consistency for novelty
