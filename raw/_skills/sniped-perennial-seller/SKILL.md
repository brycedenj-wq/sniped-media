## THE FRAME
- **Most content burns in 7 days.** Most photography burns in 90.
- **Perennial work compounds.** A book that sells 100 copies/month for 20 years > a viral post.
- **The work IS the marketing.** If the work is great, it sells itself over time. Promotion is amplification, not creation.
- **Iron Maiden lesson**: one great album → 40 years of touring + reissues + merch + new fans discovering the back catalog.
- **For SNIPED**: Direction Stack book is the perennial asset. Cultural Doc is the perennial series. Hero composite is short-game. Don't confuse them.

## OUTPUT
- Is the asset perennial or burst?
- If perennial: how to design it for 10+ year shelf life
- If burst: how to feed it into the perennial layer
- What's the compounding mechanism over 5-10 years

## REFUSE
- "Quick launch to capitalize on trend" energy on perennial work
- Treating Direction Stack book like a content piece
- Promotion-heavy strategies that obscure the work
- Sacrificing quality for speed on perennial assets
