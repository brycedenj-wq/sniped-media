## 1 · The audience-compounding principle

The audience compounds when **the same shoot produces multiple outputs across multiple surfaces, each output reinforcing the visual identity**, and each output creates a feedback signal back into the next shoot.

The error most photographers make: each post is independent. No through-line. The grid reads as 30 different photographers' work mashed together. SNIPED's discipline is the opposite: every output looks like it came from the same operator running the same methodology, and the aggregate of outputs IS the brand.

**The audience-compounding test:** if a viewer scrolls 5 SNIPED posts in a row, can they answer "what is this practice doing" without reading captions? If yes → the visual doctrine is holding. If no → drift.

## 2 · Every shoot · 5+ outputs · the operational pipeline

This is the missing operational layer. One shoot = at minimum 5 deployable outputs. Brief the editor against this. Mentally pre-load this before each shoot day.

```
ONE SHOOT DAY
       │
       ▼
    Heroes (10-12 for Reset · per v2 delivery architecture)
       │
       ├─→ OUTPUT 1 · Pixieset client gallery (paid deliverable, the work itself)
       │
       ├─→ OUTPUT 2 · Carrd Selected Work pool (1-2 strongest Heroes added if subject permission allows; replaces weakest existing entries quarterly)
       │
       ├─→ OUTPUT 3 · IG Carousel #1 · single shoot's hero frames (3-7 frames, posted 14-21 days post-delivery, with subject's permission and credit)
       │
       ├─→ OUTPUT 4 · LinkedIn POV (1 protocol-named POV anchored in this specific shoot's diagnostic correction · uses 1 hero frame · published in the weekly POV cadence)
       │
       └─→ OUTPUT 5 · IG Stories burst (BTS phone footage from shoot day · published as 5-8 story segments same day or next day · evergreen for the brand even though Stories expire)

PLUS conditional outputs:

       ├─→ OUTPUT 6 · BTS Cut video (30-60 sec) · IF the editor has bandwidth and the shoot produced strong B-roll · per video philosophy Format 1
       │
       ├─→ OUTPUT 7 · Cultural Documentation contribution · IF this shoot was Lane 2/3 (Community / Athletes) and earns the Sunday at [Institution] or After the Game series slot
       │
       ├─→ OUTPUT 8 · Substack essay (Year 2+) · IF the shoot anchors a longer-form essay on methodology · institution · subject's context
       │
       ├─→ OUTPUT 9 · Case study · IF the shoot becomes a Reset case study post (Day-7 testimonial captured + 1 hero frame + protocol named + deployment context)
       │
       └─→ OUTPUT 10 · Referral mechanism · IF the shoot generates a warm intro within 30 days that becomes Pipeline activity

PLUS network outputs:

       └─→ OUTPUT 11 · MUA / stylist / collaborator content · IF a production partner contributed (e.g., Hermine MUA), they receive curated frames they post in their own portfolio · their post is SNIPED's secondary distribution
```

**The pre-shoot question:** "What outputs will this shoot produce?" Pre-decide the 5+. Capture intentionally for them. Do not wait until edit-time to figure out what content to make.

## 3 · The Instagram-specific operational layer

The content philosophy covers IG generally. This section is operationally specific.

### 3.1 The grid as portfolio (not chronological after launch)

- **Months 1-3 (Phase 1 build):** post chronologically. Establish baseline.
- **Month 4+:** curate the grid. Pinned posts (max 3) anchor the top. Below the pin, the most recent 9-tile preview should read as a coherent visual claim. If a recent post breaks the visual claim, archive it (Instagram archive · doesn't delete · removes from grid).
- **The 9-tile rule:** open your IG profile in incognito. Look at the 9 tiles above the fold on mobile. Do they read as ONE photographer's work or 9 different ones? If 9 different, archive 5-6.

### 3.2 Carousel discipline (the primary post format)

Per the content philosophy: "Carousel preferred over single image." This is the deployment specificity:

- **First frame (cover):** a Hero frame, vertical-friendly composition, that earns the swipe. The cover frame stops the scroll.
- **Frames 2-3:** the strongest supporting Heroes from the same shoot.
- **Frames 4-6:** Selects-tier frames showing production scale or environmental context.
- **Frames 7-8 (optional):** A behind-the-scene phone frame OR a detail shot OR a process moment.
- **Final frame:** a text card with subject name + protocol applied + caption-deeper-dive · OR a single statement frame in SNIPED type.

Carousel length: 5-8 frames typical. Below 5 reads as thin. Above 8 the swipe-completion drops.

### 3.3 Single image posts (rare)

Reserve single-image posts for:
- The Art Series drops (each frame is the post; Art Series is single-image by design · Year 2+ deployment)
- The "single statement" portrait that doesn't need supporting context
- Cultural documentation moments where a single frame carries the full weight

If you find yourself posting a single image because "I only have one strong frame from this shoot," that's a signal the shoot needs more capture next time, not a permission to thin the grid.

### 3.4 Stories vs Feed (the daily cadence)

| Surface | Cadence | What lives here |
|---|---|---|
| **Feed posts** | 2-3/week max in Phase 1 · 3-5/week in Phase B | Heroes carousels · Cultural doc carousels · Art Series drops (Year 2+) |
| **Stories** | Daily during shoot weeks · 0-3/week during edit weeks | BTS · ambient studio · subject reposts · process moments · network maintenance touches |
| **Reels** | 1-2/week max in Phase 1 from existing carousels | Same content as carousel, format-flipped vertical · NOT separately produced |

**The discipline:** Feed is the body of work. Stories is the texture. Reels is the cross-post mechanic. Don't optimize for Reels separately; let them be vertical cuts of feed content.

### 3.5 Hashtag discipline

Per the content philosophy: 1-3 hashtags max on IG. Never on LinkedIn. The hashtag rules:

**Use:**
- 1 niche tag (e.g., `#dtlasstudios` or `#latfashion`) for discovery in specific scenes
- 1 brand tag (`#snipedmedia`) for self-archive findability
- 1 subject-specific tag if relevant (`#emmanuel33rd` for cultural doc · subject's Instagram handle)

**Never:**
- Hashtag soup (10+ tags)
- Generic tags (`#photography` `#blackandwhite` `#portrait`)
- Trending hashtags unrelated to the work
- Engagement-bait tags

### 3.6 Bio + link strategy

- **Profile photo:** SNIPED self-portrait (operator-coded · DTLA studio backdrop · severe register)
- **Name field:** `Sniped Media · Bryceden Jones`
- **Bio (3 lines max):**
  - Line 1: positioning · "founder photography · DTLA · LA" or similar tight phrase
  - Line 2: methodology proof · "10-protocol Direction Stack · methodology-first"
  - Line 3: CTA · "Reset · DM or [link]"
- **Link in bio:** snipedmedia.com (the Carrd) · single link · NOT Linktree (Linktree dilutes the operator-coded register)

### 3.7 Story Highlights structure

Highlights are the permanent navigation layer of the IG profile. Curate them.

| Highlight | Content | When to update |
|---|---|---|
| `Resets` | Hero frames + brief subject-name caption per delivered Reset | After every delivered Reset |
| `BTS` | Studio process · setup · methodology in operation | Monthly refresh |
| `Op Kits` | Multi-look engagements | After every delivered Op Kit |
| `Cultural Doc` | Sunday at Emmanuel · After the Game · institutional work | After every cultural doc shoot |
| `The Methodology` | Direction Stack protocol explainers (1 per protocol · 10 total) | Q3 onward, paired with book launch |
| `Press / Features` | When SNIPED gets featured externally · publications · podcasts | When they happen |

Phase 1 priority: build `Resets` + `BTS` highlights first. Others come as content accumulates.

### 3.8 What makes someone follow vs just like

The follow happens when the viewer can predict what they'll get from the next post. Predictability requires:

- **Visual consistency** (same color discipline · same crop logic · same tonal register)
- **Caption consistency** (same voice · protocol-named · operator-coded · no hedging)
- **Cadence consistency** (predictable enough that the viewer notices the absence)

A like is the response to ONE post. A follow is the response to PATTERN. The pattern is the visual doctrine deployed across 8-12 posts that look like they came from the same operator.

**Phase 1 specific:** the page might have 50-80 posts already. Audit them. If they don't read as the same operator's work, archive aggressively. The grid above-the-fold is the conversion surface; subscriber decisions happen there.

## 4 · The audience-compounding feedback loops

The compounding works when the streams feed each other. Five primary loops:

### Loop 1 · LinkedIn POV → VIB conversion lift

LinkedIn POV builds authority signal in the LA founder bubble. A founder who has seen 3-5 SNIPED LinkedIn POVs over 6 weeks is meaningfully more likely to reply to a VIB DM than a cold-cold prospect. Each weekly POV warms the next month's VIB conversion rate.

**Measure:** track VIB reply rate by trigger source. Prospects who showed in LinkedIn engagement (liked / commented on a POV in the prior 90 days) should convert at 2-3× the cold rate. If they don't, the POVs aren't doing the trust work · diagnose voice, register, or protocol-naming clarity.

### Loop 2 · Heroes shipped → portfolio growth → next inquiry

Every delivered Reset's strongest 1-2 Heroes go into the Carrd Selected Work pool (with subject permission). The Carrd is the destination of every DM-to-link conversion. A Carrd with 30 strong Heroes converts at a higher rate than a Carrd with 10. The portfolio grows ON ITS OWN as Resets ship.

**Measure:** Carrd visit-to-Calendly-click conversion rate. If it's stable as the portfolio grows, the work is consistent enough. If it climbs, the body of work is reaching critical mass.

### Loop 3 · Cultural doc → community trust → referral → paid pipeline

A Sunday at Emmanuel post seen by 30 church members generates 0-1 inquiries directly. But it puts SNIPED in the awareness layer of the entire COGIC network. 6 months in, a member of that congregation books a Reset because "I've been seeing your work at Emmanuel." That's the loop. It's slow and unmeasurable in week-by-week metrics. It compounds.

**Measure:** track the source of every referral inquiry. By Year 1 end, what percentage came through Lane 2 (Community/Church) work? If it's 0%, the content isn't compounding the right way. If it's 20-30%, the loop is working.

### Loop 4 · IG carousel → profile visit → DM → discovery

The carousel is the discovery surface. Someone scrolls IG, sees a strong carousel, clicks the profile, scrolls the grid, clicks the link, and lands on Carrd. From Carrd they DM or book Discovery. Every step has a drop-off. The carousel quality determines the top of the funnel.

**Phase 1 measurable:** profile visits per carousel post. If a carousel produces <50 profile visits, the cover frame failed; if it produces 200+, the cover frame is working.

### Loop 5 · Substack subscriber → owned audience → product launch (Year 2+)

The Substack subscriber list is the owned audience asset. When the Direction Stack book launches Q3 2026, the Substack list is the launch audience. When a course or zine launches in Year 2+, the Substack list is the buying base.

**Phase 1 (current):** Substack drafting deferred per lean override. The loop is dormant.
**Phase B (Months 4-6):** Substack drafts begin.
**Phase C (Months 7+):** Substack live and active. The loop activates.

If you find yourself doing any of these, the corrective is the 2-min decision rule: switch to VIB production or Reset edit work or Cockpit fill-in.

## 8 · "Instantly recognizable" · the operational deployment

Per the content philosophy: a SNIPED image must read as SNIPED at thumbnail size. Operationally this means:

### The 5 markers visible in every published frame

1. **Color discipline** · monochromatic / strict color-block · locked before shoot
2. **Pose architecture** · chin forward+down · A-frame base · hands with task · body off-axis
3. **Rim light** · the silhouette · the SNIPED moat
4. **Clinical retouch** · pore detail preserved · no plastic skin
5. **Graphic over atmospheric** · shapes and color carry the frame

### The 3 markers visible in every published caption

1. **Protocol-named** · 1 of the 10 Direction Stack protocols referenced (or operator-coded narrative if Series B/D)
2. **Specific deployment context** · names where the photo gets used (not generic "love this shoot")
3. **No sentimental fluff** · no "blessed" · no exclamation soup · no emoji decoration

### The 2 markers visible in every published carousel

1. **Sequencing matters** · the cover frame earns the swipe · the final frame closes the loop
2. **Subject's name + context + permissions** · always

If a viewer scans 5 SNIPED posts and can't predict the 6th's vibe, the doctrine is slipping.

## 11 · The "fake work" filter for content

Before producing or scheduling any audience-facing piece, run:

1. **Does this serve at least one of: revenue · authority · distribution · IP?**
2. **Does this match the 5 visible markers from Section 8?**
3. **Will this still hold in 5 years?**
4. **Does this fit the current phase (Phase 1 / B / C)?**
5. **Is there a delivered Reset / shipped shoot anchoring this content, or am I producing content because I "should be posting"?**

If any answer is no, the piece doesn't post. The discipline of NOT posting is what protects the brand.
