# Email · No-show or late followup

**Trigger:** Client is 15+ minutes late OR did not show. Send within 30 minutes of the scheduled start time.

**Subject:** `[First name] · we good?`

**Body (15-30 min late, no contact):**

```
[First name],

I have the studio set up at 2715 S Main · holding the slot for another 30 minutes. Reply or call [PHONE] if you are en route or running into something.

If today does not work, the deposit holds the next available date. Reply with two windows that fit and I will lock in.

· BJ
```

---

**Body (no-show, 60+ min past start):**

```
[First name],

Studio time is closed for today · life happens. The deposit holds the next available booking; no penalty on this round.

Two options:
1. Reschedule within the next 14 days · reply with two windows that fit
2. Move the deposit to a different month · reply with the rough timeframe

If neither works, the deposit converts to studio credit (good for 12 months on any SNIPED engagement). Either way, the original booking does not need to be re-paid.

Reply when you can.

· BJ
```

---

## Reschedule policy (encoded in the MSA, surfaced here for client clarity)

- First reschedule: free, no penalty, must be within 14 days of original booking
- Second reschedule: $150 rescheduling fee (covers studio prep cost)
- Third reschedule: deposit forfeited, new booking required at full price
- This policy is in the Reset MSA Section 3 and is what the email language is enforcing.
