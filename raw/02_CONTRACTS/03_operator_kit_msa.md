# SNIPED Media · Operator Kit MSA

**Use for:** every booked Tier 2 Operator Kit. Pitched only at Day 30 of a delivered Reset, only to satisfied Reset clients.

> **Legal disclaimer:** working template. CA-licensed attorney review required before first deployment. Operator Kit pricing has a soft band ($3,000–8,000); the contract should be regenerated per engagement with the specific scope and price filled in.

## SNIPED MEDIA · MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (OPERATOR KIT)

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on **[DATE]** between:

**Sniped Media** ("SNIPED"), operated by Bryceden Jones, principal place of business Los Angeles, California.

**Client:** **[CLIENT FULL LEGAL NAME OR ENTITY]** ("Client"), with address **[CLIENT ADDRESS]**.

### 1. Services
SNIPED will perform a multi-look photography engagement (the "Operator Kit Session") in accordance with this Agreement. The Operator Kit Session includes:
- **[NUMBER]** wardrobe / looks (default: three)
- **[NUMBER]** locations (default: two; one studio + one on-location)
- Up to **[DURATION]** hours of on-camera time (default: half day, four hours)
- Pre-shoot creative direction call (45 minutes, scheduled within 14 days of contract execution)
- Pre-shoot brief delivered to Client at least 48 hours before the Operator Kit Session
- One (1) on-set creative direction lead (Bryceden Jones) running the 10-protocol Direction Stack methodology

### 2. Deliverables
SNIPED will deliver, within ten (10) business days following the Operator Kit Session:
- **[NUMBER]** fully retouched final images (the "Final Images") · default: forty (40), distributed across the looks
- Delivered via password-protected Pixieset gallery link
- Available in both web-optimized and print-ready resolution (300 DPI)
- A delivery summary document specifying recommended deployment per image (LinkedIn header, press feature, deck portrait, podcast cover, etc.)

### 3. Compensation
**Total fee: [TOTAL FEE]** (within the $3,000–$8,000 Operator Kit band).

Payment schedule:
- **Deposit:** fifty percent (50%), **non-refundable**, due upon execution of this Agreement to confirm the Operator Kit Session date.
- **Balance:** remaining fifty percent (50%), due no later than the day of the Operator Kit Session, prior to the start of shooting.

### 4. Commercial License (12-Month Standard)
This Section is the primary differentiator from the Reset MSA. The Final Images are licensed for Client's commercial use under the terms below.

- **License grant:** upon full payment, SNIPED grants Client a non-exclusive, worldwide commercial license to use the Final Images for a term of twelve (12) months from the date of delivery, for the following uses:
  - Client's owned digital properties (website, blog, app, customer portals)
  - Paid social media advertising under Client's brand
  - Press and PR distribution
  - Investor and stakeholder materials (decks, board reports, annual reports)
  - Internal Client communications
  - Earned media licensed to publications featuring Client (op-eds, profiles, interviews)
- **License renewal:** at the end of the twelve (12)-month term, the license automatically renews for additional twelve (12)-month periods at **[RENEWAL RATE]** of the original Total Fee (default: 30%–50%, negotiated at execution), **unless Client notifies SNIPED in writing at least thirty (30) days before renewal that the campaign is retired and Client will discontinue use of the Final Images.**
- **License restrictions:** Client may not (a) sublicense the Final Images to a third party for that party's branded use; (b) use the Final Images in any campaign for a competitor of SNIPED's photography or creative services business; (c) sell or transfer the Final Images; (d) materially alter the Final Images by cropping, recoloring, or compositing in a way that defeats SNIPED's editorial integrity.
- **Out-of-scope uses:** uses outside the scope above (broadcast advertising, billboard / out-of-home, packaging, retail point-of-purchase, exclusive use, brand ambassador campaigns) require a separate scoped license at SNIPED's then-current rate.

### 5. Likeness and Promotional Use
Client (and any executive subjects of the Operator Kit Session) grants SNIPED a perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide license to use likenesses for SNIPED's portfolio, marketing, social media, website, and editorial use. Where Client requires confidentiality during a competitive moment (pre-launch, IPO window, M&A), Client may invoke a six (6) month embargo on SNIPED's promotional use by notifying SNIPED at the time of contract execution; the embargo is documented in the Confidentiality Addendum.

### 6. Cancellation and Rescheduling
- **Reschedule:** Client may reschedule once at no cost with seven (7) days written notice. Subsequent reschedules are subject to a $500 fee.
- **Cancellation by Client:** the Deposit is non-refundable. Cancellation within seven (7) days of the Operator Kit Session forfeits both Deposit and Balance.
- **Cancellation by SNIPED:** if SNIPED is unable to perform due to equipment failure, illness, or force majeure, SNIPED will reschedule within thirty (30) days. If rescheduling is not feasible, SNIPED will refund the Deposit and any Balance paid.

### 7. Pre-Production Scope and Change Orders
The pre-shoot creative direction call and brief lock the scope of the Operator Kit Session. Material changes after scope lock (additional looks, additional locations, additional retouched images, expanded license scope) require a written Change Order signed by both parties, with associated fees disclosed before work proceeds.

### 8. Edits and Revisions
The Final Images are SNIPED's selection, retouched to SNIPED's editorial and technical standard. The Total Fee includes one (1) round of minor revisions (skin retouching adjustment, color tone shift) on up to ten (10) of the Final Images, requested within five (5) business days of delivery. Additional revisions or revisions outside this scope are billed at $150 per image.

### 9. Force Majeure, Limitation of Liability, Indemnification
Sections 8, 10, and 11 of the Reset MSA apply equally here, with "Total Fee" understood as the Operator Kit Total Fee.

### 10. Independent Contractor, Governing Law, Entire Agreement
Sections 12, 13, and 14 of the Reset MSA apply equally here.

## Operational deployment notes

- Pitch only at Day 30 of a satisfied Reset client. The Day-30 trigger lives in the Notion Shoots DB view `Day-30 Op Kit pitch trigger`.
- Use the delivered Reset's hero image in the pitch deck. The proof is the work, not a sales narrative.
- Soft pricing band ($3,000–$8,000) means the same MSA template generates per-engagement contracts. Always specify the Total Fee, the look/location count, and the renewal rate before sending.
- The 12-month auto-renewal at 30–50% is the recurring revenue mechanism. Track renewal dates in Notion (add a `License renewal date` field on Pipeline cards in `Op Kit Delivered` status).
- Out-of-scope uses (billboards, packaging, brand ambassadorship) are upsell triggers to Brand System pricing.
