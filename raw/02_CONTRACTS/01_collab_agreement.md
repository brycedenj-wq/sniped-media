# SNIPED Media · Collab Agreement (one-page TFP-equivalent)

**Use for:** any free shoot in the `Free Collab` category. Models, creators, network warm-up shoots, demographic-fit Art Series collaborators.

**Do not use for:** paid Reset / Op Kit / Brand System (those need the MSA), brand-paid shoots (those need the brand contract), or church/community shoots (those use the simpler Community Release).

> **Legal disclaimer:** this is a working template. A California-licensed attorney should review before first deployment. Once reviewed and locked, deploy via HelloSign / Dropbox Sign / HoneyBook for digital signature.

## SNIPED MEDIA · COLLAB AGREEMENT & MODEL RELEASE

This Collab Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on **[DATE]** between:

**Photographer:** Sniped Media (operated by Bryceden Jones, "SNIPED"), with principal place of business in Los Angeles, California.

**Model / Collaborator:** **[FULL LEGAL NAME]** (the "Model"), residing at **[ADDRESS]**.

The parties agree as follows:

### 1. Engagement
SNIPED and Model agree to participate in a creative photography session (the "Shoot") at:
- **Location:** [LOCATION]
- **Date and time:** [DATE / TIME]
- **Duration:** approximately [DURATION] hours

Both parties enter the Shoot as creative collaborators. **No monetary compensation will be paid by either party.** Compensation is the deliverable described in Section 3.

### 2. Deliverable
SNIPED will deliver to Model **one (1) fully retouched final image** (the "Hero Image") of SNIPED's selection, within twenty-one (21) days of the Shoot, via Pixieset gallery link.

Additional images from the Shoot are available for purchase at SNIPED's standard rate of **eighty dollars ($80) per additional retouched image**, accessible through the Pixieset gallery for a window of forty-eight (48) hours after delivery.

### 3. Image Rights and Licensing
- **Copyright:** SNIPED retains full and exclusive copyright to all images, raw and retouched, captured during the Shoot.
- **Model's license to the Hero Image:** Model receives a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use the Hero Image for personal and **non-commercial portfolio use only**, including personal social media, personal portfolio sites, and personal printing.
- **Model's restrictions:** Model may not (a) sell, sublicense, or transfer the Hero Image; (b) use the Hero Image to promote a competing photography or media service; (c) submit the Hero Image to a stock photography agency; (d) use the Hero Image for any commercial endorsement of a brand or product.
- **Commercial use by third parties:** any commercial use of any image from the Shoot by a brand, agency, publication, or third party requires a separate paid commercial license from SNIPED at SNIPED's then-current rate.
- **Model's likeness release:** Model grants SNIPED a perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide license to use Model's likeness from the Shoot for SNIPED's portfolio, marketing, social media, website, exhibitions, books, and any other promotional or editorial purpose, in any medium now known or later developed.

### 4. Credit
- When Model uses the Hero Image, Model agrees to credit `@snipedmedia` (Instagram) or "Sniped Media" (other platforms) in the post or caption.
- When SNIPED uses Model's likeness in promotional contexts where attribution is customary, SNIPED will credit Model where reasonably feasible.

### 5. Cancellation and Rescheduling
Either party may cancel or reschedule the Shoot with at least seventy-two (72) hours notice without penalty. Cancellations within seventy-two (72) hours forfeit the right to reschedule for sixty (60) days.

### 6. No Confidentiality
The Shoot, the Hero Image, and the broader image set are not confidential. Both parties may publicly post about the collaboration after the Hero Image is delivered.

### 7. Liability
Each party is responsible for their own conduct, transportation, and personal property during the Shoot. SNIPED is not liable for personal property damage, injury, or loss except where caused by SNIPED's gross negligence.

### 8. Independent Contractors
The parties are independent collaborators. This Agreement does not create an employment, partnership, agency, or joint venture relationship.

### 9. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflict of laws principles. Any dispute arising under this Agreement will be resolved in the courts located in Los Angeles County, California.

### 10. Entire Agreement
This Agreement is the entire understanding between the parties regarding the Shoot. Modifications must be in writing and signed by both parties.
