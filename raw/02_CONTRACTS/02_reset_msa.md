## SNIPED MEDIA · MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (RESET)

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on **[DATE]** between:

**Sniped Media** ("SNIPED"), operated by Bryceden Jones, principal place of business Los Angeles, California.

**Client:** **[CLIENT FULL LEGAL NAME OR ENTITY]** ("Client"), with address **[CLIENT ADDRESS]**.

### 1. Services
SNIPED will perform a single photography session (the "Reset Session") in accordance with this Agreement. The Reset Session includes:
- One (1) wardrobe / look
- One (1) location (selected by SNIPED in consultation with Client)
- Up to forty-five (45) minutes of on-camera time
- Pre-shoot brief delivered to Client at least 24 hours before the Reset Session

### 2. Deliverables

> **v2 OVERRIDE (2026-05-06):** Deliverables structure superseded by `/01_OFFERS/delivery_architecture_v2.md`. New contract language below reflects v2; the v1 "20 retouched final images" framing is retired.

SNIPED will deliver, within five (5) business days following the Reset Session, via password-protected Pixieset gallery link:

- **Heroes:** ten to twelve (10-12) fully retouched final images, editorial-grade, available in high-resolution (300 DPI for print, sized for web)
- **Selects:** thirty to forty (30-40) color-graded final images, production-quality, available in high-resolution for personal and internal-business use
- **Proofs (upgrade gallery):** sixty to one hundred (60-100) additional images from the session, available for upgrade purchase during a fourteen (14) day window from gallery delivery (see Section 4 · Gallery Upsell)

Heroes and Selects are included in the Total Fee. Proofs are gallery-navigation and upgrade-discovery only; download access is gated behind upgrade purchase.

### 3. Compensation
**Total fee: One thousand five hundred dollars ($1,500).**

Payment schedule:
- **Deposit:** seven hundred fifty dollars ($750), **non-refundable**, due upon execution of this Agreement to confirm the Reset Session date.
- **Balance:** seven hundred fifty dollars ($750), due no later than the day of the Reset Session, prior to the start of shooting.

Payment by credit card, ACH, or other digital method via SNIPED's invoicing platform. Returned or chargedback payments incur a $50 fee.

### 4. Gallery Upsell

> **v2 OVERRIDE (2026-05-06):** Upsell mechanic superseded by `/01_OFFERS/delivery_architecture_v2.md` Section 4. The 48-hour $80/image window is retired in favor of a 14-day three-path upsell structure.

During a fourteen (14) day window from gallery delivery, Client may purchase upgrades:

- **Select-to-Hero upgrade** (Path A): any image in the Selects sub-collection upgraded to full clinical retouch (Hero tier) at sixty dollars ($60) per image. Bundle pricing: five (5) upgrades for two hundred fifty dollars ($250); ten (10) upgrades for four hundred fifty dollars ($450).
- **Proof-to-Select promotion** (Path B): any image in the Proofs sub-collection promoted to color-graded Select tier at thirty dollars ($30) per image. Bundle: five (5) for one hundred twenty dollars ($120).
- **Commercial license upgrade** (Path C): use of any Hero or Select frame for paid advertising, branded campaigns, or commercial deployment beyond the personal/internal-use license granted in Section 5 requires separate scoped license; pricing per the rate card in `/01_OFFERS/delivery_architecture_v2.md` Section 4.1.

Upgrade purchases do not extend any other term of this Agreement. The fourteen (14) day window matches the gallery expiry; the Proofs sub-collection auto-hides at expiry.

### 5. Image Rights and License
- **Copyright:** SNIPED retains full copyright to all images, raw and retouched, captured during the Reset Session.
- **Client's license:** upon full payment of the Total Fee, Client receives a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use the Final Images for **personal and internal business use only**, including:
  - Personal social media (LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter / X, Facebook, personal website)
  - Internal company communications and presentations
  - Personal portfolio
  - Press and media inquiries where Client is the primary subject (interviews, podcast appearances, news features)
- **Client's restrictions:** Client may not (a) sublicense or transfer the Final Images to any third party for that party's commercial use; (b) use the Final Images for paid advertising campaigns, branded content campaigns, or commercial endorsements without a separate commercial license from SNIPED; (c) crop, alter, edit, or apply filters to the Final Images such that the visual integrity is materially changed; (d) submit the Final Images to a stock photography agency.
- **Commercial license upgrade:** if Client wishes to use the Final Images for paid advertising or branded campaigns, a separate commercial license is available from SNIPED at SNIPED's then-current commercial rate, scoped by media, geography, and term.

### 6. Likeness and Promotional Use
Client grants SNIPED a perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide license to use Client's likeness from the Reset Session, including the Final Images, for SNIPED's portfolio, marketing, social media, website, exhibitions, books, and editorial purposes, in any medium now known or later developed. SNIPED will not use Client's name in commercial endorsements without Client's separate written consent.

If Client requests confidentiality (e.g., NDA before public release), this section is replaced with a separate Image Confidentiality Addendum negotiated in advance.

### 7. Cancellation and Rescheduling
- **Reschedule:** Client may reschedule the Reset Session once at no additional cost, with at least seventy-two (72) hours written notice. Subsequent reschedules are subject to a $250 fee per occurrence.
- **Cancellation by Client:** the Deposit is non-refundable. Cancellations within seventy-two (72) hours of the scheduled Reset Session also forfeit the Balance.
- **Cancellation by SNIPED:** in the event of equipment failure, illness, or force majeure, SNIPED will reschedule the Reset Session within thirty (30) days at no additional cost. If rescheduling is not feasible, SNIPED will refund the Deposit in full.

### 8. Force Majeure
Neither party is liable for failure or delay caused by events outside its reasonable control, including natural disasters, public health emergencies, government actions, power or internet outages, equipment failure, illness, injury, or labor disputes. The affected party will use reasonable efforts to reschedule promptly.

### 9. Edits and Revisions
The Final Images are SNIPED's selection from the Reset Session, retouched to SNIPED's editorial and technical standard. **No revisions** to retouching are included in the base fee. If Client requests edits to specific images, additional retouching is billed at $150 per image with a 5-business-day turnaround.

### 10. Limitation of Liability
SNIPED's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the Total Fee paid by Client. SNIPED is not liable for incidental, consequential, special, or indirect damages, including lost profits, lost business, or lost opportunity, even if SNIPED was advised of the possibility.

### 11. Indemnification
Client will indemnify and hold SNIPED harmless against claims arising from Client's misuse of the Final Images outside the license granted in Section 5, or from Client's misrepresentation of any third party appearing in the images.

### 12. Independent Contractor
SNIPED is engaged as an independent contractor. This Agreement does not create an employment, partnership, agency, or joint venture relationship.

### 13. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. Any dispute is resolved in the courts of Los Angeles County. The prevailing party is entitled to recover reasonable attorney's fees and costs.

### 14. Entire Agreement
This Agreement is the entire understanding between the parties regarding the Reset Session. Any modifications must be in writing and signed by both parties.
