### 1.2 Positioning (what SNIPED Media actually is)
**A visual systems business for LA founders.** Not a photography service. Not a headshot business. The product is **visual authority engineering** for founders whose personal visual presence sits below the operational level of their business.

**Thesis (from Gemini master thread, locked):** most founders don't have a content problem; they have a visual infrastructure problem. The camera reads two layers simultaneously, mechanical posture and psychological presence. Most founders fail one or both. The fix is not a better photographer; it is a directed photographer running a 10-protocol system on set.

The lane: editorial photography + operator-grade visual production + B2B founder image consulting. Face-forward, craft-forward, aesthetic-distinct from agency or commercial headshot work.

### 1.3 Aesthetic (Aesthetic Statement v1)
Highly polished, commercially-driven editorial portraits built on:
- Monochromatic / strict color-block palettes
- Clinical retouch (Evoto for backdrop + skin)
- Severe, confident posing for graphic impact
- Graphic over atmospheric (shapes and color carry the frame, not mood lighting)
- Skin as the contrast point
- Commercial editorial lane (money + fire pictures, NOT museum tier)

**Texture signature:** 10 custom Evoto backdrop overlays, grain on background only, subject surgically sharp.

**Recurring descriptors filter:** Monochromatic. Commercial. Studio. Editorial. Graphic. *If a future shoot doesn't fit at least three, it's outside the lane.*

**Two open growth edges:** depth/dimensionality (rim/hair light by default) and narrative weight (shoot the in-between, not into the pose). v1 will update after the next 5-10 shoots.

### 1.5 Outreach method: the Visual Infrastructure Board (VIB)
Static 1920×1280 px Figma asset on charcoal #1A1A1A:
- Left panel: prospect's LinkedIn photo, **completely unaltered**
- Right panel: SNIPED reference frame, demographic + aesthetic register matched
- Captions below each panel use Direction Stack protocol language
- No watermark, no logo, no SNIPED branding on the asset (work IS the branding)

**Hard rule:** prospect's photo is never edited, animated, marked-up, or AI-rendered. The contrast does the selling. This is what separates the method from gimmick outreach (animated headshots, red-circle markups) that fails the not-creepy test.

**Paired with:** DM under 80 words naming a specific Direction Stack protocol failure, connected to a deployment context (LinkedIn, press, deck, podcast), asking for a 10-min conversation. **No pricing in the first DM.**

**Escalation matrix:**
- Cold, no trigger: static Board only
- Cold, with trigger: static Board + 60–90 sec Loom
- Tier 0 (event in <14 days): Loom-only with Board on screen
- Post-conversion (delivery moment): animated Kling/Higgsfield clip

### 1.8 Strategic free work (categorized)
Three explicit categories, each with a return mechanic:

- **Access Free:** event coverage in exchange for downstream commercial introductions (Live Nation, promoters, sponsor brands). The commercial conversion is the play, not the event coverage itself.

### 1.9 Art Series and the photographer canon
Nine-photographer canon, each with a Study (research) and Art Series (Bryce's interpretation): Avedon, Eggleston, Leibovitz, Stephen Shore, Fred Herzog, Robert Frank, Joel Meyerowitz, Iturbide, Ernst Haas. The thesis: **studio discipline can produce gallery-weight bodies of work when each frame solves a stated aesthetic problem from the canon.** Each photographer closes a specific gap (narrative weight, depth, signature, atmospheric layering).

**Mechanism:** quarterly drop engine. One frame every two weeks (9 frames ≈ 18 weeks per series). Carousel format on IG. Each frame paired with a 100-word caption (the Avedon-style protocol-named teaching) and a 500–800-word essay on Substack. Prints (edition of 25, 10x15 matted, lab partner TBD) as monetization.

**Crucial discipline:** the Art Series is leverage for commercial work, not a replacement. If a frame would consume time that displaces a paid Reset, the frame waits.
