Each loop is tagged with its **primary engine** per CANONICAL_TRUTHS.md (Truth 1 · three engines: Revenue · Audience · Reputation). Several loops feed more than one engine; the tag identifies the dominant function.

**If you remove any loop, identify what fills the gap before removing.** The system is brittle to skipping; it is robust to slowing.

## 2 · What stays BJ-only (the un-delegate-able · forever)

These are sacred. Delegating them collapses the brand.

1. **Direction Stack diagnostic on every shoot.** The 10-protocol named-correction is what people pay $1,500 for. Delegating it = becoming a generic studio.
2. **The on-set 90-second opener.** The trust-build that decides the next 45 minutes. Cannot be subcontracted.
3. **The pricing decision.** Holding the price. Refusing the discount. Walking away from the wrong-tier work.
4. **The methodology refinement.** Updates to Direction Stack, Aesthetic v2/v3, Production Stack. The IP can only evolve through the operator who lives it.
5. **The Year-10 vision.** What game is being played. Which opportunities serve it, which don't. Strategic decision-making.
6. **Relationship-deepening conversations** with the top 5-10 network nodes (Larry, Pearl, Bishop Peters, Tracy, future Op Kit clients). Cannot be delegated to a VA.
7. **The aesthetic call.** Which frames make the gallery, which become Selected Work, which become Art Series. Editorial judgment.
8. **The decision to decline.** Wrong-tier client, conflicting brand, free-shoot-no-return. Saying no protects the moat.
9. **The Substack essay voice** (when started Q3 2026). The writing is the credential. Cannot be ghost-written.
10. **The book authorship.** Direction Stack + future books. Ghost-written = generic = brand-killing.

Everything else is delegate-able with the right system. The discipline is recognizing where the line is.

**The automation rule (from the v1.1 corrections):** simple > reliable > repeatable > scalable. Manual first. SOP-then-template-then-automate. Delegate only after the workflow is proven and documented.

**The anti-pattern to avoid:** building Zapier integrations for workflows you've run 3 times. The integration breaks; you spend more time fixing the integration than the manual flow would have cost.

**The first 5 workflows worth automating** (in order, with triggers):

1. **Calendly → Pre-shoot brief email at 24h** (trigger: 5+ Resets booked, manual flow proven repeatable). Mo 3-4.
2. **Stripe invoice triggered by Calendly booking** (trigger: 10+ Resets booked at $1,500, manual flow shows friction). Mo 4-6.
3. **Pixieset 24-hour-before-expiry reminder** (trigger: built-in feature; just enable). Mo 1.
4. **Tax 25-30% auto-transfer to tax account on every Stripe deposit** (trigger: separate account opened). Mo 2-3.
5. **LinkedIn POV scheduled posting via native scheduler** (trigger: 6+ POVs queued). Mo 2-3.

**Things to NOT automate even when tempting:**

- VIB sends (the personalization quality matters; auto-sends look like auto-sends)

## 5 · Founder conditioning · operator-muscle practices

Doing the work is one thing. Building the operator brain is another. These are the meta-practices that train the muscle.

### 5.1 Daily (5 min, end of work)

**The Highest-Leverage Question:**

> "Did I do the highest-leverage thing today, or did I do something easier that felt productive?"

Answer honestly. Log a one-line note in Notion (a custom property on the daily Operator Dashboard, or a journal entry · whichever is friction-free). Patterns become visible after 30 days.

The point is not to feel good. The point is to make the avoidance pattern visible so it can be corrected.

### 5.2 Weekly (Monday AM, included in the Cockpit)

**The Three Operator Questions:**

1. "What is the single Constraint this week?" (one bottleneck, daily attention until cleared)
2. "What am I refusing to do this week?" (the kill-list · explicit not-doing)
3. "Where am I likely to drift?" (anticipated avoidance pattern, named in advance)

These three answers go on the Monday Cockpit page. Reading them as you start the week is the operator practice.

### 5.3 Monthly (last Monday, Constraint Audit)

**The Compounding-vs-Motion Audit:**

For the prior 4 weeks, categorize where the time went:

| Category | Hours | % | Compounding asset produced? |
|---|---|---|---|
| VIB / outreach | | | Yes (pipeline) |
| Reset delivery | | | Yes (revenue + archive) |
| LinkedIn POV + comments | | | Yes (audience + DM warm layer) |
| Cultural Documentation | | | Yes (body of work) |
| Doc-writing / strategy | | | **No (motion)** |
| Tool fiddling / setup | | | Maybe |
| Trend research / scrolling | | | **No** |
| Recovery / non-SNIPED | | | Yes (sustainability) |

If the "No" categories total more than 15% of SNIPED hours for the month, that's the next month's Constraint.

### 5.4 Quarterly (first Monday of quarter)

**The Drift Mirror:**

Sit with the Reverse Roadmap (`REVERSE_ROADMAP.md`) Year-10 vision. Ask:

- Did this quarter's work move toward Year 10 or away from it?
- Did I take any work / opportunity / decision this quarter that, if reviewed in 5 years, would embarrass me?
- What did I refuse this quarter that I'm proud of refusing?

15 minutes, alone, with a notebook. No screen.

### 5.5 Annual (December)

**The 10-Year Test:**

Pull the year's strongest 30-50 frames into a single review screen. Read them as a body of work. Ask:

- Does this look like a body of work, or does it look like 50 separate jobs?
- What's missing (subject type, environment, register) that the next year should pursue?
- What's repeating in a way that's becoming a signature, not a rut?

This is the founder-as-curator practice. The body of work is the asset; this is how it stays curated.

### 5.6 The handling-the-loneliness practice

Solo founder work is structurally lonely. The conditioning practice that addresses it:

- **Quarterly mentor call** with Larry Bernard (already planned in spine; don't skip).
- **Monthly peer check-in** with one other operator-photographer (TBD; identify by Year 1, build by Year 2).
- **Weekly conversation with Pearl** about something that's actually happening with SNIPED (not just "how was your day"). The relationship integrity depends on Pearl seeing the work, not just hearing about its existence.
- **Travel-week protocol:** schedule one Pearl-anchored evening per travel week (FaceTime, dedicated). Field engineering miles compound; relationship miles must compound too.

### 5.7 The avoid-burnout practice

The system has to survive 10 years. Burnout = system collapse. The discipline:

- **One full-recovery day per week** (no SNIPED, no engineering work). Sleep, fitness, social, Pearl.
- **30-day rolling check:** if the prior 30 days had less than 3 full-recovery days total, the next 30 days require at least 4.
- **Vacation discipline:** 2-3 actual vacations per year (5+ days, no SNIPED work, no engineering work). Year 1 budget allows; do it.
- **Sleep floor:** 6.5 hours minimum, 7.5 target. Below 6.5 for 3+ consecutive nights = mandatory recovery day.

Not motivation. Operations. Recovery is part of the system.

## 6 · Drift detection system

Patterns that signal you're slipping, with named correctives. Run this against the Daily Highest-Leverage Question and the Weekly Cockpit.

### 6.1 Symptom: overthinking

**Signs:**
- Opening 5+ tabs of references before starting a task
- Talking through a decision out loud for 30+ minutes
- Adding to a strategy doc instead of shipping the next move
- Re-reading the Operating Brief / Content Philosophy / Reverse Roadmap in the same week

**Corrective:** 2-minute timer. Make the decision in 2 minutes. Ship. Learn from outcome. The Operating Brief is reread quarterly, not weekly.

### 6.2 Symptom: emotionally avoiding

**Signs:**
- Choosing edit work over outreach (edit feels productive; outreach feels exposed)
- Choosing planning over executing
- "I need to research this more first"
- Spending Mon Protected Hour on anything except VIB production

**Corrective:** name the avoidance out loud (literally speak it, alone, to yourself). Ship the avoided thing FIRST today. The discomfort is the signal that this is the high-leverage work.

### 6.3 Symptom: wrong game

**Signs:**
- Checking IG analytics multiple times per day
- Comparing follower counts to other LA photographers
- Feeling "behind" relative to creator-economy peers
- Tempted by viral-format content that contradicts the doctrine

**Corrective:** re-read REVERSE_ROADMAP.md Section 0 ("What game are you actually playing"). Close the analytics tab. Do one thing today that produces archive value, not metric value.

### 6.4 Symptom: drifting from moat

**Signs:**
- Considering a wedding / family / lifestyle gig "this one time" for the money
- Saying yes to a brand whose values conflict with the operator-coded thesis
- Discounting the Reset price to close a deal
- Accepting a free shoot without a written return mechanic

**Corrective:** re-read content philosophy Section 6 ("Do not become this"). Decline the wrong-tier work even at short-term cost. The body of work compounds against you when the wrong frame enters it.

### 6.5 Symptom: underprepared

**Signs:**
- Pre-shoot brief not sent at 24h
- Contract not signed before shoot date
- Arriving at studio less than 60 min before the client
- Edit running into Day 5 of the SLA without buffer

**Corrective:** the pre-flight checklist (in the Monday Cockpit). Run it Friday for the upcoming week's shoots. If anything's missing, Monday's Protected Hour is allocated to fixing it.

### 6.6 Symptom: motion confused with progress

**Signs:**
- Producing 5+ posts in a week with no VIBs sent
- Spending 4+ hours on the workspace docs in a week with no Reset shipped
- "Updating the system" feels like a rewarding accomplishment
- Posting BTS Stories without producing a paid Reset

**Corrective:** the Compounding-vs-Motion Audit (Section 5.3). Run it weekly if drift is detected; monthly otherwise.

### 6.7 Symptom: engineering job creeping into SNIPED time

**Signs:**
- SNIPED Mon Protected Hour repeatedly skipped due to engineering work
- Engineering travel weeks expanding (more frequent, longer)
- Choosing engineering OT for the money over SNIPED execution

**Corrective:** the engineering job is the funding mechanism, not the priority. If SNIPED hours are systematically displaced, the field-engineer income trajectory needs review (the Year 5 transition timeline shifts). Acceptable trade. Not acceptable: silent capacity erosion.

### 6.8 Symptom: relationship infrastructure neglected

**Signs:**
- 14+ days since meaningful conversation with Pearl about non-logistics
- 60+ days since touching base with Larry
- 30+ days since a non-transactional church / community presence
- Network nodes going stale in Notion Clients DB

**Corrective:** the relationship is the inheritance. Schedule one unsolicited touch this week (a delivered print, a thoughtful email, a coffee, a non-logistics conversation). The relationship infrastructure is the thing that compounds across 10 years. Neglect it and Year 10 becomes harder, not impossible · but harder.
