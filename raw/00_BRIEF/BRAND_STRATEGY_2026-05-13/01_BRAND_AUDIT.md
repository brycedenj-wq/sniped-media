## SNIPED · the audit

### What SNIPED means today

- Photography + creative direction brand
- 2 builds posted to IG (Yae · 01, Yae · 02)
- Card system designed in Figma (master template `AiMtRfT8W33yZRf4khjnds`)
- ~0 audience (per 100Q audit · pre-audience phase)
- 120+ docs in the OS reference SNIPED
- Direction Stack methodology lives under SNIPED
- Reset offering positioned under SNIPED ($1,500 floor)
- The chapter rollout doctrine names SNIPED

### What SNIPED communicates well

- **Distinctive.** Doesn't sound like every other photography studio.
- **Memorable phonetically.** 1 syllable, hits hard.
- **Has an edge.** Aggressive verb implies precision, taking the shot, single decisive moment.
- **Coheres with subject-as-target framing.** "I snipe portraits" · we capture the decisive frame.

### What SNIPED communicates badly

- **Register clash.** The work is Aperture / LIFE / Magnum quiet. The name reads tactical / predatory / fast-twitch. Operator-coded identity values restraint · the name shouts.
- **Creator-economy adjacent.** Sounds like a TikTok hustle brand, a sneaker resale tag, or a shooter game. Tells in 3 directions, none of them editorial.
- **Doesn't translate to founder lane.** "Bryce, founder of SNIPED Media" reads less serious than "Bryce, founder of BASEPLATE." Forbes-narrow.
- **Past-tense ending.** "Sniped" implies completion / hit / taken. Closes the loop. Editorial archives are about open arcs · multi-decade.
- **Search collision.** Sneaker sniping, ticket sniping, sniper games all dominate the search index. Hard to own.
- **Hard to wordmark.** SNIPED in Playfair Black creates visual tension with the editorial register. Most serif-Black wordmarks for SNIPED look like a streetwear logo, not a publisher.
- **Doesn't extend.** "SNIPED Studio" / "SNIPED Press" / "SNIPED Editions" all sound awkward · the past-tense verb fights the noun form.

### Estimated audience equity to migrate

- IG builds posted: 2 (Yae · 01, Yae · 02)
- Followers: ~0
- Card serial numbers issued: 0
- Web domain audience: 0
- Direct equity to migrate: minimal · maybe 50 IG impressions total
- Embedded equity: a few months of internal Claude / OS work using the name

**Migration cost in audience terms: approximately zero.**

## BASEPLATE · the audit

### What BASEPLATE means today

- LLC filed (BASEPLATE Media, LLC)
- EIN not yet filed
- Conceptually staged as parallel entity for AI / content / faceless plays separate from SNIPED
- "BASEPLATE Publishing" named as imprint inside BASEPLATE Media
- ~0 audience under this name
- Has not yet been used in any external-facing work

### What BASEPLATE communicates well

- **Engineering-aligned.** BJ is an AWS field engineer + systems architect. BASEPLATE is the foundation under everything · matches the actual identity.
- **Photography-credible.** A baseplate in photography is a real thing · the bottom of a camera that interfaces with the tripod. Direct technical reference.
- **Industrial-quiet.** Doesn't shout. Doesn't market. Matches the v3 LUXURY register of the work.
- **Product / platform / system coded.** Reads like Vercel, Datadog, Substack, Stripe · an infrastructure brand. Forbes-credible.
- **Operator-coded.** Refuses obvious "creative" naming. Like calling a fashion house WORK or LABOR. The refusal IS the signal.
- **Extends cleanly.** BASEPLATE EDITIONS, BASEPLATE STUDIO, BASEPLATE PRESS, BASEPLATE LAB all read naturally.
- **Doesn't need explaining.** Everyone knows what a baseplate is. Foundation. Infrastructure. The base.
- **Owns ground.** Search results dominated by tech/engineering context · less collision than SNIPED.
- **Time-durable.** Engineering vocabulary is centuries old. Won't feel dated in 2036.

### What BASEPLATE communicates less well

- **Less photography-explicit.** A first impression doesn't immediately scream "photography studio." But this might be a feature · BASEPLATE doesn't lock the company into one medium.
- **Industrial-coded.** Could feel cold or anonymous if not paired with the right visual register.
- **3 syllables.** Slightly longer than SNIPED's 1. Not a real problem.
- **Some collision risk.** "Baseplate" used in industrial / construction / camera-accessories markets · trademark in "media / publishing / photography" category should be filable.

### Estimated audience equity to migrate

- Used externally: never
- Internal references: minimal
- Migration cost: essentially zero

## What the 100Q audit and Operator Questions surfaced

From the work we just did:

**Identity (Operator Q):**
> "Quiet command. Restraint over volume. Editorial silence. Aperture / LIFE / Magnum archival mood."

The name should read quiet · BASEPLATE does · SNIPED doesn't.

**What I'm forcing because I think it looks successful (Operator Q):**
> "The Forbes 30-under-30 chase risks becoming performative."

A name that reads founder-credible reduces the gap. BASEPLATE closes it · SNIPED widens it.

**100Q Q19 · Circle to be known inside first:**
> "LA tech founder / operator class."

Founders take infrastructure / platform brands seriously. BASEPLATE reads as one of theirs. SNIPED reads as a content creator.

**Operator Q · Removing makes premium:**
> "Saturation. Marketing copy. Multiple subjects. Engagement bait. Hashtags. Any visible attempt to convince the viewer."

BASEPLATE removes the convincing. SNIPED tries to convince (the edge is a sell).

**Operator Q · Doesn't need explaining:**
> "Everyone knows what a baseplate is."

That's the closing argument.

## Conclusion of audit

SNIPED was a name picked when the work was still defining itself. The work has since defined itself (operator-coded · editorial-archival · methodology-led). The name no longer matches the work.

BASEPLATE is the name the work has been quietly asking for. The LLC already filed under it isn't a coincidence · the engineering identity surfaced first, then the photography brand tried to be louder than it should have been.

**Recommendation: commit BASEPLATE.** Migration plan in file 09.
