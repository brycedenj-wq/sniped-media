## The question

Does the current brand architecture (SNIPED as primary brand · BASEPLATE as parallel LLC for AI/content side-plays) communicate what BJ is actually building? Or does the work require a stronger / clearer name that doesn't need explaining?

## The answer (full rationale across files 01-09)

**Yes · rebrand the primary work.** SNIPED has a register problem the operator-coded identity work just exposed. BASEPLATE is the strongest available name across all 10 evaluation criteria. LLC already filed. Migration cost is moderate · audience cost is approximately zero (pre-audience phase).

**Recommended architecture:**

```
BASEPLATE Media, LLC  (the legal parent · already filed)
└── BASEPLATE  (the master brand)
    ├── BASEPLATE EDITIONS  (the chapter archive · what SNIPED was)
    ├── BASEPLATE STUDIO    (paid client work · Reset, brand commercial)
    ├── BASEPLATE PRESS     (publishing · Direction Stack book + future titles)
    └── BASEPLATE LAB       (experimental · AI / Higgsfield / parallel plays)
```

**What dies:** SNIPED as a top-line brand. It's not banned · it can resurface as the name of a specific series, a future edition, or a methodology trademark. But it stops being the masthead.

**What survives:** The chapter system. The Cards. The v3 LUXURY register. The Direction Stack methodology. The lineage doctrine. Every locked doctrine. The work is identical · the wordmark on top of it changes.

---

## Why this matters now (not later)

1. **Pre-audience phase.** Zero audience equity to migrate. The cost of rebranding doubles every quarter as the chapter archive grows. Now is the cheapest moment.
2. **CH01 ships tonight under SNIPED.** Acceptable · CH01 is the prototype, the proof-of-system. CH02 onward opens under BASEPLATE.
3. **LLC already exists.** Zero legal cost to commit.
4. **The Forbes 30 under 30 path requires a coherent founder narrative.** "Founder of BASEPLATE" reads as a serious operator. "Founder of SNIPED" reads as a creator-economy brand.
5. **The chapter Card masthead is the moment of canonization.** Every chapter shipped under the wrong wordmark is a card you'll want to reprint later. Print the right name first.
