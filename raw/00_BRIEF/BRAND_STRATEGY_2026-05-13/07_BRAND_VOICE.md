# 07 · BRAND VOICE · How BASEPLATE Speaks

**Date:** 2026-05-13

Voice is the most replicable layer of a brand. It must be specific enough that any copy can be tested against it.

This file governs everything BASEPLATE says publicly · captions, bio, taglines, deck copy, DMs, Card colophons, future website, future book copy.

It does NOT govern BJ's personal DMs to friends (see `feedback_dm_voice.md` · gender-specific rules + casual register hold there). BASEPLATE voice = brand voice. Personal voice = personal voice. They are different.

---

## The 4 voice anchors

These four words govern every surface. Recall from file 06.

| Anchor | Means | Looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Named** | Sign the work. No anonymous publication. | "Direction & photography by Bryce Denj." |
| **Sealed** | Editions close. Nothing endless. | "Edition I · MMXXVI · Chapter 02 · Serial 01 / 01" |
| **Quiet** | No volume. No engagement bait. No marketing copy. | "Yae · 01" as the entire caption |
| **Lineage** | Work belongs to a cultural arc, not a single moment. | "Published in the Black church lineage · 2026" |

If a draft fails any of the four · rewrite.

---

## What the voice IS

- **Declarative.** "Yae · 01." Not "Meet Yae!" Not "Excited to share Yae!"
- **Compressed.** A 4-word caption beats a 40-word one. Less is the discipline.
- **Subject-centered.** Captions name the subject before the photographer. Always.
- **Archival.** Past-tense or present-stative voice. Never future-marketing voice. Not "Coming soon" but "Now in the archive."
- **Cultural.** References cultural lineage, tradition, predecessors. Not "first ever" energy.
- **Operator-coded.** Refuses to perform expertise. The work shows · the copy doesn't tell.
- **Trust-equation aware.** Low self-orientation. Credentials in colophons · not in headlines.
- **Roman / Latin numeral comfortable.** VOL I · MMXXVI · CH 02 reads quietly archival vs "VOLUME 1 · 2026 · CHAPTER 2" which reads breathless.

## What the voice is NOT

- **Marketing copy.** No "elevated · curated · sun-drenched · stunning · iconic · unforgettable."
- **Engagement bait.** No "what do you think?" or "tag someone who..." or "swipe to see more."
- **Hashtag-heavy.** Zero hashtags on EDITIONS posts. Optional sparingly on STUDIO surfaces.
- **Performative honesty.** No "real talk," "honest answer," "let me be transparent."
- **Founder-influencer.** No "what I learned from..." or "5 lessons from..."
- **Listicle / numbered.** No "3 things every founder needs to know."
- **Em-dashes.** Lifetime rule. Replace with middle dot · or comma · or period.

## Word lists

### Words used freely (operator-coded · doctrine-aligned)

archive · chapter · edition · imprint · sealed · named · lineage · register · operator · subject · direction · methodology · cultural · documented · compounded · published · numbered · serial · volume · framed · stilled · contained · restrained

### Words used carefully (acceptable in context)

editorial · premium · founder · portrait · monograph · plate · masthead · colophon

### Words BANNED (creator-economy · marketing-coded)

elevated · curated · sun-drenched · stunning · iconic · unforgettable · vibrant · authentic · genuine · transparent · journey · grateful · blessed · honored · excited · thrilled · obsessed · slay · vibes · aesthetic (as a noun) · content · creator · personal brand

### Phrases BANNED

"Real talk" · "Honest answer" · "Let me be transparent" · "What I learned from" · "5 things every [X] needs to know" · "Coming soon" · "Stay tuned" · "Tag someone who" · "What do you think?" · "Drop a comment" · "Hit me up" · "DM me" · "Let me know your thoughts"

### Em-dashes

Lifetime ban. Replace with:
- Middle dot · for separator
- Comma or period for clause break
- Parentheses for aside

## Voice testing protocol

Before any BASEPLATE copy ships:

1. **Read it out loud.** Does it sound like the brand or like every other photographer?
2. **Check the 4 anchors.** Named · Sealed · Quiet · Lineage. Does the copy honor all four?
3. **Strip 25%.** Whatever's left after deleting 25% of the words · is it weaker, or is it cleaner? If cleaner · keep the strip.
4. **Banned-word scan.** Search for any banned word. Replace.
5. **Em-dash scan.** Always. Lifetime rule.
6. **The Magnum / Aperture test.** Could this copy appear on a Magnum/Aperture publication? If no · rewrite toward yes.

---

## Voice exceptions (when it's okay to break the rules)

- **Personal DMs from BJ to friends.** Use `feedback_dm_voice.md` voice (casual, lowercase, "bet," "lmk"). NOT brand voice. The personal lane stays personal.
- **Internal docs / OS files.** Internal voice can be more explanatory · the OS is for BJ + collaborators, not the audience.
- **Direct subject communication.** When messaging a subject 1:1 (model, founder, client), DM voice rules apply · not brand voice. The brand is what gets PUBLISHED · the relationships happen casually.

---

## Voice evolution policy

The voice is locked through 2027-12-31. Revisit annually as part of the December Locks Audit. Adjustments allowed:

- Expanding the "used freely" word list as the archive deepens (chapter-specific terms emerge)
- Tightening the "used carefully" list if any word starts feeling worn
- Adding new banned words as creator-economy register evolves

What does NOT change: the 4 anchors. Named · Sealed · Quiet · Lineage. Those carry the decade.
