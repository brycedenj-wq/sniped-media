## Why this is simpler than v1 architecture

Original architecture proposed 4 sub-imprints (EDITIONS · STUDIO · PRESS · LAB) under a BASEPLATE master. That was a Condé Nast / Penguin Random House pattern · a parent holding multiple distinct brands.

The reality: BJ already committed to BASEPLATE STUDIO as the unified brand a month ago (brand bible 2026-04-11). The handles `@baseplate.studio` are claimed across IG · TikTok · X. The domain is `baseplate.studio`. There is no separate `@baseplate.editions` or `@baseplate.press` handle established.

Reverse-engineering from what exists: BJ already chose the simpler architecture. The 4-imprint version was theoretical. The single-brand version is what's already built.

**Aperture / Magnum analog adjusted:**
- Aperture Foundation has Aperture magazine + Aperture Editions + Aperture Workshops · 3 distinct imprints
- BASEPLATE STUDIO has photography + services + publishing + experimental · all under one wordmark
- Closer analog: Vercel (one company, multiple product lines under one brand · Vercel hosting · Vercel Edge · Vercel CLI · all "Vercel")
- Or: The Criterion Collection (one brand, all editions are "Criterion" · no sub-imprints)

Single brand is the right move for pre-audience phase · builds equity in ONE name · doesn't fragment focus.

### Photography work / chapter archive

The editorial photography output. The chapter system. The Cards. The cultural-memory engine.

Public-facing on `@baseplate.studio` Instagram + `baseplate.studio` domain. Card masthead reads `BASEPLATE STUDIO` directly · no sub-imprint mark.

Chapter numbering continues across the rebrand:
- CH01 Yae · published under SNIPED 2026-05-13 (the prototype)
- CH02 Mimi · published under BASEPLATE STUDIO 2026-05-18+ (first canonical chapter)
- CH03+ · all BASEPLATE STUDIO

The historical CH01 chapter remains attributed to SNIPED in any monograph or archival reference · footnoted as "predecessor imprint."

### Services / paid client work

The Reset, brand commercial work, bespoke editorial. All branded under BASEPLATE STUDIO · client decks read `BASEPLATE STUDIO · THE RESET` etc.

The Reset stays as the offering name (the product) · BASEPLATE STUDIO is the brand. Like "Stripe Checkout" inside Stripe · the offering has its own name but the brand carries above it.

### Publishing

Direction Stack book + future titles. Book covers / front matter read `BASEPLATE STUDIO · DIRECTION STACK` · imprint mark optional.

When the chapter archive eventually publishes a printed monograph (Year 3-5 target), the book is `BASEPLATE STUDIO · CHAPTERS 01-24 · VOL I` or similar.

### Experimental / AI work

Higgsfield experiments · AI tooling · faceless content tests · any methodology productization.

Kept QUIETER than the photography work. Doesn't get its own public-facing branding. Operates inside BASEPLATE STUDIO without being marketed as a separate line. The firewall between editorial work and AI experimentation is internal · not displayed externally.

If AI work ever produces a commercial product, it can graduate to its own name/brand at that point. Pre-commercial, it lives quietly inside BASEPLATE STUDIO.

## What's NOT in the architecture

- No personal brand account for Bryce. The work publishes under BASEPLATE STUDIO · BJ appears in colophons + credits + as the named founder · not as a separate personal-brand handle.

## When the architecture might change

The architecture is locked through 2028-12-31 minimum. Revisit triggers:

1. **A specific product line generates revenue that exceeds the photography work** (e.g., Direction Stack book sells > 5K copies, or AI tooling productized). At that point, the relevant line might earn its own sub-brand.
2. **External partnership or acquisition offer.** Requires defensible brand identity in deal language.
3. **The chapter archive crosses 50 chapters.** Worth considering whether sub-lineages (Black church chapters · founder chapters · etc.) deserve their own visual treatment within the BASEPLATE STUDIO master.

Until then · single brand · single handle · single visual system. Repetition with taste compounds best when there's only one thing being repeated.
