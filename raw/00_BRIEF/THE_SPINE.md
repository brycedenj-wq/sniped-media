## Section 5 · THE 10 PROTOCOLS (The Direction Stack)

The diagnostic methodology. Every founder LinkedIn photo can be read against these. The protocol is the gap. SNIPED's job: direct, name, fix.

1. **Claw Hands** · hands clutching, fingers tense
2. **Locked Shoulders** · raised, rigid, defensive posture
3. **Squared** · facing camera flat, no angle, no asymmetry
4. **Pinned Arms** · arms stuck to torso, no breath room
5. **Soft Jawline** · slack mouth, no jaw engagement
6. **Spinal Collapse** · slumped, shortened, no height
7. **Forced Smile** · performative grin, emotional disconnection
8. **Transition Freeze** · caught mid-motion, neither pose nor candid
9. **Good Mechanics, Zero Presence** · technically fine, no operator gravity
10. **Full Psychological Shutdown** · retreat from lens, dead-eyed, dated

Common diagnostic patterns:
- Tech founder mid-launch → typically 07 or 09
- Defense / aerospace / operator → 09 or 10
- "Young founder" tier (Forbes 30 Under 30 type) → 07 + casual register
- "Dated visuals" pattern → 10 (retreat from lens) almost always

## Section 10 · THE SNIPED OPERATING SYSTEM (craft layer)

(Full doc: `/05_PRODUCTION/sniped_operating_system_v1_legacy.md`)

**Base preset note:** v1 used Adobe Portrait + saturated grading. RETIRED. Current preset is v3 LUXURY (Adobe Neutral, quiet luxury editorial). Masks and textures below still apply.

**Mask kit (6 saved masks, still active doctrine):**
- AI Subject Mask (Sharpening +20, NR -10)
- AI Background Mask + grain (35/25/50)
- Fake Rim (Subject intersected with Linear Gradient from unlit side · Exposure +0.30 Highlights +15 Whites +10)
- Skin Glow via Point Color (sample skin midtone · Sat -5 to -10 · Lum +10 to +15 · Range 30)
- BG Smooth (Texture -50, Clarity -30, Sharpness -50)
- BG Color Shift (rescue only)

**10 named texture overlays:** Sniped_01_Dust_Light through Sniped_10_Vertical_Damaged. Used in Evoto Backdrop Changer at 15-25% opacity.

**Critical craft discoveries (still active):**
- Hard light without fill or rim is the trap. Build rim or kicker BEFORE first frame.
- Floating full-body fix: keep 5-10% floor visible, paint contact shadow, or crop to mid-thigh. Never crop on the joint.
- Atmospheric depth is the consistent gap. Add an atmospheric plane between subject and backdrop.
- BG Smooth mask doesn't fix real wrinkles. Generative Remove first, PiXimperfect Photoshop fallback.
- Grid sequence: color stories diverge while system holds. Don't post two dark moody sets back-to-back.
- Client edit vs grid edit are different. Client: clean baseline. Grid: full stylization.
- No global grain. Grain only on background mask. Evoto buries Lightroom global grain anyway.
