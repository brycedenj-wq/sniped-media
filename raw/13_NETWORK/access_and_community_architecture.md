# Reputation Engine · Access · Community · Cultural Documentation · Network Architecture

**This is the Reputation Engine architecture (canonized 2026-05-06 · CANONICAL_TRUTHS.md Truth 1). It is one of three co-equal engines (Revenue · Audience · Reputation), not a supporting layer to anything.**

The **Revenue Engine** (LinkedIn → VIB → Reset → Op Kit → Brand System · paid shoots · Pixieset upsell) produces a known revenue path, fast. Spec lives in `/01_OFFERS/delivery_architecture_v2.md` and the Operating Brief offer ladder.

The **Audience Engine** (Instagram + LinkedIn POV + BTS + studio culture · what someone sees scrolling) produces cultural gravity and discovery. Spec lives in `/07_CONTENT/audience_engine.md`.

The **Reputation Engine · this doc** (Events / Promoter / Community / Cultural Documentation · what someone says about SNIPED when SNIPED isn't in the room) produces network density, authority transfer, evergreen archive, and access to the kinds of relationships that LinkedIn outreach cannot manufacture. It is what makes SNIPED unleavable in five years, regardless of which platform owns attention by then. Phase 1 lean override runs Reputation lean (ad-hoc institutional engagements only); Phase B+ expands cadence.

Note: prior framing in this doc and earlier specs called this the "compounding engine" or "Engine B." The canon now distinguishes Reputation (this doc) from Audience (the cultural-gravity output that lives on IG and LinkedIn). Both compound; they compound differently.

## The thesis

LA in this decade is the most concentrated environment for emerging Black founder, artist, athlete, and operator culture in the country. Most of that culture is being documented by:

- Photographers who chase virality and produce volume without taste
- Photographers who shoot it as one-off jobs and never assemble a body of work
- Photographers who don't have access at all and only see the surface

A photographer who runs methodology, holds taste, shows up consistently across years, and treats event/community/cultural moments as a serious archive (not a side hustle) becomes the **named documentarian** of that ecosystem. The compounding return on that position is decades, not quarters.

That photographer is not "a photographer for hire at events." That photographer is **the visual infrastructure of a moment in time.**

This is what SNIPED Media is building toward in this lane. Not as a side bet. As the **Reputation Engine**, one of the three primary engines (Revenue · Audience · Reputation) per CANONICAL_TRUTHS.md Truth 1.

## The four sub-lanes

### Lane 1 · Concerts / Festivals / Promoter Events
**Examples in network already:** 1500-day birthday concert (Jan 2026, shot), summer LA event season opportunities, future Live Nation / sponsor brand connections.

**Why this lane:** these environments are saturated with photographers but starved of photographers who run a methodology. The default event photographer produces 800 frames of the same crowd shot. SNIPED's standard produces 30-50 frames that look like editorial coverage of the moment. The work is night-and-day visible to the talent, the promoter, and the brand.

**Conversion mechanic:** Access Free coverage → introduction to sponsor brand marketing director or artist label / management → commercial license activation OR paid follow-up shoot. The introduction is what was paid for, not the photos.

**Time cost:** one event = 4-6 hours on site + 6-10 hours of edit. Real cost. Run sparingly: 1-2 per month during summer/event season, by exception off-season.

### Lane 2 · Cultural / Community Institutions
**Examples in network:** Emmanuel 33rd COGIC (multiple services already shot), broader COGIC / Black church network in LA via the same connection (Bishop Peters network as the relationship node). Tuskegee Homecoming (annual). Eagle Rock community.

**Why this lane:** these are the highest-trust environments in the LA Black community. A photographer trusted in a church is trusted by 200 families inside that church. Trust at scale, transferred through the institution. Cannot be bought; only earned through repeat presence and respectful documentation.

**Conversion mechanic:** Community Free coverage of the institution itself → paid family / individual portrait sessions at the event for attendees → ongoing relationships with members → referrals to founder / professional / executive Reset clients (the same congregation has the founders the LinkedIn lane is trying to cold-DM).

**Time cost:** 3-5 hours on site + 4-6 hours of edit. Lower cost than concerts because subjects are stationary and family-format work is more predictable.

### Lane 3 · Athletes / Coaches / Sports Culture
**Examples in network:** youth basketball refereeing exposure (winter), West Orange / Tuskegee football connection, USC ecosystem via Ramon, broader LA sports culture.

**Why this lane:** sports culture sits at the intersection of community, performance, and brand. Coaches and athletes have networks that don't overlap with LinkedIn founder networks. They also produce the kind of imagery (action, intensity, presence) that translates into editorial credibility.

**Conversion mechanic:** Community Free or Collab Free with coaches and emerging athletes → portfolio work that sells the SNIPED "operator coverage" frame to brands · sneaker, apparel, supplement · who want athlete content with editorial-grade direction.

**Time cost:** variable. Some shoots are 30 minutes (post-game portraits); some are full-day documentary work. Treat as opportunistic rather than scheduled.

### Lane 4 · Local Business / Founder Adjacent
**Examples in network:** Davis Law Group case study (shot 2025), Pure Motion Fitness (Mar 2025), Beat by Tai (Apr 2025), Almalove (Feb-Mar 2025), Nubian campaigns (Jan 2025), JD McCrary (Dec 2024).

**Why this lane:** the LA business ecosystem at the small/mid level (sub-$10M revenue, founder-led) is where the Reset and Op Kit pricing actually lives. These are the targets the LinkedIn cold-VIB engine is also trying to reach, but warm intros from Lanes 2 and 3 convert at multiples of cold conversion rates.

**Conversion mechanic:** existing client → testimonial → referral within their network. The Davis Law 90-day reactivation reminder lives in this lane.

**Time cost:** standard Reset/Op Kit pricing applies. This isn't "free work"; it's the paid-conversion endpoint of the other three lanes.

## Capacity discipline (matters more than ambition here)

These lanes are seductive. Each one feels like "I should be doing more of this." The trap: substituting access work for paid work because access feels like progress and paid feels like a grind.

**Operational rules:**

1. **Total free shoots across all lanes: maximum 4 per month.** This is a ceiling, not a quota. If paid pipeline fills (3+ Resets in a month), free drops to 0-1.
2. **Each free engagement requires a written return mechanic before booking.** "I want to be there" is not a return mechanic. "Church network event → paid family portraits at the gathering" is.
3. **The Reset paid pipeline always wins capacity allocation.** If a Reset and an event coverage compete for the same Saturday, the Reset wins. Communicate to the event contact early; don't ghost.
4. **Edit-bottleneck rule applies equally.** Event coverage produces 200-400 frames; that's 4-8 hours of edit. If the post-production queue is already at SLA risk, decline the event.

## Content output from this lane

This is where the LinkedIn POV bank Series D + Cultural Documentation thesis (`/07_CONTENT/cultural_documentation_thesis.md`) come from. Every event coverage, every community shoot, every athlete portrait is an opportunity to:

1. Pull a hero frame for the SNIPED archive (Carrd Selected Work candidate)
2. Produce 1-2 IG carousel posts documenting the moment with context
3. Capture quotes / interactions for a Substack essay (when the lane gets its own essay rhythm in Year 2)
4. Develop the body of work for the eventual zine / gallery / book project

This is not "more content for content's sake." This is the act of building the archive that makes SNIPED the named documentarian of the ecosystem.
