# Bad Strategy Audit

**Skill ID:** 1.2  
**Category:** Strategy & Competitive Analysis  
**Source Document(s):** STRATEGIC_THINKING_FRAMEWORKS.docx

## Purpose

Diagnose whether any strategy document, business plan, or competitive initiative contains the four hallmarks of bad strategy (Fluff, Failure to Face the Challenge, Mistaking Goals for Strategy, Bad Strategic Objectives) and recommend corrections.

## When to Use

When developing, evaluating, or stress-testing business strategy. When a team needs to move from vague ambition to concrete, coordinated action.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Strip all buzzwords from the strategy document. Assess what substantive meaning remains. If nothing remains, flag as Fluff.
2. Ask: What is the specific challenge this strategy addresses? If the answer is vague or absent, flag as Failure to Face the Challenge.
3. Check whether performance targets or vision statements are presented as strategy without a plan. If so, flag as Mistaking Goals for Strategy.
4. Examine whether the objectives form a coherent set or a competing list (dog's dinner) or are unreachable (blue sky). Flag accordingly.
5. Test for coherence: Do proposed actions reinforce each other or conflict? Are resources concentrated on the critical issue?
6. Test for feasibility: Are objectives achievable with current resources, or do they require unknown breakthroughs?
7. Test for isolating mechanisms: What prevents competitors from copying this? If no answer exists, the strategy lacks durability.
8. Compile findings into a diagnostic report with specific fix recommendations for each hallmark detected.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- A Strategy Audit Report scoring each of the eight diagnostic questions
- Red flags identified with specific evidence from the document
- Recommended rewrites for each deficient element (diagnosis, policy, or action)
