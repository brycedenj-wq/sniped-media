# Market Evaluation Scorecard

**Skill ID:** 4.3  
**Category:** Customer Discovery & Market Validation  
**Source Document(s):** Personal_MBA_Business_Mastery_FRAMEWORKS.docx

## Purpose

Score a potential market opportunity across ten factors before investing time or capital, using the Personal MBA's pre-investment evaluation model.

## When to Use

When validating business ideas, new features, or market opportunities. When you need real data from customers rather than opinions.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Rate Urgency (0-10): How badly do people want or need this right now?
2. Rate Market Size (0-10): How many people are actively purchasing this type of offering?
3. Rate Pricing Potential (0-10): What is the highest price a typical purchaser would pay?
4. Rate Cost of Customer Acquisition (0-10): How easy and cheap is it to acquire a new customer?
5. Rate Cost of Value Delivery (0-10): How much would it cost to create and deliver the value?
6. Rate Uniqueness of Offer (0-10): How unique versus competitors? How easy to copy?
7. Rate Speed to Market (0-10): How quickly can you create something to sell?
8. Rate Up-Front Investment (0-10): How much investment required before first sale?
9. Rate Upsell Potential (0-10): Are there related secondary offerings for purchasing customers?
10. Rate Evergreen Potential (0-10): Can initial investment continue generating sales without new creation?
11. Total the score. Below 50 = walk away. 50-75 = proceed with caution. Above 75 = strong opportunity.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- A scored 10-factor market evaluation (0-100 total)
- Go/No-Go recommendation with rationale
- Identified weak factors requiring mitigation if proceeding
