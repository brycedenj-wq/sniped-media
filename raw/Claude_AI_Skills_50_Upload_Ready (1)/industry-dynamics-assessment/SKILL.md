# Industry Dynamics Assessment (Five Guideposts)

**Skill ID:** 1.6  
**Category:** Strategy & Competitive Analysis  
**Source Document(s):** STRATEGIC_THINKING_FRAMEWORKS.docx

## Purpose

Read industry transitions and position to ride waves of change using five diagnostic guideposts.

## When to Use

When the specific situation described in the Purpose section applies to your current business challenge.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Assess Escalating Fixed Costs: Are rising fixed costs forcing industry consolidation? How is this changing business models?
2. Assess Deregulation: Is government removing controls? What new competitive dynamics does this create for incumbents?
3. Apply Predictable Bias Corrections: Discount short-term hype. Extend expected timelines for change. People overestimate speed short-term and underestimate long-term.
4. Map Incumbent Response Patterns: Where will incumbents resist transitions that threaten existing capabilities? Their inertia predicts where they'll be slow.
5. Describe the Attractor State: How 'should' the industry work given technological forces and demand structure? This gravity-like pull toward efficiency helps predict the future.
6. Synthesize guideposts into a positioning recommendation.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- A five-guidepost industry dynamics assessment
- Predicted industry trajectory with confidence levels
- Positioning recommendations for the business relative to industry transition
