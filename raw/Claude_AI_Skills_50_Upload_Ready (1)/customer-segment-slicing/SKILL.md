# Customer Segment Slicing

**Skill ID:** 4.2  
**Category:** Customer Discovery & Market Validation  
**Source Document(s):** CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY_FRAMEWORKS.docx

## Purpose

Drill from a broad market down to a specific, actionable who-where pair you can talk to today, ensuring feedback consistency and segment viability.

## When to Use

When validating business ideas, new features, or market opportunities. When you need real data from customers rather than opinions.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Start with your broad segment (e.g., 'students,' 'small businesses').
2. Ask: Within this group, who would want it most? Find the burning-hair subset.
3. Ask: Why do they want it? What is their specific problem or goal?
4. Ask: What are they already doing to solve this? Existing behavior reveals commitment level.
5. Ask: Where can I find them? If you can't answer, slice finer.
6. Choose based on three criteria: Who is most (a) profitable, (b) easy to reach, (c) personally rewarding to serve?
7. Apply the Consistency Test: If running 20 conversations produces 20 different must-have features, slice until feedback becomes consistent.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- A specific who-where pair (exact description of target customer + where to find them)
- The customer's specific problem, existing behavior, and budget indicators
- A consistency score (are you hearing the same problems repeatedly?)
