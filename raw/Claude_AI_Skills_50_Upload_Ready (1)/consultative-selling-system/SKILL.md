# Consultative Selling System

**Skill ID:** 12.2  
**Category:** Professional Development  
**Source Document(s):** Extracted_Professional_Intelligence__1_.docx, NEGOTIATION_FRAMEWORKS.docx

## Purpose

Close deals by positioning as advisor rather than salesperson, using diagnostic questioning, trust-building through expertise, and the consultative selling model.

## When to Use

When the specific situation described in the Purpose section applies to your current business challenge.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Open by asking questions to diagnose, not by pitching. Position yourself as the Doctor of Selling.
2. Identify the prospect's specific problem using the Four Ps: Person (who), Pain (what hurts), Promise (what you guarantee), Price (what they pay).
3. Build trust through demonstrated expertise and genuine interest before guiding toward a decision.
4. Address the Five Barriers to Purchase proactively: 'It costs too much' (show ROI), 'It won't work' (social proof), 'It won't work for me' (similar customer proof), 'I can wait' (cost of inaction), 'It's too difficult' (walk through the process).
5. Propose using the Ascension Model: Low-ticket entry product builds trust > Mid-ticket course/program deepens engagement > High-ticket consulting/coaching captures maximum value.
6. Make a damaging admission: Proactively acknowledge a genuine drawback to increase credibility.
7. Apply risk reversal: Transfer risk from buyer to seller with guarantees, free trials, or money-back policies.
8. Close by asking for the commitment, not by pressuring.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- A diagnostic question sequence for discovery calls
- Four Ps positioning document (Person, Pain, Promise, Price)
- Objection-handling scripts for the Five Barriers
- Ascension model pricing architecture (low > mid > high ticket)
