# Social Media Content Strategy System

**Skill ID:** 10.1  
**Category:** Content & Social Media  
**Source Document(s):** Social_Media_Content_Strategy_Frameworks.docx

## Purpose

Build a sustainable content engine using the Content Multiplication System, Validated Content Strategy, and Four Business Drivers Model to ensure every post serves a business objective.

## When to Use

When developing, evaluating, or stress-testing business strategy. When a team needs to move from vague ambition to concrete, coordinated action.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Define your content strategy approach: Niche (one topic, fastest growth), Audience (one ideal person, multiple topics), or Identity (lifestyle, slower growth).
2. Optimize profile using PBC Framework: Positioning (what they get), Branding (visual consistency), Clear CTA (one action).
3. Build a Content Pool: Save 20+ posts with 1M+ views in your niche as a reference library.
4. Apply Validated Content Strategy: From the pool, recreate 3 formats x 3 attempts each (9 posts). Analyze top performers for shared patterns.
5. Map every post to one of four business drivers: Revenue, Email List Growth, Audience Nurture, or Creative Expression.
6. Use Content Multiplication: Match each idea against multiple formats (voiceover, tutorial, B-roll, face-to-camera, carousel) to produce 3-4x content from one concept.
7. Engineer hooks: First 3 lines/seconds must stop the scroll. Use curiosity, data, or pattern interrupts.
8. Maintain a Persistent Idea Bank: Running note on phone for shower thoughts, scroll inspiration, conversation sparks.
9. Review analytics monthly: Sort by follows gained (not just views). Double down on what converts.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- Content strategy selection with rationale
- Optimized profile (positioning, branding, CTA)
- Content Pool reference library (20+ validated posts)
- Monthly content calendar with posts mapped to business drivers
- Analytics review template
