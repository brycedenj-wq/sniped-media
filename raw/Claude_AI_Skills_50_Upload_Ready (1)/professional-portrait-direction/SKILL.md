# Professional Portrait Direction Protocol

**Skill ID:** 11.2  
**Category:** Creative Production  
**Source Document(s):** Posing_101_Professional_Extraction.docx, Creative_Production_Systems_Frameworks.docx

## Purpose

Direct portrait sessions using the Composition-First Framework, Bottom-Up Body Mechanics System, and Client Direction Protocol for consistent, professional results.

## When to Use

When a persistent problem resists repeated fix attempts, suggesting the issue is structural rather than personnel-based.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Set composition first: Place subject on left or right third line. Face at intersection of two third lines. Key background elements on opposing third.
2. Manage background: Eliminate distracting elements behind the subject's head.
3. Direct body bottom-up: Hips first (angle 45 degrees), then spine alignment, then arms (create negative space), then chin and expression.
4. Apply the Turtle technique: Instruct subject to push forehead toward camera and extend neck forward and slightly down.
5. Use the Client Direction Protocol: Pre-shoot orientation > Mirror the pose yourself > Guide through micro-motions (small isolated movements) > Capture during natural transitions.
6. Build a pre-production moodboard: Convey specific mood, aesthetic, and design intent with minimal text. Share with client for alignment.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- A pre-production moodboard for the session
- Composition plan (subject placement, background management)
- Posing sequence checklist (bottom-up)
- Client direction script (orientation, mirroring cues, micro-motion prompts)
