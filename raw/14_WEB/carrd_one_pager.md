# snipedmedia.com · One-page Carrd

**Build target: 90 minutes once images are picked. Pro plan ($19/year). Custom domain: snipedmedia.com.**

The Carrd is the destination of every "where can I see your work?" question that arrives from LinkedIn, IG, or in person. It is NOT a portfolio site. It is a conversion surface that exists to convince an LA founder to book a Discovery call within 60 seconds of arriving.

---

## Site architecture · 7 sections, in order

```
01 · Hero
02 · The Problem
03 · The Method (Direction Stack diagnostic)
04 · The Reset (offer)
05 · Selected Work (10-frame grid)
06 · Testimonial(s)
07 · Footer · Book the Discovery
```

No menu. No "About" page. No blog. No services list. No 12-tab navigation. The whole site is one scroll.

## What this site is NOT

- Not a portfolio. Selected Work is 10 frames; the rest of the archive is private.
- Not a blog. SNIPED's writing surface is LinkedIn (POV posts) and Substack (Art Series essays, when those start).
- Not an "About BJ" page. Founder narrative lives in the LinkedIn promotion-announcement post (Phase 1 30-day stack) and in the Direction Shift book (held private).
- Not a multi-tier pricing page. Op Kit and Brand System are sold off the back of delivered Resets, never cold off a website.
- Not a forms-heavy site. The only form is Calendly. No "request a quote" form, no contact form, no newsletter form.

The Carrd's job is to make a Calendly booking happen within 60 seconds of arrival. Everything else is decoration.
