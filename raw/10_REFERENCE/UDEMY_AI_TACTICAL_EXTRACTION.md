**Pattern:** Prompt ChatGPT to ask YOU 8-12 follow-up questions about your buyer before generating the asset. The questions surface buyer-persona depth that direct prompts miss. Then feed answers back, generate the lead magnet.
**Maps to:**
- `/03_OUTREACH/SOP_VIB_production.md` · VIB target research, before producing the asset
- Direction Stack book launch lead magnet (Q3 2026 funnel)
- Op Kit discovery call prep (founder-specific buyer persona refinement)
**Improves:** quality of buyer understanding before asset is built. The reverse-interview forces VIB depth that ad-hoc prompting skips.
**Action:** add a `lead_magnet_reverse_interview.md` prompt template to `/07_CONTENT/prompt_library/` with the 8-12 question scaffold. Apply once when building the book lead magnet, once per Op Kit pitch prep cycle.

- **Logo generation workflows.** Commodity. Undermines the methodology layer. Off-positioning.
- **AI auto-headshot generation.** Direct collapse of anti-AI moat. The Sax / Berger memos predict the premium tier evaporates the moment the maker uses the cheap tool on client work.
- **AI image generation for client deliverables.** Same Berger violation. The made-image needs a maker. Anti-AI client guardrail holds absolutely.
- **Beginner ChatGPT prompt engineering basics** (Chain of Thought, role prompting, etc). BJ already operates above this baseline.
- **Generic Instagram post formulas / hook frameworks.** Collapses brand register. SNIPED has its own hook library tuned to operator-coded LA founder voice.
- **Generic ad copy formulas.** Same. Off-voice.
- **Generic SEO article farming.** Volume-based content strategy. Off-positioning vs. Perennial Seller principle (80% creating, depth not breadth).
- **Bulk content generation to "scale output."** Direct violation of Hit Makers + Blockbusters convergence. SNIPED concentrates bets, does not diffuse output.
- **Fiverr / Etsy / KDP / dropshipping monetization.** Off-positioning entirely. Wrong ICP, wrong register, wrong margin profile.
- **AI logo / brand identity packages.** Same.
- **Generic email blast templates** (mass cold email, lead-gen spam). VIB method is the opposite. High-context, hand-crafted, named-cluster targeting beats the spray approach by structure.
- **YouTube AI faceless channel monetization.** Off-positioning. Founder presence is the moat.
- **Course-creator hype patterns.** The instructor's own register is hype-coded. Do not absorb the voice. SNIPED's voice is operator, not hype.
- **Affiliate marketing schemes.** Off-positioning.
- **AI website builders for "fast launch."** Carrd already covers this. The course's recommendations would replace working infrastructure with shinier-but-equivalent tools.
- **Midjourney for "product ads"** (line ~16657-16775). Tempting but collapses anti-AI moat if used on client work. Internal moodboard use only, and Leonardo already covers that.
