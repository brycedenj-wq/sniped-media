# Photography Vault Index · Lighting + Posing + Vision

26-file technical reference batch. PDFs are pure tactical reference cards (diagrams, setups, posing). The masterclass transcript is a 5,548-line vision-training corpus on how to see light. None of this is strategic doctrine. All of it is on-shoot / pre-shoot / in-edit reference.

Source files live at `/Users/sniper/Downloads/Lecture+*.pdf` and `/Users/sniper/Downloads/PHOTOGRAPHY MASTERCLASS.docx`. The PDFs should be kept available offline (iPad on set, Notion mirror, or `/10_REFERENCE/lighting_pdfs/` folder).

---

## When to consult this index

- **Pre-shoot prep (Direction Stack Protocol 2-3):** picking the lighting setup for a specific founder type, mood register, or aesthetic call. Pull 1-2 PDFs into the treatment doc.
- **On-set diagnostic:** when a setup isn't working, reference the closest matching diagram. The PDFs are diagram-first, so 30-second consult.
- **Retouch tier guide:** match the captured lighting pattern to the named pattern in the index, then route to the correct retouch register.
- **Reading on the train / between shoots:** the masterclass for slow-burn vision compounding. Not session-fast.
- **Onboarding any future retoucher or assistant:** these are the canonical reference cards for SNIPED's lighting vocabulary.

## Tier 4 · The Vision Corpus

`PHOTOGRAPHY MASTERCLASS.docx` (5,548 lines, 37,635 words).

Course content: a transcript-style masterclass on observing light. Coverage:
- Hard light vs soft light · how to identify, when to use, what each does to the subject
- Direction of light (front, side, back, top) · what each direction does to face geometry
- Color temperature and white balance discipline
- Composition · negative space, leading lines, rule of thirds, symmetry, frame within frame
- Lightroom develop module basics
- Studio replication of natural light patterns

**Treatment:** this is slow-burn vision training. Not a session-fast reference. Read in 30-min blocks between shoots. Compounds over months. Do NOT try to extract content from it for SNIPED IP · the principles are foundational, not differentiated.

**Where it touches SNIPED:** the load-bearing principle of "see the light before you raise the camera" maps directly to Direction Stack Protocol 1 (the 90-second on-set opener · light read before pose direction).

**Useful to:**
- BJ as ongoing vision sharpening
- Future retoucher or assistant onboarding (foundational vocabulary)
- VIB caption work · the language of light is the language of premium photography

**Not useful to:**
- Strategic positioning (already covered in 15-book corpus)
- Workflow design (already covered in PRODUCTION_OS)
- Client-facing copy (too generic)

## What this vault is NOT

- Not strategic. Lighting and posing are tactics. They do not change the SNIPED moat.
- Not differentiated IP. Every photographer has access to the same lighting names. SNIPED's edge is the methodology that selects + sequences these · not the patterns themselves.
- Not a substitute for the Direction Stack. The Direction Stack tells you WHICH pattern fits a specific founder. The PDFs only show you what the patterns are.
- Not content. Do not turn lighting setups into LinkedIn posts (off-positioning, generic creator-tier register).
