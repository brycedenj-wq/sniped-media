# Chapter Rollout Doctrine v1

Locked 2026-05-12. The universal chapter rollout system for every SNIPED model, subject, collaborator, founder, lineage figure, and self-shoot. Same structure for all subjects. No tiers.

This doctrine supersedes any prior chapter rollout reference. Run this against every chapter going forward.

---

## THE ONE-PARAGRAPH RULE

Every chapter runs **3 to 5 grid posts** depending on look count. **Composite HERO is mandatory.** **Chapter Card is mandatory.** **Card is always an IG Collab post with the subject.** Captions are minimal and ritualized (`[FirstName] · [##]`). Breathing gap is **1 to 3 days** between chapters. No fake daily posting. The rhythm is: escalation → climax → closure → silence → re-entry.

---

## THE UNIVERSAL CHAPTER FORMULA

```
[1 frame per look · capped at 3]
            ↓
[Composite HERO · mandatory]
            ↓
[Chapter Card · mandatory · always a collab post with subject]
            ↓
[Breathing gap · 1 to 3 days]
            ↓
[Next chapter]
```

No subject tiers. A model, a casting-call collaborator, a founder, a lineage figure, a self-shoot: same structure. The chapter doesn't ask "who is this person." It asks "how many looks did the shoot produce."

**Why this is doctrine-aligned:**
- **Lock 12** (founder + creative unified hand) · the brand is one visual hand across all output
- **Operator-Coded Definition** · the register is produced by the methodology, not by the subject's status
- **Lineage Doctrine** · the discipline is the constant across subjects

## ROLLOUT BY LOOK COUNT

| Looks shot | Build frames | HERO | Card (collab) | Total grid posts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 look | 1 | mandatory | mandatory | **3** |
| 2 looks | 2 | mandatory | mandatory | **4** |
| 3 looks | 3 | mandatory | mandatory | **5** |
| 4+ looks | 3 (cap) | mandatory | mandatory | **5** (cap) |

**The rule:** 1 build frame per look, capped at 3. HERO always. Card always. Cap chapter at 5 grid posts.

**Why cap build frames at 3:** Hit Makers exposure mechanic · the audience needs repetition to recognize the subject, but more than 3 build frames before the HERO collapses the climax. Three is the sweet spot.

## ASSET HIERARCHY (locked)

### Tier 1 · The Composite HERO (the climax · mandatory)
- The main event of the chapter · strongest single image
- Composite environment (one of the seven · Brutalist Monument · Industrial Minimal · Monochromatic Void · Sculptural Gallery · Cinematic Urban · Organic Surreal · Futurist Editorial)
- Subject inside the SNIPED world-building register
- Maximum visual escalation (cinematic, atmospheric, scale)
- Built through plate composite (Higgsfield + Seedream + Photoshop) or in-camera
- One HERO per chapter, maximum. No exceptions. No skipping.

### Tier 2 · Build identity frames (the warm-up)
- Single-frame portraits from each look, NOT composited
- Quieter, character-led, restrained
- Build the subject's identity in the SNIPED visual world before the HERO lands

### Tier 3 · The Chapter Card (the closure · mandatory · collab post)
- Editorial artifact, archival receipt
- Built from a chapter frame (NOT the HERO composite itself)
- Lower visual energy than the HERO by design
- Cleaner, more restrained, more typographic
- Function: seal the chapter, mark it as part of the SNIPED canon, honor the subject through collab credit
- The 4th-slot frame is ideally the most operator-coded business-fit frame of the chapter · the chapter's psychological closer

### Tier 4 · Story-only ephemera (the heartbeat)
- Behind-the-scenes process glimpses
- Subject reposts of the chapter
- Casting moments, wardrobe details, setup process
- Lives 24 hours · never grid · builds presence without spending grid weight

### Tier 5 · The breathing gap (the silence)
- 1 to 3 days after the chapter closes
- No grid posts during the gap
- The silence is the asset

## CAPTION CONVENTION (locked · three distinct shapes)

Each post type gets a visually different caption so the audience reads the chapter structure instantly:

| Post type | Caption shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Look frame (build) | `[FirstName] · [##]` | `Yae · 01`, `Yae · 02` |
| HERO (climax) | `[FirstName] · [Environment]` | `Yae · Brutalist Monument` |
| HERO (when not introducing a new environment) | `[FirstName] · [##]` | `Yae · 03` |
| Chapter Card (closure) | `Chapter ##` | `Chapter 01` |

**The three shapes serve three jobs:**
- Numbered = build (subject + sequence)
- Subject + Environment = climax (subject + world)
- Standalone Chapter # = closure (the chapter sealed)

No overlap. No conflict. The numbered sequence stops at the build. The HERO breaks the pattern with the environment. The Card breaks even further with just the chapter number.

**Why the Card drops the subject name from caption:**
- The Card is a collab post · subject's handle appears on the post via the IG Collab credit
- The Card image itself displays `CHAPTER ## · [FIRSTNAME] · [LOCATION] · [MONTH YEAR]` inside the masthead (the artifact carries the subject's name visually)
- Caption staying minimal `Chapter 01` lets the chapter number stand as its own beat
- Across the year, the captions `Chapter 01`, `Chapter 02`, `Chapter 03` will read as a recurring ritual

## UNIVERSAL CAPTION RULES (every post)

- First name only (full name only in subject credit field if relevant)
- Number = sequential post in the chapter
- No editorial note in caption
- No adjectives
- No questions
- No emoji
- No hashtags in the caption itself (3 max in first comment if used, SNIPED-specific only)
- No CTA
- No "link in bio"
- Subject IG handle goes in the **first comment**, not the caption
- Other credits (MUA, location, composite credit) go in the first comment as well

**Why credits go in first comment, not caption:**
- Keeps the grid caption visually clean across all chapters
- Lets the caption be the ritualized 2-word object
- Audience trains on the convention over 5-10 chapters and begins to recognize `[FirstName] · [##]` as the SNIPED chapter system itself

## THE CHAPTER CARD COLLAB POST (mandatory)

Every Chapter Card is published as an IG Collab post with the subject. This is non-negotiable.

**Operational steps:**
1. Compose the Card post on SNIPED IG
2. Tap "Tag People" → "Invite Collaborator"
3. Search the subject's handle, send invite
4. Subject accepts inside IG · post appears on both grids
5. Comments, likes, saves are shared across both audiences

**Pre-send DM to subject (24 hours before, or right before posting):**

> [Subject first name] · Chapter ## closer goes up [day/time]. Sending you a collab invite right before it posts so it lands on both our grids. Just accept when you see it pop up.

**The HERO is optionally a collab too.** Default is SNIPED-solo on the HERO and collab on the Card. Promote both to collab when the subject's audience reach is worth amplifying.

## BREATHING GAP RULES (1-3 days default)

| Chapter type | Gap before next chapter starts |
|---|---|
| 3-post chapter (1 look) | **1 day** |
| 4-post chapter (2 looks) | **1-2 days** |
| 5-post chapter (3+ looks) | **2-3 days** |
| Heavy chapter (lineage subject · multi-session · exceptional frames) | **5-7 days** |
| Two HEROs landed inside 10 days | **Force 5-day gap** before any more chapter content |

**The gap rule:** Count from the LAST post of the closing chapter to the FIRST post of the next chapter. Stories during the gap are fine (1 per day max, ambient only). Grid stays silent.

The breathing gap is non-negotiable. Posting through the gap collapses the rhythm.

## THE FRAME-EARNS-COVER DECISION FRAMEWORK

When picking the Chapter Card image from chapter selects:

1. **Technical floor:** sharp, properly exposed, free of artifacts
2. **Register check:** body language demonstrates composed authority (Direction Stack visible)
3. **Eye check:** eyes carry steadiness or intentional composed-away
4. **Wardrobe + environment check:** subject + environment + wardrobe read as one unit
5. **Negative space check:** frame has room for the masthead and bottom imprint to breathe
6. **The 10-year subject test:** would the subject feel honored by this frame in 10 years?
7. **The operator-coded preference:** if multiple frames pass, pick the one where the subject is most operator-coded (composed business-fit register, less fashion-aesthetic register)

The Card image is NEVER the HERO composite itself. The Card uses a different chapter frame · ideally the most grounded operator-coded frame of the shoot.

---

## STORIES STRATEGY (parallel to chapter rhythm)

1. Stories are NEVER chapter-critical · the chapter still works if Stories disappear
2. Maximum 2 Story posts per day during a chapter · maximum 1 Story per day during the breathing gap
3. Story types in priority order:
   - Process/methodology glimpses (setup, light test, wardrobe detail)
   - Subject self-reposts of the chapter
   - Environment establishing moments (a wide of the day's location, no subject)
   - Behind-the-scenes ambient (rare)
4. Stories NEVER overlap with the HERO launch · on HERO day, Stories are for pre-tease and post-launch repost only
5. Chapter closer Story is a single quiet image of the Chapter Card with caption: `Chapter ## · [Subject first name]`

## THE B&W CARD RULE · DUAL REGISTER (LOCKED 2026-05-13)

**Chapter Cards render the image in BLACK & WHITE.** The HERO post remains full color (v3 LUXURY).

This is the dual-register rule:
- **HERO = the moment.** Color. Cinematic. World-building. v3 LUXURY locked palette. The chapter's climax in full color register.
- **Card = the document.** B&W. Archival. Canonization. The chapter sealed into the SNIPED archive.

### Why this works doctrinally
The B&W treatment is APPARATUS LAYER, not edit style change. Lock 7 ("v3 LUXURY is the only editing style") governs the source image edit. The Card displays a desaturated version for the artifact treatment · the source image remains v3 LUXURY color. Same convention as Aperture, LIFE, Magnum publishing color work in B&W on archival monographs.

### The implementation
- **Saturation:** -1 (full desaturation)
- **Contrast:** +0.08 (slight bump for editorial punch)
- **Exposure:** +0.02 (tiny lift)
- Applied as a Figma image fill filter on the IMAGE_DROP_ZONE of the Card template
- Alternative: pre-desaturate the source image in Lightroom before uploading to the Card template (same result)

### The masthead apparatus stays color
Only the image goes B&W. The masthead remains:
- Paper warm background (`#F5EFE6`)
- Black SNIPED wordmark (Playfair Display Black)
- Brass-darker colophon text (`#8B6E50` for legibility)
- Brass hairline rules + BJ signature glyph
- The apparatus carries the warm-cool tension while the photograph carries the archival register

### What this signals to the audience
After 10-50 chapters, the audience trains on the dual register:
- Color post lands on the grid → that's a HERO · the chapter is happening
- B&W post lands on the grid → that's a Card · the chapter is sealed
- The visual hierarchy becomes part of the SNIPED reading literacy

### What this does NOT mean
- HERO posts NEVER go B&W · they're always color
- Build identity frames (slot 01-03) stay color · they're chapter-progression posts, not Cards
- Source images are NOT edited B&W in Lightroom · they stay v3 LUXURY · the desaturation lives on the Card display layer
- Stories with Card content can use either color or B&W (Card B&W matches the grid post; if the Story uses a different chapter frame it can be color)

### Tradition lineage
- **Aperture** · monograph covers in B&W even when interiors are color
- **LIFE** · archival editions and "best of" compiles in B&W
- **Magnum** · contact sheets and monograph editions in B&W
- **The Crisis (Du Bois era)** · intellectual seriousness through tonal restraint
- **The New Yorker** · selective monochrome covers for editorial weight
- SNIPED extends this lineage to IG-native chapter publishing

### Production workflow (per Card)

1. BJ sends the edited JPG/PNG of the Card-worthy frame (NOT the HERO composite)
2. Resize image to under 10MB if needed (sips command: `sips -Z 2700 -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 88 [input] --out [output]`)
3. Duplicate the appropriate variant in Figma
4. Update text fields: `NO · ##`, chapter line, sequence number, optional cover line
5. Apply image fill to `IMAGE_DROP_ZONE` rectangle (scaleMode: FILL)
6. Export at 4:5, 1:1, and 9:16
7. Post to IG with collab invite to subject

Time per Card: 3-5 minutes once warm.

## CHAPTER CADENCE · LOCKED 2026-05-13

**THE PACING RULE (operator-locked):** Every grid post gets at least one breath day after it. No two chapter posts on consecutive days. This applies to BUILD frames, HERO, and CARD equally. The breath day is the doctrine, not the exception.

**THE CHAPTER ARC (locked sequence):**

```
Build 1 → breath → Build 2 → breath → [Build 3 → breath] → HERO → breath → CARD → breath → breath → next chapter opens
```

**Why the breath day between every post:**
- Each frame holds its slot in the feed instead of getting buried by the next post
- The grid reads as a curated archive, not a content firehose
- Matches BJ's stated cadence rule: HERO → day off → CARD → 1-2 days off → next chapter
- Reinforces Rule 1 (50% chapter exposure rule) over the 30-day window
- Audience trains on the rhythm · they expect breath, the silence becomes part of the chapter

## AUDIENCE FATIGUE DEFENSE

Three rules that protect the audience from getting tired of the rhythm:

### Rule 1 · The 50% chapter exposure rule
Across any 30-day window, no more than 50% of grid posts should be chapter content. The other 50% is:
- Single-frame portraits not tied to a chapter rollout
- Older archived frames re-surfacing
- Process or methodology posts
- Cultural context posts
- Quiet recurring environment posts (a wide of one of the seven environments, no subject)

### Rule 2 · The breathing gap is non-negotiable
Minimum 1-day gap between chapters. Heavy chapters earn 5-7 days. The next chapter does NOT start until the gap is observed.

### Rule 3 · No two HEROs within 7 days
Composite HEROs are the visual peaks of the entire account. Stacking them collapses the peak. Target one HERO every 7-14 days.

## WHAT THIS DOCTRINE BANS

- Skipping the Chapter Card (it's mandatory)
- Skipping the composite HERO (it's mandatory · the chapter doesn't ship as a chapter without one)
- Using the HERO composite as the Card image (the Card uses a different frame)
- Including editorial copy in the caption beyond the locked patterns
- Mentioning the subject's full name in caption (first name only)
- Subject-specific tier treatment (every subject gets the same structure)
- Posting during the breathing gap (1-3 days minimum silence)
- More than one HERO per chapter
- Two HEROs within 7 days
- Hashtags in the caption itself
- Aspirational copy ("elevated," "luxury," "premium," "icon," "boss")
- Engagement bait ("which look?", "thoughts?")
- "Link in bio" CTA in chapter post captions
- Themed issue titles (no "Power Issue" type franchise covers)
