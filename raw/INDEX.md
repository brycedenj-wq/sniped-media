**Last updated: 2026-05-06 · Phase 1 complete · Phase 2 deployed · Spine v1.1 corrections applied · Canonical Truths locked**

The full operating system for SNIPED Media. Every artifact below is deployable. The folder numbering is operational, not aesthetic; lower numbers are referenced more frequently.

**READ AT SESSION START:** [`00_BRIEF/CANONICAL_TRUTHS.md`](./00_BRIEF/CANONICAL_TRUTHS.md) is the highest-tier doc in the workspace. The 12 locked truths override anything in any other doc that conflicts. Headlines: 3 engines (Revenue · Audience · Reputation) not 2 · IG is not optional · premium and accessible (not expensive and inaccessible) · controlled abundance delivery · 3 free categories distinct · becoming known not just booking · long game / short cycle · engineering is constraint AND leverage · DTLA studio (Eagle Rock = home only) · Art Series ≠ Phase 1 revenue · Direction Stack is authority asset · MVMM is a single canonical loop.

**Spine v1.1 (still active where it doesn't conflict with canon):** the bottom of `SNIPED_OS_OPERATING_BRIEF.md` contains 6 corrections that override v1.0 conflicts: (1) free shoots are a ceiling not a quota, (2) Events/Promoter/Community is a primary engine equal to LinkedIn/VIB (now refined into the Reputation Engine per canon), (3) the product is operator-grade visual + cultural infrastructure, not photography, (4) engineering background is positioning leverage, make it explicit, (5) cultural documentation is part of the spine, (6) manual first, automation only behind proven friction.
