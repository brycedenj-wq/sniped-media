# Revenue Growth Diagnostic

**Skill ID:** 7.1  
**Category:** Business Operations & Finance  
**Source Document(s):** Personal_MBA_Business_Mastery_FRAMEWORKS.docx

## Purpose

Systematically evaluate all four revenue levers (prices, frequency, transaction size, customer count) to identify the highest-leverage growth opportunity.

## When to Use

When validating business ideas, new features, or market opportunities. When you need real data from customers rather than opinions.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. First, evaluate Increase Prices: Highest leverage, lowest cost. Can a small increase be implemented? What is the price sensitivity of current customers?
2. Second, evaluate Increase Purchase Frequency: Subscriptions, loyalty programs, reactivation campaigns for lapsed customers.
3. Third, evaluate Increase Transaction Size: Bundling, upsells, cross-sells, premium tiers.
4. Fourth, evaluate Increase Customer Count: New channels, marketing, partnerships. Most expensive lever; only pursue after maximizing the first three.
5. For each lever, estimate the revenue impact and implementation cost/effort.
6. Prioritize by ROI: impact divided by effort. Implement in priority order.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- A four-lever revenue analysis with estimated impact per lever
- Priority-ranked action plan (highest ROI first)
- Implementation timeline with expected revenue impact per phase
