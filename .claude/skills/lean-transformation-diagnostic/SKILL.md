# Lean Transformation Diagnostic (4P Assessment)

**Skill ID:** 18.3  
**Category:** Operational Excellence (Toyota Way)  
**Source Document(s):** OPERATIONAL_EXCELLENCE_FRAMEWORKS.docx

## Purpose

Assess where an organization actually is in its lean/operational excellence journey using the 4P Model (Philosophy, Process, People, Problem Solving) to identify whether improvements are systemic or superficial.

## When to Use

When a persistent problem resists repeated fix attempts, suggesting the issue is structural rather than personnel-based.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Assess Philosophy: Does leadership consistently prioritize long-term capability over short-term profit? Are decisions referenced to purpose and values? Or is lean treated as a 'cost reduction program'?
2. Assess Process: Are flow, pull, and leveling connected as a system? Do problems surface quickly? Or are lean tools applied in isolation with improvements that don't sustain?
3. Assess People: Are leaders developed from within? Do teams own their processes and solve problems daily? Are partners challenged and supported? Or do lean experts do all the improving?
4. Assess Problem Solving: Is PDCA a daily habit at every level? Are goals aligned through hoshin? Or is problem solving reactive and event-based?
5. Rate each P as 'Doing It' or 'Only Dabbling' with specific evidence.
6. Identify the weakest P: This is where the transformation will stall without intervention.
7. Produce a transformation roadmap addressing all four Ps, not just Process.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- A 4P assessment scorecard with evidence for each level
- Identification of the weakest P (the transformation bottleneck)
- A transformation roadmap addressing all four Ps with prioritized actions

User provides context about their specific situation. The skill guides them through each workflow step sequentially, producing all deliverables listed in the Output Format section. Each step builds on the previous one, and no step should be skipped.
