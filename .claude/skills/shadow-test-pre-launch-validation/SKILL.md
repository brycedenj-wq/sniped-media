# Shadow Test (Pre-Launch Validation)

**Skill ID:** 4.4  
**Category:** Customer Discovery & Market Validation  
**Source Document(s):** Personal_MBA_Business_Mastery_FRAMEWORKS.docx, Extracted_Professional_Intelligence__1_.docx

## Purpose

Validate market demand before building by selling the offering before it exists, using minimal representations to measure real purchase intent.

## When to Use

When validating business ideas, new features, or market opportunities. When you need real data from customers rather than opinions.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Create a minimal representation of the offering: landing page, prototype, mock-up, or written description.
2. Drive real traffic or attention to it: ads, outreach, existing audience.
3. Measure actual purchase intent: signups, pre-orders, letters of intent, deposits. Not compliments or 'I would buy that.'
4. Set a threshold: Define what constitutes validated demand before starting (e.g., X pre-orders in Y days).
5. If demand is confirmed, proceed to build. If not, iterate the offer or abandon.
6. Validate that people are already spending money to solve this problem (check Amazon bestseller ranks, competitor revenue, search volume).

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- Demand validation data (signups, pre-orders, deposits)
- A build/iterate/abandon decision with supporting evidence
- Refined offer based on market response
