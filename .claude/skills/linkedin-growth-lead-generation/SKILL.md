# LinkedIn Growth & Lead Generation System

**Skill ID:** 6.2  
**Category:** Outreach & Lead Generation  
**Source Document(s):** linkedin_strategy_extracted.docx

## Purpose

Operate LinkedIn as a business growth engine using the 3C Framework (Content, Conversation, Conversion) with optimized profile, engagement system, and content strategy.

## When to Use

When developing, evaluating, or stress-testing business strategy. When a team needs to move from vague ambition to concrete, coordinated action.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Optimize profile: Headline = who you help + what you help them do + outcome. Banner = billboard with CTA and social proof. Featured section = lead magnet + top post + testimonial.
2. Write About section as a sales letter: Who you help, how you help, proof, and CTA. Embed keywords prospects search for.
3. Establish one-word ownership: Embed a single concept into headline and content so your audience associates that word with you.
4. Engineer hooks: First three lines determine success. Line 1 = curiosity. Line 2 = data or identity callout. Line 3 = promise immediate value.
5. Allocate 2 hours/week to content creation. 10-15 minutes daily to engagement.
6. Build engagement flywheel: 30+ engagements within 45 minutes of posting. Engage with target audience's content before and after your own posts.
7. Maintain a featured section that funnels attention toward a conversion point.
8. Review analytics monthly. Double down on top-performing formats. Sort by follows gained, not just views.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- Optimized LinkedIn profile (headline, banner, featured, about)
- Weekly content calendar with hook-first posts
- Daily engagement routine schedule
- Monthly analytics review template
