# Cognitive Bias Audit

**Skill ID:** 2.1  
**Category:** Decision-Making & Forecasting  
**Source Document(s):** Cognitive_Bias_Decision_Architecture_FRAMEWORKS.docx, Mental_Models_Worldly_Wisdom_FRAMEWORKS.docx

## Purpose

Run a systematic pre-decision diagnostic to detect and counteract the most common cognitive biases before making any high-stakes judgment.

## When to Use

Before any high-stakes decision where cognitive biases could distort judgment.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Check WYSIATI: Am I building a coherent story from incomplete information? Actively seek disconfirming evidence.
2. Check Anchoring: Was I exposed to a number before estimating? Generate your own estimate independently.
3. Check Availability: Am I judging likelihood by ease of recall? Seek base-rate statistics instead of trusting vividness.
4. Check Representativeness: Am I judging by similarity to a stereotype? Look at actual base rates and sample sizes.
5. Check Overconfidence: Am I more certain than the evidence justifies? Assign explicit probabilities. Run a premortem.
6. Check Loss Aversion: Am I avoiding a loss more than pursuing an equivalent gain? Reframe as one of many decisions.
7. Check Sunk Cost: Am I continuing because of past investment rather than future value? Ask only what is the best decision from this point forward.
8. Check Planning Fallacy: Is my estimate based on the inside view? Seek the outside view (base rate for this type of project).
9. Check Framing: Would my choice change if the same facts were presented differently? Reframe from both gain and loss perspectives.
10. Document findings and adjust the decision accordingly.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- A completed Bias Audit Checklist with findings for each bias category
- Identified biases with specific evidence of their influence
- Adjusted decision recommendation with debiasing corrections applied
