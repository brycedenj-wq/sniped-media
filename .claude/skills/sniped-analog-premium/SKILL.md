## THE FRAME
Sax's central insight: when a category gets fully digitized + AI-commoditized, the analog version becomes a PREMIUM TIER. Examples:
- Vinyl records (post-streaming, vinyl outsells CDs)
- Paper books (post-Kindle, indie bookstores compounding)
- Film photography (post-digital, film cameras prices 10x)
- Print magazines (post-blog, premium print magazines compounding)
- Live shows (post-streaming, live music economy 10x)

The mechanism: scarcity + ritual + physical permanence + status signal. Once everyone can get the digital version free, the analog becomes the premium.

For SNIPED in 2026: photography is being AI-commoditized. The analog premium move is:
- Real photographs (vs AI-generated) = the moat
- Print editions = the upsell
- Physical Direction Stack book = the perennial asset
- Studio shoot day rituals = the analog hospitality layer
- Signed prints = the named-photographer signal

## OUTPUT
- Whether the situation has analog-premium leverage available
- The specific analog move (print, ritual, physical edition, real-photo signal)
- The pricing premium the analog move supports (typically 3-10x)
- Phase trigger for analog moves that need scale (most are Phase B+)

## REFUSE
- "Digital is good enough" framing
- Adding analog overhead without premium pricing to match
- Analog moves before Phase B trigger if they require fulfillment infrastructure
- Treating analog as nostalgia · it's premium positioning, not throwback aesthetic
