## THE 12 PHOTOSHOP ASSEMBLY SUB-STEPS

Per `track_b_frame_walkthrough.md` Step 5. Walk in order, do not skip.

5.1 Document setup (place plate behind subject TIFF)
5.2 Convert subject to Smart Object
5.3 Mask subject (Select Subject → Select and Mask → Refine Edge brush on hair)
5.4 **Hair edge cleanup · Overlay-mode brush trick** (the Gress move)
5.5 **Harmonize at 40-50%** (v3 LUXURY restraint override · NOT 75%)
5.6 Edge light from plate's dominant color (Overlay blend + Blend If restriction)
5.7 Unifying grain layer (50% gray, Overlay, Noise 8-12%)
5.8 **Color wash 3-4% max** (luxury restraint override)
5.9 Vignette / focus pull (Exposure adjustment masked off face)
5.10 Catchlight enhancement (Overlay layer, tiny white brush)
5.11 Final review (toggle Harmonize on/off, should snap together)
5.12 Save .psd + Export JPG Q90 sRGB

Plus the CEILING moves beyond Track B (when frame is truly portfolio anchor):
- Light wrap (atmospheric integration trick · plate color wraps INTO subject edges)
- Atmospheric haze layer between subject and far background (depth)
- Sharpening differential (subject crisp, plate slightly less)
- Final color sampling pass (eyedropper plate spots, push subject toward palette)
