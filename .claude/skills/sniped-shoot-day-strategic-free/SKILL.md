# SNIPED Strategic Free Shoot Day Skill

The free / collab / Cultural Doc shoot day runbook. Output target: a shoot that compounds reach, portfolio, and relationships without setting price-anchor problems.

1. `/Users/sniper/Downloads/    SNIPED_OS/05_PRODUCTION/SOP_strategic_free.md` · the locked SOP

## INVOKE WHEN
- FreeCollab / Cultural Doc shoot today
- Considering a strategic free request
- "Should I do this for free"
- Designing the strategic free experience differently from Reset

## OUTPUT
Walk in order, calibrate against the locked SOP:
1. Pre-shoot: confirm trade terms explicitly (reach, content rights, named credit)
2. Shoot day register: lighter setup, more documentary, less production
3. Direction Stack still runs but lighter version
4. Deliverables: 4-8 frames (vs 8-12 for Reset · less because price is reach, not cash)
5. Post-shoot: same SLA as Reset (7 day for strategic free per CURRENT_STATE.md)
6. Always get something written (testimonial, named credit, IG tag commit, intro to one specific person)

## REFUSE
- Strategic free shoots without explicit trade terms
- Treating strategic free as discounted Reset (different SOP)
- Promising Reset-tier deliverables on free shoots
- Trading for "exposure" alone (need specific value)


## Inputs
- Subject name and the strategic value being traded (reach, testimonial, named credit, referral, content rights)
- Whether this is FreeCollab, Cultural Doc, or portfolio-reach session
- SOP_strategic_free.md, delivery_architecture_v2.md, feedback_referral_handling.md, intel_blockbuster_strategy.md (all mandatory reads)
- Explicit trade terms (must be present; if absent, skill must surface this as a blocker)

## Gates
- REFUSE: proceeding without explicit written trade terms (exposure alone fails the gate)
- REFUSE: treating this as a discounted Reset (different SOP, different deliverable count, different register)
- REFUSE: promising Reset-tier deliverables (8-12 frames) on a free shoot
- Gate: intel_blockbuster_strategy.md must be read to assess whether this shoot earns hero-treatment bet allocation
