# Copywriting with Rule of One & Awareness Staging

**Skill ID:** 5.4  
**Category:** Communication & Messaging  
**Source Document(s):** copywriting_frameworks.docx

## Purpose

Write conversion copy that targets one reader, advances one idea, presents one offer, and drives one action, calibrated to the prospect's current stage of awareness.

## When to Use

When crafting any business communication that needs to be clear, memorable, and drive action.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Define the One Reader: A single, specific ideal customer. Write as if addressing them personally.
2. Determine the prospect's Stage of Awareness: Unaware, Problem Aware, Solution Aware, Product Aware, or Most Aware.
3. Match the opening to the awareness stage: Unaware = identity appeal; Problem Aware = show understanding; Solution Aware = prove your product achieves it; Product Aware = reiterate and prove; Most Aware = irresistible offer.
4. Identify the One Idea: The single core benefit that satisfies the reader's most intense conscious desire.
5. Structure the One Offer: Deliverables, cost, and delivery method clear enough to picture exactly.
6. Define the One Action: A single measurable action per page. No competing CTAs.
7. Apply the Big Idea criteria: Is it (a) easy to understand, (b) easy to believe, and (c) interesting or counterintuitive?
8. Layer proof: Use Voice of Customer data ranked by intensity/frequency, matched to product benefits.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- Copy structured around the Rule of One (One Reader, One Idea, One Offer, One Action)
- Awareness stage classification with matched headline and opening
- A proof stack using real Voice of Customer data
