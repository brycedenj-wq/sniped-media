## THE FRAME
The collapsing middle: low-end commoditizes, high-end premiumizes, middle dies.

The 3-ladder (Silverstein):
1. **Technical** · "this is better made"
2. **Functional** · "this does more"
3. **Emotional** · "this means something"

Premium lanes win on emotional ladder primarily, with technical/functional as supporting proof. SNIPED's emotional ladder = founder identity, status signal, methodology authorship.

Trade-up dynamic: customers will pay 10x for emotional resonance in SELECT categories. Photography for founder identity IS one of those categories. They're not paying for a photo · they're paying for identity infrastructure.

## OUTPUT
- The trade-up emotional motive in this specific situation
- Which ladder (T/F/E) the move should optimize for
- Why the "middle tier" version would fail
- Specific positioning language that names the emotional outcome (without explicitly status-claiming · per `sniped-status-psychology`)

## REFUSE
- Adding a "budget" tier below Reset (collapses positioning into the dying middle)
- Volume-tier framing
- Pure technical/functional positioning that ignores emotional layer
- "Affordable luxury" anywhere · the phrase is contradictory and reads as middle-tier collapse
