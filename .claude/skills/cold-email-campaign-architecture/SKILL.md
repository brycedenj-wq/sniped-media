# Cold Email Campaign Architecture

**Skill ID:** 6.1  
**Category:** Outreach & Lead Generation  
**Source Document(s):** cold_email_extraction.docx

## Purpose

Build and execute cold email campaigns with proper infrastructure, targeting, sequencing, and deliverability using the three-email sequence model and value-first offer architecture.

## When to Use

When building AI-powered workflows, automations, or systems that need to operate reliably at scale.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Set up infrastructure: Max 30 emails per inbox per day. 5 accounts per domain. Warm up new inboxes 2-3 weeks minimum.
2. Build the ICP Waterfall: Layer data-driven triggers (company age, funding status, leadership experience) in priority order.
3. Tailor messaging intensity: More triggers present = more personalized, higher-effort message.
4. Lead with a value-first offer: Something tangible the recipient can use immediately (free audit, custom deliverable, specific data).
5. Cap sequences at three emails maximum. Rotate value propositions across sequences (save time > make money > raise status).
6. Use plain text only. No HTML, images, or embedded links in the body. No PDFs, Loom videos, or Calendly links.
7. Use spintax for variations. Verify email lists through multi-step verification before sending.
8. Target 40-60% open rates as a deliverability health signal. Below 30% indicates inbox placement problems.
9. Re-engage your Total Addressable Market every quarter with fresh angles to prevent list fatigue.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- Infrastructure setup checklist (domains, inboxes, warm-up schedule)
- ICP Waterfall with trigger-based targeting tiers
- Three-email sequence with rotating value propositions
- Deliverability monitoring dashboard (open rates, reply rates, bounce rates)
