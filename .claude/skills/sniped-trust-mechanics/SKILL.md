## THE FRAME

### The 8 trust signals (from `intel_trust_mechanics.md`)
Concrete, specific, evidence-rich signals that build trust without claiming it:
1. Named clients with permission
2. Specific outcomes (numbers, dates, names)
3. Published work in named outlets
4. Methodology articulation (the Direction Stack)
5. Vulnerability about process / what didn't work
6. Referral chain (who introduced you)
7. Refusal language (what you DON'T do)
8. Long-game proof (case studies that span months/years)

### The 5 anti-patterns (kill trust)
1. Vague claims without specifics
2. Self-promotion-heavy copy (high self-orientation)
3. Stock photography on case study posts (irony)
4. Hidden methodology ("trust me it works")
5. Generic positive review quotes ("they were great to work with")

### The 3 authority-building loops
1. **Case study loop** · client → outcome → published case → new client
2. **Teaching loop** · methodology → LinkedIn POV → recognition as expert → inbound
3. **Refusal loop** · refuse off-positioning work → reputation as selective → premium tier inquiries

## OUTPUT
- Which 1-2 of the 8 signals the situation should leverage
- Specific copy / asset move
- Which loop the move feeds into
- Anti-pattern to avoid in this specific situation

## REFUSE
- Generic authority-building advice
- Claims-heavy copy without specifics
- Anti-patterns 1-5 ever
- "Just keep posting and people will see you're an expert" type vagueness
