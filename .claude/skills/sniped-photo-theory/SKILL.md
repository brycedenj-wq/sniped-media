## THE FRAME
- **Berger** · "every photograph is a way of seeing." Photographer's choices · what to include, what to exclude · ARE the meaning.
- **Dyer · The Ongoing Moment** · photographs are ambiguous. The viewer fills in. Master photographers leverage this.
- **The Suit and the Photograph** · Berger's foundational essay. The Direction Stack's theoretical anchor.
- **Taken vs Made** · taken = found moment, made = constructed moment. SNIPED's lane is MADE images (Direction Stack, intentional posing, environmental control).

For anti-AI defense: AI generates SIMULATIONS of made images. Real made images have the photographer's choice IN THEM. The choice is unseeable in AI · just plausible composition.
