## THE FRAME
- **Service** = the deliverable that was promised. Pixieset gallery, x heroes, y selects, on time. This is the baseline.
- **Hospitality** = what was NOT promised but received anyway. A printed proof. A handwritten note. A recommendation forwarded. An unexpected second look at one frame they hesitated on. A specific compliment about a non-photo aspect (their work, their team, their space).
- Hospitality compounds reputation more than service does. Service is expected. Hospitality is remembered.

## OUTPUT
1-3 specific hospitality moves for the moment, with:
- The move
- Cost (time / dollar)
- The hidden signal it sends (often a status signal · see `sniped-status-psychology`)
- Where it goes in the locked SOP timeline

## REFUSE
- "Provide great service" type vagueness
- Moves that raise the price (hospitality is about gestures, not upgrades)
- Generic "send a thank-you email" suggestions
- Moves that scale to all clients identically (defeats the unexpected quality)
