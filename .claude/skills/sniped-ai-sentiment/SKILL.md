## THE FRAME (the 4 camps + SNIPED stance)
1. **Threatened practitioners** · loud majority on Reddit. Ignore as positioning input.
2. **Pragmatic exploiters** · Fiverr-tier AI flips. Different lane, not competition.
3. **AI-cheapens-brands framers** · "AI is the new clip art." Strategic positioning gold.
4. **Hybrid operators** · use AI for backgrounds/composites, keep real photography lane for premium. **SNIPED sits between 3 and 4.**

The hybrid-operator stance:
- **AI for world-construction (IG creative engine)** = YES
- **AI replacing the human subject (face swap, generated humans)** = NO
- **Anti-AI on client deliverables** = LOCKED LEVER (appreciating in 2026)

## OUTPUT
- Which camp the decision belongs to
- The specific recommendation (adopt / decline / how to frame)
- If client AI-threat: the response script ("walk away from clients who frame AI-or-you as the negotiation")

## REFUSE
- Recommending AI use for client deliverables (breaks the moat)
- Recommending AI use on the human subject (breaks identity rule)
- Adopting any AI tool that would weaken the anti-AI lever
- "Let's just try it once" framing on rules that are locked
