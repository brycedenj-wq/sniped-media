# Premortem Analysis

**Skill ID:** 2.2  
**Category:** Decision-Making & Forecasting  
**Source Document(s):** Cognitive_Bias_Decision_Architecture_FRAMEWORKS.docx, SUPERFORECASTING_FRAMEWORKS.docx

## Purpose

Debiase project planning by having the team imagine a future failure and working backward to identify the causes, overcoming groupthink and overconfidence.

## When to Use

Before any high-stakes decision where cognitive biases could distort judgment.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Gather the team after a plan has been developed but before execution begins.
2. Give the instruction: 'Imagine it is one year from now. We implemented the plan exactly as written. The outcome was a disaster.'
3. Each person independently writes a brief history of that disaster (5-10 minutes, no discussion).
4. Each person shares their failure narrative with the group.
5. Collect and categorize all identified failure modes. Group by theme.
6. Assess which failure modes are most probable and most consequential.
7. Adjust the plan to mitigate the top failure modes. Add contingency triggers.
8. Document the premortem findings as a risk register for ongoing monitoring.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- A categorized list of failure modes with probability and impact ratings
- Plan modifications addressing top risks
- A risk register with contingency triggers and assigned owners
