# Business Resilience Audit

**Skill ID:** 7.2  
**Category:** Business Operations & Finance  
**Source Document(s):** Personal_MBA_Business_Mastery_FRAMEWORKS.docx

## Purpose

Assess business vulnerability across seven resilience factors and identify single points of failure before they trigger crises.

## When to Use

When evaluating business operations, financial structure, or revenue growth opportunities.

## Instructions

Follow this workflow precisely. Each step is grounded in the source document(s) listed above. Do not skip steps. Do not invent frameworks, models, or terminology not present in the source material. Execute each step in order, using the exact logic and decision criteria documented.

## Workflow

1. Assess outstanding debt: Could the business survive 6 months with no new revenue?
2. Assess overhead and fixed costs: What is the minimum monthly expense to keep operating?
3. Assess cash reserves: Do you have 3-6 months of expenses in liquid reserves?
4. Assess revenue diversification: If the largest revenue source disappeared, would you survive?
5. Assess team flexibility: Can team members handle multiple responsibilities in a crisis?
6. Assess single points of failure: Is there any one person, system, or input whose loss would halt operations?
7. Assess fail-safes: Do backup systems exist for every critical function? Are they tested?
8. Score each factor and produce a resilience rating with priority fixes.

## Output Format

Produce all of the following deliverables:

- A seven-factor resilience scorecard
- Identified vulnerabilities ranked by severity
- Priority action plan for improving resilience
